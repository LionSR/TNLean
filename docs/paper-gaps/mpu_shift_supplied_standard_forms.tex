\section{Source statement}

Definition~SF of Ref.~\cite{Cirac2017MPU} defines the standard form of the
two-site block $\mathcal U_2$ of a simple tensor $\mathcal U$ in canonical
form~II. Its gates $u$ and $v$ are the unitaries obtained from the source cuts
of $\mathcal U$.
% Source: paper_v2.tex lines 603--622, label SF.

Let $\mathbb S$ be the swap of two $d$-dimensional spins, with entries
$\mathbb S_{(p,q),(r,s)}=\delta_{p,s}\delta_{q,r}$ on
$\mathbb C^d\otimes\mathbb C^d$. For the three shift families of
Section~VII, the source says that the gates ``can be easily derived'' from
the figure of the shifted spins and displays
\begin{align}
    u_1 & =\Id\otimes\Id, & v_1 & =\Id\otimes\Id, \\
    u_3 & =\mathbb S,     & v_3 & =\mathbb S,
    \label{eq:mpu_shift_supplied_standard_forms_source_gates}
\end{align}
together with the blocked gates $u_2^{(2)},v_2^{(2)},u_3^{(2)},v_3^{(2)}$ of
the two counterpropagating families. For the swap-transformed families it
displays $\widetilde u_1=\mathbb S$, $\widetilde v_1=\Id\otimes\Id$,
$\widetilde u_2=\Id\otimes\Id$, and $\widetilde v_2=\mathbb S$.
% Source: paper_v2.tex lines 2002--2034 (eq:SF_u1_u3, eq:uv2_U2, eq:uv2_U3)
% and 2087--2099 (SFu1u3).

\section{Formalized scope}

For each displayed pair of gates, the formalization constructs an explicit
two-site decomposition with supplied gates. The gates are the displayed
unitaries, the half factors are normalized, and their open contraction
recovers the tensor.

For the blocked counterpropagating families, the half factors are pulled back
from an explicit source-cut factorization with trace-one weight
$d^{-2}\Id_{d^2}$. The gates are the source gates of that factorization.

For $U_1$, the unblocked $U_3$, $\widetilde U_1$, and $\widetilde U_2$, the
one-site tensor of physical dimension $d^2$ is read as a pair of spins and
decomposed directly. These decompositions are not identified with the
standard form of Definition~SF: the tensor is not presented as the two-site
block of a simple canonical-form-II tensor, and its gates are not identified
with the source gates of a source-cut factorization.

\section{Elimination plan}

For each unblocked family, present the one-site tensor as the two-site block
of a simple tensor in canonical form~II, or block it further as done for the
counterpropagating families. Then identify the supplied gates with the source
gates of an explicit source-cut factorization of that tensor.
