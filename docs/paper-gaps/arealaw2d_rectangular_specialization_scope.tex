\section{Source statement}

For fixed $q\ge1$ and $J,\Delta>0$, Corollary 1.2 of
\cite{OpenAI2026AreaLaw} asserts a uniform entropy bound for every subset
of a finite rectangular lattice, and the two vertex-boundary forms.
Let $V=\{0,\ldots,w-1\}\times\{0,\ldots,h-1\}$ with open
nearest-neighbor edges. Write $H=\sum_v h_v+\sum_e h_e$, where each term
has its designated support and operator norm at most $J$.
Only $H=H^*$ is needed: replacing a term by $(h_i+h_i^*)/2$ leaves the
sum unchanged and does not increase its norm.
For a unit ground vector of energy $E_0$ satisfying
$H-E_0I\ge\Delta(I-|\Omega\rangle\langle\Omega|)$, the source conclusion is
\begin{align}
 S_\Omega(A)&\le C|\partial_V A|,
 &S_\Omega(A)&\le4C|\partial_{\rm in}A|,
 &S_\Omega(A)&\le2C|Z_A|.
 \label{eq:rectangular_scope_bounds}
\end{align}
The same $C=C(q,1,J,\Delta)$ is chosen before the side lengths, matrices,
vector and cut. Empty boundaries are allowed. For $q=1$ or an empty
physical domain the source explicitly states that every entropy vanishes.
\footnote{Immutable source: \texttt{openai/math} revision
\texttt{adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a}, area-law
\texttt{00-introduction.tex}, lines 12--85, and
\texttt{01-preliminaries.tex}, lines 118--132. The Hermitian-total model is
also explicit in the polynomial-PEPS paper, \texttt{00-introduction.tex},
lines 36--49.}

\section{Established reduction and remaining premise}

The rectangular interaction construction uses the native site and undirected
edge families directly. Singleton and pair supports are injective labels.
The Hermitian parts are placed on their corresponding integer-domain
supports, with zero at every other admissible support.
The resulting sum is exactly $UHU^*$ for the canonical configuration
isometry $U$; the unit vector, gap, regional entropy and all three boundary
cardinalities are transported by proved identities.

The current theorem derives~(\ref{eq:rectangular_scope_bounds}) from the
uniform finite-domain area law as an explicit analytic premise.
It does not derive that premise from the physical hypotheses alone.
Thus this is the implication from Theorem 1.1 to Corollary 1.2, rather than
an unconditional proof of the corollary. No assumption supplies the desired
coordinate identities or an entropy inequality for the specific rectangle.

The remaining step is to prove the uniform finite-domain area law from its
source hypotheses and apply this reduction. The existing square-grid
entropy statement is recovered by setting both side lengths equal, without
altering that statement or supplying any new analytic hypothesis.

This reduction does not establish the polynomial-PEPS approximation theorem,
the region construction, collar amplification, or the source's unconditional
area law. The present candidate still requires hosted kernel and linter
validation before any completion marking.
