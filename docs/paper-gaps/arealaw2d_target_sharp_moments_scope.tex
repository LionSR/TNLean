The source sharp lower estimate of Ref.~\cite{OpenAI2026AreaLaw} requires
$10a\leq r_{\rm mom}$ and $10a\leq1$ at $t=a/2$.
These imply the upper estimate's factor-six conditions for the same positive
$a$.  The single-target construction retains the literal floors for $L,m$,
the natural-number division for $K=L/(8m)$ and the ceiling for $D$.
Its constants precede the scanner exponents and every physical instance.
Its threshold may depend on the explicit positive margin
\begin{align}
    \omega+\frac{1+\kappa}{2}&<1-\ell-\mu.
    \label{eq:target_sharp_scope_margin}
\end{align}
The ordinary scanner assumptions alone do not imply this inequality.
At the source's final parameters, its margin is $499879/2000000$.

The same truncated ground vector, energy, spectral gap, phase and trace
estimates, target eigenvalue window and positive mass are retained while
deriving the five physical status moments.  The common budget is
$B=C_BnD(\log n)^{12}$, so
$K_{\rm mom}=C_0B$ and $r_{\rm mom}=c_0/\sqrt B$.
The resulting bounds hold for every $0<W\leq n^\omega$ and therefore do
not require selecting another physical state or another target set when
the two sharp consumers use one supplied label sequence.
