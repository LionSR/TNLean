\section{Source and precise interpretation}
The discussion and outlook of~\cite{Malz2024Preparation} proposes
logarithmic-depth conversion of matrix product states in the same phase.
Equation (25) and the paragraph ``Long-range MPS using measurements''
provide the simultaneous sector construction relevant to that claim.
They do not specify a canonical logical basis of the whole sector span,
an operator-norm conversion bound, or an external-reference statement.
The operator-norm and all-accuracy results here give a precise whole-encoding
enhancement of that discussion, not a formalization of a new phase predicate.

For an explicitly labelled family of $b$ retained sectors, define the actual
column matrix and its fixed polar encoder by
\begin{align}
    V_{A,N}e_j & =|\phi_N(A_j)\rangle,                     &
    F_{A,N}    & =V_{A,N}(V_{A,N}^\dagger V_{A,N})^{-1/2}.
\end{align}
The inverse is taken on the support. The endpoint depends on the family and
ring length, and is independent of the requested accuracy or the partition
used to approximate it. Its range is exactly the original periodic-sector
span, and independent columns make it an isometry.

\section{Proved finite-length bounds}
The QICLean companion owns the generic estimates. If $W$ is an isometry,
$\eta=\|V^\dagger V-I\|$ and $\zeta=\|W^\dagger V-I\|$, then
$\|V-W\|^2\le\eta+2\zeta$. Writing $F$ for the polar isometry of $V$, the square-root bound at the
identity and the polar factorization give
\begin{align}
    \|F-W\|\le\sqrt{\eta+2\zeta}+\eta
\end{align}
for injective $V$. The cross matrix is complex; replacing its entries by
absolute overlaps would discard the relative logical phases.

One register encoding $J$ is an isometry on the logical space. The existing
simultaneous block-unitary construction gives $U_XJ=W_X$ as a whole matrix,
with $W_X$ isometric and depth at most a constant times the maximum block
length. The same chosen circuit acts on all logical amplitudes. For two
such physical unitaries, $U=U_BU_A^\dagger$ has depth $T_A+T_B$ and
\begin{align}
    \|UF_{A,N}-F_{B,N}\|
     & \le\|F_{A,N}-W_A\|+\|F_{B,N}-W_B\|.
\end{align}
There is no cost for producing the common seed from a product state: it
cancels algebraically. A measurement protocol is never inverted.

For every finite reference space and every input $\xi$, the same operator
bound $\delta$ yields a vector error at most $\delta\|\xi\|$ after tensoring
with the reference identity. The proof sums squared norms of reference
slices, with no reference-dimension factor. No channel diamond-norm theorem
is asserted by this statement.

\section{Proved exact construction through a fixed virtual space}
For injective virtual placements with disjoint ranges, let $B=\bigoplus_jA_j$
be the unweighted direct sum of bond dimension $D$. Let $C_N$ be its
blocked physical matrix on configurations, $S_N$ its polar partial
isometry, $P_N=(C_N^\dagger C_N)^{1/2}$, and $T$ the sector-trace selector.
The identity $C_NT=V_{A,N}$ gives, with $R_N=P_NT$,
\begin{align}
    V_{A,N}=S_NR_N,\qquad F_{A,N}=S_NQ_N,
\end{align}
where $Q_N$ is the polar partial isometry of $R_N$. The intermediate
space is $\mathbb C^{D^2}$, with dimension independent of the ring length.

If the blocked sum is injective on its sector-pair support and all sector
bond dimensions are positive, the trace selectors show directly that
$V_{A,N}$ is injective. Then $Q_N$ is an isometry supported on that same
virtual set. Its columns, with the one-block cyclic pair orientation,
form an orthonormal family to which the simultaneous genuine-unitary
compiler applies. For fixed encodings into registers of width $r\ge2$,
a constant $C$ is chosen before the tensors and $N$. At any supported-
injective length $N\ge3r$, one circuit satisfies $U_NJ_N=F_{A,N}$ exactly
and has depth at most $CN$. Pair support is needed only at the actual
single block. The whole logical encoder is transported; the construction
uses fixed-width pair synthesis and sequential blocked-polar unitaries.

\section{Proved uniform logarithmic-depth conversion}
Fix two explicitly matched families of $b>0$ distinct normal sectors with
common physical dimension $d\ge2$. Each sector is left canonical and has
a faithful stationary density of trace one; mixed-transfer spectral radii
within each family are strictly less than one. The formal theorem chooses
$C>0$ and $N_0\ge2$ before the ring length and accuracy. For every
$N\ge N_0$, the fixed encoders are isometries, and for every
$\varepsilon\in(0,1]$ one genuine nearest-neighbor circuit $U$ satisfies
\begin{align}
    \operatorname{depth}(U) & \le C\log(N/\varepsilon), \\
    \|UF_{A,N}-F_{B,N}\|    & \le\varepsilon.
\end{align}
The same chosen $U$ satisfies, for every finite reference space $\mathcal R$
and every logical-reference vector $\xi$,
\begin{align}
    \|[(UF_{A,N}-F_{B,N})\otimes I_{\mathcal R}]\xi\|
    \le\varepsilon\|\xi\|.
\end{align}
The endpoints remain independent of accuracy and of the selected partition.
\footnote{Formal declaration:
    \leanid{MPSPreparation.exists_log_depth_coherent_sectorEncoder_conversion}.}

The proof first obtains Gram and complex cross-matrix estimates with sums
over the fixed logical dimension. One common decay rate, register width
and eventual support-injectivity cutoff serve both families. If the common
block scale $q$ fits inside the ring, one partition has block lengths between
$q$ and $2q$. The two genuine encoders share its logical seed, so their
relative unitary has the sum of their depths and endpoint errors.
The choice $q=\lceil(2/r)\log(N/\varepsilon)+c_0\rceil$ accounts for the
square root in the endpoint estimate. If $q>N$, the exact fixed-width
whole-encoder construction above has depth $O(N)\le O(q)$ and gives the
same logarithmic bound. No product-state preparation of the logical seed
is charged, and no measurement protocol is inverted.

\section{Parent kernels at a valid interaction range}
For supplied canonical data of a tensor with physical and bond dimensions
$d,D\ge1$, the encoder of its retained normal representatives has range
exactly equal to the kernel of the range-$L$ parent Hamiltonian whenever
$3D^5\le L\le N$, including $N=L$.
\footnote{Formal declaration:
    \leanid{MPSTensor.CPSVCanonicalFormData.range_sectorEncoder_eq_ker_parentHamiltonian}.}
This is an identity of the actual periodic-sector span. Applying the
conversion theorem to such representatives still requires its explicit
canonical hypotheses and a matching of the labels; no additional gauge
substitution or column rescaling is implicit.

\section{Chosen vectors and boundaries}
Transporting a fixed logical basis does not equate the normalized periodic
amplitudes of two tensors. The biased-versus-balanced ferromagnetic example
and its chosen-vector obstruction in
\cite{gap:mswc24_same_phase_compatible_seed} remain applicable. No
symmetry-preserving conversion, arbitrary-small-$N$ claim, or implicit
transport through a canonical-gauge normalization is asserted here.
The physical gate model consists of genuine nearest-neighbor unitaries
with unrestricted gate parameters.
