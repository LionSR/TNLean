The source rough-budget argument of Ref.~\cite{OpenAI2026AreaLaw} uses
the original target entropy assumption as well as the whole-row prefix
assumption.  Regional continuity supplies the same truncated target entropy
within $n^{-247}$, uniformly over finite systems of controlled quadratic
volume.  The selected-spectrum entropy estimate concerns the same target
set and mass, and the rough endpoint theorem concerns the same supplied
Schmidt--Bell sequence.

The scalar rough budget is at most
$(6C_e+13+6\log q)Wn^{1+e}$ when $W\geq1$ and the literal geometry gives
$L\leq n$ and $D\leq2n^e$.  Its target-entropy, selected-dimension and
shell-entropy premises are precisely the established physical conclusions
to be supplied by the source application.  No abstract terminal-bound or
selected-density field is used.
