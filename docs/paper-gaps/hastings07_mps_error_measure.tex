\section{The source statement}

The section on matrix product states of~\cite{Hastings2007AreaLaw} concludes
that the error of a matrix product state of bond dimension $k'$ on a chain of
length $N$ is of order
\begin{align}
    N\,(k'/k_0)^{-1/(\xi'\ln D)}.
    \label{eq:hastings07_mps_error_source}
\end{align}
It does not name the error measure.\footnote{\path{References/0705.2024/law16.tex},
    lines~553--557.}
Let $\Phi$ be the vector obtained by truncating every cut onto its $k'$
leading Schmidt vectors, and let $\tau_j(k')$ be the tail of the Schmidt
coefficients beyond the $k'$ largest at the cut $(j,j+1)$. The bound of
Verstraete and Cirac~\cite{VerstraeteCirac2006Faithfully},
\begin{align}
    \lVert\Phi-\Psi_0\rVert^2
     & \le2\sum_{j=1}^{N-1}\tau_j(k'),
    \label{eq:hastings07_mps_error_vc}
\end{align}
together with the polynomial decay
$\tau_j(k')\le c\,e^{2/\xi'}(k'/k_0)^{-1/(\xi'\ln D)}$ of the tail, gives
(\ref{eq:hastings07_mps_error_source}) for the squared norm.

\section{What is formalized}

The formalized bound on sequential truncation is
$\lVert\Phi-\Psi_0\rVert\le\sum_j\sqrt{\tau_j(k')}$, so the theorem
proved\footnote{\leanid{TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.Hastings.isOpenMPS_norm_sub_le_of_schmidtTail_geometric}
    in \doclink{TNLean/MPS/AreaLaw/Hastings/SchmidtTailMPS}.} is
\begin{align}
    \lVert\Phi-\Psi_0\rVert
     & \le(N-1)\sqrt{c\,e^{2/\xi'}}\,(k'/k_0)^{-1/(2\xi'\ln D)}.
    \label{eq:hastings07_mps_error_norm}
\end{align}
Squaring (\ref{eq:hastings07_mps_error_norm}) gives
(\ref{eq:hastings07_mps_error_source}) with $N^2$ in place of $N$. The bound
is weaker than the source's by a factor $N$ in the squared norm. The bond
dimension needed for a given accuracy is still polynomial in $N$ and in the
inverse accuracy, with a larger exponent.

\section{Elimination}

Formalize the squared-norm bound (\ref{eq:hastings07_mps_error_vc}) for
sequential truncation and specialize the theorem to it. This is tracked in
TNLean issue~9941.
