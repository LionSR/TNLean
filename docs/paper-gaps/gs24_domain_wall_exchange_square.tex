\title{Exchange of Domain-Wall Strings: the Square of the Exchange Phase}
\author{}
\date{2026-10-02}

\begin{document}
\maketitle
\gapnote{scope-restriction}{open}

Let $U=\bigotimes O_g$ be a matrix product unitary representation of an
involution $g$ acting on a periodic chain, and let $\ket{\psi_A}$ and
$\ket{\psi_B}=U\ket{\psi_A}$ be the matrix product states of two
symmetry-broken injective tensors $A$ and $B$. A domain wall $e_{AB}$ from $A$
to $B$ is carried by the local action of $g$ to a domain wall $e_{BA}$ with a
phase $c_{AB}$, and $e_{BA}$ is carried back to $e_{AB}$ with a phase $c_{BA}$.
For sites $i<j$, the string operator $O^{[i,j]}$ applies $O_g$ strictly between
$i$ and $j$ and endpoint tensors at $i$ and $j$; on $\ket{\psi_A}$ it creates
the state $\ket{\psi(A\text{-}B\text{-}A)}$ with the walls $e_{AB}$ at $i$ and
$e_{BA}$ at $j$ \cite{GarreRubioSchuch2024DomainWall}.\footnote{Source
    traceability: \path{Papers/2405.00439/MPU-DW.tex}, lines 643--838 (domain
    walls and their phases) and lines 1341--1425 (string operators).}

For sites $i_2<i_1<j_1<j_2$, Ref.~\cite{GarreRubioSchuch2024DomainWall} states
the exchange of two strings as
\begin{align}
    O^{[i_1,j_1]}O^{[i_2,j_2]}\ket{\psi_A}
     & =c_{AB}c_{BA}\,O^{[i_2,j_2]}O^{[i_1,j_1]}\ket{\psi_A}.
    \label{eq:gs24_domain_wall_exchange_square_printed}
\end{align}\footnote{Source traceability:
    \path{Papers/2405.00439/MPU-DW.tex}, lines 1667--1672.}
The computation places the phase on the other side:
\begin{align}
    O^{[i_2,j_2]}O^{[i_1,j_1]}\ket{\psi_A}
     & =c_{AB}c_{BA}\,O^{[i_1,j_1]}O^{[i_2,j_2]}\ket{\psi_A}.
    \label{eq:gs24_domain_wall_exchange_square_computed}
\end{align}
Relation~(\ref{eq:gs24_domain_wall_exchange_square_computed}) is proved for
every pair of phases.\footnote{Formal declaration:
    \leanid{MPOTensor.GroupFamily.BlockActionData.wallString_mul_wallString_mulVec_mpv}.}
Relations~(\ref{eq:gs24_domain_wall_exchange_square_printed}) and
(\ref{eq:gs24_domain_wall_exchange_square_computed}) agree exactly when
\begin{align}
    (c_{AB}c_{BA})^2 & =1.
    \label{eq:gs24_domain_wall_exchange_square_square}
\end{align}
Ref.~\cite{GarreRubioSchuch2024DomainWall} obtains
(\ref{eq:gs24_domain_wall_exchange_square_square}) from $U^2=\mathrm{id}$:
since $U\ket{\psi(A\text{-}B\text{-}A)}=c_{BA}c_{AB}
    \ket{\psi(B\text{-}A\text{-}B)}$, applying $U$ twice returns
$(c_{BA}c_{AB})^2\ket{\psi(A\text{-}B\text{-}A)}$.\footnote{Source
    traceability: \path{Papers/2405.00439/MPU-DW.tex}, lines 835--839.}

\section{Restriction}

Relation~(\ref{eq:gs24_domain_wall_exchange_square_printed}) is formalized
with (\ref{eq:gs24_domain_wall_exchange_square_square}) as an explicit
hypothesis.\footnote{Formal declaration:
    \leanid{MPOTensor.GroupFamily.BlockActionData.wallString_mul_wallString_mulVec_mpv_of_sq_eq_one}.}
The derivation of that hypothesis from $U^2=\mathrm{id}$ is not formalized.

\section{Elimination plan}

The action of $U$ on the two-wall state is formalized.\footnote{Formal
    declaration:
    \leanid{MPOTensor.GroupFamily.BlockActionData.IsDomainWallAction.mpo_mulVec_twoWallMPV}.}
Applying it twice, with $U^2=\mathrm{id}$ as an identity of periodic
operators at the length considered and a length at which
$\ket{\psi(A\text{-}B\text{-}A)}\neq0$, gives (\ref{eq:gs24_domain_wall_exchange_square_square}). With that
lemma the hypothesis is discharged from the involution, and the printed
relation holds under the source's own assumptions.
