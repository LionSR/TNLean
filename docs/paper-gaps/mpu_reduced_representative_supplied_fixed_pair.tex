\section{Source statement and derivation of the fixed pair}

Proposition~IV.5 of Cirac--P\'erez-Garc\'ia--Schuch--Verstraete considers a
continuous tensor-valued map
$[0,1]\ni x\mapsto\mathcal W(x)$. Each tensor generates an MPU but need not be
in canonical form. The proposition states that the ranks $r(x)$ and $\ell(x)$
of the associated simple tensors are constant
\cite[Proposition~IV.5]{Cirac2017MPU}.

The proof first fixes one tensor $\mathcal W$ generating an MPU. From the MPU
property alone, after blocking $D^4$ sites, it concludes that the blocked
canonical form is simple and that its transfer operator has rank one. As a
completely positive map, this transfer operator has the form
\begin{align}
    X &\longmapsto \tr(LX)\,R.
    \label{eq:mpu_reduced_representative_supplied_fixed_pair_rank_one_transfer}
\end{align}
Here $L\succeq0$ and $R\succeq0$ are positive semidefinite left and right fixed
points normalized by
\begin{align}
    \tr(LR) &= 1.
    \label{eq:mpu_reduced_representative_supplied_fixed_pair_trace_normalization}
\end{align}

The source then reads from the displayed factorization through an isometry
$V$ that the tensor absorbs the support projections on both sides. If $P$ and
$Q$ are the orthogonal projections onto the supports of $L$ and $R$,
respectively, then
\begin{align}
    \mathcal WP
        &=\mathcal W,
    \label{eq:mpu_reduced_representative_supplied_fixed_pair_right_absorption}\\
    Q\mathcal W
        &=\mathcal W.
    \label{eq:mpu_reduced_representative_supplied_fixed_pair_left_absorption}
\end{align}
The argument in Ref.~\cite{Cirac2017MPU} treats the rank-one transfer formula,
positive semidefiniteness, trace normalization, and two-sided support
absorption as consequences of blocking an arbitrary MPU tensor.

\section{The formal scope issue}

The formal reduced-representative calculation starts after this source
derivation. It assumes the fixed pair and its consequences explicitly rather
than deriving them. In letterwise notation, it assumes
\begin{align}
    W^{ij}P
        &=W^{ij},
    \label{eq:mpu_reduced_representative_supplied_fixed_pair_letterwise_right_absorption}\\
    QW^{ij}
        &=W^{ij},
    \label{eq:mpu_reduced_representative_supplied_fixed_pair_letterwise_left_absorption}
\end{align}
together with the rank-one transfer identity
\begin{align}
    E_W(X) &= \tr(LX)\,R,
    \label{eq:mpu_reduced_representative_supplied_fixed_pair_assumed_transfer}
\end{align}
the inequalities $L,R\succeq0$, and the normalization in
Eq.~\ref{eq:mpu_reduced_representative_supplied_fixed_pair_trace_normalization}.
The formal statements do not infer any of these assumptions from the bare MPU
predicate. The module docstring states this limitation directly: all
fixed-point and support hypotheses are explicit.

Starting from the supplied pair, the formal results prove the remainder of the
source argument. Let $T=\widetilde P$ be the reduced projection constructed
from $P$ and $Q$, and define the reduced tensor by
\begin{align}
    \widetilde W^{ij} &= TW^{ij}T.
    \label{eq:mpu_reduced_representative_supplied_fixed_pair_reduced_tensor}
\end{align}
This reduced tensor generates the same periodic MPO at every system size. Its
transfer operator has the compressed rank-one formula with left and right
fixed matrices $TLT$ and $TRT$. Compression preserves the normalization:
\begin{align}
    \tr\!\left((TLT)(TRT)\right) &= 1.
    \label{eq:mpu_reduced_representative_supplied_fixed_pair_compressed_normalization}
\end{align}

Choose isometric coordinates $V$ satisfying
\begin{align}
    V^\dagger V &= I,
    \label{eq:mpu_reduced_representative_supplied_fixed_pair_isometry}\\
    VV^\dagger &= T.
    \label{eq:mpu_reduced_representative_supplied_fixed_pair_isometry_range}
\end{align}
In these coordinates, both $V^\dagger LV$ and $V^\dagger RV$ are positive
definite.

The restriction is substantive. The source obtains the fixed pair by blocking
$D^4$ sites and using the simple canonical-form structure of the blocked
tensor. The unblocked tensor, or an arbitrary representative of it, does not
carry such a pair without this construction. Until that supplier is
formalized, the formal statements apply only to tensors already equipped with
the fixed pair. The source conclusion, by contrast, applies to every MPU tensor
after blocking.

\section{Affected declarations}

The declaration
\leanid{MPOTensor.mpo_virtualSandwich_reducedProjection_eq} assumes that $P$ is
an orthogonal projection and assumes the letterwise absorptions in
Eqs.~\ref{eq:mpu_reduced_representative_supplied_fixed_pair_letterwise_right_absorption}
and
\ref{eq:mpu_reduced_representative_supplied_fixed_pair_letterwise_left_absorption}.
The declaration
\leanid{MPOTensor.transferMap_virtualSandwich_reducedProjection} assumes the
rank-one transfer formula in
Eq.~\ref{eq:mpu_reduced_representative_supplied_fixed_pair_assumed_transfer}.

The declarations
\leanid{MPOTensor.trace_compressed_fixed_pair_reducedProjection} and
\leanid{MPOTensor.compressed_fixed_pair_posDef_reducedProjection} assume,
respectively, that $P$ and $Q$ are orthogonal projections, that $L$ and $R$
satisfy the two-sided support absorptions, and that $\tr(LR)=1$; and that
$L,R\succeq0$ have support projections $P,Q$. The latter declaration also
assumes an isometry $V$ onto the range of $T$.

The comparison declaration
\leanid{MPOTensor.virtualSandwich_hat_eq_reducedProjection} assumes that $P$ is
an orthogonal projection, that the factor equations
\begin{align}
    XL &= P,
    \label{eq:mpu_reduced_representative_supplied_fixed_pair_left_factor}\\
    RZ &= Q,
    \label{eq:mpu_reduced_representative_supplied_fixed_pair_right_factor}\\
    PQY &= T.
    \label{eq:mpu_reduced_representative_supplied_fixed_pair_reduced_right_factor}
\end{align}
hold, and that the tensor satisfies the letterwise absorptions. The rank
equalities
\leanid{MPOTensor.rightRank_hat_eq_reducedProjection} and
\leanid{MPOTensor.leftRank_hat_eq_reducedProjection} use the same supplied
data and additionally assume that $X$, $Z$, and $Y$ are invertible.

Finally, the matrix-level theorem
\leanid{Matrix.exists_units_supportInvExtension_reducedProjection_rightFactor}
assumes only $L,R\succeq0$. It supplies invertible ambient support-inverse
extensions $X$ and $Z$, together with an invertible right factor $Y$ satisfying
Eq.~\ref{eq:mpu_reduced_representative_supplied_fixed_pair_reduced_right_factor};
it does not assume a fixed-point identity, trace normalization, or support
absorption.

\section{Elimination plan}

Removing the restriction requires formalizing the missing supplier. This
supplier must block an arbitrary MPU tensor to $D^4$ sites, construct the
two-projection Jordan decomposition of the resulting support projections, and
prove the isometric factorization that yields both the letterwise absorptions
and the rank-one transfer formula with positive semidefinite normalized fixed
points.

This work is tracked by \ghissue{6137} and \ghissue{6022}.
The former constructs and transports the reduced representative and supplies
the positive fixed pair with letterwise absorptions for the common blocked
tensor. The latter covers the full continuity-index route containing
Proposition~IV.5 of Ref.~\cite{Cirac2017MPU}. Until both obligations are
resolved, no formal statement may infer the fixed pair or the absorption
identities from the bare MPU predicate.

\section{Verdict}

\textbf{Verdict: scope restriction.} The paper derives $L,R\succeq0$ with
$\tr(LR)=1$, the rank-one transfer formula, and the two-sided support
absorption
\begin{align}
    \mathcal WP
        &=\mathcal W,
    \label{eq:mpu_reduced_representative_supplied_fixed_pair_verdict_right_absorption}\\
    Q\mathcal W
        &=\mathcal W.
    \label{eq:mpu_reduced_representative_supplied_fixed_pair_verdict_left_absorption}
\end{align}
from the MPU property after blocking $D^4$ sites. The current formalization
takes these ingredients as explicit hypotheses and proves only the subsequent
reduced-representative conclusions. It does not construct the supplier.

The formal results are correct as stated but have narrower scope than the
argument in Ref.~\cite{Cirac2017MPU}. Their assumptions become derived
theorems only after \ghissue{6137} and \ghissue{6022} are resolved.
