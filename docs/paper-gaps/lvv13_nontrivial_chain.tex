\section{The source statement}

The size-trimming step of Landau, Vazirani and Vidick~\cite{Landau2015Polynomial}
(arXiv:1307.5143v1, \path{algo53.tex}, equation \path{eq:conv}, lines~472--476,
and Lemma \path{l:leading}, lines~493--505) is stated for every chain of $n$
qudits of local dimension $d$.

\section{What is formalized}

Write $L$ for the number of sites and $q$ for the local dimension. The
boundary-contraction and leading-vector theorems assume $L\ge1$ and $q\ge2$. These
restrictions are separate from the constant bond dimension, which is
recorded in the note on the constant bond dimension. They give the estimate
$\Delta\le4$ for a range-one Hamiltonian with interaction norm at most one,
which in turn controls $c=(\Delta/169)^2$ and the energy error in the
leading-vector argument.

\section{Elimination}

For $q=1$, the chain and left Hilbert spaces are one-dimensional.  The theorem
\leanid{TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.exists_size_trimmed_family_localDimension_one}
therefore reduces any viable finite family to one nonzero member, with exact
viability and without increasing its bond bound.  This proves the degenerate
local-dimension case directly, without a gap upper bound.

For the empty chain, the theorem
\leanid{TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.exists\_size\_trimmed\_family\_chainLength\_zero}
gives the same singleton reduction directly.  Its statement avoids the
quantities $c/L$ and $c/(2L)$, which have no meaning at $L=0$.  Thus both
degenerate cases excluded from the boundary-contraction proof have separate
formal statements, and the scope restriction is resolved.
