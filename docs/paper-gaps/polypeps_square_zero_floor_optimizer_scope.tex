\section{The source statement}

The companion source of~\cite{OpenAI2026PolynomialPEPS} selects a family
of positive filters on supplied nested regions $D_0\subseteq\cdots\subseteq D_{m-1}$
of an open square grid. Each edge crosses at most one region and the weights
satisfy $0<a_j\le1$. Let $q$ be the local dimension and $J$ a common
operator-norm bound for the site and edge terms of the original Hamiltonian
$H$. For a unique unit ground vector $\Omega$, energy $E_0$ and full-system
projector gap $\Delta$, one family
maximizes the filtered output norm, has nonzero output $\psi$, and its
normalization $\phi=\psi/\|\psi\|$ satisfies
\begin{align}
 |\operatorname{Re}\langle\phi,H\phi\rangle-E_0|
 &\le q^2J\sum_j a_j^2|\partial D_j|,
 \label{eq:polypeps_square_zero_floor_energy_bound}\\
 \Delta(1-|\langle\Omega,\phi\rangle|^2)
 &\le q^2J\sum_j a_j^2|\partial D_j|.
 \label{eq:polypeps_square_zero_floor_fidelity_bound}
\end{align}
Only the total Hamiltonian must be Hermitian; the individual site and edge
terms need not be. The companion statement allows real $J$ subject to the
termwise norm bounds. A negative $J$ is therefore possible only when the
interaction index set is empty.\footnote{Source: \texttt{openai/math} revision
\texttt{adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a},
\texttt{lean/OAI/MathematicalPhysics/PEPSFilters/EnergyOptimizer.lean},
\texttt{exists\_maximizer\_energy}, lines 62--109; compare the model and
full-system gap in \texttt{00-introduction.tex}.}

\section{The physical specialization and its boundary}

The square-grid specialization takes the original Hamiltonian and its original
unit gapped ground vector. For every real interaction bound $J$, it transports the site and edge
terms by the canonical configuration isometry $U$ to the integer square
$\Lambda$, and uses their Hermitian parts. The sum is exactly $UHU^*$, the norm
bound is still $J$, and the projector gap is unchanged. The regional filters
are specified spectrally as
$K_j=V_j\operatorname{diag}(p_{j,r}^{a_j/2})V_j^*$ with $V_j$ unitary,
$p_{j,r}\ge0$ and $\sum_r p_{j,r}=1$.

For one such family, the global maximum, positive output norm, unit
normalization and the estimates
(\ref{eq:polypeps_square_zero_floor_energy_bound}) and
(\ref{eq:polypeps_square_zero_floor_fidelity_bound}) hold simultaneously, with
$H,\Omega$ replaced by $UHU^*,U\Omega$ and with each unordered integer-grid
boundary edge counted once. No additional hypothesis on a regional gap is
used.\footnote{Declaration:
\leanid{TNLean.PEPS.Approximation.SquareHamiltonian.exists_zero_floor_maximizer_energy_fidelity}.}
No sign hypothesis on $J$ is needed for this conversion. Every admissible
support is nonempty. If $X$ is an unused admissible support, choose a site
$v\in X$ and let $A_v$ be the original term at the corresponding square-grid
site. Its norm bound gives $0\le\|A_v\|\le J$, which also bounds the zero
term assigned to $X$. On the empty square there are no admissible supports,
so the conversion imposes no sign condition on $J$. Thus the energy and
fidelity estimates include the empty square with a negative vacuous bound.\footnote{
This removes the former nonnegative-budget restriction recorded in issue 9586.}

The identification of the source's filter coordinates and ordered products
with these spectral feasible families is recorded separately in the
QICLean note on zero-floor energy~\cite{gap:oai26_zero_floor_energy_scope}.
The present result supplies the physical Hamiltonian, exact crossing coefficient
and original full-system gap. It still takes the nested regions, single-crossing
condition and weights as inputs; it does not construct them or prove the
geometric optimizer, the two-family decomposition or Proposition 4.1.

\section{Effect and remaining work}

The nonnegative-budget restriction has been removed without changing either
estimate. The remaining scope concerns the representation of the feasible
families and the supplied geometric data, not the sign of the interaction
bound. The coordinate-family comparison and geometric choice of regions and
weights remain independent steps in issue 8741.
