\section{The comparison retained}
The sharp upper argument in Ref.~\cite{OpenAI2026AreaLaw} compares the
original replica prevector at actual scanner roots with a weighted entropy
contribution. The construction here keeps the same physical truncated
vector, actual target spectral restriction and supplied auxiliary labels
throughout the completed and pre-charge histories.\footnote{Source:
    \texttt{07-comparators.tex}, lines 383--418, and
    \texttt{08-scanner.tex}, lines 416--474, revision
    \texttt{adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a}.}
For $d=|E|$, spectral mass $z>0$, band count $K$, per-band weight $a>0$
and common quadratic moment coefficient $K_{\mathrm{mom}}$, the proposed estimate is
\begin{align}
    -\frac{\log N_{k,p}}{k}
    &\leq 2\log d-\log z
       +\frac a2\sum_g\sum_j T_p(j)
          \bigl(S(U_{jg})+S(U_{jg}\cup Y_{jg})-S(Y_{jg})\bigr)
       \nonumber\\
    &\qquad+6K K_{\mathrm{mom}}a^2-\frac K2\log z+R_{d,c,K}(k),
    \label{eq:actual_sharp_upper_rate}\\
    N_{k,p}&>0,
    & R_{d,c,K}(k)&\longrightarrow0.
    \label{eq:actual_sharp_upper_limit}
\end{align}
Here $T_p$ is the actual fill or charge tree, with its original history and
conditional-choice weights. The constant $c$ and copy threshold precede
all scan times and interpolation parameters. They may depend on the fixed
finite-domain instance, the actual selected set, and the supplied label
sequence.\footnote{Formal declarations:
    \leanid{TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.CollarScan.eventually_actualScan_suppliedPrevector_sharp_upper}
    in \doclink{TNLean/PEPS/AreaLaw/Scan/ActualSharpUpper}, and
    \leanid{TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.CollarScan.exists_actualScan_schmidtBellPrevector_sharp_upper}
    in \doclink{TNLean/PEPS/AreaLaw/Scan/ActualSharpPrevector}.}
The original physical eigenvalue, symmetry, both auxiliary labels,
selected mass and little-o dimension conclusion are retained when the
label sequence is chosen once from the selected Schmidt vector.

\section{The additional margin}
The source-scale consequence uses one common budget at every physical status.
Its moment constants and allowable interval are
\begin{align}
    B&=C_B nD(\log n)^{12}, & 1&\leq B,\nonumber\\
    g&=\frac{\Delta}{c_+}, & C_0&=512e\frac2g,
    & c_0&=\frac{1}{32\sqrt{1+2/g}},\nonumber\\
    K_{\mathrm{mom}}&=C_0B,
    & |u|&\leq\frac{c_0}{\sqrt B}.
    \label{eq:actual_sharp_common_moment_budget}
\end{align}
The target-window theorem is applied once, and the status budgets in
(\ref{eq:actual_sharp_common_moment_budget}) are evaluated on that same
truncated ground vector. There are no independent existential choices
of the target state and the state used for marginal moments.

For total weight $0<W\leq n^\omega$, the choice $a=W/K$ must satisfy
\begin{align}
    6a&\leq \frac{c_0}{\sqrt B}, & 6a&\leq1.
    \label{eq:actual_sharp_radius_requirement}
\end{align}
With $D$ of order $n^\kappa$ and $K$ of order
$n^{1-\ell-\mu}$, a sufficient condition is the explicit strict margin
\begin{align}
    \omega+\frac{1+\kappa}{2}&<1-\ell-\mu.
    \label{eq:actual_sharp_explicit_margin}
\end{align}
Indeed, the left side of the first inequality in
(\ref{eq:actual_sharp_radius_requirement}), multiplied by $\sqrt B$, is
bounded by a constant times
$n^{\omega+(1+\kappa)/2-(1-\ell-\mu)}(\log n)^6$, which tends to zero.
This strict margin is a hypothesis of the present source-scale
consequence; it is not inferred from a weaker exponent ordering.

\section{What remains}
Equations (\ref{eq:actual_sharp_upper_rate})--(\ref{eq:actual_sharp_upper_limit})
are a fixed-instance sharp upper estimate, and
(\ref{eq:actual_sharp_explicit_margin}) records the precise additional
condition used to derive its source-scale moment interval. They do not
constitute the whole comparison conclusion of Proposition~9.6.
Completing that conclusion still requires the actual sharp lower estimate,
its integration with the same filtered vector and scan telescoping, and
the final error absorption under the intended exponent hypotheses.
Any removal of (\ref{eq:actual_sharp_explicit_margin}) must supply the
missing moment-radius estimate or replace the present comparison route.
The scoped blueprint statements retain this margin and do not mark the
unrestricted proposition complete.
