\section{The printed dependence step}

The proof of Theorem~7 of
Ref.~\cite{PerezGarcia2007Matrix} compares two translation-invariant MPS
representations using matrices on a $D^2$-dimensional doubled bond space. The
local source is
\path{Papers/quant-ph_0608197/MPSarchive.tex}, lines~1080--1095. For local
matrices $B_i,C_i\in M_D(\C)$, the printed argument produces invertible
matrices
\begin{align}
    W_1,\ldots,W_{D^4}
        &\in M_{D^2}(\C),
    \label{eq:pgvwc07_intertwiner_chain_off_by_one_printed_intertwiners}
\end{align}
satisfying
\begin{align}
    W_k(C_i\otimes\Id)
        &= (B_i\otimes\Id)W_{k+1}
        \qquad(1\leq k\leq D^4-1).
    \label{eq:pgvwc07_intertwiner_chain_off_by_one_printed_chain}
\end{align}
It then selects an index $n$ such that $W_1,\ldots,W_{n-1}$ are linearly
independent and $W_n$ lies in their span.

The ambient matrix space has dimension
\begin{align}
    \dim_{\C}M_{D^2}(\C)
        &= (D^2)^2,
    \label{eq:pgvwc07_intertwiner_chain_off_by_one_ambient_dimension_square}\\
        &= D^4.
    \label{eq:pgvwc07_intertwiner_chain_off_by_one_ambient_dimension}
\end{align}
A family of $D^4$ matrices in this space need not be linearly dependent.
Therefore, the dependence claimed from
(\ref{eq:pgvwc07_intertwiner_chain_off_by_one_printed_intertwiners}) does not
follow from the printed count.

\section{The corrected cut count}

A window containing $D^4$ consecutive sites has $D^4+1$ cuts: two endpoint
cuts and $D^4-1$ internal cuts. If comparison of the two open-boundary
representations supplies one invertible intertwiner at every cut, the complete
family is
\begin{align}
    W_0,W_1,\ldots,W_{D^4}
        &\in M_{D^2}(\C).
    \label{eq:pgvwc07_intertwiner_chain_off_by_one_complete_cut_family}
\end{align}
Adjacent cuts must satisfy
\begin{align}
    W_k(C_i\otimes\Id)
        &= (B_i\otimes\Id)W_{k+1}
        \qquad(0\leq k\leq D^4-1).
    \label{eq:pgvwc07_intertwiner_chain_off_by_one_complete_chain_relations}
\end{align}
The family in
(\ref{eq:pgvwc07_intertwiner_chain_off_by_one_complete_cut_family}) contains
$D^4+1$ vectors in the $D^4$-dimensional space identified by
(\ref{eq:pgvwc07_intertwiner_chain_off_by_one_ambient_dimension}), so it is
linearly dependent.

Every $W_k$ is invertible and hence nonzero. In particular, $W_0\neq0$.
Choose the first dependent vector. There is then an index
\begin{align}
    1
        &\leq n\leq D^4
    \label{eq:pgvwc07_intertwiner_chain_off_by_one_first_dependence_index}
\end{align}
such that
\begin{align}
    W_0,\ldots,W_{n-1}
        &\text{ are linearly independent},
    \label{eq:pgvwc07_intertwiner_chain_off_by_one_independent_prefix}\\
    W_n
        &\in\operatorname{span}\{W_0,\ldots,W_{n-1}\}.
    \label{eq:pgvwc07_intertwiner_chain_off_by_one_first_dependent_vector}
\end{align}
After shifting indices, this is precisely the dependence hypothesis required
by the chain-combination lemma at
\path{Papers/quant-ph_0608197/MPSarchive.tex}, lines~1022--1049, in the proof
of Theorem~7 of Ref.~\cite{PerezGarcia2007Matrix}.

\section{Formal boundary}

The current formal quadratic-system structure stores gauges attached only to
the $D^4$ sites. From these fields, it constructs the $D^4-1$ internal-cut
intertwiners and proves the $D^4-2$ relations between consecutive internal
cuts. It contains no gauges for the two endpoint cuts.

The endpoint intertwiners in
(\ref{eq:pgvwc07_intertwiner_chain_off_by_one_complete_cut_family}) must
therefore come from a future theorem comparing the surrounding open-boundary
representations. Such a comparison is available only when both tensors are
already known to represent the same state; it cannot establish that an
arbitrary algebraic solution represents the state. The residual phase freedom
and the corrected same-state conclusion are recorded in
\path{docs/paper-gaps/pgvwc07_quadratic_reconstruction_phase.tex}. The endpoint
intertwiners do not follow from the internal quadratic equations alone.

\section{Verdict}

The $D^4$-intertwiner count in the proof of Theorem~7 of
Ref.~\cite{PerezGarcia2007Matrix} is off by one for the stated dimension
argument. The local correction uses the complete family of $D^4+1$ cut
intertwiners around the $D^4$-site window. Once both endpoint intertwiners and
all $D^4$ adjacent relations in
(\ref{eq:pgvwc07_intertwiner_chain_off_by_one_complete_chain_relations}) are
available, linear dependence and the subsequent chain-combination argument
follow.

The formal development currently establishes only the internal chain and does
not claim that this shorter family is dependent. The open-boundary comparison
needed to supply the endpoint cuts remains absent, so the reconstruction
capstone is still open.
