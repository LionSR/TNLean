\section{The source statement}

In the section on matrix product states of~\cite{Hastings2007AreaLaw}, the
squared Schmidt coefficients $|A_0(\alpha)|^2$ of the ground state across a cut
are ordered nonincreasingly, and
$k_0=\exp(2S(\rho^0_{1,j}))/2$.\footnote{\path{References/0705.2024/law16.tex},
    eq.~\texttt{constraint} is stated at line~346 and applied with $P=1/2$
    at line~540, eq.~\texttt{k0Ent} is at line~531, and eq.~\texttt{est} at
    line~548.}
With $P=1/2$, the bootstrap estimate gives, for every integer $m\ge0$,
\begin{align}
    \sum_{\alpha\ge k_0D^{2m}+1}|A_0(\alpha)|^2
     & \le 4C_1(\xi)^2e^{-2m/\xi'}.
    \label{eq:hastings07_schmidt_tail_constraint}
\end{align}
The source then states
\begin{align}
    \sum_{\alpha\ge k'}|A_0(\alpha)|^2
     & \le 4C_1(\xi)^2\exp\bigl(-\lfloor\log_D(k'/k_0)\rfloor/\xi'\bigr)
    \le 4C_1(\xi)^2e^{1/\xi'}(k'/k_0)^{1/(\xi'\ln D)}.
    \label{eq:hastings07_schmidt_tail_source_est}
\end{align}

\section{The point at issue}

The exponent on the right of (\ref{eq:hastings07_schmidt_tail_source_est})
lacks its minus sign; the bound is meant to decay in $k'$.

The first inequality of (\ref{eq:hastings07_schmidt_tail_source_est}) needs
(\ref{eq:hastings07_schmidt_tail_constraint}) at an exponent $2m$ equal to
$n=\lfloor\log_D(k'/k_0)\rfloor$. When $n$ is odd, the largest admissible
choice is $2m=n-1$, which gives only
\begin{align}
    4C_1(\xi)^2e^{-(n-1)/\xi'}
     & = 4C_1(\xi)^2e^{1/\xi'}e^{-n/\xi'}.
    \label{eq:hastings07_schmidt_tail_odd}
\end{align}
The second inequality of (\ref{eq:hastings07_schmidt_tail_source_est}) uses
$n>\log_D(k'/k_0)-1$ and loses a further factor $e^{1/\xi'}$.

\section{The corrected statement}

Let $\tau(k)$ be the mass beyond the $k$ largest squared Schmidt coefficients.
If $\tau(k)\le ce^{-2m/\xi'}$ whenever $k\ge k_0D^{2m}$, then for every
$k\ge k_0$,
\begin{align}
    \tau(k)
     & \le c\,e^{2/\xi'}\,(k/k_0)^{-1/(\xi'\ln D)}.
    \label{eq:hastings07_schmidt_tail_corrected}
\end{align}
Indeed, with $x=\log_D(k/k_0)$ and $m=\lfloor\lfloor x\rfloor/2\rfloor$, one
has $k_0D^{2m}\le k$ and $2m>x-2$.

The tail $\tau(k')=\sum_{\alpha\ge k'+1}|A_0(\alpha)|^2$ starts one index
later than the sum in (\ref{eq:hastings07_schmidt_tail_source_est}). This is a
convention, not a gap in the source: the sum from $\alpha=k'$ is also
controlled by (\ref{eq:hastings07_schmidt_tail_constraint}). When
$k'=k_0D^{2m}$ with $m\ge1$ and $k'\ge2$, the constraint at the scale $m$ says
nothing about $|A_0(k')|^2$, but since $D\ge2$ one has
$k_0D^{2(m-1)}\le k'/4\le k'-1$, so the constraint at the scale $m-1$ bounds
the sum from $\alpha=k'$ at the cost of one more factor $e^{2/\xi'}$, and that
sum decays as in the source. The later start is adopted because it is the tail
the approximation needs: a matrix product state of bond dimension $k'$ keeps
the first $k'$ Schmidt coefficients and discards the rest. Only at the initial
scale $m=0$, where $k'=k_0$, is there no smaller scale to fall back on, and
there the sum from $\alpha=k_0$ is bounded only by $1$.

The bound (\ref{eq:hastings07_schmidt_tail_corrected}) is therefore
(\ref{eq:hastings07_schmidt_tail_source_est}) with the sum started one index
later and with the constant $e^{2/\xi'}$ in place of
$e^{1/\xi'}$.\footnote{\leanid{TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.Hastings.schmidtTail_le_rpow_of_geometric}
    in \doclink{TNLean/MPS/AreaLaw/Hastings/SchmidtTail}.}
The error of the matrix product state built from this tail is the subject of
the separate note \path{hastings07_mps_error_measure.tex}.

\section{Conclusion}

The correction changes one numerical constant and a sign. Taking the tail from
the index after $k'$ is a convention matched to the cut of a matrix product
state, not a correction. The decay exponent of the tail is as in the source.
