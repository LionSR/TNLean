\section{The source statement}

In the section on matrix product states of~\cite{Hastings2007AreaLaw}, the
squared Schmidt coefficients $|A_0(\alpha)|^2$ of the ground state across a cut
are ordered nonincreasingly, and
$k_0=\exp(2S(\rho^0_{1,j}))/2$.\footnote{\path{References/0705.2024/law16.tex},
    lines~505--557; equations \texttt{k0Ent}, \texttt{constraint} and
    \texttt{est}.}
With $P=1/2$, the bootstrap estimate gives, for every integer $m\ge0$,
\begin{align}
    \sum_{\alpha\ge k_0D^{2m}+1}|A_0(\alpha)|^2
     & \le 4C_1(\xi)^2e^{-2m/\xi'}.
    \label{eq:hastings07_schmidt_tail_constraint}
\end{align}
The source then states
\begin{align}
    \sum_{\alpha\ge k'}|A_0(\alpha)|^2
     & \le 4C_1(\xi)^2\exp\bigl(-\lfloor\log_D(k'/k_0)\rfloor/\xi'\bigr)
    \le 4C_1(\xi)^2e^{1/\xi'}(k'/k_0)^{1/(\xi'\ln D)}.
    \label{eq:hastings07_schmidt_tail_source_est}
\end{align}

\section{The point at issue}

The exponent on the right of (\ref{eq:hastings07_schmidt_tail_source_est})
lacks its minus sign; the bound is meant to decay in $k'$.

The first inequality of (\ref{eq:hastings07_schmidt_tail_source_est}) needs
(\ref{eq:hastings07_schmidt_tail_constraint}) at an exponent $2m$ equal to
$n=\lfloor\log_D(k'/k_0)\rfloor$. When $n$ is odd, the largest admissible
choice is $2m=n-1$, which gives only
\begin{align}
    4C_1(\xi)^2e^{-(n-1)/\xi'}
     & = 4C_1(\xi)^2e^{1/\xi'}e^{-n/\xi'}.
    \label{eq:hastings07_schmidt_tail_odd}
\end{align}
The second inequality of (\ref{eq:hastings07_schmidt_tail_source_est}) uses
$n>\log_D(k'/k_0)-1$ and loses a further factor $e^{1/\xi'}$.

\section{The corrected statement}

Let $\tau(k)$ be the mass beyond the $k$ largest squared Schmidt coefficients.
If $\tau(k)\le ce^{-2m/\xi'}$ whenever $k\ge k_0D^{2m}$, then for every
$k\ge k_0$,
\begin{align}
    \tau(k)
     & \le c\,e^{2/\xi'}\,(k/k_0)^{-1/(\xi'\ln D)}.
    \label{eq:hastings07_schmidt_tail_corrected}
\end{align}
Indeed, with $x=\log_D(k/k_0)$ and $m=\lfloor\lfloor x\rfloor/2\rfloor$, one
has $k_0D^{2m}\le k$ and $2m>x-2$. The bound
(\ref{eq:hastings07_schmidt_tail_corrected}) is
(\ref{eq:hastings07_schmidt_tail_source_est}) with the constant $e^{2/\xi'}$
in place of $e^{1/\xi'}$, and it gives the same conclusion: the error of a
matrix product state of bond dimension $k'$ on a chain of length $N$ is of
order $N(k'/k_0)^{-1/(\xi'\ln D)}$.\footnote{\leanid{TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.Hastings.schmidtTail_le_rpow_of_geometric}
    in \doclink{TNLean/MPS/AreaLaw/Hastings/SchmidtTail}.}

\section{The error of the matrix product state}

The source concludes that the error of a matrix product state of bond
dimension $k'$ on a chain of length $N$ scales as
$N(k'/k_0)^{-1/(\xi'\ln D)}$, without naming the error
measure.\footnote{\path{References/0705.2024/law16.tex}, lines~553--557.}
Truncating every cut onto the $k'$ leading Schmidt vectors gives a vector
$\Phi$ with
\begin{align}
    \lVert\Phi-\Psi_0\rVert
     & \le\sum_{j=1}^{N-1}\sqrt{\tau_j(k')}
    \le (N-1)\sqrt{c\,e^{2/\xi'}}\,(k'/k_0)^{-1/(2\xi'\ln D)},
    \label{eq:hastings07_schmidt_tail_mps}
\end{align}
where $\tau_j$ is the tail at the cut $(j,j+1)$. The bound
(\ref{eq:hastings07_schmidt_tail_mps}) is on the norm distance, so the tail
enters through a square root, and the bond dimension stays polynomial in
$N$ and in the inverse accuracy.\footnote{\leanid{TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.Hastings.isOpenMPS_norm_sub_le_of_schmidtTail_geometric}
    in \doclink{TNLean/MPS/AreaLaw/Hastings/SchmidtTailMPS}.}

\section{Conclusion}

The correction changes one numerical constant and a sign, and the
approximation is stated for the norm distance. The decay exponent of the tail
and the polynomial dependence of the bond dimension on the accuracy are as in
the source.
