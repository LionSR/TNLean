\section{The finite identity proved here}

Let $W$ have supplied two-site standard-form data with unitary gates
$u$ and $v$, in the exact factor orders of
\cite[\texttt{StandardForm} and Definition~\texttt{SF}, lines~603--622]{Cirac2017MPU}.
For every $n\ge0$, use complete bijective coordinates on the actual
periodic MPO of length $3+n$ in the $W$ alphabet. Separate the middle
input pair from the two exterior input blocks and the arbitrary tail.
On the output side separate the four neighbouring half-sites from the
two exterior half-sites and the same tail. Write the resulting matrix as
$M_n$. The derived identity is
\begin{align}
    M_n(I\otimes A)M_n^\dagger
     & =I\otimes B_{u,v}(A),
    \label{eq:mpu_qca_complete_block_finite_heisenberg}
\end{align}
where $B_{u,v}(A)$ is the actual two-link output observable determined by
$u$ and $v$. The identity on the right includes the entire tail, so the
local output is independent of its length. The periodic seam is retained
inside the word trace, rather than discarded or replaced by a boundary
premise.

This is actual finite conjugation: the coordinate systems are bijections
on all configurations, and unitarity of $M_n$ follows from the supplied
standard form. No compatibility, stabilization, or support assertion is
introduced as a hypothesis.

\section{Remaining assembly}

The preceding formula is first obtained for the supplied blocked tensor.
There is also an original-site version: given a standard form of the
two-site block of a length-$k$ block of $U$, retain arbitrary original-site
prefix and suffix lengths $p,s$. Complete coordinate bijections give the
same identity for the actual original MPO of length
\begin{align}
    N & =p+6k+s>1.
    \label{eq:mpu_qca_original_residual_window_length}
\end{align}
The residual products stay inside the virtual trace before multiplication
by the actual unitary's adjoint. Thus the original-site result does not
require the whole chain length to be divisible by $k$ or $2k$.

The original-site identity also agrees with the canonical local-algebra
inclusions on every integer interval containing the fixed window. Its
image in the algebraic local algebra is therefore independent of both
buffers and of the ambient interval. This conclusion is derived for the
middle two complete blocks, rather than assumed as compatibility data.

finite supported regions. Place each one-site algebra at the beginning

The one-site algebras generate every finite local algebra, so the actual
finite-chain maps agree on the complete supported region. No
