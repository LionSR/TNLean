\section{The printed gates}
The example ``All phases under conjugation'' of \cite{Cirac2017MPU} takes
$u_\pm=v_\pm\in\{\openone_{r\ell},\operatorname{diag}(-1,1,\dots,1)\}$ and,
for even $r$ and $\ell$, prints
\begin{align}
    u'_\pm & =(\openone_{r\ell/4}\otimes R)\,u_\pm, &
    v'_\pm & =(\openone_{r\ell/4}\otimes R)\,v_\pm,
\end{align}
with $R=\tfrac{1-i}{2}[\openone_4+i(Y\otimes Y)]$, ``as in''
Corollary~\texttt{cor:sympl-to-orthogonal}.\footnote{Source traceability:
    \path{Papers/1703.09188/paper_v2.tex}, lines 1847--1876; the corollary is
    at lines 1168--1181.}

\section{The correction}
The gate $v\colon\mathbb C^r\otimes\mathbb C^\ell\to\mathbb C^d\otimes\mathbb C^d$
is in Case~II of Proposition~\texttt{corconj} when
$\overline v=v\,(\Sigma_r\otimes\Sigma_\ell)$: the symplectic form acts on the
input of $v$. Corollary~\texttt{cor:sympl-to-orthogonal}, which the example
cites, writes accordingly $v'=\tilde v(\openone_{r\ell/4}\otimes R)$, with $R$
on the right. Since $\overline R=(Y\otimes Y)R=R(Y\otimes Y)$, the gate
$v'_\pm=v_\pm(\openone_{r\ell/4}\otimes R)$ satisfies
$\overline{v'_\pm}=v'_\pm(\Sigma_r\otimes\Sigma_\ell)$ for the real $v_\pm$.
With $R$ on the left, $(\openone\otimes R)v_\pm$ would carry the symplectic
form on the output $\mathbb C^d\otimes\mathbb C^d$ instead, which is not the
Case~II relation. The formalization uses $v'_\pm=v_\pm(\openone_{r\ell/4}\otimes R)$.
The determinant claim $\det v'_\pm=\pm(-1)^{r\ell/4}$ is unchanged, since
$\det R=-1$ on either side.
