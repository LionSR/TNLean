\section{Source statements and derived comparisons}

Example~4.10 of Cirac--P\'erez-Garc\'ia--Schuch--Verstraete prints a channel
with a repeated left-qubit label, four decimal entropy values, and the claim
that the mutual information fails to saturate except at the stated exceptional
probabilities
\cite[lines~897--905]{Cirac2016MPDO_arXiv}. The corresponding source file is
\path{Papers/1606.00608/MPDO-22-12-17-2.tex:897--905}. The source gives no
exact ratio or integer comparison.

Replace the repeated label by the network-consistent left--right correlated
flip and set the flip probability to $p=1/4$. This correction reproduces the
four printed entropy values. The resulting exact comparison is
\begin{align}
    2^{32}7^7
        &> 3^3 5^{20}.
    \label{eq:cpsv16_exact_arithmetic_scope_example410_integer_inequality}
\end{align}
The corrected channel, reduced spectra, and logarithmic reduction leading to
Eq.~\ref{eq:cpsv16_exact_arithmetic_scope_example410_integer_inequality} are
project-derived results recorded in
\path{docs/paper-gaps/cpsv16_examples_4_10_4_11_entropy.tex}; they are not
printed in the source.

Example~4.11 gives an arbitrary-length family, its diagonal bond weights, and
the claim that the family saturates the area law but does not have zero
correlation length
\cite[lines~907--924]{Cirac2016MPDO_arXiv}. The source contains no four-site
specialization, reduced spectra, entropy values, or exact integer comparison.

Specializing the printed family independently to a four-site periodic ring
gives
\begin{align}
    41^{82}5^{50}
        &> 2^{48}13^{52}7^{112}.
    \label{eq:cpsv16_exact_arithmetic_scope_example411_integer_inequality}
\end{align}
The finite-ring distribution, reduced spectra, and logarithmic reduction
leading to
Eq.~\ref{eq:cpsv16_exact_arithmetic_scope_example411_integer_inequality} are
also recorded in
\path{docs/paper-gaps/cpsv16_examples_4_10_4_11_entropy.tex}. They are not
statements from Ref.~\cite{Cirac2016MPDO_arXiv}.

Both sides of
Eqs.~\ref{eq:cpsv16_exact_arithmetic_scope_example410_integer_inequality}
and~\ref{eq:cpsv16_exact_arithmetic_scope_example411_integer_inequality} are
positive integers. Each strict inequality is therefore equivalent to
positivity of the real logarithm of the corresponding ratio.

\section{Scope boundary}

For a normalized four-site state, let $S_L$ denote the entropy of $L$
consecutive sites. Define the chain mutual information by
\begin{align}
    I_L
        &= S_L+S_{4-L}-S_4.
    \label{eq:cpsv16_exact_arithmetic_scope_mutual_information}
\end{align}
The arithmetic module proves only scalar statements. It neither identifies
reduced-state spectra nor establishes an area-law conclusion.

For the corrected Example~4.10 tensor at $p=1/4$,
\doclink{TNLean/MPS/MPDO/CPSVExample410Spectrum} supplies the root multisets
for blocks of one through four sites. The module
\doclink{TNLean/MPS/MPDO/CPSVExample410Entropy} then proves
\begin{align}
    I_2
        &> I_1
    \label{eq:cpsv16_exact_arithmetic_scope_example410_mutual_information_defect}
\end{align}
and derives the source-zero-correlation-length, non-saturation conclusion. This
completes \ghissue{6301} without asserting the universal parameter range
printed in Example~4.10 of Ref.~\cite{Cirac2016MPDO_arXiv}.

For the literal four-site family in Example~4.11,
\doclink{TNLean/MPS/MPDO/CPSVExample411Entropy} proves
\begin{align}
    I_2
        &> I_1
    \label{eq:cpsv16_exact_arithmetic_scope_example411_mutual_information_defect}
\end{align}
and hence failure of saturation. Independently,
\doclink{TNLean/MPS/MPDO/CPSVExample411SourceZCL} proves failure of source zero
correlation length through finite-marginal instability, both for the effective
binary model and for its ambient two-qubit realization. These results complete
\ghissue{6302}: they refute the printed saturation clause and verify the
printed non-zero-correlation-length clause. Neither conclusion follows from
the arithmetic module alone.

\section{Verdict}

The exact arithmetic endpoints are certified and connected to the relevant
tensor statements. For the corrected Example~4.10 tensor at $p=1/4$, the
tensor and entropy results establish source zero correlation length without
area-law saturation. For the literal four-site Example~4.11 family, the
entropy result refutes the printed saturation clause, while the finite-marginal
result verifies the independent non-zero-correlation-length clause.

The scope restriction is that the arithmetic statements alone prove neither
the tensor spectra nor the area-law conclusions. These project-derived
finite-ring results do not formalize the universal-in-$p$ claim in
Example~4.10 or any thermodynamic statement for Example~4.11 of
Ref.~\cite{Cirac2016MPDO_arXiv}.
