\section{What the source states}
Let $O_B,O_L,O_R$ be positive semidefinite contractions with
$\lVert O_BO_LO_R-\ketbra{\Psi}{\Psi}\rVert\le\varepsilon$, the conclusion of
Lemma~1 of Hastings' area law~\cite{Hastings2007AreaLaw}
(arXiv:0705.2024v4, file \path{law16.tex}, lines~413--446). In the bound on the
relative entropy of the product of the two marginals the source needs the
expectation
\begin{align}
    y=\operatorname{tr}\bigl((\rho_L\otimes\rho_R)\,O_LO_R\bigr)
    \label{eq:hastings07_relent_constants_y}
\end{align}
of $O_L=M_L\otimes\mathbf 1$ and $O_R=\mathbf 1\otimes M_R$ in the product
$\sigma=\rho_L\otimes\rho_R$ of the two marginals of $\Psi$. Line~446 asserts
without derivation that $y\ge1-2\varepsilon$, and feeds this into an unnamed
additive constant of the relative-entropy estimate.

\section{What is formalized}
The formalization first proves the single-operator bounds of line~422,
$\langle\Psi,O_B\Psi\rangle\ge(1-\varepsilon)^2$ and the same for $O_L$ and
$O_R$, and then the product-state bound
\begin{align}
    y\ge(1-\varepsilon)^4\ge1-4\varepsilon .
    \label{eq:hastings07_relent_constants_formal}
\end{align}
For the two marginals the expectation $y$ factors as the product of the two
single-operator expectations $\langle\Psi,O_L\Psi\rangle$ and
$\langle\Psi,O_R\Psi\rangle$, each at least $(1-\varepsilon)^2$ when
$\varepsilon\le1$, so their product is at least $(1-\varepsilon)^4$; the
elementary inequality $(1-\varepsilon)^4\ge1-4\varepsilon$ closes the
bound, and for $\varepsilon>1$ the right-hand side is negative while $y$ is
nonnegative.\footnote{Declarations:
    \leanid{TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.Hastings.sq_one_sub_le_re_inner_boundary},
    \leanid{TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.Hastings.sq_one_sub_le_re_inner_left},
    \leanid{TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.Hastings.sq_one_sub_le_re_inner_right}, and
    \leanid{TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.Hastings.one_sub_four_mul_le_re_trace_kronecker_marginals_mul}
    in \doclink{TNLean/MPS/AreaLaw/Hastings/ProductStateExpectation}.}

\section{The deviation}
The printed $y\ge1-2\varepsilon$ is not derived in the source; the derivable
bound from the factorization of $y$ is $y\ge(1-\varepsilon)^4\ge1-4\varepsilon$,
which replaces the printed slope $2$ by $4$. This moves only the unnamed
additive constant $C_2$ of the relative-entropy estimate by an absolute factor
and leaves the structure of the argument, the operators, and the rate of the
area law unchanged.

\section{Elimination plan}
No formal change is needed: the formal bound
(\ref{eq:hastings07_relent_constants_formal}) is valid in place of the printed
line~446, and the source does not use a slope sharper than the derivable one.
The deviation is recorded on the module docstring of
\path{TNLean/MPS/AreaLaw/Hastings/ProductStateExpectation.lean} as a
\texttt{Local fix (explicit constants)} marker. A revision of the source may
adopt the derivable slope $4$, or a sharper derivation of
$y\ge1-2\varepsilon$ would discharge the note.
