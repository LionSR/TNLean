\section{The source passage}

In the proof of the one-dimensional area law of
Ref.~\cite{Hastings2007AreaLaw}, lines~440--461 of
\path{References/0705.2024/law16.tex}, the operator $O_B$ of Lemma~1 has
expectation at least $1-2\epsilon$ in the ground state but small expectation
$x=\tr(\sigma O_B)$ in the product
$\sigma=\rho_{1,j}\otimes\rho_{j+1,N}$ of its two marginals. The operator
factorizes as $O_B O_L O_R\approx P_0$, with $O_L$ and $O_R$ positive
semidefinite commuting contractions and $P_0$ a rank-one projector satisfying
$\lVert O_B O_L O_R-P_0\rVert\le\epsilon$, and $\tr(\sigma P_0)\le2\epsilon$.
Writing $y=\tr(\sigma O_L O_R)$, lines~449--454 bound $x$ by the
Cauchy--Schwarz inequality
\begin{align}
    x\,y-\sqrt{x-x^2}\,\sqrt{y-y^2}
        \le\tr(\sigma O_B O_L O_R)
        \le\tr(\sigma P_0)+\epsilon,
    \label{eq:hastings07_relent_constants_cauchy_schwarz}
\end{align}
which the source reads as the bound labelled $\mathtt{xbd}$.

\section{The two explicit-constant local fixes}

The formal statement
\leanid{TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.Hastings.re_trace_mul_le_of_projector_factorization}
reproduces Eq.~\ref{eq:hastings07_relent_constants_cauchy_schwarz} with two
constants made explicit.

\subsection{The expectation of $O_L O_R$}

Line~446 asserts $y\ge1-2\epsilon$ without derivation. For the product state
$\sigma$ the bound that the preceding estimates actually yield is
$y\ge1-4\epsilon$, and that is the hypothesis the formal theorem carries. The
weaker constant does not affect the shape of the argument: with
$y\in[0,1]$ one has $y-y^2=y(1-y)\le1-y\le4\epsilon$, hence
\begin{align}
    \sqrt{y-y^2}\le\sqrt{4\epsilon}=2\sqrt{\epsilon}.
    \label{eq:hastings07_relent_constants_y_root}
\end{align}

\subsection{The threshold and the final constant}

Combining Eq.~\ref{eq:hastings07_relent_constants_cauchy_schwarz},
$\tr(\sigma P_0)\le2\epsilon$, $\sqrt{x-x^2}\le\sqrt{x}$ and
Eq.~\ref{eq:hastings07_relent_constants_y_root} gives
\begin{align}
    x\,(1-4\epsilon)\le x\,y
        \le 3\epsilon+2\sqrt{\epsilon}\,\sqrt{x}.
    \label{eq:hastings07_relent_constants_quadratic}
\end{align}
Treating Eq.~\ref{eq:hastings07_relent_constants_quadratic} as a quadratic
inequality in $\sqrt{x}$ yields $\sqrt{x}\le6\sqrt{\epsilon}$, hence
\begin{align}
    x=\tr(\sigma O_B)\le36\,\epsilon,
    \label{eq:hastings07_relent_constants_xbd}
\end{align}
provided $\epsilon\le1/68$. The source states the bound $\mathtt{xbd}$ with an
unspecified small constant; the explicit threshold $\epsilon\le1/68$ and the
explicit factor $36$ are the local fix. Both are recorded in the docstring of
\doclink{TNLean/MPS/AreaLaw/Hastings/EffectExpectation}, which also records the
supporting Cauchy--Schwarz covariance inequality
\leanid{Matrix.PosSemidef.sub_sqrt_mul_sqrt_le_re_trace_mul_mul}.

\section{Verdict}

The two constants change no hypothesis of the area-law argument and no
conclusion beyond making the implicit small constant of the bound
$\mathtt{xbd}$ explicit. Eq.~\ref{eq:hastings07_relent_constants_xbd} is proved
in \Lean{} with these constants, so this is a resolved local correction.
