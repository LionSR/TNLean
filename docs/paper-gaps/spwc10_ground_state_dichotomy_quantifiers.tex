\section{Source and quantified asymptotic result}

Theorem~5 of \cite{Sanz2010Quantum}, lines~850--880 of
\path{Papers/0909.5347/main.tex}, asserts a length-independent matrix size or
an order-$N^{1/5}$ obstruction at prime lengths for the unique ground states of
one fixed frustration-free local interaction.

The source-written quantified version takes a positive $L$-site interaction
$h$ and assumes that the zero-energy space of $H_M=\sum_i\tau^i(h)$ has
dimension one at every permitted volume $M\ge L$. It proves the alternative
\begin{align}
     & \exists B,M_0\quad
    \ker H_M=\C V^{(M)}(B),\quad V^{(M)}(B)\ne0
    \quad\text{for every }M\ge M_0; \label{eq:spwc10_eventual_uniform_branch} \\
     & \text{or}\quad
    N<L+\max\{q(D),3(D-1)(q(D)+1)\},\qquad
    q(D)=(D^2+1)^2, \label{eq:spwc10_prime_obstruction}
\end{align}
where (\ref{eq:spwc10_prime_obstruction}) quantifies over every prime $N$ and
every bond-$D$ tensor whose nonzero periodic vector is in $\ker H_N$.
The interaction and its range are fixed before those quantifiers. The
polynomial bound is an explicit fifth-degree obstruction; it is not a
statement only about the W state or a chosen sequence of representations.

\section{Derived data and the remaining boundary}

When the threshold in (\ref{eq:spwc10_prime_obstruction}) fails, primality and
$D<N$ remove every nonprimitive periodic block at length $N$. Grouping phase
classes and discarding zero combined coefficients produces an actual
expansion into separated blocks, with common injectivity length $q(D)$.
Complementary simultaneous spanning then gives $G_L(B_j)\subseteq\ker h$.
Consequently each block gives zero energy at every permitted volume. Its
injectivity ensures a nonzero periodic vector for $M\ge q(D)+1$, and the
one-dimensional ground-space hypothesis identifies the entire kernel with
that line. This derives (\ref{eq:spwc10_eventual_uniform_branch}) with
$M_0=\max\{L,q(D)+1\}$ and a block of bond dimension at most $D$.

The fixed-tensor conclusion is explicitly eventual. The finitely many
shorter volumes require a length-dependent family at one common matrix
size, rather than equality with that same tensor at every length.

\section{The actual finite-prefix construction}

Every nonzero vector on a positive-length ring which is fixed exactly by
cyclic translation now has an actual periodic trace-MPS representation
with matrix size $M d^{\lfloor M/2\rfloor}$. The open-boundary
representation is constructed from the existing finite cut-rank theorem,
and the existing cyclic-block averaging makes the matrices site
independent. This is stated in
\leanid{MPSTensor.exists_mpvState_eq_of_cyclic_invariant}.
Unused bond coordinates preserve every positive-length periodic vector,
as proved in \leanid{MPSTensor.mpvState_padBond}.

Consequently the eventual tensor of bond dimension $D$ and threshold
$M_0$ gives one common size
\begin{align}
    K & = D+M_0d^{M_0}+1
    \label{eq:spwc10_finite_prefix_common_size}
\end{align}
for all permitted positive-volume ground lines containing nonzero
exactly cyclic-invariant vectors. Short representations are derived,
not supplied as hypotheses. The theorem
\leanid{MPSTensor.exists_uniform_bond_on_cyclic_ground_sector}
states this result. Its tensors may depend on the volume; it does not
force a false same-tensor conclusion at the finitely many short lengths.

\section{The exact remaining momentum condition}

A periodic trace vector is fixed by every cyclic translation, as follows
from cyclicity of the trace. Conversely the finite representation above
shows that every nonzero exactly cyclic-invariant vector is representable.
The equivalence is
\leanid{MPSTensor.exists_mpvState_eq_iff_cyclic_invariant}.
Therefore the literal all-positive-volume common-size family is equivalent
to the following precise finite condition: for each permitted positive
$M<M_0$, there exists a nonzero ground vector on which every cyclic
translation acts as the identity. This is proved in
\leanid{MPSTensor.uniform_bond_family_iff_short_cyclic_ground}.

Uniqueness of a translation-invariant Hamiltonian's ground space gives an
invariant line. On a one-dimensional line a cyclic translation may act by
a root of unity; line invariance does not itself identify that eigenvalue
with one. The source proof supplies the eventual zero-momentum branch,
but no derivation of this additional finite-prefix zero-momentum condition
from its complete global frustration-free hypotheses is supplied here.
This is an identified remaining obligation, not a counterexample to the
source's full hypothesis set. The separate formalization issue
\href{https://github.com/LionSR/TNLean/issues/10386}{\#10386} asks for a
derivation from the entire all-volume positive, frustration-free uniqueness
and derived local-kernel regime, or a counterexample satisfying that full
hypothesis set. It blocks completion of issue \#10280; invariant-line
considerations alone do not resolve it.

The original asymptotic dichotomy and its hypotheses are unchanged. No
canonical decomposition, phase separation, transfer gap, local-kernel
inclusion, future-volume equation, finite-volume periodic representation,
or short zero-momentum conclusion is assumed in its proof.

All new proofs and Blueprint entries remain pending hosted acceptance;
no local Lean build or elaboration was run. The full source theorem
remains open in issue \#10280 until the finite-prefix zero-momentum
obligation, validation and integration are complete.

\section{Verdict}

\textbf{Verdict: scope restriction.} The quantified eventual dichotomy
and the actual common-bond finite-prefix construction on the exact
zero-momentum sector are source-written. The literal all-volume family
has been reduced to an explicit finite zero-momentum condition; that
condition is not inferred from uniqueness alone.
