\section{The printed coefficient argument}

Let $\mathcal B_1$, $\mathcal B_2$, and $\mathcal B_3$ be
finite-dimensional algebras, and suppose that
\begin{align}
    \mathcal A_1&\subseteq\mathcal B_1\otimes\mathcal B_2,
    &
    \mathcal A_2&\subseteq\mathcal B_2\otimes\mathcal B_3.
\end{align}
The support-algebra commutation lemma assumes that
$\mathcal A_1\otimes\operatorname{Id}_3$ commutes with
$\operatorname{Id}_1\otimes\mathcal A_2$.  Its coefficient proof chooses
bases and writes
\begin{align}
    a&=\sum_\mu e_\mu\otimes a_\mu,
    &
    a'&=\sum_\nu a'_\nu\otimes e'_\nu.
    \label{eq:outer_basis_expansion}
\end{align}
For $a\in\mathcal A_1$ and $a'\in\mathcal A_2$, commutation of the lifts
then gives
\begin{align}
    0
    &=[a\otimes\operatorname{Id}_3,
        \operatorname{Id}_1\otimes a']
      =\sum_{\mu,\nu}
        e_\mu\otimes[a_\mu,a'_\nu]\otimes e'_\nu.
    \label{eq:outer_basis_commutator}
\end{align}
This is Schumacher--Werner Lemma \path{sppcomm},
quant-ph/0405174, lines~1174--1194 \cite{SchumacherWerner2004QCA}, and
GNVW Lemma \path{sppcomm}, arXiv:0910.3675v2, lines~1221--1246
\cite{Gross2012IndexTheory}.  The published GNVW DOI is
\href{https://doi.org/10.1007/s00220-012-1423-1}
{10.1007/s00220-012-1423-1}.

\section{The outer-factor correction}

Both printed proofs place the basis elements $e'_\nu$ in
$\mathcal B_2$.  This is incompatible with
(\ref{eq:outer_basis_expansion}): since
$a'\in\mathcal B_2\otimes\mathcal B_3$, the coefficients $a'_\nu$ lie in
$\mathcal B_2$ and the basis elements $e'_\nu$ lie in $\mathcal B_3$.
It is also incompatible with the next step of the printed proof, which uses
the tensors $e_\mu\otimes e'_\nu$ as a basis of
$\mathcal B_1\otimes\mathcal B_3$.

The corrected basis assignment is therefore
\begin{align}
    e_\mu&\in\mathcal B_1,
    &
    a_\mu,a'_\nu&\in\mathcal B_2,
    &
    e'_\nu&\in\mathcal B_3.
    \label{eq:outer_basis_types}
\end{align}
With (\ref{eq:outer_basis_types}), linear independence of the outer tensors
in (\ref{eq:outer_basis_commutator}) gives
\begin{align}
    [a_\mu,a'_\nu]&=0
    &&\text{for every $\mu$ and $\nu$}.
\end{align}
Thus the correction changes no hypothesis or conclusion of the lemma; it
only restores the factor type required by its proof.

\section{Formal resolution}

The formal coefficient statement uses first-factor indices for the blocks of
$a$, third-factor indices for the slices of $a'$, and matrices on the common
second factor as its conclusion.  It therefore implements
(\ref{eq:outer_basis_types}) directly.  Pairwise commutation of these
coefficients is
\leanid{Matrix.middleSlices_commute_of_overlappingLifts_commute}; passage to
the two generated support algebras is
\leanid{Matrix.supportAlgebras_commute_of_overlappingLifts_commute}.

The separate need for star-closed inputs is documented in
\path{docs/paper-gaps/gnvw12_support_algebra_star_closure.tex}.  The distinct
restriction to full complex matrix factors remains open and is documented in
\path{docs/paper-gaps/gnvw12_support_algebra_full_matrix_scope.tex}.

\section{Verdict}

The replacement of $\mathcal B_2$ by $\mathcal B_3$ for the basis
$\{e'_\nu\}_\nu$ is a resolved local correction.  It is forced by both the
typed expansion of $a'$ and the outer-factor linear-independence argument.
