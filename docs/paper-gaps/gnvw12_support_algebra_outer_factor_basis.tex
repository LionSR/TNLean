\section{The printed coefficient argument}

Let $\mathcal B_1$, $\mathcal B_2$, and $\mathcal B_3$ be
finite-dimensional algebras, and let
\begin{align}
    \mathcal A_1
        &\subseteq \mathcal B_1\otimes\mathcal B_2,
    \label{eq:gnvw12_support_algebra_outer_factor_basis_first_inclusion}\\
    \mathcal A_2
        &\subseteq \mathcal B_2\otimes\mathcal B_3.
    \label{eq:gnvw12_support_algebra_outer_factor_basis_second_inclusion}
\end{align}
The support-algebra commutation lemma assumes that
$\mathcal A_1\otimes\operatorname{Id}_3$ commutes with
$\operatorname{Id}_1\otimes\mathcal A_2$. Its coefficient proof chooses
bases and, for $a\in\mathcal A_1$ and $a'\in\mathcal A_2$, writes
\begin{align}
    a
        &=\sum_\mu e_\mu\otimes a_\mu,
    \label{eq:gnvw12_support_algebra_outer_factor_basis_first_expansion}\\
    a'
        &=\sum_\nu a'_\nu\otimes e'_\nu.
    \label{eq:gnvw12_support_algebra_outer_factor_basis_second_expansion}
\end{align}
Commutation of the lifted elements then gives
\begin{align}
    0
        &=[a\otimes\operatorname{Id}_3,
            \operatorname{Id}_1\otimes a']
    \label{eq:gnvw12_support_algebra_outer_factor_basis_lifted_commutator}\\
        &=\sum_{\mu,\nu}
            e_\mu\otimes[a_\mu,a'_\nu]\otimes e'_\nu.
    \label{eq:gnvw12_support_algebra_outer_factor_basis_commutator_expansion}
\end{align}
This argument appears in the Schumacher--Werner lemma labelled
\path{sppcomm}, at lines~1174--1194 of arXiv:quant-ph/0405174
\cite{SchumacherWerner2004QCA}, and in the GNVW lemma with the same source
label, at lines~1221--1246 of arXiv:0910.3675v2
\cite{Gross2012IndexTheory}. The published GNVW article has DOI
\href{https://doi.org/10.1007/s00220-012-1423-1}
{10.1007/s00220-012-1423-1}.

\section{The outer-factor correction}

Both printed proofs place the basis elements $e'_\nu$ in $\mathcal B_2$.
That assignment is incompatible with
(\ref{eq:gnvw12_support_algebra_outer_factor_basis_second_expansion}).
Because $a'\in\mathcal B_2\otimes\mathcal B_3$, its coefficients
$a'_\nu$ lie in $\mathcal B_2$, whereas its basis elements $e'_\nu$ lie in
$\mathcal B_3$. The printed assignment is also incompatible with the next
proof step, which treats the tensors $e_\mu\otimes e'_\nu$ as a basis of
$\mathcal B_1\otimes\mathcal B_3$.

The corrected types are
\begin{align}
    e_\mu
        &\in\mathcal B_1,
    \label{eq:gnvw12_support_algebra_outer_factor_basis_first_basis_type}\\
    a_\mu,\ a'_\nu
        &\in\mathcal B_2,
    \label{eq:gnvw12_support_algebra_outer_factor_basis_middle_coefficient_type}\\
    e'_\nu
        &\in\mathcal B_3.
    \label{eq:gnvw12_support_algebra_outer_factor_basis_third_basis_type}
\end{align}
With these assignments, the outer tensors in
(\ref{eq:gnvw12_support_algebra_outer_factor_basis_commutator_expansion})
are linearly independent. Therefore
\begin{align}
    [a_\mu,a'_\nu]
        &=0
        \qquad\text{for every $\mu$ and $\nu$}.
    \label{eq:gnvw12_support_algebra_outer_factor_basis_coefficient_commutation}
\end{align}
The correction changes neither the hypotheses nor the conclusion of the
lemma. It restores the factor type required by the coefficient argument.

\section{Formal resolution}

The formal coefficient statement indexes the blocks of $a$ by the first
factor and the slices of $a'$ by the third factor. Its conclusion concerns
matrices on the common second factor, so it directly implements the corrected
types above. Pairwise commutation of the coefficients is formalized by
\leanid{Matrix.middleSlices_commute_of_overlappingLifts_commute}; passage to
the generated support algebras is formalized by
\leanid{Matrix.supportAlgebras_commute_of_overlappingLifts_commute}.

The separate star-closure correction is documented in
\path{docs/paper-gaps/gnvw12_support_algebra_star_closure.tex}. The distinct
restriction to full complex matrix factors remains open and is documented in
\path{docs/paper-gaps/gnvw12_support_algebra_full_matrix_scope.tex}.

\section{Verdict}

Replacing $\mathcal B_2$ by $\mathcal B_3$ as the ambient algebra of the
basis $\{e'_\nu\}_\nu$ is a resolved local correction. Both the typed
expansion of $a'$ and the outer-factor linear-independence argument require
this replacement.
