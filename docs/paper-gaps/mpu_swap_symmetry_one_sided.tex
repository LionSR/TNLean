\section{Source and correction}

The transformed examples in Ref.~\cite[lines~2053--2063]{Cirac2017MPU}
are obtained by multiplying the periodic operator on one side by
$S_N=\mathbb S^{\otimes N}$: $\widetilde U^{(N)}=S_NU^{(N)}$.
The proof of the swap-transformation lemma instead writes
$\widetilde U(x)^{(N)}=S_NU(x)^{(N)}S_N$
\cite[lines~2076--2084]{Cirac2017MPU}. This conjugation is inconsistent with
the stated transformed examples. The correction is to use the one-sided
map $U\mapsto S_NU$ throughout that proof.

\section{Operator identities}

The swap is real, symmetric, unitary, and involutive. For every operator $U$,
\begin{align}
    S_NU^\dagger S_N=U
     &\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad (S_NU)^\dagger=S_NU,
    \label{eq:mpu_swap_one_sided_adjoint} \\
    S_NU^{\mathsf T}S_N=U
     &\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad (S_NU)^{\mathsf T}=S_NU,
    \label{eq:mpu_swap_one_sided_transpose} \\
    \overline U=U
     &\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \overline{S_NU}=S_NU.
    \label{eq:mpu_swap_one_sided_conjugation}
\end{align}
The first two equivalences follow by multiplying by $S_N$ and using
$S_N^2=I$; the third uses $\overline{S_N}=S_N$.
The same involutive linear map carries a continuous path to a continuous
path. Multiplication by a one-site unitary preserves unitarity of every
periodic operator and canonical endpoint tensors.

The three operator equivalences and their strict path comparisons
are proved by the corresponding adjunction, transposition, and conjugation
results, which rest on the general transformation of paths by left
multiplication with a one-site unitary and on the preservation of canonical
endpoints.\footnote{Formal declarations: the results in
\doclink{TNLean/MPS/MPU/Examples/SwapSymmetryTransport};
\leanid{MPOTensor.strictlyEquivalentUnderSymmetry_iff_ketLeftMul};
\leanid{MPOTensor.isMPUCanonicalForm_ketLeftMul}.}

\section{Scope}

These identities justify the swap comparison of strict symmetry-preserving
paths, with the two tensors compared after unused virtual directions are
adjoined up to a common dimension, as in
\doclink{docs/paper-gaps/mpu_equivalence_fixed_bond.tex}. They do not
establish the stabilized comparison with general identity ancillas and
blocking. The remaining questions are recorded in
\doclink{docs/paper-gaps/mpu_symmetry_ancilla_transport.tex}.
