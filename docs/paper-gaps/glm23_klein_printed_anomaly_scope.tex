\section{The source statement}

Ref.~\cite{GarreRubio2023MPOSym}, subsection ``MPO algebras representing
$\mathbb{Z}_2\times\mathbb{Z}_2$'', realizes one of the classes by the
operators $U_a=\prod_i CZ_{i,i+1}Z_i$, $U_b=\prod_i X_i$, and
$U_{ab}=U_aU_b$ on a periodic chain, and states: ``In this realization the
only non-trivial $3$-cocycle element is $\omega(ab,ab,ab)=-1$.''
Read entrywise, this is impossible: a normalized cochain whose only value
different from one is $\omega(ab,ab,ab)=-1$ violates the cocycle equation
\begin{align}
    \omega(h,k,l)\,\omega(g,hk,l)\,\omega(g,h,k)
     & =\omega(gh,k,l)\,\omega(g,h,kl)
    \label{eq:glm23kleinprinted_cocycle}
\end{align}
at $(g,h,k,l)=(a,b,ab,ab)$, where the left side is $1$ and the right side
is $-1$. The source defines $\omega_g=\omega(g,g,g)$
(\path{Papers/2203.12563/REsubmission.tex}, line 1852), and its table row for
the class $(0,0,1)$ (line 1865) lists $(\omega_a,\omega_b,\omega_{ab})=(+1,+1,-1)$
with trivializing subgroups $\{H_e,H_a,H_b\}$; line 1886 places the two phases of
the printed realization at $H=H_a$ and $H=H_b$. The statement is therefore
read as a statement about the diagonal values. In a normalized gauge the three diagonal
values determine the class, and the statement asserts
\begin{align}
    \bigl(\omega(a,a,a),\omega(b,b,b),\omega(ab,ab,ab)\bigr)
     & =(+1,+1,-1).
    \label{eq:glm23kleinprinted_source_signs}
\end{align}

\section{What is formalized}

For every choice of fusion tensors of the four printed tensors, the cyclic
products $\omega(g,e,g)\,\omega(g,g,g)$ at $g=a,b,ab$ are $+1$, $+1$ and $-1$.
The value at $g$ is computed on the restriction of the family to the subgroup
$\{e,g\}$. On $\{e,ab\}$ the family is the decorated CZX family. On $\{e,b\}$
all tensors have bond dimension one and the one-by-one identity fusion tensors
give the trivial cochain. On $\{e,a\}$ the stacked square of the bond-two
diagonal tensor $A$ of $U_a$ reduces to the identity tensor through the row
$\langle 00|$ and the column $|00\rangle+|11\rangle$, and the two fusion trees
of three copies of $A$ agree against every nonempty word.

The classification of Klein three-cocycles by these three cyclic products then
identifies the class of $\omega$ with that of the representative of the row
$(0,0,1)$, the type-II cocycle
\begin{align}
    \omega_{\mathrm{II}}(x,y,z) & =(-1)^{x_1y_2z_2},
    \label{eq:glm23kleinprinted_type_two}
\end{align}
where $x=(x_1,x_2)$ with $a=(1,0)$ and $b=(0,1)$. Equivalently, the class
corresponds to $(0,0,1)$ under $H^3(\mathbb{Z}_2\times\mathbb{Z}_2,
\mathbb{C}^\times)\cong\mathbb{Z}_2^3$, and every normalized representative
has the diagonal values~\eqref{eq:glm23kleinprinted_source_signs}.\footnote{

\section{The L-symbol relations}

The source continues (line 1886): for normalized $L$-symbols, in both phases
$L^x_{ab,ab}=-L^y_{ab,ab}$, and $L^x_{a,a}=L^y_{a,a}$ for $H=H_a$ and
$L^x_{b,b}=L^y_{b,b}$ for $H=H_b$, where $x,y$ are the two blocks. For an
involution $g$, the compatibility of normalized $L$-symbols with a normalized
cocycle $\nu$ at $(g,g,g)$ gives
\begin{align}
    L^x_{g,g} & =\nu(g,g,g)\,L^{g\cdot x}_{g,g},
    \label{eq:glm23kleinprinted_involution}
\end{align}
so the diagonal values give $L^x_{ab,ab}=-L^{ab\cdot x}_{ab,ab}$,
$L^x_{a,a}=L^{a\cdot x}_{a,a}$ and $L^x_{b,b}=L^{b\cdot x}_{b,b}$. This is
what is formalized.\footnote{
    \leanid{KleinSymmetry.lSymbol\_signs\_printedFamily}.}
Since $ab$ lies in neither $H_a$ nor $H_b$, it exchanges the two blocks in both
phases, which is the first printed relation. The relation for $a$ relates two
different blocks exactly when $a$ moves $x$, that is, in the phase $H=H_b$;
likewise the relation for $b$ is a relation between the two blocks in the phase
$H=H_a$. In the phase $H=H_a$ the element $a$ fixes both blocks, and the
action-tensor gauge with $\gamma_a(x)=i$ and $\gamma_g(z)=1$ otherwise keeps
the $L$-symbols normalized and compatible while changing the sign of
$L^x_{a,a}$ but not of $L^y_{a,a}$. The printed relation for $a$ in the phase
$H=H_a$ therefore holds for a particular solution, not for every normalized
one; the gauge-invariant relation is~\eqref{eq:glm23kleinprinted_involution}.

\textbf{Verdict: local correction, resolved.} The printed sentence on
$\omega(ab,ab,ab)$ is read through the diagonal values, and the class of the
printed realization is identified for every choice of fusion tensors. The
$L$-symbol relations are formalized in the gauge-invariant
form~\eqref{eq:glm23kleinprinted_involution}.
