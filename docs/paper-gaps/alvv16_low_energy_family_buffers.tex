\section{The source regime}

Theorem~4.8 of Arad, Landau, Vazirani and Vidick~\cite{Arad2016RigorousRG},
\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/1602.08828v2}{arXiv:1602.08828v2}, has source
label \texttt{t:aisgapless}. In \path{local_alg_arxiv_v2.tex}, line~1027,
its bandwidths satisfy
\begin{align}
    \eta_{\mathrm{LD}}\ge\eta_1\ge\frac{2\mu}{\log n},\qquad \mu>0.
    \label{eq:ld_source_bandwidth}
\end{align}
The proof at lines~1037--1040 uses the buffer
\begin{align}
    w=\frac{\mu}{3\log n}.
    \label{eq:ld_source_buffer}
\end{align}
For an integer $n\ge2$, one has $\log n>0$, hence $w>0$ and
$\eta_1\ge6w>3w$. The source's two clipped cutoffs differ by $w$, while
the lower original cutoff is smaller than the upper original cutoff by $3w$.
In the bounded statement below, $\eta$ denotes this source cutoff $\eta_1$,
rather than the density bandwidth $\eta_{\mathrm{LD}}$.
These inequalities concern $n\ge2$. They do not supply an argument for
$n=0$ or $n=1$, where the positive-log substitution is unavailable.

\section{The bounded five-region conclusion}

The physical construction considered here has local dimension $q\ge2$,
window parameter $\ell\ge1$, and integer positions satisfying
\begin{align}
    a+2\ell\le b,\qquad b+2\ell\le L-1.
    \label{eq:ld_five_region_geometry}
\end{align}
In particular, $L\ge4\ell+1\ge5$; small chains are not covered by this
geometry. Let the supported nearest-neighbour terms satisfy $0\le h_i\le I$.
Their actual regional minima $e_j$ define the centered original Hamiltonian
$H_0=H-(\sum_j e_j)I$, whose ground energy $E_0$ lies in $[0,4]$.
Clip the three bulks, retain the two windows, and denote the resulting
centered Hamiltonian by $\widetilde H$.

For $\varepsilon>0$, $w>0$ and $3w<\eta$, put
\begin{align}
    C_*&=288e^{11/8},\notag\\
    t&=21+\eta+8\log\!\left(
       \frac{C_*}{\min\{1,\varepsilon w^2/[2(6+\eta)]\}}\right),\notag\\
    R&=3t+4\ell.
    \label{eq:ld_common_threshold}
\end{align}
The bounded conclusion uses this one actual truncation for all
spectral comparisons and for the polynomial decomposition. Its targets are
\begin{align}
    T_-&=\mathbf1_{(-\infty,E_0+\eta-2w]}(\widetilde H)\mathcal H,\notag\\
    T_+&=\mathbf1_{(-\infty,E_0+\eta-w]}(\widetilde H)\mathcal H.
    \label{eq:ld_nested_targets}
\end{align}
They satisfy $T_-\subseteq T_+$, and the one-sided comparisons are
\begin{align}
    \mathbf1_{(-\infty,E_0+\eta]}(H_0)\mathcal H
      &\text{ is }\varepsilon\text{-close to }T_+,\notag\\
    T_-&\text{ is }\varepsilon\text{-close to }
      \mathbf1_{(-\infty,E_0+\eta-3w]}(H_0)\mathcal H.
    \label{eq:ld_physical_sandwich}
\end{align}
The reference energy is the original centered ground energy $E_0$.
No equality with the clipped ground energy, equality of the two target
spaces, or spectral gap between their cutoffs is asserted.

The strict inequality $3w<\eta$ makes the lower original bandwidth positive.
The existing finite spectral-window comparison uses lower floor zero and
requires its source cutoff to exceed that floor. At $\eta=3w$ and $E_0=0$
this condition fails. A negative lower floor with an adjusted window-count
constant could address that endpoint, but the bounded statement does not
claim it. The source regime (\ref{eq:ld_source_bandwidth}) for $n\ge2$
already lies strictly inside the stated buffer range.

The common polynomial family on $J=[a+\ell,b+\ell+1)$ has at most $D^2$
operators, where $D=(q\ell^2)^{20\ell}$. Applying the actual degree-$\ell^2$
Chebyshev polynomial gives the squared shrinking coefficient
\begin{align}
    \Delta=\left(2\exp\!\left[-2\ell^2
      \sqrt{\frac{w}{R-(E_0+\eta-2w)}}\right]\right)^2.
    \label{eq:ld_explicit_filter_error}
\end{align}
The finite decomposition concerns that same polynomial of
$\widetilde H$. It transfers viability from $T_+$ to $T_-$ with error
$\Delta/(1-\delta)^2$. The unsquared polynomial need not be positive;
the relevant closed-cutoff and sign corrections are documented in the
companion spectral-projector and Chebyshev-positivity notes.

\section{Returning to the original spectral windows}

The same constructed family can be applied to a subspace viable for the
original upper window, with both comparison losses retained. If
$\delta\ge0$ and $2(\delta+\varepsilon)<1$, the first comparison gives
viability $2(\delta+\varepsilon)$ for $T_+$. After the filter, the error
for $T_-$ is $\Delta/[1-2(\delta+\varepsilon)]^2$. Transferring once more
to the original lower window gives
\begin{align}
    \delta_{\mathrm{out}}=
      2\left(\frac{\Delta}{[1-2(\delta+\varepsilon)]^2}
        +\varepsilon\right).
    \label{eq:ld_original_window_error}
\end{align}
Both transfers use ALVV's closeness-composition lemma
(\texttt{l:close}, source lines 484--489).
The family, actual polynomial and exterior tensor factors are the same
ones chosen before the viable subspace. No additional closeness or
factorization hypothesis is introduced. This corollary does not remove
the geometric or buffer restrictions and does not identify
(\ref{eq:ld_original_window_error}) with the clipped-target error.

\section{What remains for the full source theorem}

The full source theorem must retain its separate uncompleted status until
all of the following are derived: the substitution
(\ref{eq:ld_source_buffer}) with its length hypotheses, the desired
$D^{12}\Delta$ estimate and printed asymptotic parameters, placement at
arbitrary prescribed middle blocks including short and boundary blocks,
and the finite exceptional chain lengths. In particular, $n=2,3,4$ have
positive logarithm but do not satisfy (\ref{eq:ld_five_region_geometry})
for $\ell\ge1$. Direct short-region constructions are required there.
The low-density bootstrap and the sampled merge-tree induction are
additional results. The false soft-cluster estimate is not used in this
hard-truncation argument.
