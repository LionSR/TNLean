\section{The source lemma}

Fix an $L\times L$ square lattice $\{0,\ldots,L-1\}^2$ and its dyadically
padded grid $\{0,\ldots,N-1\}^2$. Lemma~8.2 of the September~24, 2026
polynomial-PEPS manuscript~\cite{OpenAI2026PolynomialPEPS}
(\path{07-assembly.tex}, lines~122--127) states that the virtual links of the
party tensor network can be routed along nearest-neighbor paths on the padded
grid $\{0,\ldots,N-1\}^2$ with constant edge congestion, and that folding the
padded grid back into $\{0,\ldots,L-1\}^2$ by reflection preserves constant
congestion and leaves the physical output coordinates unchanged.

In the paper, Lemma~8.2 is stated without explicit hypotheses because it refers
to the specific party network produced by the distribution protocol of
Proposition~7.1 (\path{06-geometry.tex}, lines~12--53), whose parties are
placed at the anchor vertices of the dyadic blocks (\path{07-assembly.tex},
lines~103--114). The proof of Lemma~8.2 (lines~128--162) uses four properties
of that network:
\begin{enumerate}
\item every block anchor lies on the padded grid $\{0,\ldots,N-1\}^2$
  (lines~113--114);
\item each dyadic block has at most $B$ attached parties (lines~113--114, from
  item~1 of Proposition~7.1);
\item each party is incident to at most $\Delta$ virtual links
  (lines~140--141, from item~2 of Proposition~7.1);
\item for every virtual link, the $\ell_\infty$-distance between the anchors of
  its two endpoint parties is at most $c$ times either endpoint's dyadic scale
  (lines~130--132, from item~3 of Proposition~7.1).
\end{enumerate}
Proposition~7.1 also guarantees that the two endpoint scales of every link are
equal or adjacent, though the congestion bound does not need that property.

\section{What is proved and the extra hypotheses}

Lemma~8.2 is proved for
an arbitrary party network whose parties are assigned to dyadic blocks and satisfy
properties 1--4 as explicit hypotheses. Under those four hypotheses, the edge
congestion on the padded grid is at most $2(2c+1)B\Delta$, the edge congestion
after folding onto $\{0,\ldots,L-1\}^2$ is at most $8(2c+1)B\Delta$, and if
every virtual link has dimension at most $D$, the folded network forms a
projected entangled pair state (PEPS) on the open $L\times L$ square grid with
bond dimension at most
$\max(D,1)^{8(2c+1)B\Delta}$.\footnote{The padded congestion bounds are
\leanid{TNLean.PEPS.Approximation.DyadicPlacement.load_horizontal_le} and
\leanid{TNLean.PEPS.Approximation.DyadicPlacement.load_vertical_le}; the
folded congestion bound is
\leanid{TNLean.PEPS.Approximation.foldedRouting_card_traversal_le}; the PEPS
construction is
\leanid{TNLean.PEPS.Approximation.exists_squarePEPS_of_dyadicRouting}.}

\section{Resolution}

The routing argument itself is complete, and its congestion constants depend
only on $B$, $\Delta$, and $c$, as in the paper. The four hypotheses are now
derived inside the assembly of Theorem~1.1 for the party network that the
protocol actually produces, with constants chosen before $L$ and the
Hamiltonian.

The protocol is constructed from the adaptive patch constraint of
Proposition~4.1 and the angular information bound of Proposition~3.4, and it
satisfies the consequences of Proposition~7.1 that Section~8
uses.\footnote{\leanid{TNLean.PEPS.Approximation.largeSizeDyadicProtocolConsequences_of_adaptivePatchConstraint}
in \path{TNLean/PEPS/Approximation/DyadicProtocolConsequences.lean}, through
\leanid{TNLean.PEPS.Approximation.exists_isDistributionProtocol_of_largeRepaintings}
in \path{TNLean/PEPS/Approximation/DyadicProtocolFromEvents.lean} and
\leanid{TNLean.PEPS.Approximation.exists_isDistributionProtocol_of_frames} in
\path{TNLean/PEPS/Approximation/DyadicProtocolAssembly.lean}.} Its parties are
attached to the anchors of the dyadic squares of $[0,2^k]^2$ with
$L\le 2^k<2L$, at most two parties per square, so property~1 holds and
property~2 holds with $B=b$.\footnote{\leanid{TNLean.PEPS.Approximation.protocolPlacement_inGrid}
and \leanid{TNLean.PEPS.Approximation.protocolPlacement_blockBounded} in
\path{TNLean/PEPS/Approximation/DyadicProtocolParties.lean}, recorded as the
fields \leanid{TNLean.PEPS.Approximation.DyadicProtocol.IsDistributionProtocol.inGrid}
and \leanid{TNLean.PEPS.Approximation.DyadicProtocol.IsDistributionProtocol.blockBounded}.}
Distributed density compression (Theorem~5.2) applied to the protocol gives an
operator network in which each party meets at most $2b(b-1)$ links, which is
property~3 with $\Delta=2b(b-1)$, and in which every link joins two parties of
a common gate.\footnote{\leanid{TNLean.PEPS.PairEffect.DistributedConstruction.exists_uniform_sampled_network}
in \path{TNLean/Lattice/Compression/DistributedGateMapConstruction.lean}.}
Item~3 of Proposition~7.1 then gives property~4 with
$c=b$.\footnote{\leanid{TNLean.PEPS.Approximation.DyadicProtocol.IsDistributionProtocol.separationBounded}
in \path{TNLean/PEPS/Approximation/DyadicProtocol.lean}, applied in
\leanid{TNLean.PEPS.Approximation.DyadicProtocol.exists_partyNetwork_of_contractedOperator}
in \path{TNLean/PEPS/Approximation/DyadicProtocolCompression.lean}.}
Here $b$ is a fixed constant of the protocol, independent of $L$ and the
Hamiltonian. The resulting network with these four bounds is passed to the
routing lemma in the proof of Theorem~1.1.\footnote{\leanid{TNLean.PEPS.Approximation.dyadicOperatorNetworkAt_of_dyadicProtocolBoundsAt}
in \path{TNLean/PEPS/Approximation/DyadicProtocolCompression.lean} supplies the
bounds;
\leanid{TNLean.PEPS.Approximation.hasPEPSApproximation_of_dyadicOperatorNetworkAt}
in \path{TNLean/PEPS/Approximation/GroundStateApproximation.lean} applies
\leanid{TNLean.PEPS.Approximation.hasPEPSApproximation_of_fixInput_dyadicRouting};
the composite is
\leanid{TNLean.PEPS.Approximation.polynomialPEPSApproximation_of_ratios}
in \path{TNLean/PEPS/Approximation/PolynomialPEPSExplicitConstant.lean}.}
The restriction recorded in this note is therefore resolved.

The surrounding results on the path to Theorem~1.1 hold without further
hypotheses. The adaptive patch constraint of Proposition~4.1 is
proved;\footnote{\leanid{TNLean.PEPS.Approximation.adaptivePatchConstraint}.}
Theorem~1.1 is proved without
hypotheses;\footnote{\leanid{TNLean.PEPS.Approximation.polynomialPEPSApproximation}.}
and every clause of Proposition~7.1 is carried by the formal distribution
protocol (\path{docs/paper-gaps/polypeps_prop71_consequences_interface.tex}).
