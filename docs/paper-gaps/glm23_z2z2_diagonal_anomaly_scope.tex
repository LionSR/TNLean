\section{The target statement}

Ref.~\cite{GarreRubio2023MPOSym}, subsubsection ``MPO algebras representing
$\mathbb{Z}_2\times\mathbb{Z}_2$'', lists the three-cocycles of
$\mathbb{Z}_2\times\mathbb{Z}_2$; the mixed type-II component is
$\omega(a,b,c)=(-1)^{a_1b_2c_2}$ in the coordinates $a=(a_1,a_2)$. The
anomaly three-cocycle of an exact matrix product operator representation
of a finite group by normal tensors is defined from its fusion tensors in
Ref.~\cite{FrancoRubio2025MPUGauging}, in the display preceding the
three-cocycle equation, and its cohomology class does not depend on the
choice of fusion tensors. Neither source prints a local tensor for the
mixed class.

\section{The formalized statement}

The two-qubit tensors $M_e,M_x,M_y,M_{xy}$ of the condensation-defect
example are a construction of this development. Their periodic operators
satisfy $U_gU_h=\lambda(g,h)^LU_{gh}$ on a ring of $L$ sites with
$\lambda(g,h)=(-1)^{g_1h_2}$. Since $\lambda(x,y)=-1\neq1=\lambda(y,x)$,
the operators $U_x$ and $U_y$ anticommute on odd rings, and no rescaling
$M_g\mapsto c_gM_g$ makes the family exact: a rescaling multiplies
$\lambda(g,h)$ by the symmetric factor $c_gc_h/c_{gh}$. The anomaly
three-cochain of Ref.~\cite{FrancoRubio2025MPUGauging} is therefore not
defined for the full family.

On the diagonal subgroup $\{e,xy\}$ the sign is $\lambda(xy,xy)=-1$, and
the rescaled family $e\mapsto M_e$, $xy\mapsto iM_{xy}$ is an exact
representation of $\mathbb{Z}_2$ by normal tensors. For it the formalization
proves $\omega(s,e,s)\,\omega(s,s,s)=-1$ for every choice of fusion
tensors, with $s=xy$, and hence that the anomaly class of the diagonal
subgroup is nontrivial. This is the value of the same gauge-invariant
product for the mixed cocycle at $s=(1,1)$; at $(1,0)$ and $(0,1)$ that
product is $1$, so the diagonal subgroup is the one on which the mixed
class restricts nontrivially.

\section{Missing piece and elimination plan}

The statement about the class of the full $\mathbb{Z}_2\times\mathbb{Z}_2$
family is not made. Two routes would supply it: blocking two sites, after
which $\lambda^2=1$ and the family is exact at the cost of a pair alphabet
of size $256$; or an anomaly three-cochain for projective families
$U_gU_h=c(g,h)^LU_{gh}$. Either would be combined with the restriction of
cochains to the diagonal subgroup, which preserves triviality of the class.
