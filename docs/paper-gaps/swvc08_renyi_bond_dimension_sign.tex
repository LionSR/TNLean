\section{The printed inequality}

Let $\rho$ be the reduced density matrix of a pure chain state across a single bond cut,
let $\alpha>1$, and let $\delta\in[0,1)$ bound its approximation error by a
normalized open-boundary matrix product state of bond dimension $D\ge1$,
measured in the
trace norm of the difference of the two pure-state density matrices.
All logarithms in this note have base two, and
$S_\alpha(\rho)=(1-\alpha)^{-1}\log\tr(\rho^\alpha)$.
Schuch, Wolf, Verstraete and Cirac~\cite{Schuch2008EntropyScaling}
print the lower bound
\begin{align}
    \log D &\ge S_\alpha(\rho)
      +\frac{\alpha}{\alpha-1}|\log(1-\delta)|.
    \label{eq:swvc08_renyi_printed}
\end{align}
The sign before the absolute value is incorrect.\footnote{arXiv:0705.0292v2,
    source file \path{paper_v2.tex}, lines 333--345; the final displayed
    inequality in the discussion of orders $\alpha>1$ is unnumbered.}

\section{The correction}

Write $p_1\ge p_2\ge\cdots\ge0$ for the eigenvalues of $\rho$, padded by
zeros, and put $\varepsilon=\sum_{i>D}p_i$. The source~\cite{Schuch2008EntropyScaling} proves
$\varepsilon\le\delta$ and
$S_\alpha(\rho)\le\log D-\frac{\alpha}{\alpha-1}\log(1-\varepsilon)$
before stating (\ref{eq:swvc08_renyi_printed}). Independently, convexity gives
$\sum_i p_i^\alpha\ge D^{1-\alpha}(1-\varepsilon)^\alpha$, which yields
the same entropy inequality because $1-\alpha<0$. Rearranging it and using
$\varepsilon\le\delta<1$ gives
\begin{align}
    \log D
      &\ge S_\alpha(\rho)+\frac{\alpha}{\alpha-1}\log(1-\varepsilon)
        \notag\\
      &\ge S_\alpha(\rho)+\frac{\alpha}{\alpha-1}\log(1-\delta)
        \notag\\
      &= S_\alpha(\rho)-\frac{\alpha}{\alpha-1}|\log(1-\delta)|.
    \label{eq:swvc08_renyi_corrected}
\end{align}

For a counterexample to (\ref{eq:swvc08_renyi_printed}), take the two-qubit
state $(|00\rangle+|11\rangle)/\sqrt2$. It has an exact matrix product state
representation of bond dimension $D=2$, and $S_\alpha(\rho)=1$ for every
$\alpha>1$. Its error is zero, hence at most any $0<\delta<1$.
The printed inequality would require
$1\ge1+\frac{\alpha}{\alpha-1}|\log(1-\delta)|>1$.

\section{Conclusion}

The printed inequality is false on nondegenerate data. Replacing its plus
sign by a minus sign gives (\ref{eq:swvc08_renyi_corrected}). For fixed
$\alpha>1$ and $\delta<1$, the subtracted term is independent of chain
length, so super-logarithmic entropy growth still rules out polynomial bond
dimension. This note resolves the sign discrepancy, not the formalization
of that approximation obstruction, which remains tracked in issue
\href{https://github.com/LionSR/TNLean/issues/9857}{\#9857}.
