\section{The source passage}

Schuch--Wolf--Verstraete--Cirac bound the R\'enyi entropy of a target state in
terms of the bond dimension of an approximating matrix product state
\cite{Schuch2008EntropyScaling}. Let $\psi$ be a unit vector on a chain and let
$\phi$ be an open-boundary matrix product state of bond dimension at most $D$,
with
\begin{align}
    \bigl\lVert\ketbra{\psi}{\psi}-\ketbra{\phi}{\phi}\bigr\rVert_1
        &\le\delta,
    \qquad \delta<1.
    \label{eq:swvc08_renyi_bond_dimension_sign_error}
\end{align}
For a prefix cut after $0<k<L$ sites, let $\rho_k$ be the reduced density matrix
of $\psi$ on the first $k$ sites. For $\alpha>1$ the source derives a
logarithmic R\'enyi lower bound.\footnote{Source:
\cite{Schuch2008EntropyScaling}, Eq.~(eps-lower), lines~269--270 and~333--345.}

\section{The deviation}

The reduced matrix $\rho_{\phi,k}$ of the approximant has rank at most $D$, and
the partial trace contracts the full trace-norm error, so
\begin{align}
    \bigl\lVert\rho_k-\rho_{\phi,k}\bigr\rVert_1
        &\le\delta.
    \label{eq:swvc08_renyi_bond_dimension_sign_reduced_error}
\end{align}
The finite-dimensional R\'enyi approximation inequality then gives
\begin{align}
    S_\alpha(\rho_k)+\frac{\alpha}{\alpha-1}\log(1-\delta)
        &\le\log D.
    \label{eq:swvc08_renyi_bond_dimension_sign_corrected}
\end{align}
Because $\delta<1$, the term $\log(1-\delta)$ is nonpositive, so moving it to the
right side weakens the bound: the correction can only raise the admissible
R\'enyi entropy. The source prints the logarithmic error term with the opposite
sign relative to~\eqref{eq:swvc08_renyi_bond_dimension_sign_corrected}, which
would instead tighten the bound and make it false for approximants of positive
error. The correction is purely in the sign of this additive term; the bond
dimension $\log D$ and the entropy order $\alpha$ are unchanged.

\section{Conclusion}

The verdict is a local correction. The source's qualitative claim --- a
logarithmic R\'enyi lower bound controlled by the bond dimension --- is
unchanged; only the sign of the additive logarithmic error term is corrected so
that the inequality holds for approximants of positive trace-norm error.
