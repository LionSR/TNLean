\section{The source statement}

In the proof of the bootstrap lemma of~\cite{Hastings2007AreaLaw}, $\rho$ is a
density matrix of unit trace with $\langle\Psi_0,\rho\Psi_0\rangle=P>0$ for the
ground state $\Psi_0$, and $P_0=|\Psi_0\rangle\langle\Psi_0|$. For each $m$ an
operator $O=O_B(j,m)O_L(j,m)O_R(j,m)$ satisfies
$\lVert O-P_0\rVert\le\epsilon$ with $\epsilon=C_1(\xi)e^{-m/\xi'}$, and
$\rho(m)=O\rho O^\dagger$.\footnote{\path{References/0705.2024/law16.tex},
    lines~315--349; equations \texttt{prodapprox}, \texttt{hg} and
    \texttt{constraint}.}
The source records the two estimates
\begin{align}
    \operatorname{tr}\bigl(P_0\rho(m)\bigr)
     & \ge\bigl(\sqrt P-\epsilon\bigr)^2,
    &
    \operatorname{tr}\bigl((1-P_0)\rho(m)\bigr)
     & \le\epsilon^2,
    \label{eq:hastings07_overlap_two_estimates}
\end{align}
and concludes
\begin{align}
    \frac{\langle\Psi_0,\rho(m)\Psi_0\rangle}{\operatorname{tr}\rho(m)}
     & \ge1-\frac{2C_1(\xi)^2e^{-2m/\xi'}}{P}
    =1-\frac{2\epsilon^2}{P}.
    \label{eq:hastings07_overlap_source_hg}
\end{align}

\section{The point at issue}

Write $a=\operatorname{tr}(P_0\rho(m))$ and $b=\operatorname{tr}((1-P_0)\rho(m))$.
The estimates (\ref{eq:hastings07_overlap_two_estimates}) give
$1-a/(a+b)=b/(a+b)\le\epsilon^2/(\sqrt P-\epsilon)^2$ when $\epsilon<\sqrt P$.
This is at most $2\epsilon^2/P$ exactly when
$(\sqrt P-\epsilon)^2\ge P/2$, that is $\epsilon\le(1-1/\sqrt2)\sqrt P$. For
$(1-1/\sqrt2)\sqrt P<\epsilon<\sqrt{P/2}$ the right side of
(\ref{eq:hastings07_overlap_source_hg}) is positive, and it does not follow
from (\ref{eq:hastings07_overlap_two_estimates}).

\section{The corrected statement}

If $\epsilon\le\sqrt P/2$, then $(\sqrt P-\epsilon)^2\ge P/4$, so
$\operatorname{tr}\rho(m)\ge P/4>0$ and
\begin{align}
    \frac{\langle\Psi_0,\rho(m)\Psi_0\rangle}{\operatorname{tr}\rho(m)}
     & \ge1-\frac{4\epsilon^2}{P}=1-\frac{4C_1(\xi)^2e^{-2m/\xi'}}{P}.
    \label{eq:hastings07_overlap_corrected}
\end{align}
If $\epsilon>\sqrt P/2$, the right side of
(\ref{eq:hastings07_overlap_corrected}) is negative and there is nothing to
prove.\footnote{\leanid{TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.Hastings.overlap_ge_of_norm_sub_rankOneProjection_le}
    in \doclink{TNLean/MPS/AreaLaw/Hastings/ProjectorOverlap}.}
The rest of the proof is unchanged with $2C_1(\xi)^2/P$ replaced by
$4C_1(\xi)^2/P$: equation \texttt{constraint} holds with the new constant, $m'$
is the least integer with $4C_1(\xi)^2e^{-2m'/\xi'}/P\le1$, and the bootstrap
bound becomes
\begin{align}
    S(\rho^0_{1,j})
     & \le\ln k+\xi'\ln\bigl(4C_1(\xi)^2/P\bigr)\ln D+F(\xi',D).
    \notag
\end{align}
At $P=1/2$, as in the section on matrix product states, the constant of
equation \texttt{constraint} is $8C_1(\xi)^2$ instead of $4C_1(\xi)^2$.

\section{Conclusion}

The correction changes one numerical constant inside a logarithm. The entropy
bound keeps its form and its dependence on $\xi'$, $D$, $k$ and $P$.
