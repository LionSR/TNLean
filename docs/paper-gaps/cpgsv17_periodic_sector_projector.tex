\section{The source argument}

The proof of Proposition~4.13 of Cirac, P\'erez-Garc\'ia, Schuch, and
Verstraete \cite[Proposition~4.13]{Cirac2016MPDO_arXiv} excludes nontrivial
periodic sectors of the vertically viewed tensor in one paragraph.  The local
source is \path{Papers/1606.00608/MPDO-22-12-17-2.tex}, lines~1888--1893: if
there were a $p$-periodic vector, ``there would exist an orthogonal projector
$Q$'' with, for every~$N$,
\[
    QH^{(N)}\ne H^{(N)}Q,
    \qquad
    Q\bigl[H^{(N)}\bigr]^p=\bigl[H^{(N)}\bigr]^pQ,
\]
where $H^{(N)}$ is the density operator generated in the horizontal
direction.  Since $H^{(N)}$ is positive semidefinite, the second family of
identities forces $QH^{(N)}=H^{(N)}Q$, which contradicts the first.

The projector $Q$ is drawn from the general theory of the peripheral
spectrum of completely positive maps; the source does not spell out its
construction.  A nontrivial $p$-periodic vector is an eigenvector of the
(rescaled) transfer map with peripheral eigenvalue $e^{2\pi iq/p}\ne 1$
(lines~225--230 of the source).

\section{What is formalized}

The file \path{TNLean/MPS/MPDO/PeriodicExclusion.lean} formalizes this step
as follows.

\begin{enumerate}
    \item The commutation identities above, with $Q$ an orthogonal projector
      on the physical space acting on the first site --- as does the
      projector of the invariant-projection step at lines~1874--1887 --- are
      collected in the notion of a \emph{periodic-sector projector} with
      exponent $p\ge 1$ (\leanid{MPOTensor.PeriodicSectorProjector}).
    \item The contradiction is proved unconditionally
      (\leanid{MPOTensor.isEmpty\_periodicSectorProjector}): a tensor
      generating positive semidefinite density operators at every length
      admits no periodic-sector projector.  This uses the power-commutation
      theorem for positive semidefinite matrices already formalized in
      \path{TNLean/Algebra/HermitianHelpers.lean}.
    \item The implication \emph{nontrivial vertical period $\Rightarrow$
      periodic-sector projector} is stated as an explicit hypothesis
      (\leanid{MPOTensor.PeriodicVectorYieldsProjector}).  Granted it, the
      vertically viewed tensor has no nontrivial $p$-periodic vectors
      (\leanid{MPOTensor.hasNoPeriodicVectors\_verticalTensor}).
\end{enumerate}

\section{The gap}

The unformalized content is the construction of the projector: the source
asserts, from the general theory of the peripheral spectrum, that a
nontrivial vertical period supplies an orthogonal projector with the two
all-length families of identities.  The conditional theorem does not close
this.  Indeed, under the global positivity hypothesis the periodic-sector
projector type is uninhabited by item~2 above, so the hypothesis of item~3
is logically equivalent to its conclusion; the unconditional mathematical
content of the formalized step is the projector exclusion of item~2, and
the hypothesis of item~3 is the named interface for the missing
construction.

\section{Elimination plan}

The intended derivation of the hypothesis from the positivity assumption
has three ingredients.

\begin{enumerate}
    \item The cyclic decomposition of the peripheral spectrum of an
      irreducible positive map \cite[Theorem~6.6]{Wolf2012Quantum},
      formalized in
      \path{TNLean/Channel/Peripheral/CyclicDecomposition}, applied to the
      transfer map of the vertically viewed tensor: a period-$p$ sector
      carries a family of orthogonal projectors permuted cyclically by one
      application of the map.
    \item The vertical stacking identity: the density operator generated by
      $p$ vertically stacked copies of the tensor at length $N$ is
      $[H^{(N)}]^p$.  This identity is not yet formalized.
    \item The first-site invariance argument of lines~1874--1887, already
      formalized in \path{TNLean/MPS/MPDO/InvariantProjection.lean}, applied
      to the stacked tensor: invariance of a cyclic projector under $p$
      stacked layers gives commutation with $[H^{(N)}]^p$ at every length,
      while its nontrivial cyclic displacement under one layer gives
      non-commutation with $H^{(N)}$ at every length.
\end{enumerate}

Once these are combined into a proof that global positivity implies the
projector-supplying hypothesis, the conditional theorem of item~3 above
becomes the unconditional exclusion of periodic vectors asserted by the
source, and this note can be retired.

\paragraph{Verdict.}
This is an open gap: the projector exclusion (the positivity part of the
source paragraph) is formalized unconditionally, while the passage from a
nontrivial vertical period to the projector remains a stated hypothesis.
