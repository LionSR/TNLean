\section{The printed statement}

Section~7.1, Lemma~7 of arXiv:0910.3675v2 assumes that
$\mathcal A\subseteq\mathcal B_1\otimes\mathcal B_2$ is a subalgebra
\cite{Gross2012IndexTheory}. It defines
$\operatorname{Spp}(\mathcal A,\mathcal B_1)$ as the smallest
$C^*$-subalgebra generated by the first-factor coefficients and asserts
\begin{align}
    \operatorname{Spp}(\mathcal A,\mathcal B_1)'
        &=\{b\in\mathcal B_1:
            b\otimes\Id\in\mathcal A'\}.
    \label{eq:gnvw12_support_algebra_star_closure_printed_commutant}
\end{align}
The statement and leastness argument occupy lines~1181--1191 of the v2
source, coefficient generation appears at lines~1188--1191, and the
commutant argument occupies lines~1192--1208. The source label is
\path{lem:spp}. The published article has DOI
\href{https://doi.org/10.1007/s00220-012-1423-1}
{10.1007/s00220-012-1423-1}.

Coefficient generation and leastness remain valid for an arbitrary algebra
input. Coefficient expansion proves containment, and dual-basis coefficient
maps prove leastness. The commutant identity
(\ref{eq:gnvw12_support_algebra_star_closure_printed_commutant}), however,
requires closure of the input algebra under the involution.

\section{Counterexample and correction}

Take $\mathcal B_1=M_2(\mathbb C)$ and $\mathcal B_2=\mathbb C$, and define
\begin{align}
    \mathcal A
        &=\operatorname{span}\{\Id,E_{12}\}
          \subset M_2(\mathbb C).
    \label{eq:gnvw12_support_algebra_star_closure_counterexample_algebra}
\end{align}
The algebra $\mathcal A$ is unital but not closed under adjoints. Its
coefficient set generates $M_2(\mathbb C)$ as a $C^*$-algebra because it
contains $E_{12}$ and therefore also generates $E_{21}=E_{12}^*$. Hence
\begin{align}
    \operatorname{Spp}(\mathcal A,\mathcal B_1)'
        &=\mathbb C\Id.
    \label{eq:gnvw12_support_algebra_star_closure_support_commutant}
\end{align}
By contrast, direct computation gives
\begin{align}
    \mathcal A'
        &=\operatorname{span}\{\Id,E_{12}\}.
    \label{eq:gnvw12_support_algebra_star_closure_input_commutant}
\end{align}
Equations~(\ref{eq:gnvw12_support_algebra_star_closure_support_commutant})
and~(\ref{eq:gnvw12_support_algebra_star_closure_input_commutant}) contradict
the printed identity
(\ref{eq:gnvw12_support_algebra_star_closure_printed_commutant}). Thus the
commutant clause is false for a general non-star-closed algebra.

To state coefficient generation, leastness, and the commutant clause as one
package, assume that $\mathcal A$ is a $C^*$-subalgebra. In the
finite-dimensional formal setting, this is equivalently a star-subalgebra.
For an element of $\mathcal A$, the adjoint of each coefficient is an
appropriately indexed coefficient of an adjoint element of $\mathcal A$.
This supplies the involution closure used in the commutant argument. The
correction does not claim that coefficient generation or leastness alone
requires star closure.

\section{Star closure in the applications}

Schumacher and Werner distinguish the algebraic support construction from the
closure property used for observable algebras
\cite{SchumacherWerner2004QCA}. At lines~1161--1165 of
arXiv:quant-ph/0405174, they define the support algebra as the ordinary
algebra generated by the coefficient support. At lines~1170--1171, they
deduce adjoint closure from adjoint closure of the input observable algebra.
This is a conditional closure argument; it does not identify every
``subalgebra'' with a ``$C^*$-subalgebra.''

The GNVW quantum-cellular-automaton application satisfies the corrected
hypothesis. At lines~1251--1265 of arXiv:0910.3675v2, the input is the image
of a local observable algebra under a $*$-automorphism
\cite{Gross2012IndexTheory}. The matrix-product-unitary application has the
same form. At lines~2300--2306 of arXiv:1703.09188, the map $\omega$
preserves the local star-algebra operations \cite{Cirac2017MPU}; at
lines~2313--2334, the support algebra is formed from
\begin{align}
    \omega(\mathcal A_{2x}\otimes\mathcal A_{2x+1}).
    \label{eq:gnvw12_support_algebra_star_closure_mpu_input}
\end{align}
This input is the image of a local full matrix star-algebra under a
star-automorphism and is therefore star closed.

\section{Formal resolution and remaining scope}

The formal support-algebra API takes a star-subalgebra as input. This is the
corrected hypothesis, and both intended QCA and MPU applications provide it.
The local correction is therefore resolved; the false commutant
characterization for non-star-closed inputs need not be formalized.

The distinct restriction to full complex matrix factors remains open and is
recorded in
\path{docs/paper-gaps/gnvw12_support_algebra_full_matrix_scope.tex}. Removing
that restriction requires a support-algebra construction for arbitrary
finite-dimensional $C^*$-algebra factors. It does not require weakening the
corrected star-closed input.
