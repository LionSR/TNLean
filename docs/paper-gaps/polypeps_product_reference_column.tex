\section{The source construction}

Fix a site dimension $q\ge2$, an interaction norm bound $J>0$, and a
full-system spectral-gap lower bound $\Delta>0$. Throughout this note, $C>0$
denotes a constant that depends only on $q,J,\Delta$ and fixed numerical
exponents, geometric templates, or tolerances, uniformly in the grid side
length $L$ and the Hamiltonian~\cite{OpenAI2026PolynomialPEPS}.

Section~8.1 of the September~24, 2026 polynomial-PEPS
manuscript~\cite{OpenAI2026PolynomialPEPS} (\path{07-assembly.tex},
lines~21--63) constructs a joint physical-and-private state vector $\Psi$ and a
private reference vector $\Xi$ on the private register space $E$ such that, for
the unit ground vector $\Omega$ on the $L\times L$ square lattice,
\begin{align}
    \|\Psi\| &\leq 1,
    &1-CL^{-57}\leq\|\Xi\| &\leq 1,
    &\|\Psi-\Omega\otimes\Xi\| &\leq CL^{-17}.
    \label{eq:polypeps_reference_source}
\end{align}
Tracing out $E$ and applying the distributed density compression of
Theorem~5.2 yields an operator tensor network on the party graph whose
contracted operator $\sigma$ approximates $\tr_E|\Psi\rangle\langle\Psi|$ in
trace norm. Lemma~8.1 (lines~67--94) selects a computational-basis input column
of $\sigma$ to obtain a nonzero state vector network on the party graph close
to $\Omega$, and Section~8.3 routes that network onto the open $L\times L$
square grid with polynomially bounded bond dimension.

\section{What is proved and the extra hypotheses}

The deduction from the product-reference and compression bounds to the column
extraction and grid routing is formalized as a conditional theorem taking those
bounds as hypotheses.

First, let $\Omega$ be a unit vector on a finite-dimensional physical Hilbert
space, $\Xi$ a vector on a finite-dimensional private space $E$, and $\Psi$ a
joint vector satisfying, for tolerances $\delta,\varepsilon\ge 0$,
\begin{align}
    \|\Omega\| &=1,
    &\|\Psi\| &\leq1,
    &1-\delta\leq\|\Xi\| &\leq1,
    &\|\Psi-\Omega\otimes\Xi\| &\leq\varepsilon.
    \label{eq:polypeps_reference_hypotheses}
\end{align}
Since $\tr_E|\Omega\otimes\Xi\rangle\langle\Omega\otimes\Xi| =
\|\Xi\|^2|\Omega\rangle\langle\Omega|$ and partial trace is contractive in the
trace norm $\|\cdot\|_1$, the triangle inequality and the elementary bound
$1-s^2\le 2(1-s)\le 2\delta$ for $s=\|\Xi\|\le 1$ give
\begin{align}
    \bigl\|\tr_{E}|\Psi\rangle\langle\Psi|
      -|\Omega\rangle\langle\Omega|\bigr\|_1
    &\leq 2\varepsilon+1-\|\Xi\|^2
     \leq 2\varepsilon+2\delta.
    \label{eq:polypeps_reference_marginal}
\end{align}
This derivation neither squares $1-\delta$ nor divides by $\|\Xi\|$, so it
holds even when $\Xi=0$ or $E$ is trivial.\footnote{The trace-norm inequalities
are proved in \path{QICLean/Analysis/ProductReferenceDensityError.lean} in the
companion library; in particular,
\leanid{Matrix.rectangularTraceNorm_sub_pure_le_of_product_reference} combines
the marginal bound with the operator-compression error.}

Second, suppose an operator tensor network with local tensors $A$ and virtual
bond dimensions $D_e$ has contracted operator $\sigma$ satisfying, for a
compression error $\kappa\ge 0$,
\begin{align}
    \bigl\|\sigma-\tr_{E}|\Psi\rangle\langle\Psi|\bigr\|_1
      &\leq\kappa,
    &\alpha:=\kappa+2\varepsilon+2\delta &<1.
    \label{eq:polypeps_reference_compression}
\end{align}
Then $\|\sigma-|\Omega\rangle\langle\Omega|\|_1\le\alpha<1$. By Lemma~8.1,
there exist a computational-basis label $z$ and a phase $\theta\in\mathbb R$
such that fixing the input leg of each tensor $A$ to $|z\rangle$ produces a
state tensor network with contracted vector $\Phi=\sigma|z\rangle$ satisfying
\begin{align}
    \Phi &=\sigma|z\rangle\ne0,
    &\left\|\frac{\Phi}{\|\Phi\|}-e^{i\theta}\Omega\right\|
       &\leq2\alpha.
    \label{eq:polypeps_reference_column}
\end{align}
No Hermiticity or positivity of $\sigma$ is needed. When the operator network
is already on the open $L\times L$ square grid, fixing the input preserves
every bond dimension $D_e>0$.\footnote{Declarations:
\leanid{TNLean.PEPS.Approximation.Pinned.exists_fixInput_contractPEPS_of_product_reference}
and
\leanid{TNLean.PEPS.Approximation.Pinned.exists_peps_of_product_reference} in
\path{TNLean/PEPS/Approximation/ProductReferenceColumn.lean}.}

The paper's order of operations---extracting a column on the party network
first and then routing it to the $L\times L$ grid---is also formalized. Given an
operator network on dyadic block anchors satisfying the four routing hypotheses
of Lemma~8.2 with folded congestion bound $\chi$ and link dimensions at most
$D$, any bound $\|\sigma-|\Omega\rangle\langle\Omega|\|_1\le\eta<1$ (either
given directly or obtained from the product-reference and compression bounds with
$\eta=\alpha$) yields a nonzero column on the party graph whose routing onto
the open $L\times L$ square grid is a PEPS with bond dimensions at most
$\max(D,1)^\chi$ (hence at most $(C_1 L^k)^\chi$ when $D\le C_1 L^k$ with
$C_1\ge 1,k\ge 0$) and normalized error at most $2\eta$.\footnote{Declarations:
\leanid{TNLean.PEPS.Approximation.exists_squarePEPS_of_fixInput_dyadicRouting},
\leanid{TNLean.PEPS.Approximation.exists_squarePEPS_of_product_reference_dyadicRouting},
and
\leanid{TNLean.PEPS.Approximation.hasPEPSApproximation_of_fixInput_dyadicRouting}
in \path{TNLean/PEPS/Approximation/PartyColumnRouting.lean}.}

Finally, the output of Section~8.1 for sufficiently large $L$---the existence
of a compressed dyadic operator network within trace distance $CL^{-5}$ of
$|\Omega\rangle\langle\Omega|$ with polynomial link dimensions and constants
chosen before $L$ and the Hamiltonian---is formulated as a hypothesis, and
Theorem~1.1 is proved from that hypothesis by combining the routed column for
large $L$ with the exact PEPS representation for bounded $L$.\footnote{The
hypothesis is \leanid{TNLean.PEPS.Approximation.LargeSizeDyadicOperatorNetwork}
and the implication is
\leanid{TNLean.PEPS.Approximation.polynomialPEPSApproximation_of_largeSizeDyadicOperatorNetwork}
in \path{TNLean/PEPS/Approximation/GroundStateApproximation.lean}.}

\section{Effect on the paper and what remains}

These conditional theorems follow the paper's proof in Section~8 without
modifying any mathematical claim. What remains to make Theorem~1.1
unconditional is to construct the protocol's joint vector $\Psi$, reference
vector $\Xi$, and compressed party network, and to verify the three
product-reference bounds, the compression error of Theorem~5.2, and
the four routing hypotheses of Lemma~8.2 with constants uniform in $L$ and the
Hamiltonian.\footnote{The assembly of Theorem~1.1 is tracked at
\url{https://github.com/LionSR/TNLean/issues/8773}; distributed density
compression is tracked at \url{https://github.com/LionSR/TNLean/issues/8769}.}
