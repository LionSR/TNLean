\section{The source construction}

Section~8.1 of the polynomial PEPS approximation manuscript of
September~24, 2026~\cite{OpenAI2026PolynomialPEPS}
(\path{07-assembly.tex}, lines~21--63) constructs an actual joint vector
$\Psi$ and a private reference vector $\Xi$ such that, for the unit ground
vector $\Omega$,
\begin{align}
    \|\Psi\| &\leq 1,
    &1-CL^{-57}\leq\|\Xi\| &\leq 1,
    &\|\Psi-\Omega\otimes\Xi\| &\leq CL^{-17}.
\end{align}
It traces the private coordinates and applies distributed compression to
this same joint vector. Lemma~8.1 (lines~67--94) extracts a nonzero column
of the compressed operator on the party network. Section~8.3 then routes
the ket network to the open square, preserving its contraction and bounding
the resulting bond dimensions. The final constants must be uniform in the
lattice size and Hamiltonian.

\section{The conditional estimate}

For arbitrary finite physical and private spaces, suppose
\begin{align}
    \|\Omega\| &=1,
    &\|\Psi\| &\leq1,
    &1-\delta\leq\|\Xi\| &\leq1,
    &\|\Psi-\Omega\otimes\Xi\| &\leq\varepsilon.
\end{align}
The product marginal equals
$\|\Xi\|^2|\Omega\rangle\langle\Omega|$. The subnormalized pure-state
estimate and contraction under partial trace therefore give
\begin{align}
    \bigl\|\operatorname{Tr}_{E}|\Psi\rangle\langle\Psi|
      -|\Omega\rangle\langle\Omega|\bigr\|_1
    &\leq 2\varepsilon+1-\|\Xi\|^2
     \leq 2\varepsilon+2\delta.
\end{align}
Here the last inequality uses
$1-s^2\leq2(1-s)\leq2\delta$ for $s=\|\Xi\|\leq1$.
It neither squares a possibly negative lower bound nor divides by $s$.
The accepted generic result includes a zero private vector and an empty
private coordinate set.\footnote{The generic inequalities are in
\path{QICLean/Analysis/ProductReferenceDensityError.lean}, in the companion
library. In particular,
\leanid{Matrix.rectangularTraceNorm_sub_pure_le_of_product_reference}
includes the additional compressed-operator error.}

Now suppose an operator network with tensors $A$ and edge dimensions $D_e$
is already on the open $L\times L$ square. Write $\sigma$ for its contracted
operator, and assume
\begin{align}
    \bigl\|\sigma-\operatorname{Tr}_{E}|\Psi\rangle\langle\Psi|\bigr\|_1
      &\leq\kappa,
    &\alpha:=\kappa+2\varepsilon+2\delta &<1.
\end{align}
The triangle inequality yields
$\|\sigma-|\Omega\rangle\langle\Omega|\|_1\leq\alpha$.
Lemma~8.1 then supplies a product-basis index $z$ and a real $\theta$ such
that fixing the input of each tensor $A$ to $z$ gives
\begin{align}
    \Phi &=\sigma|z\rangle\ne0,
    &\left\|\frac{\Phi}{\|\Phi\|}-e^{i\theta}\Omega\right\|
       &\leq2\alpha.
\end{align}
Every edge keeps exactly its original dimension $D_e$. A zero dimension
would make the contraction vanish, so $D_e>0$; every bound $D_e\leq B$
is preserved. No positivity or Hermiticity of $\sigma$ is assumed.
The two error estimates use the same $\Psi$.\footnote{The two statements
are
\leanid{TNLean.PEPS.Approximation.Pinned.exists_fixInput_contractPEPS_of_product_reference}
and
\leanid{TNLean.PEPS.Approximation.Pinned.exists_peps_of_product_reference},
in \path{TNLean/PEPS/Approximation/ProductReferenceColumn.lean}.}

\section{Remaining construction}

The conditional estimate does not construct $\Psi$, $\Xi$, their terminal
physical and private coordinates, or the compressed network. To apply it
to the source theorem, those objects must be identified with the actual
protocol, and their three estimates must be proved with the same vector
and constants uniform in the lattice size and Hamiltonian.

The statement above assumes an operator network already on the square,
whereas the source fixes a column of the party network before routing it.
The source order is now also available. For an operator party network on
dyadic anchors satisfying the four routing properties of Lemma~8.2, an
estimate $\|\sigma-|\Omega\rangle\langle\Omega|\|_1\leq\eta<1$ for its
operator read at the folded anchors gives a nonzero column on the party
graph, and routing that ket network gives a PEPS on the open square with the
same contraction, positive bond dimensions at most $\max(D,1)^{\chi}$, and
normalized error at most $2\eta$. The estimate may be supplied directly or
through the product-reference bound above with $\eta=\alpha$. With link
dimensions at most $C_1L^k$, where $C_1\geq1$ and $k\geq0$, the bond
dimensions are at most $(C_1L^k)^{\chi}$.\footnote{The statements are
\leanid{TNLean.PEPS.Approximation.exists_squarePEPS_of_fixInput_dyadicRouting},
\leanid{TNLean.PEPS.Approximation.exists_squarePEPS_of_product_reference_dyadicRouting}
and
\leanid{TNLean.PEPS.Approximation.hasPEPSApproximation_of_fixInput_dyadicRouting},
in \path{TNLean/PEPS/Approximation/PartyColumnRouting.lean}.}
The order difference is therefore resolved. The routing properties remain
hypotheses on the party network; they are recorded in the separate note on
the routing lemma.

Finally, the output of Section~8.1 for large sizes, a compressed dyadic
operator network within $CL^{-5}$ of $|\Omega\rangle\langle\Omega|$ with
polynomial link dimensions and constants chosen before $L$ and the
Hamiltonian, is stated as a proposition. That proposition implies the uniform
approximation statement of Theorem~1.1, through the routed column and the
exact finite-size construction.\footnote{The proposition is
\leanid{TNLean.PEPS.Approximation.LargeSizeDyadicOperatorNetwork} and the
implication is
\leanid{TNLean.PEPS.Approximation.polynomialPEPSApproximation_of_largeSizeDyadicOperatorNetwork},
in \path{TNLean/PEPS/Approximation/GroundStateApproximation.lean}.}
The proposition itself is not proved.

These are remaining implications in the formal construction, not errors
in the source. The conditional statement is a separate mathematical
consequence of supplied estimates; it is not Theorem~1.1. Resolving the
restriction requires the actual protocol and compression estimates, with the
routing properties of the actual party network, to prove the large-size
proposition; the routing and the uniform large-size and finite-size
arguments are already in place.\footnote{The full target is tracked
at \url{https://github.com/LionSR/TNLean/issues/8773}; distributed
compression is tracked at \url{https://github.com/LionSR/TNLean/issues/8769}.
The already established generic nonzero-column result is unchanged.}
