\section{The assertion}

The periodic-MPS paper states that normal tensors are exactly periodic tensors
of period $1$. It also states that an irreducible decomposition whose blocks
are all normal is the canonical form called Ci15 in the source
\cite[Section~II]{DeLasCuevas2017Irreducible}.\footnote{The local source
passage is arXiv:1708.00029, lines~258--271.}
The same paragraph fixes the irreducible-form weights by the convention
$\mu_j>0$.

The blueprint records the corresponding implication: a normal canonical block
family assembles into an irreducible-form tensor whose block periods all equal
$1$.

\section{The formal statements}

The formal irreducible-form predicate records the positive-weight convention
from Ref.~\cite{DeLasCuevas2017Irreducible}: for every block weight $\mu_j$,
\begin{align}
    \operatorname{Re}\mu_j
        &> 0,
    \label{eq:dccsp17_normal_canonical_irreducible_form_weights_positive_real_part}\\
    \operatorname{Im}\mu_j
        &= 0.
    \label{eq:dccsp17_normal_canonical_irreducible_form_weights_zero_imaginary_part}
\end{align}
By contrast, the formal predicate for normal canonical block hypotheses is
inherited from the nonperiodic canonical-form formulation. It assumes only
that the complex weights are nonzero and ordered by modulus.

Section~II of Ref.~\cite{DeLasCuevas2017Irreducible} later introduces a
minimal non-repeated basis of periodic tensors. That basis condition is
separate from the irreducible-form definition considered here.

The formalization now proves the required phase-normalized construction.
Starting with a normal canonical block family whose complex weights satisfy
$\mu_j\neq0$, it replaces each weight and block by
\begin{align}
    \mu_j
        &\longmapsto |\mu_j|,
    \label{eq:dccsp17_normal_canonical_irreducible_form_weights_weight_normalization}\\
    A_j
        &\longmapsto \frac{\mu_j}{|\mu_j|}A_j.
    \label{eq:dccsp17_normal_canonical_irreducible_form_weights_block_phase_absorption}
\end{align}
The assembled tensor has the same matrix-product-vector family. Its
irreducible-form weights are positive real numbers, and all block periods are
$1$.\footnote{Formal declarations:
\leanid{MPSTensor.toIsIrreducibleFormOfPhaseNormalized} and
\leanid{MPSTensor.toIsIrreducibleFormOfPhaseNormalized_period_eq_one} in
\doclink{TNLean/MPS/Periodic/NormalCanonicalPeriodOne.lean}.}

\section{Conclusion}

This note records a resolved discrepancy. The irreducible-form convention in
Ref.~\cite{DeLasCuevas2017Irreducible} requires positive weights, whereas the
local formal normal-canonical predicate permits arbitrary nonzero complex
weights. The phase normalization in
Eqs.~\ref{eq:dccsp17_normal_canonical_irreducible_form_weights_weight_normalization}
and~\ref{eq:dccsp17_normal_canonical_irreducible_form_weights_block_phase_absorption}
bridges these formulations without changing the represented
matrix-product-vector family. The resulting weights satisfy the positive-real
conditions in
Eqs.~\ref{eq:dccsp17_normal_canonical_irreducible_form_weights_positive_real_part}
and~\ref{eq:dccsp17_normal_canonical_irreducible_form_weights_zero_imaginary_part},
and every resulting block has period $1$.
