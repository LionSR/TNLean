Schuch, Cirac, and P\'erez-Garc\'ia,
\cite[Lemma~6.14]{Schuch2010PEPS}, source
\path{Papers/1001.3807/paper_v3.tex}, lines 2199--2214, deform flux strings
by virtual invariance. The following is an auxiliary algorithm for the
explicit relation generated by permitted square moves and backtracks.
It does not identify all strings having the same endpoints.

Let $X=\mathbb Z_w\times\mathbb Z_h$ with $w,h>0$, and let $R\subseteq X$
be the permitted primal square supports. Write $p\sim_Rq$ for the generated
relation on finite labelled paths with common endpoints. The reverse-ordered
native word $W(p)$ belongs to the free group on the actual positive east and
north edges. At lower-left base $v$, the face relator is
$r_v=BAC^{-1}D^{-1}$, with bottom, right, left and top generators $A,B,C,D$.
A west tag replaces $C$ by $D^{-1}BA$; a south tag replaces $A$ by $B^{-1}DC$.
All other generators are fixed. A tagged list $l$ composes these substitutions
with the tail acting after the head; write the result $\Sigma_l$.

Define the finite certificate
\begin{align}
    C_R(l)=
    [\,|l|\le |X|\ \land\
    \forall(v,b)\in l,\ v+(1,1)\in R\ \land\
    \forall v,\ v+(1,1)\in R\Rightarrow
    \operatorname{red}(\Sigma_l(r_v))=[]\,].
    \label{eq:scp10_executable_face_certificate}
\end{align}
Here brackets denote a Boolean test, and $\operatorname{red}$ is finite
free-word reduction. All quantifiers range over finite native data.
First try a coordinate-rank greedy list based at an excluded face.
Its success is checked rather than assumed. If its certificate fails,
a depth-first search enumerates tagged native lists of length at most $|X|$,
checking each prefix before its extensions. Both branches compute finite data;
no certificate, word equality or homotopy witness is an algorithmic input.

For proper $R$, geometric face elimination supplies at least one certified
list of length at most $|X|$. Induction on the remaining search depth proves
that a failed search excludes every extension within that depth. Therefore
the bounded search must succeed. The computed list $l_R$ satisfies
\begin{align}
    C_R(l_R)=\mathrm{true},\qquad
    p\sim_Rq\ \Longleftrightarrow\
    \operatorname{red}(\Sigma_{l_R}(W(p)))
    =\operatorname{red}(\Sigma_{l_R}(W(q))).
    \label{eq:scp10_executable_proper_support}
\end{align}
For the forward implication, left multiplication by transformed edge words
is a reversible transport flat on all allowed faces. For the converse, the
native presentation quotient $\pi_R$ is unchanged by each permitted
substitution, hence $\pi_R\circ\Sigma_{l_R}=\pi_R$. Equal transformed words
give equal original quotient words, and native presentation completeness
gives the supported homotopy.

For $R=X$, use the complete lifted integer displacement
$\Delta(p)\in\mathbb Z^2$ instead. Thus the Boolean algorithm is
\begin{align}
    D_R(p,q)&=
    \begin{cases}
        [\,\Delta(p)=\Delta(q)\,],&R=X,\\
        [\,\operatorname{red}(\Sigma_{l_R}(W(p)))
        =\operatorname{red}(\Sigma_{l_R}(W(q)))\,],&R\ne X,
    \end{cases}\\
    D_R(p,q)=\mathrm{true}&\ \Longleftrightarrow\ p\sim_Rq.
    \label{eq:scp10_executable_general_support}
\end{align}
The implementation computes $W(p)$ from its finite signed native letter
list. Its definitions are computable, and its correctness theorem supplies
no homotopy or quotient-word oracle.

The positive-period domain includes periods one and two, with their separate
native edge labels. Zero periods are outside this finite-torus algorithm.
The bounded fallback can be exponential; no complexity bound or universal
adequacy of the greedy candidate is asserted. Missing-face loop words and
winding are retained. Physical reduced-density equality and annular
Wilson-loop observations are separate questions; this algorithm does not
remove their geometric obstructions. The broader source interpretation
remains open under \ghissue{8703}. Hamiltonian work under \ghissue{8947}
is unchanged.
