\section{What the source states}
Let $A^i=\bigoplus_{j=1}^b\operatorname{diag}(\mu_{j,1},\dots,\mu_{j,m_j})
    \otimes A_j^i$ be a basis of normal tensors, let $\ket{\omega_j}$ be the
fixed-point pair of the normal block $A_j$, embedded in the bond space
$\C^D\otimes\C^D$ of $A$, and put
\begin{align}
    \ket{\Omega'}
     & =\sum_{j=1}^b\alpha_j\bigotimes_{k=1}^{M}\ket{\omega_j}_{R_kL_{k+1}}.
    \label{eq:mswc24_fixed_point_state}
\end{align}
After eq.~(S7) of~\cite{Malz2024Preparation} the pairs are asserted to be
locally orthogonal,
\begin{align}
    \braket{\omega_j}{\omega_{j'}} & =\delta_{jj'},
    \label{eq:mswc24_local_orthogonality}
\end{align}
with a reference to the canonical-form theory of matrix product density
operators. The paragraph after eq.~(19) then concludes that
$W\colon\ket{j}\mapsto\ket{\omega_j}$ is an isometry and that
$\ket{\Omega'}=W^{\otimes M}\ket{\chi_M}$ with
$\ket{\chi_M}=\sum_j\alpha_j\ket{j}^{\otimes M}$. In the source,
(\ref{eq:mswc24_local_orthogonality}) is a property of these particular pairs,
not a hypothesis.

\section{The restriction adopted}
The formal statements are made for an arbitrary family
$\omega_1,\dots,\omega_b\in\C^D\otimes\C^D$ and arbitrary coefficients
$\alpha$.\footnote{Formal declarations:
    \leanid{MPSTensor.isIsometry_pairIsometry},
    \leanid{MPSTensor.nonNormalFixedPointState_eq_tensorPower_mulVec_ghzState}
    and \leanid{MPSTensor.nonNormalFixedPointState_norm_sq} in
    \doclink{TNLean/MPS/Preparation/NonNormalFixedPoint}.}
The identity $\ket{\Omega'}=W^{\otimes M}\ket{\chi_M}$ holds for every such
family, since
$W^{\otimes M}\ket{j}^{\otimes M}=\bigotimes_k\ket{\omega_j}$. The isometry of
$W$ and the normalization $\braket{\Omega'}{\Omega'}=1$ take
(\ref{eq:mswc24_local_orthogonality}) as a hypothesis. For the pairs of the
source they are therefore proved only once
(\ref{eq:mswc24_local_orthogonality}) is supplied.

One half of the hypothesis is available: if the components $\omega_{(a,c)}$ vanish
for $a\notin S$ and the components $\omega'_{(a,c)}$ vanish for $a\notin S'$,
where $S$ and $S'$ are disjoint sets of virtual indices, then $\braket{\omega}{\omega'}=0$.\footnote{Formal declaration:
    \leanid{MPSTensor.inner_pair_eq_zero_of_disjoint} in the same module.}
The embedded pairs of distinct blocks are supported in this way.

\section{A discharged instance}
The hypothesis is discharged for one identification of the pairs, which the
source does not make.\footnote{Formal declarations:
    \leanid{MPSTensor.SectorDecomposition.inner_embeddedFixedPointPair},
    \leanid{MPSTensor.SectorDecomposition.isIsometry_pairIsometry_embeddedFixedPointPair},
    \leanid{MPSTensor.SectorDecomposition.canonicalFixedPointState_eq_tensorPower_mulVec_ghzState}
    and \leanid{MPSTensor.SectorDecomposition.canonicalFixedPointState_norm_sq} in
    \doclink{TNLean/MPS/Preparation/NonNormalCanonicalForm}.}
The canonical form is taken as a sector decomposition, whose bond space is
$\C^D$ with $D=\sum_jm_jD_j$ and whose blocks carry no normality or gauge
condition. The pair of block $j$ is the fixed-point pair of a positive
semidefinite, unit-trace matrix $\sigma_j$ on $\C^{D_j}$, a free parameter,
placed on both legs on the bond coordinates of one chosen copy $k_j$ of that
block. The coordinates of distinct blocks are distinct, so the right legs of
the pairs of distinct blocks have disjoint supports and the pairs are
orthogonal by the disjoint-support lemma; the coordinates of one copy are
distinct, so $\braket{\omega_j}{\omega_j}=\operatorname{Tr}\sigma_j=1$. For
these pairs (\ref{eq:mswc24_local_orthogonality}) holds, and the isometry of
$W$, the GHZ form and the normalization hold without the hypothesis.

This orthogonality follows from the placement alone. It uses neither the
normality of the blocks nor the basis-of-normal-tensors property, which is the
reason the source gives. The source's pairs are the fixed points of the
positive parts $P_j$ of the blocked tensor, and the block form of the positive
part that would identify them with the pairs above is not formalized. When some
block has multiplicity $m_j\ge2$ that block form fails and no product of pairs
carrying the weights $\beta_j$ gives the approximating state
\cite{gap:mswc24_multiplicity_fixed_point}.

\section{Elimination}
Formalize the block form of the positive part of the blocked tensor for
$m_j=1$, identify its fixed-point pairs with the embedded pairs above, and
derive (\ref{eq:mswc24_local_orthogonality}) for them; the case $m_j\ge2$ is
governed by the false-source record
\cite{gap:mswc24_multiplicity_fixed_point}.
