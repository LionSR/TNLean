\section{The cyclic mismatch}

Theorem~4.9 of Ref.~\cite[lines~851--893]{Cirac2016MPDO_arXiv} assigns the
following labels: condition~(ii) consists of SAL and literal ZCL;
condition~(iii) consists of Gibbs form and literal ZCL; condition~(iv) is the
BNT physical-sector structure; and condition~(v) is the renormalization
fixed-point property. The propositions at source lines~1740--1817 have the
literal component conclusions
\begin{align}
    \mathtt{prop2to3}
        &:\quad (ii)\Longrightarrow(iv),
    \label{eq:cpgsv17_mpdo_theorem_4_9_implication_label_prop2to3}\\
    \mathtt{prop3to4}
        &:\quad (iv)\Longrightarrow
        \text{the GSNNCH-form clause of (iii)},
    \label{eq:cpgsv17_mpdo_theorem_4_9_implication_label_prop3to4}\\
    \mathtt{prop4to2}
        &:\quad \text{the GSNNCH-form clause of (iii)}
        \Longrightarrow\text{the SAL clause of (ii)},
    \label{eq:cpgsv17_mpdo_theorem_4_9_implication_label_prop4to2}\\
    \mathtt{prop2to5}
        &:\quad (ii)\Longrightarrow
        \text{a channel pair between the two- and three-site tensors}.
    \label{eq:cpgsv17_mpdo_theorem_4_9_implication_label_prop2to5}
\end{align}
In particular,
Eq.~\ref{eq:cpgsv17_mpdo_theorem_4_9_implication_label_prop3to4} does not
include the ZCL conjunct of condition~(iii), and
Eq.~\ref{eq:cpgsv17_mpdo_theorem_4_9_implication_label_prop4to2} does not
include the ZCL conjunct of condition~(ii). The proof of
\texttt{prop3to4} also invokes \texttt{lemmus}, at source
lines~1646--1658, to show that the repeated-copy weights do not depend on the
copy label. That lemma assumes ZCL, whereas the displayed statement of
\texttt{prop3to4} assumes only condition~(iv). The printed signature is
therefore not established by its proof; the proof path uses a load-bearing
hypothesis absent from that signature.

Equation~\ref{eq:cpgsv17_mpdo_theorem_4_9_implication_label_prop2to5} likewise
does not conclude condition~(v). Nevertheless, the concluding proof at source
lines~1819--1825 successively identifies the propositions with
\begin{align}
    (ii)&\Longrightarrow(iii),
    \label{eq:cpgsv17_mpdo_theorem_4_9_implication_label_concluding_ii_iii}\\
    (iii)&\Longrightarrow(iv),
    \label{eq:cpgsv17_mpdo_theorem_4_9_implication_label_concluding_iii_iv}\\
    (iv)&\Longrightarrow(ii),
    \label{eq:cpgsv17_mpdo_theorem_4_9_implication_label_concluding_iv_ii}\\
    (iii)&\Longrightarrow(v).
    \label{eq:cpgsv17_mpdo_theorem_4_9_implication_label_concluding_iii_v}
\end{align}
The discrepancy affects both the proof path and the component conclusions. It
cannot be repaired merely by replacing the last occurrence of ``(iii)'' with
``(iv)''.

Proposition~\texttt{3to5} of
Ref.~\cite[lines~1510--1563]{Cirac2016MPDO_arXiv} constructs a pair of
channels from one supplied physical-sector factorization.
Proposition~\texttt{prop2to5} applies that construction separately in each
locally orthogonal BNT subspace and adds the step that combines the resulting
maps over all BNT sectors. Source lines~1821--1825 then apply the
two-to-three-site channels twice to obtain the one-to-two-site maps for the
blocked tensor in condition~(v).

\section{The literal implication (iv)$\Rightarrow$(v) is false}

Condition~(iv) constrains each normal BNT representative but does not constrain
the raw weights of its repeated copies. This omission is essential. Let the
only scalar representative be $A=1$, and take two copies with weights $1$ and
$1/2$. The resulting one-letter tensor has the local virtual matrix
\begin{align}
    K^{00}
        &=\operatorname{diag}(1,1/2).
    \label{eq:cpgsv17_mpdo_theorem_4_9_implication_label_counterexample_tensor}
\end{align}
This tensor is a BNT canonical presentation with a global unit-weight copy. It
generates positive semidefinite periodic operators at every positive length,
and its only BNT representative has a nonnilpotent physical-trace transfer.
The singleton BNT family has simultaneous one-letter span. The representative
generates MPDOs, satisfies the distinct-layer equation vacuously, and admits
the scalar physical-sector factorization with a positive neighboring operator
and normalized rank-one trace coefficients. Thus every standing hypothesis
and every clause of condition~(iv) has an explicit component witness. The
two-site block also generates MPDOs.

The only local matrix of the two-site block is $(K^{00})^2$. If this block
satisfied Definition~4.1 of Ref.~\cite{Cirac2016MPDO_arXiv}, trace preservation
would make its physical-trace transfer idempotent. Hence
\begin{align}
    (K^{00})^4
        &=(K^{00})^2,
    \label{eq:cpgsv17_mpdo_theorem_4_9_implication_label_idempotence}\\
    \tr\!\left((K^{00})^4\right)
        &=\tr\!\left((K^{00})^2\right),
    \label{eq:cpgsv17_mpdo_theorem_4_9_implication_label_trace_idempotence}\\
    1+2^{-4}
        &=1+2^{-2},
    \label{eq:cpgsv17_mpdo_theorem_4_9_implication_label_trace_contradiction}
\end{align}
which is false. The kernel-checked capstone is
\leanid{MPOTensor.CaseIIAbsorptionCounterexample.printed_theorem49_iv_to_v_is_false}.
Consequently, the literal implication $(iv)\Rightarrow(v)$ is refuted. Its
mathematical status is an \emph{unfaithful statement}; presenting
projector-controlled outer-BNT assembly as its sole missing step would instead
be \emph{proof-path drift}. No channel-assembly argument can establish the
assertion with unrestricted raw copy weights.

This counterexample alone does not refute the source proposition
\texttt{prop2to5}, because that proposition starts from condition~(ii), not
condition~(iv). The separate five-letter ambient counterexample
\leanid{MPOTensor.NeighboringTraceObstructionAmbientCounterexample.%
printed_theorem49_ii_to_iv_is_false} has a two-representative BNT presentation
in biCF. It satisfies the complete condition-(ii) and line-246 normalization
hypotheses, but its first absorbed representative has no normalized rank-one
neighboring-trace factorization. This refutes Proposition~\texttt{prop2to3}
and the printed route through it. The projector-controlled assembly formalized
in \ghissue{6632} remains valid conditionally for supplied representative
factorizations. The ambient witness does not rule out different blocking
channels. The source-facing Lean statement for
Proposition~\texttt{prop2to5} is therefore still missing, and its bare
conclusion is neither proved nor refuted.

\section{Formal treatment}

The formal development does not attach the one-factorization construction to
any incorrect implication label in the concluding paragraph. It instead cites
Proposition~\texttt{3to5} and the twice-applied channel observation at source
lines~1821--1825 directly. The resulting theorem assumes one factorization of
its entire input tensor. It is therefore a conditional helper. Condition~(iv)
provides data only for each representative and does not supply this hypothesis
for a raw repeated-copy tensor. The formal development accordingly does not
present the false implication $(iv)\Rightarrow(v)$ as an unfinished
direct-sum construction.

Files that rely on the concluding paragraph carry a \emph{Local fix (cyclic
implication labels)} marker and cite this note. The marker records the printed
proof-path drift, including the hidden use of ZCL in \texttt{prop3to4}. The
restriction to one supplied factorization is recorded separately in
\url{https://sirui-lu.com/QICLean/paper-gaps/cpgsv17_pf_rank_one.pdf}. The projector-controlled
assembly used in the source proof of \texttt{prop2to5}, after common-weight
absorption derived from ZCL, is formalized in \ghissue{6632}. It is a
conditional construction. Since the printed route obtains its premise from the
false Proposition~\texttt{prop2to3}, it does not prove \texttt{prop2to5}. The
source proposition therefore still has no source-facing Lean formalization,
and no counterexample to its bare channel conclusion is claimed.
