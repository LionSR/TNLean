\section{The printed estimate}

In the section on soft truncation, Arad, Landau, Vazirani and
Vidick~\cite{Arad2016RigorousRG},
\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/1602.08828v2}{arXiv:1602.08828v2}, state
Theorem~\path{thm:cluster-approx} for
$H=\sum_{i=1}^{n-1}h_i$ on a chain of $n$ qudits, with $h_i$ supported on
$\{i,i+1\}$ and $\|h_i\|\le1$. The paragraph defining the expansion explicitly
allows terms that are not nonnegative. For a word $w$ in the interaction
labels, put
\begin{align}
    f(w)=\frac{(-\beta)^{|w|}}{|w|!}h_w,
    \qquad h_w=h_{w_1}\cdots h_{w_{|w|}}.
    \label{eq:alvv_cluster_word}
\end{align}
The operator $M_r(H)$ keeps the words whose support has connected components
of fewer than $r$ physical sites. The printed assertion is
\begin{align}
    \|e^{-\beta H}-M_r(H)\|
    \le \exp\bigl(n^2(e^\beta-1)^r\bigr)-1,
    \qquad 0<\beta<\log2.
    \label{eq:alvv_cluster_printed}
\end{align}
The source locations are \path{local_alg_arxiv_v2.tex}, lines 1270--1291
(definition and statement), and the appendix \emph{Constructing an MPO for the
    cluster expansion}, lines 1924--1968 (proof).
There are two independent obstructions: the proof counts interaction labels
where the statement counts physical sites, and it bounds a complementary
exponential by one without assuming positivity.

\section{Two sites: the support cutoff}

Take two qubits and the single term
$H=h_1=P=|00\rangle\langle00|$, with
$r=2$ and $\beta=\log(11/10)$. This term is positive, has norm one,
and acts nontrivially on both qubits. Every nonempty word evaluates to $P$,
so its connected support has two sites and it is excluded. The empty word
is retained. Thus
\begin{align}
    M_2(H)                  & =I,                 &
    e^{-\beta P}            & =I+(e^{-\beta}-1)P, &
    \|e^{-\beta P}-M_2(H)\| & =\frac1{11}.
    \label{eq:alvv_cluster_two_sites}
\end{align}
The right side of (\ref{eq:alvv_cluster_printed}) is
$\exp(4(1/10)^2)-1=\exp(1/25)-1$. For $0<x<1$, comparison of the
exponential and geometric series gives $e^x<1/(1-x)$. Consequently
\begin{align}
    \exp(1/25)-1<\frac1{24}<\frac1{11}.
    \label{eq:alvv_cluster_two_sites_comparison}
\end{align}
This contradicts the printed bound even for positive local terms. A connected
interval of $r$ sites requires only $r-1$ distinct nearest-neighbour
interaction labels. The factor $(e^\beta-1)^{|I|}$ in the appendix counts
labels, not sites.

\section{Forty sites: negative local terms}

Changing the cutoff to count interaction labels does not resolve the
unrestricted statement. On forty qubits let
\begin{align}
    h_i   & =-P_i,                                                          &
    P_i   & =|00\rangle\langle00|_{i,i+1}\otimes I_{\{i,i+1\}^{\mathrm c}},
    \qquad 1\le i\le39,
    \label{eq:alvv_cluster_negative_terms}                                             \\
    \beta & =\log(3/2),                                                     & r & =20.
    \notag
\end{align}
These are commuting Hermitian terms of norm one with two-site
supports. Let $\Omega=|0\rangle^{\otimes40}$. For every word of length $k$,
\begin{align}
    f(w)=\frac{\beta^k}{k!}P_{w_1}\cdots P_{w_k},
    \qquad \langle\Omega,f(w)\Omega\rangle=\frac{\beta^k}{k!}\ge0.
    \label{eq:alvv_cluster_positive_coefficients}
\end{align}
Every nonempty product of the $P_i$ fixes precisely the qubits in the union
of its edges to zero and is the identity elsewhere. Its actual physical
support is therefore that union; there is no cancellation of supports.

Consider only words containing each of labels $1,\ldots,20$ at least once,
with labels $21,\ldots,39$ unrestricted. Each such word has a connected
component with at least twenty interaction labels and twenty-one physical
sites. It is omitted for either interpretation of the cutoff $r=20$.
Moreover,
\begin{align}
    \sum_w\|f(w)\|
    \le\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}\frac{39^k\beta^k}{k!}=e^{39\beta},
    \label{eq:alvv_cluster_absolute_convergence}
\end{align}
so selecting and regrouping these words is justified. At fixed
multiplicities $m_1,\ldots,m_{39}$, their number is
$(\sum_i m_i)!/\prod_i m_i!$. Hence their total expectation is
\begin{align}
    \prod_{i=1}^{20}\Bigl(\sum_{m_i\ge1}\frac{\beta^{m_i}}{m_i!}\Bigr)
    \prod_{i=21}^{39}\Bigl(\sum_{m_i\ge0}\frac{\beta^{m_i}}{m_i!}\Bigr)
     & =(e^\beta-1)^{20}e^{19\beta}
    =\frac{3^{19}}{2^{39}}.
    \label{eq:alvv_cluster_selected_weight}
\end{align}
All omitted words have nonnegative expectation by
(\ref{eq:alvv_cluster_positive_coefficients}). Since $\|\Omega\|=1$,
\begin{align}
    \|e^{-\beta H}-M_{20}(H)\|
    \ge\langle\Omega,(e^{-\beta H}-M_{20}(H))\Omega\rangle
    \ge\frac{3^{19}}{2^{39}}.
    \label{eq:alvv_cluster_negative_lower_bound}
\end{align}
This also holds for the alternative truncation by interaction-label
components. Put $x=40^2(1/2)^{20}=25/16384$. The proposed upper bound satisfies
\begin{align}
    e^x-1<\frac{x}{1-x}=\frac{25}{16359}<\frac{3^{19}}{2^{39}},
    \label{eq:alvv_cluster_negative_comparison}
\end{align}
where the final inequality is the exact integer comparison
\begin{align}
    25\cdot2^{39}=13743895347200
    <19013435338653=16359\cdot3^{19}.
    \label{eq:alvv_cluster_integer_comparison}
\end{align}
Equations (\ref{eq:alvv_cluster_negative_lower_bound}) and
(\ref{eq:alvv_cluster_negative_comparison}) contradict
(\ref{eq:alvv_cluster_printed}) under either cutoff convention.

\section{The missing hypotheses and the remaining problem}

The appendix uses $\|e^{-\beta H_{\overline I}}\|\le1$ at line 1948.
This follows for positive local terms, but not from Hermiticity and
$\|h_i\|\le1$. If the complementary sum has $m$ terms, these hypotheses
give only $\|e^{-\beta H_{\overline I}}\|\le e^{\beta m}$. The general
positive-term convention earlier in the paper (lines 267, 806 and 1175)
cannot supply the missing assumption here: lines 1271--1272 explicitly
allow terms that are not nonnegative.

A corrected theorem must specify its cutoff and its energy dependence.
For positive terms with an interaction-label cutoff, the occupancy factor
$(e^\beta-1)^r$ has the intended meaning. For a physical-site cutoff, an
interval of $r$ sites gives only $r-1$ mandatory labels. For arbitrary
Hermitian terms an additional bound on the complementary exponential is
needed. Each alternative requires its own statement and proof. Adding
positivity alone, or changing the cutoff alone, does not repair
(\ref{eq:alvv_cluster_printed}). Shifting the Hamiltonian by a scalar also
requires checking the induced change of the word-defined truncation.

The printed theorem is not formalized in TNLean. The existing Hastings
cluster estimates retain the partition function and the weight
$(e^{\beta J}-1)e^{SJ\beta}$; they do not assert
(\ref{eq:alvv_cluster_printed}). Their formalized statements remain
unaffected. The efficient soft-truncation approximation in
Lemma~\texttt{lem:exp-approx-eff}, whose proof invokes this theorem, is
formalized instead through the corrected interaction-label estimate of the
section on the corrected normalized estimate and the section on the soft
interval at compatible heat times below.
The exact matrix product operator construction of the selected expansion
is a separate algebraic question. The correction is tracked by
\href{https://github.com/LionSR/TNLean/issues/9854}{issue 9854} and
\href{https://github.com/LionSR/TNLean/issues/9847}{issue 9847}.

\section{A corrected normalized interaction-label estimate}

Let $K=\{0,\ldots,N-1\}$ be the path of interaction labels, let
$H=\sum_{i\in K}h_i$, and suppose the $h_i$ are Hermitian with
$\|h_i\|\leq1$. Let $\lambda$ be any real number with $H\geq\lambda I$.
For an integer $r\geq1$, denote by $M_r^{\mathrm{lab}}$ the same word
expansion as in (\ref{eq:alvv_cluster_word}), retaining the words whose
label supports have no path component of at least $r$ labels. If
$\beta\geq0$ and $b=2(e^\beta-1)\leq1$, then
\begin{align}
    e^{\beta\lambda}\|e^{-\beta H}-M_r^{\mathrm{lab}}\|
    \leq\exp(N^2b^r)-1.
    \label{eq:alvv_cluster_normalized_correction}
\end{align}
This estimate is proved for the existing selected word expansion. Neither
positivity of the terms nor commutation between different terms is needed
for (\ref{eq:alvv_cluster_normalized_correction}).

Here is a proof. For a finite set $U$ of required labels define
\begin{align}
    R_U^H(\beta)
    =\sum_{D\subseteq U}(-1)^{|D|}e^{-\beta(H-H_D)},
    \qquad H_D=\sum_{i\in D}h_i.
    \label{eq:alvv_cluster_mandatory_labels}
\end{align}
The normalized mandatory-label estimate gives
$e^{\beta\lambda}\|R_U^H(\beta)\|\leq(e^\beta-1)^{|U|}$.
Let $\mathcal W_r$ be the family of intervals of exactly $r$ labels.
A support has a component of size at least $r$ exactly when it contains
one of these windows. Finite inclusion and exclusion, followed by grouping
according to $U=\bigcup F$, therefore gives
\begin{align}
    e^{-\beta H}-M_r^{\mathrm{lab}}
        & =\sum_{U\in\mathcal U_r}c_UR_U^H(\beta),
        & \mathcal U_r                                           & =\{\bigcup F:\varnothing\ne F\subseteq\mathcal W_r\},
    \label{eq:alvv_cluster_mandatory_union}                                                                              \\
    c_U & =\sum_{\substack{\varnothing\ne F\subseteq\mathcal W_r                                                         \\
            \bigcup F=U}}(-1)^{|F|+1},
        & |c_U|                                                  & \leq2^{|U|}.
    \label{eq:alvv_cluster_union_coefficient}
\end{align}
The coefficient bound holds because distinct length-$r$ windows have
distinct left endpoints. At most $|U|$ windows are contained in $U$,
so at most $2^{|U|}$ families can contribute to $c_U$.

Every component of $U\in\mathcal U_r$ is an interval of at least $r$
labels. Its component family determines $U$, and its components are
disjoint. Let $\mathcal I_r$ denote all nonempty intervals of at least
$r$ labels. An interval is determined by its endpoints, so
$|\mathcal I_r|\leq N^2$. Consequently
\begin{align}
    \sum_{U\in\mathcal U_r}b^{|U|}
     & \leq\sum_{\varnothing\ne F\subseteq\mathcal I_r}
    \prod_{C\in F}b^{|C|}
    =\prod_{C\in\mathcal I_r}(1+b^{|C|})-1                  \\
     & \leq\exp\Bigl(\sum_{C\in\mathcal I_r}b^{|C|}\Bigr)-1
    \leq\exp(N^2b^r)-1.
    \label{eq:alvv_cluster_interval_weight}
\end{align}
The triangle inequality in (\ref{eq:alvv_cluster_mandatory_union}), the
mandatory-label estimate, and (\ref{eq:alvv_cluster_union_coefficient})
now imply (\ref{eq:alvv_cluster_normalized_correction}). Empty chains
and cutoffs longer than the label chain have no discarded support.

The normalization by a lower energy, the interaction-label cutoff and
the factor two are explicit changes from the printed assertion. Thus
(\ref{eq:alvv_cluster_normalized_correction}) does not establish
(\ref{eq:alvv_cluster_printed}). It also supplies no bond-dimension
bound or exact matrix product operator factorization.


\section{The soft interval at compatible heat times}

The corrected label estimate also yields an actual matrix product
operator approximation for one soft-truncated interval. This is a
restricted corrected version of the soft-truncation MPO lemma in ALVV, source lines 1262--1268. It does not replace
the false printed physical-support assertion.

For $t>0$, the defining finite sum has the exact heat expansion
\begin{align*}
    h_{m,t}(x)        & =\sum_{a=0}^m c_a e^{-ax/t},             &
    c_a               & =t(-1)^a\sum_{j=1}^m\frac{\binom ja}{j}, &
    \sum_{a=0}^m|c_a| & \leq t2^{m+1}.
\end{align*}
The constant term is included. The scale factor $t$ is retained in the
coefficient budget.
Let $A=H_J$, and let $e$ be its actual minimum eigenvalue, with
$e-10\leq\epsilon\leq e$. The positive matrix to be truncated is
$U=A-\epsilon I$. For each $\beta=a/t$, set
$Q_a=e^{\beta\epsilon}M_\ell^{\mathrm{lab}}(A;\beta)$.
The normalized estimate at $e$ gives
\begin{align*}
    \|e^{-\beta U}-Q_a\|
     & \leq e^{-\beta(e-\epsilon)}
    (e^{N^2[2(e^\beta-1)]^\ell}-1)        \\
     & \leq e^{N^2[2(e^\beta-1)]^\ell}-1.
\end{align*}
Thus the large scalar in the shifted exponential cancels the
normalization. It is not distributed among the original positive local
terms.

Choose $t=3m$ and $m\geq1$. Then
$b=2(e^{1/3}-1)\in(0,1)$ and every required heat time is at most $1/3$.
Writing $W=3m2^{m+1}$, choose the explicit order
\begin{align*}
    \ell=\max\left\{1,\left\lceil
    \frac{\log\max\{1,N^2\}-\log\log(1+\xi/W)}{-\log b}
    \right\rceil\right\}.
\end{align*}
Then $W(e^{N^2b^\ell}-1)\leq\xi$. Each heat operator is approximated
directly by its actual cluster tensors. Diagonal blocks combine these
heat tensors with dimension $(m+1)\ell^2q^\ell$; an approximate heat MPO
is never raised to the power $m$. The exterior local sum and scalar
identity have an exact representation of dimension $1+Nq^2$.
Consequently the full actual replacement
\begin{align*}
    \widetilde H=H-A+\epsilon I+h_{m,3m}(A-\epsilon I)
\end{align*}
has actual site-dependent operator tensors $T$ with
\begin{align*}
    \|\widetilde H-T\|\leq\xi,\qquad
    D\leq1+Nq^2+(m+1)\ell^2q^\ell.
\end{align*}
For fixed local dimension and soft order $m$, this dimension bound is
polynomial in the chain length and the inverse accuracy $\xi^{-1}$. The formal result constructs the tensors and the
norm estimate; it does not establish a computational running time or the
full multi-region algorithm.

The same scale $t=3m$ also permits the corrected physical residual/window
comparisons: the exact scaling identity gives a remainder bounded by
$16e^{x/8-t/16}$ when $0<t\leq4m$, using the already constructed
physical exponential moment of $U/8$. Thus the approximation and both
directed comparisons can be obtained for one literal physical soft
Hamiltonian. This does not prove the false printed uniform energy
estimate or its claimed gap prescription.
