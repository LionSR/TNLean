\section{The physical theorem proved here}
Let $h_i$ be Hermitian local terms with supports $X_i$ of bounded diameter
and cardinality. Choose $a_i\in X_i$, and assume
$\sum_{i:x\in X_i}\|h_i\|\le b_0$. For a finite graph $G$ assume explicitly
\[
 \#\{y:d_G(x,y)=r\}\le K_g(r+1)^{k_g}
\]
with constants independent of system size. Suppose $H=\sum_i h_i$ has a
nonzero ground eigenspace of dimension $n$ and a positive gap.
The actual commutator estimate gives a rate $\gamma>0$ and constant $C$
depending on the displayed local, gap and geometric parameters. There is
an actual orthonormal ground-space basis with
\[
 \sum_i\|\operatorname{offdiag}(Ph_iP)\|
 \le (n-1)^2 A_n(C)|b_0||K_g|S(\gamma/2^n,k_g),
\]
where $A_n(C)=\sqrt{n^3C(1+2n^3)^n}$ and $S$ is the stated exponential
shell-sum bound. The same bound holds for terms filtered under $H$, since
their ground-space compressions are exactly $Ph_iP$.

The constant is uniform in the numbers of sites and terms when $n$ and
the local, gap and growth parameters are fixed. It is not uniform in $n$.
The earlier estimate containing an inverse quotient commutator-map norm is
a different, system-dependent statement; no bound on it is used here.

\section{Scope of the source comparison}
The source metric and finite-range assumptions at lines 169--190 do not
imply polynomial sphere growth. The theorem above is thus a restriction,
not the unrestricted printed equation \texttt{oibu}. See
\path{hastings05_geometric_growth_scope.tex}.
Choosing $K_g$ separately as the number of sites does not prove uniformity.

This theorem concerns filtering under the full Hamiltonian. The source's
actual $M_i$ are filtered under locally truncated generators. Their
compressed-commutator estimate is a separate subsequent statement.
The complete mathematical construction is recorded in
\path{hastings05_tree_basis_counterexample.tex}.
That tree counterexample must also be distinguished
from its partial Lean certificates; no full counterexample theorem is
asserted here.
