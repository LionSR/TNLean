Schuch, Cirac, and P\'erez-Garc\'ia,
\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/1001.3807v3}{arXiv:1001.3807v3}~\cite{Schuch2010PEPS},
Lemma 6.14, source
\path{Papers/1001.3807/paper_v3.tex}, lines 2199--2214,
compare the local physics of a flux string with the vacuum away from its
ends. Arbitrary endpoint-free annular observations cannot satisfy such a
vacuum-density claim: a Wilson loop can detect an enclosed endpoint.
Restricted homotopy also retains nonabelian information beyond winding.

The theorem
\leanid{TNLean.PEPS.IsGIsometric.torusAvoidingFlux_normalized_density_eq_vacuum}
derives the actual vacuum normalized density for an avoiding native flux
path from regular group-isometry, connectedness of the physical observation
graph and simple connectedness of its actual closed-cell realization.
The group is finite and its insertion element is arbitrary. Avoidance
means that every visited dual plaquette has all four physical corners
outside the observation region. This supplies incident identity, while
the actual exterior collar supplies an untwisted complementary component
containing every boundary endpoint. The noncentral contraction theorem
then applies, and the original native and ordered-edge coefficient formulas
agree entry by entry. Both actual cuts are proved nonzero.

The main construction
\leanid{TNLean.PEPS.IsGIsometric.torusFlux_normalized_density_eq_vacuum_of_isSimplyConnected}
starts from any actual finite native dual path. It additionally assumes
simple connectedness of the closed-cell realization of the computed
four-corner forbidden dual set and exclusion of both endpoints from that
set. These assumptions construct an avoiding detour with the same lifted
displacement. Full-support generated homotopy and virtual invariance give
equality of the actual coefficient cuts, so the detour's normalized density
and nonvanishing conclusions transfer to the original path. No avoiding
path, homotopy, component, root, gauge, factorization, Gram identity,
desired density equality or nonzero-cut witness is supplied.

The simple connectedness assumptions on the physical region and on its
computed forbidden dual set are separate and explicit. Neither is inferred
from endpoint exclusion, and no endpoint-only classification of restricted
homotopy is asserted. Both periods are at least three; arbitrary winding,
nonabelian finite groups and noncentral insertions are covered under these
conditions. Equality concerns normalized observed density; noncentral
insertions can change the unnormalized scalar. The theorem does not equate
global winding states with vacuum or cover arbitrary endpoint-free annuli.
The broader source wording remains under \ghissue{8703}, and the
full-lattice Hamiltonian scope of \ghissue{8947} is unchanged.
