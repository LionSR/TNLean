\section{What the source prints}
Section~3.2 of \cite{Bultinck2017Anyons} writes the projector matrix product
operator on a ring of $L$ sites as $P_L=\sum_a w_aO_a^L$, where
$O_a^L=\sum_{i,j}\tr(B_a^{i_1j_1}\cdots B_a^{i_Lj_L})\ket{i_1\cdots i_L}\bra{j_1\cdots j_L}$,
and requires
\begin{align}
    P_L^2=\sum_{a,b}w_aw_bO_a^LO_b^L=\sum_aw_aO_a^L=P_L,
    \label{eq:fib_proj}
\end{align}
which ``has to hold for all $L$''.\footnote{Source traceability:
    \path{References/1511.08090/AnyonsPEPS.tex}, lines 143--156.}
No lower bound on $L$ is stated. For the Fibonacci theory, Appendix~D.1.1
gives two blocks $B_1,B_\tau$ of bond dimensions $2$ and $3$ with weights
$w_1=1/(1+\phi^2)$ and $w_\tau=\phi/(1+\phi^2)$, where
$\phi=(1+\sqrt5)/2$.\footnote{Source traceability:
    \path{References/1511.08090/AnyonsPEPS.tex}, lines 1257--1269.}

\section{The empty chain}
At $L=0$ the Hilbert space of the chain is one-dimensional and each $O_a^0$ is
the trace of the empty product, the bond dimension $\chi_a$ of the block:
$O_1^0=2$ and $O_\tau^0=3$. Using $\phi^2=\phi+1$,
\begin{align}
    P_0
     & =2w_1+3w_\tau
    =\frac{2+3\phi}{1+\phi^2}
    =\frac{2+3\phi}{2+\phi}\approx1.894,
    \label{eq:fib_empty}
\end{align}
which is neither $0$ nor $1$, so $P_0^2\neq P_0$. The source derives from
(\ref{eq:fib_proj}), required for all $L$, the fusion rules
$O_a^LO_b^L=\sum_cN_{ab}^cO_c^L$.\footnote{Source traceability:
    \path{References/1511.08090/AnyonsPEPS.tex}, lines 157--160.}
These also fail at $L=0$: the fusion $\tau\otimes\tau=1\oplus\tau$ would give
$O_\tau^0O_\tau^0=9$ against $O_1^0+O_\tau^0=5$, the unit law $1\otimes1=1$ would
give $O_1^0O_1^0=4$ against $O_1^0=2$, and the unit laws $1\otimes\tau=\tau\otimes1=\tau$
would give $O_1^0O_\tau^0=6$ against $O_\tau^0=3$. The identity
(\ref{eq:fib_proj}) is a statement about rings with at least one site, and the
empty chain carries no physical content.

\section{The reading adopted}
The empty chain is read as a degenerate case the source does not intend.
Idempotence, the fusion rule $O_\tau^LO_\tau^L=O_1^L+O_\tau^L$, and the unit laws
$O_1^LO_1^L=O_1^L$, $O_1^LO_\tau^L=O_\tau^LO_1^L=O_\tau^L$ are stated under the
added hypothesis $L\geq1$.\footnote{Results:
    \leanid{FibonacciCompression.fibProjector_mul_self},
    \leanid{FibonacciCompression.fibonacci_fusion_rule},
    \leanid{FibonacciCompression.fibOne_mul_fibOne},
    \leanid{FibonacciCompression.fibOne_mul_fibTau},
    \leanid{FibonacciCompression.fibTau_mul_fibOne}.}
This hypothesis is the only restriction recorded here: at every $L\geq1$ the
source's identity (\ref{eq:fib_proj}), with the source's weights, holds for the
blocks used in the formalization. Those blocks carry the bare F-symbols and are
not identified with the source's diagram tensor; that separate deviation is
recorded in~\cite{gap:bmwshv17_fibonacci_block_entries_provenance}.
