\section{The printed clause}

Proposition~\texttt{corconj} of Ref.~\cite{Cirac2017MPU} chooses the
standard-form unitaries $u',v'$ of a conjugation-invariant matrix product
unitary. In Case~II they satisfy
\begin{align}
    \overline{u'} & =(\Sigma_\ell\otimes\Sigma_r)u', &
    \overline{v'} & =v'(\Sigma_r\otimes\Sigma_\ell),
    \label{eq:sympuv}
\end{align}
with $\Sigma_x=\mathbb 1_{x/2}\otimes Y$ and
$Y=\left(\begin{smallmatrix}0&1\\-1&0\end{smallmatrix}\right)$.
The uniqueness clause \cite[lines~1121--1124]{Cirac2017MPU} states that if
$u'',v''$ also satisfy \eqref{eq:sympuv} and generate the same matrix product
unitary, then there are $S_\ell,S_r$ with $S_x^T\Sigma_xS_x=\Sigma_x$ such that
\begin{align}
    u'' & =(S_\ell\otimes S_r)u', &
    v'' & =v'(S_r^T\otimes S_\ell^T).
    \label{eq:printed}
\end{align}
The proof \cite[lines~1151--1163]{Cirac2017MPU} starts from the Fundamental
Theorem, which gives unitaries $a,b$ with $u''=(a\otimes b)u'$ and
$v''=v'(b^\dagger\otimes a^\dagger)$, and then rephases $a,b$ to symplectic
matrices.

\section{The correction}

The rephasing gives $S_\ell=wa$ and $S_r=w^{-1}b$ for a phase $w$, so
$S_\ell\otimes S_r=a\otimes b$ and
$S_r^\dagger\otimes S_\ell^\dagger=b^\dagger\otimes a^\dagger$. The relation
for $v''$ that the proof establishes is therefore
\begin{align}
    v'' & =v'(S_r^\dagger\otimes S_\ell^\dagger).
    \label{eq:corrected}
\end{align}
For a real orthogonal matrix the transpose and the adjoint agree, so the Case~I
clause $v''=v'(O_r^T\otimes O_\ell^T)$ is correct as printed. A unitary
symplectic matrix need not be real, and then \eqref{eq:printed} fails.

\paragraph{Example.} Take $\ell=r=2$, let $u',v'$ satisfy \eqref{eq:sympuv},
and let $S_\ell=\operatorname{diag}(i,-i)$, $S_r=\mathbb 1_2$. Since
$\det S_\ell=1$, one has $S_\ell^TYS_\ell=Y$, and since
$\overline{S_\ell}\,Y=YS_\ell$ the pair
$u''=(S_\ell\otimes\mathbb 1)u'$, $v''=v'(\mathbb 1\otimes S_\ell^\dagger)$
again satisfies \eqref{eq:sympuv} and generates the same matrix product
unitary. The gate $v'$ is unitary, so the printed formula would force
$\mathbb 1\otimes S_\ell^\dagger=\mathbb 1\otimes S_\ell^T$, that is,
$\operatorname{diag}(-i,i)=\operatorname{diag}(i,-i)$.

\section{Formal statement}

The Case~II uniqueness theorem
\leanid{Matrix.exists_symplectic_factors_of_kronecker_mul} states the corrected
relation $b^\dagger\otimes a^\dagger=S_r^\dagger\otimes S_\ell^\dagger$, and
the matrix product unitary statement
\leanid{MPOTensor.IsMPUCanonicalFormII.exists_symplectic_gauge_of_mpo_eq}
states \eqref{eq:corrected}. The hypotheses are those of the source; only the
transposes in the conclusion are replaced by adjoints.
