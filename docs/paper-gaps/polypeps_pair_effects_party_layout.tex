\section{The source lemma}

Lemma~5.1 of the September~24, 2026 polynomial-PEPS
manuscript~\cite{OpenAI2026PolynomialPEPS} (\path{04-compression.tex},
lines~53--71) eliminates two-party postselection effects (pair effects) from a
distributed gate. Let $G=\sum_\xi c_\xi M_\xi$ be a contraction on a
multipartite memory expanded as a linear combination of $K$ allowed monomials
$M_\xi$ with complex coefficients $c_\xi$, where each $M_\xi$ contains at most
$r$ pair effects. An allowed monomial (lines~32--35) is a composition of
single-party contractions, preparations of normalized two-party states (pair
sources), and contractions against normalized two-party bras (pair effects).
For every integer $m\ge1$, Lemma~5.1 constructs a gate $\widetilde G_m$ and a
normalized auxiliary state $\Gamma_m$ such that
\begin{align}
  \bigl\|\widetilde G_m-G\otimes\ket{\Gamma_m}\bigr\|
  &\le\frac{r}{\sqrt m}\sum_\xi|c_\xi|,
  \label{eq:polypeps_pair_effects_party_layout_error}
\end{align}
where $\widetilde G_m$ satisfies four properties: (i) $\widetilde G_m$ expands
into monomials built solely from single-party contractions and normalized pair
sources; (ii) the expansion has at most $Km^r$ monomials whose coefficients
have absolute sum at most $\sum_\xi|c_\xi|$; (iii) all auxiliary registers in
$\Gamma_m$ belong to the parties participating in $G$; and (iv) all pair
sources shared by a given pair of parties in a monomial can be merged into a
single normalized pair source.

\section{What is proved}

An initial formalization modeled each monomial without party labels as a chain
$F_n\circ E_n\circ\cdots\circ E_1\circ F_0$ of arbitrary contractions $F_i$ and
distinguished pair effects $E_i=\bra{\eta_i}\otimes\mathbf{1}$, proving the
operator-norm bound, the normalization of $\Gamma_m$, the monomial count
$Km^r$, and the preservation of $\sum_\xi|c_\xi|$ in that setting.\footnote{The
party-free operator theorem is
\leanid{TNLean.PEPS.PairEffect.pairEffectElimination}; the rescaling at a fixed
stack length is
\leanid{TNLean.PEPS.PairEffect.replaceGate_rescaled_stackLength}.} Because a
party-free model cannot distinguish single-party contractions from two-party
effects, clauses (i) and (iii) could not be expressed there.

The full party-indexed version is now proved. Each register carries an owner
party and a finite-dimensional Hilbert space, and the total memory is the
tensor product of the registers. A source-only word is a composition of
single-party contractions, normalized pair-source preparations on fresh
registers of two distinct parties, and tensor-factor permutations. An allowed
monomial is a chain of source-only words separated by normalized pair effects
between distinct parties $p\ne q$. Up to local unitary identifications
$\mathcal U\cong\mathbb C^\alpha$ and $\mathcal V\cong\mathbb C^\beta$ and a
factor permutation absorbed into the preceding source-only word, each pair
effect acts on the leading pair of registers $\mathbb C^\alpha$ (at $p$) and
$\mathbb C^\beta$ (at $q$).

Replacing each pair effect on $(p,q)$ by a stack of $m$ bipartite registers
(with the $\mathbb C^\alpha$ halves owned by $p$ and the $\mathbb C^\beta$
halves owned by $q$) and routing the input to slot $k\in\{1,\ldots,m\}$ while
preparing $m-1$ copies of the pair source $\eta$ in the remaining slots yields
a source-only word that realizes the insertion operator $I_k$ on
$(\mathbb C^{\alpha\times\beta})^{\otimes m}$. This construction establishes:
\begin{itemize}
\item every term in the expansion of $\widetilde G_m$ is the operator of a
  source-only word with normalized pair sources and single-party contractions
  (clause (i));
\item the output register list consists of the $m$-register stacks of all pair
  effects (each alternating between the two endpoint parties $p$ and $q$)
  followed by the output registers of $G$ (clause (iii));
\item the auxiliary state $\Gamma_m$ is prepared by normalized pair sources
  between the endpoint parties of the effects;
\item preparing two adjacent pair sources on the same pair of parties and
  merging each party's two registers produces a single normalized pair source
  whose norm is the product of the two norms.
\end{itemize}
Next, for any source-only word $F$ with finite list $S$ of pair-source
occurrences, induction on $F$ commutes all pair-source preparations $P_S$ to
the beginning of the word, giving $F=B\circ P_S$ for a source-free word $B$ of
single-party contractions and factor permutations. A second induction merges all
pair sources in $S$ that share the same unordered pair of parties (aligning
opposite endpoint orders by a tensor swap and regrouping locally) into a list
$G$ with at most one normalized pair source per unordered party pair, so that
$P_S=E\circ P_G$ for a source-free word $E$ and hence $F=(B\circ E)\circ P_G$
(clause (iv)).\footnote{Clauses (i)--(iii) and the error bound are proved in
\leanid{TNLean.PEPS.PairEffect.partyPairEffectElimination}, with adjacent-source
merging in \leanid{TNLean.PEPS.PairEffect.eval_combineSources}. The general
source-commutation and grouping theorems are
\leanid{TNLean.PEPS.PairEffect.Word.eval_eq_localPart_comp_prepare},
\leanid{TNLean.PEPS.PairEffect.SourceInventory.exists_grouped}, and
\leanid{TNLean.PEPS.PairEffect.Word.exists_grouped_source_preparation}, which
combine to give the complete Lemma~5.1 statement in
\leanid{TNLean.PEPS.PairEffect.partyPairEffectElimination_with_grouped_sources}.}

\section{Effect on the paper and what remains}

The earlier restriction to a party-free model is resolved. Lemma~5.1 is proved
with all four clauses (i)--(iv) and no extra hypotheses; in fact, the proof
does not even require $G$ itself to be a contraction. Beyond Lemma~5.1, the
consolidation of the source-free part $B\circ E$ into a tensor product of one
contraction per party, together with padding by one-dimensional pair sources on
unused party pairs of a fixed gate party set, is also proved.\footnote{The
party-wise consolidation theorems are
\leanid{TNLean.PEPS.PairEffect.Word.exists_tensorPartyMaps_parties},
\leanid{TNLean.PEPS.PairEffect.Word.exists_prepared_tensorPartyMaps}, and
\leanid{TNLean.PEPS.PairEffect.Word.exists_complete_prepared_tensorPartyMaps}.}
Nothing in Lemma~5.1 remains open; the later steps of Theorem~5.2 (matching
source spaces across different monomials in \path{04-compression.tex},
lines~253--267, followed by importance sampling) are separate results.
