\section{The source claim}

Section~III.A of Ref.~\cite{Cirac2021Matrix} defines a symmetry of a
translation-invariant matrix product state up to a global phase: a normal
tensor $A$ is symmetric under a physical operation when the fundamental
theorem gives a gauge $X$ and a phase $\phi$ relating the transformed tensor to
$e^{i\phi}X^{\dagger}A^iX$.\footnote{Source location:
\path{Papers/2011.12127/TN-Review-main.tex}, lines 1080--1090, and line 1116
for time reversal.} For the Wigner time reversal
$\ket{\psi}\mapsto\sigma_y^{\otimes N}\ket{\bar\psi}$ on spin
$\tfrac12$, applying the relation twice gives $A^i=-A^i$ up to gauge, which
contradicts the uniqueness of gauges and phases for normal tensors. The source
concludes that the ground state of a system with this symmetry is not a normal
or injective matrix product state.\footnote{Source location:
\path{Papers/2011.12127/TN-Review-main.tex}, line 1117, and line 1218 for the
comparison with Kramers' theorem.} The hypothesis is therefore that, for
every chain length $N$, the time-reversed vector is
$c_N\ket{\psi_N(A)}$ for a phase $c_N$ with $|c_N|=1$.

\section{The formal statement}

The gauge-level obstruction is formalized with the source's hypotheses: no
normal tensor of positive bond dimension admits $X\in\mathrm{GL}_D(\mathbb C)$
and $\zeta\in\mathbb C$ with $\sum_j(\sigma_y)_{ij}\bar A^j=\zeta X^{-1}A^iX$
for all $i$.\footnote{Formal declaration:
\leanid{MPSTensor.not_wignerTimeReversal_gauge}.} The state-level corollary for
injective tensors assumes instead that the matrix product vectors of $A$ and
of its time-reversed tensor are equal at every length, that is, $c_N=1$ for
all $N$.\footnote{Formal declaration:
\leanid{MPSTensor.not_sameMPV_wignerTimeReversal_of_isInjective}.} It
therefore excludes only exact invariance, while the source excludes invariance
up to a phase.

\section{Elimination plan}

State the corollary for phase-proportional families: for each $N$ a scalar
$c_N$ with $\ket{\psi_N(\tilde A)}=c_N\ket{\psi_N(A)}$, where
$\tilde A^i=\sum_j(\sigma_y)_{ij}\bar A^j$. The proportional form of the
fundamental theorem for injective tensors turns this into a gauge relation
$\tilde A^i=\zeta X^{-1}A^iX$ with $\zeta\neq0$, and the gauge-level
obstruction concludes. The exact-invariance corollary is then the special
case $c_N=1$. The same pattern already gives the phase version of the
spin-$\tfrac12$ $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ obstruction.\footnote{Formal declaration:
\leanid{MPSTensor.not_forall_mpv_twistedTensor_eq_smul_specialUnitaryGroup_of_isNormal}.
The plan is recorded on
\href{https://github.com/LionSR/TNLean/pull/8284}{pull request \#8284}.}

\textbf{Verdict: open scope restriction.} The state-level Kramers obstruction
is formalized for exact invariance of the matrix product vectors; invariance
up to a phase at every length, as in the source, remains to be formalized.
