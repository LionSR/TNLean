\section{Original physical energy at total weight four}
The rough comparison in Ref.~\cite{OpenAI2026AreaLaw} has total band
weight $W=4$, $K\geq8$ bands, $a=4/K$ and inverse exponent $s=1/4$.
The scanner has positive parameters $m,M$; its actual fill and charge
roots use metric parameter $a/2=2/K$.
For the same supplied unit truncated vector $\widetilde\Omega$ and
actual truncated Hamiltonian $\widetilde H$, retain the physical gap
\begin{align}
    \widetilde H-e_0 I
      &\succeq\gamma\bigl(I-|\widetilde\Omega\rangle
                               \langle\widetilde\Omega|\bigr),
    &\gamma&>0.
    \label{eq:rough_lower_scope_gap}
\end{align}
The actual mean energy in its normalized filtered vector is $\mu_k$,
and its defect is $\delta_k=(\mu_k-e_0)/\gamma\geq0$.
Neither $e_0=0$ nor an auxiliary one-vector Hamiltonian is substituted.
The actual root compression and energy-coordinate equalities identify
this vector with the same inverse-quarter-power vector used in the
rough comparison.

\section{The selected spectral data and supplied labels}
The selected set $E$ is a spectral index set.
Its normalized Schmidt auxiliary vector is $\psi$, and the entropy
parameter is
$S'=S(\operatorname{Tr}_{\rm left}|\psi\rangle\langle\psi|)$.
It is not the entropy of a physical region called $E$.
The comparison allows arbitrary real $S'$, so it consumes this exact
entropy and the same supplied label sequence satisfying
$\log\dim\ell_k-kS'=o(k)$.
The literal physical replica prevector retains
$\widetilde\Omega$ and $\ell_k$; its simultaneous symmetry and both
auxiliary label equations are derived from that vector.
With $\varepsilon_k=2|\log\dim\ell_k-kS'|$, set
\begin{align}
    r_k&=\log(k+1)+c\log(k+2)+a\varepsilon_k,\nonumber\\
    R_k&=\frac{K}{2k}
      \left(2|r_k|+C_f\log(k+2)+2\log2+\log(3/2)\right),
    &R_k&\longrightarrow0.
    \label{eq:rough_lower_scope_remainder}
\end{align}
For every actual fill and charge interpolation and every
$0<\tau\leq1/2$, its actual squared filtered norm $\mathcal N_k$ is
positive, its filtered vector is unit, and
\begin{align}
    4S'-6\left(\tau\log d+\frac{h(\tau)}a\right)
      -3\frac{\delta_k}{\tau}\max\{0,2S'\}-R_k
      &\leq-\frac1k\log\mathcal N_k.
    \label{eq:rough_lower_scope_rate}
\end{align}
No marginal-moment estimate, sharp smallness condition, or small-energy
premise is used.
The defect is not assumed bounded by $\tau$.
Both logarithms of two in (\ref{eq:rough_lower_scope_remainder}) are
retained.

\section{Order of limits and remaining composition}
For a fixed finite site space, dimensions and band count, $c,C_f$ are
chosen before the scanner, horizon, physical vectors, labels, copy
number and interpolation parameter.
For each fixed physical instance, the replica threshold associated
with any positive error allowance precedes the copy number, actual
round and interpolation parameter.
Thus the remainder in (\ref{eq:rough_lower_scope_rate}) is uniform at
any already selected interpolation.
The source-scale order remains: fix the physical size, take the
replica limit, and only then enlarge the physical-size threshold to
absorb the coefficient loss between four and two.

The physical selected spectral set, its normalized auxiliary vector,
prevector existence, label asymptotic, initial and final norm budgets,
actual point selection and selected-point energy estimate must be
supplied by the surrounding argument.
The rough lower comparison itself does not reselect any of them.
Combining both comparisons at that same selected point, controlling
$S'$ and $\log d$ by the original target entropy, and the final
source-scale bootstrap absorption remain outside this result.
No full scanner theorem, Proposition~9.6 or area law is asserted.
