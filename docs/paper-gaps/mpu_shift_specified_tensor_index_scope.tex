\section{Source statement}

For a simple matrix product unitary tensor with positive right and left
source-cut ranks $r$ and $\ell$, Definition~IV.1 of
Ref.~\cite{Cirac2017MPU} uses
\begin{align}
    \frac12(\log_2 r-\log_2\ell).
    \label{eq:mpu_shift_specified_tensor_index_scope_expression}
\end{align}
Proposition~IV.2 proves that this value is independent of the chosen simple
blocking. The resulting blocking-independent quantity is the public MPU
index.
% Source: paper_v2.tex lines 681--704, labels def:index and
% index-well-defined.

For the topological-insulator examples, the paper states that the right shift
$T^{(N)}$ has index $\log_2 d$, its adjoint left shift $T^{(N)\dagger}$ has
index $-\log_2 d$, and the three displayed families
\begin{align}
    U_1^{(N)} & = (\Id\otimes\Id)^{\otimes N},   \\
    U_2^{(N)} & = T^{(N)\dagger}\otimes T^{(N)}, \\
    U_3^{(N)} & = T^{(N)}\otimes T^{(N)\dagger}
    \label{eq:mpu_shift_specified_tensor_index_scope_families}
\end{align}
have index zero.
% Source: paper_v2.tex lines 1980--2001 and 2037--2041, label threeMPU.

\section{Resolution}

Let $E$ be the transfer matrix of a normal tensor with bond dimension $D$,
and let $(\Phi|$ and $|\rho)$ be its left and right eigenvectors for the
eigenvalue one, regarded as $D\times D$ matrices. Following
Ref.~\cite{Cirac2017MPU}, the tensor is in canonical form II when
$(\Phi|=\sum_{n=1}^D(n,n|$ and $|\rho)=\sum_{n=1}^D\rho_n|n,n)$ with every
$\rho_n>0$ and $(\Phi|\rho)=1$; that is, $\Phi$ is the identity matrix and
$\rho$ is a positive diagonal matrix of unit trace. A canonical-form-II
representative of an MPU tensor is a tensor in this form generating the same
periodic operators.
% Source: paper_v2.tex lines 269--281, label Erightleft.

The source-rank calculations are now identified with the public,
blocking-independent index. The normalized right-shift transfer map is
$X\mapsto\operatorname{tr}(X)I/d$, so the positive diagonal matrix $I/d$
supplies a canonical-form-II representative. The existing source-index
calculation then gives $\operatorname{ind}(T)=\log_2 d$.
Physical adjunction negates the index and tensor products add indices.
Thus $\operatorname{ind}(T^\dagger)=-\log_2 d$ and all three displayed
families have index zero.

periodic operators. No equality of the raw source ranks of arbitrary
unreduced presentations is required or asserted.

\section{Scope}

The original numerical gap is resolved. The older specified-tensor
calculations remain useful intermediate results, but the source's public
index claim no longer depends on treating them as the public index itself.
The separate assertion that shifts cannot be approximated by short-range
time evolution~\cite{Cirac2021Matrix} is not a consequence established by
these numerical theorems.
