\section{The assertion}

The review describes the Majumdar--Ghosh state as the one-dimensional
resonating-valence-bond state: the superposition of the two nearest-neighbour
singlet coverings of a periodic spin-$\tfrac12$ chain
\cite[lines~2397--2405]{Cirac2021Matrix}. It then gives the tensor in the
shorthand
\begin{align}
    A
        &=
        \left[\ket{0}((02|+(20|)
            +
            \ket{1}((12|+(21|)\right]\otimes Y,
    \label{eq:rmp_majumdar_ghosh_tensor_gap_review_tensor}\\
    Y
        &=
        \begin{pmatrix}
            0 & -1\\
            1 & 0
        \end{pmatrix}.
    \label{eq:rmp_majumdar_ghosh_tensor_gap_singlet_matrix}
\end{align}
Here $Y$ is the singlet tensor introduced earlier in the examples appendix of
Ref.~\cite[lines~2397--2405]{Cirac2021Matrix}.

The formal example instead defines a tensor of bond dimension three with
matrices
\begin{align}
    A^0
        &=
        \begin{pmatrix}
            0 & 1 & 0\\
            0 & 0 & 0\\
            1/\sqrt{2} & 0 & 0
        \end{pmatrix},
    \label{eq:rmp_majumdar_ghosh_tensor_gap_formal_matrix_zero}\\
    A^1
        &=
        \begin{pmatrix}
            0 & 0 & 1\\
            -1/\sqrt{2} & 0 & 0\\
            0 & 0 & 0
        \end{pmatrix}.
    \label{eq:rmp_majumdar_ghosh_tensor_gap_formal_matrix_one}
\end{align}
The formalized properties of this tensor are its left-canonical equation, its
transfer map on the identity, non-injectivity, and
non-normality.\footnote{Formal declarations:

\section{The point at issue}

The review formula
(\ref{eq:rmp_majumdar_ghosh_tensor_gap_review_tensor}) is not literally a
bond-dimension-three tensor. Suppose that $(ab|$ denotes the matrix unit
$\ketbra{a}{b}$ on a three-dimensional auxiliary space. A literal reading of
the tensor product with $Y$ then gives the bond space
\begin{align}
    \mathcal V_{\mathrm{literal}}
        &=
        \C^3\otimes\C^2,
    \label{eq:rmp_majumdar_ghosh_tensor_gap_literal_bond_space}\\
    \dim\mathcal V_{\mathrm{literal}}
        &=
        6.
    \label{eq:rmp_majumdar_ghosh_tensor_gap_literal_bond_dimension}
\end{align}

Let $E_{ab}=\ketbra{a}{b}$ denote a matrix unit on $\C^3$, and define
\begin{align}
    B^0
        &=
        E_{02}+E_{20},
    \label{eq:rmp_majumdar_ghosh_tensor_gap_auxiliary_matrix_zero}\\
    B^1
        &=
        E_{12}+E_{21}.
    \label{eq:rmp_majumdar_ghosh_tensor_gap_auxiliary_matrix_one}
\end{align}
Under the literal reading, the physical matrices would be
\begin{align}
    \widetilde A^i
        &=
        B^i\otimes Y.
    \label{eq:rmp_majumdar_ghosh_tensor_gap_literal_tensor_matrices}
\end{align}
Their two-site periodic contractions factor as
\begin{align}
    \tr\left(\widetilde A^i\widetilde A^j\right)
        &=
        \tr\left((B^i\otimes Y)(B^j\otimes Y)\right)
    \label{eq:rmp_majumdar_ghosh_tensor_gap_contraction_tensor_product}\\
        &=
        \tr(B^iB^j)\tr(Y^2).
    \label{eq:rmp_majumdar_ghosh_tensor_gap_contraction_factorization}
\end{align}
The singlet matrix satisfies
\begin{align}
    Y^2
        &=
        -I_2.
    \label{eq:rmp_majumdar_ghosh_tensor_gap_singlet_square}
\end{align}
The auxiliary factors satisfy
\begin{align}
    \tr\left((B^0)^2\right)
        &=
        2,
    \label{eq:rmp_majumdar_ghosh_tensor_gap_same_letter_zero_trace}\\
    \tr\left((B^1)^2\right)
        &=
        2,
    \label{eq:rmp_majumdar_ghosh_tensor_gap_same_letter_one_trace}\\
    \tr(B^0B^1)
        &=
        0,
    \label{eq:rmp_majumdar_ghosh_tensor_gap_opposite_trace_zero_one}\\
    \tr(B^1B^0)
        &=
        0.
    \label{eq:rmp_majumdar_ghosh_tensor_gap_opposite_trace_one_zero}
\end{align}
Equations
(\ref{eq:rmp_majumdar_ghosh_tensor_gap_contraction_factorization})--%
(\ref{eq:rmp_majumdar_ghosh_tensor_gap_opposite_trace_one_zero}) show that the
literal tensor has nonzero two-site amplitudes on equal-letter words and zero
amplitudes on opposite-letter words. A two-site singlet has the opposite
support pattern: its nonzero coefficients occur on $01$ and $10$, with opposite
signs.

The review expression must therefore be read as valence-bond shorthand rather
than as a literal $6\times6$ MPS representation. The matrices
(\ref{eq:rmp_majumdar_ghosh_tensor_gap_formal_matrix_zero}) and
(\ref{eq:rmp_majumdar_ghosh_tensor_gap_formal_matrix_one}) are the contracted
bond-dimension-three representative used in the formal example. Its three bond
states retain the two spin-$\tfrac12$ values of an open singlet partner and the
closed inter-dimer link. The antisymmetry of $Y$ supplies the relative sign in
$A^1$.

\section{Current formal boundary}

The repository does not yet prove that the matrix product vectors of the
bond-dimension-three tensor equal the Majumdar--Ghosh dimer superposition on
even periodic chains. Such a theorem must fix the parity, normalization, and
cyclic convention.

For a physical word $(i_1,\ldots,i_N)$, the matrix product coefficient is
\begin{align}
    \psi_A(i_1,\ldots,i_N)
        &=
        \tr\left(A^{i_1}\cdots A^{i_N}\right).
    \label{eq:rmp_majumdar_ghosh_tensor_gap_mpv_coefficient}
\end{align}
For even $N$, the desired comparison would identify
(\ref{eq:rmp_majumdar_ghosh_tensor_gap_mpv_coefficient}) with the sum of the
products of singlet coefficients associated with the two periodic coverings
\begin{align}
    \mathcal C_{\mathrm{even}}
        &=
        (1,2)(3,4)\cdots(N-1,N),
    \label{eq:rmp_majumdar_ghosh_tensor_gap_even_dimer_covering}\\
    \mathcal C_{\mathrm{odd}}
        &=
        (2,3)(4,5)\cdots(N,1).
    \label{eq:rmp_majumdar_ghosh_tensor_gap_odd_dimer_covering}
\end{align}
No such finite-size vector identity has yet been formalized.

Until that identity is proved, the formal object should be described as the
bond-dimension-three contracted representative used in the algebraic example,
not as a formally established MPS representation of the Majumdar--Ghosh state.
The already proved algebraic properties do not depend on this physical
identification.

\section{Verdict}

This is an open gap in the example's physical identification. The literal
interpretation of the review shorthand produces the wrong two-site singlet
support pattern, while the formal development has not proved that the
contracted $D=3$ tensor generates the Majumdar--Ghosh dimer superposition. The
present formalization therefore establishes only the concrete tensor and its
algebraic properties.
