\section{Status}

\textbf{Historical proof archaeology, not an active gap.}
The corrected proportional fundamental theorem is
\leanid{MPSTensor.fundamentalTheorem_proportional_canonicalForm}. It compares
BNT families whose copy weights are nonzero, following the standing convention
at line~246 of Ref.~\cite{Cirac2016MPDO_arXiv} recorded in
\path{docs/paper-gaps/cpsv16_bnt_uniqueness_zero_coefficient.tex}. The theorem
constructs a bijection between the basis sectors and gives gauge-phase
equivalences for matched sectors. The fixed-block cancellation sentence
therefore records only the history of a superseded proof route. This note
explains why that route was abandoned.

\section{Source passage}

The source passage occurs in the proof of CPSV16
Theorem~\texttt{thm1}, Appendix MPV proof, line~1182 of
Ref.~\cite{Cirac2016MPDO_arXiv}. Let
$\{A_j\}_{j=1}^{r_A}$ and $\{B_k\}_{k=1}^{r_B}$ be the two BNT block
families, and fix $k_0\in\{1,\ldots,r_B\}$. The source obtains
\begin{align}
    \left\langle
        V^{(N)}(B_{k_0}),V^{(N)}(A_j)
    \right\rangle
        &\xrightarrow[N\to\infty]{}0
        \qquad (1\leq j\leq r_A).
    \label{eq:cpsv16_fixed_block_cancellation_fixed_block_overlap_decay}
\end{align}
It treats this decay as contradictory to eventual proportionality of the two
full MPV families.

Under the BNT decomposition, the hypothesis is projective proportionality:
the scalar $c_N$ is nonzero at each length used in the comparison, as recorded
in
\path{docs/paper-gaps/cpsv16_nonzero_proportionality_reading.tex}. Thus
\begin{align}
    \sum_j\mu_j^NV^{(N)}(A_j)
        &= c_N\sum_k\nu_k^NV^{(N)}(B_k),
    \label{eq:cpsv16_fixed_block_cancellation_full_projective_proportionality}\\
    c_N
        &\neq 0.
    \label{eq:cpsv16_fixed_block_cancellation_nonzero_projective_scalar}
\end{align}
Isolating the selected block gives
\begin{align}
    \sum_j\mu_j^NV^{(N)}(A_j)
        -c_N\nu_{k_0}^NV^{(N)}(B_{k_0})
        &= c_N\sum_{k\neq k_0}\nu_k^NV^{(N)}(B_k).
    \label{eq:cpsv16_fixed_block_cancellation_selected_block_isolation}
\end{align}

Corollary~\texttt{Lem1} of Ref.~\cite{Cirac2016MPDO_arXiv} implies from
Eq.~(\ref{eq:cpsv16_fixed_block_cancellation_fixed_block_overlap_decay}) that,
for all sufficiently large $N$,
\begin{align}
    \dim\operatorname{span}
        \left(\left\{V^{(N)}(A_j)\right\}_{j=1}^{r_A}
            \cup
            \left\{V^{(N)}(B_{k_0})\right\}\right)
        &= r_A+1.
    \label{eq:cpsv16_fixed_block_cancellation_fixed_block_span_dimension}
\end{align}
Equivalently, the fixed-block family in
Eq.~(\ref{eq:cpsv16_fixed_block_cancellation_fixed_block_span_dimension}) is
linearly independent.

Line~1182 of Ref.~\cite{Cirac2016MPDO_arXiv} treats this conclusion as a
contradiction to the full proportionality identity
Eq.~(\ref{eq:cpsv16_fixed_block_cancellation_full_projective_proportionality}).
The inference is insufficient because
Eq.~(\ref{eq:cpsv16_fixed_block_cancellation_selected_block_isolation}) also
contains the vectors $V^{(N)}(B_k)$ for $k\neq k_0$. The single-block
linear-independence statement does not control those vectors, and the source
passage does not assert linear independence of the full combined family; see
Appendix MPV proof, line~1182 of Ref.~\cite{Cirac2016MPDO_arXiv}.

\section{Formal boundary}

The formal consequence corresponding to Corollary~\texttt{Lem1} of
Ref.~\cite{Cirac2016MPDO_arXiv} is narrower:
\begin{align}
    \forall^\mathrm{cof}N,\quad
    \dim\operatorname{span}
        \left(\left\{V^{(N)}(A_j)\right\}_{j=1}^{r_A}
            \cup
            \left\{V^{(N)}(B_{k_0})\right\}\right)
        &= r_A+1.
    \label{eq:cpsv16_fixed_block_cancellation_formal_fixed_block_boundary}
\end{align}
The surviving \path{TNLean/MPS/FundamentalTheorem/SectorBNT} API does not formalize the source sentence as
a standalone fixed-block contradiction. The corrected theorem instead uses a
restricted coefficient-comparison argument. It separates the $P$-blocks whose
overlaps with every $Q$-block decay, represents the remaining $P$-blocks by
scalar multiples of matched $Q$-blocks, and applies
\leanid{MPSTensor.restricted_combined_family_eventually_li} to the decaying
subfamily together with all $Q$-blocks. The full-family lemma
\leanid{MPSTensor.IsBNTCanonicalForm.combined_family_eventually_li} is the
special case in which every cross-family overlap decays.

This argument repairs and replaces the insufficient fixed-block inference; it
does not claim that line~1182 of Ref.~\cite{Cirac2016MPDO_arXiv} proves the
replacement. The historical target was
\begin{align}
    \biggl(    \forall j,\
        \left\langle
            V^{(N)}(B_{k_0}),V^{(N)}(A_j)
        \right\rangle
        \xrightarrow[N\to\infty]{}0
    \biggr)
        &\Longrightarrow
        \neg\biggl(        \sum_j\mu_j^NV^{(N)}(A_j)
            =
            c_N\sum_k\nu_k^NV^{(N)}(B_k)
        \biggr).
    \label{eq:cpsv16_fixed_block_cancellation_retired_fixed_block_target}
\end{align}
No current \Lean{} declaration carries the retired fixed-right or fixed-left
one-copy fixed-block names. Reintroducing
Eq.~(\ref{eq:cpsv16_fixed_block_cancellation_retired_fixed_block_target})
would reconstruct an abandoned proof architecture rather than close a current
theorem.

The earlier conditional helpers requiring combined-family linear independence,
whose names ended in \path{_phase_sum_li}, and the residual-span variants were
retired in the 2026-05-13 audit; see
\path{docs/audits/2026-05-13_cpsv16_ft_paper_vs_code_structural_map.md}. They
must not be presented as consequences of the fixed-block sentence in Appendix
MPV proof, line~1182 of Ref.~\cite{Cirac2016MPDO_arXiv}. Corollary
\texttt{Lem1} supplies only the single-block family in
Eq.~(\ref{eq:cpsv16_fixed_block_cancellation_fixed_block_span_dimension}).
The current restricted-family argument is a separate repair. This mismatch
explains why the fixed-block route was retired and is not a pending obligation
for the corrected proportional theorem.

This note intentionally omits the retired no-tail, leading-only,
partner-identification, leading-tail, residual-span, and
\path{_phase_sum_li} declarations. They were deleted as wrong-direction or
orphan scaffolding in the same audit.

\section{Retired discharge plan}

The plan in
\path{docs/audits/2026-05-13_cpsv16_ft_sorry_discharge_plan.md} proposed an
exact eventual identity for the leading coefficients:
\begin{align}
    c_N(\mu^B_0)^N\zeta^N
        &= (\mu^A_0)^N
        \qquad\text{for all sufficiently large }N.
    \label{eq:cpsv16_fixed_block_cancellation_retired_leading_coefficient_identity}
\end{align}
The proposed derivation used the proportionality identity and BNT linear
independence on the CPSV fixed-block family. It would then have copied the
equal-MPV tail-subtraction and induction architecture without assuming linear
independence of all residual blocks on both sides. Direct coefficient
comparison on BNT families superseded this plan.

\subsection{Factored analytic discharge and its lower-bound obstruction}

\textit{Classification: historical analytic obstruction.}

The proposed factored analytic discharge required the following hypotheses:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Every sector weight in both families has unit modulus.
    \item Off-diagonal cross-overlaps at the fixed block decay:
    \begin{align}
        \left\langle
            V^{(N)}(B_{k_0}),V^{(N)}(B_k)
        \right\rangle
            &\xrightarrow[N\to\infty]{}0
            \qquad (k\neq k_0).
        \label{eq:cpsv16_fixed_block_cancellation_fixed_block_off_diagonal_decay}
    \end{align}
    \item The fixed-block self-overlap has a nonzero limit:
    \begin{align}
        \left\langle
            V^{(N)}(B_{k_0}),V^{(N)}(B_{k_0})
        \right\rangle
            &\xrightarrow[N\to\infty]{}\ell,
    \label{eq:cpsv16_fixed_block_cancellation_fixed_block_self_overlap_limit}\\
        \ell
            &\neq 0.
    \label{eq:cpsv16_fixed_block_cancellation_nonzero_self_overlap_limit}
    \end{align}
    \item The projective scalar has an eventual positive lower bound:
    \begin{align}
        \lVert c_N\rVert
            &\geq\delta>0
            \qquad\text{for all sufficiently large }N.
        \label{eq:cpsv16_fixed_block_cancellation_projective_scalar_lower_bound}
    \end{align}
\end{enumerate}

The first hypothesis was an extra assumption of the retired plan. Line~246 of
Ref.~\cite{Cirac2016MPDO_arXiv} states only that every weight has modulus at
most one and that at least one weight has unit modulus globally; it does not
assert unit modulus for every sector weight. The second and third hypotheses
come from the normal-block comparison at the selected block. The fourth
hypothesis is not supplied directly. The cited argument appeals to a
dominant-adjusted scalar limit, but the retired plan did not derive the
explicit lower bound in
Eq.~(\ref{eq:cpsv16_fixed_block_cancellation_projective_scalar_lower_bound})
from the canonical-form data.

The earlier non-cancellation helpers carrying these extra hypotheses belonged
to the retired Tier~3 route and must not be cited as current declarations. No
current theorem depends on this lower-bound argument.
