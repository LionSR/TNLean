\section{The source claim}
For a fixed translation-invariant tensor of constant bond dimension,
the paragraph ``Long-range MPS using measurements'' of
\cite{Malz2024Preparation} asserts preparation on the physical chain in
depth $O(\log(N/\varepsilon))$ when measurements prepare only the
GHZ-type state, and $O(\log\log(N/\varepsilon))$ when they also assist
the tree circuit. Normalization presupposes a nonzero periodic vector.
The starting tensor need not already be a direct sum of normal blocks.

\section{The algebraic reduction}
For every tensor $A$ there exist $p\ge1$ and normal, left-canonical,
pairwise gauge-phase-inequivalent tensors $C_j$ such that, for every
positive blocked length $M$,
\begin{align*}
    V^{(M)}(A^{[p]})
     &=\sum_j\beta_j(M)V^{(M)}(C_j), &
    \beta_j(M)&=\sum_{k=1}^{m_j}\nu_{j,k}^{M}.
\end{align*}
Every multiplicity is positive and every copy weight is nonzero.
The family may be empty, in which case every vector on the left vanishes.
Distinct representatives are eventually linearly independent.
This combines the canonical representative after blocking, the
trace-preserving gauge, and grouping by gauge phases from
\cite[Section~2.3 and Appendix~A]{Cirac2016MPDO_arXiv}.
The declaration is
\leanid{MPSTensor.exists_normal_leftCanonical_sectors_after_blocking}
in \doclink{TNLean/MPS/CanonicalForm/BNTSectorsAfterBlocking}.

Each $C_j$ has a positive-definite trace-one fixed point $\sigma_j$.
Normality and inequivalence give a strict mixed-transfer spectral gap.
Consequently there is $c$, fixed before $M$ and $\varepsilon$, such that
every $M\ge2$ with $V^{(M)}(A^{[p]})\ne0$ and every
$0<\varepsilon\le1$ have a unit vector $\psi$ prepared in measurement
rounds of depth $T$ on the chain of $d^p$-dimensional sites, with
\begin{align*}
    T&\le c\log\bigl(1+\log(M/\varepsilon)\bigr), &
    1-\left|\left\langle\psi,
    \frac{V^{(M)}(A^{[p]})}{\|V^{(M)}(A^{[p]})\|}\right\rangle\right|
    &\le\varepsilon.
\end{align*}
The declaration is
\leanid{MPSPreparation.exists_isPreparedWithMeasurementRoundsInDepth_blockTensor_le_log_log}
in \doclink{TNLean/MPS/Preparation/ArbitraryBlockedPreparation}.
The coefficients may cancel separately at particular lengths; only the
total target vector is assumed nonzero.

\section{Remaining conclusions}
The algebraic equality above applies to physical lengths divisible by
$p$. It does not assert that the same normal family represents all
other lengths. Moreover, a circuit on $d^p$-dimensional sites is not yet
a circuit on the original $d$-dimensional sites under the formal depth
predicate. The remaining steps are:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Replace each blocked nearest-neighbour gate, onsite
          instrument, and conditional correction by an explicit
          protocol on the original sites, with resources bounded in
          terms of the fixed $p$ and $d$. Establish equality of branch
          operations, including normalization and feedforward.
    \item Treat every residue class of physical lengths modulo $p$.
          Retain the residual boundary contraction or use an unequal
          blocking theorem that preserves its coefficient identity.
          A periodic tensor may produce nonzero states at some
          residues and zero states at others, so nonvanishing must be
          imposed on the actual target at its actual length.
    \item For the single-GHZ-measurement conclusion, preserve that
          measurement placement when compiling the later circuit.
          The measurement-round conclusion alone does not prove that
          stronger restriction on the protocol.
\end{enumerate}
These are scope restrictions of the stated partial result, rather than
counterexamples to the source's asymptotic claim. The corrected unweighted
polar construction and the multiplicity correction remain as documented in
\cite{gap:mswc24_measurement_preparation_scope,gap:mswc24_multiplicity_fixed_point}.
