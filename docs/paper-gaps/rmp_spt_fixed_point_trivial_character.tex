\section{What the source states}
In the paragraph ``Symmetry protected topological order'' of
\cite{Cirac2021Matrix}, a symmetry acts on an MPS by
$S_g(A^i)=e^{i\phi(g)}X_g^\dagger A^iX_g$ with $\phi$ a 1-cocycle, and the
zero-correlation-length representative is constructed so that it ``transforms
according to a given 1- and 2-cocycle $\phi$ and $\omega$''.\footnote{Source
    traceability: \path{Papers/2011.12127/TN-Review-main.tex}, lines 1147--1157.}
The phase $e^{i\phi(g)}$ is part of the symmetry: on a ring of $N$ sites the
matrix product vector is multiplied by $e^{iN\phi(g)}$.

\section{What is formalized}
For a projective representation $\rho$ with factor system $\omega$, a character
$\phi$ and $W_g=\rho(g^{-1})$, the twist identity
\begin{align}
    \sum_jU(g)_{ij}A^j & =\phi(g)\,W_gA^iW_g^{-1}
    \label{eq:rmp_spt_trivial_character_twist}
\end{align}
is proved for every $\phi$. On-site symmetry, the existence of a virtual
projective representation, and the identification of its factor system with
$\omega$ up to a coboundary are proved only for $\phi=1$. The formal on-site
symmetry predicate asks that each twisted tensor generate the same matrix
product vectors as the tensor itself, and for $\phi\neq1$ the twisted vector on
$N$ sites is $\phi(g)^N$ times the original one, so the predicate fails.

\section{Elimination plan}
Introduce on-site symmetry up to a character, asking that each twisted tensor be
gauge equivalent to $\phi(g)$ times the tensor, and state the symmetry and
cocycle-class results for it with the character $\phi$ of
(\ref{eq:rmp_spt_trivial_character_twist}). Since $\phi(g)^{-1}U(g)$ is the
action with trivial character, the factor system of the virtual representation
is again cohomologous to $\omega$. The affected declarations are
\leanid{MPSTensor.sptFixedPointTensor_isOnSiteSymmetric},
\leanid{MPSTensor.cohomologousTo_of_sptFixedPointTensor} and
\leanid{MPSTensor.exists_virtualRep_sptFixedPointTensor}.
