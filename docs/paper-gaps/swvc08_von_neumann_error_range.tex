\section{The finite-error assertion}

Let $\psi$ be a unit vector on a chain of $N$ sites of local dimension
$d\ge2$, and let $\phi$ be an open-boundary matrix product state of bond
dimension $D\ge1$, with no normalization condition. Write
$E=\lVert|\psi\rangle\langle\psi|-|\phi\rangle\langle\phi|\rVert_1$ for
the full trace-norm error. Let $\rho$ and $\sigma$ be their reductions to a
prefix of $L<N$ sites. All logarithms and entropies in this note have base two.
The von Neumann argument of Schuch, Wolf, Verstraete and
Cirac~\cite{Schuch2008EntropyScaling} uses $E\le\delta$ to assert
\begin{align}
    \log D & \ge S(\rho)-\frac{\delta}{2}L\log d-1.
    \label{eq:swvc08_vn_printed_bound}
\end{align}
For $S(\rho)\ge cL$ and $L\ge\eta N$, it concludes exponential growth
whenever $\delta<2c/\log d$.\footnote{arXiv:0705.0292v2,
    \path{paper_v2.tex}, lines 278--305. The conventions allowing unnormalized
    approximants and optimizing their scalar multiple occur at lines 160--173.}
The prefix crosses only one bond, so its rank bound is $D$.

The proof applies Audenaert's continuity inequality to density matrices,
but $\operatorname{tr}\sigma=\lVert\phi\rVert^2$ need not equal one.
Even $S(\sigma)\le\log D$ fails for an unnormalized rank-one matrix:
$S(t|u\rangle\langle u|)=-t\log t>0$ for a unit $u$ and $0<t<1$.
Normalizing $\phi$ preserves its bond dimension but can increase the
trace-norm error. The argument therefore needs a further justification.

\section{Failure at error greater than one}

Set $N=2L$ and $K=d^L$. In the computational bases of the two halves, take
\begin{align}
    \psi & =K^{-1/2}\sum_{j=0}^{K-1}|j\rangle\otimes|j\rangle,
         & \rho                                                & =K^{-1}I_K,
         & S(\rho)                                             & =L\log d.
    \label{eq:swvc08_vn_maximal_prefix}
\end{align}
The zero vector has a bond-one matrix product representation and error
$E=1$. For every $1<\delta<2$, the right side of
(\ref{eq:swvc08_vn_printed_bound}) is
$(1-\delta/2)L\log d-1$, which is positive for sufficiently large $L$,
whereas $\log D=0$.

The failure also occurs for nonzero approximants. For $0<t<\delta-1$,
the product vector $\phi=\sqrt t\,|0\rangle^{\otimes 2L}$ has bond dimension
one, and the triangle inequality gives
$E\le1+t<\delta$. For example, $d=2$, $L=5$, $\delta=3/2$, and $t=1/4$
give $E\le5/4<3/2$, while (\ref{eq:swvc08_vn_printed_bound}) would require
$0\ge1/4$. Thus the stated range genuinely fails under the source's
unnormalized-approximant convention.

\section{A repair for error below one}

Suppose now that $E\le\delta<1$. Then $\phi\ne0$. Put
$t=\lVert\phi\rVert^2$, $u=\phi/\lVert\phi\rVert$, and
$f=|\langle\psi,u\rangle|^2$. Diagonalizing the difference of the two
rank-one matrices on their joint span gives
\begin{align}
    E^2 & =(1+t)^2-4tf=(t-(2f-1))^2+4f(1-f).
    \label{eq:swvc08_vn_pure_error}
\end{align}
If $f\le1/2$, the first expression in
(\ref{eq:swvc08_vn_pure_error}) is at least one. Hence $f>1/2$, and
\begin{align}
    1-f & \le\sqrt{f(1-f)}\le\delta/2.
    \label{eq:swvc08_vn_lost_overlap}
\end{align}
This is the small-error branch of the optimized pure-state geometry
used in~\cite{Schuch2008EntropyScaling}.

Let $r$ be the Schmidt rank of $u$ across the prefix cut; $1\le r\le D$.
If $r\ge K$, the bound follows from $S(\rho)\le\log K\le\log D$.
Otherwise let $p$ be the sum of the eigenvalues of $\rho$ outside its
$r$ largest eigenvalues. The Schmidt overlap bound gives $f\le1-p$,
so (\ref{eq:swvc08_vn_lost_overlap}) implies $p\le\delta/2$.
For $0<p<1$, splitting the eigenvalue distribution into these two parts
gives
\begin{align}
    S(\rho)
     & \le H_2(p)+(1-p)\log r+p\log(K-r)\notag \\
     & \le\log r+p\log K+1
    \le\log D+\frac{\delta}{2}L\log d+1.
    \label{eq:swvc08_vn_entropy_split}
\end{align}
Here $H_2(p)=-p\log p-(1-p)\log(1-p)\le1$ is binary entropy.
If $p=0$, use $S(\rho)\le\log r$ directly. Thus
(\ref{eq:swvc08_vn_printed_bound}) is valid for $0\le\delta<1$, even
for unnormalized approximants. This proof uses spectral mass rather than
applying a density-matrix continuity theorem to $\sigma$.

\section{The qualitative obstruction}

For prefix entropy $S(\rho)\ge cL$ with $L\ge\eta N$, choose
$0<\delta<\min\{1,2c/\log d\}$. The repaired bound gives
\begin{align}
    \log D & \ge\eta\bigl(c-\tfrac12\delta\log d\bigr)N-1.
    \label{eq:swvc08_vn_growth}
\end{align}
Its coefficient of $N$ is positive. Efficient approximation in the
source requires polynomial bond dimension for every fixed positive
accuracy~\cite{Schuch2008EntropyScaling}; choosing this small accuracy
therefore suffices for the von Neumann growth obstruction.
The qualitative conclusion survives the correction of the finite-error range.

This note gives a mathematical counterexample and repair. It does not
claim a Lean proof of the pure-state error identity, its combination with
the entropy split, or the full growth obstruction.\footnote{The remaining
    formalization is tracked in
    \href{https://github.com/LionSR/TNLean/issues/9857}{issue 9857}.}
