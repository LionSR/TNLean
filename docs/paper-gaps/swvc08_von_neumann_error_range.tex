\section{The source statement}

The entropy obstruction of~\cite{Schuch2008EntropyScaling} bounds the von
Neumann entropy of a block $R$ of a unit vector $\psi$ on a chain of local
dimension $q$, approximated by a matrix product state $\phi$ of bond dimension
$D$ with trace error $\delta=\lVert\ketbra{\psi}{\psi}-\ketbra{\phi}{\phi}\rVert_1$,
by a rank term together with a continuity term controlled by the error. For a
prefix $R$ the estimate is
\begin{align}
    S_\psi(R)&\le\log D+\frac{\delta}{2}\lvert R\rvert\log q+\log2.
    \label{eq:swvc08_von_neumann_prefix}
\end{align}
The source presents this estimate without restricting the error $\delta$ to
which it applies.\footnote{\path{paper_v2.tex}, lines~269--305, and the
    obstruction, lines~278--305.}

\section{The point at issue}

The continuity term in (\ref{eq:swvc08_von_neumann_prefix}) comes from a
Fannes-type estimate of the entropy difference between the exact reduced state
and the approximant's reduced state, controlled by their trace distance. That
estimate is informative only when the error is strictly below one: at
$\delta\ge1$ the two rank-one matrices may be orthogonal, the reduced states
need share no support, and the entropy difference is no longer controlled by
$\delta$. The half-error coefficient $\delta/2$ and the additive $\log2$ are
features of the small-error regime, so the estimate must carry the hypothesis
$\delta<1$.

\section{The corrected statement}

Under $\delta<1$, every prefix $R$ satisfies
(\ref{eq:swvc08_von_neumann_prefix}), and every interior block satisfies the
two-boundary form
\begin{align}
    S_\psi(R)&\le2\log D+\frac{\delta}{2}\lvert R\rvert\log q+\log2,
    \label{eq:swvc08_von_neumann_block}
\end{align}
with the factor two recorded in the companion note
\path{swvc08_interior_block_bond_rank.tex}. The approximant $\phi$ need not be
normalized. Empty and full prefixes, boundary blocks, whole chains, and chains
of length zero or one are included, with
$\log0=0$.\footnote{\leanid{TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.IsOpenMPS.regionEntropy_leftBlock_le_log_add_half_error}
    and \leanid{TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.IsOpenMPS.regionEntropy_sdiff_leftBlock_le_two_log_add_half_error}
    in \doclink{TNLean/MPS/AreaLaw/EntropyScaling/VonNeumannApproximationLowerBound}.}
These corrected finite estimates feed the linear-entropy obstruction, which is
qualitative and so unaffected by the restriction of the range.

\section{Conclusion}

The verdict is a resolved local correction: the entropy estimate
(\ref{eq:swvc08_von_neumann_prefix}) holds in the small-error range
$\delta<1$, and the formalized statement carries that hypothesis. The
unrestricted printed statement is not asserted.
