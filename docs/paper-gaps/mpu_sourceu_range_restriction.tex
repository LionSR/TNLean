\section{The graphical step}

The proof of the source-tensor isometry in
Ref.~\cite[lines~536--557]{Cirac2017MPU} contracts a normalized open tail
between source-cut factors and simplifies the resulting diagram. Interpreted
as an ambient matrix identity, this simplification would identify the
open-tail coefficient with the identity on the full product-index space. That
identity is false in general because the source factors need not be
surjective. The available isometry relation is only
\begin{align}
    X_2^\dagger X_2
        &= 1.
    \label{eq:mpu_sourceu_range_restriction_source_isometry}
\end{align}
Equation~\ref{eq:mpu_sourceu_range_restriction_source_isometry} does not imply
the ambient coisometry relation $X_2X_2^\dagger=1$.

\section{Range-restricted interpretation}

Let $M_1$ and $M_2$ denote the source-cut factors, let $X_1$ and $X_2$ denote
the corresponding source isometries, and define
$W_\rho=\rho\otimes 1_d$. The valid interpretation retains the external
source-$u$ tensors and closes the complete tensor network before moving the
cut. The two projection identities used in this closed network are
\begin{align}
    X_1X_1^\dagger W_\rho M_1
        &= M_1,
    \label{eq:mpu_sourceu_range_restriction_first_range_identity}\\
    X_2X_2^\dagger M_2
        &= M_2.
    \label{eq:mpu_sourceu_range_restriction_second_range_identity}
\end{align}
These identities follow from the source-cut factorizations and the recovery
formulas for $Y_1$ and $Y_2$. They assert that the relevant source-cut factors
lie in the ranges of $X_1$ and $X_2$; neither identity asserts that a source
isometry is an ambient coisometry.

The forward internal kernel also depends on orientation. It consists of an
output-first double-layer letter followed by the $K$th power of its normalized
diagonal. Conjugate transposition reverses the virtual chain. Consequently, the
reflected simple-contraction equations cannot be substituted pointwise into
this coefficient.

The remaining independent obligation compares two complete contractions: the
ordinary source-$u$ Gram matrix and the normalized closed output tail on
$K+2$ sites. This equality must be proved while retaining the full external
network. The normalized open-tail metric alone does not establish it.

\section{Verdict}

The discrepancy is a graphical clarification. The source argument in
Ref.~\cite{Cirac2017MPU} must be read as a range-restricted contraction of the
closed network, not as an identity on the ambient product-index space. This
reading does not make the source lemma false because its diagram retains the
external network. An indexed translation must preserve that restriction.

\leanid{MPOTensor.sourceUForwardKernel} and

The range identities in
Eqs.~\ref{eq:mpu_sourceu_range_restriction_first_range_identity} and
\ref{eq:mpu_sourceu_range_restriction_second_range_identity} are formalized by
\leanid{MPOTensor.sourceX₁_mul_conjTranspose_mul_weight_mul_sourceCutM₁} and

The fully expanded normalized metric is formalized by
\leanid{MPOTensor.IsMPU.normalized_sourceU_openTail_metric}. This theorem does
not identify the normalized metric with the ordinary source-$u$ Gram matrix;
the final closed-network equality remains a separate obligation.
