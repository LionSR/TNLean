\section{Source condition}

The commuting-Hamiltonian condition in Appendix~\cpsvref{AppendixLooseEnds} of
Ref.~\cite{Cirac2016MPDO_arXiv} is not the route used to prove the forward
implication from RFP to NNCPH. At source lines~1305--1307, the proof of
Theorem~\cpsvref{thm:main-MPS} merely states that this implication follows
immediately from the structural characterization in
Theorem~\cpsvref{thm:charact-MPS} of Ref.~\cite{Cirac2016MPDO_arXiv}.
Definition~D.2 belongs instead to the later, independent discussion of
decorrelation.

Definition~D.2 of Ref.~\cite{Cirac2016MPDO_arXiv} considers a tripartite
Hilbert space $\mathcal H_A\otimes\mathcal H_X\otimes\mathcal H_B$ with local
projectors $Q_{AX}$ and $Q_{XB}$, writing $K_{AX}$ and $K_{XB}$ for their
kernels. Their lifts to the full tripartite space are
\begin{align}
    \widehat Q_{AX}
        &=Q_{AX}\otimes I_B,
    \label{eq:cpsv16_parent_commuting_hamiltonian_scope_qax_lift}\\
    \widehat Q_{XB}
        &=I_A\otimes Q_{XB}.
    \label{eq:cpsv16_parent_commuting_hamiltonian_scope_qxb_lift}
\end{align}
The source condition is\footnote{Local source:
\path{Papers/1606.00608/MPDO-22-12-17-2.tex}:2205--2218, corresponding to
Eqs.~(\cpsvref{QAXQXB}) and~(\cpsvref{KAXBKAXKXB}).}
\begin{align}
    [\widehat Q_{AX},\widehat Q_{XB}]
        &=0,
    \label{eq:cpsv16_parent_commuting_hamiltonian_scope_source_commutation}\\
    K_{AXB}
        &=(K_{AX}\otimes\mathcal H_B)
          \cap(\mathcal H_A\otimes K_{XB}).
    \label{eq:cpsv16_parent_commuting_hamiltonian_scope_source_kernel_intersection}
\end{align}

The proof of Proposition~D.3 in Ref.~\cite{Cirac2016MPDO_arXiv} introduces the
complementary projectors and their lifts:
\begin{align}
    P_{AX}
        &=I_{AX}-Q_{AX},
    \label{eq:cpsv16_parent_commuting_hamiltonian_scope_pax_complement}\\
    P_{XB}
        &=I_{XB}-Q_{XB},
    \label{eq:cpsv16_parent_commuting_hamiltonian_scope_pxb_complement}\\
    \widehat P_{AX}
        &=P_{AX}\otimes I_B,
    \label{eq:cpsv16_parent_commuting_hamiltonian_scope_pax_lift}\\
    \widehat P_{XB}
        &=I_A\otimes P_{XB}.
    \label{eq:cpsv16_parent_commuting_hamiltonian_scope_pxb_lift}
\end{align}
These lifted projectors satisfy
\begin{align}
    \widehat P_{AX}\widehat P_{XB}
        &=P_{AXB},
    \label{eq:cpsv16_parent_commuting_hamiltonian_scope_projector_product}
\end{align}
which is the product identity for commuting orthogonal projectors onto the two
local kernels.\footnote{Local source:
\path{Papers/1606.00608/MPDO-22-12-17-2.tex}:2259--2289, in particular
Eqs.~(\cpsvref{PAXQAX}), (\cpsvref{PXBQXB}), and~(\cpsvref{arrggg}).}

The tripartite formal development formalizes this range-intersection
condition directly.\footnote{Formal structure:
\leanid{TripartiteDecorrelation.CommutingParentHamiltonian}.} For commuting
orthogonal projectors,
\begin{align}
    \operatorname{ran}P_{AXB}
        =\operatorname{ran}\widehat P_{AX}
          \cap\operatorname{ran}\widehat P_{XB}
        \quad&\Longleftrightarrow\quad
        \widehat P_{AX}\widehat P_{XB}=P_{AXB}.
    \label{eq:cpsv16_parent_commuting_hamiltonian_scope_range_product_equivalence}
\end{align}
\footnote{Formal equivalence:
\leanid{TripartiteDecorrelation.hasGroundSpaceIntersection_iff_product_eq};
forward direction:
\leanid{TripartiteDecorrelation.CommutingParentHamiltonian.hproduct}.}

\section{Current boundary}

The algebraic decorrelation predicate captures only the idempotent-product
part of the source condition.\footnote{Formal predicate:
\leanid{Decorrelation.HasCommutingParentHam} in
\doclink{TNLean/MPS/ParentHamiltonian/Decorrelation}.} On a complex vector
space $E$, it requires endomorphisms $P_{AX},P_{XB}:E\to E$ satisfying
\begin{align}
    P_{AX}^{2}
        &=P_{AX},
    \label{eq:cpsv16_parent_commuting_hamiltonian_scope_abstract_pax_idempotent}\\
    P_{XB}^{2}
        &=P_{XB},
    \label{eq:cpsv16_parent_commuting_hamiltonian_scope_abstract_pxb_idempotent}\\
    P_{AX}P_{XB}
        &=P_{XB}P_{AX},
    \label{eq:cpsv16_parent_commuting_hamiltonian_scope_abstract_commutation}\\
    P_{AX}P_{XB}
        &=P_K.
    \label{eq:cpsv16_parent_commuting_hamiltonian_scope_abstract_product}
\end{align}
The predicate does not assume a tensor decomposition of $E$, local factor
actions on $AX$ and $XB$, self-adjointness, orthogonality, or the existence of
the source projectors $Q_{AX}$ and $Q_{XB}$. Every idempotent $P_K$ therefore
admits the tautological choice
\begin{align}
    P_{AX}
        &=P_K,
    \label{eq:cpsv16_parent_commuting_hamiltonian_scope_tautological_pax}\\
    P_{XB}
        &=P_K,
    \label{eq:cpsv16_parent_commuting_hamiltonian_scope_tautological_pxb}
\end{align}
\footnote{Formal constructor:
\leanid{Decorrelation.HasCommutingParentHam.ofIdem}.} This formal condition is
thus not Definition~D.2 of Ref.~\cite{Cirac2016MPDO_arXiv}, but rather the
algebraic consequence sufficient for the proved backward decorrelation
implication.

A separate three-site support layer formalizes the $AX$ and $XB$ faces and
their overlapping commutation.\footnote{Formal declarations:
\leanid{MPSTensor.leftPairLift}, \leanid{MPSTensor.rightPairLift}, and
\leanid{MPSTensor.HasOverlappingTwoSiteCommutation}.} The source
kernel-intersection condition is represented by
(\ref{eq:cpsv16_parent_commuting_hamiltonian_scope_lifted_kernel_intersection})
in the three-site parent Hamiltonian structure,\footnote{Formal structure:
\leanid{MPSTensor.HasAppendixD2ParentCommutingHamiltonian}.} whose forgetful
projection yields the algebraic overlapping-commutation predicate:
\begin{align}
    K_{AXB}
        &=\ker((Q_{AX})_{AX})\cap\ker((Q_{XB})_{XB}).
    \label{eq:cpsv16_parent_commuting_hamiltonian_scope_lifted_kernel_intersection}
\end{align}

The scalar pair-index relation defines the physical map
\begin{align}
    (Uv)_i
        &=\sum_{\alpha,\beta}U^i_{\alpha,\beta}v_{\alpha,\beta},
    \label{eq:cpsv16_parent_commuting_hamiltonian_scope_physical_map}\\
    U^*U
        &=\Id,
    \label{eq:cpsv16_parent_commuting_hamiltonian_scope_physical_isometry}
\end{align}
\footnote{Formal declarations:
\leanid{MPSTensor.AppendixBStructuralData.physicalIsometry} and
\leanid{MPSTensor.AppendixBStructuralData.physicalIsometryLeftInverse_comp}.}
For every $L$, the corresponding tensor-power maps satisfy
\begin{align}
    U^{*\otimes L}U^{\otimes L}
        &=\Id,
    \label{eq:cpsv16_parent_commuting_hamiltonian_scope_tensor_power_isometry}
\end{align}
\footnote{Formal declaration:
\leanid{MPSTensor.AppendixBStructuralData.physicalIsometryTensorPowerLeftInverse_comp}.}

On two sites, the inserted virtual bond is
\begin{align}
    (I_\varphi v)_{(a,b),(b',c)}
        &=\delta_{b,b'}\lambda_bv_{a,c}.
    \label{eq:cpsv16_parent_commuting_hamiltonian_scope_virtual_bond_embedding}
\end{align}
Its physical image satisfies
\begin{align}
    \operatorname{ran}(U^{\otimes 2}I_\varphi)
        &=G_2(A_{\mathrm{core}}),
    \label{eq:cpsv16_parent_commuting_hamiltonian_scope_two_site_image}
\end{align}
\footnote{Formal theorem:
\leanid{MPSTensor.AppendixBStructuralData.twoSiteBasicEmbedding_range}.} The
orthogonal projector onto this image\footnote{Formal projector:
\leanid{MPSTensor.AppendixBStructuralData.twoSiteBasicSupportProjection}.} has
complement equal to $q_2(A_{\mathrm{core}})$.\footnote{Formal identity:
\leanid{MPSTensor.AppendixBStructuralData.twoSiteBasicSupportProjection_eq_complement}.}

Complementary projectors commute under the same conditions.\footnote{Formal
theorem: \leanid{MPSTensor.HasOverlappingTwoSiteCommutation.complement}.}
Three-site transport holds directly for $Q_{AX}$ and $Q_{XB}$ and their
complements.\footnote{Formal declarations:
\leanid{MPSTensor.localTerm_two_three_zero_one_commute_of_appendixD2} and
\leanid{MPSTensor.localTerm_two_three_zero_one_commute_of_appendixD2_complement}.}

For the Appendix~\cpsvref{AppendixPure} structural datum, the coefficient-space
representatives are denoted by $\widehat Q_{AX}$ and $\widehat
Q_{XB}$,\footnote{Formal declarations:
\leanid{MPSTensor.AppendixBStructuralData.appendixBQAXOnCoeffSpace} and
\leanid{MPSTensor.AppendixBStructuralData.appendixBQXBOnCoeffSpace}.} each
identified with $q_2(A)$ before placement on the $AX$ and $XB$ faces. Under
canonical reindexing, their factor actions are
\begin{align}
    [\widehat Q_{AX}]_{AX}
        &=[\widehat Q_{AX}]\otimes I_d,
    \label{eq:cpsv16_parent_commuting_hamiltonian_scope_qax_factor_action}\\
    [\widehat Q_{XB}]_{XB}
        &=I_d\otimes[\widehat Q_{XB}].
    \label{eq:cpsv16_parent_commuting_hamiltonian_scope_qxb_factor_action}
\end{align}
The generic factor actions and their Appendix~\cpsvref{AppendixPure}
specializations are verified.\footnote{Formal declarations:
\leanid{MPSTensor.leftPairLift_toMatrix_reindex},
\leanid{MPSTensor.rightPairLift_toMatrix_reindex},
\leanid{MPSTensor.AppendixBStructuralData.appendixBQAX_factor_action}, and
\leanid{MPSTensor.AppendixBStructuralData.appendixBQXB_factor_action}.}

This construction realizes only the homogeneous setting
\begin{align}
    \mathcal H_A
        &=\mathbb C^d,
    \label{eq:cpsv16_parent_commuting_hamiltonian_scope_homogeneous_a}\\
    \mathcal H_X
        &=\mathbb C^d,
    \label{eq:cpsv16_parent_commuting_hamiltonian_scope_homogeneous_x}\\
    \mathcal H_B
        &=\mathbb C^d,
    \label{eq:cpsv16_parent_commuting_hamiltonian_scope_homogeneous_b}
\end{align}
introduced at source lines~2185--2186, rather than the arbitrary tensor
factorization used in Definition~D.2 at source lines~2205--2218 of
Ref.~\cite{Cirac2016MPDO_arXiv}.

Because $q_2(A)$ is the canonical orthogonal projector onto $G_2(A)^\perp$,
the coefficient-space maps are orthogonal projectors. They do not by themselves
construct the source projectors on $\mathcal H_A\otimes\mathcal H_X$ and
$\mathcal H_X\otimes\mathcal H_B$ in
\path{TNLean/MPS/RFP/AppendixBStructuralData.lean}; the transported
identification is an explicit hypothesis in the conditional identification
theorem.\footnote{Formal theorem:
\leanid{MPSTensor.AppendixBStructuralData.hasOverlappingTwoSiteCommutation_of_appendixD2_identified_projectors}.}

Without this identification, commutation still follows from the adjacent
virtual-bond projectors and their isometric transport.\footnote{Formal theorems:
\leanid{MPSTensor.AppendixBStructuralData.twoSiteBasicSupportProjection_commute_lifts}
and \leanid{MPSTensor.AppendixBStructuralData.hasOverlappingTwoSiteCommutation}.}
The adjacent parent terms commute on every periodic chain of length
$N>2$.\footnote{Formal theorem:
\leanid{MPSTensor.AppendixBStructuralData.adjacent_twoSite_localTerms_commute}.}

For an injective tensor, the three-site intersection identity is
\begin{align}
    G_3(A)
        &=\ker(q_2(A)_{AX})\cap\ker(q_2(A)_{XB}),
    \label{eq:cpsv16_parent_commuting_hamiltonian_scope_three_site_intersection}
\end{align}
\footnote{Formal theorem:
\leanid{MPSTensor.groundSpace_three_eq_adjacent_twoSite_parent_kernels}.}
The Appendix~\cpsvref{AppendixPure} representatives therefore satisfy the
coefficient-space analogues of idempotence, commutation, and kernel
intersection, with
\begin{align}
    \widehat K_{AXB}
        &=G_3(A),
    \label{eq:cpsv16_parent_commuting_hamiltonian_scope_coefficient_ground_space}
\end{align}
as established in the local structural development.\footnote{Formal theorem:
\leanid{MPSTensor.AppendixBStructuralData.hasAppendixD2ParentCommutingHamiltonian}.}
That theorem does not identify the lifted maps with source projectors on the
explicitly factored spaces. For a normal left-canonical RFP tensor, the RFP
hypotheses supply the required injectivity.\footnote{Formal theorem:
\leanid{MPSTensor.rfp_hasAppendixD2ParentCommutingHamiltonian_of_leftCanonical}.}

\section{Elimination plan}

The general finite-dimensional tripartite equivalence is proved
unconditionally.\footnote{Formal theorem:
\leanid{TripartiteDecorrelation.parentHamiltonian_iff_decorrelated}.} Its parent
condition uses orthogonal projectors on the explicit factors and asserts their
lifted range intersection. This intersection equals
(\ref{eq:cpsv16_parent_commuting_hamiltonian_scope_projector_product}):\footnote{Formal
equivalence:
\leanid{TripartiteDecorrelation.hasGroundSpaceIntersection_iff_product_eq}.}
the forward direction constructs the projectors as supports of the two
marginals, while the reverse direction derives and uses the product identity.
The local correction to the marginal-support expansion at source
lines~2236--2252 is documented in
\path{docs/paper-gaps/cpsv16_decorrelation_slice_expansion_fix.tex}.

While the coefficient-space analogues are established, constructing the source
projectors on the explicit tensor factors and identifying their transported
actions with the coefficient-space maps remains open. A conditional theorem
isolates this remaining identification.\footnote{Formal theorem:
\leanid{MPSTensor.AppendixBStructuralData.hasOverlappingTwoSiteCommutation_of_appendixD2_identified_projectors}.}

This comparison belongs to the RFP application rather than to the general
decorrelation equivalence. The algebraic predicate\footnote{Formal predicate:
\leanid{Decorrelation.HasCommutingParentHam}.} isolates only the algebraic
idempotent-product consequence, and the local three-site
condition\footnote{Formal structure:
\leanid{MPSTensor.HasAppendixD2ParentCommutingHamiltonian}.} does not itself
assert self-adjointness. By contrast, the explicit tripartite condition
includes self-adjointness and establishes both directions of Proposition~D.3
in Ref.~\cite{Cirac2016MPDO_arXiv}.

The remaining obligations for Theorem~\cpsvref{thm:main-MPS} in
Ref.~\cite{Cirac2016MPDO_arXiv} are the full canonical-form scope and the
all-chain ground-space spanning statement.\footnote{\ghissue{2633}.}
Definition~D.2 does not serve as their missing proof route.
