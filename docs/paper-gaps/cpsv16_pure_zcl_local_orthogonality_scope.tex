\section{Source statement}

Section~3.2 of Ref.~\cite{Cirac2016MPDO_arXiv} defines correlations independent
of distance (CID) by requiring that, for separated observables, translating one
observable through any allowed separation leaves the expectation value
unchanged.\footnote{Local source:
\path{Papers/1606.00608/MPDO-22-12-17-2.tex:437--445}, label
\path{ZCL}.}
For a basis-of-normal-tensors (BNT) family $\{A_j\}_j$, define the mixed
transfer matrices by
\begin{align}
    \mathbb E_{j,j'}
        &:= \sum_i A_j^i\otimes\overline{A_{j'}^i}.
    \label{eq:cpsv16_pure_zcl_local_orthogonality_scope_mixed_transfer}
\end{align}
The same subsection defines local orthogonality by
\begin{align}
    \mathbb E_{j,j'} &= 0,
    \qquad j\ne j',
    \tag{LO}
    \label{eq:cpsv16_pure_zcl_local_orthogonality_scope_source_lo}
\end{align}
and defines zero correlation length (ZCL) as the conjunction of CID and local
orthogonality.\footnote{Local source:
\path{Papers/1606.00608/MPDO-22-12-17-2.tex:467--479}.}

For a single tensor $A=\{A^i\}_i$, write
\begin{align}
    \mathbb E_A
        &:= \sum_i A^i\otimes\overline{A^i}
    \label{eq:cpsv16_pure_zcl_local_orthogonality_scope_transfer_matrix}
\end{align}
for its transfer matrix. The pure-state theorem in
Ref.~\cite{Cirac2016MPDO_arXiv} states that a canonical-form tensor satisfies
\begin{align}
    \mathrm{ZCL}(A)
        &\Longleftrightarrow \mathbb E_A^2=\mathbb E_A.
    \label{eq:cpsv16_pure_zcl_local_orthogonality_scope_source_equivalence}
\end{align}
Its proof first identifies local orthogonality with
Eq.~\ref{eq:cpsv16_pure_zcl_local_orthogonality_scope_source_lo}. After
assuming those mixed-sector equations, it identifies transfer idempotence with
CID.\footnote{Local source:
\path{Papers/1606.00608/MPDO-22-12-17-2.tex:1248--1268}.}

\section{Formal boundary}

The formal predicate \leanid{MPSTensor.IsLocallyOrthogonal} adopts a
single-tensor convention:
\begin{align}
    \leanid{MPSTensor.IsLocallyOrthogonal}(A)
        &\Longleftrightarrow \mathbb E_A^2=\mathbb E_A.
    \label{eq:cpsv16_pure_zcl_local_orthogonality_scope_formal_local_orthogonality}
\end{align}
Consequently, the corresponding ZCL predicate is
\begin{align}
    \leanid{MPSTensor.IsZCL}(A)
        &\Longleftrightarrow
        (\mathbb E_A^2=\mathbb E_A)\wedge\mathrm{CID}(A).
    \label{eq:cpsv16_pure_zcl_local_orthogonality_scope_formal_zcl}
\end{align}
The theorem \leanid{MPSTensor.zcl_iff_idempotent_transfer} therefore proves
only the single-block equivalence
\begin{align}
    (\mathbb E_A^2=\mathbb E_A)\wedge\mathrm{CID}(A)
        &\Longleftrightarrow \mathbb E_A^2=\mathbb E_A.
    \label{eq:cpsv16_pure_zcl_local_orthogonality_scope_single_block_equivalence}
\end{align}
The nontrivial direction follows from
\begin{align}
    \mathbb E_A^2=\mathbb E_A
        &\Longrightarrow \mathbb E_A^n=\mathbb E_A
        \quad\text{for every }n\geq 1,
    \label{eq:cpsv16_pure_zcl_local_orthogonality_scope_positive_power_idempotence}\\
    \mathbb E_A^n=\mathbb E_A
        \quad\text{for every }n\geq 1
        &\Longrightarrow \mathrm{CID}(A).
    \label{eq:cpsv16_pure_zcl_local_orthogonality_scope_idempotence_implies_cid}
\end{align}
The reverse direction of
Eq.~\ref{eq:cpsv16_pure_zcl_local_orthogonality_scope_single_block_equivalence}
is immediate because its left-hand side already contains transfer
idempotence.

This result does not prove Theorem~3.8 of
Ref.~\cite{Cirac2016MPDO_arXiv}, whose proof appears at source
lines~1248--1268. That theorem combines unrestricted physical CID with the
BNT-family equations in
Eq.~\ref{eq:cpsv16_pure_zcl_local_orthogonality_scope_source_lo}.

The source predicates now have distinct formal counterparts.
\leanid{MPSTensor.IsPhysicalCID} quantifies over two nonempty physical blocks
and arbitrary natural complementary gap lengths, including zero. It requires
the two-region expectation to remain unchanged when the gaps are redistributed
with fixed total length. The predicate
\leanid{MPSTensor.IsPhysicalBNTZCL} is the conjunction of the BNT relation,
unrestricted physical CID, and BNT local orthogonality. These predicates encode
Definitions~3.3 and~3.6 of Ref.~\cite{Cirac2016MPDO_arXiv}; they do not prove
the equivalence asserted in its Theorem~3.8. In particular,
\leanid{MPSTensor.zcl_iff_idempotent_transfer} still concerns the older
single-block and virtual-insertion predicates.

\section{BNT predicates and the raw-weight obstruction}

The predicate \leanid{MPSTensor.IsBNTLocallyOrthogonal} records
Eq.~\ref{eq:cpsv16_pure_zcl_local_orthogonality_scope_source_lo} for every
pair of distinct sectors. Let $\mathrm{BNT}(A)$ mean that the family is a basis
of normal tensors for $A$. The source predicate is
\begin{align}
    \mathrm{ZCL}_{\mathrm{BNT}}(A)
        &:=
        \mathrm{BNT}(A)
        \wedge \mathrm{CID}_{\mathrm{physical}}(A)
        \wedge
        (\forall j\ne j',\ \mathbb E_{j,j'}=0).
    \label{eq:cpsv16_pure_zcl_local_orthogonality_scope_physical_bnt_zcl}
\end{align}
Its formal name is \leanid{MPSTensor.IsPhysicalBNTZCL}. By contrast, the older
predicate \leanid{MPSTensor.IsBNTZCL} uses arbitrary virtual insertions:
\begin{align}
    \mathrm{ZCL}^{\mathrm{virt}}_{\mathrm{BNT}}(A)
        &:=
        \mathrm{BNT}(A)
        \wedge \mathrm{CID}_{\mathrm{virt}}(A)
        \wedge
        (\forall j\ne j',\ \mathbb E_{j,j'}=0).
    \label{eq:cpsv16_pure_zcl_local_orthogonality_scope_virtual_bnt_zcl}
\end{align}
The positive-gap physical predicate is
\begin{align}
    \mathrm{ZCL}^{\mathrm{pos}}_{\mathrm{BNT}}(A)
        &:=
        \mathrm{BNT}(A)
        \wedge \mathrm{CID}^{\mathrm{pos}}_{\mathrm{physical}}(A)
        \wedge
        (\forall j\ne j',\ \mathbb E_{j,j'}=0),
    \label{eq:cpsv16_pure_zcl_local_orthogonality_scope_positive_gap_bnt_zcl}
\end{align}
where $\mathrm{CID}^{\mathrm{pos}}_{\mathrm{physical}}$ requires both
complementary gaps to be positive.\footnote{Formal predicates:
\leanid{MPSTensor.IsPositiveGapBNTZCL} and
\leanid{MPSTensor.IsPositiveGapPhysicalCID}.}
The unrestricted condition implies its positive-gap restriction through
\leanid{MPSTensor.isPositiveGapPhysicalCID_of_isPhysicalCID}; the analogous
BNT implication is
\leanid{MPSTensor.isPositiveGapBNTZCL_of_isPhysicalBNTZCL}.

The unrestricted BNT-level equivalence is not a remaining proof target because
it is false under the raw-weight canonical-form normalization of
Ref.~\cite{Cirac2016MPDO_arXiv}. Consider the one-letter tensor
\begin{align}
    A^0
        &= \operatorname{diag}\left(1,\frac12\right).
    \label{eq:cpsv16_pure_zcl_local_orthogonality_scope_raw_weight_tensor}
\end{align}
It has one normal BNT component with raw weights $1$ and $1/2$. Local
orthogonality is vacuous, and the tensor satisfies physical CID, but its
transfer matrix is not idempotent. The companion note
\path{docs/paper-gaps/cpsv16_pure_zcl_raw_weight_counterexample.tex} gives the
counterexample and identifies the failed inference at source
lines~1248--1251.\footnote{Tracked by \ghissue{2597}.}
The existing single-block theorem therefore remains a scope restriction: it
records the one-block idempotence--CID consequence without asserting the false
raw-weight BNT equivalence.

\section{Physical correlations and complementary gaps}

Definition~3.3 of Ref.~\cite{Cirac2016MPDO_arXiv} quantifies over physical
observables on arbitrary nonempty finite regions. For a block observable $O$,
define its insertion map by
\begin{align}
    \mathbb E_O(X)
        &:=
        \sum_{\boldsymbol i,\boldsymbol j}
        \bra{\boldsymbol j}O\ket{\boldsymbol i}
        A^{\boldsymbol i}X(A^{\boldsymbol j})^\dagger.
    \label{eq:cpsv16_pure_zcl_local_orthogonality_scope_observable_insertion}
\end{align}
This is the two-observable construction at source lines~490--496 of
Ref.~\cite{Cirac2016MPDO_arXiv}. For observed blocks $O_1,O_2$ on a periodic
chain with complementary gap lengths $n_1,n_2$, the contraction is the
operator trace on the virtual matrix space:
\begin{align}
    \tr_{M_D(\C)}
    \left(\mathbb E_{O_2}\circ\mathbb E_A^{n_2}
        \circ\mathbb E_{O_1}\circ\mathbb E_A^{n_1}\right).
    \label{eq:cpsv16_pure_zcl_local_orthogonality_scope_periodic_contraction}
\end{align}
This is not the matrix trace obtained by applying the composition in
Eq.~\ref{eq:cpsv16_pure_zcl_local_orthogonality_scope_periodic_contraction}
to the identity matrix; the two contractions differ for a general Kraus
family.

The formal positive-gap theorem proves
\begin{align}
    \mathbb E_A^2=\mathbb E_A
        &\Longrightarrow
        \mathrm{CID}^{\mathrm{pos}}_{\mathrm{physical}}(A).
    \label{eq:cpsv16_pure_zcl_local_orthogonality_scope_idempotence_positive_gap_cid}
\end{align}
\footnote{Formal theorem:
\leanid{MPSTensor.isPositiveGapPhysicalCID_of_isTransferIdempotent}.}
This result does not establish Definition~3.3 of
Ref.~\cite{Cirac2016MPDO_arXiv}, which also includes adjacent regions. For an
adjacent pair, one complementary transfer power in the source formula is
\begin{align}
    \mathbb E_A^0
        &= \operatorname{id}_{M_D(\C)}.
    \label{eq:cpsv16_pure_zcl_local_orthogonality_scope_zero_gap_power}
\end{align}
Transfer idempotence gives
Eq.~\ref{eq:cpsv16_pure_zcl_local_orthogonality_scope_positive_power_idempotence}
only for $n\geq 1$ and does not identify $\mathbb E_A^0$ with $\mathbb E_A$.
The positive-gap theorem is therefore a scope restriction, not the full
forward implication of Theorem~3.8 in
Ref.~\cite{Cirac2016MPDO_arXiv}.

The older predicate \leanid{MPSTensor.IsCID} quantifies directly over arbitrary
virtual matrices $X,Y$. Its converse to idempotence is valid, but it is
stronger than physical CID. For the multiplicity-one, unit-weight direct sum,
the simultaneous block-injectivity theorem supplies the physical observables
used at source lines~1250--1258 of Ref.~\cite{Cirac2016MPDO_arXiv}. More
precisely, the theorem identified below selects one BNT basis sector and
realizes the insertion $|R)(l|$ on that sector using a region of size at most
$3D^5$, while producing zero insertion on every other sector
pair.\footnote{Formal theorem:
\leanid{MPSTensor.IsBNTCanonicalForm.exists_basis_physicalObservableTransfer_rankOne_le_three_totalDim_pow_five}.}
This theorem acts on \leanid{directSumTensor P.basis}, not on the raw weighted
repeated-copy tensor \leanid{P.toTensor}. The source equations include all copy
pairs and their weight powers, so unrestricted realization for the raw tensor
remains separate.

A second obstruction occurs at source line~1250. The proof in
Ref.~\cite{Cirac2016MPDO_arXiv} claims that
\begin{align}
    \mathbb E_1^2\ne\mathbb E_1
        &\Longrightarrow
        \text{there exists a nonzero eigenvalue $\lambda$ with
        $|\lambda|<1$.}
    \label{eq:cpsv16_pure_zcl_local_orthogonality_scope_false_spectral_inference}
\end{align}
This implication fails when the only subleading spectral value is zero and the
generalized zero-eigenspace has a nontrivial nilpotent Jordan part. Thus the
printed proof does not establish the unconditional reverse direction, even for
the multiplicity-one, unit-weight representative. The conditional theorems
whose names end in \path{of_spectral_pair} retain exactly the nonzero
subleading left and right eigenvectors used at source lines~1250--1262.

\paragraph{Local correction at source line~1250.}

The multiplicity-one, unit-weight case admits a proof that avoids
Eq.~\ref{eq:cpsv16_pure_zcl_local_orthogonality_scope_false_spectral_inference}.
Fix a BNT sector $j$, let $\rho_j$ be its trace-one stationary state, and choose
arbitrary $u,v\in M_{D_j}(\C)$. Simultaneous physical-observable realization
gives sector-supported rank-one insertions
\begin{align}
    \mathbb E_{O_1}
        &= |\rho_j)(u|,
    \label{eq:cpsv16_pure_zcl_local_orthogonality_scope_first_rank_one_insertion}\\
    \mathbb E_{O_2}
        &= |v)(1|.
    \label{eq:cpsv16_pure_zcl_local_orthogonality_scope_second_rank_one_insertion}
\end{align}
Sector compression, stationarity of $\rho_j$, and the operator trace of a
rank-one map give, for all complementary gap lengths $n_1,n_2$,
\begin{align}
    \tr_{M_D(\C)}
    \left(\mathbb E_{O_2}\mathbb E_A^{n_2}
        \mathbb E_{O_1}\mathbb E_A^{n_1}\right)
        &= \tr\left(u\mathbb E_j^{n_1}(v)\right).
    \label{eq:cpsv16_pure_zcl_local_orthogonality_scope_rank_one_contraction}
\end{align}
No transpose occurs in
Eq.~\ref{eq:cpsv16_pure_zcl_local_orthogonality_scope_rank_one_contraction},
and its right-hand side is independent of $n_2$. Positive-gap CID at
$(n_1,n_2)=(1,2)$ and $(2,1)$ therefore gives
\begin{align}
    \tr\left(u\mathbb E_j(v)\right)
        &= \tr\left(u\mathbb E_j^2(v)\right)
        \qquad\text{for every }u,v.
    \label{eq:cpsv16_pure_zcl_local_orthogonality_scope_trace_pairing_identity}
\end{align}
Nondegeneracy of the trace pairing implies
\begin{align}
    \mathbb E_j^2 &= \mathbb E_j.
    \label{eq:cpsv16_pure_zcl_local_orthogonality_scope_sector_idempotence}
\end{align}
Local orthogonality eliminates the off-diagonal mixed transfer maps, so the
multiplicity-one direct sum is idempotent.

This repair assumes neither diagonalizability nor semisimplicity at zero, and
it requires no nonzero subleading eigenvalue. It is formalized by
\leanid{MPSTensor.IsBNTCanonicalForm.%
isTransferIdempotent_basisDirectSum_of_isPositiveGapBNTZCL}.
It is a local correction to source line~1250 rather than a formalization of the
printed spectral argument. It does not remove the raw-weight counterexample or
the adjacent-gap scope restriction.

Combining this repair with the fixed-point characterization of the all-chain
commuting parent ground spaces gives the representative-level equivalence
formalized by
\leanid{MPSTensor.IsBNTCanonicalForm.%
isPositiveGapBNTZCL_basisDirectSum_iff_hasNNCPHGroundSpaces}.
This theorem concerns the multiplicity-one, unit-weight basis direct sum and
positive complementary gaps. It excludes repeated copies, arbitrary raw
weights, and zero complementary gaps.

The older virtual-insertion predicate also quantifies over positive-definite
fixed points and is therefore vacuous for a tensor without such a fixed point.
The periodic-chain predicate avoids this boundary-state issue. In the
unrestricted raw-weight setting, the argument also fails when it passes from
non-idempotence of the full transfer matrix to non-idempotence of a normal BNT
component, because the full transfer matrix contains eigenvalues contributed
by the raw copy weights.

Accordingly, \leanid{MPSTensor.IsBNTZCL} remains the virtual-insertion
surrogate, and \leanid{MPSTensor.IsPositiveGapBNTZCL} denotes the physical
positive-gap condition. Neither predicate is Definition~3.6 of
Ref.~\cite{Cirac2016MPDO_arXiv}; that definition is represented by
\leanid{MPSTensor.IsPhysicalBNTZCL}. The raw-weight counterexample disproves
the unrestricted equivalence between that predicate and transfer idempotence.
