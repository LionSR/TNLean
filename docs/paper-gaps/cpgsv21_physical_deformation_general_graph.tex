\section{Source statement}

Section~IV.C of Cirac--P\'erez-Garc\'ia--Schuch--Verstraete~\cite{Cirac2021Matrix}
considers an invertible map $\Lambda$ acting on the physical space $\C^d$ of a
tensor network state $\ket\psi$ defined on a general graph $G=(V,E)$. Under the
action of $\Lambda^{\otimes V}$, the deformed state $\ket{\psi'}=\Lambda^{\otimes V}\ket\psi$
has deformed local parent interactions
\begin{align}\label{eq:cpgsv21_physical_deformation_general_graph_interaction}
    h'_e & = ({\Lambda^{-1}}^\dagger)^{\otimes \partial e}\,h_e\,(\Lambda^{-1})^{\otimes \partial e},
\end{align}
where $\partial e$ is the support of the interaction term. The source asserts
that the map $\Lambda^{\otimes V}$ establishes a one-to-one correspondence
between the ground spaces of the parent Hamiltonians of $\ket\psi$ and $\ket{\psi'}$.

\section{Formalization scope}

The formal development in \texttt{TNLean/MPS/ParentHamiltonian/PhysicalDeformation.lean}
and Chapter~13 of the blueprint formalizes this correspondence specifically for
one-dimensional matrix product states on a periodic chain with $N$ sites,
establishing
\begin{align}\label{eq:cpgsv21_physical_deformation_general_graph_ground_space}
    \mathcal G_{N,L}(\Lambda A) & = \Lambda^{\otimes N}\,\mathcal G_{N,L}(A)
\end{align}
and $\ker H_N(\Lambda A) = \Lambda^{\otimes N}\ker H_N(A)$ for an invertible matrix
$\Lambda\in\mathrm{M}_d(\C)$ in the finite-chain range $0 < N$ and $L\le N$.\footnote{Formal
    declaration: \leanid{MPSTensor.ker_parentHamiltonian_rotatePhysical}.} The general-graph
formulation of Section~IV.C of Ref.~\cite{Cirac2021Matrix} is not formalized.

\section{Elimination plan}

Closing the scope restriction requires formalizing tensor network states and their
parent Hamiltonians on an arbitrary finite graph $G=(V,E)$, with interaction terms
supported on neighbourhoods $\partial e$ of edges $e\in E$. For an invertible on-site
operator $\Lambda$, the corresponding graph-level physical deformation
$\Lambda^{\otimes V}$ must be shown to transform each local interaction term according
to~(\ref{eq:cpgsv21_physical_deformation_general_graph_interaction}) and to map the kernel
intersection $\bigcap_{e\in E} \ker h_e$ onto $\bigcap_{e\in E} \ker h'_e$. The present
periodic-chain theorem follows by specializing the general-graph statement to a cycle
graph $C_N$ with contiguous edge windows of length $L$.
