\section{The source statement}

Let $\mathcal U$ be a matrix product unitary with physical dimension $d$ and
bond dimension $D$.  After blocking $J$ sites, let $\mathcal W'$ denote the
physical-adjoint double layer of the blocked local tensor.  The decomposition
called \texttt{WIsom} in the source is
\begin{align}
    \mathcal W'&=E'\otimes\operatorname{Id}
      +\sum_\alpha S'_\alpha\otimes\sigma_\alpha,
\end{align}
where $E'$ and $S'_\alpha$ act on the doubled virtual space and every physical
matrix $\sigma_\alpha$ is traceless.  Thus $S'_\alpha$ denotes a residual
virtual coefficient.  Transfer stabilization chooses $J$ so that
$E'=|\rho)(\Phi|$, where $\rho$ and $\Phi$ are respectively the right and left
fixed vectors of the normalized transfer operator.

In the proof of Proposition~III.3(ii), Cirac--P\'erez-Garc\'ia--Schuch--
Verstraete state that the trace of every positive-length product of the
coefficients $E'$ and $S'_\alpha$ containing at least one residual coefficient
vanishes \cite[lines~405--409]{Cirac2017MPU}.  This includes the one-letter
identity
\begin{align}
    \operatorname{tr}(S'_\alpha)&=0.
\end{align}
For every $m\geq1$, the rank-one form of $E'$ then gives
\begin{align}
    E'S'_{\alpha_1}\cdots S'_{\alpha_m}E'&=0.
\end{align}

\section{The present scope restriction}

The predicate \leanid{MPOTensor.IsMPU} assumes closed-chain unitarity only at
lengths strictly greater than one.  Consequently,
\leanid{MPOTensor.IsMPU.trace_prod_mixedResidualFactor_doubleLayerTensor_eq_zero}
proves the mixed residual trace identity for $N>1$, but does not prove the
one-letter trace $\operatorname{tr}(S'_\alpha)=0$.

The rank-one sandwich for every nonempty residual word is nevertheless
available.  The theorem
\leanid{MPOTensor.IsMPU.normalizedDiagonal_mul_prod_residualSlice_mul_normalizedDiagonal_eq_zero}
adjoins the normalized diagonal as an extra factor, so the closed trace used in
its proof has length at least two.  Its blocked form,
\leanid{MPOTensor.IsMPU.blocked_normalizedDiagonal_sandwich_residual_prod_eq_zero},
combines physical blocking, explicit product-index reindexing, the mixed trace,
trace cyclicity, and the rank-one sandwich formula.

\section{Elimination plan}

To close the remaining source-faithfulness gap, strengthen the mathematical MPU
hypotheses with the length-one closed-chain identity or derive that identity
from a source-faithful canonical-form theorem, and then prove
$\operatorname{tr}(S'_\alpha)=0$ by the same physical coefficient extraction
used at lengths greater than one.  This obligation is tracked by
\href{https://github.com/LionSR/TNLean/issues/5807}{TNLean issue~\#5807}, a
child of the MPU blocking campaign.  Until that issue is resolved, the
all-positive-length mixed trace assertion remains unmarked in the blueprint;
only the $N>1$ theorem and the nonempty rank-one sandwich are marked as
formalized.
