\section*{Scope of the angular annular geometry}

The geometric part of Proposition 3.4 of the September 24, 2026
polynomial-PEPS manuscript uses the ambient maximum metric on the real
plane. The source is \texttt{02-information.tex}, lines 662--744, at
revision \texttt{adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a}.

The candidate supplies dyadic scales, the literal outer, intermediate and
final layers, their cumulative separation, actual translated grid cells,
a uniform finite count of the newly selected cells, containment of the old
group, and physical core buffers. The capped marked-point distance is
defined to equal $n$ when the marked set is empty. At depth zero the sole
layer is all of $X$. Every enlargement is performed in the ambient plane
before intersecting with the physical domain.

The finite-cover arguments use only the source diameter bound
$\operatorname{diam}_{\infty}(X)\le A_0n$ and the bound $|\mathcal V|\le m_0$.
For $R=\max\{A_0,2\}$, a sufficient cell bound is
\begin{align*}
 (m_0+1)\left(2\left\lceil\frac{R+3a'/2}{d}\right\rceil_{+}+3\right)^2.
\end{align*}
It is independent of the marked-point locations, the grid origin, the
scale, the shapes of the layers and the physical domain. No estimate for
the size of the old group is needed.

The following obligations remain open:
\begin{itemize}
\item Conditional mutual information of one cell, Lemma 3.2, tracked by
  \href{https://github.com/LionSR/TNLean/issues/9340}{issue 9340}.
\item The physical information reset, Lemma 3.3, tracked by
  \href{https://github.com/LionSR/TNLean/issues/9341}{issue 9341}.
\item Combining these analytic inputs with the cell additions and the
  reset at each scale to obtain the mutual-information invariant and
  Proposition 3.4, tracked by
  \href{https://github.com/LionSR/TNLean/issues/8766}{issue 8766}.
\end{itemize}
The accepted uniform numerical reset-width estimate is reused at
$c_*=a'/3$. It is not a proof of either analytic input.
