\section{The source proposition}

Proposition~3.4 of the September~24, 2026 polynomial-PEPS
manuscript~\cite{OpenAI2026PolynomialPEPS}
(\texttt{02-information.tex}, lines 662--744, at revision
\texttt{adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a}) establishes a multiscale
mutual-information bound for a planar region $X\subset\mathbb R^2$ of
$\ell_\infty$-diameter $\operatorname{diam}_{\infty}(X)\le A_0n$ at scale $n>0$
relative to a set $\mathcal V\subset\mathbb R^2$ of at most $m_0$ marked
points, with angular clearance parameter $a'>0$. Using the $\ell_\infty$-metric
on $\mathbb R^2$, the proof decomposes $X$ across dyadic scales according to
the capped distance $\min\{n,d_{\mathcal V}(y)\}$ to $\mathcal V$, covers each
scale by translated grid cells of side length $d$ times the dyadic scale, and
maintains a mutual-information invariant by alternating single-cell additions
(Lemma~3.2) with physical information resets across buffers of relative width
$c_*=a'/3$ (Lemma~3.3).

\section{What is proved}

Only the geometric and numerical parts of Proposition~3.4 are formalized so
far. The formalization defines the dyadic scales, the outer, intermediate, and
final annular layers at each scale, their cumulative $\ell_\infty$-separation,
the covering by translated grid cells, the containment of the previous scale's
group, and the physical core buffers. Every neighborhood enlargement is taken
in the ambient plane $\mathbb R^2$ before intersecting with the physical
lattice domain. When $\mathcal V=\emptyset$, the capped distance
$\min\{n,d_{\mathcal V}(y)\}$ is set equal to $n$, so at depth zero the sole
layer is all of $X$.

From the diameter bound $\operatorname{diam}_{\infty}(X)\le A_0n$ and the
marked-point bound $|\mathcal V|\le m_0$, with $R=\max\{A_0,2\}$, the number of
grid cells newly adjoined at a dyadic scale is bounded above by
\begin{align*}
 (m_0+1)\left(2\left\lceil\frac{R+3a'/2}{d}\right\rceil_{+}+3\right)^2.
\end{align*}
This bound is uniform in the marked-point locations, the grid origin, the
dyadic scale, the shapes of the layers, and the physical lattice domain, and
requires no separate bound on the cardinality of the previous scale's group.
The uniform numerical reset-width estimate is also proved at $c_*=a'/3$.

\section{Effect on the paper and what remains}

The formalized geometric decomposition matches the paper's construction
without extra hypotheses, but it establishes only the geometric and counting
steps of Proposition~3.4. The quantum information steps remain open:
\begin{itemize}
\item Conditional mutual information of one cell, Lemma 3.2, tracked by
  \href{https://github.com/LionSR/TNLean/issues/9340}{issue 9340}.
\item The physical information reset, Lemma 3.3, tracked by
  \href{https://github.com/LionSR/TNLean/issues/9341}{issue 9341}.
\item Combining these analytic inputs with the cell additions and the
  reset at each scale to obtain the mutual-information invariant and
  Proposition 3.4, tracked by
  \href{https://github.com/LionSR/TNLean/issues/8766}{issue 8766}.
\end{itemize}
