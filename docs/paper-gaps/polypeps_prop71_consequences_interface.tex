\section{The source proposition}

Proposition~7.1 of the polynomial PEPS approximation manuscript of
September~24, 2026~\cite{OpenAI2026PolynomialPEPS} (\path{06-geometry.tex},
lines~12--53, at revision \texttt{adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a})
asserts, for all sufficiently large $L$ and with constants uniform in the
Hamiltonian and in $L$, a protocol that distributes the ground vector $\Omega$
through the padded dyadic hierarchy. Its four items describe the parties and
the product initialization; the changes, their gates and the lifetime
participation of each party; the scales and addresses of the parties of a
gate; and the reference vectors, ending at $\Omega\otimes\Xi$.

\section{What is formalized}

The formal statement records only the consequences of the proposition that
the assembly of Section~8 uses. A protocol is a distributed construction in
the sense of Theorem~5.2 whose chronology is cut into changes, with a dyadic
attachment of its parties and a reference vector $r_t$ at every stage, with
$r_0$ equal to the initial product vector. The bounds are: at most $CL^2$
parties, each attached to a square of the padded hierarchy, at most $b$ to
any square; at most $CL^3$ changes, each with at most $b$ nonprivate gates of
at most $b$ parties, each gate a sum of at most $CL^k$ monomials with
absolute coefficient sum at most $CL^k$ and at most $r$ pair effects per
monomial; lifetime participation at most $b$; equal or adjacent scales and
addresses at distance at most $b$ times either side for two parties of a
gate; $\lVert W_tr_t-r_{t+1}\rVert\le CL^{-20}$; and
$r_n=\Omega\otimes\Xi$ with $1-CL^{-57}\le\lVert\Xi\rVert\le1$. From these
bounds the density estimate of Section~8.1, the separation hypothesis of
Lemma~8.2 and the lifetime count of $64G$ gates per block party are
proved.\footnote{The bounds are
\leanid{TNLean.PEPS.Approximation.DyadicProtocol.IsDistributionProtocol};
the existence statement is
\leanid{TNLean.PEPS.Approximation.LargeSizeDyadicProtocolConsequences}.}

\section{Omitted clauses}

The following clauses of the source are not part of the formal statement.
\begin{enumerate}
\item Every square of the hierarchy, including the root, carries a party
  (lines~64--67). The formal placement bounds the parties of each square
  from above only.
\item One party holds the main copy of $\Omega$, and every auxiliary copy is
  initialized privately at its designated party (lines~22--23). The formal
  initialization is an arbitrary product of normalized party vectors.
\item Each reference vector is formed from the main encoded frame and the
  auxiliary encoded frames, and all its factors have norm at most one
  (lines~39--41). The per-change error and the endpoint identities do not
  imply that the intermediate reference vectors have norm at most one.
\item Each additional party takes the scale and address of its repainting
  event (lines~36--37).
\item Theorem~5.2, item~3 (\path{04-compression.tex}, lines~143--147), also
  bounds the pair sources and the messages of each monomial before their
  expansion. Only pair effects are counted formally.
\item The source allows expansions accurate to any fixed inverse power of
  $L$. The formal statement fixes one instantiated contraction per change and
  bounds its reference error by $CL^{-20}$.
\end{enumerate}
None of these clauses enters the density estimate, the separation
hypothesis or the lifetime count. The bounded messages and the bounded
replacement slots that the compression step needs are derived separately,
from the message expansion and from the nonprivate gate count and arity.
The formal statement is therefore a weakening of Proposition~7.1, not the
proposition; the source proposition remains unformalized.

\section{Elimination}

Construct a richer certificate of the protocol that carries the omitted
clauses: the map from hierarchy squares to their parties, the registers of
the main and auxiliary copies of $\Omega$, the frame factorization of every
reference vector with its norm bounds, the placement of each additional party
at its repainting event, and the counts of pair sources and messages before
expansion, for every fixed accuracy. Prove Proposition~7.1 for that
certificate, and obtain the present statement by forgetting the extra data.
