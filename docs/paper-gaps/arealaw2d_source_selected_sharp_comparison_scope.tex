\section{Remaining restrictions}

The theorem assumes an original unit gapped ground vector $\Omega$, the
induced domain graph, the ambient depth of the nonempty target, and anchors
lying in their original admissible supports. These are physical input
hypotheses, not consequences of the comparator construction.

The source theorem explicitly assumes the transport smallness
$a\ell_{\rm dim}\le c_t$ and the geometric inequalities
$2r_0\le D$, $1\le D$ and $4D\le m$. The remaining eventual scale proof
must establish these from the literal rounded scales. The anchor
multiplicity, shell-size and clearance hypotheses are also explicit.

The explicit density budget still needs its rounded-scale power estimate;
it is not yet a bound by a prescribed negative power of $n$. Bounds on the
pre-gap and its maximum require the physical safe-box/prefix entropy input.
The final six-error power estimates, mutual-information limiting step and
original-state continuity transfer are not proved here. Consequently the
statement is not yet Proposition 9.6 or a complete physical scanner and
one-step bootstrap construction.
