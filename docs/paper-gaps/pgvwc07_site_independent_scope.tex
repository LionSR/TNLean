\section{Source theorem}

Theorem~3 of Ref.~\cite{PerezGarcia2007MPSReps} states that every
translation-invariant pure state on a finite periodic chain has an MPS
representation with site-independent matrices. Let $N>0$ be the ring length.
The source first applies successive Schmidt or singular value decompositions
to obtain a varying-bond open-boundary representation. It then embeds the
rectangular virtual spaces into a common square virtual space and averages the
padded chain over cyclic translations. If $D$ bounds the intermediate bond
dimensions, the resulting site-independent tensor has bond dimension
\begin{align}
    D_{\mathrm{site}}
        &= ND.
    \label{eq:pgvwc07_site_independent_scope_site_independent_bond_dimension}
\end{align}

\section{Formal boundary}

The structure \leanid{OBCChainTensor} records a supplied varying-bond
open-boundary representation. For a fixed positive length $N$, the theorem
\leanid{OBCChainTensor.pgvwc07_site_independent_matrices} proves that cyclic
averaging of the zero-padded representation preserves its coefficients when
those coefficients are translation invariant. Its existential form is
\leanid{OBCChainTensor.exists_pgvwc07_site_independent_tensor}.

These declarations formalize the square-padding and cyclic-averaging part of
Theorem~3 of Ref.~\cite{PerezGarcia2007MPSReps}. They do not construct an
\leanid{OBCChainTensor} from an arbitrary pure state. Purity is therefore not
a hypothesis of the formal theorem: the varying-bond representation whose
existence purity supplies in the source is already an input.

\section{Elimination plan}

Closing the scope restriction requires a finite-chain Schmidt-decomposition
theorem. Given an arbitrary pure state, that theorem must construct a
varying-bond open-boundary MPS whose endpoint bond dimensions are one. It must
also bound every intermediate bond dimension by a common value $D$, instantiate
\leanid{OBCChainTensor}, and show that translation invariance of the state
implies
\leanid{OBCChainTensor.IsTranslationInvariantState}.

Composing this missing existence theorem with the formal cyclic-averaging
existence theorem identified in the preceding section would give the
source-facing statement for every translation-invariant pure state, including
the bond-dimension bound
(\ref{eq:pgvwc07_site_independent_scope_site_independent_bond_dimension}).
Issue \href{https://github.com/LionSR/TNLean/issues/6086}{\#6086} tracks the
missing existence theorem.

\section{Verdict}

\textbf{Verdict: scope restriction.} The formal declarations prove the
fixed-length zero-padding and cyclic-averaging construction conditional on a
supplied varying-bond open-boundary representation. The Schmidt-decomposition
argument that produces this representation from an arbitrary pure state
remains open. The current result is therefore not the full statement of
Theorem~3 of Ref.~\cite{PerezGarcia2007MPSReps}.
