    \Large The Squared Error of Sequential Truncation
\end{center}
\gapnote{false-source}{resolved}

\section{The printed inequality}

Let $\psi$ be a unit vector on an open chain of $N$ sites. At each internal
cut $k$, let $\epsilon_k(D)$ be the sum of the eigenvalues beyond the $D$
largest eigenvalues of the reduced density matrix.
Schuch, Wolf, Verstraete and Cirac~\cite{Schuch2008EntropyScaling} assert
that there is a matrix product state $\phi_D$ of bond dimension at most $D$
with
\begin{align}
    \|\psi-\phi_D\|_2 &\le 2\sum_{k=1}^{N-1}\epsilon_k(D).
    \label{eq:swv07_truncation_printed}
\end{align}
The left-hand side is missing a square.\footnote{arXiv:0705.0292v2,
    \path{paper_v2.tex}, lines 239--245, equation label \path{eq:eps-upper}.}
The cited result of Verstraete and Cirac~\cite[Lemma~1]{VerstraeteCirac2006Faithfully}
has the squared norm on the left.\footnote{arXiv:cond-mat/0505140v6,
    \path{convexPRA2.tex}, first lemma.}

\section{A two-qubit counterexample}

Take $\psi=(\sqrt{15}/4)|00\rangle+(1/4)|11\rangle$ and $D=1$.
There is one internal cut, with reduced eigenvalues $15/16$ and $1/16$,
so $\epsilon_1(1)=1/16$. A bond-one open-boundary matrix product state is
a product vector, without a normalization requirement.
For unit one-qubit vectors $u,v$, the Cauchy--Schwarz inequality gives
$|\langle u\otimes v,\psi\rangle|\le\sqrt{15}/4$.
Optimizing its scalar coefficient therefore gives
\begin{align}
    \inf_{c\in\mathbb C}\|\psi-c(u\otimes v)\|_2^2
    &=1-|\langle u\otimes v,\psi\rangle|^2\ge\frac1{16}.
    \label{eq:swv07_truncation_product}
\end{align}
Equality is attained by the product vector $(\sqrt{15}/4)|00\rangle$.
Thus the best bond-one error is $1/4$, whereas
(\ref{eq:swv07_truncation_printed}) would require $1/4\le1/8$.
The counterexample also excludes normalized product approximants.

\section{The corrected bound and its formalization}

The squared-norm bound of Verstraete and Cirac is
$\|\psi-\phi_D\|_2^2\le2\sum_k\epsilon_k(D)$.
For the unnormalized vector $\Phi$ obtained by sequential projection onto
the leading eigenspaces, the formalization proves the stronger estimate
\begin{align}
    \|\psi-\Phi\|_2^2 &\le\sum_{k=1}^{N-1}\epsilon_k(D).
    \label{eq:swv07_truncation_corrected}
\end{align}
The same vector is an open-boundary matrix product state of bond dimension
at most $D$.\footnote{\leanid{TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.norm_sequentialTruncation_leadingEigenprojection_sub_sq_le}
    and \leanid{TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.isOpenMPS_sequentialTruncation_leadingEigenprojection}
    in \doclink{TNLean/MPS/AreaLaw/SequentialTruncation}.}
No normalization of $\Phi$ is asserted. The older estimate
$\|\psi-\Phi\|_2\le\sum_k\sqrt{\epsilon_k(D)}$ is weaker than
(\ref{eq:swv07_truncation_corrected}).
This note resolves the missing square; the deduction of efficient
approximation from a family of R\'enyi entropy bounds remains open in
\ghissue{9855}.
