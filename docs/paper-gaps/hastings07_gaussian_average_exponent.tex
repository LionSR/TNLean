\section{The source statement}

Lemma~1 of~\cite{Hastings2007AreaLaw} (arXiv:0705.2024v4, \path{law16.tex},
lines~246--267) asserts, for a Hamiltonian with a unique gapped ground state,
operators $O_B,O_L,O_R$ of norm at most one and the appropriate supports with
\begin{align}
    \Vert O_BO_LO_R-P_0\Vert & \le C_1(\xi)e^{-l/\xi'},
    \label{eq:hastings07_gaussian_average_exponent_source}
\end{align}
and the lemma is stated with the decay length $\xi'=6\xi$.

\section{The point at issue}

The final estimate of the proof (lines~903--907) bounds the error by a quantity
of the form
\begin{align}
    \mathcal O\!\left(\sqrt{l/\xi}\,\xi^2\,e^{-l/6\xi}\right),
    \label{eq:hastings07_gaussian_average_exponent_final}
\end{align}
a square-root prefactor $\sqrt{l}$ times the exponential $e^{-l/6\xi}$. The
prefactor $\sqrt{l}$ is \emph{not} bounded by a constant at the rate
$e^{-l/6\xi}$: the function $l\mapsto\sqrt{l}\,e^{-l/6\xi}$ grows away from the
origin before it decays, so no constant $C_1(\xi)$ makes
\eqref{eq:hastings07_gaussian_average_exponent_source} hold with $\xi'=6\xi$ for
all $l$. The printed decay length $\xi'=6\xi$ is therefore not attained by the
bound the proof produces.

\section{The statement proved}

The prefactor \emph{can} be absorbed into any strictly slower exponential. For
$\xi'>6\xi$ set
\begin{align}
    \delta & = \frac1{6\xi}-\frac1{\xi'}>0.
\end{align}
Then $e^{-l/6\xi}=e^{-l/\xi'}e^{-\delta l}$, and the elementary inequality
$x\,e^{-x}\le e^{-1}$ at $x=2\delta l$ gives, for every $l\ge0$,
\begin{align}
    \sqrt{l}\,e^{-\delta l} & \le \frac1{\sqrt{2e\delta}},
    \label{eq:hastings07_gaussian_average_exponent_sup}
\end{align}
the supremum being attained at $l=1/(2\delta)$. Consequently, from a
factorization with error $(a+b\sqrt{l})e^{-l/6\xi}$ (the shape
of~\eqref{eq:hastings07_gaussian_average_exponent_final}) one obtains, for every
$\xi'>6\xi$,
\begin{align}
    \Vert O_BO_LO_R-P_0\Vert & \le C_1\,e^{-l/\xi'},
    \qquad
    C_1 = a+\frac{b}{\sqrt{2e\delta}},
    \label{eq:hastings07_gaussian_average_exponent_proved}
\end{align}
with $C_1$ depending on $\xi'-6\xi$ and diverging as $\xi'\downarrow6\xi$.%

\section{Conclusion}

Lemma~1 holds in the form~\eqref{eq:hastings07_gaussian_average_exponent_source}
for every decay length $\xi'>6\xi$, with a constant $C_1$ that grows as $\xi'$
approaches $6\xi$. It does \emph{not} hold at the printed boundary value
$\xi'=6\xi$, because of the $\sqrt{l}$ prefactor of the final estimate. The
correction loses only an arbitrarily small amount of decay length and does not
affect the area law, whose bound keeps its form with adjusted constants.
