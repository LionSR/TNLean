\section{The source statement}

In the appendix of~\cite{Hastings2007AreaLaw} the Hamiltonian $H$ of a chain
with nearest-neighbour interactions has a unique ground state $\Psi_0$, ground
energy zero and gap $\Delta E$. Let $v$ and $\xi_C$ be the velocity and the
length scale of its Lieb--Robinson bound, and
$\xi=\max(2v/\Delta E,\xi_C)$. The integer $l$ is the half-width of the
window about the cut on which the approximate projector is built.\footnote{\path{References/0705.2024/law16.tex},
    lines~96--112, 126--142 and~766--767.}
With $q=(l/3)\Delta E/(2v)$, the operator
\begin{align}
    P_q=\frac{\Delta E}{\sqrt{2\pi q}}\int e^{iHt}e^{-(t\Delta E)^2/2q}\,dt
    \label{eq:hastings07_gaussian_average}
\end{align}
is said to satisfy
\begin{align}
    \|P_q-P_0\| & \le O\bigl(e^{-l/3\xi}\bigr),
    \label{eq:hastings07_gaussian_average_source}
\end{align}
where $P_0$ is the ground-state projector.\footnote{\path{References/0705.2024/law16.tex},
    lines~792 and~843--848.}

\section{The point at issue}

The operator (\ref{eq:hastings07_gaussian_average}) is diagonal in an eigenbasis
of $H$, with eigenvalue $e^{-qE^2/2\Delta E^2}$ at energy $E$. It is $1$ on the
ground state, and on the excited states, where $E\ge\Delta E$, it is at most
$e^{-q/2}$, with equality on an eigenvector of energy exactly $\Delta E$.
Hence
\begin{align}
    \|P_q-P_0\| & \le e^{-q/2},
    \label{eq:hastings07_gaussian_average_corrected}
\end{align}
and this is sharp when $\Delta E$ is attained. Since $\xi\ge2v/\Delta E$, the
choice of $q$ gives $q=l\Delta E/(6v)\ge l/(3\xi)$, so
(\ref{eq:hastings07_gaussian_average_corrected}) yields
$\|P_q-P_0\|\le e^{-l/6\xi}$. The exponent $l/3\xi$ printed in
(\ref{eq:hastings07_gaussian_average_source}) is twice the one that follows,
and with $\xi=2v/\Delta E$ and $\Delta E$ attained it is false.

\section{The corrected statement}

For every real $q$, the bound (\ref{eq:hastings07_gaussian_average_corrected})
holds, with the Gaussian of variance $\max(q/\Delta E^2,0)$ in place of the
normalized density.\footnote{\leanid{TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.Hastings.norm_gaussianTimeAverage_sub_groundProjector_le}
    in \doclink{TNLean/MPS/AreaLaw/Hastings/GaussianTimeAverage}.}
With the source's choice of $q$ it gives $O(e^{-l/6\xi})$.

\section{Propagation and repair}

The same factor enters elsewhere. The estimates \texttt{tighter} and
\texttt{tighter2} on $|\tilde H^0_L\Psi_0|$, $|\tilde H^0_B\Psi_0|$ and
$|\tilde H^0_R\Psi_0|$ get their factor $e^{-l/3\xi}$ from the same Gaussian
multiplier $e^{-q/2}$ on the excited states, so with the printed $q$ they also
give only $e^{-l/6\xi}$.\footnote{\path{References/0705.2024/law16.tex},
    lines~796--814.}
The spectral cut-off $O_L$ projects onto eigenvalues of $M_L$ at most
$\theta=\Delta E^{-1}J^2e^{-l/6\xi}$, and the estimate \texttt{obd} rests on
$|(1-O_L)\Psi_0|\le|M_L\Psi_0|/\theta$.\footnote{\path{References/0705.2024/law16.tex},
    lines~829--840.}
With $|M_L\Psi_0|$ of order $e^{-l/6\xi}$ this ratio is only $O(1)$, so the
margin of \texttt{obd}, and the estimates after it, are affected in the same
way.

With $q=(l/3)\Delta E/v$ the rate $e^{-l/3\xi}$ of line~848 is recovered,
since then $e^{-q/2}\le e^{-l/3\xi}$, and so are the rates of \texttt{tighter},
\texttt{tighter2} and the margin of \texttt{obd}. The Gaussian truncation
exponent $(T\Delta E)^2/2q$ halves and the factor $\sqrt q$ grows by $\sqrt2$,
which changes only constants. The final bound of the appendix,
\begin{align}
    \|O_BO_LO_R-P_0\| & \le O\bigl(\sqrt{l/\xi}\,\xi^2e^{-l/6\xi}\bigr),
    \label{eq:hastings07_gaussian_average_final}
\end{align}
keeps the polynomial prefactor $\sqrt{l/\xi}$, which comes from the factor
$\sqrt q$.\footnote{\path{References/0705.2024/law16.tex}, lines~903--907.}
It therefore gives $\|O_BO_LO_R-P_0\|\le C_1e^{-l/\xi'}$ for every
$\xi'>6\xi$, with $C_1$ depending on $\xi'-6\xi$. This argument does not
establish the printed endpoint $\xi'=6\xi$ of line~142; it does not
show that the endpoint factorization is false.\footnote{\path{References/0705.2024/law16.tex},
    lines~141--142.}
Indeed, with $\delta=1/(6\xi)-1/\xi'>0$,
\begin{align}
    \sup_{l>0}\sqrt l\,e^{-\delta l} & =(2e\delta)^{-1/2}
    =O\bigl((1/6\xi-1/\xi')^{-1/2}\bigr),
    \label{eq:hastings07_gaussian_average_prefactor}
\end{align}
the supremum being attained at $l=1/(2\delta)$, while no constant independent of
$l$ absorbs $\sqrt l$ at $\delta=0$. Lemma~1 is therefore stated with
$6\xi<\xi'$ at a fixed length. The printed endpoint is reached instead at the
length $\tfrac54\hat\xi$ obtained by raising the velocity of the chain
Lieb--Robinson estimate, which the source allows because it fixes $\xi$ only
through some Lieb--Robinson pair; that length carries the same restriction on
the pair as $\hat\xi$; see \path{hastings07_lemma1_decay_length.tex}. The printed claim (\ref{eq:hastings07_gaussian_average_source})
is false for the printed $q$, while the corrected statement follows from the
source's own estimates, with the larger $q$ and any decay length $\xi'>6\xi$.

This is the rate of the product $O_BO_LO_R$ as constructed in the appendix.
The note added after it observes that $O_B$ need not be positive, that the
argument of the paper goes through without positivity, and that $O_B$ may be
replaced by a positive operator at the cost $\epsilon\mapsto O(\sqrt\epsilon)$,
which halves the decay rate again.\footnote{\path{References/0705.2024/law16.tex},
    lines~912--943.}
That positive version is treated where Lemma~1 is assembled.

The Lean statements of the Gaussian time average keep $q$ free and are
unaffected.
