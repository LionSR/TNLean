\section{The source lemma}

Lemma~6.3 of the polynomial PEPS approximation manuscript of September~24,
2026 (\path{05-frames.tex}, lines~188--219) considers old and new one-sheet
encoded frames that agree outside boundedly many affected holes, and $m$
additional square patches satisfying four conditions: (i) the patch outer
squares and the affected holes avoid the outer footprints of the untouched
holes; (ii) the patch inner squares cover every site whose raw owner changes
and the whole outer footprint of every affected hole; (iii) in each patch
outer square, the sites outside the old inner holes have at most two old
owners, and the sites outside the new inner holes have at most two new owners;
(iv) all involved raw owners and affected tag owners belong to a specified
bounded list of parties. The lemma asserts:
\begin{enumerate}
\item the canonical map $M=K_{\rm new}P_m\cdots P_1K_{\rm old}^{\dagger}$ is a
  contraction with
  $\|M\Omega_{\mathcal F_{\rm old}}-\Omega_{\mathcal F_{\rm new}}\|
   \le(m+r_{\rm old})\varepsilon$;
\item for every fixed $a>0$, $M$ has a contractive approximation $M_a$ with
  $\|M_a-M\|\le L^{-a}$ and a polynomial expansion into monomials allowed by
  Theorem~5.2, using only the specified parties.
\end{enumerate}

\section{What the formal statement covers}

The first assertion is formalized with exactly the source hypotheses, for
frames whose tags are listed in any order; the reference error uses only
condition~(i).\footnote{%
\leanid{TNLean.PEPS.EncodedFrame.SmallPatchRewrite.norm_rewrite_le_one_and_refVec_sub_le},
\leanid{TNLean.PEPS.EncodedFrame.SmallPatchRewrite.norm_rewrite_refVec_sub_le}, and, for
arbitrary tag orderings,
\leanid{TNLean.PEPS.EncodedFrame.SmallPatchRewrite.exists_norm_le_one_and_refVec_sub_le_of_perm}.}
For the second assertion the following steps of the proof
(\path{05-frames.tex}, lines~254--341) are formalized: the exact expansion of
$M$ into complete branches and the count of branches; the site-level wire
classification of every branch, for every choice of cylinder terms, including
unequal radii, holes and patches with empty outer sample (whose identity term
selects the empty square), and overlapping patches and holes; the summation of
branchwise errors; the choice of the truncation rank $k$; and the final
rescaling by $1+\tfrac12L^{-a}$.

The residual network of a branch is also formalized. For a branch $(a,s,b)$,
the raw operator $B_aC_sA_b^\dagger$ is an ordered product of rank-one
operators $\ket{k}\bra{b}_S\otimes\mathbb{1}$: the encodings $\ket0\bra{u'_r}$ of the
affected new holes, the patch terms $\ket{w_i}\bra{w_i}$, and the decodings
$\ket{u_t}\bra0$ of the affected old holes. Every such product is the
contraction of the network whose vertices are the bras and the kets of the
factors, kept distinct, and whose wires join the output of a ket at a site to
the next bra containing that site; open input legs are those of bras at sites
reached by no earlier ket, open output legs those of kets at sites met by no
later bra, the legs of the product zero vectors are fixed to $0$, and sites in
no factor are passed through.\footnote{%
\leanid{TNLean.PEPS.SiteChain.chainOp_apply}.}
No wire joins a vertex to itself and every vertex tensor is normalized, so
Lemma~6.2 applies with one group per vertex. An operator read off from a tensor
on the open-leg groups is that tensor, reshaped as a matrix, tensored with the
identity on the untouched sites, so its operator norm is at most the
Hilbert--Schmidt norm of the tensor, with no factor depending on the site
dimensions.\footnote{%
\leanid{TNLean.PEPS.SiteChain.norm_approxOp_le},
\leanid{TNLean.PEPS.SiteChain.exists_chainOp_truncation}.}
Under the four conditions only the patch bras and kets have open legs, so there
are at most $g=2m$ groups; the open inputs of a patch bra have at most two old
owners and the open outputs of a patch ket at most two new owners, all in the
specified list. Hence every branch is approximated within $2m/\sqrt k$ by at most
$k^{2m}$ product terms with coefficients of modulus at most one, and for every
$\delta>0$ (for instance $\delta=L^{-a}$) the rescaled sum
$M_\delta=(1+\tfrac12\delta)^{-1}\sum_\beta\ket a\bra b\otimes
\bigl(\sum_ic_{\beta,i}T_{\beta,i}\bigr)\otimes\mathbb{1}$ is a contraction with
$\|M_\delta-M\|\le\delta$.\footnote{%
\leanid{TNLean.PEPS.EncodedFrame.SmallPatchRewrite.exists_branch_truncation},
\leanid{TNLean.PEPS.EncodedFrame.SmallPatchRewrite.exists_contractive_approx}.}
Each product term $T_{\beta,i}$ is an explicit tensor product of normalized
vectors on the open output groups of the patch kets, normalized covectors on the
open input groups of the patch bras, product zero vectors on the selected hole
squares, and identities.

The last reading of the proof (lines~306--316), that each product term
together with the tag basis vectors is the operator of a monomial allowed by
Theorem~5.2, is also formalized. The registers of a frame are listed as the
source has them, one raw register per site and one register per tag, each held
by its owner, and their tensor product is identified with the canonical
coordinates in which the rewrite acts.\footnote{%
\leanid{TNLean.PEPS.EncodedFrame.layoutRegs},
\leanid{TNLean.PEPS.EncodedFrame.layoutIso},
\leanid{TNLean.PEPS.EncodedFrame.layoutIso_layoutVec}.}
For a branch $(a,s,b)$ and one unit vector on every open-leg group, the product
term in the tag block, $\ket a\bra b\otimes T\otimes\mathbb{1}$, is a scalar of
modulus at most one times the operator of the following monomial from the
registers of $\mathcal F_{\rm old}$ to those of $\mathcal F_{\rm new}$:
\begin{enumerate}
\item on the open input legs of each patch bra, the normalized covector of the
  term, contracted at the at most two old owners of these legs (a normalized
  pair effect, or a private map when there is one owner), the registers then
  being prepared in $\ket0$ at the same owners;
\item at every site of a selected patch or hole square, the bra $\bra0$ at the
  old owner and the ket $\ket0$ at the new owner; this imposes the zero bras of
  the old-hole decoders and transfers the reset raw register to its new owner;
\item on the open output legs of each patch ket, the bra $\bra0$ at the at most
  two new owners followed by the normalized vector of the term (a normalized pair
  source, or a private map);
\item the bra $\bra b$ on the affected old tags and the ket $\ket a$ on the
  affected new tags, each at its tag owner.
\end{enumerate}
Every other register is untouched; a site in no selected square keeps its owner
by condition~(ii). Each group block is placed on the registers of the frame by
exchanges of tensor factors and unitaries grouping the registers of each owner.
The monomial uses only the specified parties and has at most $2m$ normalized
pair sources and at most $2m$ normalized pair effects, one of each per patch
vertex with open legs; a vertex without open legs contributes a scalar of
modulus at most one.\footnote{%
\leanid{TNLean.PEPS.EncodedFrame.exists_rankOneBlock_chain},
\leanid{TNLean.PEPS.SiteChain.termOp_eq_smul_prod},
\leanid{TNLean.PEPS.EncodedFrame.SmallPatchRewrite.exists_termChain}.}
Hence $M_\delta$ is a sum of allowed monomials on the registers of the frames,
with coefficients of modulus at most one.\footnote{%
\leanid{TNLean.PEPS.EncodedFrame.SmallPatchRewrite.exists_allowed_monomial_approx}.}

The polynomial count is formalized as well. The formal statement gives at most
$Nk^{2m}$ monomials with $k=\lceil(4Nm/\delta)^2\rceil+1$, where $N$ is the
number of branches; when every affected hole and every patch has at most $D$
cylinder terms, $N\le D^{\,r_{\rm new}+m+r_{\rm old}}$ and the count is at most
$D^{E}k'^{2m}$ with $E=r_{\rm new}+m+r_{\rm old}$ and
$k'=\lceil(4D^{E}m/\delta)^2\rceil+1$.\footnote{%
\leanid{TNLean.PEPS.EncodedFrame.SmallPatchRewrite.card_branch_le},
\leanid{TNLean.PEPS.EncodedFrame.SmallPatchRewrite.exists_allowed_monomial_approx_of_card_le}.}
The source's bound $N\le L^{C_1}$ (lines~331--332) and its total $Nk^{g_0}$
(lines~341--342) use two standing bounds of the setting: (a) the projectors of
every hole and every patch are sums of $\sum_jd_j$ cylinder projectors, with
$\sum_jd_j$ bounded by one fixed power of $L$
(\path{05-frames.tex}, lines~34--46, applied to the patch projectors at
lines~254--256); and (b) $m$, $r_{\rm old}$ and $r_{\rm new}$ are bounded, by
the hypotheses of the lemma (lines~189--192), and so is the specified list of
parties of condition~(iv) (line~204). With $\sum_jd_j\le CL^{c}$,
$m,r_{\rm old},r_{\rm new}\le b$, at most $b$ specified parties, $L\ge1$ and
$\delta=L^{-a}$, the formal count
is at most
\[
  (C+1)^{3b}\bigl(16b^2(C+1)^{6b}+1\bigr)^{2b}\,
  L^{3bc+4b(3bc+\lceil a\rceil)},
\]
a polynomial in $L$ whose coefficient and degree depend only on $C$, $c$, $b$
and $a$, and the participating parties of all monomials lie in one fixed list
of at most $b$ parties, as the bounded change of Theorem~5.2 requires.\footnote{%
\leanid{TNLean.PEPS.EncodedFrame.SmallPatchRewrite.exists_allowed_monomial_approx_polynomial}.}
The bound on the participating parties cannot be derived from
conditions~(i)--(iii) alone. Every site of a selected hole square, including
the sites of the inner hole, receives a zero bra at its old owner and a zero
ket at its new owner (lines~258--268), and condition~(iii) restricts the owners
only outside the inner holes; an inner hole of side of order $\log L$ may have
of order $\log^2L$ distinct raw owners. The bound is the boundedness of the
list in condition~(iv), which the formal statement carries as the hypothesis
that the list has at most $b$ members.
Bound (a) is a choice made in the source's setting, so it enters the formal
statement as a hypothesis on the cylinder data of the rewrite; that the choice
can be made is Proposition~4.1, which is a separate result.

Two differences in form remain, and neither changes the statement. Each formal
monomial has at most $2m$ normalized pair sources and $2m$ normalized pair
effects, one of each per patch vertex with open legs, since the zero side of a
two-owner block is also prepared and contracted as a pair vector; the source
counts one per open-leg group, and Theorem~5.2 asks only for a bounded number.
The expansion into monomials is stated in the canonical coordinates in which
the tags of the untouched holes come first in both frames, as the source
compares frames after the canonical identification of tag orderings
(lines~74--75 and~84--85).

The formal frames record the pairwise disjointness of the physical outer
footprints of holes rather than of the outer squares in the plane; the source
condition implies the formal one, so this is a generalization. The rewrite is
constructed in canonical coordinates in which the tags of the untouched holes
come first in both frames. Since the encodings of holes with disjoint
footprints commute, moving every tag to the new position of its hole is a
relabelling of the tag basis that carries one encoding to the other; along it
the canonical rewrite becomes a contraction between frames with arbitrary tag
orderings, with the same reference error. This is the canonical identification
of tag orderings of Definition~6.1, and it is formalized.

\section{Resolution}

Both assertions of Lemma~6.3 are formalized with the source's hypotheses and
the standing bounds of its setting. The scope restriction recorded in earlier
versions of this note, first the reading of the product terms as allowed
monomials and then the polynomial count, is removed.
