\section{The source statement}

Lemma~1 of~\cite{Hastings2007AreaLaw} (arXiv:0705.2024v4, \path{law16.tex},
lines~246--267) asserts, for a Hamiltonian with a unique gapped ground state
$\Psi_0$ and $P_0=|\Psi_0\rangle\langle\Psi_0|$, Hermitian positive definite
operators $O_B,O_L,O_R$ of norm at most one, supported on the $2l$ sites
around the cut, on the sites before it and on the sites after it, with
\begin{align}
    \Vert O_BO_LO_R-P_0\Vert & \le C_1(\xi)e^{-l/\xi'}=\epsilon(l).
    \label{eq:hastings07_lemma1_positivity_rate_source}
\end{align}

\section{The point at issue}

The note added to the source (lines~912--943) observes that the construction
in the appendix does not make $O_B$ positive. The operators $O_L$ and $O_R$ it
builds are commuting orthogonal projections, and $O_B$ is a contraction
satisfying~\eqref{eq:hastings07_lemma1_positivity_rate_source}. The note
replaces $O_B$ by $O_B^+=O_B^\dagger O_B$, which is positive semidefinite, has
norm at most one and the same support, and shows
$\Vert O_B^+O_LO_R-P_0\Vert\le\mathcal O(\sqrt{\epsilon(l)})$. So the positive
version holds at the rate $e^{-l/(2\xi')}$, not at the rate $e^{-l/\xi'}$
printed in the lemma.

\section{The statement proved}

With the explicit bound
$\sqrt{1-(1-\epsilon)^2}+3\epsilon+\epsilon^2\le(\sqrt2+4)\sqrt\epsilon$ for
$0\le\epsilon\le1$, and the trivial bound $2$ for $\epsilon>1$,
\begin{align}
    \Vert O_B^+O_LO_R-P_0\Vert & \le(\sqrt2+4)\sqrt{C_1}\,e^{-l/(2\xi')}.
    \label{eq:hastings07_lemma1_positivity_rate_proved}
\end{align}
The decay length doubles and the constant becomes $(\sqrt2+4)\sqrt{C_1}$.%

    in \path{TNLean/MPS/AreaLaw/Hastings/PositiveFactorization.lean}.}

\section{An enlarged source length}

The loss of a factor of two concerns a fixed decay length. The source
chooses its Lieb--Robinson pair existentially, so a larger admissible
length can absorb this loss. Let $\zeta$ be the length of the established
factorization with a constant polynomial in $\zeta$. Its positive form
satisfies
\begin{align}
    \|O_BO_LO_R-P_0\|\le K\zeta^3e^{-l/(12\zeta)},
    \label{eq:hastings07_lemma1_positivity_rate_fixed_length}
\end{align}
with $K$ depending only on the range. For every $s\ge2\zeta$, the same
operators satisfy
\begin{align}
    \|O_BO_LO_R-P_0\|\le Ks^3e^{-l/(6s)}.
    \label{eq:hastings07_lemma1_positivity_rate_enlarged_length}
\end{align}
Indeed $\zeta^3\le s^3$ and $12\zeta\le6s$, so both the coefficient and
the exponential on the right increase.

With $c_R=2(R+1)2^{2R+1}e^{2R}$, the pair
$v=40c_RJ$, $\xi_C=80$ has numerical prefactor $4$, arbitrary finite
observable supports, and the reciprocal-length commutator decay of
\path{hastings07_lemma1_decay_length.tex}. Its source length
$s=\max\{2v/\Delta E,80\}$ is at least
$2\zeta=40(1+c_RJ/\Delta E)$. Thus
(\ref{eq:hastings07_lemma1_positivity_rate_enlarged_length}) has the source
rate $\xi'=6s$, for a new admissible length. It is proved by
\leanid{TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.exists_projectorFactorization_posSemidef_source}.
The contraction form at the same length and rate is
\leanid{TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.exists_projectorFactorization_source}.

\section{Conclusion}

At a fixed length the correction changes the decay length of the positive
version of Lemma~1 from $\xi'$ to $2\xi'$. Choosing the enlarged source
length above restores the common rate $\xi'=6s$; it does not strengthen
the fixed-length repair or make its operators positive definite.
The note added also shows that the rest of the proof
of the area law needs no positivity of $O_B$, so the bound of the main
theorem keeps its form, and only its constants change if the positive version
is used.
