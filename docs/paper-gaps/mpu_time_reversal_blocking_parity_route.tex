\section{The source statement and proof}

Proposition~\texttt{prop:sigma1-and-sigma2-equal} of Ref.~\cite{Cirac2017MPU}
(source \path{Papers/1703.09188/paper_v2.tex}, lines 1643--1655) states: ``Let
$\mathcal U$ be a time reversal invariant MPU $\mathcal U$ in SF described by
$u$, $v$, and let the blocked MPU $\mathcal U_k$ be described by $u^{(k)}$,
$v^{(k)}$. Denote by $\sigma^{(k)}$ the phase obtained from $u^{(k)}$ and
$v^{(k)}$ in Eq.~(UUdaggertrick-2). Then $\sigma^{(k)}=\sigma^{(1)}$ if $k$ is
odd and $\sigma^{(k)}=\sigma^{(2)}$ if $k$ is even.''

The proof (lines 1657--1732) begins: ``Because of
Prop.~\texttt{prop:sigma-well-defined}, we can choose the representations
$u^{(k)}$, $v^{(k)}$ at will.'' It then chooses
\begin{align}
    u^{(k)} & =(\Id\otimes v^{\otimes(k-1)}\otimes\Id)u^{\otimes k}, &
    v^{(k)} & =\Id^{\otimes(k-1)}\otimes v\otimes\Id^{\otimes(k-1)},
\end{align}
derives the relation
$\zeta[(\Id\otimes v\otimes\Id)(u\otimes x)]^\dagger
    =(\Id\otimes v\otimes\Id)(x^\dagger\otimes u)$ with
$\zeta=\sigma^{(1)}\sigma^{(2)}$, and unwinds it across the block to obtain
$\sigma^{(k)}=\sigma^{(1)}\zeta^{k-1}$.

\section{The formal route}

The statement is formalized with the source's hypotheses and conclusion, for
arbitrary supplied standard forms of the blocked tensors, as the statement
allows. Only the proof differs.
\begin{enumerate}
    \item The sign of any standard form with gates $u,v$ of a two-site tensor
          $W$ equals an intrinsic quantity of its periodic operators: the
          normalized trace of the exchange of the two sites against $W^{(2)}$
          (\leanid{MPOTensor.TwoSiteStandardFormData.timeReversalSign_eq_intrinsic}).
          This is the content of Proposition~\texttt{prop:sigma-well-defined}
          used at line 1657.
    \item The intrinsic trace is unchanged by a sitewise relabelling of the
          physical index. Hence $\sigma^{(k)}$ is the intrinsic trace of the
          direct blocking of $\mathcal U$ by $2k$ sites.
    \item The intrinsic trace of the $k$-fold blocking of an MPU whose
          operators are self-adjoint on every ring of even length is constant on
          odd $k$ and constant on even $k$
          (\leanid{MPOTensor.IsMPU.intrinsic_blocking_trace_parity}). It is
          applied to the two-site block $\mathcal U_2$, whose rings of even
          length are the rings on which time reversal is imposed (lines
          1201--1252, read in two-site blocks as in
          \path{docs/paper-gaps/mpu_standard_form_parity_gap.tex}).
\end{enumerate}
The unwinding relation and the explicit gates above are not constructed. The
result is \leanid{MPOTensor.TwoSiteStandardFormData.timeReversalSign_blocking_parity}.
It does not assert $\sigma^{(1)}=\sigma^{(2)}$.

\section{Status}

Resolved. The proposition is formalized with the source's hypotheses; the
deviation concerns only the proof route. The explicit blocked gates of the
source proof for general $k$ remain unconstructed; they are a choice within
the source proof and are not needed for the statement.
