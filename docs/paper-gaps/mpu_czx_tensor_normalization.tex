\section{The 2017 contraction convention and its obstruction}
In arXiv:1703.09188, equation \texttt{coefficients}
(\texttt{paper\_v2.tex}, lines 226--246) defines the periodic contraction
as an ordinary trace, with no length-dependent prefactor. Section III,
lines 318--350, requires the resulting operator to be unitary and separately
normalizes its transfer matrix by $1/d$. That transfer normalization does
not rescale the physical operator.

Write $u,d,\ell,r\in\{0,1\}$ for upper, lower, left and right indices,
$X_{ud}=\delta_{u,1-d}$, and $H_{\ell r}=2^{-1/2}(-1)^{\ell r}$.
Example \texttt{example:czx}, lines 1914--1930, prints
\[
 P^{ud}_{\ell r}=X_{ud}\delta_{u,\ell}H_{\ell r}.
\]
For binary output and input configurations $a,b$ of length $n>0$, with
cyclic indices $a_n=a_0$, the copy deltas force the unique virtual path
$\ell_j=a_j$. Consequently
\[
 \operatorname{MPO}_n(P)_{a,b}
 =2^{-n/2}\prod_{j=0}^{n-1}\delta_{a_j,1-b_j}
       (-1)^{\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}a_ja_{j+1}}.
\]
Every row and column has one nonzero entry, of modulus $2^{-n/2}$, so
$\operatorname{MPO}_n(P)\operatorname{MPO}_n(P)^\dagger=2^{-n}I$,
not $I$. At $n=2$ the row indexed by $00$ has only the entry
$\operatorname{MPO}_2(P)_{00,11}=1/2$; its squared norm is $1/4$.
Blocking $L>0$ sites replaces $n$ by $Ln$ and cannot remove this obstruction.
This is a nondegenerate normalization error, not a zero-sector convention.

\section{What the 2025 diagram actually says}
In arXiv:2502.20257, \texttt{main.tex}, equations \texttt{eq:CZ} and
\texttt{eq:deltas} (lines 3795--3851), the blue two-leg tensor is explicitly
$W=\sqrt{2}H$, with entries $W_{\ell r}=(-1)^{\ell r}$; the wire junctions
are ordinary copy deltas. Equation \texttt{eq:MPU\_CZX}
(lines 4503--4547) has two such blue tensors, two lower physical $X$
gates and two upper physical $Z$ gates. None may be dropped.

For comparison only, let $A=\sqrt{2}P$. Reading the two physical legs
left to right, let their outputs be $a,b$ and inputs $c,d$.
The ordinary two-site block of $A$ is
\[
 (A_2)^{ab,cd}_{\ell r}
 =\delta_{a,1-c}\delta_{b,1-d}\delta_{\ell,a}(-1)^{ab+br}.
\]
Since $Z_{aa}=(-1)^a$, the displayed 2025 tensor is instead
\[
 B^{ab,cd}_{\ell r}
 =\delta_{a,1-c}\delta_{b,1-d}\delta_{\ell,a}(-1)^{a+b+ab+br}
 =(-1)^{a+b}(A_2)^{ab,cd}_{\ell r}.
\]
Thus $B=(Z\otimes Z)A_2$ on the output physical leg, equivalently the
block of the one-site tensor with entries $(-1)^u A^{ud}_{\ell r}$.
It is neither $P_2$ nor $A_2$ in these coordinates. For example, at
$(a,b,c,d,\ell,r)=(0,1,1,0,0,0)$, their entries are respectively
$B=-1$, $P_2=1/2$, $A_2=1$.
On a ring of $m$ blocked sites the additional factor is
$(-1)^{\sum_{j=0}^{2m-1}a_j}$, which is not a constant phase.
At $m=2$, compare all-zero output with output $0100$, each against its
bitwise-complement input. This also excludes an identification merely by
periodic virtual gauge transformation or a global scalar.

The left-to-right pair $(a,b)$ is encoded as $2a+b$, consistent with
\leanid{MPOTensor.CZX.siteBits}; successive pairs give the four-bit order
of \leanid{MPOTensor.CZX.localBits}. No new gate or bit encoding is needed.
The formulas for $A$ and $B$ above are an informal source audit, not new
public tensor definitions or a formal proof of their correspondence.

\section{Checked boundary and elimination plan}
\leanid{MPOTensor.CZX.printed2017Tensor} records only the literal printed
2017 witness. Theorems
\leanid{MPOTensor.CZX.printed2017Tensor\_two\_zero\_row} and
\leanid{MPOTensor.CZX.printed2017Tensor\_not\_isMPU} formally prove the
two-site row formula and the failure of the MPU predicate.
The all-length contraction and the 2025 formulas above are mathematical
derivations, not claims of additional checked Lean theorems.

Issue \#7738 must not be closed as though its requested exact identity
were proved. Its source identification needs correction: expose one
shared, Z-decorated, unnormalized tensor for the 2025 target, use the
existing blocking equivalence and bit ordering, and prove its all-length
monomial formula and unitarity. Any connection to the 2017 gate pair
$u=CZ(X\otimes X), v=CZ$ must explicitly specify the normalization and
physical change of representative, rather than assert literal equality.
No classification signs, action data, defect tensors, or completion
certificates are supplied here. The two original CZX blueprint claims
remain unformalized.
