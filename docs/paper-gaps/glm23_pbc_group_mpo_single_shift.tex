\gapnote{local-correction}{resolved}

Let $G$ be a finite group and $\omega$ a normalized three-cocycle of $G$
with values in $U(1)$. On $\mathbb{C}[G]\otimes\mathbb{C}[G]$ write
\begin{align}
    W_g|k,l\rangle
        &=\omega(g,l,l^{-1}k)|k,l\rangle,
    \qquad
    L_g|k\rangle=|gk\rangle .
    \label{eq:glm23_gate_shift}
\end{align}
The periodic construction of Ref.~\cite{GarreRubio2023MPOSym}, in the
subsubsection on periodic boundary conditions, prints
\begin{align}
    U_g
        &=\bigotimes_i \bigl(L_g^{i}\otimes L_g^{i+1}\bigr)\cdot W_g^{i,i+1}
    \label{eq:glm23_printed_display}
\end{align}
on a ring of sites $i$.\footnote{Source traceability:
\path{Papers/2203.12563/REsubmission.tex}, lines 2068, 2070 and
2204--2222.}
Read literally, every site $i$ receives one factor $L_g$ from the pair
$(i-1,i)$ and one from the pair $(i,i+1)$, so the product acts on each
site by $L_g^2=L_{g^2}$. For $G=\mathbb{Z}_2$ this would give
$U_g=\prod_i CZ_{i,i+1}Z_i$ without the spin flips, contradicting the
printed $\mathbb{Z}_2$ example $U_g=\prod CZ_{i,i+1}Z_i\prod X_i$ on
line 2224.

The pair $L_g^{i}\otimes L_g^{i+1}$ descends from the arbitrary-boundary
operator
$O_g=\bigotimes_i (L_g^{[i]_l}\otimes L_g^{[i]_r})\cdot
W_g^{[i]_l,[i]_r}\cdot\mathcal{P}^{[i]_r,[i+1]_l}$ of line 2094, where the
two factors act on the two halves of one site. The isometry
$\Gamma:|g,g\rangle_{[i]_r,[i+1]_l}\mapsto|g\rangle_i$ merges the halves
$[i]_r$ and $[i+1]_l$ into one site, on which the two shifts act jointly
as a single $L_g$. The tensor $\hat T_g$ drawn on lines 2208--2222 carries
exactly one $L_g$ per site. The intended operator is therefore
\begin{align}
    U_g
        &=\Bigl(\bigotimes_i L_g^{i}\Bigr)\cdot\prod_i W_g^{i,i+1},
    \label{eq:glm23_corrected_display}
\end{align}
with entries
$\langle s|U_g|t\rangle=\prod_i\delta_{s_i,gt_i}\,
\omega(g,t_{i+1},t_{i+1}^{-1}t_i)$.
With this reading $U_gU_h=U_{gh}$ follows from the cocycle identity, whose
correction factors
$\omega(g,h,t_{i+1})/\omega(g,h,t_i)$ telescope around the ring, and
$U_e=\mathrm{id}$ from normalization.\footnote{Formal declarations:
\leanid{MPOTensor.GroupCocycle.mpo\_tensor\_apply\_eq\_leftShift\_wGate},
\leanid{MPOTensor.GroupCocycle.mpo\_tensor\_mul},
\leanid{MPOTensor.GroupCocycle.mpo\_tensor\_one} and
\leanid{MPOTensor.GroupCocycle.mpo\_tensor\_cyclicTwo} in
\doclink{TNLean/MPS/MPU/GroupCocycleMPO} and
\doclink{TNLean/MPS/MPU/GroupCocycleMPO/Instances}.}

\textbf{Verdict: resolved local fix.}
The printed tensor and the printed $\mathbb{Z}_2$ example both fix the
reading (\ref{eq:glm23_corrected_display}).
