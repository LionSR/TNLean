\section{What the source states}
Let $A$ be a normal tensor of physical dimension $d$ and bond dimension $D$, and
let $\ket{\phi_N}$ be the normalized translation-invariant state it generates on
a ring of $N$ sites. The paragraph ``Connection to MERA''
of~\cite{Malz2024Preparation} reads the tree circuit of eq.~(16) as a
finite-range multiscale entanglement renormalization ansatz with
$O(\log\log N)$ layers: the isometries $V^{(i)}$ of eq.~(16) are the isometries
of the network, every disentangler is the identity except those of the first
layer, which prepare the fixed-point state $\ket\Omega$, and hence, within the
approximation error $\varepsilon$, normal translation-invariant matrix product
states are such networks with $O(\log\log N)$ layers.

The paragraph ``The tree-RG circuit'' of the source describes the layers as
isometries from dimension $D^2$ to $D^4$, except the lowest, and its picture
starts from a blocked tensor. A layer $V^{(i)}$ is the partial isometry of the
polar decomposition of a two-site blocked map; it is an isometry exactly when
that map is injective. For $d^2<D^2$ the lowest layer, a map from $\mathbb{C}^{D^2}$ to
$(\mathbb{C}^d)^{\otimes2}$, cannot be an isometry at all.

\section{The restrictions formerly adopted}
The first formal statements\footnote{Formal declarations:
    \leanid{MPSPreparation.approximatingMPVState_eq_state_treeMERA},
    \leanid{MPSPreparation.exists_state_treeMERA_approximationError_le}, and
    \leanid{MPSPreparation.exists_state_treeMERA_approximationError_le_and_le_logb}
    in \doclink{TNLean/MPS/Preparation/TreeMERA}.}
assume two things.
\begin{enumerate}
    \item The two-site blocked tensor of $A$ is injective. Then every layer
          $V^{(i)}$ is an isometry: blocking the two-site blocked tensor
          $V^{(1)}T_1$ twice gives $(V^{(1)})^{\otimes2}$ applied to $T_1$
          blocked twice, so the two-site blocked tensor of $T_1$ is injective,
          and so on up the tree.
    \item The chain length is $N=M2^{k+1}$, so that the $M$ blocks of the
          approximating state of eq.~(10) all have $q=2^{k+1}$ sites and the
          tree of each block is complete.
\end{enumerate}
Under these assumptions the approximating state of eq.~(10) is exactly the state
of the network with $k+1$ isometry layers, and its error against $\ket{\phi_N}$
is at most $\varepsilon$ once $2^{k+1}\ge\xi\log(CN/\varepsilon)$, with
$\xi=-1/\log t$ for a bound $0<t<1$ on the moduli of the subleading
eigenvalues of the transfer map and $C$ depending on $A$ and $t$. For every
number $M$ of blocks such a $k$ exists, and the least one satisfies
$k\le\log_2\max(1,\xi\log(CN/\varepsilon))$. These statements remain, as
the case of injective two-site blocked tensors.

\section{Resolution of the injectivity restriction}
The finest isometries of the network now write \emph{registers} of $s$ sites,
where $A$ blocked over $s$ sites is injective, as in the source's picture,
which starts from a blocked tensor.\footnote{Formal declarations:
    \leanid{MPSPreparation.BinaryMERA.registerState},
    \leanid{MPSPreparation.approximatingMPVState_eq_registerState_treeMERA},
    \leanid{MPSPreparation.exists_registerState_treeMERA_approximationError_le},
    \leanid{MPSPreparation.exists_registerState_treeMERA_approximationError_le_and_le_logb},
    and
    \leanid{MPSPreparation.exists_binaryMERA_registerState_approximationError_le}
    in \doclink{TNLean/MPS/Preparation/TreeMERARegisters}.}
Let $B$ be $A$ blocked over $s$ sites. The two-site blocked tensor of $B$ is
$A$ blocked over $2s$ sites, which is injective, so the network of $B$ is
defined, with finest isometry $\mathbb{C}^{D^2}\to\mathbb{C}^{d^s}\otimes\mathbb{C}^{d^s}$. Blocking $B$
over $2^{k+1}$ sites is blocking $A$ over $q=s2^{k+1}$ sites, read through the
grouping of the sites into registers, so the state of the network of $B$, read
on the $N=Ms2^{k+1}$ sites, is the approximating state of eq.~(10) of $A$ itself
with block length $q$. The approximation error of $A$ applies directly, so no
gauge data need to be transported to $B$: there is $C>0$, independent of $s$,
such that the error is at most $\varepsilon$ once
$s2^{k+1}\ge\xi\log(CN/\varepsilon)$, and the least such $k$ satisfies
$k\le\log_2\max(1,\xi\log(CN/\varepsilon))$.

For an arbitrary normal tensor $A$ with $D\ge1$, the gauge of eq.~(5) is
reached by a gauge transformation and a rescaling, which changes the
normalized state by a phase only, and the blocked tensors of the gauged tensor
are injective from some length $L$ on. Hence there are $s_0\ge1$, $\xi>0$ and
$C>0$ such that for every $s\ge s_0$, every $\varepsilon>0$ and every $M\ge1$
some network with sites of dimension $d^s$ and $k+1$ isometry layers,
$k\le\log_2\max(1,\xi\log(CN/\varepsilon))$, has error at most
$\varepsilon$ against $\ket{\phi_N}$, $N=Ms2^{k+1}$. Every normal tensor is
covered.

\section{The remaining restriction: the chain length}
The chain length is $N=Ms2^{k+1}$ with $k$ given by the threshold: the network
consists of $M$ complete binary trees on equal registers. The layer count holds
along the chain lengths so obtained, not for every fixed $N$ and $\varepsilon$
with registers of bounded length: for $N=2p$ with $p$ odd only $k=0$ is
available, so the threshold forces $s\ge\xi\log(CN/\varepsilon)/2$, and the
dimension $d^s$ of the sites of the network grows with $N/\varepsilon$.

\section{Elimination plan}
The unequal blocks of the depth bound for every chain length (the note on
divisible lengths) let the last block have length $q'$ with $q\le q'<2q$, and
the trees on leaves of unequal widths of the preparation with measurements (the
note on the tree circuit with measurements) cut, for every $h\ge0$, a block of
$\ell\ge2^{h+2}s$ sites into $2^{h+1}$ leaves of at least $2s$ sites. A network
whose trees have leaves of unequal widths, with finest isometries from
$\mathbb{C}^{D^2}$ to the sites of a leaf, would cover every $N$; it is not
translation invariant at the finest level, and it extends the definition of the
network. This is left open.
