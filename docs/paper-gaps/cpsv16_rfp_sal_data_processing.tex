\section{Source statement and normalization boundary}

Definition~4.1 of Cirac--P\'erez-Garc\'ia--Schuch--Verstraete defines a
mixed-state renormalization fixed point by trace-preserving completely positive
maps $\mathcal S$ and $\mathcal T$ satisfying\footnote{Source label
\texttt{RFPMixedTS},
\path{Papers/1606.00608/MPDO-22-12-17-2.tex:638--660}.}
\[
  \mathcal S[M_2(X)]=M_1(X),
  \qquad
  \mathcal T[M_1(X)]=M_2(X)
\]
for every virtual boundary operator $X$. Proposition \texttt{propsimple} in
Appendix~C states that this condition implies source zero correlation length
and saturation of the area law.\footnote{Source:
\path{Papers/1606.00608/MPDO-22-12-17-2.tex:1331--1341}.}

The entropic convention immediately preceding Definition~4.6 divides each
finite-chain operator by its trace.\footnote{Source:
\path{Papers/1606.00608/MPDO-22-12-17-2.tex:792--793}.}
The precise boundary of the proposition is therefore:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item $\rho^{(N)}(M)$ is positive semidefinite for every $N\geq1$;
  \item $\operatorname{tr}\rho^{(N)}(M)\neq0$ for every $N\geq1$;
  \item $M$ satisfies the local renormalization equations of Definition~4.1.
\end{enumerate}
Positivity alone does not imply the second condition: the zero tensor generates
zero positive semidefinite operators at every length. Thus the nonzero-trace
clause is the normalization boundary implicit in the source's density-operator
language, not a dispensable consequence of positivity.

\section{Area-law calculation}

For a bipartition of an $N$-site ring into consecutive intervals of lengths
$a+1$ and $b+2$, the localized maps satisfy
\[
  (\widehat{\mathcal T}_a\otimes\widehat{\mathcal S}_b)
  (\sigma^{(N)}_{a+1:b+2})
  =\sigma^{(N)}_{a+2:b+1},
\]
and the reverse pair satisfies
\[
  (\widehat{\mathcal S}_a\otimes\widehat{\mathcal T}_b)
  (\sigma^{(N)}_{a+2:b+1})
  =\sigma^{(N)}_{a+1:b+2}.
\]
These are identities of normalized density operators because the corresponding
ring trace is nonzero. Mutual-information data processing in the two directions
gives
\[
  I(\sigma^{(N)}_{a+2:b+1})
  \leq I(\sigma^{(N)}_{a+1:b+2})
  \leq I(\sigma^{(N)}_{a+2:b+1}).
\]
Hence the two mutual informations are equal. The consecutive-cut
identification turns this equality into $I_L=I_{L+1}$ for
$1\leq L<\lfloor N/2\rfloor$.

\leanid{MPOTensor.isSAL_of_isRFPViaTS_of_trace_ne_zero} in
\doclink{TNLean/MPS/MPDO/RFPViaTSSAL}. The channel identities and the
bipartite-to-chain identification are supplied by
\leanid{MPOTensor.tensorMapBoth_bipartitionedNormalizedMPO},
\leanid{MPOTensor.tensorMapBoth_bipartitionedNormalizedMPO_reverse}, and
\leanid{MPOTensor.mutualInfoChain_eq_mutualInformation}.

\section{Source zero correlation length}

Trace preservation in the one-to-two renormalization equation gives, for every
virtual matrix $X$,
\[
  \operatorname{tr}(\mathcal T_M^2X)
  =\operatorname{tr}(M_2(X))
  =\operatorname{tr}(M_1(X))
  =\operatorname{tr}(\mathcal T_MX).
\]
Nondegeneracy of the trace pairing yields
$\mathcal T_M^2=\mathcal T_M$. If $\mathcal T_M=0$, then
\[
  \operatorname{tr}\rho^{(1)}(M)
  =\operatorname{tr}(\mathcal T_M)=0,
\]
contradicting the positive-length nonvanishing hypothesis. Therefore
$\mathcal T_M\neq0$, and $M$ has source zero correlation length.

Combining this argument with the area-law theorem gives the primary declaration
\leanid{MPOTensor.isSourceZCL_and_isSAL_of_isRFPViaTS_of_trace_ne_zero} in
\doclink{TNLean/MPS/MPDO/RFPViaTSSAL}. It states exactly that positive
semidefinite rings, nonzero trace at every positive length, and the Definition
4.1 local renormalization equations imply source ZCL and SAL.

\section{Horizontal specialization}

The declarations
\leanid{MPOTensor.isSAL_of_isRFPViaTS} and
\leanid{MPOTensor.isSourceZCL_and_isSAL_of_isRFPViaTS} are specializations of
the two explicit-trace theorems. Their normalized BNT-refined horizontal
hypothesis is stronger than the density-family boundary above. It supplies
\[
  \operatorname{tr}\rho^{(N)}(M)\neq0
\]
for every $N\geq1$ through
\leanid{MPOTensor.trace_mpo_ne_zero_of_isHorizontalCF_isMPDO_isRFPViaTS}.
No horizontal decomposition is used in the mutual-information calculation
itself.

The literal source canonical-form construction permits an additional all-zero
virtual summand because it allows $\sum_kD_k\leq D$.\footnote{Source:
\path{Papers/1606.00608/MPDO-22-12-17-2.tex:214--219}.}
That observation no longer leaves Proposition \texttt{propsimple} open: the
proposition is stated and proved at its actual density-operator normalization
boundary, while horizontal form gives a stronger sufficient condition for that
boundary.

\paragraph{Classification: closed.}
Proposition \texttt{propsimple} is complete at the explicit nonzero-trace
density-family boundary. Theorem~4.9 remains partial for independent reasons:
the reverse and intermediate implications still include the commuting-form,
selected-tensor comparison, and false Lemma~C.5 obstructions. No simplicity
hypothesis is used in the completed forward implication.
