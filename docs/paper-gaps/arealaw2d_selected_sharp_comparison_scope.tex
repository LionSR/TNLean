\section{The source calculation}

The proof of the sublinear collar bound subtracts the sharp norm comparisons
at the charge stage and common interpolation parameter selected by the
scanner estimate. It then controls the actual defect energy, the maximum
physical entropy difference and the six final error terms.\footnote{
  \cite{OpenAI2026AreaLaw}, \path{08-scanner.tex}, lines 480--533 and 839--946,
  revision \texttt{adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a}.}
Every comparison uses the same truncated physical ground vector, typical
spectral set and auxiliary label sequence. The original ground vector still
centers the truncated Hamiltonian terms.

Fix the physical system and put $W=aK>0$. For the literal actual charge
tree with terminal weights $w_j$, write
\begin{align}
 G_{\rm pre}&=K^{-1}\sum_g\sum_jw_j g^{\rm pre}_{j,g},&
 G_{\rm post}&=K^{-1}\sum_g\sum_jw_j g^{\rm post}_{j,g}.
 \label{eq:selected_sharp_averages}
\end{align}
The terminal indexing contains every completed and pre-charge status.
Mapping the actual mean tree into this finite family preserves its weights
and physical partitions; it introduces no independent probability law.
For a terminal partition, let $Q$ be its near receiving region and $Y$ its
middle region, so the far receiving region is $(Q\cup Y)^c$. Let $U$ be
the positive-depth part of $Q$. All entropies below concern the same
truncated physical state, and
\[
 g^{\rm pre}=S(Q)+S((Q\cup Y)^c)-S(Y),\qquad
 g^{\rm post}=S(U)+S(U\cup Y)-S(Y).
\]
Here $g_{\max}$ is the maximum of zero and every $g^{\rm pre}_{j,g}$ in
the full finite terminal family, rather than only in a selected support.

At inverse power $1/4$ and metric parameter $a/2$, the upper comparison has
quadratic error $6K K_{\rm mom}a^2$. The lower rate has error
$25K_{\rm mom}a$. Dividing their difference by $W/2$ therefore gives exactly
$37K_{\rm mom}a$, not an unspecified change of normalization.

Let $S'$ be the entropy of the actual selected auxiliary marginal, $d$ its
enumerated dimension, $z>0$ the selected mass and
$M=\max\{0,2S'+g_{\max}\}$. For the actual normalized charge mean energy
$\mu$, truncated ground energy $e_0$ and truncated gap $g/2$, put
$\delta=(\mu-e_0)/(g/2)$. The derived comparison is
\begin{align}
2S'+G_{\rm pre}-G_{\rm post}
\le{}& \frac{2(2\log d-\log z)}{W}
 +\tau G_{\rm pre}+2\tau\log d
 +(3+1/a)h(\tau)+37K_{\rm mom}a-\frac{\log z}{a}
 \notag\\
&+\frac{\delta}{\tau}M
 +\frac{2R_+(k)}{W}+R_-(k),\qquad 0<\tau\le\tfrac12.
 \label{eq:selected_sharp_comparison}
\end{align}
The two remainders tend to zero for each fixed physical system and weight.
The lower one uses the same label sequence through
$\epsilon_k=2|\log\dim\lambda_k-kS'|$ and is
\begin{align}
R_-(k)={}&\frac{2|\log(k+1)+c\log(k+2)+a\epsilon_k|
 +|\log((k+2)^{-C_f}/2)|+\log2+\log(3/2)}{ak}.
 \label{eq:selected_sharp_lower_remainder}
\end{align}
No label is selected again. The original common moment hypotheses require
$10a\le r$ and $10a\le1$; these also imply the upper comparison's
$6a\le r,1$.\footnote{The actual uniform consumer is

\section{The same selected point and its actual energy}

The accepted actual averaging theorem selects a stage $s_k$ and
$p_k\in[p_0,p_1]\subset(0,1)$ from the literal endpoint log-norm difference
and transport entropy cost. The finite-family transport theorem provides
one nonnegative remainder $r_k\to0$ before the round, replica count, vector
and parameter. Applying its energy clause at exactly $s_k,p_k$ gives
\begin{align}
 \mu_k&\le2e_0+C_{\rm en}a^2\ell_{\dim}^{e_{\rm en}}
                 \operatorname{error}_k+r_k,\notag\\
 \delta_k&\le
 \frac{e_0+C_{\rm en}a^2\ell_{\dim}^{e_{\rm en}}
                 \operatorname{error}_k+r_k}{g/2}.
 \label{eq:selected_sharp_actual_energy}
\end{align}
The retained $e_0$ cannot be discarded: it remains after subtracting one
copy of $e_0$ from the transport bound. The denominator is the actual
truncated gap $g/2$, where
$g=\Delta/\operatorname{positiveNormalization}(1,\Delta/2,J)$.
No one-vector gap is asserted after adding auxiliary spectator factors.
Here $\ell_{\dim}=\operatorname{transportLogDimBound}(q,r_0)$.
It is distinct from the fixed scanner exponent
$\ell_{\rm sc}=1/100000$ used in the next display.

The actual energy error is nonnegative. Hence the accepted coefficient
estimate with $|C_{\rm en}|$ applies at that unchanged point. Its explicit
right side is
\begin{align}
\operatorname{cap}_k={}&CW^2n^{\ell_{\rm sc}}D^2(\log n)^{C_\ell}
 \left[(1-p_k)
  \left(\frac{\mathcal Q_k}{KnD}\right)^{1/8}

 \label{eq:selected_sharp_density_cap}
\end{align}
where $\mathcal Q_k$ is the literal actual charge entropy defect.
Consequently $\delta_k\le(e_0+\operatorname{cap}_k+r_k)/(g/2)$, and this
quantity can replace $\delta$ in \eqref{eq:selected_sharp_comparison}.
The selected stage, interpolation parameter, prevector and weights are

\section{What remains outside these consumers}

The uniform actual comparison derives both norm inequalities from the
original physical gap, status moments and the already chosen physical
prevector conclusions. It assumes neither norm inequality. The selected
consumer is a conditional composition taking the exact conclusions of that
derived comparison, the selector--transport composition and the accepted
coefficient theorem. Its three premises still require instantiation with
the same physical data; it does not perform that instantiation itself.

The following are still needed for Proposition 9.6: compose the source
target-window and factor-ten moment provider with these consumers for the
same vector; identify the same selected prevector's actual energy equation;
bound the endpoint log norms to obtain the required selected-density
decay; provide the safe-box and prefix entropy input that bounds
$G_{\rm pre}$ and $g_{\max}$; and prove the final six-error power estimates
at the literal rounded scales. The term $\tau G_{\rm pre}$ in
\eqref{eq:selected_sharp_comparison} remains explicit rather than silently
discarded. The already accepted selected-spectrum entropy estimates,
weighted mutual-information identity and continuity theorem can then be
applied. The present statements do not prove an unconditional sublinear
collar bound or a complete physical scanner construction.

All limits here fix the physical system, dimensions, $a$ and $\tau$ first.
The replica remainders are uniform in the selected stage and parameter,
which may vary with $k$. No dimension-independent bound on their
intermediate logarithmic coefficients is asserted.
