The native integer rectangle $Q$ has two nonempty integer intervals.
Its size $s$ is the maximum number of sites on an axis, so both thin
rectangles and singletons are retained.
The literal finite-history scanner has ambient target $T=Q\cap\mathbb Z^2$,
row scale $n=\lceil24s\rceil=24s$, the actual induced graph and cut,
ambient target depth, and one anchor chosen inside every original
nonempty interaction support.
Its window, separation, band count and radius are the original rounded
scales of the supplied scanner exponent tuple.
\footnote{Definition:
    \leanid{TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.rectangleBootstrapScanner}
    in \doclink{TNLean/PEPS/AreaLaw/Scan/RectangleBootstrapGeometry}.}

For a fixed nonnegative radius coefficient $C_r\geq0$, one threshold,
after the fixed exponents but
before all finite data and every $D_0\geq1$, gives the required target
cardinality, row counts and strict scanner clearance from the weak
safe-box assumption $\operatorname{dist}_\infty(Q,Z)>D_0s$.
The target-size coefficient is one.
The ambient layer estimate is $4(s+2r)\leq12s\leq n$ for
$1\leq r\leq L\leq s$ and does not depend on the rectangle's aspect
ratio.
The rounded-clearance theorem supplies $D_0L+L\leq D_0s$ and
$2L+10r_0\leq D_0s$.
The latter gives the strict scanner condition after composing with
weak safety; no $4D_0s$ template-clearance premise is introduced.
The standard support multiplicity is retained at every anchor.
\footnote{Declaration:
    \leanid{TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.exists_rectangleBootstrapScanner_geometry}
    in \doclink{TNLean/PEPS/AreaLaw/Scan/RectangleBootstrapGeometry}.}

The actual target is $A\cap Q$.
Every nonnegative positive-depth prefix is exactly
$A\cap(Q^{+j}\setminus Q)$, and every truncation set is exactly
$A\cap Q^{+L'}$.
These identities preserve the original physical region and vector
when the raw weak-rectangle shell entropy theorem is applied.
The physical target may be empty; no nonempty-intersection premise is
needed for this geometric construction.

This result derives the actual geometric inputs only.
It does not establish entropy transport, select a replica point,
prove the original-state entropy improvement, uniformize its entropy
coefficient, or take the safe-box supremum.
No full scanner theorem, Proposition~9.6 or area law is asserted.
