\section{The source definitions}
Sun considers translation-invariant density operators
\begin{align}
    \rho^{(L)}(\mathcal X,M) & =\sum_{i,j}\operatorname{tr}\bigl[\mathcal X
        M^{i_1j_1}\cdots M^{i_Lj_L}\bigr]\,\lvert i_1\cdots i_L\rangle\langle
    j_1\cdots j_L\rvert,
    \label{eq:sun25_mpdo}
\end{align}
where the boundary matrix $\mathcal X$ commutes with every $M^{ij}$, and calls
such a family strongly symmetric under matrix product operators
$O_a^{(L)}$ when $O_a^{(L)}\rho^{(L)}=\lambda_a^{(L)}\rho^{(L)}$ for all $a$
and $L$, and weakly symmetric when $[O_a^{(L)},\rho^{(L)}]=0$ for all $a$ and
$L$~\cite{Sun2025Anomalous}.\footnote{Source traceability:
    \path{References/2504.16985/main.tex}, lines 175--182.}
Every purification $\Psi$ of a strongly symmetric $\rho^{(L)}$ satisfies
$(O_a^{(L)}\otimes\mathbb 1_{\mathrm{anc}})\Psi=\lambda_a^{(L)}\Psi$.\footnote{Source
    traceability: lines 183--187.}

\section{What is formalized}
The operator family (\ref{eq:sun25_mpdo}) is formalized for an arbitrary
virtual boundary $\mathcal X$. The boundary matrices commuting with all
letters form their centralizer, a complex subalgebra. Positivity of the
resulting operators at every positive length is a separate condition:
commutation alone does not imply positivity.

The family predicates for strong and weak symmetry retain the boundary
$\mathcal X$. The eigenvalue $\lambda_a^{(L)}$ may depend on $L$ and need not
be a phase. Strong symmetry implies weak symmetry for a positive family
under the source's proviso that the adjoint of every operator belongs to
the family. Fusion and on-site character relations apply to the
boundary-weighted family wherever the operator is nonzero.

For any supplied purification
$\rho^{(L)}(\mathcal X,M)=\Phi_L\Phi_L^\dagger$, strong symmetry is equivalent
to $O_a^{(L)}\Phi_L=\lambda_a^{(L)}\Phi_L$. This is the source's purification
lemma for arbitrary commuting boundaries, and requires no factorization
of $\mathcal X$ on the virtual space. The vectorized-state identities
likewise retain $\mathcal X$ in the word traces and prove the equivalences
with the actions $U_g\otimes\overline{U_g}$ and $U_g\otimes\mathbb 1$.
The identity-boundary statements follow by setting $\mathcal X=\mathbb 1$.

\section{Local purification statements}
A commuting boundary on a doubled virtual space need not factor as
$Y\otimes\overline Y$, nor does it automatically define a positive family.
The existing local-purifying-tensor sufficient conditions and the converse
characterization using a normal periodic purification therefore remain
explicit identity-boundary results. They are not arbitrary-boundary local
gauge characterizations. This does not restrict the family definitions,
the general global-purification lemma, or the boundary-weighted
vectorization identities.
