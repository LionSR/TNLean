\section{The source definitions}
Sun considers translation-invariant density operators
\begin{align}
    \rho^{(L)}(\mathcal X,M) & =\sum_{i,j}\operatorname{tr}\bigl[\mathcal X
    M^{i_1j_1}\cdots M^{i_Lj_L}\bigr]\,\lvert i_1\cdots i_L\rangle\langle
    j_1\cdots j_L\rvert,
    \label{eq:sun25_mpdo}
\end{align}
where the boundary matrix $\mathcal X$ commutes with every $M^{ij}$, and calls
such a family strongly symmetric under matrix product operators
$O_a^{(L)}$ when $O_a^{(L)}\rho^{(L)}=\lambda_a^{(L)}\rho^{(L)}$ for all $a$
and $L$, and weakly symmetric when $[O_a^{(L)},\rho^{(L)}]=0$ for all $a$ and
$L$~\cite{Sun2025Anomalous}.\footnote{Source traceability:
    \path{References/2504.16985/main.tex}, lines 175--182.}
Every purification $\Psi$ of a strongly symmetric $\rho^{(L)}$ satisfies
$(O_a^{(L)}\otimes\mathbb 1_{\mathrm{anc}})\Psi=\lambda_a^{(L)}\Psi$.\footnote{Source
    traceability: lines 183--187.}

\section{What is formalized}
The formal predicates are stated for the periodic operators generated by $M$,
which is (\ref{eq:sun25_mpdo}) with $\mathcal X=\mathbb 1$. The eigenvalue
$\lambda_a^{(L)}$ may depend on $L$ and is not required to be a phase. The
purification statement is formalized for an arbitrary factorization
$\rho=\Phi\Phi^\dagger$ and, for a locally purified tensor, for the
spin-ancilla matrix product state. The strong-to-weak implication is
formalized under the source's proviso that the adjoint of each operator
belongs to the family.\footnote{Formal definitions:
    \leanid{MPOTensor.IsStrongMPOSymmetry},
    \leanid{MPOTensor.IsWeakMPOSymmetry}. Results:
    \leanid{Matrix.mul_mul_conjTranspose_eq_smul_iff},
    \leanid{MPOTensor.isStrongMPOSymmetry_iff_purification},
    \leanid{MPOTensor.IsStrongMPOSymmetry.isWeakMPOSymmetry}.}

\section{Boundary}
Not formalized: a general commuting boundary $\mathcal X$. The single-length
statements (strong implies weak, the purification form, unit modulus of a
unitary eigenvalue) are proved for arbitrary matrices and therefore apply to
$\rho^{(L)}(\mathcal X,M)$ unchanged; only the family predicates fix
$\mathcal X=\mathbb 1$.

Elimination plan: define the boundary-weighted density operators of
(\ref{eq:sun25_mpdo}) together with the arbitrary-boundary operator algebras,
and restate the family predicates over them.
