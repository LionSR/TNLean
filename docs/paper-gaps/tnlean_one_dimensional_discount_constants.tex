\section{The source statement}

Lemma~4.2 of the one-dimensional note~\cite{TNLean2026OneDimensional}
(label \texttt{lem:buffer}, file \path{part1_arealaw.tex}, lines~405--409)
is the buffered discount. For an interval $X$ of the chain with the shells
$X_j=X^{+jR}$, $1\le j\le m$, the flanks $T_j=X_j\setminus X$ and the uniform
weights $a_j=2/m$, the note fixes the constants (equation \texttt{eq:CE},
lines~366--373)
\begin{align}
    C_E
     & = 8\,N_R\,J\,q^{2(R+1)},
    \label{eq:tnlean_one_dimensional_discount_constants_source_energy_constant}                \\
    m
     & = \Bigl\lceil\max\Bigl\{4,\ \frac{4C_E}{\Delta},\ 128\sqrt{(1+\vartheta)\mathcal B_R},\
    4096\,e\,(1+\vartheta)\,\mathcal B_R\Bigr\}\Bigr\rceil,
    \label{eq:tnlean_one_dimensional_discount_constants_source_shell_count}
\end{align}
with $\vartheta=J/\Delta$, notes $m\le C_1(q,R)(1+\vartheta)$
(equation \texttt{eq:mbound}, lines~374--378), and concludes
(equation \texttt{eq:discount})
\begin{align}
    S(X\,|\,T_m)
     & \le \tfrac12\,S(X)+\tfrac12+\frac{C_E}{\Delta}.
    \label{eq:tnlean_one_dimensional_discount_constants_source_discount}
\end{align}
In the proof, the energy of the filtered vector is bounded by
$\sum_j N_R\cdot 2a_j^2q^{2(R+1)}J=C_E/m$ (equation \texttt{eq:energy},
lines~517--522), so that by the second entry of $m$ it is at most $\Delta/4$
and the overlap defect $1-|\langle\Omega,\phi\rangle|^2\le C_E/(m\Delta)$ is
at most $1/4$ (equation \texttt{eq:overlap}, lines~523--526); the overlap
losses of the $m$ prefixes are then summed with $-\frac12\log(1-v)\le v$ to
$C_E/\Delta$, and the tail term
$512\,e\,\vartheta\,\mathcal B_R\sum_ja_j^2=2048\,e\,\vartheta\,\mathcal B_R/m$
is bounded by $\frac12$ through the fourth entry of $m$ (lines~560--577,
equation \texttt{eq:lowerN}). The closing step (equation \texttt{eq:final},
lines~668--679) then gives the interval bound
$C_{\mathrm{int}}=8mR\log q+1+2C_E/\Delta\le C_0(q,R)(1+J/\Delta)$ with
$C_0(q,R)=8C_1(q,R)R\log q+1+16N_Rq^{2(R+1)}$.

\section{What is proved and the deviations}

The formalization proves the discount on the chain for every interval
$X=[l,r)$ with the buffer $T=X^{+mR}\setminus X$, as an instance of the
finite-dimensional discount of the companion library,\footnote{Declarations:
    \leanid{TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.chainQuadraticEnergyThreshold},
    \leanid{TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.chainLogEnergyThreshold},
    \leanid{TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.chainEnergyThreshold},
    \leanid{TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.chainTailThreshold},
    \leanid{TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.chainShellCount},
    \leanid{TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.chainDiscountConst},
    \leanid{TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.two_mul_condEntropy_interval_le}; the
    finite-dimensional lemmas are \leanid{Entropy.two_mul_condEntropy_le}
    and \leanid{Entropy.two_mul_condEntropy_le_logarithmic} of QICLean.} in the form
\begin{align}
    2\bigl(S(X\cup T)-S(T)\bigr)
     & \le S(X)+\frac{4096\,e\,\vartheta\,\mathcal B_R}{m}+m\log2,
    \label{eq:tnlean_one_dimensional_discount_constants_formal_discount}                                  \\
    m
     & = \Bigl\lceil\max\Bigl\{4,\ E_R\vartheta,\ 128\sqrt{(1+\vartheta)\mathcal B_R}\Bigr\}\Bigr\rceil ,
    \label{eq:tnlean_one_dimensional_discount_constants_formal_shell_count}                               \\
    E_R
     & = \min\bigl\{32N_Rq^{2(R+1)},\ 64\,e\,N_R\,\ell_R^2\bigr\},
    \qquad \ell_R=\log(e\,q^{R+1}).
    \label{eq:tnlean_one_dimensional_discount_constants_formal_energy_constant}
\end{align}
The first entry of $E_R$ is $4C_E/J$, the note's second entry of $m$ divided
by $\vartheta$. The second comes from the logarithmic energy estimate of
Remark~6.3 of the note (label \texttt{rem:sharper}, \texttt{part1\_arealaw.tex},
lines~711--734): the three-lines device bounds the energy change of one split
interaction by $8e\,a_j^2\log^2(e\dim\mathcal H_Y)\,J$, so the energy of the
filtered vector is at most $32\,e\,N_R\ell_R^2J/m$. Taking the smaller entry
is never worse than the note's choice, and is smaller once $q^{R+1}$ is
moderately large.
The shells, the flanks, the weights, the crossing count $N_R$ and the cut
budget $\mathcal B_R$ are those of the note. The constants deviate from the
printed ones in three connected places, each at the level of an absolute
constant.

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \emph{Energy threshold $\Delta/2$ in place of $\Delta/4$.} The
          finite-dimensional lemma bounds the energy of the filtered vector by
          $\sum_j4a_j^2\sum_{Y\text{ split by }X_j}(\dim\mathcal H_Y)^2J$, that is by
          $16N_Rq^{2(R+1)}J/m=2C_E/m$, twice the printed estimate $C_E/m$. With the
          same second entry $m\ge4C_E/\Delta$ of $m$ this is at most $\Delta/2$, so the
          discount runs at the energy threshold $\Delta/2$, and the overlap defect of
          every prefix is at most $1/2$ rather than $1/4$. When the logarithmic entry
          of $E_R$ is the smaller one, the logarithmic estimate
          $32\,e\,N_R\ell_R^2J/m$ is at most $\Delta/2$ at the same threshold.
    \item \emph{Overlap losses $m\log2$ in place of $2C_E/\Delta$.} The
          finite-dimensional lemma keeps the overlap losses of the $m$ prefixes as
          $-m\log(1-E/\Delta)$ with $E$ the energy threshold; at $E=\Delta/2$ this is
          $m\log2$. The note instead uses $-\frac12\log(1-v)\le v$ at
          $v=C_E/(m\Delta)\le\frac14$ and sums to $C_E/\Delta$ in $\log N$, that is
          $2C_E/\Delta$ in the discount. When $E_R$ is the quadratic entry,
          $m\ge4C_E/\Delta$ and the formal term $m\log2$ dominates the printed
          $2C_E/\Delta$; in either case both are at most a constant times
          $1+\vartheta$.
    \item \emph{The tail term stays explicit and the fourth entry of $m$ is
              omitted.} The error terms of the tail bound sum to
          $\sum_j1024\,e\,\vartheta\,\mathcal B_ja_j^2\le4096\,e\,\vartheta\,\mathcal B_R/m$
          in the discount, which is twice the printed $2048\,e\,\vartheta\,\mathcal B_R/m$
          of $\log N$. The note bounds this by $1$ (that is, $\frac12$ in $\log N$)
          through the fourth entry $m\ge4096\,e\,(1+\vartheta)\mathcal B_R$; the
          formalization keeps the term $4096\,e\,\vartheta\,\mathcal B_R/m$ in the
          discount constant and omits the fourth entry from $m$, so its $m$ is at most
          the printed one.
\end{enumerate}

Consequently the formal interval bound of Theorem~A
is\footnote{Declarations:
    \leanid{TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.chainIntervalEntropyBound},
    \leanid{TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.chainShellConst},
    \leanid{TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.chainAreaLawConst},
    \leanid{TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.chainIntervalEntropyBound_le}.}
$C_{\mathrm{int}}=8mR\log q+4096\,e\,\vartheta\,\mathcal B_R/m+m\log2$ in
place of the printed $8mR\log q+1+2C_E/\Delta$, with the explicit
$C_1(q,R)=5+E_R+128\mathcal B_R$ in \texttt{eq:mbound} and
$C_0(q,R)=(8R\log q+\log2)\,C_1(q,R)+1024\,e\,\mathcal B_R$ in place of the
printed $C_0$. Every deviation is a change of absolute constants; the
structure of the argument, its hypotheses, and the linear dependence of the
area-law constant on $1+J/\Delta$ are those of the note.

\section{Elimination plan}

None of the deviations needs a formal change: the formal constants are valid
in place of the printed ones, and the printed ones are not sharper in any
respect that the note uses. A revision of the note may adopt the formal
constants (energy threshold $\Delta/2$, overlap losses $m\log2$, the tail
term kept explicit with the three-entry $m$), or the formalization may be
tightened to the printed ones by proving the finite-dimensional discount with
the sharper energy coefficient $2a_j^2$ and the inequality
$-\frac12\log(1-v)\le v$ for the overlap losses and by adding the fourth
entry to $m$. Until then the deviations are recorded on the module docstring
of \texttt{TNLean/MPS/AreaLaw/BufferDiscount.lean}, in the blueprint statements of
the buffered discount and of Theorem~A, which print the formal constants, and
in the status table of \path{docs/formalization/one-dimensional-chain.md}.

\section{The original polynomial constants}

The discount also holds with the original constants
(\ref{eq:tnlean_one_dimensional_discount_constants_source_energy_constant})--
(\ref{eq:tnlean_one_dimensional_discount_constants_source_shell_count}).
For the chain $I=[0,L)\cap\mathbb Z$ and natural endpoints $l,r$,
put $X=[l,r)\cap I$ and use
$T=([l-mR,r+mR)\cap I)\setminus X$, with truncated subtraction
at the left endpoint. This includes arbitrary endpoints and empty intervals.
For the weights $a_j=2/m$, the quadratic energy comparison with coefficient
$2$ gives an energy bound $E=C_E/m\le\Delta/4$.
The fourth entry of $m$ bounds the marginal error by one, and
$-m\log(1-E/\Delta)\le2C_E/\Delta$ bounds the prefix loss.
Thus the original discount
(\ref{eq:tnlean_one_dimensional_discount_constants_source_discount})
holds with its printed buffer and additive constant.\footnote{Declarations:
    \leanid{Entropy.two_mul_condEntropy_le_quadratic} and
    \leanid{TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.two_mul_condEntropy_interval_le_polynomial}.}
These are the constants of the one-dimensional note~\cite{TNLean2026OneDimensional},
\path{part1_arealaw.tex}, lines~366--409 and~517--645.

The Araki--Lieb argument gives the original interval bound
$C_{\mathrm{int}}=8mR\log q+1+2C_E/\Delta$.
Put $u_0=(32\sqrt{(1+\vartheta)\mathcal B_R})^{-1}$.
The same marginal-tail estimate and sequential truncation then give a unit,
nonzero matrix product state with error at most $1/L$ and bond dimension at most
$2e^{C_{\mathrm{int}}}(32e^{e/2}L^4)^{1/u_0}$ for $L\ge2$.
The accuracy-dependent extension gives error at most $\varepsilon$ for
$L\ge1$ and $0<\varepsilon\le1$; the printed statement is recovered at
$\varepsilon=1/L$.\footnote{Declarations:
    \leanid{TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.regionEntropy_interval_le_polynomial},
    \leanid{TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.exists_isOpenMPS_norm_sub_le_of_error_polynomial}, and
    \leanid{TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.exists_isOpenMPS_norm_sub_le_polynomial}.}
These are the original interval constant and quantitative approximation in
\path{part1_arealaw.tex}, lines~674--679, and
\path{part2a_mps_information.tex}, lines~73--92.
The polynomial quantities are local expressions in these statements.
The chain statements of the previous section use the formal constants
instead; no numerical ordering between the two choices is asserted.

\section{Full consecutive windows}

For interactions whose every nonzero designated support is a full window of
$R+1$ consecutive sites contained in the chain, at most $2R$ terms cross an
interval and the cut budget is at most $B_{\mathrm{win}}=1+2R\log^2(e q^{R+1})$.
The constants $C_1$ and $C_0$ above, with $N_R$ replaced by $2R$ and
$\mathcal B_R$ by $B_{\mathrm{win}}$, then bound the entropy of every set of
sites.\footnote{Declarations:
    \leanid{TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.two_mul_condEntropy_interval_le_of_family_budget}
    and \leanid{TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.chainAreaLaw_of_consecutive_terms}.}

There is a separate specialization to interactions whose every nonzero
designated support is a full window of $R+1$ consecutive sites contained
in the chain. Only in that specialization may one replace $B$ by
$B_{\mathrm{win}}=1+2R\log^2(e q^{R+1})$, as in
Remark~6.3 of the one-dimensional note~\cite{TNLean2026OneDimensional}
(\path{part1_arealaw.tex}, lines~706--709). Shorter boundary supports must
have zero terms; separately norm-bounded terms are not combined at fixed
$J$. The same argument then gives the absolute polynomial-range bound of
that remark~\cite{TNLean2026OneDimensional}
(\path{part1_arealaw.tex}, lines~736--748). Range zero and local dimension one have zero
entropy separately. The finite-domain entropy reduction and the
contiguous-chain restrictions on the Part~II estimates are unchanged.

The full-window polynomial-range bound also holds on every finite
$\Lambda\subset\mathbb Z$, with the same absolute constant chosen before
$q,R,J,\Delta,\Lambda$ and all Hamiltonian, ground-vector and region data.
For every finite $A\subseteq\mathbb Z$, the bound is
$S_\Omega(A\cap\Lambda)\le C_{\mathrm{abs}}(1+J/\Delta)R\log^3(q^R)|\partial_\Lambda A|$.
Here every nonzero designated support must be a full interval
$[a,a+R+1)\cap\mathbb Z$ contained in $\Lambda$.
Labelling the sites in increasing order preserves each interaction term,
its norm bound $J$, the Hamiltonian sum and the global gap.
Flipping the region on zero-entropy prefixes gives the exact same entropy
and physical boundary cardinality, without a factor for the number of components.\footnote{Declaration:
    \leanid{TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.domainAreaLaw_of_consecutive_terms_polynomial_range}.}
This is the finite-domain full-window specialization in the remark on sharper
constants of the note~\cite{TNLean2026OneDimensional},
\path{part1_arealaw.tex}, lines~706--748.
The improvement is restricted to full designated windows.
