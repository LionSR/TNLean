\section{Source theorem}

Theorem~5 of Ref.~\cite[lines~849--858]{PerezGarcia2007MPSReps} gives a
periodic decomposition for translation-invariant matrix product states with
periodic boundary conditions. Let
$\psi\in(\C^d)^{\otimes N}$ be a translation-invariant state whose canonical
translation-invariant MPS representation consists of one block
$A=(A_i)_{i=0}^{d-1}$ with $A_i\in M_D(\C)$. The associated completely
positive transfer map is
\begin{align}
    \mathcal E(X)
     & = \sum_{i=0}^{d-1} A_i X A_i^\dagger.
    \label{eq:pgvwc07_periodic_decomposition_scope_transfer_map}
\end{align}
Suppose $\mathcal E$ has exactly $p$ eigenvalues of modulus one. The source
concludes:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item If $p$ divides $N$, then $\psi$ is a superposition of $p$
          $p$-periodic states, each admitting an MPS representation of bond
          dimension $D$.
    \item If $p$ does not divide $N$, then $\psi=0$.
\end{enumerate}

The proof invokes the peripheral spectral structure of the completely positive
map: there are orthogonal projectors $P_k$ that the matrices $A_i$ permute
cyclically. Inserting their resolution of the identity,
\begin{align}
    \sum_{k=0}^{p-1}P_k
     & = \Id,
    \label{eq:pgvwc07_periodic_decomposition_scope_source_projector_resolution}
\end{align}
into the periodic trace separates $\psi$ into the claimed $p$-periodic
summands
\cite[lines~860--880]{PerezGarcia2007MPSReps}.

\section{Formal boundary}

The source-facing theorem
\leanid{MPSTensor.pgvwc07_unital_stateVector_decomposition} now starts from
exactly the one-block canonical conditions: unitality, a positive-definite
adjoint fixed matrix, scalar fixed matrices, and the peripheral eigenvalue
count. It constructs cyclic orthogonal projectors for the original tensor and
proves both branches at all positive lengths. Diagonality of the adjoint fixed
matrix is not needed; the printed diagonal case is included.

The normalized companion declarations
\leanid{MPSTensor.pgvwc07_periodic_stateVector_boundary_decomposition_of_dvd},
\leanid{MPSTensor.pgvwc07_periodic_stateVector_decomposition_of_dvd}, and
\leanid{MPSTensor.pgvwc07_stateVector_eq_zero_of_not_dvd}
remain available for tensors already in the trace-preserving
\leanid{MPSTensor.IsPeriodic} convention.

Let $A=(A^i)_{i=0}^{d-1}$ be the formal MPS matrix family, and let
$(Q_u)_{u\in\mathbb Z/p\mathbb Z}$ be its cyclic projections. For a physical
word
\begin{align}
    \sigma
     & = (i_0,\ldots,i_{N-1}),
    \label{eq:pgvwc07_periodic_decomposition_scope_physical_word}
\end{align}
define the boundary component product
\begin{align}
    F_u(\sigma)
     & =
    Q_u A^{i_0}Q_{u+1}A^{i_1}\cdots
    Q_{u+N-1}A^{i_{N-1}}Q_{u+N}.
    \label{eq:pgvwc07_periodic_decomposition_scope_boundary_component_product}
\end{align}
At every length $N\geq0$, the boundary-aware theorem gives
\begin{align}
    \tr\!\left(A^{i_0}\cdots A^{i_{N-1}}\right)
     & =
    \sum_{u\in\mathbb Z/p\mathbb Z}
    \tr\!\left(F_u(\sigma)\right).
    \label{eq:pgvwc07_periodic_decomposition_scope_boundary_trace_decomposition}
\end{align}

For $N>0$,
\leanid{MPSTensor.projectorComponentChain_coeff_eq_trace_cornerProd}
identifies each term on the right-hand side of
(\ref{eq:pgvwc07_periodic_decomposition_scope_boundary_trace_decomposition})
with the corresponding coefficient of the explicit periodic component chain.
At $N=0$, the word is empty and the boundary product retains its projector:
\begin{align}
    F_u(\varnothing)
     & = Q_u.
    \label{eq:pgvwc07_periodic_decomposition_scope_empty_boundary_product}
\end{align}
Consequently, the length-zero instance of
(\ref{eq:pgvwc07_periodic_decomposition_scope_boundary_trace_decomposition})
is
\begin{align}
    \tr(\Id)
     & =
    \sum_{u\in\mathbb Z/p\mathbb Z}\tr(Q_u).
    \label{eq:pgvwc07_periodic_decomposition_scope_empty_trace_decomposition}
\end{align}

This boundary-aware coefficient differs from the coefficient of an ordinary
empty component chain. An empty chain evaluates to $\Id$ for every $u$ and
therefore forgets the boundary projector $Q_u$. The boundary-aware theorem
closes the length-zero part of issue~\#6233 while leaving the positive-length
explicit component-chain statement unchanged.

\section{Derivation from the canonical hypotheses}

Unitality fixes the identity and makes the spectral radius one. The faithful
adjoint fixed matrix and uniqueness of fixed matrices supply the spectral
characterization of irreducibility. The cyclic peripheral spectrum then has a
primitive generator, whose distinct powers identify its order with the given
count $p$. Positivity and unitality rule out peripheral Jordan blocks, and the
irreducible peripheral eigenspaces have dimension one. Thus the count agrees
with algebraic multiplicity as well as distinct eigenvalues.

The formal declarations are
\leanid{MPSTensor.isIrreducible_transferMap_of_unital_canonical},
\leanid{MPSTensor.exists_peripheral_generator_of_unital_canonical}, and
\leanid{MPSTensor.peripheral_genEigenspace_finrank_eq_one_of_unital_canonical}.
The resulting cyclic projections act on the original virtual space and obey
$Q_uA^i=A^iQ_{u+1}$. The existing corner-product calculation gives the state
sum and the vanishing of every component when $p\nmid N$.

The proof stays in the source's unital orientation. Unitality alone does not
imply the trace-preserving hypothesis in \leanid{MPSTensor.IsPeriodic}; no
such equivalence is asserted for the same tensor. Nor is the state silently
conjugated or reversed by an adjoint-family substitution.

\section{Verdict}

\textbf{Verdict: resolved.} The source-facing periodic decomposition and
vanishing dichotomy follow from the printed one-block canonical hypotheses.
The former normalized-orientation restriction of issue~\#6232 is removed.
The length-zero boundary identity remains the explicit convention already
settled under issue~\#6233; the physical component-chain theorem is stated
for positive lengths, as in the source.
