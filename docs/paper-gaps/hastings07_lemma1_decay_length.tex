\section{The source and the length convention}

Hastings' main theorem and Lemma~1 use
\begin{align*}
    \xi=\max(2v/\Delta E,\xi_C),\qquad\xi'=6\xi,
\end{align*}
where $v$ and $\xi_C$ come from a Lieb--Robinson bound
\cite[lines 96--112, 139--142]{Hastings2007AreaLaw}.
The printed bound is
\begin{align*}
    \|[A(t),B]\|\le c|X|\|A\|\|B\|
       e^{-\xi_C\operatorname{dist}(X,Y)},\qquad |t|\le l/v.
\end{align*}
The length $l$ is undefined there, and multiplying distance by $\xi_C$
is inconsistent with the subsequent use of $\xi_C$ as a length.
The formalization adopts the intended convention
$e^{-\operatorname{dist}(X,Y)/\xi_C}$ for
$v|t|\le\operatorname{dist}(X,Y)$.
The source chooses the pair existentially, with $v$ of order $J$ and
$\xi_C$ of order one; it does not prescribe a minimal pair.

\section{The constructed pair}

For range $R$, write
$c_R=2(R+1)2^{2R+1}e^{2R}$.
The graph Lieb--Robinson estimate on the path gives
\begin{align*}
    \|[A(t),B]\|\le4|X|\|A\|\|B\|e^{2c_RJ|t|-d}
\end{align*}
whenever $d$ is an integer lower bound for the distance between the
arbitrary finite supports $X,Y$.
The prefactor is numerical, and there is no factor $|Y|$.
Set
\begin{align*}
    V=40c_RJ,\qquad \Xi=\max(2V/\Delta E,80).
\end{align*}
If $V|t|\le d$, the exponent is at most $-19d/20\le-d/80$.
Thus $(V,80)$ is an admissible pair for $J>0$; $V$ is independent of the
gap, chain length and local dimension. For $J=0$ the multiplication-cone
statement remains valid without dividing by a zero velocity.
These are actual commutator estimates, not merely scalar comparisons
with a prospective pair.\footnote{\leanid{TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.ChainHamiltonian.norm_heisenberg_commutator_le_numeric}
    and \leanid{TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.ChainHamiltonian.norm_heisenberg_commutator_le_hastings_cone}
    in \doclink{TNLean/MPS/AreaLaw/Hastings/LiebRobinson} and
    \doclink{TNLean/MPS/AreaLaw/Hastings/SourceLength}.}

\section{Factorization and entropy at the same length}

The preceding gap-normalized construction uses
$\zeta=20(1+c_RJ/\Delta E)$ and gives a contraction factorization with
error $K\zeta^3e^{-l/(6\zeta)}$; its positive semidefinite version has
error $K\zeta^3e^{-l/(12\zeta)}$.
Here and below the constants depend only on $R$.
Since $2\zeta\le\Xi$, the same positive operators satisfy
\begin{align*}
    \|O_BO_LO_R-P_0\|\le K\Xi^3e^{-l/(6\Xi)}.
\end{align*}
This is formalized by
\leanid{TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.exists_projectorFactorization_posSemidef_sourceLength}.
The support conditions, commuting exterior projections, and norm bounds
are retained. Positivity is semidefinite, as in the source's note added;
strict positivity of nontrivial projections is not asserted.

At the smaller length, the corrected doubling argument gives exponential
factor $2^{2(6\zeta)\log q}$. Its other terms are bounded by
$c_0(6\zeta)\log(6\zeta)\log q$ because the factorization constant is cubic
in $\zeta$. Enlarging to $\Xi\ge2\zeta$ gives
\begin{align*}
    S(\Omega_{[0,i)})\le c_0(6\Xi)\log(6\Xi)\log q\,
        2^{(6\Xi)\log q}.
\end{align*}
The constant is numerical for nearest neighbours. This is
\leanid{TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.Hastings.exists_regionEntropy_prefix_le_sourceLength}
in \doclink{TNLean/MPS/AreaLaw/Hastings/SourceLength}.
It follows from the actual Hamiltonian gap and supported bounded terms,
without supplied factorization operators or entropy assumptions.

\section{What this choice does not change}

The fixed-length arithmetic corrections remain necessary.
The Gaussian absorption at the original length proves rates
$\kappa\hat\xi$ for $\kappa>6$, not the endpoint $6\hat\xi$; see
\path{hastings07_gaussian_average_exponent.tex}.
The positive replacement loses a square root in its error; see
\path{hastings07_lemma1_positivity_rate.tex}.
The doubling iteration has twice the printed exponent at a fixed length;
see \path{hastings07_doubling_constants.tex}.
The new length absorbs these losses rather than removing them.
The literal body version of Lemma~1 with strictly positive operators
remains distinct from the semidefinite formulation justified by the
note added. The previously missing numerical, arbitrary-support
Lieb--Robinson pair is now constructed, and no step of that construction
remains open in this note.
