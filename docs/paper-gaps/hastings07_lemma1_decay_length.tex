\section{The source statement}

The main theorem of~\cite{Hastings2007AreaLaw} and its Lemma~1 use the
lengths
\begin{align}
    \xi=\max(2v/\Delta E,\xi_C),\qquad\xi'=6\xi,
    \label{eq:hastings07_lemma1_decay_length_source}
\end{align}
where $\Delta E$ is the gap and $v$, $\xi_C$ are a velocity and a length
scale of a Lieb--Robinson bound.\footnote{\path{References/0705.2024/law16.tex},
    lines~139--142.} That bound is printed as
\begin{align}
    \|[A(t),B]\|\le c\,|X|\,\|A\|\,\|B\|\,e^{-\xi_C\operatorname{dist}(X,Y)}
    \quad\text{for }|t|\le l/v,
    \label{eq:hastings07_lemma1_decay_length_lrb}
\end{align}
for operators $A$, $B$ supported on sets $X$, $Y$; the source says only that
such $v$ and $\xi_C$ exist, with $v$ of order $J$ and $\xi_C$ of order
one.\footnote{\path{References/0705.2024/law16.tex}, lines~96--112.}

\section{The reading}

As printed, (\ref{eq:hastings07_lemma1_decay_length_lrb}) multiplies the
distance by $\xi_C$ and bounds the time by $l/v$, a length not defined at
that point. The reading consistent with the name ``length scale'' and with
the use of $2v/\Delta E$ as a competing length is that the commutator decays
as $e^{-\operatorname{dist}(X,Y)/\xi_C}$ for $|t|\le\operatorname{dist}(X,Y)/v$.
Any pair $(v,\xi_C)$ for which this holds may be used.

The chain Lieb--Robinson bound proved for range-$R$ Hamiltonians with terms of
norm at most $J$ has the form $a\,|X|^2e^{v_0|t|-k/2}$ at distance $k$, with explicit constants
$a$ and $v_0$ depending only on $R$ and $J$. Its prefactor is quadratic rather
than linear in $|X|$; in the argument this only adds a polynomial factor, which
the exponential absorbs. For
$|t|\le k/v$ this is at most $e^{-k(1/2-v_0/v)}$, so a pair $(v,\xi_C)$ is
admissible whenever
\begin{align}
    \frac1{\xi_C}\le\frac12-\frac{v_0}{v}.
    \label{eq:hastings07_lemma1_decay_length_pair}
\end{align}
The pair $(v,\xi_C)=(8v_0,8)$ satisfies
(\ref{eq:hastings07_lemma1_decay_length_pair}), since
$1/8\le1/2-1/8$, and for it
(\ref{eq:hastings07_lemma1_decay_length_source}) gives
\begin{align}
    \xi=\max(16v_0/\Delta E,8)=8\max(2v_0/\Delta E,1).
    \label{eq:hastings07_lemma1_decay_length_value}
\end{align}
This is the length used in the formalization of Lemma~1. The larger pair
leaves the slack needed to absorb the polynomial prefactors of the
construction into the exponential.\footnote{\leanid{TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.lemmaOneLength}
    and \leanid{TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.exists_projectorFactorization} in
    \doclink{TNLean/MPS/AreaLaw/Hastings/ProjectorFactorization}.}
With this $\xi$, Lemma~1 holds at every rate $\xi'>6\xi$ but not at
$\xi'=6\xi$, which is recorded in
\path{hastings07_gaussian_average_exponent.tex}.

\section{Conclusion}

The value (\ref{eq:hastings07_lemma1_decay_length_value}) is the source's
(\ref{eq:hastings07_lemma1_decay_length_source}) for an admissible
Lieb--Robinson pair. The constant of Lemma~1 then depends only on $R$, $J$,
$\Delta E$ and $\xi'$, and not on the length of the chain or the ground state.
