\section{Source statement}

Let $A$, $B$, and $C$ be finite-dimensional quantum systems. Define the
partial-trace channel $T$ and product reference state $\sigma_{ABC}$ by
\begin{align}
    T
        &=\operatorname{id}_A\otimes\operatorname{tr}_C,
    \label{eq:hjpw04_petz_factorization_maximally_mixed_scope_partial_trace}\\
    \sigma_{ABC}
        &=\rho_A\otimes\rho_{BC}.
    \label{eq:hjpw04_petz_factorization_maximally_mixed_scope_product_reference}
\end{align}
Hayden--Jozsa--Petz--Winter write the recovery operation in their
Equation~(10) as
\begin{align}
    \widehat T
        &=\operatorname{id}_A\otimes\widehat R.
    \label{eq:hjpw04_petz_factorization_maximally_mixed_scope_source_factorization}
\end{align}
Here $\widehat R$ is the recovery operation associated with $\rho_{BC}$ and
$\operatorname{tr}_C$ \cite{HJPW2004}. Their Petz formula is specified on
the support of $T\sigma_{ABC}$. Thus
(\ref{eq:hjpw04_petz_factorization_maximally_mixed_scope_source_factorization})
holds literally on the reduced support spaces. On the full ambient matrix
algebras, it requires an extension away from those supports.

\section{Global raw formula}

Let $P_A$ denote the support projection of $\rho_A$, and define
$\rho_B=\operatorname{tr}_C\rho_{BC}$. The product reference and its retained
marginal satisfy
\begin{align}
    \sigma_{ABC}
        &=\rho_A\otimes\rho_{BC},
    \label{eq:hjpw04_petz_factorization_maximally_mixed_scope_reference_again}\\
    \operatorname{tr}_C\sigma_{ABC}
        &=\rho_A\otimes\rho_B,
    \label{eq:hjpw04_petz_factorization_maximally_mixed_scope_reference_marginal}\\
    \rho_B
        &=\operatorname{tr}_C\rho_{BC}.
    \label{eq:hjpw04_petz_factorization_maximally_mixed_scope_right_marginal}
\end{align}
For positive semidefinite factors, square roots, support inverse square roots,
and support projections factor across tensor products. Therefore the raw
support-Petz map on the full ambient algebra factors as
\begin{align}
    \mathcal R^{\mathrm{raw}}_{\rho_A\otimes\rho_{BC}}
        &=\mathcal S_{P_A}\otimes
          \mathcal R^{\mathrm{raw}}_{\rho_{BC}},
    \label{eq:hjpw04_petz_factorization_maximally_mixed_scope_global_raw_factorization}\\
    \mathcal S_{P_A}(X)
        &=P_AXP_A.
    \label{eq:hjpw04_petz_factorization_maximally_mixed_scope_support_compression}
\end{align}
Equivalently, for $A_0$ acting on $A$ and $X_B$ acting on $B$,
\begin{align}
    \mathcal R^{\mathrm{raw}}_{\rho_A\otimes\rho_{BC}}
        (A_0\otimes X_B)
        &=(P_AA_0P_A)\otimes
          \mathcal R^{\mathrm{raw}}_{\rho_{BC}}(X_B).
    \label{eq:hjpw04_petz_factorization_maximally_mixed_scope_elementary_tensor_formula}
\end{align}
These identities use the canonical reassociation
$(A\times B)\times C\simeq A\times(B\times C)$.

\section{Identity-tensored corollaries and extension boundary}

Suppose that an input $X$ is supported on the first factor:
\begin{align}
    (P_A\otimes\Id_B)X(P_A\otimes\Id_B)
        &=X.
    \label{eq:hjpw04_petz_factorization_maximally_mixed_scope_left_supported_input}
\end{align}
Then support compression disappears, and
\begin{align}
    \mathcal R^{\mathrm{raw}}_{\rho_A\otimes\rho_{BC}}(X)
        &=(\operatorname{id}_A\otimes
          \mathcal R^{\mathrm{raw}}_{\rho_{BC}})(X).
    \label{eq:hjpw04_petz_factorization_maximally_mixed_scope_supported_identity_factorization}
\end{align}
If $\rho_A$ is positive definite, then
\begin{align}
    P_A
        &=\Id_A,
    \label{eq:hjpw04_petz_factorization_maximally_mixed_scope_full_left_support}
\end{align}
so
(\ref{eq:hjpw04_petz_factorization_maximally_mixed_scope_supported_identity_factorization})
holds globally. These conclusions are formalized by
\leanid{Matrix.tensorMapId_supportCompressionMap},
\leanid{Matrix.partialTraceRightPetzMap_productTensor_apply_of_left_supported},
and
\leanid{Matrix.partialTraceRightPetzMap_productTensor_of_posDef_left}.

If $\rho_A$ is singular, the ambient raw map does not globally equal
$\operatorname{id}_A\otimes
\mathcal R^{\mathrm{raw}}_{\rho_{BC}}$. An operator supported in
$\ker\rho_A$ is annihilated by $X\mapsto P_AXP_A$ but not by the identity
map. A global channel of the form
$\operatorname{id}_A\otimes\widehat{\mathcal R}_{\rho_{BC}}$ is therefore a
chosen tensor-compatible extension of the support formula.

The generic completed channel in TNLean uses the complementary projection
\begin{align}
    \Id_{AB}-P_A\otimes P_B.
    \label{eq:hjpw04_petz_factorization_maximally_mixed_scope_completion_projection}
\end{align}
For singular $\rho_A$, this projection does not generally factor as the
identity on $A$ tensored with a projection on $B$. No factorization of that
generic completed channel is claimed. The existing maximally mixed completed
factorization is valid because the first-factor support is full.

\section{SSA product-reference specialization}

For every positive semidefinite bipartite operator $\omega_{XY}$, its support
is contained in the tensor product of the marginal supports. Equivalently,
\begin{align}
    \ker(\omega_X\otimes\omega_Y)
        &\subseteq\ker\omega_{XY}.
    \label{eq:hjpw04_petz_factorization_maximally_mixed_scope_product_marginal_support}
\end{align}
Thus the product-marginal reference automatically satisfies the support
condition in Theorem~3 of Hayden--Jozsa--Petz--Winter
\cite{HJPW2004}. If a tripartite density matrix saturates strong
subadditivity, the raw product-reference Petz map recovers it:
\begin{align}
    \mathcal R^{\mathrm{raw}}_{\rho_A\otimes\rho_{BC}}(\rho_{AB})
        &=\rho_{ABC}.
    \label{eq:hjpw04_petz_factorization_maximally_mixed_scope_raw_ssa_recovery}
\end{align}
The input $\rho_{AB}$ is supported by both
$P_A\otimes\Id_B$ and $\Id_A\otimes P_B$. Consequently, the supported
factorization and the local trace-preserving completion give
\begin{align}
    (\operatorname{id}_A\otimes
        \mathcal R^{\mathrm{raw}}_{\rho_{BC}})(\rho_{AB})
        &=\rho_{ABC},
    \label{eq:hjpw04_petz_factorization_maximally_mixed_scope_local_raw_recovery}\\
    (\operatorname{id}_A\otimes
        \widehat{\mathcal R}_{\rho_{BC}})(\rho_{AB})
        &=\rho_{ABC}.
    \label{eq:hjpw04_petz_factorization_maximally_mixed_scope_local_completed_recovery}
\end{align}
These identities are Equations~(10)--(11) of
Hayden--Jozsa--Petz--Winter on the supported input \cite{HJPW2004}. They are
formalized in
\doclink{TNLean/Channel/Schwarz/SSAEqualityPetzRecovery} by
\leanid{Matrix.product_marginal_support},
\leanid{Matrix.partialTraceRightPetzMap_product_marginal_recovery_of_isSSAEquality},
\leanid{Matrix.idTensor_partialTraceRightPetzMap_traceC_ABC_eq_of_isSSAEquality},
\leanid{Matrix.partialTraceRightPetzChannel_traceA_ABC_isKrausCPTP}, and
\leanid{Matrix.idTensor_partialTraceRightPetzChannel_traceC_ABC_eq_of_isSSAEquality}.
No global identity-tensored factorization of TNLean's generic completed
singular product-reference channel is claimed.

\section{Completed structural implication}

The product-reference recovery identity, the invariant conditional family,
the joint-support block theorem, and the ambient extension give
\begin{align}
    H_B
        &\cong\bigoplus_j H_{B_j^L}\otimes H_{B_j^R},
    \label{eq:hjpw04_petz_factorization_maximally_mixed_scope_middle_decomposition}\\
    W^\dagger\rho_{ABC}W
        &=0_\perp\oplus\bigoplus_j
          \omega_j\otimes\widehat\rho_j,
    \label{eq:hjpw04_petz_factorization_maximally_mixed_scope_tripartite_blocks}\\
    W
        &=\Id_A\otimes(U_B\otimes\Id_C).
    \label{eq:hjpw04_petz_factorization_maximally_mixed_scope_block_unitary}
\end{align}
The supported matrices $\widehat\rho_j$ are trace-one sector output states,
and the complementary blocks vanish. The forward characterization defines
\begin{align}
    p_j
        &=\operatorname{Re}\tr(\omega_j),
    \label{eq:hjpw04_petz_factorization_maximally_mixed_scope_sector_weight}\\
    p_j
        &\geq 0,
    \label{eq:hjpw04_petz_factorization_maximally_mixed_scope_sector_weight_nonnegative}\\
    \sum_jp_j
        &=1.
    \label{eq:hjpw04_petz_factorization_maximally_mixed_scope_sector_weight_normalization}
\end{align}
It reverses each middle fibre to the order
$B_j^L\otimes B_j^R$ and chooses the Hayashi unitary $U_B^\dagger$. Every
supported right factor remains its sector output state. Total
positive-semidefinite normalization supplies the left density matrices. A
zero-weight supported sector may use a left filler, while a complementary
sector may use fillers on both factors; their weighted contributions vanish.

The formal declaration
\leanid{Matrix.hayashi_ssa_equality_characterization_forward}, with public
root name \leanid{hayashi_ssa_equality_characterization_forward}, proves this
structural implication.

The singular-support boundary is unchanged. The raw support-Petz map factors
as in
(\ref{eq:hjpw04_petz_factorization_maximally_mixed_scope_global_raw_factorization}),
and the identity-tensored formula holds on supported inputs. For the SSA
marginal, the chosen completed local channel agrees with the raw map on that
supported input. No global identity-tensored factorization is claimed for
TNLean's generic completed singular product-reference channel away from
support.

\section{Common invariant algebra under full support}

A full-support specialization of the operator-algebraic Koashi--Imoto
argument in Appendix~A, lines~755--882, of
Hayden--Jozsa--Petz--Winter \cite{HJPW2004} is formalized independently of
the product-reference recovery step. Let
$\rho_1,\ldots,\rho_K$ be a finite nonempty family of states. Define
\begin{align}
    \mathbf F
        &=\{F:\ F\rho_k=\rho_k\ \text{for every $k$}\}.
    \label{eq:hjpw04_petz_factorization_maximally_mixed_scope_preserving_family}
\end{align}
Each $F\in\mathbf F$ is a trace-preserving completely positive operation.
Define its adjoint fixed-point algebra and the common invariant algebra by
\begin{align}
    A_F
        &=\{X:\ F^*(X)=X\},
    \label{eq:hjpw04_petz_factorization_maximally_mixed_scope_fixed_point_algebra}\\
    A_0
        &=\bigcap_{F\in\mathbf F}A_F.
    \label{eq:hjpw04_petz_factorization_maximally_mixed_scope_common_invariant_algebra}
\end{align}
These are the constructions at lines~825--827 and 843 of
Ref.~\cite{HJPW2004}. The formal development represents $\mathbf F$ as a
bundled Kraus-family type and $A_0$ as the intersection of the fixed-point
star-subalgebras. It proves the membership characterization and applies the
generic block form of a star-subalgebra to $A_0$.

Finite dimensionality gives
\begin{align}
    A_0
        &=A_{F_1}\cap\cdots\cap A_{F_M},
    \label{eq:hjpw04_petz_factorization_maximally_mixed_scope_finite_intersection}\\
    F_0
        &=\frac{1}{M}\sum_{\mu=1}^M F_\mu,
    \label{eq:hjpw04_petz_factorization_maximally_mixed_scope_averaged_operation}\\
    A_0
        &=A_{F_0}.
    \label{eq:hjpw04_petz_factorization_maximally_mixed_scope_single_witness}
\end{align}
This is the finite-dimensional content of lines~844--849 of
Ref.~\cite{HJPW2004}. The trace-pairing adjoint $P_0^*$ of the
Schr\"odinger mean-ergodic projection for $F_0$ is positive, unital, and
idempotent, with range $A_0$. Hayden--Jozsa--Petz--Winter express this
projection as the Ces\`aro limit of the Heisenberg iterates at lines~838--851
\cite{HJPW2004}; the formal adjoint averages converge to the trace-adjoint
projection.

\begin{align}
    F_j(\sigma_j)
        &=\sigma_j,

statement concerns diagonal summands. It does not claim the Stinespring form
of Property~2 or describe the action on off-diagonal coherences.

\section{Joint-support and ambient forms}

Hayden--Jozsa--Petz--Winter reduce to the case in which
$K^{-1}\sum_k\rho_k$ has full support by restricting the Hilbert space to
the joint support of the family \cite[lines~761--763]{HJPW2004}. The
declaration \leanid{Kraus.exists_commonAverageSupportCompression} constructs
this support coordinate space, proves that the compressed average is positive
definite, reconstructs every family member, and restricts every preserving
operation to a trace-preserving operation on the support. It also proves the
intertwining identity that transports the compressed channel action through
the support isometry.

The declaration
\leanid{Kraus.exists_commonInvariant_normalizedStateBlockForm_preservingBlockAction_jointSupport}
packages the normalized state blocks and the action of every preserving
operation on the minimum joint support. Ambient preserving operations inherit
this action by restriction and intertwining.

The finite invariant conditional family and one preserving Kraus realization
are constructed from SSA equality. The declaration
\leanid{Matrix.exists_normalizedConditionalSliceBlockForm_preservingBlockAction_jointSupport}
applies the block theorem to this family and retains the Petz middle-system
channel, the support intertwining, and the diagonal block action.

The declaration
\leanid{Matrix.exists_markovBipartiteBlockForm_jointSupport} uses the same
support isometry and block coordinates to reconstruct the bipartite marginal.
It produces positive unnormalized factors $\omega_j$, proves that their traces
sum to one, and establishes
\begin{align}
    \rho_{\mathrm{joint}}
        &=\bigoplus_j\sigma_j\otimes\omega_j.
    \label{eq:hjpw04_petz_factorization_maximally_mixed_scope_joint_support_bipartite_form}
\end{align}
The tensor order is reversed from that of
Ref.~\cite{HJPW2004}. This is a scope-restricted joint-support variant of
Equation~(14) of Ref.~\cite{HJPW2004}, not an ambient equivalence on $H_B$.

The declaration
\leanid{Matrix.exists_ambientMarkovBipartiteBlockForm} completes the support
isometry to an ambient middle-system unitary and splits the orthogonal
complement into one-dimensional zero-weight tensor sectors. It retains the
supported positive factors, proves positivity on every ambient sector, and
proves that the total conditional trace is one. Consequently,
Equations~(13)--(14), lines~493--502, of Ref.~\cite{HJPW2004} hold on the
ambient $H_B$ without an additional source hypothesis.

The declaration \leanid{Matrix.exists_markovDilationBlockForm} proves
Equation~(15) of Ref.~\cite{HJPW2004} for one chosen pure-ancilla unitary,
together with supported Petz agreement, the exact recovery identity, and
normalized sector output states on the supported common-factor sectors. The
declaration
\leanid{Matrix.MarkovDilationBlockForm.ambient_tripartite_block_form}
combines Equations~(11), (14), and (15) of Ref.~\cite{HJPW2004} and proves
\begin{align}
    W
        &=\Id_A\otimes(U_B\otimes\Id_C),
    \label{eq:hjpw04_petz_factorization_maximally_mixed_scope_ambient_unitary}\\
    W^\dagger\rho_{ABC}W
        &=0_\perp\oplus\bigoplus_j
          \omega_j\otimes\widehat\rho_j.
    \label{eq:hjpw04_petz_factorization_maximally_mixed_scope_ambient_tripartite_form}
\end{align}
The theorem
\leanid{Matrix.hayashi_ssa_equality_characterization_forward} normalizes these
blocks into the quantum-Markov decomposition. Every supported right factor
remains its sector output state, including at zero weight. A zero-weight
supported sector may use a filler only on the left, while a complementary
sector may use fillers on both factors.

\section{Classification}

The product-reference recovery and the structural implication are complete.
The formal development covers the general positive-semidefinite product
reference, its marginal and support calculus, the global support-compressed
raw formula, and the supported-input and positive-definite-left corollaries.
The exact SSA specialization proves Equation~(11) of Ref.~\cite{HJPW2004}.
The invariant-family decomposition, joint-support reduction, ambient form of
Equation~(14), and recovery-dilation action of Equation~(15) give the ambient
tripartite blocks. Probability normalization and fibre reordering prove the
Hayashi forward implication without axioms.

The raw/completed singular boundary remains explicit. The generic completed
singular product-reference channel is not claimed to factor as the identity
on the first subsystem tensored with a local channel. The completed local
channel is used only through its proved agreement with the raw support map on
the supported marginal.
