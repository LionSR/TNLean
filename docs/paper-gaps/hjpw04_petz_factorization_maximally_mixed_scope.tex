\section{Source statement}

Let
\begin{align}
  T&=\operatorname{id}_A\otimes\operatorname{tr}_C,
  &\sigma_{ABC}&=\rho_A\otimes\rho_{BC}.
\end{align}
Hayden--Jozsa--Petz--Winter write the recovery operation as
\begin{align}
  \widehat T=\operatorname{id}_A\otimes\widehat R
  \label{eq:hjpw-general-factorization}
\end{align}
in equation~(10), where $\widehat R$ is associated with $\rho_{BC}$ and
$\operatorname{tr}_C$. Their Petz formula is specified on the support of
$T\sigma$. Thus equation~\eqref{eq:hjpw-general-factorization} is literal on
the reduced support spaces. On the full ambient matrix algebras it requires a
choice of extension away from those supports.

\section{Global raw formula}

Let $P_A$ be the support projection of $\rho_A$. The product reference and its
retained marginal satisfy
\begin{align}
  \sigma_{ABC}&=\rho_A\otimes\rho_{BC},
  &\operatorname{tr}_C\sigma_{ABC}
    &=\rho_A\otimes\rho_B,
  &\rho_B&=\operatorname{tr}_C\rho_{BC}.
\end{align}
For positive semidefinite factors, square roots, support inverse square roots,
and support projections factor across the tensor product. Consequently the raw
support-Petz map on the full ambient algebra is
\begin{align}
  \mathcal R^{\mathrm{raw}}_{\rho_A\otimes\rho_{BC}}
  &=\mathcal S_{P_A}\otimes
    \mathcal R^{\mathrm{raw}}_{\rho_{BC}},
  &\mathcal S_{P_A}(X)&=P_AXP_A.
  \label{eq:hjpw-global-support-factorization}
\end{align}
Equivalently,
\begin{align}
  \mathcal R^{\mathrm{raw}}_{\rho_A\otimes\rho_{BC}}(A_0\otimes X_B)
  &=(P_AA_0P_A)\otimes
    \mathcal R^{\mathrm{raw}}_{\rho_{BC}}(X_B).
\end{align}
All identities use the canonical reassociation
$(A\times B)\times C\simeq A\times(B\times C)$.

\section{Identity-tensored corollaries and extension boundary}

If an input $X$ is supported on the first factor,
\begin{align}
  (P_A\otimes\mathbf 1_B)X(P_A\otimes\mathbf 1_B)=X,
\end{align}
then support compression disappears and
\begin{align}
  \mathcal R^{\mathrm{raw}}_{\rho_A\otimes\rho_{BC}}(X)
  &=(\operatorname{id}_A\otimes
    \mathcal R^{\mathrm{raw}}_{\rho_{BC}})(X).
\end{align}
If $\rho_A$ is positive definite, then $P_A=\mathbf 1_A$, so this identity
holds globally. These conclusions are formalized by
\begin{center}

  \leanid{Matrix.partialTraceRightPetzMap_productTensor_of_posDef_left}.
\end{center}

If $\rho_A$ is singular, the ambient raw map is not globally
$\operatorname{id}_A\otimes\mathcal R^{\mathrm{raw}}_{\rho_{BC}}$. For
example, an operator supported in the kernel of $\rho_A$ is killed by
$X\mapsto P_AXP_A$ but not by the identity map. A global channel of the form
$\operatorname{id}_A\otimes\widehat{\mathcal R}_{\rho_{BC}}$ is therefore a
chosen tensor-compatible extension of the support formula.

TNLean's generic completed channel uses the complementary projection
\begin{align}
  \mathbf 1_{AB}-P_A\otimes P_B.
\end{align}
For singular $\rho_A$ this projection does not generally factor as the
identity on $A$ tensored with a projection on $B$. No factorization of that
generic completed channel is claimed. The existing maximally mixed completed
factorization is valid because the first-factor support is full.

\section{Remaining structural theorem}

Equation~\eqref{eq:hjpw-global-support-factorization} supplies the
product-reference recovery factorization required before the structural part
of the HJPW argument. It does not prove the Koashi--Imoto decomposition for a
family of recovered states. The remaining step must identify one common direct
sum
\begin{align}
  H_B\cong\bigoplus_j H_{B_j^L}\otimes H_{B_j^R}
\end{align}
and describe the action of the middle-system channel on every summand so that
the full family has the quantum-Markov form. The singular-support
equality-to-recovery theorem remains a separate analytic obligation. Beyond
it, this family-level invariant-state and channel-action
theorem is the remaining structural obstruction to replacing the forward
Hayashi strong-subadditivity equality assumption used in the CPSV16 argument.

\section{Classification}

\emph{Raw factorization complete; structural implication open.} The general
positive-semidefinite product reference, its marginal and support calculus, the
global support-compressed raw formula, and the supported-input and
positive-definite-left corollaries are formalized. The generic completed
singular channel is outside this claim. Singular-support equality-to-recovery
and the family-level Koashi--Imoto channel-action theorem remain open as
separate later steps.
