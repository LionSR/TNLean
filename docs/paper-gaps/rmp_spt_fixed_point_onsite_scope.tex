\section{What the source states}
In the paragraph ``Symmetry protected topological order'' of
\cite{Cirac2021Matrix}, the symmetry group $\mathcal G$ may combine on-site,
time-reversal and reflection symmetries. An element $x$ acts on the tensor by
$S_x(A^i)=e^{i\phi(x)}X_x^\dagger A^iX_x$, and the associated map on the bond
space is $X\mapsto X_xX$ or $X\mapsto X_x\bar X$, ``dependent on whether
time-reversal and/or reflection invariance is involved''. The cocycles lie in
the twisted cohomology groups $H^2_\alpha(\mathcal G,U(1))$ and
$H^1_\beta(\mathcal G,U(1))$. The zero-correlation-length representative is
constructed for every such pair of cocycles, with the twists $\alpha$ and
$\beta$ entering its phases.\footnote{Source traceability:
    \path{Papers/2011.12127/TN-Review-main.tex}, lines 1142--1157.}

\section{What is formalized}
Only on-site symmetries are formalized. For a projective representation $\rho$
of $G$ on $\mathbb C^D$ with an ordinary (untwisted) factor system $\omega$, a
character $\phi$ of $G$ and $W_g=\rho(g^{-1})$, the group acts on the physical
space $\mathbb C^D\otimes\mathbb C^D$ of the dimer fixed point
$A^{(a,b)}=D^{-1/2}\ket{a}\bra{b}$ by the linear representation
$U(g)=\phi(g)\,W_g^{T}\otimes W_g^{-1}$, and
\begin{align}
    \sum_jU(g)_{ij}A^j & =\phi(g)\,W_gA^iW_g^{-1}
    \label{eq:rmp_spt_onsite_scope_twist}
\end{align}
involves no complex conjugation of $A^i$ and no reversal of the order of the
sites. Time reversal, which conjugates the tensor, and reflection, which
transposes it, are not represented, and neither are the twists $\alpha$ and
$\beta$.

\section{Elimination plan}
Introduce symmetry actions on tensors in which a time-reversing element
conjugates the letters, $A^i\mapsto\sum_jU(x)_{ij}\bar A^j$, and a reflection
also reverses the order of the sites and transposes the letters. State the
cocycle conditions twisted by $\alpha$ and $\beta$ for the corresponding
bond-space maps $X\mapsto X_xX$ and $X\mapsto X_x\bar X$, construct the dimer
fixed point from a projective representation with a twisted factor system, and
prove the analogue of (\ref{eq:rmp_spt_onsite_scope_twist}) and of the class
statements for it. The on-site results are the special case in which $\alpha$ and $\beta$ are
trivial.\footnote{Affected formal declarations:
