\section{Printed determinant identity}

In the proof of \cite[Lemma~\texttt{lemma:G\_cocycle}]{FrancoRubio2025MPUGauging},
the regular-action matrices satisfy
\begin{align}
    X^{h x}_g X^x_h
        &=\Omega^x_{g,h}X^x_{gh}.
    \label{eq:fbc25_generalized_cocycle_operator}
\end{align}
The next display prints the numerator
$\det(X^{h x}_g)\det(X^x_g)$, repeating the index $g$ in the second factor.

\section{Corrected determinant identity}

Taking determinants in
Eq.~\ref{eq:fbc25_generalized_cocycle_operator} gives
\begin{align}
    \left(\Omega^x_{g,h}\right)^{|G|}
        &=\frac{\det(X^{h x}_g)\det(X^x_h)}{\det(X^x_{gh})}.
    \label{eq:fbc25_generalized_cocycle_determinant_corrected}
\end{align}
Thus the second numerator factor carries the index $h$. This is also the factor
required by the stated choice $\chi^x_g=\det(X^x_g)$ and the preceding
definition
$[d\chi]^x_{g,h}=\chi^{h x}_g\chi^x_h/\chi^x_{gh}$.

\section{Formal treatment}

The formal proof takes determinants of
Eq.~\ref{eq:fbc25_generalized_cocycle_operator} and uses
Eq.~\ref{eq:fbc25_generalized_cocycle_determinant_corrected}.
This changes no hypothesis or conclusion of the lemma.\footnote{Formal
declaration: \leanid{TNLean.Algebra.LSymbol.pow\_card\_eq\_coboundary} in
\doclink{TNLean/Algebra/GeneralizedCocycle}.}

\textbf{Verdict: resolved local fix.}
The operator identity, generalized-coboundary definition, and declared
cochain determine the missing index uniquely.
