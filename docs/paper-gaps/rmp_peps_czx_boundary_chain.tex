\section{What the source states}
The on-site $\mathbb Z_2$ symmetry of the CZX model is generated by
$U_{CZX}=U_XU_{CZ}$, with $U_X=X_1X_2X_3X_4$ and
$U_{CZ}=CZ_{12}CZ_{23}CZ_{34}CZ_{41}$ on the four qubits of a site, and the
plaquette ground state is invariant under it \cite{ChenLiuWen2011CZX}.%
\footnote{Source traceability:
    \path{References/1106.4752/source/dDSPTmodel.tex}, lines 283--312.}
On a region with boundary, the two qubits of a boundary half plaquette are
constrained to the span of $\ket{00}$ and $\ket{11}$ and form one effective
spin, and projecting the symmetry of the region onto the support of its
reduced state gives the effective boundary symmetry
$\prod_i\tilde X_i\prod_i\widetilde{CZ}_{i,i+1}$, a matrix product unitary of
bond dimension two.\footnote{Source traceability:
    \path{References/1106.4752/source/dDSPTmodel.tex}, lines 330--345 and
    377--385.}
The review states that the CZX PEPS of bond dimension four is invariant under
this on-site symmetry and that its virtual action is the CZX matrix product
unitary \cite{Cirac2021Matrix}.\footnote{Source traceability:
    \path{Papers/2011.12127/TN-Review-main.tex}, lines 2502--2516 and
    2582--2587.}

\section{What is formalized}
With the bond orientation of the note on the CZX tensor, each virtual leg
carries two qubits $(a,b)$, and the following hold.
\begin{enumerate}
    \item The CZX PEPS on a torus of width and height at least three is
          invariant under $U_{CZX}$ applied at every site.
    \item For one site tensor $A$, applying $U_{CZX}$ to the physical index
          equals applying $V=(X\otimes X)\,CZ$, that is
          $V\ket{ab}=(-1)^{ab}\ket{\bar a\bar b}$, to each of the four
          virtual legs.
    \item Let a closed chain of $N$ legs carry the pairs
          $(c_m,c_{m+1})$, indices modulo $N$, so that neighbouring legs
          carry the same qubit of their common plaquette, and let $\iota$ be
          the resulting isometry from $N$ effective spins into the legs. Then
          $V^{\otimes N}\iota=\iota\,U_N$ with
          $U_N=X^{\otimes N}D_N$ and
          $U_N\ket{c}=(-1)^{\sum_mc_mc_{m+1}}\ket{\bar c}$, and
          $\iota^\dagger V^{\otimes N}\iota=U_N$.
\end{enumerate}

The review's two printed local matrices instead contract to
\[
    O(A)=D_NX^{\otimes N}=(-1)^NU_N.
\]
Its bra-ket display with normalized
$\ket\pm$ has an additional factor $2^{-N/2}$ and is not the unitary
normalization. These order and normalization distinctions are recorded in
\path{mpu_czx_tensor_normalization.tex}. The rectangle perimeter $P$ is
even, so the printed-matrix operator agrees exactly with $U_P$ in the
physical rectangle theorem below.

\section{The actual rectangle map}
The rectangular-region part of the former restriction is now proved for
$R=[x_0,x_0+w)\times[y_0,y_0+h)$ in a $W\times H$ torus, with
$W,H\geq3$, $w,h>0$, $w<W$, $h<H$, $x_0+w\leq W$, and
$y_0+h\leq H$. Rectangles may meet a coordinate seam. Write $P=2w+2h$.

Let $A_R$ denote the actual open-region contraction: only incident bond
labels occur, internal bonds are summed, and crossing bonds are fixed.
The clockwise enumeration of the $P$ crossing bonds reverses bottom and
left pairs. A nonzero physical coefficient forces those oriented labels
to have the form $(c_i,c_{i+1})$, with the successor taken modulo $P$.
This follows from actual shared internal bonds along each side and from
one site tensor at each corner. It is not a hypothesis on the region map.


Define $F_R\ket c=A_R\ket{\mu(c)}$, where $\mu(c)$ is the corresponding
native boundary assignment. Conversely, arbitrary bits $c$ on the distinct
perimeter plaquettes extend to torus plaquette bits. Copying them into the
four physical qubits at each site gives a configuration $\sigma_c$ for which
$F_R(\sigma_c,c')=\delta_{c,c'}$. Thus $F_R$ is injective and its columns
span exactly the image of $A_R$, of dimension $2^P$.


Applying the one-site pull-through identity inside the actual contraction
cancels every internal controlled phase twice. The remaining factors are
on the actual crossing bonds. In the effective coordinates this gives
\[
    U_RF_R\ket c=(-1)^{\sum_i c_ic_{i+1}}F_R\ket{\bar c}.
\]
Pair reversal preserves its controlled phase and commutes with simultaneous
bit flip. The identification is therefore compatible with the source's
physical on-site symmetry and with the native bottom/left bond convention.
Since $P$ is even, it also gives $U_RF_R=F_RO(A)$ for the review's printed
matrix product operator, without a remaining sign.


\section{Remaining scope}
The theorem identifies the actual open-region image as a vector space,
with a faithful effective boundary action. It does not identify a normalized
reduced density matrix, assert that $F_R$ itself is an isometry, or compute
its Gram normalization or entropy. The source's explicit reduced-state
projection and general nonrectangular-region statement remain to be
formalized. No parent-Hamiltonian ground-space completeness result is
asserted here. The torus-size and bond-orientation conventions in the
separate notes are unchanged.
