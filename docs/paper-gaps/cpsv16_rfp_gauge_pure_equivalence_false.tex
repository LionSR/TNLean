\section{The source claim}

Appendix~D of \cite{Cirac2016MPDO_arXiv}, equation \texttt{RFP-gauge} and
lines~2100--2107 of the local source, allows a renormalization step to introduce
a gauge transformation on the virtual indices. The paragraph following the
diagram claims that, for pure tensors in canonical form, this condition is
equivalent to the ordinary renormalization fixed-point condition. Its first
step asserts that similarity of the transfer maps $\mathcal E^2$ and
$\mathcal E$ forces their eigenvalues to lie in $\{0,1\}$.

That spectral inference is false. A finite spectrum can be invariant under
squaring because its nonzero eigenvalues form a nontrivial root-of-unity cycle.
Similarity of $\mathcal E^2$ and $\mathcal E$ does not imply
$\mathcal E^2=\mathcal E$.

Definition \texttt{RFPMixedTS}, lines~657--660, imposes canonical form on the
MPDO tensor $M$.  We use that literal reading of ``in CF'' in the formal
refutation.  Thus the canonical-form and weight-normalization hypotheses are
proved for the doubled MPO tensor $M=A\otimes\overline A$, not merely for the
underlying pure MPS tensor $A$.

\section{A source-shaped counterexample}

Let $\omega=e^{2\pi i/3}$ and consider the pure one-letter tensor
\begin{align}
  A&=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&\omega\end{pmatrix}.
\end{align}
Let
\begin{align}
  S&=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}.
\end{align}
Since $\omega^3=1$, direct multiplication gives
\begin{align}
  A&=\omega S A^2S^{-1}.
  \label{eq:cube-block-gauge}
\end{align}
This has the orientation of the printed diagram: the original tensor is the
phase-scaled virtual conjugate of its two-site blocking. The corresponding
pure MPDO tensor is the doubled tensor
\begin{align}
  M&=A\otimes\overline A.
\end{align}
For a chain length $N\geq1$, let $V^{(N)}(A)$ denote the MPS wavefunction
generated by $A$. The tensor $M$ generates the rank-one operator
$\ketbra{V^{(N)}(A)}{V^{(N)}(A)}$, which is positive semidefinite. Thus $M$
satisfies the MPDO standing hypothesis in Definition \texttt{RFPMixedTS}.
To verify its canonical-form hypothesis, set $\mu_0=1$ and $\mu_1=\omega$, and
let $I_1$ denote the bond-one scalar unit tensor. After identifying a doubled
bond coordinate with a pair $(p,q)\in\{0,1\}^2$, the tensor decomposes as
\begin{align}
  M&=\bigoplus_{p,q\in\{0,1\}}
    \mu_p\overline{\mu_q}\,I_1.
\end{align}
Each copy of $I_1$ is normal because its transfer map is the identity, and
$|\mu_p\overline{\mu_q}|=1$. This gives CPSV canonical-form data whose four
weights satisfy the source normalization convention.

For one physical letter, both physical matrix algebras are one-dimensional.
Every unitary-conjugation channel on either algebra is therefore the identity,
so the source's physical channels contribute no phase. Instead, the concrete
MPS amplitude phase cancels when the tensor is doubled:
\begin{align}
  (\omega S A^{[2]}S^{-1})\otimes
  (\overline\omega\,\overline S\,\overline{A^{[2]}}\,\overline S^{-1})
  &=(\omega\overline\omega)
    (S\otimes\overline S)M^{[2]}(S\otimes\overline S)^{-1}\notag\\
  &=(S\otimes\overline S)M^{[2]}(S\otimes\overline S)^{-1}.
\end{align}
Thus $M^{[2]}$ and $M$ are related by the literal virtual gauge condition in
both directions of the Appendix~D diagram. The concrete MPS identity
in~\eqref{eq:cube-block-gauge} is used only to construct this exact doubled-MPDO
witness.

For a bond matrix $\rho$, define the one-letter transfer map by
\begin{align}
  \mathcal E_A(\rho)&=A\rho A^\dagger.
\end{align}
This transfer map is not idempotent. On the off-diagonal matrix unit $e_{21}$,
\begin{align}
  \mathcal E_A(e_{21})&=\omega e_{21}.
\end{align}
A second application gives
\begin{align}
  \mathcal E_A^2(e_{21})&=\omega^2e_{21}.
\end{align}
Because $\omega\ne1$, these values differ. Equivalently, the dyadic powers
$\mathcal E_A^{2^n}(e_{21})$ oscillate between the two nontrivial cube-root
phases and cannot converge, whereas an idempotent transfer map has every
positive power equal to itself.

\section{Verdict}

\textbf{Verdict: false as printed.}  The pure-state equivalence following
equation \texttt{RFP-gauge} fails even when the one-letter doubled MPO tensor
generates an MPDO family, is canonical, and satisfies the line-246
normalization. The ordinary theorem that a virtual gauge preserves an already
established renormalization fixed point is unaffected: gauge covariance cannot turn the
blocking-up-to-gauge hypothesis into transfer-map idempotence.

The printed pure spectral argument therefore cannot support the mixed-state
continuation in the next paragraph. This counterexample does not decide the
mixed-state question.
