\section{Source and restricted statement}
For a blocked matrix product map $B=VP$,
\cite[p.~3, footnotes~3--4 to eqs.~(13)--(15)]{Malz2024Preparation}
allows $B$ to be non-injective, interprets the inverse of $P$ as its
pseudoinverse, and permits the input to be placed at any chosen tensor,
with sequential decompositions from both boundaries toward that site.

The mixed polar factorization presently assumes that $B$ is injective.
Its positive polar factor is then invertible and $V^\dagger V=\Id_{D^2}$.
The two inward sweeps give isometric evaluations $E_L,E_R$ and a central
map $C$ with
\begin{align}
    V & =(E_L\otimes\Id_d\otimes E_R)C, & C^\dagger C & =\Id_{D^2}.
    \label{eq:mswc24_mixed_injective}
\end{align}
Both outer bonds have dimension one, and every bond has dimension at most
$D^2$. The input may be attached at either endpoint as well as at an
interior site.\footnote{Formal declarations:
    \leanid{MPSPreparation.exists_mixed_sequential_polarIsoMatrix_of_split} and
    \leanid{MPSPreparation.exists_mixed_sequential_polarIsoMatrix} in
    \doclink{TNLean/MPS/Preparation/MixedSequentialFactorization}.}

\section{Missing extension and elimination}
For non-injective $B$, let $\Pi$ be the orthogonal projector onto the
support of $P$ and let $P^+$ be its pseudoinverse. Then $V=BP^+$ satisfies
$V^\dagger V=\Pi$. If $V=EC$ with
$E=E_L\otimes\Id_d\otimes E_R$ isometric, then
$C^\dagger C=V^\dagger V=\Pi$, so $C$ need not be an isometry on all
$D^2$ input coordinates. Thus (\ref{eq:mswc24_mixed_injective}) cannot
simply be retained after dropping injectivity.

To remove this restriction, factor the pseudoinverse polar map by the same
inward sweeps and allow the central map to be a partial isometry with
initial projector $\Pi$. Equivalently, choose an isometry
$J\colon\C^r\to\C^{D^2}$ onto the support, where $r=\operatorname{rank}B$,
and state the mixed factorization for $VJ$, with a central isometry on
$\C^r$. No mixed theorem of either form is asserted here. The one-sided
factorization already treats the polar support after a unitary change of
input coordinates.\footnote{Formal declaration:
    \leanid{MPSPreparation.exists_isometric_chain_polarIsoMatrix_mul_unitary}
    in \doclink{TNLean/MPS/Preparation/SequentialFactorization}.}
