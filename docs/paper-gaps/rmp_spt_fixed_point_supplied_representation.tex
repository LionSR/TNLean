\section{What the source states}
In the paragraph ``Symmetry protected topological order'' of
\cite{Cirac2021Matrix}, a representative MPS with zero correlation length is
constructed ``starting from any solution of the 1- and 2-cocycle condition'': the
input is a 2-cocycle $\omega$ and a 1-cocycle $\phi$ of a finite group $G$
alone.\footnote{Source traceability:
    \path{Papers/2011.12127/TN-Review-main.tex}, lines 1147--1157.}

\section{What is formalized}
The dimer tensor $A^{(a,b)}=D^{-1/2}\ket{a}\bra{b}$ depends on the bond
dimension $D$ alone, and its transfer, idempotence, injectivity, normality and
linear-independence results involve no representation; the correction of the
printed tensor is recorded in \cite{gap:rmp_spt_fixed_point_tensor}. The
symmetry results take as input a projective representation $\rho$ of $G$ on
$\mathbb C^D$ whose factor system is $\omega$, together with a character $\phi$:
the physical action $\phi(g)\,W_g^{T}\otimes W_g^{-1}$, $W_g=\rho(g^{-1})$, its
twist identity, the on-site symmetry, and the realization of the class of
$\omega$ (\leanid{MPSTensor.sptFixedPointAction},
\leanid{MPSTensor.twistedTensor_sptFixedPointTensor},
\leanid{MPSTensor.sptFixedPointTensor_isOnSiteSymmetric},
\leanid{MPSTensor.cohomologousTo_of_sptFixedPointTensor},
\leanid{MPSTensor.exists_virtualRep_sptFixedPointTensor}). These results
hold for any group. For a finite group, the next section supplies such a
representation for every 2-cocycle class. Its factor system is a representative
of the class of $\omega$, not $\omega$ itself. Composed with these results, it
gives the source's construction from the cocycle alone.

\section{Resolution for finite groups}
For finite $G$ every 2-cocycle class arises. The twisted left regular matrices
\begin{align}
    L^{\phi}_g\ket{h} & =\phi(g,h)^{-1}\ket{gh}
    \label{eq:rmp_spt_supplied_twisted_regular}
\end{align}
on $\mathbb C^G$, built from a circle-valued 2-cocycle $\phi$, are unitary and
satisfy $L^{\phi}_gL^{\phi}_h=\phi(g,h)^{-1}L^{\phi}_{gh}$. Apply this to
$\phi=\bar\omega^{-1}$, where $\bar\omega=\omega/|\omega|$ is the pointwise phase of $\omega$; for
finite $G$ it is cohomologous to $\omega$. The result is a unitary projective representation with factor
system $\bar\omega$, which is cohomologous to $\omega$
(\leanid{TNLean.Algebra.ScalarCocycle.regularProjectiveForClass},
\leanid{TNLean.Algebra.ScalarCocycle.regularProjectiveForClass_mem_unitaryGroup}).
Feeding it to the construction gives
\leanid{MPSTensor.exists_unitary_sptFixedPoint_of_isCocycle}: from $\omega$ alone,
the fixed point of bond dimension $|G|$ is symmetric under a unitary physical
action, \leanid{MPSTensor.sptFixedPointAction_mem_unitaryGroup}, and every virtual
projective representation of that symmetry has a factor system cohomologous to
$\omega$.

The factor system of the supplied representation is $\bar\omega$, not $\omega$
itself. The source's claim concerns the class of $\omega$, so the class-level
statement is the faithful one. The results that take a representation $\rho$ as
input remain available for arbitrary groups. The trivial-character and on-site
restrictions are recorded separately in
\cite{gap:rmp_spt_fixed_point_trivial_character} and
\cite{gap:rmp_spt_fixed_point_onsite_scope}.
