\section{The source statement}

The entropy estimates of~\cite{Schuch2008EntropyScaling} bound the entropy of
a block of a state approximated by a matrix product state $\phi$ of bond
dimension $D$ through the rank of the block's reduced matrix, and use the
single-bond expression $\log D$ for that rank.\footnote{\path{paper_v2.tex},
    lines~269--305.}

\section{The point at issue}

The single-bond expression counts one cut. A prefix (or suffix) of an
open-boundary matrix product state is separated from its complement by a
single cut, and its reduced matrix has rank at most $D$. An interior block,
written as a difference of nested prefixes $B=T\setminus R$, is separated by
two cuts. Its reduced-matrix rank is bounded by the product of the two
complementary cut ranks,
\begin{align}
    \operatorname{rank}\rho_{\phi,B}
      &\le\operatorname{rank}\rho_{\phi,R}\,
        \operatorname{rank}\rho_{\phi,T}
      \le D\cdot D=D^2.
    \label{eq:swvc08_interior_rank_product}
\end{align}
Using $\log D$ in place of $\log(D^2)$ therefore understates the rank budget
of an interior block by a factor of two in the logarithm.

\section{The corrected statement}

For an interior block the rank budget is $\max(1,D)^2$, and its logarithmic
contribution is
\begin{align}
    \log\bigl(\max(1,D)^2\bigr)&=2\log D,
    \label{eq:swvc08_interior_two_log}
\end{align}
with the convention $\log0=0$, so chains of length zero or one and bond
dimension zero are included. At an endpoint one of the two cut ranks is at
most one, and (\ref{eq:swvc08_interior_rank_product}) recovers the single-bond
value $\log D$ for a prefix or suffix. The corrected factor two enters both
the R\'enyi and the von Neumann block
bounds.\footnote{\leanid{TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.IsOpenMPS.rank_regionState_sdiff_leftBlock_le_sq}
    and \leanid{TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.IsOpenMPS.renyiEntropy_sdiff_leftBlock_add_log_error_le_two_log}
    in \doclink{TNLean/MPS/AreaLaw/EntropyScaling/RenyiApproximationLowerBound};
    \leanid{TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.IsOpenMPS.regionEntropy_sdiff_leftBlock_le_two_log_add_half_error}
    in \doclink{TNLean/MPS/AreaLaw/EntropyScaling/VonNeumannApproximationLowerBound}.}

\section{Conclusion}

The verdict is a resolved local correction: the single-bond expression is
correct for a boundary block but understates an interior block, whose two cuts
give the rank bound (\ref{eq:swvc08_interior_rank_product}) and hence the
logarithmic contribution (\ref{eq:swvc08_interior_two_log}).
