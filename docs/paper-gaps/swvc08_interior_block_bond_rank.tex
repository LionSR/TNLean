\section{The source passage}

Schuch, Wolf, Verstraete and Cirac~\cite{Schuch2008EntropyScaling}
define matrix product states with open boundaries and bond dimension $D$,
but subsequently allow $\rho_N^L$ to be any reduced block of $L$ sites.
The estimate $S(\sigma_{N,D}^L)\le\log D$ used in their von Neumann entropy
argument is valid for a block at a boundary of the chain. For an interior
contiguous block the bound is $2\log D$. The same distinction applies to
the rank cutoff in the subsequent R\'enyi argument.\footnote{%
arXiv:0705.0292v2, \path{paper_v2.tex}: open-boundary definition,
lines 181--196; single-bond rank bound, lines 234--264; reduced blocks,
lines 269--270; von Neumann bound, lines 298--305; R\'enyi argument,
lines 317--345.}
All logarithms below have base two. Entropies are taken on normalized
density matrices; the local dimension is $d\ge2$ and $D\ge1$.

\section{Rank bound and an exact counterexample}

Let $B=\{a+1,\ldots,b\}$ with $0<a<b<N$. Cutting the two bonds at its
ends gives an expansion
\[
 |\phi\rangle=\sum_{u=1}^{D_a}\sum_{v=1}^{D_b}
 |L_u\rangle\otimes|M_{uv}\rangle\otimes|R_v\rangle .
\]
The reduced operator $\sigma_B$ is supported on the span of the $D_aD_b$
vectors $M_{uv}$. Thus
\[
 \operatorname{rank}\sigma_B\le D_aD_b\le D^2,
 \qquad S_\alpha(\sigma_B)\le\log\operatorname{rank}\sigma_B\le2\log D
 \quad(0\le\alpha\le\infty).
\]
The rank assertion does not require $\phi$ to be normalized; the entropy
assertion does. A proper prefix or suffix cuts only one bond and retains
the bound $\log D$.

For sharpness, take four qubits in the state
\[
 |\phi\rangle=|\Phi^+\rangle_{12}\otimes|\Phi^+\rangle_{34},
 \qquad |\Phi^+\rangle=(|00\rangle+|11\rangle)/\sqrt2.
\]
This is an open-boundary MPS of bond dimension $2$. Explicitly, for the
standard columns $e_0,e_1\in\mathbb C^2$, its matrices are
\[
 A^{[1]}_i=e_i^{\mathsf T}/\sqrt2,\quad
 A^{[2]}_j=e_je_0^{\mathsf T},\quad
 A^{[3]}_k=e_0e_k^{\mathsf T}/\sqrt2,\quad
 A^{[4]}_l=e_l.
\]
Their product is $\delta_{ij}\delta_{kl}/2$. The block $B=\{2,3\}$ has
reduced state $I_4/4$, rank $4$, and $S_\alpha=2$ for every order, whereas
$\log D=1$. Taking the target and approximant equal gives error zero, so
this contradicts the rank-$D$ reading independently of any error constant
or sign convention.

\section{Corrected approximation bounds and status}

For normalized target and approximant, the source's continuity argument
with full trace-norm error at most $\delta$ gives
\[
 2\log D\ge S(\rho_N^L)-\tfrac12\delta L\log d-1.
\]
If $S(\rho_N^L)\ge cL$, $L\ge\eta N$, and $\delta<2c/\log d$, the right
side is at least $\eta(c-\delta\log d/2)N-1$. The factor two only halves
the resulting exponential lower bound on $D$.

For the R\'enyi argument, let the target be normalized and $\alpha>1$.
The approximating vector may be unnormalized. Its reduced positive operator
still has rank at most $r=D^2$, so the target's spectral tail beyond $r$
obeys $\varepsilon(r)\le\delta$ by trace-norm contractivity and the
rank-constrained spectral-tail bound. Convexity gives
\[
 S_\alpha(\rho_N^L)\le\log r
       -\frac{\alpha}{\alpha-1}\log(1-\varepsilon(r)).
\]
Consequently, for $0\le\delta<1$,
\[
 2\log D\ge S_\alpha(\rho_N^L)
       -\frac{\alpha}{\alpha-1}|\log(1-\delta)|.
\]
The minus sign is the separate correction recorded in
\path{swvc08_renyi_bond_dimension_sign.tex}. If
$S_\alpha(\rho_N^L)\ge c\eta^\kappa N^\kappa$ with $c,\eta,\kappa>0$,
then $D\ge2^{(c\eta^\kappa N^\kappa-C_\delta)/2}$, where
$C_\delta=\alpha|\log(1-\delta)|/(\alpha-1)$ is independent of $N$.
This still excludes polynomial bond dimension. For a prefix or suffix,
replace $2\log D$ by $\log D$ throughout.

This note resolves the two-boundary correction mathematically. It does not
claim a new Lean proof of the interior-block rank estimate or of the full
approximation obstruction. That development remains open in
\ghissue{9857}; a proof restricted to boundary blocks must be identified as
such. No assertion about disconnected sets of sites is made here.
