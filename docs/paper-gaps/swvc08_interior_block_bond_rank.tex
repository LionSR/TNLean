\section{The source passage}

Schuch--Wolf--Verstraete--Cirac bound the R\'enyi entropy of a reduced state of
a target vector by the logarithm of the bond dimension of an approximating
open-boundary matrix product state \cite{Schuch2008EntropyScaling}. The printed
estimate is stated for a single cut, where the reduced matrix of the
approximant has rank at most the bond dimension $D$.\footnote{Source:
\cite{Schuch2008EntropyScaling}, lines~269--270 and~333--345.}

\section{The deviation}

A single bond bound governs only a prefix or suffix of the chain, where the
block is separated from its complement by one cut. An interior contiguous block
$B$ is separated from its complement by two cuts. Writing $B=T\setminus R$ for
nested prefixes $R\subseteq T$, the complement of $B$ is the disjoint union
$R\cup T^{c}$, and equality of complementary ranks together with the product
bound for ranks on a disjoint union gives
\begin{align}
    \operatorname{rank}\rho_{\phi,B}
        &\le\operatorname{rank}\rho_{\phi,R}\,
            \operatorname{rank}\rho_{\phi,T}
        \le D^{2},
    \label{eq:swvc08_interior_block_bond_rank_product}
\end{align}
at an internal cut each factor being at most $D$ and at an endpoint at most
one. Consequently, for a unit target $\psi$, an approximant $\phi$ of bond
dimension at most $D$ with trace-norm error at most $\delta<1$, and
$\alpha>1$,
\begin{align}
    S_\alpha(\rho_{\psi,B})+\frac{\alpha}{\alpha-1}\log(1-\delta)
        &\le 2\log D.
    \label{eq:swvc08_interior_block_bond_rank_corrected}
\end{align}
The right side of the source's single-bond expression must therefore be doubled
for interior blocks. Empty blocks, the whole chain, and chains of length zero or
one are included, with the convention $\log 0=0$ for the zero bond budget.

\section{Conclusion}

The verdict is a local correction. The source's single-bond expression is the
prefix and suffix special case; for an interior block cut by two bonds the
reduced rank is bounded by $D^{2}$, so the logarithmic right side carries a
factor of two. The qualitative obstruction is unchanged.
