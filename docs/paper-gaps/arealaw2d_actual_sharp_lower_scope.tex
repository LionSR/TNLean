\begin{align}

\section{The same supplied label sequence}
The physical Schmidt--Bell construction already gives nonzero original
prevectors of norm at most one and
$\log\dim\ell_k-kS'=o(k)$ for its selected entropy $S'$.
The lower consumer uses these conclusions for the supplied sequence;
it does not select another sequence or postulate a lower norm bound.
Its literal prevector gives simultaneous symmetry and the same label on
both original auxiliary sites.
With $\varepsilon_k=2|\log\dim\ell_k-kS'|$, define
\begin{align}
    r_k&=\log(k+1)+c\log(k+2)+a\varepsilon_k,
    &b_k&=\tfrac12(k+2)^{-C_f},\nonumber\\
    R_k^-&=\frac{2|r_k|+|\log b_k|+\log2+\log(3/2)}{ak},
    &R_k^-&\longrightarrow0.
    \label{eq:actual_lower_remainder}
\end{align}
For every $0<\tau\leq1/2$, the resulting actual lower rate is
\begin{align}
    \frac a2\sum_g\left[
        2S'+(1-\tau)\sum_jw_jg_{jg}^{\rm pre}
        -2\tau\log d-(3+a^{-1})h(\tau)-25K_{\rm mom}a
        -\frac{\delta_k}{\tau}\max\{0,2S'+g_{\max}\}-R_k^-
    \right]
    &\leq\frac{-\log N_k}{k}.
    \label{eq:actual_lower_rate}
\end{align}
Here $N_k$ is the actual squared filtered norm, $w_j$ are the actual tree
weights, and $g_{jg}^{\rm pre}=S(Q_{jg})+S((Q_{jg}\cup Y_{jg})^c)-S(Y_{jg})$.
The lower moment conditions are exactly $10a\leq r_{\rm mom}$ and
$10a\leq1$, obtained from $t=a/2$ in the regional theorem.
The five original status marginal estimates supply these three moments.

\section{What remains}
Equation (\ref{eq:actual_lower_rate}) retains the actual defect rather than
assuming that it is small. The selected-charge energy estimate, the upper
comparison and this lower comparison must still be evaluated at one common
selected point with one target-window witness and one supplied sequence.
The source-scale specialization must establish the factor-ten moment
conditions, then the entropy telescope, density estimate and final
parameter absorption must bound the remaining terms.
This result does not assert the full sharp comparator, Proposition~9.6,
or the two-dimensional area law.
