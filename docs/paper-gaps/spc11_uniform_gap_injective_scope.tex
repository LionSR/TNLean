\section{What the source states}
Appendix~A of \cite{Schuch2011Phases} proves that the family $H_\gamma$ of
parent Hamiltonians along the path from a matrix product state to its
isometric form has a spectral gap bounded below uniformly in
$\gamma\in[0,1]$ and in the chain length. The tensors of the path are in
normal form, $A_i(\gamma)=\bigoplus_\alpha A_i^\alpha(\gamma)$, possibly with
several blocks. The argument applies Nachtergaele's bound, controlled by the
second transfer eigenvalue of each block, by the overlaps of the local spaces
of different blocks, and by the gap $\Delta_{2p}(\gamma)$ of $H_\gamma$
restricted to $2p$ sites. The last quantity has a uniform lower bound
``as the restricted Hamiltonian is continuous in $\gamma$''.\footnote{Source
    traceability: \path{Papers/1010.3732/paper_v3.tex}, lines 2475--2580; the
    continuity step is lines 2575--2578.}

\section{What is formalized}
Let $x\mapsto A_x$ be a continuous family of one-site injective tensors with
nonzero bond dimension, indexed by a topological space, and let $S$ be a
compact set of parameters. For the parent Hamiltonian $H_{N,2}(A_x)$ of the
canonical two-site interaction on a periodic chain of $N$ sites, there is
$\delta>0$ such that
\begin{align}
    \delta\lVert v\rVert & \leq\lVert H_{N,2}(A_x)v\rVert
    \qquad\text{for all }x\in S,\ N\geq2,\ v\perp\ker H_{N,2}(A_x).
    \label{eq:spc11_uniform_gap_injective_scope_bound}
\end{align}
The proof uses the continuity step of the source for finite windows. In
place of Nachtergaele's bound it uses Knabe's nearest-neighbor criterion
together with compactness of $S$. Non-injective normal forms with several
blocks, where the overlaps between blocks enter, are not covered.
\footnote{Affected formal declarations:

The finite-window continuity input is also established for several blocks,
provided the joint boundary map is injective at a common length. The joint boundary map uses only diagonal
virtual boundaries; its range is the sum of the block ground spaces and
its injectivity gives a continuous range projection by the inverse-Gram
formula. This proves continuity of canonical interactions and of the
finite-volume open and periodic parent Hamiltonians. Nonzero block
weights do not affect the ground spaces. Joint-boundary injectivity is
open and persists at larger lengths under trace-preserving normalization.
For a compact continuous family, pointwise simultaneous word spanning
therefore gives one common injectivity threshold. In particular, this
applies to normalized primitive blocks with positive definite fixed points
that are pairwise inequivalent under similarity and phase. Continuity of
the fixed-point matrices is unnecessary. These facts do not yet supply
the uniform multi-block gap.

\section{Elimination plan}
For a continuous family of tensors in normal form with a fixed number of
normal blocks, apply the block-diagonal parent-Hamiltonian gap with its
finite-window input, and show that this input depends continuously on the
parameter. The ingredients are the second transfer eigenvalues of the blocks
and the overlaps between the local spaces of different blocks, which stay
bounded away from one on a compact parameter set. The finite-window
continuity and compactness steps already formalized then apply without
change. The injective case
(\ref{eq:spc11_uniform_gap_injective_scope_bound}) is the case of one block.
