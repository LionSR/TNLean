\section{The source statement}

Wolf's proposition "Extending cp maps from operator systems" quantifies over
two arbitrary finite-dimensional $C^\ast$-algebras $\mathcal A,\mathcal B$: let
$T:S\to\mathcal B$ be a completely positive linear map from an operator system
$S\subseteq\mathcal A$; then there is a completely positive map
$\tilde T:\mathcal A\to\mathcal B$ agreeing with $T$ on $S$
\cite[Proposition, lines~614--626]{Wolf2012Quantum}. The local source is
\path{Notes/WolfNoteTexSource/ch01_deconstructing_quantum.tex}, lines~617--619
(statement) and 620--626 (proof). Neither $\mathcal A$ nor $\mathcal B$ is
assumed to be a full matrix algebra: a finite-dimensional $C^\ast$-algebra is,
by the finite-dimensional structure theorem, a direct sum
$\bigoplus_k M_{d_k}(\mathbb C)$, and the proposition covers every such
algebra, not only the one-summand case $\mathcal A=M_m(\mathbb C)$.

\section{The formalized scope}

The formal development takes both ambient algebras to be full matrix algebras,
$\mathcal A=\MN{m}$ and $\mathcal B=\MN{n}$: \leanid{Matrix.IsOperatorSystem}
(\doclink{TNLean/Channel/OperatorSystem}) is a predicate on a
\leanid{Submodule ℂ} of $\MN{m}$, and the extension theorem
\leanid{Matrix.exists_cp_extension_of_operatorSystem}
(\doclink{TNLean/Channel/OperatorSystemExtension}) produces
$\tilde T:\MN{m}\to\MN{n}$. This is not merely an ambient convenience: the
choice of $\mathcal B=M_n(\mathbb C)$ tracks the source proof's own reduction,
which fixes the ancilla dimension $n$ so that $\mathcal B$ is a subalgebra of
$M_n(\mathbb C)$ and never revisits the direct-sum structure of $\mathcal A$
after that point. What remains unformalized is the direct-sum case of the
\emph{domain} algebra $\mathcal A=\bigoplus_k M_{d_k}(\mathbb C)$ with more
than one summand, together with the corresponding operator-system predicate
and Hahn--Banach argument stated for a subspace of that direct sum.

\section{Verdict}

\textbf{Open scope restriction.} The full-matrix-algebra case
$\mathcal A=M_m(\mathbb C)$ is a faithful formalization of Wolf's proposition
restricted to a one-summand domain algebra; no hypothesis is added beyond that
restriction (the operator-system and complete-positivity predicates match the
source's definitions verbatim on $M_m(\mathbb C)$). Generalizing to an
arbitrary finite-dimensional $\mathcal A=\bigoplus_k M_{d_k}(\mathbb C)$ is not
attempted here. The proof technique embeds directly (a subspace of a direct
sum of matrix algebras is again a subspace of a single larger matrix algebra
via block-diagonal embedding, so the extension argument itself should carry
over), but the embedding step and the transport of the operator-system and
complete-positivity predicates along it are separate work, tracked in
\ghissue{5589}.
