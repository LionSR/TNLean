\section{The source lemmas}

Lemmas~6.5 and~6.6 of the polynomial PEPS approximation manuscript of
September~24, 2026 (\path{05-frames.tex}, lines~396--570) change the ownership
of registers of encoded frames.

\emph{Lemma~6.5 (homogeneous birth and death).} In an encoded frame
$\mathcal F$, let $T$ be a set of raw sites owned by a party $P_\circ$ and
disjoint from every outer hole footprint, and put
$E=\{x:\text{the raw owner of }x\neq P_\circ\}\cup H^+(\mathcal F)$ and
$U=\Lambda\setminus(T\cup E)$. If $I_\Omega(T:E)\le L^{-60}$, changing the owner
of $T$ to $Q_\circ$, with every hole, encoding and tag owner kept, ``is a bounded
change involving only $P_\circ,Q_\circ$, with reference-vector error at most
$L^{-30}$''; the reversed death obeys the same bound when the conditions hold in
the frame after the death.

\emph{Lemma~6.6 (two-sheet exchange).} For two encoded frames and a region $Y$
such that every hole's outer square lies on one side of $\partial Y$, with
$Z=\{x:\text{the two old raw owners of }x\text{ are not both }P_\circ\}\cup
H^+(\mathcal F_1)\cup H^+(\mathcal F_2)$, $T=Z\cap Y$, $E=Z\setminus Y$ and
$U=\Lambda\setminus Z$: if $I_\Omega(T:E)\le L^{-60}$, exchanging the raw
ownership assignments and the encoded holes inside $Y$ ``can be implemented by
private contractions and register renaming, with reference-vector error at most
$4L^{-30}$'', using $P_\circ$ and the owners of the renamed registers and tags,
hence is a bounded change in the sense of Theorem~5.2.

In both lemmas the information bound is a hypothesis; it is supplied later by the
manuscript's proposition \texttt{prop:angular}.

\section{What the formal statements cover}

Both lemmas are formalized with exactly their source hypotheses, the information
bound included.\footnote{%
\leanid{TNLean.PEPS.EncodedFrame.Frame.birth},
\leanid{TNLean.PEPS.EncodedFrame.Frame.death},
\leanid{TNLean.PEPS.EncodedFrame.TwoSheetExchange.exchange} and
\leanid{TNLean.PEPS.EncodedFrame.TwoSheetExchange.exchange_ofFrames}.}
The formal statements produce the canonical maps of the proofs and prove:
\begin{enumerate}
\item for the birth and the death, that the canonical map
  $B=\mathcal V^\dagger(\ket s\bra s_{T\mathcal B_T}\otimes\mathrm{id}_{E\mathcal
  B_E})\mathcal V$ is a Hermitian contraction, that it acts as the identity on
  $E$ and on all tags, that it commutes with every hole encoder, and that the
  reference error is at most $L^{-30}$; the map factors as
  $B=(\mathcal V^\dagger S)(S^\dagger\mathcal V)$, where $S$ inserts the single
  normalized vector $s$ on $T\mathcal B_T$, and every register on which $B$ acts
  lies outside $E$, so it is held by $P_\circ$ before the birth and by $P_\circ$
  or $Q_\circ$ after it; since the frames before and after differ only in raw
  owners, their encodings and reference vectors coincide, so the change of owner
  itself is carried entirely by the bounded-change clause below;
\item for the exchange, the exact identity $\mathcal RK_{\rm in}=K_{\rm out}F_{Y\cap
  \Lambda}$ for the renaming $\mathcal R$, the identity $D_UF_T=(\mathcal
  V^{\otimes2})^\dagger F_{T\mathcal B_T}\mathcal V^{\otimes2}$, the identity
  $CK_{\rm in}=K_{\rm out}D_UF_T$ for $C=D_UF_A\mathcal R$, contractivity of $C$,
  and the error bound $4L^{-30}$ on the product of the two encoded reference
  vectors, which are not assumed normalized; the corrections $D_U$ and $F_A$
  act on the registers of $U$ alone, which are held by $P_\circ$ on both sheets
  before and after the exchange, and the renaming keeps every register at its
  party.
\end{enumerate}

The bounded-change clauses are formalized on the party layout of Theorem~5.2
used for Lemma~5.1, in which a monomial is a composition of contractions acting
on registers of one party, normalized pair sources and normalized pair effects
on registers of two named parties, and exchanges of tensor factors.\footnote{%
\leanid{TNLean.PEPS.EncodedFrame.Frame.birth_monomial},
\leanid{TNLean.PEPS.EncodedFrame.Frame.death_monomial} and
\leanid{TNLean.PEPS.EncodedFrame.TwoSheetExchange.exchange_correction_monomial}.}
The raw registers of $T$, and those of $U$, are written as one register each,
and all other registers (those of $E$, the tags and any other sheet) form an
arbitrary list of registers that the monomial does not touch. This grouping is
a convention: the registers of $T$ have one owner before and one after the
change, those of $U$ are held by $P_\circ$ throughout, and regrouping registers
of one party is a private unitary. In these terms:
\begin{enumerate}
\item for $Q_\circ\neq P_\circ$ the birth is the private contraction
  $\bra s(\mathrm{id}_T\otimes V)$ at $P_\circ$ from $T U$ to $\mathcal B_E$, the
  normalized pair source $s$ on a fresh register $T$ of $Q_\circ$ and a fresh
  register $\mathcal B_T$ of $P_\circ$, and the private contraction
  $V^\dagger$ at $P_\circ$ from $\mathcal B_T\mathcal B_E$ to $U$; its operator,
  with the registers of $T\cup U$ identified with $\mathcal H_T\otimes\mathcal
  H_U$ before and after, is $K^\dagger K\otimes\mathrm{id}$ with
  $K=\bra s(\mathrm{id}_T\otimes V)$, and $B=\mathrm{id}_E\otimes K^\dagger K$;
\item the death is the private isometry $V$ at $P_\circ$, the normalized pair
  effect $\bra s$ on $T$ held by $Q_\circ$ and $\mathcal B_T$ held by $P_\circ$,
  and the private contraction $K^\dagger$ at $P_\circ$, with the same operator;
\item for $Q_\circ=P_\circ$ both are the single private contraction
  $K^\dagger K$ at $P_\circ$;
\item for the exchange, the corrections $D_UF_A$ are $\mathrm{id}\otimes W$ with $W$ acting
  on the two copies of $U$, and $W\otimes\mathrm{id}$ is one private contraction at
  $P_\circ$ on the two registers of $U$, both held by $P_\circ$.
\end{enumerate}

Not formalized is the reading of the renaming $\mathcal R$ of the exchange as a
word of exchanges of tensor factors. The renaming is formalized as the
identification of the tensor factors of the two layouts that keeps every
register at its party; writing it as a composition of exchanges of tensor
factors needs a list of registers with one register per site and per tag,
identified with the canonical coordinates of the two sheets, which the library
does not construct. Since the renaming changes no register and no owner, it
adds no private contraction, pair source or pair effect.

The formal frames record the outer footprints of holes by their physical
samples. The source's condition that every outer square lies on one side of
$\partial Y$ enters as a classification of the holes as outside or inside $Y$,
which is part of the data, together with its consequence for the samples: the
sample of a hole classified as outside avoids $Y$, and that of a hole classified
as inside lies in $Y$. A hole whose outer square has empty sample is compatible
with both sides, so the side read from the plane must be supplied, and the
formal statement accepts it. The exchange is also stated for two frames whose
holes are listed in any order: a relabelling $G$ of the tags identifies their
encodings with those of the listings by side (\path{05-frames.tex},
lines~84--85), and the statement concerns the explicit map $CG$, with
$CG(K_{\mathcal F_1}\otimes K_{\mathcal F_2})=K_{\rm out}D_UF_T$ and the
same error bound.

\section{Elimination plan}

Introduce, for a frame, the list of registers with one raw register per site
and one register per tag, each owned by its owner in the frame, together with
the identification of its tensor product with the canonical coordinates. The
renaming of the exchange is then a permutation of this list that keeps every
owner, hence a word of exchanges of tensor factors; composed with the private
contraction of the corrections proved here, after moving the registers of $U$
to the front by exchanges, it gives the whole exchange as an allowed monomial.
