\section{The source passage}

In the standing Case~I setting of Cirac--P\'erez-Garc\'ia--Schuch--Verstraete,
$\mathcal K$ is an injective normal tensor generating matrix product density
operators \cite{Cirac2016MPDO_arXiv}. Lemma~\texttt{propSN} additionally
assumes saturation of the area law and produces an isometry $U$ and factor
families $l_k$ and $r_k$ such that
\begin{align}
    U\mathcal K_{\beta,\alpha}U^*
        &=
    \bigoplus_k (l_k)_\beta\otimes(r_k)_\alpha.
    \label{eq:cpsv16_active_physical_support_compression_sector_factorization}
\end{align}
It defines
\begin{align}
    \eta_{k,h}
        &=
    \sum_a (r_k)_a\otimes(l_h)_a,
    \label{eq:cpsv16_active_physical_support_compression_neighboring_operator}\\
    \eta_{k,h}
        &\geq 0,
    \label{eq:cpsv16_active_physical_support_compression_neighboring_positivity}\\
    T_{k,h}
        &=
    \tr(\eta_{k,h}).
    \label{eq:cpsv16_active_physical_support_compression_transition_matrix}
\end{align}
The source proves that the nonnegative matrix $T$ is primitive
\cite{Cirac2016MPDO_arXiv}.\footnote{Source:
\path{Papers/1606.00608/MPDO-22-12-17-2.tex}:1374--1471. The definitions of
$U$, $r_k$, $l_k$, and $\eta_{k,h}$ occur at lines~1436--1450. The definition
of $T_{k,h}$ occurs at lines~1452--1455, and its primitivity is proved through
line~1471.}

The cited passage does not construct the active physical support compression.
It does not choose isometries onto the joint column supports of the left and
right factor families, assign zero-dimensional coordinate spaces to inactive
sectors, or assemble the compressed injective tensor.

Proposition~\texttt{3to4} uses the uncompressed neighboring operators on the
full factor spaces to construct commuting positive bonds
\cite{Cirac2016MPDO_arXiv}.\footnote{Source:
\path{Papers/1606.00608/MPDO-22-12-17-2.tex}:1570--1594.}
The later Proposition~\texttt{prop3to4} concerns a simple tensor with a basis
of normal tensors and invokes the preceding injective result
\cite{Cirac2016MPDO_arXiv}.\footnote{Source:
\path{Papers/1606.00608/MPDO-22-12-17-2.tex}:1786--1797.}
These are distinct source statements.

\section{The compression construction}

The formal development adds an auxiliary compression after
Lemma~\texttt{propSN} and before the argument corresponding to
Proposition~\texttt{prop3to4} of Ref.~\cite{Cirac2016MPDO_arXiv}. The
construction appears in
\doclink{TNLean/MPS/MPDO/PhysicalSectorActiveFactorSupport},
\doclink{TNLean/MPS/MPDO/PhysicalSectorActiveSupportData},
\doclink{TNLean/MPS/MPDO/PhysicalSectorActiveRestriction},
\doclink{TNLean/MPS/MPDO/PhysicalSectorActiveNeighboring}, and
\doclink{TNLean/MPS/MPDO/PhysicalSectorCoordinateTransport}.

\begin{enumerate}
    \item For each active sector $k$, choose isometries $V^L_k$ and $V^R_k$
    whose ranges are the joint column supports of
    $\{l_{k,\beta}\}_\beta$ and $\{r_{k,\alpha}\}_\alpha$. Give each inactive
    sector zero-dimensional left and right coordinate spaces. The product
    range is exactly the family-support projection of the sector product
    family. Injectivity of the original tensor and positivity of every
    neighboring operator imply the adjoint-closure relation required for
    two-sided absorption by these support projections.

    \item Define the compressed factors by
    \begin{align}
        \widetilde l_{k,\beta}
            &=
        (V^L_k)^*l_{k,\beta}V^L_k,
        \label{eq:cpsv16_active_physical_support_compression_left_factor}\\
        \widetilde r_{k,\alpha}
            &=
        (V^R_k)^*r_{k,\alpha}V^R_k.
        \label{eq:cpsv16_active_physical_support_compression_right_factor}
    \end{align}

    \item Assemble the sector support isometries into a block-diagonal
    isometry, transport it to the original physical coordinates through
    $U^*$, and obtain
    \begin{align}
        V
            &:
        \C^e\longrightarrow\C^d.
        \label{eq:cpsv16_active_physical_support_compression_physical_inclusion}
    \end{align}
    The projection $VV^*$ is the canonical joint column support of the
    original physical slices.

    \item Define the active restriction by
    \begin{align}
        K_{\mathrm{act}}
            &=
        V^*KV.
        \label{eq:cpsv16_active_physical_support_compression_restricted_tensor}
    \end{align}
    It acts on $\C^e$, where $e$ is the total dimension of the retained
    supports. Its injectivity follows from injectivity of $K$ and positivity
    of every neighboring operator.

    \item The compressed neighboring operator is the congruence of the
    ambient one by the Kronecker product of the source right-support isometry
    and target left-support isometry. Congruence preserves positive
    semidefiniteness.

    \item Suppose the ambient operators satisfy the notation and normalization
    of CPSV16 Theorem~4.9(iv):
    \begin{align}
        \tr(\eta_{k,h})
            &=
        a_kb_h,
        \label{eq:cpsv16_active_physical_support_compression_trace_factorization}\\
        \sum_k a_kb_k
            &=
        1.
        \label{eq:cpsv16_active_physical_support_compression_trace_normalization}
    \end{align}
    The published theorem appears in
    Ref.~\cite{Cirac2017MPDO_AnnPhys}. Compression preserves
    $\tr(\eta_{k,h})$ when $k$ and $h$ are active. If $k$ is inactive, then
    \begin{align}
        \eta_{k,k}
            &= 0,
        \label{eq:cpsv16_active_physical_support_compression_inactive_neighbor}\\
        a_kb_k
            &= 0.
        \label{eq:cpsv16_active_physical_support_compression_inactive_weight}
    \end{align}
    Restricting $a_k$ and $b_k$ to active sectors therefore preserves
    Eqs.~\ref{eq:cpsv16_active_physical_support_compression_trace_factorization}
    and
    \ref{eq:cpsv16_active_physical_support_compression_trace_normalization}.
    This transport is auxiliary; it does not prove condition~(iv).

    \item The coordinate operation
    \begin{align}
        M
            &\longmapsto
        VMV^*.
        \label{eq:cpsv16_active_physical_support_compression_coordinate_transport}
    \end{align}
    transports an MPO tensor together with its two-site and four-site
    closures.
\end{enumerate}

None of these compression steps appears in lines~1406--1471 of
Ref.~\cite{Cirac2016MPDO_arXiv}. The source supplies the sector factorization
and neighboring operators. The formal route adds compression to remove
inactive dimensions before applying the theorem corresponding to
Proposition~\texttt{prop3to4}. Injectivity and neighboring-operator positivity
imply that the joint column-support projections absorb every physical slice on
both sides. Compression by their adjoints therefore preserves exact two-sided
recovery.

\section{Conclusion}

The verdict is a scope restriction. The formal development first restricts to
the canonical active physical support and records the coordinate changes
separately. The source propositions in
Ref.~\cite{Cirac2016MPDO_arXiv} do not contain these constructions. The
compression itself is verified; this note records the distinction between the
source argument and the route used in the formal development.
