\section{The printed example}

The source Heisenberg shift sends the observable algebra at site $y$ to
that at $y-1$ \cite[lines~609--621]{Gross2012IndexTheory}. Thus it moves
observables left and states right. For each $x\in\mathbb Z$ write
$E_x=\{2x,2x+1\}$, $L_x=\{2x-1,2x\}$ and $P_x=\{2x+1,2x+2\}$, and let
$\mathcal R_{2x}$ and $\mathcal R_{2x+1}$ be the left and right support
algebras of the image of $\mathcal A_{E_x}$ in the ordered
$L_x\times P_x$ coordinates, as in equation \texttt{RR2x} of
\cite[lines~1251--1266]{Gross2012IndexTheory}. The shift example at
source line~1268 lists the two support algebras of the shift with the even
subscript repeated for the scalar factor.

\section{The corrected subscript}

Translation by $-1$ sends $E_x$ onto $L_x$, so its finite image is the
full left factor in the ordered $L_x\times P_x$ coordinates.
Consequently
\begin{align}
    \mathcal R_{2x}   & =\mathcal A_{L_x}, &
    \mathcal R_{2x+1} & =\mathbb C I.
    \label{eq:gnvw12_shift_corrected_support_subscript}
\end{align}
The second subscript must be odd, as in
(\ref{eq:gnvw12_shift_corrected_support_subscript}). For nontrivial
on-site dimension, the full left factor and the scalar factor are
distinct algebras, so the repeated even subscript cannot be read
literally. The correction changes only the subscript; the surrounding
argument of the source is unchanged.

The actual sizes are $r(2x)=d^2$ and $r(2x+1)=1$, hence the positive
rational index is $d$, as stated in
\cite[lines~1325--1329]{Gross2012IndexTheory}. Translation by $+1$ has
the opposite pair of support factors and index $d^{-1}$. The latter is a
direct orientation check in the same coordinates, not a use of an
index-composition or index-inversion law.

\section{Status}

The corrected support factors and both index values are proved for the
homogeneous chain.%
