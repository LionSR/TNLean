\paragraph{Source.}
The one-dimensional note~\cite{TNLean2026OneDimensional}, Part~II,
Construction \texttt{con:band} and Proposition \texttt{prop:sweep}
(\path{part2b_mps_protocol.tex}, lines~248--276 and 349--360),
requires two local corrections. Neither introduces a new hypothesis.

\paragraph{Lattice interfaces.}
The source fixes an integer width $w\ge1$ and initially specifies
$[a,a+w)$, but writes the subsequent scaled bands as open intervals.
Choose an interior band with $8w\le a$ and $a+2w\le L$.
Then $0<a-3w<L$, so $z=a-3w$ is an actual chain site with scaled
coordinate $-3$. It is initially held by $C$ and lies outside the exchange
window $(-7,-7/2)$. The first two main-sheet deaths send it to $A$ and
then $B$. It belongs to neither $(-3,0)$ nor $(-6,-3)$, so remains at
$B$, contrary to the asserted final $C$ ownership left of zero. This
witness persists at every positive integer width; it is not a degenerate
case.

Interpret integer-ended bands as half-open lattice intervals $[u,v)$,
including their first sites, consistently with the initial band and the
inclusive sweep intervals (lines~333--342). Retain the open real exchange
window $(-7,-7/2)$; no parity assumption on $w$ is needed. The ownership
calculation then restores the original main-sheet owner outside
$[a,a+w)$ and assigns that band to $B$. The auxiliary sheet ends at $A$
on $[a-3w,a-2w)$, at $B$ on $[a-2w,a-w)$, and at $C$ elsewhere.
The seven information margins and the exchange margin are at least $w$.

\paragraph{Distinguished physical outputs.}
The source explicitly retains auxiliary registers at their actual owners
(lines~271--272), yet later says that each output party holds precisely
its block registers and no other party holds any raw register
(lines~355--360). The auxiliary ownership just computed contradicts that
all-register assertion. Restrict the block ownership and dimension bound
$q^{|B_i|}\le q^{4t}$ to the distinguished main sheet. Retain every
auxiliary register at its actual owner. Their reference factor $\Xi$ is a
tensor product across copies, not necessarily across parties.

\paragraph{Constructed terminal readout.}
The terminal regrouping is implemented by a word of party-private operations.
At each owner it encodes the main registers into the physical block, gives
a scalar physical output when that block is empty, and retains every
auxiliary copy-site label privately at its actual owner. The evaluation of
this word is the prescribed coordinate isometry. For every terminal vector,
its physical density is exactly the partial trace over the original
auxiliary labels, expressed in the main-block coordinates. This identity
requires neither normalization nor a product assumption on that vector.\footnote{
Declarations \leanid{TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.ChainSweep.eval_terminalRegroupingWord}
and \leanid{TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.ChainSweep.physicalDensity_terminalRegrouping}.}

For $q\ge1$, the specified reference has the exact readout
$\Omega\otimes\Xi$, where
$\Xi$ is the product of the original labelled auxiliary copies. A unit
$\Omega$ gives $\|\Xi\|=1$, including the empty family of copies. Thus
\begin{align}
    \operatorname{Tr}_{\rm aux}
    \bigl|\Omega\otimes\Xi\bigr\rangle
    \bigl\langle\Omega\otimes\Xi\bigr|
     & =|\Omega\rangle\langle\Omega|.
    \label{eq:tnlean_one_dimensional_band_sweep_corrections_reference_partial_trace}
\end{align}
The normalization and this identity are proved for the constructed reference,
without requiring a product across final parties.\footnote{Declaration
\leanid{TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.ChainSweep.partialTrace_terminalStackReference}.}

\paragraph{Physical preparation and compression.}
For fixed $q,R,J,\Delta$ with $q\ge1$ and $\Delta>0$, the full physical
preparation chooses its logarithmic width constant and a length cutoff
before the length, Hamiltonian, ground vector and register positions.
Above that cutoff, the original full-system gapped-ground-state assumption
constructs the actual private initialization, local operations, chronological
composition and terminal regrouping. No circuit family, marginal identity,
or information bound is supplied as an additional physical hypothesis.\footnote{
Declaration \leanid{TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.ChainSweep.exists_preparation_of_gappedGroundState}.}
In the specified main/auxiliary coordinates the prepared vector obeys
\begin{align}
 \|\widehat\Psi-\Omega\otimes\Xi\|&\le26L^{-11}.
 \label{eq:band_sweep_vector_error}
\end{align}
If $U$ is the fixed identification of the original main sites with the
physical blocks, the actual physical density satisfies
\begin{align}
 \bigl\|\rho_{\rm phys}-|U\Omega\rangle\langle U\Omega|\bigr\|_1
 &\le52L^{-11}.
 \label{eq:band_sweep_density_error}
\end{align}
The underlying prepared vector has norm at most one. The circuit has
participation at most forty, at most three parties per nonprivate gate,
and polynomially bounded physical dimensions, uniformly in the instance.

These constructions form one uniform family to which compression applies.
One actual nonzero product-basis column is chosen before all cuts; its
normalization gives the simultaneous block-prefix rank bounds. The inverse
main-coordinate map then gives one unit, nonzero open-boundary matrix
product state. With constants chosen before the length, Hamiltonian and
ground vector, its bond dimension is at most $CL^n$ and its phase error is
at most $1/L$ for every $L\ge2$. The finitely many lengths below the
preparation cutoff have exact representations and are absorbed into the
same constant.\footnote{Declarations
\leanid{TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.ChainSweep.exists_uniform_sampled_block_column}
and \leanid{TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.ChainSweep.exists_protocol_isOpenMPS_norm_sub_phase_le}.}
Compression bounds only the distinguished physical outputs. Private spaces
remain finite-dimensional without an additional numerical size bound.

\paragraph{Quantitative scope.}
The preparation selects its width from the information estimate at exponent
$60$ and may enlarge that constant. The printed intermediate protocol uses
exponent $24$; the formal result is not asserted for every width constant
chosen only at that exponent. The existential constants in Theorem~B allow
the stated preparation and compression argument. Resolution here concerns
the two local corrections and their actual constructions. It does not
assert all printed intermediate protocol constants or completion of the
whole paper. The nondegenerate lattice witness and the correction to the
all-register output assertion remain valid and are retained above.
