\section{Source and actual conclusion}
PGVWC07~\cite{PerezGarcia2007Matrix}, arXiv:quant-ph/0608197, Theorem 9 and proof,
\path{Papers/quant-ph_0608197/MPSarchive.tex}, lines 1195--1237,
starts from a canonical open-boundary representation whose blocked site
families satisfy C1. The local interaction is the orthogonal projector
onto the complement of the corresponding two-site boundary range.

The formalization constructs these projectors and their open
sum, derives the adjacent coefficient sections from the actual kernel,
reconstructs the full chain by the printed canonical adjoint method,
and closes the singleton endpoint bonds. The dependent-physical-space
continuation allows arbitrary physical dimensions at the different sites.
Let $B^{[j]}_i\in M_{D_j,D_{j+1}}(\mathbb C)$ be the site matrices of an
open chain of $N$ sites with $D_0=D_N=1$. Let $h_{j,j+1}$ be the local
projector on the adjacent pair $j,j+1$, extended by the identity, and let
$H=\sum_{j=0}^{N-2}h_{j,j+1}$ be the open parent Hamiltonian. Let $\Omega$
be the open-chain vector with coefficients
$\Omega(i_0,\ldots,i_{N-1})=(B^{[0]}_{i_0}\cdots B^{[N-1]}_{i_{N-1}})_{0,0}$.
For $N\ge2$, the conclusion is
\begin{align}
    H\ge0,\qquad \|\Omega\|=1,\qquad
    \ker H=\mathbb C\Omega,\qquad \dim\ker H=1.
    \label{eq:pgvwc07_open_parent_domain_ground_state_line}
\end{align}
The unit norm and both kernel inclusions are derived from the supplied
site matrices. There is no assumed ground state, local annihilation
condition, larger-block boundary, or canonical-form supplier.

\section{The length convention}
The source regroups the spins of an open chain into consecutive blocks,
writes $B^{[1]},\ldots,B^{[M]}$ for the blocked site families, and defines
the Hamiltonian as the sum of the adjacent terms,
\begin{align}
    H=\sum_jh_{j,j+1}
    \label{eq:pgvwc07_open_parent_domain_source_hamiltonian}
\end{align}
(lines~1195--1205). Its proof begins with the equation
$h_{j,j+1}\otimes I\,\ket\phi=0$ for every adjacent pair
(lines~1212--1217). The setup is therefore a blocked chain with at least one
adjacent pair, $M\ge2$; in the notation above, $N\ge2$.

On a chain of one blocked site there is no adjacent pair, the sum
(\ref{eq:pgvwc07_open_parent_domain_source_hamiltonian}) is empty, and every
vector is annihilated. That case is not a local Hamiltonian in the sense of the
source; it is a degenerate reading of the setup, not a gap in the theorem. On
the empty chain the configuration space is one-dimensional and the
statement holds trivially.

\textbf{Local fix (chains with an adjacent pair).} The formal theorem assumes
$N\ge2$, the source's setup of an open chain with at least one adjacent pair of
blocked sites. With this convention the formal conclusion
(\ref{eq:pgvwc07_open_parent_domain_ground_state_line}) is Theorem~9 for
arbitrary physical and bond dimensions. Uniqueness of the ground state is
uniqueness of the ground-state line, as in the source's normalization step
(lines~1233--1236). The intermediate statements for a common physical alphabet
are special cases used in the proof, not a restriction of the final theorem.

\section{Verdict}
\textbf{Verdict: local correction, resolved.} The length bound $N\ge2$ is the
source's setup, a chain with an adjacent interaction term. Under it the
varying-bond open parent Hamiltonian has the normalized canonical state as its
unique ground-state line, with arbitrary physical dimensions.
