\section{The site-dependent GNVW construction}

Gross--Nesme--Vogts--Werner allow the cell algebra at each site
$y\in\mathbb Z$ to have a site-dependent positive matrix size
\cite[Section~7.2]{Gross2012IndexTheory}:
\begin{align}
    \mathcal A_y
    &=M_{d(y)}(\mathbb C).
    \label{eq:gnvw12_site_dependent_adjacent_generation_scope_cell_algebra}
\end{align}
For each $x\in\mathbb Z$, define the even pair and its left and right
neighbouring pairs by
\begin{align}
    E_x
    &=\{2x,2x+1\},
    \label{eq:gnvw12_site_dependent_adjacent_generation_scope_even_pair}\\
    L_x
    &=\{2x-1,2x\},
    \label{eq:gnvw12_site_dependent_adjacent_generation_scope_left_pair}\\
    P_x
    &=\{2x+1,2x+2\}.
    \label{eq:gnvw12_site_dependent_adjacent_generation_scope_right_pair}
\end{align}
For a nearest-neighbour automorphism $\alpha$, locality gives
\begin{align}
    \alpha(\mathcal A_{E_x})
    &\subseteq
      \mathcal A_{L_x}\otimes\mathcal A_{P_x}.
    \label{eq:gnvw12_site_dependent_adjacent_generation_scope_locality}
\end{align}

Using the factor order in
(\ref{eq:gnvw12_site_dependent_adjacent_generation_scope_locality}), source
equation \texttt{RR2x} defines the support algebras
\begin{align}
    \mathcal R_{2x}
    &=\operatorname{Spp}_{\mathrm L}
      (\alpha(\mathcal A_{E_x}))
      \subseteq\mathcal A_{L_x},
    \label{eq:gnvw12_site_dependent_adjacent_generation_scope_left_support}\\
    \mathcal R_{2x+1}
    &=\operatorname{Spp}_{\mathrm R}
      (\alpha(\mathcal A_{E_x}))
      \subseteq\mathcal A_{P_x}.
    \label{eq:gnvw12_site_dependent_adjacent_generation_scope_right_support}
\end{align}
These definitions appear at lines 1251--1266 of the arXiv v2 source for
Ref.~\cite{Gross2012IndexTheory}. The site-dependent cell structure and the
causal-automorphism assumptions appear at lines 557--600. Neither
(\ref{eq:gnvw12_site_dependent_adjacent_generation_scope_locality}) nor
(\ref{eq:gnvw12_site_dependent_adjacent_generation_scope_left_support})--%
(\ref{eq:gnvw12_site_dependent_adjacent_generation_scope_right_support})
requires translation covariance.

\section{Adjacent generation and dimensions}

Let $r(y)>0$ denote the matrix size of the support algebra at site $y$:
\begin{align}
    \mathcal R_y
    &\cong M_{r(y)}(\mathbb C).
    \label{eq:gnvw12_site_dependent_adjacent_generation_scope_support_size}
\end{align}
The adjacent-generation argument yields two ordered product identities:
\begin{align}
    d(2x)d(2x+1)
    &=r(2x)r(2x+1),
    \label{eq:gnvw12_site_dependent_adjacent_generation_scope_even_dimension}\\
    r(2x+1)r(2x+2)
    &=d(2x+1)d(2x+2).
    \label{eq:gnvw12_site_dependent_adjacent_generation_scope_odd_dimension}
\end{align}
The first identity is the matrix-size consequence of source equation
\texttt{AARReven}, lines 1276--1287, and the second follows from source
equation \texttt{AARRodd}, lines 1306--1315, of
Ref.~\cite{Gross2012IndexTheory}. Dividing these identities gives
\begin{align}
    \frac{r(2x)}{d(2x)}
    &=\frac{d(2x+1)}{r(2x+1)},
    \label{eq:gnvw12_site_dependent_adjacent_generation_scope_even_odd_ratio}\\
    \frac{d(2x+1)}{r(2x+1)}
    &=\frac{r(2x+2)}{d(2x+2)}.
    \label{eq:gnvw12_site_dependent_adjacent_generation_scope_odd_even_ratio}
\end{align}
Together, these equalities are the site-independent multiplicative index
relation:
\begin{align}
    \frac{r(2x)}{d(2x)}
    &=\frac{d(2x+1)}{r(2x+1)}
     =\frac{r(2x+2)}{d(2x+2)}.
    \label{eq:gnvw12_site_dependent_adjacent_generation_scope_index_ratio}
\end{align}
The source labels this relation \texttt{dimtransfer}; it appears at lines
1317--1329 of Ref.~\cite{Gross2012IndexTheory}.

\section{The homogeneous specialization}

The present spin-chain algebra uses one fixed positive on-site matrix size
$d$. Hence
\begin{align}
    d(y)
    &=d
    \qquad\text{for every }y\in\mathbb Z.
    \label{eq:gnvw12_site_dependent_adjacent_generation_scope_constant_size}
\end{align}
Under
(\ref{eq:gnvw12_site_dependent_adjacent_generation_scope_constant_size}), the
dimension identities become
\begin{align}
    d^2
    &=r(2x)r(2x+1),
    \label{eq:gnvw12_site_dependent_adjacent_generation_scope_homogeneous_even}\\
    r(2x+1)r(2x+2)
    &=d^2,
    \label{eq:gnvw12_site_dependent_adjacent_generation_scope_homogeneous_odd}\\
    \frac{r(2x)}{d}
    &=\frac{d}{r(2x+1)}
     =\frac{r(2x+2)}{d}.
    \label{eq:gnvw12_site_dependent_adjacent_generation_scope_homogeneous_ratio}
\end{align}
Cirac--P\'erez-Garc\'ia--Schuch--Verstraete use this homogeneous setting:
their Appendix fixes $\mathcal A_y\cong M_d(\mathbb C)$ and constructs the
even support algebra at lines 2320--2329
\cite{Cirac2017MPU}.

The theorems currently proved for the fixed-$d$ spin chain establish the
homogeneous forms of
(\ref{eq:gnvw12_site_dependent_adjacent_generation_scope_locality}) and
(\ref{eq:gnvw12_site_dependent_adjacent_generation_scope_left_support})--%
(\ref{eq:gnvw12_site_dependent_adjacent_generation_scope_right_support}),
together with two-sided support containment. They also prove that the
canonically embedded support algebras at distinct site indices commute
pairwise. This is the homogeneous specialization of the consecutive/disjoint
commutation argument at lines 1270--1274 of
Ref.~\cite{Gross2012IndexTheory}; it does not use translation covariance.%

The fixed-$d$ development does not classify the support algebras as full
matrix factors, prove the adjacent-generation equalities or their dimension
consequences
(\ref{eq:gnvw12_site_dependent_adjacent_generation_scope_homogeneous_even})--%
(\ref{eq:gnvw12_site_dependent_adjacent_generation_scope_homogeneous_ratio}),
or construct the GNVW index. Extending the construction and the commutation
theorem to site-dependent cell sizes also remains outside the present scope.

This restriction differs from the full-matrix-factor restriction recorded in
\path{docs/paper-gaps/gnvw12_support_algebra_full_matrix_scope.tex}. That note
compares full complex matrix factors with arbitrary finite-dimensional
$C^*$-algebras. The present note compares a fixed matrix size along the chain
with the site-dependent full matrix sizes allowed by
Ref.~\cite{Gross2012IndexTheory}.

\section{Elimination plan}

Removing this restriction requires an algebraic local-observable system with
site-dependent cell algebras
(\ref{eq:gnvw12_site_dependent_adjacent_generation_scope_cell_algebra}),
canonical inclusions for arbitrary finite regions, and a norm completion. The
finite-region restriction of a causal automorphism must then be expressed in
the ordered $L_x\times P_x$ coordinates of
(\ref{eq:gnvw12_site_dependent_adjacent_generation_scope_locality}).

The same coefficient definitions yield the support algebras
(\ref{eq:gnvw12_site_dependent_adjacent_generation_scope_left_support}) and
(\ref{eq:gnvw12_site_dependent_adjacent_generation_scope_right_support}).
Support commutation, the scalar-center argument, and adjacent generation then
give
(\ref{eq:gnvw12_site_dependent_adjacent_generation_scope_even_dimension}) and
(\ref{eq:gnvw12_site_dependent_adjacent_generation_scope_odd_dimension})
without assuming that $d(y)$ is constant. The ratio
(\ref{eq:gnvw12_site_dependent_adjacent_generation_scope_index_ratio}) then
defines the site-independent GNVW index. The fixed-$d$ theorems should follow
by specializing $d(y)$ to the constant value $d$.

Until this site-dependent observable algebra is available, the corresponding
formal statements remain restricted to homogeneous chains.
