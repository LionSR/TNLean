\section{The source proposition}

Proposition~9.2 of the two-dimensional area-law manuscript of September~24,
2026~\cite{OpenAI2026AreaLaw} (\path{08-scanner.tex}, lines~349--397) states
that, under the scan scales and the entropy input
$S_\Omega(X)\le C_en^{1+e}$, $S_\Omega(Q_j)\le C_e(nL^e+n)$, for every
sufficiently large replica count $k$ some charge round and some common
parameter $p_k\in[\epsilon/2,\epsilon]$ give the defect-energy bound
\[
    E_{\mathrm{def},k}\le Cn^\ell D^2W^2(\log n)^C
    \bigl(\delta_n^{1/8}+\epsilon+n^{-100}\bigr)+Cn^{-1000}+o_k(1),
\]
with $\delta_n=C_e\epsilon^{-1}(n^{e-\mu}+a^{1/4}(\log n)^C)$, and with the
comparator budgets $B_{\mathrm{sh}}\le C_e(nL^e+n)$,
$B_{\mathrm{exc}}\le CnD$ and $\mathcal B\le CnD(\log n)^C$.

Its proof (lines~399--533) uses Lemma~9.1 (the scan histories),
Proposition~7.4 (transport), Proposition~8.1 (comparators), Lemma~2.1
(continuity) and Lemma~2.3 (tails) for the concrete scan.

\section{The restriction}

The formal statement takes the conclusions of those five results, for one
scan at scale $n$, as the fields of one data package, and proves the step of
the source from these conclusions to Proposition~9.2: the integrated entropy
balance over the charge rounds, the averaging selection of a round and of a
parameter with $\mathcal Q(p_k)/(KnD)\le\delta_n+\rho_k$, the H\"older
accounting of the defect energy at the selected point, and the three
comparator budgets.\footnote{The packaged statement is
    \leanid{TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.scanner_estimate}.} Each field transcribes
one displayed conclusion of an input and cites its source line; no field
asserts more than the cited statement. The constants depend only on the fixed
exponents and on the constants of the inputs, as in the source.

Two harmless differences in the bookkeeping are recorded. The continuity
input bounds the change of the shell entropies by
$C\sqrt{n^{-500}}(1+Cn^2\log q)$, which is $O(n^{-248})$ but not at most
$n^{-248}$ itself; the formal budget lemmas carry the error $n^{-247}$. The
marginal parameter is bounded with logarithmic power $12$, coming from the
crossing count $(\log n)^4$ and the squared dimension term $(\log n)^8$; the
source writes an unspecified power $C$.

\section{Finite history construction now available}

The physical partitions, independent uniform offsets and charge choices,
padded row fills, and fixed-length labelled charge lists are now constructed
as a finite classical history process. For positive offset and slot counts,
the weights are normalized probabilities. Blank and previously assigned fill
slots consume the deterministic schedule; charges preserve the nominal
fronts. The actual unassigned sites lie beyond every completed row.

For the induced lattice graph and actual ambient target depth, the ambient
row bound, bounded anchor multiplicity, and the source cut clearance give
on every good history a unique slot for each splitting radius-$r_0$ ball.
Its conditional probability is exactly $1/(2\lceil C_1nD\rceil)$, bounded
below by $[2(C_1+1)nD]^{-1}$, and the evaluated extended history moves
exactly its old unassigned part. Goodness bounds assigned sites of $A$;
it does not constrain the exterior sites initially on the far side.
The probabilities are classical history weights, not the transported
state measures. Nonnegative costs depending on band, receiving side, and
moved subset also satisfy the corresponding expected-cost lower bound,
by summing the disjoint labelled events within each band and then summing
the bands. Identifying these costs
with the transported conditional entropies remains a separate step.
\footnote{The physical sampling statement is
    \leanid{TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.CollarScan.good_split_sampling_domainGraph}.}

These results do not construct the entire data package. The actual old
middle lies in the compact truncation set, so the existing crossing-support
result now identifies every splitting variable-radius truncated designated
support with its radius-$r_0$ charge ball. The sampling conclusion therefore
applies to the designated support itself, and the expected-cost lower bound
also holds for its band--side--label incidences.\footnote{The composition is
    \leanid{TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.CollarScan.good_designated_split_sampling_domainGraph},
    using \leanid{TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.truncationRadius_eq_of_mem_crossingLabels}.}
The unconditional assigned-site lead is now derived from the actual evaluator.
Together with consumed-row coverage, it confines each receiving--middle
incidence to an anchor interval of width $D+3r_0+1$. Actual affine offset
counting, exact uniform offset marginals, and the separation of band centers
give total incidence weight at most $10D/m$, separately for old and new
histories and for their common convex mixtures in fill and charge rounds.
The designated support is identified with its base-radius ball from the
actual middle's containment in the truncation set. No good-history or
state-dependent independence assumption enters this dilution estimate.
\footnote{The charge and fill mixture statements are
    \leanid{TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.CollarScan.designated_split_mixture_dilution_domainGraph}
    and \leanid{TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.CollarScan.fill_split_mixture_dilution_domainGraph}.}
The bounded incidence event contains the terminal metric split event of
\path{06-transport.tex}, lines~336--352. It is not an arbitrary entropy cut
such as $U=P_0\setminus X$, and it is not yet an identification of the
arbitrary split predicates stored in the scanner data package with concrete
transported-state measures.

The finite ancestry probability argument is now derived from the same actual
history process. Every bad endpoint has a strictly time-ordered selected-charge
path. The physical cut clearance keeps its predecessors in $A$, including for
the far side. If its length is $s$ and the tested pair time is $k$, then
$D<4r_0s$ and every path time lies in the last $4n(r_0s+1)+1$ pair indices.
Independent selected-coordinate probabilities, exact binomial time counting,
and the lattice diamond count give the finite tail. Union over actual prefix
times, both sides, all bands, and compact endpoints, and averaging the initial
offsets, give
\begin{align}
    2K(|T|+nL)(N+1)
    \sum_{\substack{0\le s\le N \\D<4r_0s}}\rho^s,
    \qquad
    \rho=\frac{9e\,nr_0\mu(1+2(2r_0)(2r_0+1))}{2\lceil C_1nD\rceil}.
\end{align}
The compact endpoint bound is proved from the actual ambient layers, so
no exterior-domain volume enters. Label paths suffice for the spatial count;
predecessor witnesses need not be counted because they do not enter a selected
coordinate event. This gives a sharper ratio than the source's loose
predecessor-inclusive estimate.\footnote{The all-time finite statement is
    \leanid{TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.CollarScan.sum_historyWeight_bad_through_geometric_domainGraph_le}.}

The rounded source scales now give arbitrary inverse-power decay of this
actual all-prefix probability, uniformly in the finite domain, target, label
family, and scanner. The threshold is chosen from the fixed exponents and
constants alone. In particular, the actual bad-history probability is at most
$n^{-200}$ for sufficiently large $n$.\footnote{The result is
    \leanid{TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.eventually_bad_history_probability_le}.}
This closes the classical asymptotic probability estimate. The remaining
geometric conclusions and transported-state estimates remain separate;
identifying quantum entropy costs and constructing the entire scanner data
package are not consequences of a probability bound alone.

The physical nesting of distinct bands is now derived for independent actual
histories and times. Actual designated receiving--middle incidences satisfy the
full terminal split rule in both completed and pre-charge statuses. Their
labelled occurrence count is at most $10KnD\mu$, with anchor multiplicity
$\mu$, under the expanded numerical margin $4(D+2r_0)\le m$.
Actual completed and pre-charge statuses now have deterministic whole-row
comparison prefixes from one nested family. Under $D\ge1$, $r_0\le D$,
$4D\le m$, $8Km\le L$, the ambient row bound, and the respective $k\le nm$
or $k+1\le nm$ horizon, the sets $U$ and $UY$ differ from their comparison
prefixes by at most $3nD$ sites. The comparisons are inclusions in opposite
directions: the near comparison is contained in $U$, while $UY$ is contained
in its far comparison. The partially consumed row is counted in the error.
With the source cut clearance, physical undirected cut-edge counts are at most
$20nD$ for $U$ and $UY$, $40nD$ for $Y$, and $16nD$ for $P_0$ and $V$.
These bounds are derived from the actual history evaluator and physical lattice
geometry, without a prefix or cut-count hypothesis.\footnote{The physical

The missing direct near--far case is now excluded from the actual geometry.

The auxiliary factors remain outside the physical supports. The augmented
finite-tree compatibility condition therefore holds for every old and new
leaf of an actual charge round.

\section{Elimination}

The restriction disappears once the data package is constructed from the
scan geometry of Lemma~9.1 and the transported states of Proposition~7.4,
with Propositions~8.1 and Lemmas~2.1 and~2.3 supplying the remaining fields.
Those inputs are separate items of the same formalization program.
