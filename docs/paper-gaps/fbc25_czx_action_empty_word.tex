\section{The assertion and its boundary}

The interior action identity \papereqref{fbc25}{eq:action_interior} in
Ref.~\cite[lines~1841--1870]{FrancoRubio2025MPUGauging} includes words of
length $n\geq0$. On a bond-one block it requires
\begin{align}
    p_x B_x^{\mathbf a}q_x & =A_{1-x}^{\mathbf a}
    \qquad (|\mathbf a|\geq0),
    \label{eq:fbc25_czx_empty_interior}
\end{align}
where $B_x$ is the tensor obtained by acting with the CZX symmetry on GHZ
block $x\in\{0,1\}$, $A_{1-x}$ is the target block, and $p_x,q_x$ are the
left and right action tensors. Products over the empty word are identities,
so (\ref{eq:fbc25_czx_empty_interior}) requires $p_xq_x=1$.
The source prints a specific action pair in
Ref.~\cite[lines~4690--4694]{FrancoRubio2025MPUGauging}. That pair satisfies
(\ref{eq:fbc25_czx_empty_interior}) for every nonempty word but not for the
empty word. This note isolates that boundary and gives a different pair
which satisfies the identity at every length. It does not change the
all-word requirement of a reduction.

\section{The printed pair and the acted tensor}

Use the physical alphabet $2a+b$, with $a,b\in\{0,1\}$. The once-blocked
GHZ sectors are $|00\rangle$ and $|11\rangle$, and the generator exchanges
their labels \cite[lines~4660--4670]{FrancoRubio2025MPUGauging}. Thus
$A_x^i=\delta_{i,3x}$. Let $T^{i,j}$ be the bond-two CZX tensor in
\papereqref{fbc25}{eq:MPU_CZX} of
Ref.~\cite[lines~4505--4547]{FrancoRubio2025MPUGauging}, retaining both
output $Z$ gates and the unnormalized Hadamard weights. Contracting its input
with block $x$ gives
\begin{align}
    B_x^i & =\sum_{j=0}^3 T^{i,j}A_x^j
    =\delta_{i,3(1-x)}C_x,                                                                                   \\
    C_0   & =T^{3,0}=\begin{pmatrix}0&0\\-1&1\end{pmatrix},
          & C_1                                             & =T^{0,3}=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\0&0\end{pmatrix}.
\end{align}

Denote the source's printed left action tensor by $p_x$ and its printed
right action tensor by $q_x$. In the normalized convention
$|\pm\rangle=(|0\rangle\pm|1\rangle)/\sqrt2$, their coordinates are
\begin{align}
    p_0 & =(0,-i),                                   & p_1                                       & =(1,0),
    \label{eq:fbc25_czx_empty_printed_rows}                                                                \\
    q_0 & =\frac12\begin{pmatrix}-i\\i\end{pmatrix},
        & q_1                                        & =\frac12\begin{pmatrix}1\\1\end{pmatrix}.
    \label{eq:fbc25_czx_empty_printed_columns}
\end{align}
These are the formulas printed in
Ref.~\cite[lines~4690--4694]{FrancoRubio2025MPUGauging}. The source does not
explicitly specify the normalization of $|\pm\rangle$ there. The convention
in (\ref{eq:fbc25_czx_empty_printed_columns}) is consistent with the black
factor in the tensor decomposition and the later defect evaluations
\cite[lines~4589--4601 and 4800--4859]{FrancoRubio2025MPUGauging}.
The left and right action symbols are ordinary row and column contractions;
there is no extra matrix between them in the interior diagram.\footnote{The
    archived drawing definitions are in \path{Papers/2502.20257/Commands.tex},
    lines 372--399.}

Direct multiplication gives, for each $x$,
\begin{align}
    C_x^2     & =C_x, & p_xq_x       & =\frac12,
    \label{eq:fbc25_czx_empty_idempotent_and_empty} \\
    p_xC_xq_x & =1,   & C_xq_xp_xC_x & =C_x.
    \label{eq:fbc25_czx_empty_nonempty_and_exterior}
\end{align}
For a nonempty word supported on the sole nonzero letter of $B_x$, its
matrix product is $C_x$; for any other nonempty word it is zero. Consequently
(\ref{eq:fbc25_czx_empty_nonempty_and_exterior}) proves the interior
identity at every positive length. At length zero,
(\ref{eq:fbc25_czx_empty_idempotent_and_empty}) gives $1/2$ instead of $1$.
Changing the common normalization of the two printed kets cannot remove the
conflict: replacing $q_x$ by $tq_x$ gives $p_xC_x(tq_x)=t$ and
$p_x(tq_x)=t/2$. These two quantities cannot both equal one.

\section{The valid exterior identity and a local replacement}

For a word $\mathbf a$, let $B_x^{\mathbf a}$ and
$A_{1-x}^{\mathbf a}$ denote the ordered products of the corresponding
letters. The exterior action identity
\papereqref{fbc25}{eq:action_exterior} in
Ref.~\cite[lines~1788--1838]{FrancoRubio2025MPUGauging} has two buffers of
length $m\geq1$ after the stipulated blocking and a middle word of length
$n+1\geq1$. The printed pair satisfies precisely that identity:
\begin{align}
    B_x^{\mathbf a}q_x A_{1-x}^{\mathbf c}p_x B_x^{\mathbf b}
     & =B_x^{\mathbf a\mathbf c\mathbf b},
    \qquad |\mathbf a|=|\mathbf b|\geq1,\quad |\mathbf c|\geq1.
    \label{eq:fbc25_czx_empty_buffered_exterior}
\end{align}
If any letter is unsupported, both sides vanish. Otherwise the left side is
$C_xq_xp_xC_x=C_x$, which is also the right side.

A local replacement of the left boundary gives an all-word reduction. Set
\begin{align}
    V_x & =p_xC_x, & W_x & =q_x,
    \label{eq:fbc25_czx_empty_dressed_pair} \\
    V_0 & =(i,-i), & V_1 & =(1,1).
    \label{eq:fbc25_czx_empty_dressed_rows}
\end{align}
Then $V_xW_x=p_xC_xq_x=1$. Idempotency of $C_x$ gives, for every word,
\begin{align}
    V_xB_x^{\mathbf a}W_x & =A_{1-x}^{\mathbf a}
    \qquad (|\mathbf a|\geq0).
\end{align}
For the empty word this is $V_xW_x=1$; for nonempty words it follows from
the same supported-letter calculation. Moreover,
\begin{align}
    V_xB_x^{\mathbf b} & =p_xB_x^{\mathbf b}
    \qquad (|\mathbf b|\geq1).
    \label{eq:fbc25_czx_empty_buffered_agreement}
\end{align}
Thus replacing $p_x$ by $V_x$ leaves
(\ref{eq:fbc25_czx_empty_buffered_exterior}) unchanged. This replacement
is not a scalar gauge transformation: the rows in
(\ref{eq:fbc25_czx_empty_dressed_rows}) are not scalar multiples of the
corresponding rows in (\ref{eq:fbc25_czx_empty_printed_rows}). In particular,
(\ref{eq:fbc25_czx_empty_buffered_agreement}) does not permit substitution
in an unbuffered defect formula.

\section{Verdict and formal boundary}

For the source's actual nonzero injective GHZ sectors, the printed CZX
pair fails the interior identity at length zero, which the source explicitly
includes by $n\geq0$ \cite[line~1870]{FrancoRubio2025MPUGauging}.
This nondegenerate counterexample warrants the false-source classification
for the specific assertion that the printed pair satisfies the all-length
interior identity, not for general existence of action tensors or later
defect claims. Its nonempty interior identities and buffered exterior
identities remain valid. A different, dressed pair
satisfies the genuine all-word reduction relation without an extra
hypothesis. The general action-tensor audit retains that all-word relation;
this calculation does not weaken it.\footnote{See
    \path{docs/paper-gaps/fbc25_mpu_action_stabilizer_source_audit.tex},
    particularly its discussion of the interior identity.}

The later defect evaluations are not invalidated by this empty-word
calculation; this is not a certificate of their global compatibility.
The source gives
$g[0]=|00\rangle$ and $g[1]=-i|11\rangle$
\cite[lines~4800--4859]{FrancoRubio2025MPUGauging}.
These evaluations use the literal printed action tensors, not the replacement
(\ref{eq:fbc25_czx_empty_dressed_pair}). Their existing derivation remains
separate, as does the normalization issue for the different 2017 printed
CZX tensor.\footnote{The respective notes are
    \path{docs/paper-gaps/fbc25_czx_defect_domains.tex} and
    \path{docs/paper-gaps/mpu_czx_tensor_normalization.tex}.}
No correction of fusion tensors, defect vectors, or the global symmetry
is asserted here.

The finite identities, the nonempty printed interior and exterior
identities, the failure of the printed empty contraction, and the all-word
dressed reduction with exterior buffer length one are formally verified.
The discrepancy and the separate all-word replacement are resolved;
the printed pair itself still fails at length zero.\footnote{In \doclink{TNLean/MPS/MPDO/CZXActionTensors},
    \leanid{MPOTensor.CZX.printedAction_finite_identities},
