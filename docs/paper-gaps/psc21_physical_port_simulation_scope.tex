\section{The source resource convention}
Piroli, Styliaris and Cirac~\cite{Piroli2021LOCC}, Supplement pp.~7--8,
distinguish counted nearest-neighbor gates on fixed-dimensional physical
qudits from free onsite operations involving those qudits and their local
ancillas. In the proof of Proposition~2, the entanglement increase per
intersite layer depends on the physical dimension, independently of the
number of onsite ancillas. This prevents replacing a physical gate by an
arbitrary operation on two enlarged sites at the same unit cost without a
simulation argument.

The existing enlarged-site local-channel model allows arbitrary
intermediate local dimensions. Its composition and trace-norm estimates
remain statements about that model. The construction below supplies a
bounded-register communication theorem; it is not an inclusion theorem
for unrestricted intermediate dimensions.

The depth used here counts only physical-port intersite layers. In the
main text, p.~1, the source defines depth by nearest-neighbor pair-gate
layers and treats local unitaries on each spin and its ancillas as free.
The displayed constants therefore do not count onsite operations as extra
gate layers. Every finite protocol in this reduced-channel model now has
an explicit unitary realization with fresh local product-zero environments,
unchanged physical ports and intersite depth, and one final environment trace.

\section{The proved bounded matching theorem}
Fix a physical dimension $d\ge2$, an upper bound $B$ on the local input
dimension, and $m\le B$. The density witness below forces $m\ge1$, hence $B\ge1$. Set
\begin{align}
    k & =\lceil\log_d B\rceil.
\end{align}
The local basis code into $k$ qudits is chosen independently of the chain
length. Its matrix $E$ is isometric. A completely positive trace-preserving
decoder is obtained by completing $Y\mapsto E^\dagger YE$ outside the code:
\begin{align}
    D(Y) & =E^\dagger YE+
    \operatorname{tr}[(I-EE^\dagger)Y(I-EE^\dagger)]\rho,
\end{align}
where $\rho$ is a fixed density matrix. Then $D(EXE^\dagger)=X$ for every
operator $X$, and the identity persists after tensoring with the identity
on any finite reference. The two-site encoding is the tensor product of
the separate onsite encodings.\footnote{Formal declarations:
    \leanid{QuantumCircuit.encodeCircuit}, \leanid{QuantumCircuit.decodeCircuit}
    and \leanid{QuantumCircuit.map_decodeCircuit_comp_map_encodeCircuit} in
    QICLean's \path{QICLean/Circuit/Compile/Encoding}, where the encoding is the
    product of the onsite encodings, $\rho$ is a basis state, and the identity is
    stated without the reference.}

On a ring of $N\ge2$ sites, give each site one physical port, a $k$-qudit
data register and a $k$-qudit scratch register. For an arbitrary matching
of CPTP pair channels, extend each channel to its full two-register space
by encoding its output after applying it to a completed decoding of the
input. These extensions are CPTP on the entire register algebra and agree
exactly with the original channels on encoded inputs. There are normalized
finite Kraus representations whose actual placed matching channel has
physical depth at most
\begin{align}
    2k & =2\lceil\log_d B\rceil,
\end{align}
independently of $N$ and the number of selected bonds. Products of $L$
such certified layers have depth at most $2kL$.\footnote{Formal declarations:
    \leanid{QuantumCircuit.Layer.exists_portRing} and
    \leanid{QuantumCircuit.IsBoundedProtocol.exists_portRing} in QICLean's
    \path{QICLean/Circuit/Compile/Port} and
    \path{QICLean/Circuit/Compile/PortProtocol}, on the ring with physical
    ports \leanid{QuantumCircuit.Geometry.portRing}.}
For $B=1$, the data register has zero qudits and the bound is zero.

\section{Why the communication cost is uniform}
To exchange one memory digit across a selected bond, swap that digit with
the physical port locally at both endpoints, perform one physical-port
SWAP, and undo the two local swaps. This exchanges the selected memory
digits and restores the ports exactly, including when they carry arbitrary
correlations. Every bond of a matching runs in parallel. Repeating over the
$k$ digits moves each left data register into the right scratch register,
while leaving the right data register in place.

All pair channels can now be executed onsite. Reverse the one common
routing afterward. The total cost is $k+k$, not $2k$ times the number of
bonds. Conjugation by the routing preserves products of channel maps, so
the resulting map is exactly the original placed family on the complete
operator space. Outputs return to their original data registers and all
other wires are restored.\footnote{Formal declarations: the routing layers
    are layers of permutation gates (\leanid{QuantumCircuit.kraus_ofCells_permGate}
    in QICLean's \path{QICLean/Circuit/Compile/PermGate}), assembled with the
    local execution in \leanid{QuantumCircuit.Layer.exists_portRing} in
    \path{QICLean/Circuit/Compile/Port}.}
This construction follows the same gather, execute locally, and return
principle used in the source's proof of Theorem~1 (Supplement pp.~8--9),
but the displayed channel-resource bound is a derived result.

The completed decoder for a joint code need not factor into separated
onsite operations outside that code. No such assertion is used: the joint
extension is applied only after the registers have been colocated. Separate
input/output encoding and decoding use products of local maps. Decoding after encoding
is the identity on every chain operator, with any finite
reference.\footnote{Formal declaration, without the reference:
    \leanid{QuantumCircuit.map_decodeCircuit_comp_map_encodeCircuit} in
    QICLean's \path{QICLean/Circuit/Compile/Encoding}.}

\section{The encoded bounded-protocol compiler}
The dimension bound now records every positive intermediate local space of
an actual native protocol, not just its endpoint dimensions. For fixed
$d\geq2$, $N\geq2$, and one common bound $B$, set
$k=\lceil\log_d B\rceil$. A native protocol $\Psi$ of depth $T$ compiles into
an explicit word on the alphabet $d^k$. Its onsite operations cost zero and
its $T$ native matching layers cost at most $2kT$ in total.\footnote{Formal
    declarations: the encoding into the alphabet $d^k$ is
    \leanid{QuantumCircuit.IsBoundedProtocol.exists_encoded} in QICLean's
    \path{QICLean/Circuit/Compile/Encoding}, and the compilation to the ports is
    \leanid{QuantumCircuit.IsBoundedProtocol.exists_portRing} in
    \path{QICLean/Circuit/Compile/PortProtocol}, stated in the unitary-dilation
    form of~(\ref{eq:scope_unitary_port_io}) below. The channel identity with the
    complementary reference $R$ displayed next is not formalized.}

The resulting physical channel $\Gamma$ obeys the exact identity
\begin{align}
    \Gamma\mathcal E_{\mathrm{in}}
    =\mathcal E_{\mathrm{out}}(\Psi\otimes\operatorname{id}_{R}),
\end{align}
where $R$ is the entire complementary port/scratch system. The equation
holds on all joint input-reference operators. The maps $\mathcal E$ are
product onsite codes transported through explicit sitewise digit and
selected/complementary coordinate equivalences. Encoders and completed
decoders are CPTP, and decoding after encoding is exactly the identity.
The coordinate changes are not assumed free intersite gates.

This is an encoded logical-input statement: the data occupy local data
registers. The next construction supplies the original-port input/output
interface when the native input and output dimensions both equal $d$.

\section{Original-port input, output, and designated memory reset}
Let $J_{\mathrm{port}}$ be the explicit product code that stores the input
qudit on each original physical port and initializes its $2k$ data/scratch
digits to zero. A second product code stores the register encoding on the
data digits, with zero port and scratch digits. Completed channels on each
whole spatial-site block convert these codes in both directions at zero
intersite depth. They are CPTP on the entire physical operator space, not
merely on the code.

Compose this preprocessing and postprocessing with the encoded compiler.
For a bounded native endomorphism $\Psi$ whose input/output dimension is
$d$, the resulting physical-port protocol satisfies
\begin{align}
    \Gamma_{\mathrm{port}}J_{\mathrm{port}}
    = J_{\mathrm{port}}\Psi,
    \qquad \operatorname{depth}_{\mathrm{intersite}}(\Gamma_{\mathrm{port}})
    \le 2kT.
\end{align}
Thus the output returns to the same physical ports, and all designated
data/scratch memory is again in its product zero state and uncorrelated
with the output. The identity holds on all logical operators and persists
with an arbitrary external reference. This port-code form is not
formalized: \leanid{QuantumCircuit.IsBoundedProtocol.exists_portRing} in
QICLean's \path{QICLean/Circuit/Compile/PortProtocol} keeps the registers in
the data wires, with zero port and scratch wires, at input and output, and the
transfers between the two product codes are not formalized.

The explicit initializer is identified with the earlier physical encoding
by splitting data from its complementary wires and preparing the latter
in their zero configuration. The reset assertion concerns the
designated data/scratch wires. The additional dilation environments in the
unitary realization below are discarded; they are not asserted to reset.

\section{One unitary circuit and one final environment trace}
Let $P$ assign the $W$ existing $d$-level wires to $N\geq1$ spatial sites
and designate one communication port at each site. For every finite depth-$T$
physical-port channel protocol $\Phi$, with $d\geq1$, there are finitely
many appended environment wires, an explicit assignment of each new wire
to a spatial site, and an actual unitary circuit $U$ on the enlarged layout
such that
\begin{align}
    \mathcal D_E\circ\operatorname{Ad}_U\circ\mathcal I_E & =\Phi,
                                                          & \operatorname{depth}_{\mathrm{intersite}}(U) & \leq T.
    \label{eq:scope_deferred_dilation}
\end{align}
Here $\mathcal I_E(X)=X\otimes\ketbra{0_E}{0_E}$ in the explicit
original/environment coordinates, $\mathcal D_E$ discards exactly the
appended wires, and $\operatorname{Ad}_U(Y)=UYU^\dagger$.
Every original wire retains its spatial owner, and the designated physical
ports are unchanged. All environments are initialized at the start and
remain in the joint system until the one final discard. The identity
holds on all original-wire operators, including their arbitrary external
references.\footnote{Formal declarations:
    \leanid{QuantumCircuit.IsUnitaryDilation} and
    \leanid{QuantumCircuit.IsProtocol.isUnitaryDilation} in QICLean's
    \path{QICLean/Circuit/Compile/Dilation}. The formal statement attaches a
    finite environment to every wire instead of appending wires, prepares it in a
    basis vector, and is stated without the external reference.}

For a channel on $m$ qudits within one spatial site, $2m$ fresh qudits at
that same site suffice. The retained output is the original local register;
all other existing wires are untouched, including their correlations with
that register. This uses the $d^{2m}$ Choi-rank bound, followed by a unitary
extension of the fixed pure-input isometry. For composition, the two
circuits use disjoint fresh environment blocks. The second circuit acts
as the identity on the earlier environment, so its trace can be postponed
even when that environment is correlated with the system. Only the
original physical ports participate in counted intersite layers.\footnote{Formal
    declarations: \leanid{QuantumCircuit.Layer.isUnitaryDilation},
    \leanid{QuantumCircuit.Circuit.isUnitaryDilation} and
    \leanid{QuantumCircuit.IsUnitaryDilation.comp} in QICLean's
    \path{QICLean/Circuit/Compile/Dilation}. There the environment of a gate is
    one finite basis attached to a wire of its cell; the count of $2m$ qudits is
    not formalized.}

Applying~(\ref{eq:scope_deferred_dilation}) to the bounded original-port
protocol gives a unitary $U$ satisfying
\begin{align}
    \mathcal D_E\circ\operatorname{Ad}_U\circ\mathcal I_E\circ J_{\mathrm{port}}
                                                 & =J_{\mathrm{port}}\circ\Psi,\label{eq:scope_unitary_port_io} \\
    \operatorname{depth}_{\mathrm{intersite}}(U) & \leq2\lceil\log_d B\rceil T.
    \notag
\end{align}
Thus both the designated memory and the additional environments start in
product-zero states. After the final environment trace, the output is on
the original ports and the designated data/scratch memory is reset to
zero. The discarded environment may contain information about the input;
no reset of that environment is claimed.\footnote{Formal theorem:
    \leanid{QuantumCircuit.IsBoundedProtocol.exists_portRing} in QICLean's
    \path{QICLean/Circuit/Compile/PortProtocol}, with the environments at the
    non-port wires and $k\ge1$. It stores the registers in the data wires, with
    zero port and scratch wires, at input and output, in place of
    $J_{\mathrm{port}}$.}
The formal statement uses finite environment types attached to the sites
(one-element outside the non-port wires at input and output, every wire outside
them of dimension at most $d$ throughout the dilating circuit) rather than
appended qudit wires; the padding of the environments into qudits is not
formalized.
The total number of environment wires is finite for each protocol; this
existence theorem does not impose a uniform environment-size bound on an
entire family.

\section{Exact pure outputs of the unitary realization}
Let $U$ and the appended environment $E$ be as
in~(\ref{eq:scope_deferred_dilation}). If the physical-port channel sends
$\ketbra{\psi}{\psi}$ to the normalized pure state $\ketbra{\phi}{\phi}$,
the joint output of its unitary realization factors as
\begin{align}
    U(\psi\otimes\ket{0_E})=\phi\otimes\eta,
    \qquad \langle\eta,\eta\rangle=1.
    \label{eq:scope_pure_output_product}
\end{align}
The environment vector is derived from the joint output; its decoupling
is not an input assumption. Write $C_{s,e}=(U(\psi\otimes\ket{0_E}))_{s,e}$
for the coefficient matrix of the joint output in the explicit
original/environment coordinates, and $P=\ketbra{\phi}{\phi}$. Then
\begin{align}
    CC^\dagger & =\operatorname{tr}_E\bigl(U(\ketbra{\psi}{\psi}\otimes\ketbra{0_E}{0_E})U^\dagger\bigr)
    =\Phi(\ketbra{\psi}{\psi})=P,
    \label{eq:scope_pure_output_marginal} \\
    (I-P)CC^\dagger & =P-P^2=0,
    \notag \\
    (I-P)C & =0,
    \label{eq:scope_pure_output_annihilation}
\end{align}
where~(\ref{eq:scope_pure_output_annihilation}) follows from $MM^\dagger=0$
for $M=(I-P)C$, since $MM^\dagger=0$ forces $M=0$. Hence $C=PC$, and with
$\eta=\phi^\dagger C$ every coefficient is $C_{s,e}=\phi_s\eta_e$. Taking
the trace of~(\ref{eq:scope_pure_output_marginal}) gives
$\langle\eta,\eta\rangle=\operatorname{tr}P=\langle\phi,\phi\rangle=1$.
The unitary, spatial ownership, physical ports and intersite-depth bound
are those of~(\ref{eq:scope_deferred_dilation}).\footnote{Formal theorems:
    \leanid{QuantumCircuit.Circuit.exists_unit_product_approx} at
    $\varepsilon=0$, applied to the same-depth unitary dilation
    \leanid{QuantumCircuit.IsProtocol.isUnitaryDilation} of a protocol on
    \leanid{QuantumCircuit.Geometry.portRing}, in QICLean's
    \path{QICLean/Circuit/Compile/DilationOutput} and
    \path{QICLean/Circuit/Compile/Dilation}. The branch statement below is
    not formalized.}
The environment may remain internally entangled and need not reset to zero.
The environment-column operators also realize the channel, and each
branch sends the input vector to its environment amplitude times the target
vector. Their amplitudes are normalized, so every branch of nonzero probability
has the same pure system state. This does not supply a source QCcc control
order or a fixed number of blocks.

The same projection gives the approximate estimate of the section on
approximate pure outputs below. Neither result supplies a source QCcc
block/control decomposition.


\section{Pure native input and output on their original ports}
For a $B$-dimension-bounded native channel $\Psi$ with physical input and
output dimension $d\ge2$, suppose
$\Psi(\ketbra{\psi}{\psi})=\ketbra{\phi}{\phi}$ and
$\langle\phi,\phi\rangle=1$. For $N\ge2$ and
$k=\lceil\log_d B\rceil$, let $V_{\rm port}$ be the explicit product
isometry that places each qudit on its original port and initializes its
data and scratch digits to zero. The bounded compiler and the exact
pure-output theorem give the vector identity
\begin{align}
    U(V_{\rm port}\psi\otimes\ket{0_E})
     & =V_{\rm port}\phi\otimes\eta,
     & \langle\eta,\eta\rangle                 & =1,
     & \operatorname{depth}_{\rm intersite}(U) & \le2kT.
    \label{eq:scope_bounded_port_pure_vector}
\end{align}
The same unitary retains the full logical channel identity
(\ref{eq:scope_unitary_port_io}). Both the compiled channel and its
normalized environment are derived, not supplied as additional premises.
The designated memory is zero in the final vector, while the
additional environment need not reset or factor over sites. The dimension
bound must be fixed independently of the chain length in a
family.\footnote{No single formal theorem states this identity. Its
    ingredients are \leanid{QuantumCircuit.IsBoundedProtocol.exists_portRing},
    \leanid{QuantumCircuit.IsProtocol.isUnitaryDilation} and
    \leanid{QuantumCircuit.Circuit.exists_unit_product_approx} in QICLean's
    \path{QICLean/Circuit/Compile}; their composition with the port
    isometry is not stated.}
It does not settle the adaptive block count, measurement/control order,
approximate pure-output estimates or unrestricted enlarged-site comparison.

\section{Approximate pure outputs from the actual bounded compiler}
For unit input and target vectors, suppose the actual native channel
satisfies
$\|\Psi(\ketbra{\psi}{\psi})-\ketbra{\phi}{\phi}\|_1\le\varepsilon$.
With the same fixed $d,B$, $N\ge2$, and $k=\lceil\log_d B\rceil$, its
actual compiled unitary admits a unit vector $\eta$ on its unchanged
additional environment space with
\begin{align}
    \|U(V_{\rm port}\psi\otimes\ket{0_A})
    -V_{\rm port}\phi\otimes\eta\|_2 & \le\sqrt{2\varepsilon},
                                     & \operatorname{depth}_{\rm intersite}(U) & \le2kT.
\end{align}
The vector norm is Euclidean and the matrix error is the full trace norm.
The actual marginal and its normalization follow from the all-operator
compiler/dilation identity and trace preservation. Its error is transported
exactly by the explicit port isometry. Projecting its coefficient matrix
onto the target vector derives an environment row of squared norm at least
$1-\varepsilon$. Normalizing that row gives the estimate; the zero-row case
forces $\varepsilon\ge1$ and is included.

The generic finite-dimensional estimate is
\leanid{Matrix.exists_unit_product_approx_of_pure_marginal} in QICLean's
\path{QICLean/Channel/PureMarginalApproximation}, and its reading on a
unitary dilation is \leanid{QuantumCircuit.Circuit.exists_unit_product_approx}
in QICLean's \path{QICLean/Circuit/Compile/DilationOutput}. The
specialization to the bounded compiler and the port isometry is obtained by
composing it with \leanid{QuantumCircuit.IsBoundedProtocol.exists_portRing}
and \leanid{QuantumCircuit.IsProtocol.isUnitaryDilation}; no single formal
theorem states it. The estimate supplies neither environment
reset nor source adaptive measurement/control or block-count conditions.

\section{Remaining comparison and source conditions}
The finite local-unitary dilation, product-zero environment initialization,
and one final disposal are proved by~(\ref{eq:scope_deferred_dilation}).
A full source protocol witness must still satisfy the source's final
system--ancilla condition when claiming a pure-state preparation, together
with its measurement and classical-control rules. No clean unitary identity
that returns every dilation environment to zero is asserted. For the asymptotic \textit{QCcc} relation, the source fixes the number
of composed \textit{QCcc} protocols independently of $N$. This is not a
bound on individual local measurement/correction steps: one protocol can
already contain sequential steps at many sites. A source-block decomposition
must also satisfy the within-block control order and one-measurement-per-site
restriction. None of these conditions follows from a quantum-depth bound.

Every finite positive-input native protocol admits some finite dimension
budget. For a family this gives only a potentially size-dependent $B(N)$,
which does not supply the uniform constant above. The encoded compiler
therefore does not identify the unrestricted enlarged-site phase relation
with the source's relation. No MPS classification is inferred.\footnote{The
    resource comparison is tracked by \ghissue{8640}, under \ghissue{8622};
    approximate and adaptive conversions are tracked by \ghissue{8380} and
    \ghissue{8382}.}
