\section{Source statement}

\cite[Proposition~1, lines~349--354]{Cirac2017MPU} asserts: if $E$ is the
transfer matrix of a matrix-product unitary with physical dimension $d$ and
bond dimension $D$, then $\tr(E^N)=1$ for all $N>1$, and consequently the
spectrum of $E$ contains a single eigenvalue equal to one with the rest
vanishing.  The paper cites Lemma~A.5 of
\cite{Cirac2016MPDO_arXiv}\footnote{Lemma~A.5, lines~1155--1163 of the
preprint arXiv:1606.00608.} for this conclusion.

\section{The point at issue}

The present formalization (PR~\ghpr{5732}) proves the algebraic core
under the stronger hypothesis $\tr(A^k)=1$ for \emph{all} $k\ge 1$,
including $k=1$.  The source only provides $\tr(E^N)=1$ for $N>1$ via
unitarity of blocked tensors.

Newton's identities determine the elementary symmetric polynomials
(hence the characteristic polynomial) from the power-sum traces
$p_k=\tr(A^k)$.  The recurrence
$k e_k = \sum_{i=1}^k (-1)^{i-1} e_{k-i} p_i$ needs $p_1$ to
determine $e_1$, $p_1$ and $p_2$ to determine $e_2$, etc.  With only
$p_2,p_3,\dots$ the system is underdetermined; no purely algebraic
argument can recover the characteristic polynomial from $k>1$ moments
alone.

In the source context the missing first moment follows from the
\emph{definition} of $E$ as a normalized partial trace of a unitary,
which gives $\tr(E)=1$ independently of the blocking argument.  The
formalization therefore supplies the algebraic prerequisite under the
all-positive-moment hypothesis it actually needs, leaving the
definitional first-moment recovery to the downstream MPU transfer
argument (\ghissue{5706}).
