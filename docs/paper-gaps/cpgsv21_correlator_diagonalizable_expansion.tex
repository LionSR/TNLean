\section{Source statement}

Section~II.B.3 of Cirac--P\'erez-Garc\'ia--Schuch--Verstraete~\cite{Cirac2021Matrix}
notes that the transfer matrix $E$ need not be Hermitian and ``may have some
Jordan blocks'', and assumes only that the leading eigenvalue is simple.
It then states that a connected correlator at distance $n$ has the form
\begin{align}
    C(X,Y;n) & =\sum_{i=2}^{D^2} c_{XY}(i)\,\lambda_i^n,
    \label{eq:cpgsv21_correlator_pure_exponentials}
\end{align}
a sum of $D^2-1$ pure exponentials, with decay rate set by
$|\lambda_2|$.\footnote{Source location:
\path{Papers/2011.12127/TN-Review-main.tex}:433--440.}

\section{The deviation}

Equation~(\ref{eq:cpgsv21_correlator_pure_exponentials}) holds when the
transfer map, restricted to the traceless matrices, is diagonalizable. If that
restriction has a Jordan block $J=\lambda I+N$ of size $k+1$, then
\begin{align}
    J^n & =\sum_{j=0}^{\min(k,n)}\binom nj\lambda^{n-j}N^j,
    \notag
\end{align}
and a correlator whose insertions detect $N$ is a polynomial-exponential
sequence rather than a sum of pure exponentials. Likewise, a uniform bound $|C(X,Y;n)|\le C_{XY}|\lambda_2|^n$
can fail at a defective subleading eigenvalue. A bound $C_{XY}r^n$ still holds
for every rate $r$ strictly above the spectral radius of the complementary map,
by the Gelfand formula.

\section{Corrected statements}

The blueprint chapter on correlations states the expansion under the explicit
hypothesis that the restriction of the transfer map to the traceless matrices
is diagonalizable, and states the decay bound at every rate $r$ with
$\rho(E-P)<r<1$, with $\rho$ the spectral radius, where $P$ is the projection onto the fixed state; the rate $|\lambda_2|$ is recorded only for
the diagonalizable case. Neither statement has a formal declaration yet.

\section{Elimination plan}

Formalize the eigenbasis expansion of the centered insertion in the
diagonalizable case and the Gelfand-formula bound for the connected correlator.
The general polynomial-exponential expansion is a corrected replacement for
the source statement, not a proof of its unqualified pure-exponential form.
