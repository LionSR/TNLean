# Original-target histories in the actual interval recursion

This contribution records the literal QICLean stopped-history event on an
actual native interval candidate. Its target is the original global ground
space in interval and full-exterior coordinates. The event requires error
0.04 at every stopped state and error 3/200 at every active next state.
It is not inferred from viability of the final retained space.

The event is jointly measurable in a candidate with measurable orthogonal
projection and its prescribed finite sequence. Applied to the existing
balanced recursion, this proves measurability of the actual history at each
long parent. Replacing that parent's own sequence leaves its two shorter
child spaces unchanged, giving the exact fixed-input section. The proof
uses the existing independent-coordinate dependence theorem; it introduces
no second recursion and no measurable basis-selection assumption.

These are supporting statements for ALVV, arXiv:1602.08828v2, lines 749–755
and 1777–1817. They do not by themselves prove the physical failure bound
or a terminal-image energy estimate. In particular, simultaneous closeness
to all soft spectral targets is not asserted to be a measurable event.
The fixed-input probability theorem and physical improving-family data
remain separate parts of the construction.
