# One-sided energy updates on the actual interleaved tree

The contribution attaches regional energy updates to the targets, operator
families and retained spaces of the existing calibrated interval tree. The
physical tree theorem is invoked once. Its dimension bounds, generator rank
bounds, literal sampled merge identities and ordered-word properties remain
conclusions for those same objects. No second improving family is chosen.

## Mathematical content

A lower regional estimate of width c is certified by an attained normalized
minimum e of the original regional Hamiltonian A, the positive shift A-eI,
and e-c <= p <= e. Two child estimates of width 49/16 have sum within
57/8 below the parent minimum: the children omit at most one positive
contraction crossing their common boundary. Since 57/8 <= 8, the existing
central-center conversion supplies an admissible middle estimate after
subtracting the two attained collar minima and two.

The node statement then forms the literal soft operator with order twelve
and scale 768. Its raw native matrix is the fixed-complement principal slice
already used in the same-family energy theorem. It constructs the actual
compression on the given family's literal one-word image of the actual
child merge. The returned complete orthonormal eigenbasis satisfies the
compression eigenvector equations, with eigenvalues in decreasing order.
The update is the last eigenvalue minus three. Its one-sided original-parent
error is at most 3+rho < 49/16, where rho = 32 exp(-42)+1/64 < 1/16.
The valid middle-center certificate is also retained explicitly.

All collar minima are selected before the child scalar estimates. They are
not inputs. Independent normalized minimizing vectors in the successive
regional decompositions are identified using the shared uniqueness lemma
for the minimum value; no common minimizing vector for three regions is
assumed.

The tree adapter takes collars [l,l+ell) and [r-ell,r) at every long parent.
Length greater than 4ell guarantees their admissible geometry. It derives
50ell^2 <= D from the actual count formula and identifies the spectral
parameter mu with the canonical minimum using the original gap condition.
The existing word bound at power one gives exactly the certificate required
by the node theorem. The actual targets, families and child spaces are
substituted directly.

## Source and limits

The source is ALVV, arXiv:1602.08828v2, Figure fig:algo1 at line 1631 and
its energy proof at line 1807, in
References/1602.08828/local_alg_arxiv_v2.tex. The one-sided middle-center
interface is Definition def:soft-trunc, lines 1178–1185.

The downward bias by three and the collar-center conversion are corrected
auxiliaries. The calibrated family is constructed using hard truncation;
this result does not identify it with literal soft Generate. The exact
printed energy-power budget is not asserted. The false printed cluster
and soft-energy findings remain unchanged. See
[the regional centering note](https://sirui-lu.com/TNLean/paper-gaps/alvv16_soft_regional_energy_centering.pdf).

The result supplies a node update for every admissible pair of child scalar
estimates on the actual space tree. A single simultaneous recursive scalar
assignment, with true attained minima at the short leaves, is not constructed
in this contribution. No adaptive joint sampling law or running-time theorem
is claimed.
