# An actual low-energy viable space on every physical interval

The new dimension theorem applies to supported positive-contraction
nearest-neighbour interactions, every prescribed interval, and every
positive deficit. It assumes no global spectral gap. For a fixed bandwidth
`etaLD > 0`, the scalar constant is chosen before the chain length, local
dimension, interactions, interval and deficit. The statement retains the
local-dimension scope `q ≥ 2` and a freely chosen numerical count base
`m ≥ q` with `log m ≥ 100`. This numerical base changes no physical data;
`max q (ceil (exp 100))` is an available choice.

Let `G(c)` be the canonical raw-Hamiltonian spectral span through its
actual ground energy plus `c`. The conclusion is an actual regional
subspace that is `3/200`-viable for `G(etaLD - v)`, of dimension at most
`dim G(etaLD) * D0³`. Here `D0` is the actual initial count for the same
degree and suppression choice used by the physical improving families.
The result does not assume a density bound on `dim G(etaLD)`; the source's
low-density bound can subsequently be substituted for that multiplicity.

The targets at all dyadic scales use one canonical raw Hamiltonian and
one fixed interval isometry. Starting at `min(v, etaLD/2)` derives the
buffer requirement. Every least viable dimension is an attained natural
minimum. The actual ceiling count exceeds the finite regional dimension
at an explicit finite index. At a first bad-to-good pair, the calibrated
sample size uses the previous minimum `p`, while the space in which it is
drawn has the next minimum dimension `Q`. Its actual improving image has
dimension at most `p/2 < p` and remains viable for the preceding original
target. This contradicts the attained minimum. No family, count
comparison, sampling estimate or termination premise is supplied to the
physical theorem.

The proof supports ALVV, arXiv:1602.08828v2, Proposition
`prop:albootstrap-ld`, lines 1112–1128. The strict-decrease correction is
owned by the companion library:
https://sirui-lu.com/QICLean/paper-gaps/alvv16_low_density_sampling.pdf.
The fixed-bandwidth and nontrivial local-dimension scope is recorded in
`docs/paper-gaps/alvv16_low_energy_family_scope.tex`. The printed
logarithmic simplification, low-energy entropy, MPS and running-time
conclusions remain separate; none is asserted by this checkpoint.
