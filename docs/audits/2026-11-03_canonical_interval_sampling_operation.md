# A sampled operation for the existing balanced recursion

A realization of the fixed interval sampling choices determines an ordinary interval merge operation. Every valid long parent uses its own endpoint coordinate, the actual tensor merge of its adjacent child spaces, and the fixed family's literal sampled interleaved iteration. The families precede every sampling choice. Short or out-of-range parents are assigned the full space; a valid balanced root never uses them as internal merges.

The recursively retained spaces are obtained by applying the existing balancedIntervalSpace construction to this operation. No second recursive construction or final-trim replacement is introduced.

This definition supports Arad, Landau, Vazirani and Vidick, arXiv:1602.08828v2, Figure fig:algo1 and lines 1777–1817. Joint measurability of the recursive output, independence of the parent coordinate from its child outputs, and the complete success-probability estimate remain subsequent arguments.
