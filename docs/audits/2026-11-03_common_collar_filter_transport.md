# Common collar families for the same global filter

The new supporting lemma chooses one enlarged interval family before the
whole operator and its target. It derives the larger-interval matrix and
tensor decompositions from the original whole-matrix decomposition. Unitary
conjugation transfers the four global operator inequalities; the tensor
word theorem then derives viability on the larger interval. The argument
does not transfer viability directly between distinct tensor splits.

The family has exactly `n0*q^(2*card(J\E))` members and open matrix product
bond bound `max 1 B`. The original decomposition and global operator bounds
are explicit intermediate hypotheses of this algebraic lemma. It is not
an unconditional physical soft-family theorem, a running-time result, or
an assertion of the source's sharper parameter rates.

The duration exceeds the 25-second warning threshold but is below the
50-second limit. No timing-only repeat was run. The temporary inspection
commands were deleted. The ordinary integrated build remains a subsequent
check.

The larger physical long-interval theorem is excluded from this contribution.
No resource option was increased. The two complete algebraic contributions do
not depend on that unfinished statement. A later physical composition must
keep the natural certificate and apply the same-operator transport; it must
not select a second filter.
