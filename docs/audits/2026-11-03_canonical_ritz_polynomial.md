# Ritz and polynomial MPS output from the same successful canonical root

The new theorem consumes the physical canonical joint-probability factory
exactly once. Its common reference, clipped targets, operator families,
ordered-word certificates and all-seed profiles are retained together.
No older existential merge operation or independent MPS output is selected.

The polynomial coefficient and natural degree are chosen at fixed local
dimension and supplied gap before length, interaction, ground multiplicity,
requested accuracy or seeds. The physical family is chosen before accuracy.
For each positive accuracy, the automatic arbitrary-candidate refinement
chooses one common threshold and final reference before every seed. The
choice `Dtree = Dref*ceil(1/a)` places this threshold below the same physical
family's threshold ceiling. The Borel good event is the actual root and
actual root-list joint viability event at that threshold; it may therefore
depend on accuracy. Its failure bound is `Nat(L-1)/(L+1)^2`.

For every good seed, the initial candidate is exactly its canonical
full-interval root. Full-interval viability gives native closeness, while
the unconditional profile gives its dimension bound. The public refinement
theorem applies to that unchanged candidate. The final space is its literal
raw-filter-and-trim iterate, and the returned space is the actual compressed
Ritz subspace of that same terminal space for the original Hamiltonian.
It has dimension equal to the full ground multiplicity and both closeness
errors at most half the requested accuracy. Every vector in that Ritz space
and every vector in the initial native root has the same displayed bond
budget. The existing numerical theorem bounds this exact budget by one
polynomial independent of the seed. The final reference is an auxiliary
trimming control, rather than a replacement for the actual root or output.

The source is ALVV, arXiv:1602.08828v2, lines 1777–1817. The statement retains
positive nearest-neighbour contractions, two actual distinct spectral
values and the supplied gap lower bound. The block leaves, capped reference
gap, hard-clipped families and corrected parent and final counts remain
explicit in `docs/paper-gaps/alvv16_approximation_parameters.tex` and
`docs/paper-gaps/alvv16_final_filter_gain.tex`. Neither the printed
soft-truncated Generate procedure, measurable Ritz-basis selection, nor a
running-time bound is asserted.
