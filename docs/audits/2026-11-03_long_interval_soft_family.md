# A common physical soft family on a prescribed long interval

The new natural-core corollary uses the original physical soft improving
factory once. It retains the five attained regional minima and their
positive-shift certificates, a common unit minimizing vector, the residual
ground energy, and one matrix family before all three bulk-center estimates.
The native tensors remain internal witnesses. Each estimated center retains
the represented Hermitian matrix, its own closed low-energy target, both
comparisons with the original ground space, and the original filter.

The long-interval theorem applies this same construction to the inward core
of the prescribed parent interval. One collar family is then fixed before all
estimated centers. Its cardinality is at most the square of the original
count budget, and its member bond bound changes only by a maximum with one.
Both individual collars from the parent endpoints to the true middle are
at most twice the window width. In particular the original middle minimum
is retained for the terminal-energy estimate on the parent itself.

For each center, the original filter is pulled back to its whole-chain
matrix and expressed through the already chosen collar family. The private
continuation lemma only separates this proved algebraic composition; its
matrix-decomposition and operator-bound inputs come from the actual natural
factory, and its conclusion is supplied by the proved collar theorem. The
public theorem assumes neither a decomposition nor an improving family.
Its ordered-word estimate is derived in the enlarged tensor split. No
viability assertion is moved directly between different tensor splits.

The temporary inspection commands were deleted. Ordinary integrated
verification remains subsequent work.

Earlier attempts at retaining the complete native formula in the public
statement reached the default normalization limit. Bounded diagnostics
confirmed that the matrix-action arguments and the whole decomposition are
definitionally equal after substituting local coordinate definitions. The
final proof uses core unfolding of those local definitions, separates the
natural-core conclusion, and checks the global pullback over abstract finite
spaces. No resource or transparency option was changed.

These are explicit-budget, long-interval auxiliaries to ALVV Proposition 5.7.
They do not assert the printed fourth-order parameter choice, its sharper
gap dependence, or an efficient algorithm. The residual and cluster
corrections remain those documented in
`docs/paper-gaps/alvv16_soft_regional_residuals.tex` and
`docs/paper-gaps/alvv16_cluster_approximation.tex`.
