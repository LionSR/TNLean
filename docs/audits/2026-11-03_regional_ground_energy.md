# Regional energy from the original ground space

The interval Hamiltonian is centered at its genuine attained minimum. Positive nearest-neighbour contractions then give a regional excitation operator with norm at most the chain length and quadratic-form energy at most two on the entire original ground space. No spectral gap or simple-ground-space hypothesis is supplied.

For every candidate whose projection loss is at most `1/[100(L+1)]`, the actual projected ground space has energy at most three. An actual nonzero vector of the candidate is constructed by projecting a unit eigenvector at the original minimum. This rules out an empty-candidate reading of the conclusion.

The original Hamiltonian, its canonical minimum and ground space remain in the
statement. The regional minimum is certified by a normalized attaining vector
and positivity. Only the original regional ground-data part of the existing
hard-truncation theorem is used; its cutoff-dependent residual conclusions are
discarded.
