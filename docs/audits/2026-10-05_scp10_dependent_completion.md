# Dependent native parents and coherent regular blocking

This packet extends the source results in PRs #8694, #8696 and #8700.
Code size is not a measure of paper completeness. The new source files,

## Mathematical conclusions

For native simple tori with both periods at least three, each edge may
carry an independent semi-regular representation and dimension, and each
site an independent G-injective tensor. The complete existing positive
microscopic plaquette-parent kernel equals the actual native commuting-
closure span. Its dimension is the number of simultaneous conjugacy
classes of commuting pairs.

Independent finite physical alphabets are faithfully zero-extended into
the existing common-alphabet parent framework. Product restriction is the
inverse on the entire ambient kernel: no extra local-image or support
premise is imposed on a ground vector. The explicit equivalence identifies
the original physical closure span with that full kernel.

The proof obtains actual correlated seam boundaries, coherent bond
coefficients and flatness from microscopic parent membership. Arbitrary-
period gauge classification then yields native closure spanning directly.
It does not assume a four-block identification or closure expansion.

The separately useful four-block theorem has eight independent bond
alphabets and four physical alphabets. Its closure-class independence and
dimension follow from the derived diagonal coefficient
|G|^(-4) times the simultaneous centralizer cardinality. It is a theorem
about actual four-cut spaces, not a substitute definition of the parent.

For regular G-isometric tensors, a single block-local physical support map
is chosen before the native seam labels and coherent coefficients.
It carries every doubled-torus closure to the same original coarse tensor
times one fixed normalized Bell product, retaining all positive scalars.
It preserves coherent coefficients and phases, proves simultaneous
vanishing, and gives the normalized identity for nonzero sums.
Noncommuting labels satisfy the algebraic identity; their states are not
thereby declared parent ground states. Physical separation still requires
coarse periods at least three; the bond-indexed geometry permits all
positive periods.

## Integration checks and limits

All 155 compiled declaration names are checked together, and every public
declaration has a blueprint owner. Source synchronization, reverse
coverage, generated imports, repository formatting, module policies and
paper-gap references are integration checks, distinct from remote CI.

The parent/seam companion adds four displays with seven typed panels.
Individual and combined renders, exact tuple/incidence checks, seven
negative mutations and an independent contraction/padding fixture pass.
The four-cut dimension fragment has a checked inverse/extraction diagram.
The source and scope audits for each packet give the detailed statement
and normalization evidence.

Remaining work includes exceptional native periods with parallel bonds or
loops, other boundary/tiling RG models, and the still-open source string,
excitation and Section 7 obligations. No spectral-gap preservation or
conjugacy of arbitrary local Hamiltonians follows from a linear
ground-space equivalence. MPU-gauging code is outside this work.
