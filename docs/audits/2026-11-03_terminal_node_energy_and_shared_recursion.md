# Terminal node energy and the existing scalar recursion

The terminal node theorem selects actual original parent and middle minima,
then actual collar minima, before all child estimates. The two child lower
estimates of width `49/16` determine the middle center by subtracting the
collar minima and two. Its admissibility is proved internally. The calibrated
terminal theorem is then applied at precisely that center, retaining the same
regional soft matrix, fixed-complement native restriction and actual terminal
untrimmed family image. Its least compressed eigenvalue, minus three, has
one-sided original parent error at most `49/16`.

The parent minimum is retained with its original unit eigenvector and positive
scalar shift; the final explicit bounds on the actual compressed eigenvalue
are exactly the corresponding lower-energy certificate. A redundant second
expansion of that predicate at the dependent eigenvalue caused an elaborator
scope diagnostic. Removing that repeated conclusion loses no mathematical
information. All diagnostic sources and logs are preserved privately.

The existing relation-based scalar assignment theorem is now public in
`BalancedRegionalEnergy`. Its proof and private well-founded recursion are
unchanged. All previously public signatures and existing proof bodies in
that module are byte-identical. The relation and local energy hypotheses are
explicit auxiliary assumptions; no filtering procedure, sampling success or
new realization predicate is hidden in the recursion. For root-only use, the
existing root-subtree argument uses the specified relation on the actual
merge list and attained regional minima outside that list. Thus the reusable
recursion does not require sampling success on all overlapping intervals.

Temporary commands were removed. All selected dependencies, root inputs and
prebuilt Mathlib objects were guarded, with serialized elaboration and
unchanged default limits.

No provider, pin, cache or Mathlib source was modified. Combined ordinary
gates remain required.

Target dimension, comparison and exact terminal-image viability remain
explicit certificates for the same physical family and successful actual
sampling history. Final retained-space viability alone does not supply the
terminal-image certificate. The node theorem does not prove the complete
recursive physical soft-energy assignment, energy of the final trimmed
space or a runtime bound.
