\title{Nontrivial fusion and intrinsic length dependence in matrix product density operator fixed points}
\date{16 September 2026}

\begin{abstract}
	Length-dependent fusion coefficients can coexist with nonproduct states,
	nontrivial canonical fusion, and anomalous boundary symmetry. These
	properties do not, however, establish that the positive weights are
	inseparable from the fusion intertwiners. We distinguish these questions
	for finite-group, Ising, Fibonacci, and CZX constructions. Finite-group
	bond twists admit an exact local circuit separating their weights from
	the ordered group constraint. For the categorical constructions, a fixed
	regrouping gives the original Ising or Fibonacci representatives with
	sector-dependent one-site multiplicities. We determine their canonical
	normalizations and multiplicity spectra and construct full-space
	fixed-point channels. Their states are not products of identical
	independent bonds. Their multiplicity ranks also exclude a common mixed
	spectator in the displayed sector-preserving one-site decomposition,
	but do not exclude every bounded-depth separation. The shifted CZX
	tensor, by contrast, has identical multiplicities in both sectors and
	separates exactly. Thus existing examples establish length dependence
	with nontrivial algebra and nonproduct states, but do not yet establish
	inseparable weight-intertwiner coupling under the stronger local
	equivalence considered here.
\end{abstract}
