\title{An obstruction to coefficient compatibility in the environment-four MPDO search}
\date{16 September 2026}

\begin{abstract}
	We investigate the trace-one, full-span matrix product density operator
	ansatz with physical dimension five, horizontal bond dimension four,
	and local purification Gram ranks $(1,2)$. We give an exhaustive
	parameterization of its rank-one idempotent trace transfer modulo
	specified virtual and physical gauges. Rank-deficient transfer weights
	cannot support coefficient span five. On the faithful locus, the
	parameters reduce to a diagonal qubit state and a two-dimensional
	Hilbert--Schmidt subspace of $M_2(\C)$. We then prove that the one-site
	coefficient kernel is never contained in the two-site coefficient
	kernel. The proof uses the left and right orbits of the rank-one
	transfer: closure would force both orbit dimensions to be two, which
	in turn forces an active matrix pencil whose products violate closure.
	Thus no linear refinement map, and hence no full-space quantum-channel
	fixed-point certificate, exists in this bounded stratum. Positivity
	of the periodic operators follows independently from local purification.
	The same obstruction holds for any scalar Gram rank while the active
	Gram rank remains one. It does not exclude active Gram rank at least two
	or idempotent trace transfers of rank greater than one.
\end{abstract}
