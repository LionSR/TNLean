\section{Reproducible numerical controls}
\label{sec:numerics}

A deterministic finite search and exact structured regressions were run
independently of the obstruction proof. The implementation is
\path{docs/audits/rfp_intrinsic/checks/environment_four_search.py}.
Its seed is $20260916$. The script saves its bounds, versions, initial
and final parameters, Kraus matrices, singular values, and reconstruction
checks. It does not use a channel solver or infer a no-go from optimization.

The searches used
\begin{align}
	K_\mu=\sum_{a,b}U_{\mu,ab}\sqrt R E_{ab}\sqrt L,
	\qquad \tr(LR)=1,
\end{align}
with the first two Kraus matrices active. Twelve initial trials used
$R=I$, $L=\diag(p,1-p)$ and $0.1\leq p\leq0.5$; four used independently
parameterized faithful input and output weights. Finite Grassmann charts
around seeded unitary frames do not assert global numerical coverage.
Eight recentered sensitivity refinements completed the saved set of
24 endpoints. For the physical Hilbert--Schmidt coefficient matrices,
the forward residual was
\begin{align}
	\epsilon_{\rm fwd}
	=\frac{\|F_2(I-F_1^+F_1)\|_{\rm F}}{\|F_2\|_{\rm F}},
\end{align}
where $+$ is the Moore--Penrose inverse and $\|\cdot\|_{\rm F}$ is the
Frobenius norm. Active coordinates include the factor $\|\tau\|_{\rm F}$.
This metric is recorded because it need not be invariant under arbitrary
nonunitary virtual similarities.

The best initial canonical-weight residual was $0.0326371963671$,
with smallest-to-largest one-site singular-value ratio $0.0823107997208$.
The smaller residual $0.0262344347885$ in the independent-weight runs
had span ratio $0.0498860484088$, below the prescribed $0.05$ floor,
and was rejected. Soft conditioning penalties were not treated as
hard feasibility certificates. Separately well-conditioned $R$ and $L$
also need not make $\sqrt R L\sqrt R$ comparably well conditioned;
validation records that canonical spectrum explicitly.
No endpoint passed the survivor tests. Independent reconstruction of
all 24 endpoints gave physical-coordinate and purification errors below
$4\times10^{-16}$, Kraus-frame unitarity error below $1.2\times10^{-14}$,
and normalized trace-idempotence error below $1.1\times10^{-15}$.
A fresh replay reproduced all 24 forward residuals exactly at the
stored floating-point precision.

Exact rational controls reproduce the Pauli kernel witness $1/24$.
For diagonal active pencils, common-output pencils, and common-input
pencils, respectively, they give
\begin{align}
	(a,b,\dim\mc W,\dim(\mc W+\mc W^2))
	=(2,2,5,6),\ (2,4,5,8),\ (4,2,5,8),
\end{align}
where $a,b$ are the orbit dimensions in \cref{eq:orbits}.
These checks illustrate the mechanism. In particular, an ideal dimension
of four does not by itself imply that the ideal is contained in $\mc W$.
Neither the finite residuals nor the structured examples prove universal
nonexistence; that conclusion comes from \cref{thm:no-go,cor:scalar-extension}.
