\section{Outcome and precise remaining scope}
\label{sec:outcome}

The trace-one, full-span search with physical dimension five,
horizontal bond dimension four, and Gram ranks $(1,2)$ is
analytically excluded. The exclusion is stronger than a failure of
full-space channel feasibility: every tensor in the stratum has a
one-site coefficient relation that fails after blocking. Hence even
a complex-linear refinement on the encoded letter span is impossible.

The reduction treats all transfer weights and all Kraus choices.
Rank-deficient input or output weights cannot support coefficient
span five. Faithful weights admit the exhaustive normal form
\cref{eq:frame-param,eq:parameter-space}; no restriction to a Pauli
frame or a generic subspace is made. On that entire domain,
closure forces two-dimensional transfer orbits. Their matrix-pencil
structure then gives the incompatible off-diagonal entries in
\cref{eq:T-mixed-bases,eq:ET-mixed-bases}. The argument also includes
the degenerate output state $R=\Id_2/2$.

This provides a verified stopping point for Priority A at the stated
dimensions and hypotheses. There is no surviving coefficient locus
for a subsequent Choi search or stationary-encoding reconstruction
within this stratum. The positive periodic operators supplied by
local purification remain valid states after the imposed trace-one
normalization, but they cannot satisfy the required tensor refinement
identities.

The scope of the result is limited. Trace one was used together with
trace idempotence to obtain a rank-one transfer; it was not imposed
by arbitrary scalar rescaling. Idempotent transfers of rank greater
than one require a different analysis. Increasing only the scalar Gram rank does not help, by
\cref{cor:scalar-extension}. Active Gram rank at least two, trace ranks
greater than one, and different physical encodings require separate
analysis. The first unexcluded same-encoding pair is $(2,1)$, with
purification environment dimension at least five. Blocking can change
the active Gram rank and physical encoding, so no all-blocking exclusion
is claimed. Nor does the proof establish a statement about all MPDO
fixed points or about intrinsic fusion weighting.
For any construction outside this excluded stratum, complete
positivity, full-space recovery, canonical form, nonproduct structure,
and vertical fusion data remain separate requirements.
