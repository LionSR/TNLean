\section{Finite-group boundary states}
\label{sec:intrinsic-groups}

Let $G$ be a finite group of order $n$ and identity $e$. Each site
has registers $L,R,f$ with Hilbert space $\C[G]^{\otimes3}$.
Define orthonormal bond vectors and positive weights by
\begin{align}
	\ket{\phi_b}
	                & =n^{-1/2}\sum_{r\in G}\ket{r,rb},        &
	\Pi_b           & =\ket{\phi_b}\bra{\phi_b},                 \\
	\bra{\phi_b}\ket{\phi_c}
	                & =n^{-1}\sum_r\delta_{rb,rc}=\delta_{bc}, &
	\sum_b\lambda_b & =1.
\end{align}
The label $b_m$ belongs to $(R_m,L_{m+1})$. The ordered constraint
is $f_1b_1\cdots f_Nb_N=e$. The family is
\begin{align}
	\rho_N^\lambda
	 & =n^{1-N}
	\sum_{\substack{\boldsymbol f,\boldsymbol b\\
			f_1b_1\cdots f_Nb_N=e}}
	\left(\prod_m\lambda_{b_m}\right)
	\bigotimes_m\ket{f_m}\bra{f_m}
	\otimes\bigotimes_m\Pi_{b_m}.
	\label{eq:group-state}
\end{align}
Every term is positive. For fixed $\boldsymbol b$ and
$f_1,\ldots,f_{N-1}$, there is one allowed $f_N$, so
\begin{align}
	\tr\rho_N^\lambda
	 & =n^{1-N}n^{N-1}
	\sum_{\boldsymbol b}\prod_m\lambda_{b_m}=1.
\end{align}

\subsection{An exact local separation}

Let $R_b\ket f=\ket{fb}$ on the flag, and
$\Pi_\perp=\Id-\sum_b\Pi_b$ on the bond. Define
\begin{align}
	U_m
	 & =\sum_b\Pi_b^{R_mL_{m+1}}\otimes R_b^{f_m}
	+\Pi_\perp^{R_mL_{m+1}}\otimes\Id_{f_m}.
	\label{eq:group-gate}
\end{align}
Orthogonality gives
\begin{align}
	U_m^\dagger U_m
	 & =\sum_b\Pi_b\otimes R_b^\dagger R_b
	+\Pi_\perp\otimes\Id
	=\Id.
\end{align}
The gates use disjoint subregisters. They have bounded depth even
with a site-based schedule, by coloring neighboring bonds.

\begin{proposition}[Finite-group spectator separation]
	\label{prop:group-separation}
	For every positive weight vector and every $N\geq1$,
	\begin{align}
		U_\partial\rho_N^\lambda U_\partial^\dagger
		           & =\tau_N^G\otimes
		\bigotimes_m\left(\sum_b\lambda_b\Pi_b\right), \\
		U_\partial & =\prod_mU_m,                      \\
		\tau_N^G
		           & =n^{1-N}\sum_{g_1\cdots g_N=e}
		\bigotimes_m\ket{g_m}\bra{g_m}.
		\label{eq:group-separation}
	\end{align}
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
	For each $\boldsymbol b$, set $g_m=f_mb_m$. This is a bijection,
	and
	\begin{align}
		f_1b_1\cdots f_Nb_N=e
		\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
		g_1\cdots g_N=e.
	\end{align}
	The circuit implements this substitution and leaves every bond
	projector unchanged. The constraint no longer involves
	$\boldsymbol b$, so the two sums factor. No commutation of group
	elements is used.
\end{proof}

The core is nonproduct for $n>1$: at $N=2$, its flags satisfy
$g_2=g_1^{-1}$ while both marginals are uniform. Thus this family
contains more than dimers, but all dependence on $\lambda$ is
spectator dependence.

\subsection{Fixed-cut canonical fusion}

The same family has length-dependent canonical data at the original
cut. Let $\pi$ range over unitary irreducible representations of $G$,
with dimension $d_\pi$. There are $d_\pi$ horizontal copies of
\begin{align}
	Q_\pi^{(r,s,i),(t,u,j)}
	 & =\frac1{n^2}\sum_{b,f}\lambda_b[\pi(bf)]_{ij}
	\ket{rb}\bra{sb}_L\otimes\ket t\bra u_R
	\otimes\ket f\bra f.
	\label{eq:group-tensor}
\end{align}
Contraction of a bond gives
\begin{align}
	\sum_{r,s}\ket r\bra s\otimes\ket{rb}\bra{sb}
	 & =n\Pi_b.
\end{align}
The representation indices give
$\tr\pi(f_1b_1\cdots f_Nb_N)$. The identity
$\sum_\pi d_\pi\tr\pi(g)=n\delta_{g,e}$ then produces
\cref{eq:group-state}, including its normalization.

Let $B_f$ be the restriction to physical flag $f$. Matrix-coefficient
orthogonality gives
\begin{align}
	\sum_\pi d_\pi\sum_{i,j}
	              [\pi(bf)]_{ij}\overline{[\pi(b'f)]_{ij}}
	 & =n\delta_{bb'},                                \\
	\mc E_{B_f}(X)
	 & =\frac{\sum_b\lambda_b^2}{n^3}\tr(X)\Id_{n^2}.
\end{align}
Hence
\begin{align}
	\nu^2        & =\frac1n\sum_b\lambda_b^2, &
	\widehat B_f & =B_f/\nu,                  &
	\mu_f        & =(\nu).
\end{align}
If $K_{c,l}=\lambda_{c^{-1}l}$ is invertible, the horizontal
blocks are injective and the flag representatives are inequivalent.
To see the latter, a relation among their one-letter traces implies
\begin{align}
	\widehat\lambda(\pi)\sum_fa_f\pi(f) & =0,
	                                    &
	\widehat\lambda(\pi)                & =\sum_b\lambda_b\pi(b).
\end{align}
Invertibility of every Fourier block gives
$\sum_fa_f\pi(f)=0$ for every $\pi$, and Fourier inversion gives
$a_f=0$. A common linear combination of trivial-representation
letters is $\Id_{n^2}/n^2$, so padding with this combination
also proves independence of the closures at every $L$.
At singular $K$, the displayed canonical labels require a new
reduction.

Inserting $\phi_b$ into the inner bond gives orthogonal isometries
$J_b$. Concatenation yields
\begin{align}
	B_fB_{f'}
	 & =\sum_b\frac{\lambda_b}{n}J_bB_{fbf'}J_b^\dagger,      \\
	\widehat B_f\widehat B_{f'}
	 & =\sum_b\frac{\lambda_b}{n\nu}
	J_b\widehat B_{fbf'}J_b^\dagger,                          \\
	c_{ff'g}(L)
	 & =\left(\frac{\lambda_{f^{-1}gf'^{-1}}}{n\nu}\right)^L.
	\label{eq:group-fusion}
\end{align}
The unused inner-bond subspace is a zero complement. For each
target $g$ and bond label $b$, there are $n$ input pairs, so
\begin{align}
	\sum_{f,f'}m_fm_{f'}\tr\chi_{ff'g}
	 & =\sum_b n\nu^2\frac{\lambda_b}{n\nu}
	=\nu=m_g.
\end{align}
This verifies the equal-mass certificate. Equivalently, refinement
prepares an inner bond and two flags with probability $\lambda_b/n$
conditional on $fbf'=g$; coarse-graining reads $b$ and outputs
$fbf'$, with any normalized completion on $\Pi_\perp$.

Flattening requires $s_fs_{f'}\theta_b=s_{fbf'}$, where
$\theta_b=\lambda_b/(n\nu)$. The equations with $b=e$ make
$t_f=\theta_es_f$ a positive homomorphism:
\begin{align}
	t_ft_{f'}                   & =t_{ff'}, &
	t_e                         & =1,       &
	t_f^{\operatorname{ord}(f)} & =1.
\end{align}
Thus $t_f=1$, and the remaining equations force all $\theta_b$
to be equal. Nonconstant regular weights therefore resist fixed
positive representative rescaling, despite
\cref{prop:group-separation}.

For the phase-Bell $\Z_2$ version, the corresponding gate is
$\Pi_0\otimes\Id+\Pi_1\otimes X+\Pi_\perp\otimes\Id$.
It toggles the flag by the Bell label and converts correlated
parity into independent even parity. An adapted toric-code Schmidt
cut supplies that parity core; bounded-size purified-dimer clusters
supply the mixed bonds without changing the bulk phase.
This boundary statement does not make a Schmidt-coordinate flag
flip local on the original toric-code region. A single flip leaves
the even support. A physical implementation can instead use
additional flags copied from retained cut-edge bits. Such an
enlarged realization does not establish a local lift through an
arbitrary PEPS boundary map.
