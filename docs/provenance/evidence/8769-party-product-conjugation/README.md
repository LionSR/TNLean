# Conjugation and private traces of party products

The module proves that the adjoint of a party product is the product of its adjoints, that two local map families conjugate a product source matrix separately at each party, and that the resulting global private trace is the product of the local physical matrices. No positivity or norm bounds are required, and the two map families may differ.
