# Coordinates and partial traces of party products

Three modules construct the ordered and party-labelled tensor bases, identify the matrix entries of the actual tensor product of local linear maps, and prove that the private partial trace of a product matrix is the product of its local partial traces. The matrices may be arbitrary. The statements impose no bounds on local dimensions or map norms.
