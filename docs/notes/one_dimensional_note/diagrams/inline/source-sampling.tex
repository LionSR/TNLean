% Section 11 (sec:compression), Theorem thm:compression, proof sketch (part2b_mps_protocol.tex): each source operator $X=\ket\eta\bra{\tilde\eta}$ on a pair of registers is replaced by $\widehat X=\frac1k\sum_{j=1}^k U_j\otimes V_j$ with Gaussian coefficients, and $\mathbb E\,\widehat X=X$. Left: the upper box is the shared vector $\eta$, its two upward legs the two registers of the pair; the lower box is the covector $\tilde\eta^{\,\dagger}$, its two downward legs the same two registers; the two boxes are not joined (the operator is the rank-one product $\ket\eta\bra{\tilde\eta}$). Right: the box $U_j$ acts on the first register (upward leg output, downward leg input) and the box $V_j$ on the second; the bond between them carries the sample index $j$, which is averaged with weight $1/k$ and then the expectation over the Gaussians is taken. All physical legs are open.
