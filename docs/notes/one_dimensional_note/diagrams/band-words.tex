% Section 10 (The sweep), Construction con:band (part2b_mps_protocol.tex), the band dissolution and the proof of Lemma lem:band: x = (. - a)/w is the scaled coordinate near the band [a, a+w), so one unit of x is the band width w. Row "start" is the main sheet C | A | B with C on x < 0 (down to at least -8), A on (0,1), B on x > 1 (up to at least 2). Row (i), marked aux, is the auxiliary sheet, initialized at C, after the births of B on (-6,-1), A on (-5,-2) and C on (-4,-3): the word C|B|A|C|A|B|C at -6,-5,-4,-3,-2,-1. Row (ii) is the main sheet after the exchange of the two sheets inside Y = (-7, -7/2) (the bracket above the bar) with P_circ = C: the word C|B|A|C|A|B at -6,-5,-4,0,1. Rows (iii)-(vi) are the main sheet after the death of the C band (-4,0) into A (word C|B|A|B at -6,-5,1), after the death of the A band (-5,1) into B (word C|B at -6), after the birth of C on (-3,0) inside B (word C|B|C|B at -6,-3,0), and after the death of the B band (-6,-3) into C (final word C|B at 0). Ink-to-index: each bar is the chain near the band with the site coordinate x increasing to the right, each coloured segment is the set of sites owned by the party written in it (blue C, grey A, orange B), the heavier outline marks the sites whose owner changed in that move (in row (i) the union (-6,-1) of the three nested births, in row (ii) the part (-6,-4) of Y where the two sheets differed), and the ticks are the interfaces used by the construction. No tensor is drawn and no leg is contracted.
