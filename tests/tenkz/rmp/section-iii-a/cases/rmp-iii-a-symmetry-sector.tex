% Formula: S_{alpha,g} is the two-loop string-order construction with
%   R_{bar alpha}, R_alpha, X_g, and X_g^dagger insertions.
% Ink: Two fixed-point sandwich loops with R on the internal physical bonds and
%   the on-site symmetry action on the lower ket rails.
% Boundary: Both loops close their virtual ends through rho_l and rho_r; all
%   physical indices are contracted.
% Source: paper TN-Review-main.tex line 1184; author source ImagesReview Section
%   III/ImagesReview Section III.tex lines 114-132
% Capabilities: grid, typed-ports
% The string-order expectation <S_{alpha,g}>: two sandwich loops, each a
% bra-A/ket-A pair closed west/east through the fixed points rho_l, rho_r,
% carrying the symmetry insertion R (on the ket-bra physical bond) and the
% on-site action X_g on the lower ket rail, as at y=-0.75 in the author
% source.  The op row is side-sealed so the west/east cups bend only the
% bra and ket rails.
\[
  \begin{tenkz}[rows={bra, op:west none:east none, ket}, tensor style=box,
                west={cup=$\rho_l$}, east={cup=$\rho_r$}]
    \tn{A} & \tnghost{}\\
    \tn[dot]{R_{\bar\alpha}} & \tnskip\\
    \tn{A} & \tnX{X_g}
  \end{tenkz}
  \qquad
  \begin{tenkz}[rows={bra, op:west none:east none, ket}, tensor style=box,
                west={cup=$\rho_l$}, east={cup=$\rho_r$}]
    \tnghost{} & \tn{A}\\
    \tnskip & \tn[dot]{R_\alpha}\\
    \tnX{X_g^\dagger} & \tn{A}
  \end{tenkz}
\]
