% t2_lassoC — RMP arXiv:2011.12127, fig2-pepe_Figboundary-theoryc: the
% region/lasso decomposition behind the PEPS boundary theory.
%
% Formula: |psi> = sum_{I, Ibar} A^{I}_{\partial R} B^{\bar I}_{\partial R}
%          |I>_R |Ibar>_{Rbar},
% where I collects the physical indices of the sites inside region R,
% Ibar those of the complement Rbar, and \partial R is the multi-index of
% the virtual bonds cut by the region boundary.  A^I is the contraction
% of all tensors inside R (the "lasso"), B^{\bar I} the contraction of
% everything outside; the single fat wire \partial R joining them is the
% only contraction between the two halves.
%
% Left half: the oblique lattice window with region R marked (filled
% slot=selected region: tint carries membership, its rounded outline the
% cut).  Right half: the two-atom equation — dot A^I with its physical
% bundle open (up leg), the bond labelled \partial R, dot B^{Ibar} with
% its bundle open; no other virtual index survives, and both outer ends
% seal SYMMETRICALLY, so boundary=none is the exact spelling (the
% per-side west=/east= keys parse but are not yet consulted by the
% renderer — not needed here).
%
% Noted gaps (recorded in the verdict):
%  * the RMP draws R as an ELLIPSE; \tnregion traces the rectilinear
%    rounded hull of the member cells — same set, different silhouette;
%  * no complement-label primitive: the RMP's "Rbar" annotation on the
%    outside of the window has no lattice-genre spelling;
%  * monochrome: the RMP's red-vs-black sublattice colouring has no
%    per-atom hue key (themes rebind colours globally only).
