Left orthonormality (RMP orthoL): $\sum_s (A_L^s)^\dagger A_L^s = \mathbb{I}$
\[
  % LHS: ket A_L over bra conj(A_L); physical s pairs automatically
  % (sandwich); west=cup contracts the shared left index.
  \begin{tenkz}[sandwich, west=cup]
    \tn[tri=l]{A_L}\\
    \tn*[tri=l]{A_L}
  \end{tenkz}
  \;=\;
  % RHS: the identity on the open (beta, beta') pair — a bare cup; the
  % ghosts are the wire endpoints (the suite idiom for a glyph-free wire).
  \begin{tenkz}[rows={wire,wire}, west=cup]
    \tnghost{}\\
    \tnghost{}
  \end{tenkz}
\]

Right orthonormality (RMP orthoR): $\sum_s A_R^s (A_R^s)^\dagger = \mathbb{I}$
\[
  % LHS: same sandwich with the right-canonical triangles and the cup on
  % the east (the contraction over the shared right index beta).
  \begin{tenkz}[sandwich, east=cup]
    \tn[tri=r]{A_R}\\
    \tn*[tri=r]{A_R}
  \end{tenkz}
  \;=\;
  % RHS: the identity on the open (alpha, alpha') pair — the mirrored cup.
  \begin{tenkz}[rows={wire,wire}, east=cup]
    \tnghost{}\\
    \tnghost{}
  \end{tenkz}
\]
