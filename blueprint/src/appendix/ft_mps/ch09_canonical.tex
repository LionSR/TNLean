The canonical form reductions of Chapter~\ref{ch:canonical} rely on the
algebraic and spectral behavior of projection, gauge transformation, blocking,
and cyclic-sector reindexing developed here.

Throughout, $\E_A(X)=\sum_iA^iX(A^i)^\dagger$, $A^{[p]}$ denotes blocking by $p$, and $V^{(N)}(A)_\sigma=\tr(A^{\sigma_1}\cdots A^{\sigma_N})$. Weighted direct sums are written $\bigoplus_k\mu_kA_k$.
