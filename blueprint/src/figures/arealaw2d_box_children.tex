% paper's M; the note writes N_b because M_j are filters), labelled above.

% padding leaves the parent (label below the parent); the paper bounds their

% the proof uses the cruder count (2C_pad+5)^2 - 1. The eight other children
% of the block are drawn as boxes carrying their ranks among the nine in one
% uniform random order of all children (3, 7, 1 / 5, X_i, 2 / 8, 4, 6 by

% precedes it, the event "X_i last in its padding". Under a uniform random
% order it has
% probability at least p_0 = (2C_pad+5)^{-2}; on it the preceding sites

% boxes above and below it, and tenkz cannot inscribe X_i. No tint either: at
