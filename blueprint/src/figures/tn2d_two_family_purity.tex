% Both panels draw the partition of Lemma 11.1 on the same sites, one column per
% piece: B = Lambda \ A (col 1), the cut (the six thick red strings between cols 1
% and 2), the exceptional set D (col 2) and the tiles X_1 .. X_4 (cols 3, 4, 5, 6).
% Each panel inks only the members of its set, in their own species, and leaves the
% other sites as bare dots: the left panel is B u A_0 = B (dashed grey) u X_1 u X_3
% (teal, family 0), the right panel is D u A_1 = D (brown) u X_2 u X_4 (olive,
% family 1). The order within a family is by index. Same-family tiles are separated,
% here by one tile of the other family; in Prop 11.2 by more than 2 n_i^beta. The
% inked sites of one panel are exactly the bare dots of the other, so the two sets
% are complementary in Lambda and the equality S_psi(B u A_0) = S_psi(D u A_1) is
% the purity of psi. Each piece is labelled once, at label pos=n, in the panel
% where it is inked; each union is named once, B u A_0 and D u A_1, under its panel
% on the blank wire row 7 (two inkless atoms anchor it), so the set names keep a
% station of their own, clear of the piece labels and of the tints.
% Check: the only relation is the = inside the middle joiner; it compares the two
% panels, both of empty signature, through the scalar text S_psi( ... ). The leading
% joiner S_psi\Biggl( is mathematics and not a relation, so there is nothing to opt
% out of: check= takes no waiver here (a waiver naming a relation the equation does
% not perform is refused by tenkz), and the pinned tenkz 08a6493 records no audit
% failure.
% Boundary signature: empty in both panels (geometry, no open index).
% Not to scale: the tiles of Prop 11.2 have scales n_i >= n_* and the separation
% exceeds 2 n_i^beta with beta = 1 - 2 10^{-6}; each piece is drawn one column wide.
