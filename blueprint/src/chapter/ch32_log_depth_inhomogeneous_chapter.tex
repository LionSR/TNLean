\chapter{Preparation of Inhomogeneous Matrix Product States}
\label{ch:log_depth_inhomogeneous}

The log-depth preparation of Chapter~\ref{ch:log_depth_preparation} extends to
matrix product states that are not translation invariant and to bond dimensions
that vary along the chain~\cite{Malz2024Preparation}. This chapter treats the
contraction of contiguous blocks, normalized preparation from ordered mixing,
explicit blocking scales, and varying references and bond dimensions.
