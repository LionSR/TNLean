\subsection{Open gaps from changes at the two boundary sites}

For $p\in\{0,1\}$, let $A_p$ consist of $D_p^2$ matrices in
$\MN{D_p}$, where $D_p\geq0$. These are the given endpoint tensors
in their square physical-support coordinates. The mixed physical space
at length $N$ is $(\C^{(D_0+D_1)^2})^{\otimes N}$.
Write $H'^{\mathrm{open}}_{\gamma,N}$ for the nonwrapping sum of the
extended two-site interaction of Definition~\ref{def:mpo_mixed_extended_support}.
A norm gap $\eta$ above the kernel of a positive operator $H$ means
$\eta\|v\|\leq\|Hv\|$ for every $v\perp\ker H$.

    Let $Q_N$ select the configurations for which every column register
    except the last and every row register except the first lies in
    the first sector. For $N\geq2$, set
    $H_N=H'^{\mathrm{open}}_{0,N}$. This endpoint Hamiltonian satisfies
    \begin{align}
        [Q_N,H_N] & =0,
                  & I-Q_N & \leq2H_N.
        \label{eq:mpo_mixed_open_sectors}
    \end{align}
    The active coordinates retain two unrestricted exterior registers,
    two first-sector inner boundary registers, and $N-2$ bulk sites.
    Let $J_N$ denote zero extension from these coordinates and
    $K_N=J_N^*H_NJ_N$. Then $J_N^*J_N=I$, $J_NJ_N^*=Q_N$, and
    $J_N(\ker K_N)=\ker H_N$. A gap $\eta\geq0$ of $K_N$ gives
    a gap $\min\{\eta,1/2\}$ of $H_N$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
    \uses{thm:mpo_mixed_endpoint_projector_comparison,
        thm:mpo_mixed_reducing_sectors,
        thm:periodic-fixed-interaction-order}
    Sum the inner row and column penalties over all nonwrapping bonds.
    Each violated register contributes one, while each interaction bounds
    the two penalties assigned to it. This gives
    (\ref{eq:mpo_mixed_open_sectors}). Every row and column selector
    reduces each overlapping interaction, so their active product also
    commutes with the sum. The orthogonal decomposition
    $v=J_Nx+y$, $Q_Ny=0$, gives
    \begin{align}
        \langle H_Nv,v\rangle
         & =\langle K_Nx,x\rangle+\langle H_Ny,y\rangle,
         & \|v\|^2                                       & =\|x\|^2+\|y\|^2.
    \end{align}
    Positivity converts the active norm gap into its quadratic-form
    lower bound. The inactive estimate excludes inactive ground vectors
    and gives the stated gap comparison.
\end{proof}

    the physical coordinates at only the first and last active sites
    yields an invertible map $F_N$. There are constants $B,C>0$,
    independent of $N$, such that
    $\|F_Nv\|\leq B\|v\|$ and $\|F_N^{-1}v\|\leq C\|v\|$.

    corresponding normalized local kernel. The normalized Hamiltonian
    acts on the interior coordinates independently of the two free
    exterior registers.

\begin{proof}
    \uses{thm:mpo_mixed_open_active_sectors}
    The $D_0^2$ endpoint letters form a basis of $M_{D_0}$, so their
    physical coefficient matrix is invertible. Its inverse changes
    each diagonal boundary letter into a matrix unit, leaving the mixed
    boundary letters and all interior $A_0$ letters unchanged. The
    first and last normalized local ranges are respectively
    $Y\mapsto A_0^qY$ and $Y\mapsto YA_0^q$.
    Their exterior columns and rows are independent spectators.
    The coordinate inclusions of these smaller edges have range
    projections equal to products of the reducing sector selectors.
    Thus cropping a boundary range equals pulling it back under the
    inclusion, and its complement projection is the actual compressed
    interaction. Extending either fixed boundary change by exterior
    identities leaves its operator norm bound unchanged; composing the
    two changes gives $B$ and $C$ independent of the number of bulk sites.
\end{proof}

    \uses{def:mpo_mixed_extended_support,def:injective}
    If both given endpoint tensors are one-site injective, there exist $W\geq3$ and
    $\delta>0$ such that, for $p\in\{0,1\}$, $N\geq W$, and
    $v\perp\ker H'^{\mathrm{open}}_{p,N}$,
    \begin{align}
        \delta\|v\|\leq\|H'^{\mathrm{open}}_{p,N}v\|.
    \end{align}
    The kernels are the actual extended open kernels with free
    exterior registers.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
    \uses{thm:mpo_mixed_open_boundary_normalization,
        thm:mpo_mixed_open_active_sectors,
        thm:mpo_mixed_second_endpoint_comparison,
        thm:injective_strict_knabe_window}
    For orthogonal projections $P_i$ and an invertible $F$, let $Q_i$
    project onto $(F^{-1}\ker P_i)^\perp$. Positive forward and inverse
    norm bounds $B,C$ give
    \begin{align}
        C^{-2}\sum_iQ_i\leq F^*\Bigl(\sum_iP_i\Bigr)F
        \leq B^2\sum_iQ_i.
    \end{align}
    Hence a gap $\eta$ of $\sum_iP_i$ gives a gap
    $\eta/(B^2C^2)$ of $\sum_iQ_i$.
    If $D_0=0$, the active space is zero. Otherwise first take the
    second-sector dimension to be zero. The actual
    interaction is then the ordinary injective $A_0$ parent interaction,
    whose open gap is uniform at all sufficiently large lengths.
    Normalize its two boundaries, replace its free exterior registers
    by the enlarged registers, and undo the boundary normalization.
    The middle step preserves the gap exactly, since the same interior
    operator acts independently on each exterior fiber. Both normalization
    losses are independent of volume. Apply the inactive-sector bound
    and then exchange the endpoint sectors. The smaller of the two
    positive constants and the larger starting length give the conclusion.
\end{proof}
