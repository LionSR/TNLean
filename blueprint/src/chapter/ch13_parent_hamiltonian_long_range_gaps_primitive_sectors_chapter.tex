\chapter{Infinite-volume gaps for sums of primitive tensors}
\label{ch:parent_hamiltonian_long_range_gaps_primitive_sectors}

This chapter proves the infinite-volume gap of a positive parent interaction
for a finite weighted sum of inequivalent primitive tensors, following
\cite[Theorems~1.1--1.2]{Nachtergaele1996Spectral}. Each primitive block
defines a pure translation-invariant state of the infinite chain, and in every
such state $\omega$ the local commutator energy of a centered local observable
$X$ is bounded below by one common constant times $\omega(X^*X)$
(Theorem~\ref{thm:eventual_kernel_pure_ground_state_gap}). When the
kernels of its long open-chain Hamiltonians are the spaces of matrix product
vectors, its zero-energy states are exactly the convex combinations of these
block states
(Theorem~\ref{thm:primitive_multiblock_quasi_local_ground_state_classification}),
so its pure ground states are the primitive sectors.
The next chapter extends these results to periodic tensors.
