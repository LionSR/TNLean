\chapter{Fans of triangles in the cells}%
\label{ch:peps_area_law_cell_fans}

Each belt cell is divided from its center into a fan of eight triangles,
and the colors of the triangles decide which neighboring region each of them
joins \cite[Section~11]{OpenAI2026AreaLaw}. This chapter constructs the fans,
matches the sides of neighboring cells with their opposing regions, merges
triangles of equal color into runs, and describes the connected pieces left
after radial cuts.
