\chapter{The distribution protocol and the information reset}
\label{ch:peps_frames_protocol}

This chapter states the distribution protocol
of~\cite[Proposition~7.1]{OpenAI2026PolynomialPEPS} as a distributed
construction whose chronology is cut into changes of the encoded frames of
Chapter~\ref{ch:peps_frames}, and derives it from the angular and patch bounds
by keeping the memory of several copies of the ground state. It ends with the
information reset that keeps the information bound of
Chapter~\ref{ch:peps_approximation} valid after each change.
