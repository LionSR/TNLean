\chapter{Distributing the sites over dyadic squares and reading off a PEPS}
\label{ch:peps_dyadic_protocol}

\cite[Sections~7 and~8]{OpenAI2026PolynomialPEPS} arrange the changes of
ownership into a protocol on dyadic squares in which every party takes part in
boundedly many operations, route the resulting network of parties into the
square lattice, and read off a PEPS. This chapter formalizes the protocol, the
routing, and the exact representations used for finitely many small sizes.
