\chapter{\texorpdfstring{$G$}{G}-injective and \texorpdfstring{$G$}{G}-isometric tensors}%
\label{ch:peps_quantum_double}

Let a finite group $G$ act on the virtual space of a tensor. If the invariant
subspace is proper, an invariant tensor is not injective on the full virtual
space. $G$-injectivity requires injectivity on the invariant subspace.
Such tensors describe the quantum double models of
\cite{Kitaev2003FaultTolerant}, whose simplest instance, for
$G=\mathbb{Z}_2$, is the toric code. This chapter introduces $G$-injective
and $G$-isometric tensors, treats matrix product tensors and their parent
Hamiltonians, shows that both properties survive the contraction of links,
and proves that the parent Hamiltonians of $G$-isometric tensors have
commuting terms. The following chapters place these tensors on the torus
PEPS of Section~\ref{sec:peps_torus_site_tensor}.
