\section{Choice of the patch energy parameter}
\label{sec:peps_patch_energy_choice}

The patch energy estimate has the form
$\langle\phi,H\phi\rangle-E_0\le C_E\kappa^2$, where
$C_E=36q^2J$ depends only on the fixed physical parameters.
The following scalar choice is made before the regions, radius and minimizing
densities are chosen. It does not establish the energy estimate itself.

\begin{lemma}[A uniformly small energy parameter]
    \label{lem:peps_patch_energy_parameter_choice}
    \lean{Real.exists_pos_le_half_mul_sq_le_div_1024}
    \leanok
    Let $C_E\ge0$ and $\Delta>0$. There exists $\kappa>0$ such that
    \begin{align*}
        \kappa\le\frac12,
        \qquad C_E\kappa^2\le\frac{\Delta}{1024}.
    \end{align*}
    The choice depends only on $C_E$ and $\Delta$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
    \leanok
    If $C_E=0$, take $\kappa=1/2$.
    If $C_E>0$, take
    \begin{align*}
        \kappa=\min\left\{\frac12,
        \sqrt{\frac{\Delta}{1024C_E}}\right\}.
    \end{align*}
    Both entries of the minimum are positive. The first gives
    $\kappa\le1/2$, and the second gives
    $C_E\kappa^2\le\Delta/1024$.
\end{proof}
