\part{Symmetries and phases}
\label{part:symmetries}

Symmetries of a state are reflected in symmetries of its tensor, possibly up to
a projective representation; this leads to the classification of
symmetry-protected phases in one dimension \cite[Section~III.A]{Cirac2021Matrix}.
This part treats on-site and virtual symmetries, string order, gauging of
matrix product unitaries, and symmetries given by matrix product operators and
their algebras.
