\section{Original-template information from the physical bootstrap}
\label{sec:physical-template-information}

The physical safe-box bootstrap discharges the sole fixed-exponent
safe-box premise of the previously constructed actual-template information
theorem.  This step adds no new scanner, entropy certificate or limiting
argument.  The physical source chain remains subject to validation and
integration, and the existing cut and compact-volume packaging is unchanged.

\begin{theorem}[Physical original-template information]
    \label{thm:physical-template-information}
    \lean{TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.exists_template_final_mutualInformation}
    Fix $q,R,D_0\in\mathbb N$ and $J,\Delta,C_{\rm tpl}\in\mathbb R$ with
    \[
        q\geq1,\qquad J\geq0,\qquad\Delta>0,\qquad
        D_0>2R+10,\qquad C_{\rm tpl}\geq24.
    \]
    There are $C>0$ and $N\geq2$, chosen before every finite instance,
    such that the following holds.  For all natural $n,s_0$ with $n\geq N$,
    every finite induced domain $\Lambda$, every local Hamiltonian with
    parameters $(q,R,J)$, every original gapped ground vector $\Omega$
    with gap $\Delta$, every cut $A$, and every template $T$ with parameters
    $(C_{\rm tpl},n,s_0)$, assume the source conditions
    \[
        \operatorname{dist}_\infty(T,\partial A)>4D_0s_0,
        \qquad L=\lfloor n^{1-1/100000}\rfloor\leq s_0.
    \]
    Here the cut set is the ambient set of endpoints of edges crossing $A$
    in the induced domain.  Set $X=A\cap T$ and $S_0=A\cap T_L$.
    Then
    \[
        2I_\Omega(X:\Lambda\setminus S_0)\leq Cn^{62497/62500},\qquad
        2I_\Omega(X:\Lambda\setminus S_0)\leq Cn^{1-1/100000}.
    \]
    The same $C,N$ apply to all the displayed finite data.  The theorem
    does not assert that they are independent of the fixed $D_0$ or
    $C_{\rm tpl}$.  No nonempty physical-core hypothesis is required.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
    The physical safe-box theorem supplies $C_{\rm box}>0$, depending only
    on $q,R,J,\Delta$, before every integer $D_0>2R+10$ and every
    integer $r\geq1$, with
    \[
        \operatorname{boxEntropy}(q,R,J,\Delta,D_0,r)
        \leq C_{\rm box}r^{1+e_*},\qquad e_*=2\cdot10^{-6}.
    \]
    Specialize this uniform bound to the fixed $D_0$.  The strict safety
    threshold implies $D_0\geq1$, and positivity gives
    $C_{\rm box}\geq0$.  These are exactly the hypotheses needed by the
    conditional actual-template information theorem.

    Apply that theorem with the unchanged physical parameters and fixed
    template constant.  It supplies $C,N$ before all finite data and
    returns both information powers on the original state and the literal
    template core and collar.  No state replacement, exchange of limits,
    extra analytic premise or change to the rounded scales occurs here.
\end{proof}
