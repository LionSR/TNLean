\chapter{Wielandt Bound}\label{ch:wielandt}

Quantum Wielandt theory, established by Sanz et al.~\cite{Sanz2010Quantum},
bounds the word length required for products of local MPS matrices to span the
full matrix algebra $\MN{D}$. For a primitive normalized tensor with bond
dimension $D$, the uniform vector-spreading index $q(\E_A)$ and the injectivity
length $\iota(A)$ satisfy $\iota(A)\le(D^2-\krausrk(A)+1)D^2$, with the cumulative
bound $T_{D^2}(A)=\MN{D}$ serving as an intermediate step.
