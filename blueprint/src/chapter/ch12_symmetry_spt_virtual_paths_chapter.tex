\chapter{Virtual actions along symmetric paths}
\label{ch:symmetry_spt_virtual_paths}

This chapter develops the virtual actions along continuous symmetric paths
that enter both directions of the classification of
Theorem~\ref{thm:spt_classification}. If two one-site injective tensors carry
virtual projective representations with cohomologous factor systems, they
satisfy the independent exact MPS phase condition
(Lemma~\ref{thm:spt_exact_phase_forward}). Conversely, along a continuous path
of one-site injective tensors of fixed bond dimension, the virtual symmetry
matrices can be chosen continuously and the endpoint factor systems are
cohomologous (Lemma~\ref{thm:spt_tensor_path_class_invariance}). The chapter
ends with local continuous minimal realizations of periodic rays, reconstructed
from their finite traces
(Lemma~\ref{thm:spt_continuous_canonical_ray_realization}); these are the
local input for the compact limits of the next chapter.
