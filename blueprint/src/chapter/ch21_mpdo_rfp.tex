\chapter{Mixed States}\label{ch:mpdo_rfp}

Starting from the MPO, MPDO, and LPDO notation of Chapter~\ref{ch:mpdo},
this chapter studies renormalization fixed points for density-operator chains.
The analysis passes through zero correlation length, the physical-trace
transfer $\mathcal T_M$, the doubled-index transfer map $\E_M$, and fixed-point
structures obtained from local purifications.  Its organization follows the
mixed-state analysis of \cite[Section~4]{Cirac2016MPDO_arXiv}.
