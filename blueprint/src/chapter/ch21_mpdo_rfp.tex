\chapter{Mixed States: Renormalization of Matrix Product Operators}\label{ch:mpdo_rfp}

Building on the matrix product density operators of Chapter~\ref{ch:mpdo},
renormalization fixed points and zero-correlation-length conditions characterize
mixed-state chains \cite[Section~4]{Cirac2016MPDO_arXiv}. Local channels govern
the fixed-point equation, the saturation of the area law for mutual
information, the structure of simple and commuting fixed points, and the
induced boundary theory for two-dimensional PEPS. The general algebraic
classification continues in Chapter~\ref{ch:mpdo_rfp_algebraic}.
