\chapter{Mixed States: Renormalization of Matrix Product Operators}\label{ch:mpdo_rfp}

Starting from the matrix product density operator formalism of
Chapter~\ref{ch:mpdo}, this chapter develops renormalization fixed points and
zero-correlation-length conditions for mixed-state chains
\cite[Section~4]{Cirac2016MPDO_arXiv}. We analyze local renormalization
conditions, area-law saturation for mutual information, the structure of
simple and commuting fixed points, and the induced boundary theory for
two-dimensional PEPS. The general algebraic classification continues in
Chapter~\ref{ch:mpdo_rfp_algebraic}.
