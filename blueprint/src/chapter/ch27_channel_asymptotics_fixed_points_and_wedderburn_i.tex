\section{Stationary support}

We record the matrix-product-tensor form of corner invariance used at the
interface with the stationary-support theory developed in the companion
quantum-channel volume~\cite[Chapter ``Channel asymptotics]{QICLean2026Blueprint}.

\begin{lemma}[Lower-triangular Kraus condition implies corner invariance]%
    \label{lem:lower_zero_invariance}
    \lean{MPSTensor.lowerZero_implies_invariance}
    \leanok
    Let $A$ be an MPS tensor and let $P$ be an orthogonal projection.
    If $(\Id-P)A^iP=0$ for every physical index~$i$, then
    $P\E_A(PXP)P=\E_A(PXP)$ for every $X\in\MN{D}$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}\leanok
    Expand the transfer map using (\ref{eq:mps_transfer_map}) and use
    $(\Id-P)A^iP=0$ to reduce each Kraus term to the $P$-corner.
\end{proof}
