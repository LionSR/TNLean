\section{Least viable dimensions for original low-energy targets}

Let $H=\sum_i h_i$ be a nearest-neighbour chain of supported positive
contractions, with local dimension $q\ge2$. Write
$\lambda_0=\min\sigma(H)$ and
$G(c)=\mathbf1_{(-\infty,\lambda_0+c]}(H)\mathcal H$.
For a prescribed interval $J=[p,r)\subseteq[0,L)$, use its fixed
configuration splitting to identify
$\mathcal H=\mathcal H_J\otimes\mathcal H_{J^c}$.

\begin{lemma}[A viable subspace at a fixed bandwidth]
    \label{thm:physical_low_density_minimum_dimension}
    \lean{TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.exists_fixed_bandwidth_interval_low_energy_viable_subspace}
    \leanok
    For every $\eta_{\rm LD}>0$ there is a constant
    $C\ge\max\{100,19+\eta_{\rm LD}\}$ with the following property.
    Choose an integer $m\ge q$ with $\log m\ge100$, and let $v>0$.
    Put $\bar v=\min\{v,\eta_{\rm LD}/2\}$ and define
    \begin{align}
      w_j&=\frac{\bar v}{6\cdot2^j},&
      g_j&=\min\{w_j,1\},&
      u_j&=1+\log m+\log(1/g_j),\nonumber\\
      \ell_j&=\lceil Cu_j^2/g_j\rceil,&
      D_j&=(m\ell_j^2)^{22\ell_j}.&&
      \label{eq:ld_minimum_parameters}
    \end{align}
    Every interval $J$ admits a $3/200$-viable subspace $W$ for
    $G(\eta_{\rm LD}-v)$ satisfying
    \begin{align}
      \dim W\le\dim G(\eta_{\rm LD})\,D_0^3.
      \label{eq:ld_minimum_dimension}
    \end{align}
    The constant $C$ precedes the chain length, local dimension,
    interactions, interval and deficit. No spectral gap is assumed.
    The numerical base $m$ changes only the count estimate, not the
    Hamiltonian or the physical local dimension; one may take
    $m=\max\{q,\lceil e^{100}\rceil\}$.
    These fixed-bandwidth quantifiers and the restriction $q\ge2$ are
    recorded in~\cite{gap:alvv16_low_energy_family_scope}.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
    \leanok
    \uses{thm:interval_low_energy_family}
    Set $\rho=\dim G(\eta_{\rm LD})$ and
    $T_j=G(\eta_{\rm LD}-6w_j)$. An actual eigenvector at
    $\lambda_0$ gives $\rho\ge1$. The targets increase, and
    $\dim T_j\le\rho$.

    At width $w_j$, apply Lemma~\ref{thm:interval_low_energy_family}
    with upper cutoff $\eta_j=\eta_{\rm LD}-3w_j$.
    Since $6w_j\le\eta_{\rm LD}/2$, its buffer
    $6w_j\le\eta_j\le\eta_{\rm LD}$ holds. Its original upper and
    lower targets are exactly $T_{j+1}$ and $T_j$, respectively.
    It supplies $D_j^2$ actual regional operators and the suppression
    estimate $D_j^{12}\Delta_j\le10^{-5}$ for this same count.
    The upper closeness comparison also bounds the upper perturbed
    target dimension by $\rho$.

    Let $p_j$ be the least dimension of a $3/200$-viable subspace
    for $T_j$, and choose an actual minimizing space $W_j$.
    The whole regional space shows that these natural minima exist
    and satisfy $p_j\le\dim\mathcal H_J$; target restriction gives
    $p_j\le p_{j+1}$. The actual ceiling counts in
    (\ref{eq:ld_minimum_parameters}) satisfy
    $\log D_{j+1}\le(13/6)\log D_j$ and $\log D_j\ge100$.
    Moreover, at the finite index
    $j=\lceil\dim(\mathcal H_J)\bar v/(6C)\rceil$,
    one has $D_j\ge\dim\mathcal H_J$.

    Choose the first index with $p_j\le\rho D_j^3$.
    If it is positive, write it as $k+1$. Then
    $\rho D_k^3<p_k\le p_{k+1}\le\rho D_{k+1}^3$.
    The calibrated sample in the actual space $W_{k+1}$ has dimension
    $s=\lfloor p_k/(2D_k^2)\rfloor$. Its improving image is
    $3/200$-viable for $T_k$ and has dimension at most
    $p_k/2<p_k$, a contradiction. The strict-decrease constants are
    explained in~\cite{gap:alvv16_low_density_sampling}.
    Hence $\dim W_0=p_0\le\rho D_0^3$.
    Finally $\bar v\le v$ gives
    $G(\eta_{\rm LD}-v)\subseteq T_0$, so the same $W_0$ is
    viable for the requested target.
\end{proof}

This gives the exact natural count bound underlying the low-density
argument of~\cite[Proposition~prop:albootstrap-ld,
lines 1112--1128]{Arad2016RigorousRG}. Simplifying that bound to the
printed logarithmic form, estimating entropy and constructing low-energy
matrix product approximations require further arguments.
