\section{Commuting Local Terms for Parent Hamiltonians}\label{sec:commuting_parent_ham}

In \cite[Definition~3.9 and Theorem~3.10]{Cirac2016MPDO_arXiv}, the commutation
equation $h_i(A,2)h_j(A,2)=h_j(A,2)h_i(A,2)$ for local terms on a periodic
chain relates renormalization fixed points to tensors with commuting
nearest-neighbor parent terms. Conversely, for normal left-canonical BNT
representatives, commuting local terms imply the renormalization fixed-point
structure under the all-chain ground-space condition (zero energy and kernel
spanned by the periodic MPS vectors for every $N>2$).
