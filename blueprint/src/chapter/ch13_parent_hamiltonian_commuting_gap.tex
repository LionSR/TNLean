\section{Commuting Local Terms for Parent Hamiltonians}\label{sec:commuting_parent_ham}

This section concerns the commutation equation for local terms in a
periodic chain, relating renormalization fixed points to
tensors with commuting nearest-neighbor terms,
$h_i(A,2)h_j(A,2)=h_j(A,2)h_i(A,2)$~\cite[Definition~3.9 and Theorem~3.10]{Cirac2016MPDO_arXiv}.
The reverse implication from commuting local terms to a renormalization
fixed point is proved for normal left-canonical BNT representatives assuming
the all-chain ground-space condition (zero energy and kernel spanned by the
periodic MPS vectors for every $N>2$).
