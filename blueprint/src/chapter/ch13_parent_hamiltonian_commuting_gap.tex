\section{Commuting Local Terms for Parent Hamiltonians}\label{sec:commuting_parent_ham}

For normal matrix product states, the commutation equation for local terms in
a periodic chain, $h_i(A,2)h_j(A,2)=h_j(A,2)h_i(A,2)$, characterizes
renormalization fixed points and tensors with commuting nearest-neighbor
terms~\cite[Definition~3.9 and Theorem~3.10]{Cirac2016MPDO_arXiv}. In a
left-canonical gauge, commuting local terms imply that the tensor is a
renormalization fixed point.
