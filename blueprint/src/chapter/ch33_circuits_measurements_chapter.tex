\chapter{Measurements, teleportation and long-range gates}
\label{ch:circuits_measurements}

Local circuits may be combined with measurements in the computational basis,
followed by corrections on single sites chosen from all the outcomes
\cite{Piroli2021LOCC}. This chapter shows that such circuits prepare GHZ-type
states in constant depth, teleport sites along chains of neighbouring pairs,
and apply gates on pairs of distant sites in constant depth.
