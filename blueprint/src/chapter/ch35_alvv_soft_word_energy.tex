\section{Raw compressed energy of the specified word family}

The following conditional lemma uses a specified target and its own
family with stated bounds. It constructs the soft regional operator and its actual
compression; no candidate quadratic-energy bound or compressed minimum is assumed.
It supports the separate energy-estimation image in
\cite[Figure \texttt{fig:algo1}, line 1631]{Arad2016RigorousRG}.

\begin{lemma}[A regional energy estimate from the same word family]
    \label{lem:soft_same_word_compressed_energy}
    \lean{TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.ALVV.exists_native_soft_word_compressed_energy_estimate}
    \leanok
    Let $H=\sum_i h_i$ be a chain with positive local dimension,
    $0\le h_i\le I$ and the designated nearest-neighbour supports.
    Write $G$ for its full ground space. Fix $l\le u\le v\le r\le L$
    with $u-l,r-v\le2\ell$, and integers
    $\ell\ge21$, $D\ge2$, $s\ge1$, $50\ell^2\le D$. Put
    \begin{align}
       a&=\frac{3}{32D^{12}s^5},&
       \tau&=\frac{a^2}{2^{20}},&
       \Delta&=\frac{1}{200000D^{12}}.
    \end{align}
    Let $T$ be a specified full-chain space of dimension $\dim G$,
    with $G$ $\tau$-close to $T$. Let $F_1,\ldots,F_N$ be a specified
    family on the native Hilbert space of $[l,r)$. Assume that it satisfies the following
    bounds: for every $1/4$-viable $S$ for $T$, the actual one-word
    image $\sum_iF_iS$ has dimension at most $D^2\dim S$ and is
    $\Delta/(3/4)^2$-viable for that same $T$. All viability statements
    use the canonical regional tensor coordinates and retain the full
    environment.

    Let $P,M$ be the original internal sums on $[l,r)$ and $[u,v)$.
    Their actual attained minima $e_P,e_M$ are chosen before $p$ and the
    child spaces. For $e_M-10\le p\le e_M$, define
    \begin{align}
       R&=P-M+pI+h_{12,768}(M-pI),&
       \rho&=32e^{-42}+\frac1{64}<\frac1{16}.
       \label{eq:soft_word_raw_operator}
    \end{align}
    Define $K_{\rm raw}$ by restricting $R$ to one fixed complementary
    configuration. It is Hermitian and $R=K_{\rm raw}\otimes I$.
    Its matrix is defined before the attained minimum $e_{\rm s}$ of
    $R$, which satisfies $e_{\rm s}\le e_P\le e_{\rm s}+\rho$.

    Fix any split $l\le k\le r$ and child spaces $S_1,S_2$ that are
    $3/200$-viable for the original $G$ on their respective intervals.
    Let $W$ be their actual tensor merge in $[l,r)$ coordinates and
    let $V=\sum_iF_iW$. Then $\dim V\le D^2\dim W$ and $V\ne\{0\}$.
    The actual orthogonal compression of $K_{\rm raw}$ to $V$ has a
    constructed orthonormal eigenbasis, with eigenvalues in decreasing
    order. Its last eigenvalue $\lambda_V$ satisfies
    \begin{align}
       e_{\rm s}\le\lambda_V\le e_{\rm s}+3,\\
       e_P-3-\rho\le\lambda_V-3\le e_P.
       \label{eq:soft_word_raw_energy}
    \end{align}
    The definition of the estimate $p_V=\lambda_V-3$ does not use the
    unknown scalar $e_{\rm s}$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}\leanok
    \uses{lem:native_soft_parent_energy,thm:actual_adjacent_interval_merge}
    The actual central replacement gives $C=R-e_{\rm s}I\ge0$ with
    norm at most $A=768(1+\log12)+4\ell+14$, and energy at most
    $2+\rho$ on $G$. The scalar estimates give $A+1\le D$,
    $\tau\le1/[1024(D+1)]$, and
    $\Delta/(3/4)^2\le1/[100(D+1)]$.

    The child merge is $3/100$-viable for $G$. Transport through the
    $\tau$-comparison makes it $1/4$-viable for the same $T$.
    Apply the word bound for that same family to this actual $W$ to obtain
    $V$. Pull $V\otimes\mathcal H_{[l,r)^c}$ back to a physical
    subspace $U$. The positive-energy transfer gives energy at most
    $9/4$ on $T$, and its actual projection onto $U$ gives energy
    at most three. Since $\dim T=\dim G>0$ and the projection loss
    is less than one, some such projected vector is nonzero.

    The exact regional tensor identity carries $R$ to
    $K_{\rm raw}\otimes I$. Adding the scalar $e_{\rm s}$ to its
    centered energy gives a nonzero tensor trial vector of raw energy
    at most $e_{\rm s}+3$ times its squared norm. An environmental
    slice gives a local unit trial vector in $V$, so the actual least
    compressed energy is at most $e_{\rm s}+3$. The positive shift
    $K_{\rm raw}-e_{\rm s}I\ge0$ gives its lower bound. Choose the
    actual ordered eigenbasis of that compression and apply the
    minimum comparison to obtain (\ref{eq:soft_word_raw_energy}).
\end{proof}

The properties of the target and family above hold for the same objects
in the calibrated hard-truncation construction. The following results apply the conditional lemma to those actual tree
families and construct one simultaneous recursive assignment of estimates. The downward bias by three is a
correction for the one-sided center interface; the source's printed
energy update uses the raw compressed minimum. No literal soft-Generate,
MPO implementation of this energy routine, or running-time conclusion
is asserted; see \cite{gap:alvv16_soft_regional_energy_centering}.


\begin{definition}[Certified one-sided regional updates]
    \label{def:soft_word_energy_update}
    \lean{TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.ALVV.IsRegionalLowerEnergyEstimate}
    \lean{TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.ALVV.IsSoftWordEnergyUpdate}
    \lean{TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.ALVV.HasSoftWordNodeEnergyUpdate}
    \leanok
    A lower energy estimate of width $c$ on an original regional operator
    $A$ is a scalar $p$ for which there exist a scalar $e$ and a unit
    vector $x$ such that $Ax=ex$, $A-eI\ge0$ and $e-c\le p\le e$.
    For fixed child spaces and a fixed family, a soft word energy update
    at center $p_M$ is the scalar $\lambda-3$, where $\lambda$ is the
    last eigenvalue in an actual complete orthonormal eigenbasis, ordered
    by decreasing eigenvalues, of the raw native soft operator compressed
    to the literal one-word child image.

    The node update property means that attained collar minima $c_L,c_R$
    can be selected before the child estimates, so that every pair of
    lower child estimates of width $49/16$ haves such an update at center
    $p_1+p_2-c_L-c_R-2$, again of width $49/16$ for the parent.
\end{definition}

\begin{lemma}[A one-sided node energy update]
    \label{lem:soft_word_node_energy_update}
    \lean{TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.ALVV.exists_soft_word_node_energy_update}
    \leanok
    Retain the physical hypotheses, parameters, specified target and its
    own family from Lemma~\ref{lem:soft_same_word_compressed_energy}.
    Fix a split $l\le k\le r$ and child spaces that are $3/200$-viable
    for the original ground space. The actual collar minima $c_L,c_R$
    can be chosen before the child estimates. If
    \[
       e_{[l,k)}-\frac{49}{16}\le p_1\le e_{[l,k)},\qquad
       e_{[k,r)}-\frac{49}{16}\le p_2\le e_{[k,r)},
    \]
    where each regional minimum is attained, put
    \[
       p_M=p_1+p_2-c_L-c_R-2.
    \]
    Then $e_M-10\le p_M\le e_M$. Form the same raw soft operator
    and its actual compression on the same one-word child image as
    in~(\ref{eq:soft_word_raw_operator}). Its least compressed eigenvalue
    $\lambda$ determines
    \[
       p=\lambda-3,\qquad e_P-\frac{49}{16}\le p\le e_P.
    \]
    An actual complete orthonormal eigenbasis of the compression, with
    eigenvalues in decreasing order, is constructed as part of this assertion.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}\leanok
    \uses{lem:soft_same_word_compressed_energy,lem:regional_central_energy_estimate,thm:actual_adjacent_interval_merge}
    The single connecting bond gives
    $e_{[l,k)}+e_{[k,r)}\le e_P\le
    e_{[l,k)}+e_{[k,r)}+1$. Consequently
    \[
       e_P-\frac{57}{8}\le p_1+p_2\le e_P.
    \]
    Since $57/8\le8$, subtracting the two actual collar minima and two
    gives the stated middle center. Apply the same-family compression
    estimate with that center. Its bound $e_P-3-\rho\le\lambda-3\le e_P$
    and $\rho<1/16$ imply the asserted width $49/16$.
    Attained minima chosen in the two arguments agree by their positive
    spectral shifts and normalized eigenvectors; no common minimizing
    vector for the different decompositions is needed.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Energy updates for the actual interleaved tree]
    \label{thm:physical_interleaved_tree_energy_updates}
    \lean{TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.exists_physical_dg_interleaved_tree_with_energy_updates}
    \leanok
    The calibrated interleaved interval-tree construction may retain
    exactly its same targets, operator families, retained spaces, dimension
    bounds and generator rank bounds, while also satisfying the following
    assertion. For every long parent $[l,r)$, take collars $[l,l+\ell)$
    and $[r-\ell,r)$. For every split and the actual two child spaces
    of that tree, all one-sided child estimates of width $49/16$ have
    the same-family node update of Lemma~\ref{lem:soft_word_node_energy_update}.
    The constants remain uniform in the chain length, local dimension,
    ground-space dimension and trimming accuracy. The explicit parameter
    formulas and their dependence on these data are unchanged.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}\leanok
    \uses{lem:physical_interleaved_same_word_family,lem:soft_word_node_energy_update}
    Choose the interleaved tree once, including its targets and word
    families. Its actual child spaces are $3/200$-viable for the full
    original ground space. The spectral-gap assumption identifies the
    specified lowest spectral value with the canonical minimum. The
    actual count satisfies $D\ge50\ell^2$, and the retained word estimate
    at power one is exactly the hypothesis of the node lemma. Since a
    long parent has length greater than $4\ell$, the displayed collars
    are disjoint and satisfy the required width bounds. Apply the node
    lemma to these same targets, families and child spaces.
\end{proof}

The node update now supplies a simultaneous recursive scalar assignment
on that same tree, as proved below. The distinction between the calibrated
hard-clipped family and literal soft Generate, and the absence of a
running-time conclusion, remain.


\begin{definition}[A realized recursive energy assignment]
    \label{def:balanced_regional_energy_assignment}
    \lean{TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.ALVV.IsSoftWordNodeEnergyRealization}
    \lean{TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.ALVV.IsBalancedRegionalEnergyAssignment}
    \leanok
    Fix the physical interactions, the operator families on long intervals,
    and the retained-space tree. A realized node update consists of attained
    collar minima $c_L,c_R$, a valid middle center
    \begin{align}
       p_M=p_1+p_2-c_L-c_R-2\in[e_M-10,e_M],
    \end{align}
    and the actual raw-compression update $p=\lambda_V-3$ using the
    specified word family and actual child spaces.

    A balanced regional energy assignment is one function $(l,r)\mapsto p_{l,r}$
    with the following properties. For every valid interval,
    \begin{align}
       e_{[l,r)}-\frac{49}{16}\le p_{l,r}\le e_{[l,r)}.
    \end{align}
    If $r-l\le4\ell$, then $p_{l,r}=e_{[l,r)}$ and this minimum is
    attained by a normalized eigenvector with positive spectral shift.
    Otherwise, put $k=l+\lfloor(r-l)/2\rfloor$. The value $p_{l,r}$
    realizes the node update with child values $p_{l,k},p_{k,r}$ and
    with the actual retained spaces on those two children.
\end{definition}

\begin{lemma}[Recursive estimates on a fixed physical tree]
    \label{lem:balanced_regional_energy_assignment}
    \lean{TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.ALVV.exists_balanced_regional_energy_assignment}
    \leanok
    Let $0\le h_i\le I$ have the designated nearest-neighbour supports,
    with positive local dimension. Fix a balanced retained-space tree of
    positive leaf width $4\ell$ and fixed operator families at its long
    intervals. Suppose that the same-family node update property of
    Definition~\ref{def:soft_word_energy_update} holds at each balanced split.
    Then one balanced regional energy assignment exists on this fixed tree.
    No regional minimum or scalar estimate is supplied as an input.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}\leanok
    \uses{def:soft_word_energy_update,def:balanced_regional_energy_assignment,
      thm:regional_energy_node_relation_assignment,thm:actual_adjacent_interval_merge}
    Recurse on the interval length. At a short leaf, choose an actual
    attained minimum of its original regional Hamiltonian. At a long node,
    first compute the two shorter child estimates. Their certified errors
    permit the fixed node update, which constructs a parent value of the
    same width $49/16$ and its literal compression certificate.

    The recursion returns each value together with its error certificate.
    Its defining equation proves that every long-node value uses precisely
    the two values already assigned to its children. Thus the same function
    works simultaneously on all valid intervals, including endpoints and
    intervals of length zero.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Recursive energy estimates for the actual interleaved tree]
    \label{lem:physical_interleaved_tree_recursive_energy}
    \lean{TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.exists_physical_dg_interleaved_tree_with_recursive_energy}
    \leanok
    The calibrated physical interleaved tree can retain its same targets,
    operator families, retained spaces and literal merge identities while
    carrying one balanced regional energy assignment. All the original
    parameter formulas, dimension bounds, generator rank bounds and
    ordered-word conclusions remain valid for those same objects.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}\leanok
    \uses{thm:physical_interleaved_tree_energy_updates,lem:balanced_regional_energy_assignment}
    Construct the physical tree once. Its actual node updates hold for
    every split and hence for each balanced split. Apply the preceding
    recursion to these same families and retained spaces.
\end{proof}

This construction proves the corrected simultaneous one-sided energy
invariant on the existing hard-clipped interval tree. It does not identify
its family with literal soft Generate, establish the source's printed
energy-power count, or prove a running-time bound.


\begin{lemma}[Scalar estimates restricted to one actual root]
    \label{lem:balanced_root_regional_energy_assignment}
    \lean{TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.ALVV.exists_balanced_root_regional_energy_assignment}
    \leanok
    Fix a valid root, the operator families and the retained-space
    operation as in Lemma~\ref{lem:balanced_regional_energy_assignment}.
    It suffices that the certified node update hold at each internal
    interval in that root's actual merge list. There is then one
    scalar function with one-sided error at most $49/16$ at the root
    and those listed internal nodes, attained minima at their actual
    short leaves, and the literal same-family raw update at each
    listed internal node.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}\leanok
    \uses{def:soft_word_energy_update,def:balanced_interval_merge_list,
        thm:actual_adjacent_interval_merge}
    Use the same well-founded scalar recursion. Its node relation
    requires the actual raw update when the interval belongs to the
    root list and an original attained minimum otherwise. Thus the
    extension outside the chosen root depends on the fixed interval
    geometry, with no test against an unknown ground space. The shared
    recursive construction proves the lower-energy bound and its
    defining node relation. Restrict the conclusions to the root and
    its listed nodes. The original minima at short leaves are unchanged.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Energy estimates on the actual jointly viable sampled root]
    \label{lem:canonical_interval_root_energy_assignment}
    \lean{TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.ALVV.exists_canonical_interval_root_energy_assignment}
    \leanok
    Retain the physical interactions and count bounds of
    Lemma~\ref{lem:soft_same_word_compressed_energy}, and its fixed
    calibrated targets and one-word families on each long interval.
    The target and family bounds are fixed before the threshold and
    all sampling choices. For a choice $\omega$, let $S_\omega$ be
    the existing balanced tree of
    Definition~\ref{def:canonical_interval_sampling_operation}.
    Suppose its actual root and all listed internal intervals are
    $3/200$-viable for the same full original ground space.

    There is one scalar function $E$ with
    \begin{align}
       e_J-\frac{49}{16}\le E(J)\le e_J
    \end{align}
    at the root and each of its listed internal intervals. At an
    actual short root or a short child of a listed interval, $E$
    is the attained original regional minimum. At every listed long
    node, take the actual balanced children and their already-assigned
    scalar values. The parent value is the actual raw compressed
    minimum on the \emph{same} family's one-word child image, minus
    three. Its middle center is obtained from those child values and
    the two attained collar minima, and lies in $[e_M-10,e_M]$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}\leanok
    \uses{lem:balanced_interval_merge_closure,lem:soft_word_node_energy_update,
        lem:balanced_root_regional_energy_assignment,
        def:canonical_interval_sampling_operation}
    A short child is the full local space and hence viable. A long
    child is in its parent's merge list and therefore in the original
    root list by closure. Its actual viability is among the stated
    joint-success conditions. Thus both children of every used long
    node satisfy exactly the viability hypotheses of the proved
    physical energy update. Apply that result to its fixed target and
    own family. The preceding scalar recursion now applies to these
    used node updates, without selecting a second family or retained
    space.
\end{proof}

The last conclusion is restricted to the actual root subtree. It does
not infer viability or raw energy updates at all overlapping intervals
from a joint-success event on one root. Values on other valid intervals are
only a mathematical minimum extension. Literal soft Generate, its
printed energy-power count and the source running time remain separate;
see \cite{gap:alvv16_soft_regional_energy_centering}.
