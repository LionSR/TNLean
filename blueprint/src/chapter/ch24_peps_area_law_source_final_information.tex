\section{Conditional final-scale physical information}
\label{sec:source-final-information}

The same-source physical information inequality and the final numerical
absorption now compose.  The result is conditional on the complete physical
scanner hypotheses and the original target and positive-prefix entropy
inputs.  It does not construct those inputs or claim the full area law.
The source is \emph{A two-dimensional area law from a global spectral gap},
\texttt{08-scanner.tex}, lines 353--357, 399--533 and 839--941, at revision
\texttt{adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a}.

\begin{theorem}[The target dimension]
    \label{thm:physical-information-dimension}
    \lean{TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.log_exp_configuration_card_le_of_card}
    \leanok
    For a finite physical region $X$ with local dimension $q\geq1$, if
    $|X|\leq C_{\rm vol}n^2$, then
    \[
        \log(e\dim\mathcal H_X)
        \leq 1+(C_{\rm vol}\log q)n^2.
    \]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
    \leanok
    The configuration dimension is $q^{|X|}$.  Take its logarithm,
    use $\log q\geq0$ and substitute the cardinality estimate.  No dimension
    of the far region enters the estimate.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The concrete source's final-scale conditional information]
    \label{thm:source-final-information}
    \lean{TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.CollarScan.exists_sourceTarget_finalScale_mutualInformation}
    \leanok
    Fix $q\geq1$, an interaction radius $R$, a norm bound $J\geq0$,
    a spectral gap $\Delta>0$, a nonnegative quadratic target-volume
    coefficient $C_T$, a positive scanner coefficient $C_1$, an
    anchor-multiplicity bound $m_{\rm anc}$ with $3m_{\rm anc}\leq C_1$,
    and a tail power $P\geq\nu$.
    There is a positive radius coefficient $C_{\rm tr}$ such that, for every
    fixed nonnegative source entropy coefficient $C_e$, there are constants
    $C>0$ and $N\geq2$, chosen before the finite physical system, with the
    following property.

    Let the original vector $\Omega$ be a gapped ground state and let the
    given physical scanner have a nonempty ambient target and obey
    all the source graph, support-anchor,
    template-volume, ambient-depth, layer-cardinality, anchor-multiplicity
    and boundary-clearance hypotheses.  Retain the scales
    \[
        r_0=\lceil C_{\rm tr}(\log n)^2\rceil,\quad
        m=\lfloor n^\mu\rfloor,\quad D=\lceil n^\kappa\rceil,\quad
        L=\lfloor n^{1-\ell}\rfloor,\quad K=L/(8m),
    \]
    where the final division is natural-number division, together with
    $n\geq N$, $2r_0\leq D$, $1\leq D$ and $4D\leq m$.
    Put $X=A\cap T$ and let $S_0$ be the truncation set at depth $L$.
    Assume the genuine original-state source entropy inputs
    \[
        S_\Omega(X)\leq C_e n^{1+e_{\rm box}},\qquad
        S_\Omega(\text{positive prefix }j)
        \leq C_e(nL^{e_{\rm box}}+n)\quad(0\leq j\leq L).
    \]
    Then
    \[
        2I_\Omega(X:S_0^c)\leq Cn^{62497/62500},\qquad
        2I_\Omega(X:S_0^c)\leq Cn^{1-1/100000}.
    \]
    In particular, the exact maximum exponent is $0.999952$ and the
    displayed paper exponent is $0.99999$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
    \leanok
    \uses{thm:source-actual-information,
        thm:physical-information-dimension,
        thm:arealaw-target-entropy-transfer,
        thm:arealaw-final-sharp-source,
        def:arealaw-final-sharp-error}
    Invoke the same-source information theorem once.  Its concrete
    data give $X\subseteq S_0$, $|S_0|\leq(C_T+1)n^2$ and the
    trace-distance bound.  Therefore the preceding dimension lemma applies
    with $C_{\rm vol}=C_T+1$.  Quantitative regional entropy continuity gives
    \[
        S_{\Omega_t}(X)\leq C_e n^{1+e_{\rm box}}+n^{-247}
    \]
    for the same truncated vector.  The same original prefix input gives
    the already-derived shell bound with its unchanged $n^{-247}$ error.

    Use $W=n^\omega$ and $\tau=n^{-1/4000}$.  The uniform numerical scale
    theorem gives $0<\tau\leq1/2$ and the rounded per-band weight;
    the round-step smallness theorem puts that same weight in the required
    interval.  Identify the scalar right-hand side with the complete final
    error expression, including the selected mass, window, marginal bound
    and original-state continuity correction.  Apply the final source error
    bound at these data.

    The same-source theorem has already taken the replica limit at fixed
    physical size, weight and smoothing parameter.  This proof only applies
    the subsequent uniform size estimate; the two limits are not exchanged.
    The state, spectral window, label sequence and selected points are never
    reselected.
\end{proof}

The scanner, geometric antecedents and source target/prefix entropy
hypotheses have not been constructed here.  Their safe-box application,
the remaining geometric information telescope and the full area law stay
outside the scope of this conditional final-scale bound.
