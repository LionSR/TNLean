\chapter{Circuits for Matrix Product Unitaries}
\label{ch:mpu_circuits}

Every matrix product unitary is a circuit of bounded depth. This chapter bounds
the depth of the merging construction of~\cite{Styliaris2025MPUCircuits} and
constructs exact circuits for unitaries whose consecutive operator Schmidt
ranks are bounded~\cite{RankTwoMPUCircuits2026}.
