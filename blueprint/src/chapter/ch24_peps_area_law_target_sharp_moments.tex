\section{One target window for the two sharp moment conditions}
\label{sec:arealaw-target-sharp-moments}

The upper and lower comparisons require two fixed multiples of the same
per-band weight to lie in the same physical marginal-moment interval.
The larger multiple suffices for both.  This assertion uses a positive
exponent margin beyond the ordinary scanner inequalities; the threshold
may depend on that margin, while the geometric constants do not.

\begin{theorem}[Common moments at the target scales]
    \label{thm:arealaw-target-sharp-moments}
    \lean{TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.CollarScan.exists_truncated_target_sharp_moments}
    Fix the local dimension, interaction parameters and a coefficient
    $C_T\geq0$ for the target-size bound.  There are positive constants
    $C_{\rm tr},C_{\rm target},C_B$ with the following property.
    For fixed scanner exponents and a real weight exponent $\omega$ satisfying
    \begin{align}
        \omega+\frac{1+\kappa}{2}&<1-\ell-\mu,
        \label{eq:arealaw-target-sharp-moment-margin}
    \end{align}
    there is an integer threshold $N\geq2$ before every physical instance.
    Let $\Omega$ be an original unit ground state, of energy $E_0$ and
    gap $\Delta>0$, for the finite-domain local Hamiltonian.  The scanner
    graph is the induced domain graph, its depth is the target's ambient
    depth, and each anchor belongs to its original admissible support.
    Suppose $n\geq N$, the target has size at most $C_Tn^2$, its ambient
    layers have size at most $n$, and the target clearance is greater than
    $2L+10r_0$.  Retain the literal scales
    \begin{align}
        L&=\lfloor n^{1-\ell}\rfloor,&
        m&=\lfloor n^\mu\rfloor,&
        D&=\lceil n^\kappa\rceil,\nonumber\\
        K&=\left\lfloor\frac{L}{8m}\right\rfloor,&
        r_0&=\lceil C_{\rm tr}(\log n)^2\rceil,&
        B&=C_BnD(\log n)^{12}.
        \label{eq:arealaw-target-sharp-moment-scales}
    \end{align}
    Then $K\geq1$, $m>0$, $8Km\leq L$ and $B\geq1$.

    Put $g=\Delta/\nu$, where $\nu$ is the positive physical normalization,
    and $\varepsilon=\min\{n^{-1000},g/4\}$.  One unit truncated ground
    state $\widetilde\Omega$ has energy $e$ and gap $g/2$, with
    \begin{align}
        0&\leq e\leq\varepsilon,&
        \|\widetilde\Omega-e^{i\theta}\Omega\|
          &\leq2\sqrt{\varepsilon/g}\quad\text{for some }\theta\in\mathbb R,
        \nonumber\\
        \operatorname{dist}_{\rm tr}
          (|\widetilde\Omega\rangle\langle\widetilde\Omega|,
           |\Omega\rangle\langle\Omega|)
          &\leq\sqrt{2\varepsilon/g}.
        \label{eq:arealaw-target-sharp-truncation}
    \end{align}
    This same state has a target
    typical set of width $w=n^{3/5}$ and mass $z$ satisfying
    \begin{align}
        0&<1-n^{-100}\leq z\leq1.
        \label{eq:arealaw-target-sharp-moment-mass}
    \end{align}
    The target budget is at most $C_{\rm target}n(\log n)^{12}$, and every
    eigenvalue in this set lies between
    $\exp(-S(\widetilde\Omega_X)-w)$ and
    $\exp(-S(\widetilde\Omega_X)+w)$.
    Put
    \begin{align}
        C_0&=512\exp(1)\frac2g,&
        c_0&=\frac1{32\sqrt{1+2/g}},&
        r_{\rm mom}&=\frac{c_0}{\sqrt B},&
        K_{\rm mom}&=C_0B.
        \label{eq:arealaw-target-sharp-common-moments}
    \end{align}
    For every completed or pre-charge history within the physical horizon
    and every one of its five marginal regions $Q$, the same state satisfies
    \begin{align}
        \log\operatorname{tr}(\widetilde\rho_Q^{\,1-u})
        &\leq uS(\widetilde\rho_Q)+K_{\rm mom}u^2
        &&\text{for }|u|\leq r_{\rm mom}.
        \label{eq:arealaw-target-sharp-common-moment-bound}
    \end{align}
    Finally, for every $0<W\leq n^\omega$, with $a=W/K$,
    \begin{align}
        0&<a,&10a&\leq r_{\rm mom},&10a&\leq1,
        &6a&\leq r_{\rm mom},&6a&\leq1.
        \label{eq:arealaw-target-sharp-smallness}
    \end{align}
    The physical state and target set are chosen before $W$ and before
    any auxiliary label sequence.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
    Choose the truncation and target-budget constants from the quantitative
    target window, then the common status-budget constant.  The rounded
    scale separation and the positive margin in
    (\ref{eq:arealaw-target-sharp-moment-margin}) give a common threshold
    with
    \begin{align}
        10a\sqrt B&\leq\min\{c_0,1\}.
        \label{eq:arealaw-target-sharp-product-smallness}
    \end{align}
    Since $B\geq1$, division by $\sqrt B$ gives both factor-ten bounds;
    positivity of $a$ gives the factor-six bounds.
    Apply the target-window construction once.  Each of the five physical
    status cuts has geometric budget at most $B$, so the marginal-moment
    estimate for this same truncated state applies on
    $|u|\leq c_0/\sqrt B$.  The target mass, eigenvalue window and
    quantitative truncation estimates are unchanged.
\end{proof}

The conclusion supplies common physical data for a single supplied label
sequence in both sharp comparisons.  It does not choose that sequence,
select a charge point, estimate its actual defect, or perform the entropy
telescope and final absorption.  The stronger margin is recorded explicitly;
at the source's final parameter choice its positive value is
$499879/2000000$.
