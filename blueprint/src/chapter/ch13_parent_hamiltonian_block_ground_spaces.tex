\chapter{Block-injective parent Hamiltonians and degenerate ground spaces}
\label{ch:parent_hamiltonian_block_ground_spaces}

For a block-injective tensor $A^i=\bigoplus_j \mu_j A_j^i$, the local ground
spaces decompose according to the normal blocks. Following
\cite[Theorem~IV.16]{Cirac2021Matrix} and
\cite[Theorem~12]{PerezGarcia2007Matrix}, open-segment intersection relations
and trace identities on sufficiently long chains with boundary closure separate
distinct blocks and identify the periodic ground space with
$\spn\{V^{(N)}(A_j)\}_j$.
