\chapter{Propagation and localization on lattice domains}
\label{ch:lattice_propagation}

The Lieb--Robinson bounds of Chapter~\ref{ch:circuits_lieb_robinson} control how
far a local operator spreads under the time evolution of a finite-range
Hamiltonian. This chapter specializes them to finite lattice domains, where
distances are measured in the induced graph: it bounds the propagation of an
operator in graph distance, localizes an evolved operator near an arbitrary
region, and localizes a Gaussian average of evolutions
\cite[Sections~2 and~4]{OpenAI2026AreaLaw}.
