\chapter{Finding ground states by hierarchical projection}
\label{ch:hastings_hierarchy}

The local operators of the previous chapter have every gapped ground state
as an approximate eigenvector. Hastings~\cite{Hastings2006Solving} uses them
to find the ground states of a chain. Operators on pairs of neighbouring
blocks of sites are compressed level by level: each level keeps a spectral
window of a pair operator around the ground-state expectation, inside the
ranges of the projections of the two blocks below, and doubles the block
size. After logarithmically many levels on a chain of $N$ sites a small family of final
states remains. This chapter bounds the loss of the ground state at one
level and over all levels, counts the final states, and shows that each of
them has nearly the ground-state energy.
