\chapter{Successive viable sets and a matrix product state}
\label{ch:gapped_chain_construction}

This chapter constructs successive viable families from sampled powers
when the ratio of ground energy to gap is bounded, and extracts a matrix
product state from their final span. The Gaussian construction of
Huang~\cite[Section~5.4]{Huang2015GroundState} gives successive viable
families without a bound on the ground energy: one polynomial controls
their cardinalities and individual bond dimensions, uniformly over the
chain length and interaction. Product-state extension gives arbitrary
accuracy with polynomial bond bounds. These existence theorems do not
assert a running time. Independent truncation parameters also give normalized
ground-state approximations and a common reference space for collective
trimming. These estimates construct a balanced interval tree of viable
spaces, with explicit dimension and generator Schmidt-rank bounds. The
tree uses block leaves and one final trim at each long interval; its
distinction from the adaptive algorithm is stated explicitly.
