\chapter{Concrete Examples}\label{ch:examples}

illustrate injectivity, canonical forms, symmetries, and parent Hamiltonians.
Each example is specified by its physical dimension~$d$, bond dimension~$D$,
and matrix family~$\{A^i\}$.
