\chapter{Concrete Examples}\label{ch:examples}

Concrete matrix product state tensors illustrate injectivity, canonical forms,
symmetries, and parent Hamiltonians~\cite[Section~III]{Cirac2021Matrix}. Each
example is specified by its physical dimension~$d$, bond dimension~$D$, and
matrix family~$\{A^i\}$. The cases formalized here comprise foundational models
from the quantum information and many-body literature:
product states,
the Greenberger--Horne--Zeilinger (GHZ) state~\cite{GreenbergerHorneZeilinger1989,Greenberger1990Bell},
the Affleck--Kennedy--Lieb--Tasaki (AKLT) spin-$1$ valence-bond state~\cite{AKLT1987Rigorous,AKLT1988ValenceBond},
the Majumdar--Ghosh dimer ground state~\cite{MajumdarGhosh1969,MajumdarGhosh1969II},
the W state~\cite{DurVidalCirac2000},
the one-dimensional cluster state~\cite{BriegelRaussendorf2001,RaussendorfBriegel2001},
the Kitaev (Majorana) chain, of which only the ungraded contraction is formalized~\cite{Kitaev2001Majorana},
and the correlation-independent non-fixed-point tensor of Cirac et al.~\cite{Cirac2017MPDO_AnnPhys},
followed by their parent-Hamiltonian constructions~\cite{Fannes1992Finitely,PerezGarcia2007Matrix}.
