\chapter{Torus closures and quantum double models}%
\label{ch:peps_torus_closures}

On the torus a $G$-injective PEPS can be closed with group elements
inserted along the two seams. This chapter studies the resulting closure
vectors: they depend only on the pair-conjugacy class of the two group
elements, distinct classes give linearly independent vectors, and
superpositions of closures have computable entanglement entropies
\cite{Schuch2010PEPS}. It then groups the sites of a regular tensor into
two-by-two squares, and constructs the quantum double models of
\cite{Kitaev2003FaultTolerant} as $G$-isometric PEPS; for $G=\mathbb{Z}_2$
they give the toric code.
