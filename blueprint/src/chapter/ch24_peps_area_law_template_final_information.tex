\section{Original-template information from a uniform safe-box bound}
\label{sec:template-final-information}

The physical scanner can now be constructed directly from the actual
finite domain, cut and separated template.  All scanner geometry and
entropy antecedents are derived from accepted geometric results and one
uniform safe-box estimate at the fixed improved exponent.  The improved
safe-box estimate itself remains a genuine analytic hypothesis.

\begin{definition}[The literal final template scanner]
    \label{def:final-template-scanner}
    \lean{TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.finalTemplateScanner}
    \leanok
    For a finite domain $\Lambda$, a cut $A$, a nonempty ambient target $T$,
    and fixed coefficients $C_r,C_1$, use the accepted actual-history
    scanner with graph equal to the induced domain graph, depth equal to
    ambient sup-norm distance from $T$, and each original interaction
    anchored at a chosen member of its nonempty admissible support.
    At size $n$, use
    \[
        r_0=\lceil C_r(\log n)^2\rceil,\quad
        m=\lfloor n^{1/4}\rfloor,\quad
        D=\lceil n^{1/1000000}\rceil,\quad
        L=\lfloor n^{1-1/100000}\rfloor,\quad K=L/(8m),
    \]
    with natural-number division in $K$.  No entropy, probability or
    geometric estimate is included as a field of this construction.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[The original template collar is the truncation set]
    \label{thm:final-template-scanner-truncation}
    \lean{TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.finalTemplateScanner_truncationSet}
    \leanok
    For every nonnegative integer $L$, the actual truncation set of the
    constructed scanner is exactly
    \[
        S_0=A\cap T_L.
    \]
    This identity requires no separation, scale or entropy estimate.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
    \leanok
    \uses{def:final-template-scanner,
        thm:al_scan_ambient_rows}
    The actual truncation set contains the sites of $A$ at ambient depth
    at most $L$.  The accepted ambient-depth sublevel theorem identifies
    these sites with the radius-$L$ dilation of $T$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Conditional final information for actual templates]
    \label{thm:template-final-information}
    \lean{TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.exists_template_final_mutualInformation_of_boxEntropy}
    \leanok
    Fix the physical parameters $q\geq1$, $R$, $J\geq0$, $\Delta>0$,
    an integer safe-box clearance $D_0\geq1$, and $C_{\rm tpl}\geq24$.
    Suppose one fixed $C_{\rm box}\geq0$ satisfies the genuine uniform
    original-state safe-box estimate
    \[
        F_{\rm box}(r)\leq C_{\rm box}r^{1+e_*}
        \quad(r\geq1),\qquad e_*=2\cdot10^{-6}.
    \]
    Then there are $C>0$ and $N\geq2$, preceding every physical instance,
    such that the following holds.  Let $\Omega$ be an original gapped
    ground state on any finite induced domain $\Lambda$, let $A$ be any
    cut, and let $T$ be a separated template with parameters $(n,s_0)$,
    $n\geq N$ and $L=\lfloor n^{1-1/100000}\rfloor\leq s_0$.
    For the literal regions $X=A\cap T$ and $S_0=A\cap T_L$,
    \[
        2I_\Omega(X:S_0^c)\leq Cn^{62497/62500},\qquad
        2I_\Omega(X:S_0^c)\leq Cn^{1-1/100000}.
    \]
    No scanner, support-anchor choice, row count, multiplicity estimate,
    target/prefix entropy bound or auxiliary replica datum is assumed
    in this theorem.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
    \leanok
    \uses{thm:final-scanner-admissibility,
        thm:source-rounded-information,
        thm:final-template-scanner-truncation,
        thm:template-final-source-entropy,
        thm:al_lattice_counts}
    Fix the original interaction multiplicity
    $\mu_R=2^{(1+2R(R+1))-1}$ and $C_1=3\mu_R$, independently of all
    entropy coefficients and physical instances.  The accepted anchor-fiber
    bound applies to the support-member anchors of the constructed scanner.
    Choose the radius coefficient from the physical source theorem.  The
    uniform safe-box-to-template entropy theorem supplies a single $C_e$
    before the domain, Hamiltonian, ground vector, cut, target and collar.

    The template scale inequality gives $s_0\leq n$, and its accepted
    cardinality bound gives $|T|\leq9ns_0\leq9n^2$.  The accepted
    template layer bound gives at most $n$ sites in every positive layer
    through $L$.  The final rounded-scale theorem gives
    $2r_0\leq D$, $4D\leq m$, $K\geq1$ and $8Km\leq L$.
    Therefore $8m\leq L$ and $5r_0\leq L$, so
    \[
        2L+10r_0\leq4L\leq4s_0\leq4D_0s_0.
    \]
    The strict template separation consequently supplies the scanner's
    strict cut-clearance condition.  One maximum threshold precedes all
    physical instances.

    Apply the conditional physical information theorem to this actual
    scanner and the two entropy estimates already derived from the single
    safe-box input.  Its state and all intermediate witnesses are retained.
    The exact region identity above converts the conclusion to the original
    template collar.  The fixed-instance replica limit was taken in the
    preceding physical theorem; no new limit is introduced or exchanged.
\end{proof}
