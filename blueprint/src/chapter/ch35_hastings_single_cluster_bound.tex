\section{The single-cluster bound in Hastings' cluster expansion}
\label{sec:hastings_single_cluster_bound}

Let $H$ be a Hermitian Hamiltonian, let $\beta\ge0$, and put
$Z=\operatorname{tr}e^{-\beta H}$. For a cluster $C$ and its disjoint boundary
$B(C)$, suppose that the Hermitian terms $H_k$ have operator norm at most $J$.
Write $n_C=|C|$, $n_{B(C)}=|B(C)|$, $H_C=\sum_{k\in C}H_k$ and
$H_{B(C)}=\sum_{k\in B(C)}H_k$. The finite-sum perturbation estimate in the
companion quantum-channel volume~\cite[``The single-cluster bound'']{QICLean2026Blueprint},
applied to $T=C\cup B(C)$, gives
\begin{align}
    Z^{-1}\left|\operatorname{tr}\left(Oe^{-\beta(H-H_C-H_{B(C)})}\right)\right|
    \le\|O\|e^{(n_C+n_{B(C)})J\beta}.
    \label{eq:hastings_single_cluster_application}
\end{align}
This is the single-cluster estimate of~\cite[Section IV]{Hastings2006Solving},
with an absolute value on the trace, and holds for every operator $O$.
The source proves it by an imaginary-time Dyson expansion. The proof in the
companion volume instead uses positivity of the matrix exponential and
monotonicity of its trace under the order on Hermitian matrices.
For positive dimension, $Z>0$; in dimension zero the estimate uses $0^{-1}=0$.
