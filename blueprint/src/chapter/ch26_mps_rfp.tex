\chapter{Pure States: Renormalization of Matrix Product States}\label{ch:mps_rfp}

Renormalization fixed points (RFPs) for matrix product states are characterized
by physical two-site blocking, transfer map idempotence, zero correlation
length (ZCL), and the square-root isometry canonical form. The presentation
follows the pure-state analysis of \cite[Section~3]{Cirac2016MPDO_arXiv}.
