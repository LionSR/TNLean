\chapter{Renormalization fixed points of pure states}\label{ch:mps_rfp}

Following \cite[Section~3]{Cirac2016MPDO_arXiv}, a matrix product state is a
renormalization fixed point (RFP) when two-site blocking leaves its tensor
invariant up to a physical isometry; this chapter equates that blocking
relation with transfer-map idempotence, zero correlation length (ZCL), and
the square-root isometry canonical form.
