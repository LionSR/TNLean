\chapter{Symmetry examples and the induced cellular automaton}
\label{ch:mpu_symmetry_examples}

This chapter computes the symmetry classes and indices of the examples of
\cite{Cirac2017MPU}, among them the CZX tensor and the shift unitaries, and
relates the shift examples by swaps and ancillas. It then expresses blocked
time-reversal traces through a twisted transfer matrix to derive the blocking
parity of the time-reversal sign, constructs the quantum cellular automaton
induced by a matrix product unitary with a given standard form, and compares
its index with the index of Chapter~\ref{ch:mpu_fundamental_theorem}.
