\section{A finite Gaussian sum from the physical interaction}
\label{sec:huang_physical_gaussian}

The propagation construction and positive Gaussian quadrature combine directly.
The energy estimate enters through scalar phases, so the propagated Hamiltonian
continues to be the sum of the original positive interactions. This supplies the
finite operator approximation in \cite[Section 5.4, Lemma 19 and equations
    (19)--(22)]{Huang2015GroundState}, using the propagation construction of
\cite[Figure 2 and equations (12)--(13)]{Osborne2006Dynamics}.

\begin{lemma}[Finite physical Gaussian approximation]
    \label{thm:huang_physical_gaussian}
    \lean{TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.exists_positive_chain_gaussian_approximation}
    \leanok
    \uses{thm:huang_unitary_quadrature, thm:huang_physical_propagation,
        thm:huang_weighted_interval_layer_ranks}
    Let $q\ge1$ and let $H=\sum_{i=0}^{L-2}h_i$, where $h_i$ is supported on
    sites $i,i+1$ and $0\le h_i\le I$. Let $a\ge0$, $E\in\mathbb R$, $T\ge0$
    and $\eta,\rho>0$. Put
    \[
        r=\left\lceil2\left(vT+
        \log\frac{(L+1)C(T+1)}{\min\{\rho,1\}}\right)\right\rceil_+,
    \]
    where $v$ and $C$ are the propagation constants. There is a single finite sum
    $Q$ of $N\ge1$ operators such that
    \[
        N\le\frac{2T(L+|E|+1)}{\eta}+2,\qquad
        \|G_a(H-EI)-Q\|
        \le2e^{-T^2/(2a)}+\eta+\rho,
    \]
    and, simultaneously at every cut $k$,
    \[
        \operatorname{OSR}_k(Q)\le Nq^{8r+8}.
    \]
    Here $G_a(X)=\int e^{itX}\,d\gamma_a(t)$ for the centered Gaussian
    measure of variance $a$. For $a=0$, the displayed error bound is read with
    the convention $T^2/(2a)=0$; the measure itself is the point mass at zero.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
    \leanok
    Since $\|H-EI\|\le L+|E|$, use the Gaussian interval masses on a mesh
    of width $\eta/(\|H-EI\|+1)$ in $[-T,T]$. Write these masses as
    $w_j\ge0$ and their representatives as $t_j$. They satisfy
    $\sum_jw_j\le1$ and the stated sample count. The quadrature error is
    at most $2e^{-T^2/(2a)}+\eta$.

    The physical construction gives one family $K(s)$ with
    $\|e^{-isH}-K(s)\|\le\rho$ for $|s|\le T$ and all cut ranks at most
    $q^{8r+8}$. Set
    \[
        Q=\sum_j w_j e^{-it_jE}K(-t_j).
    \]
    The identity $e^{it(H-EI)}=e^{-itE}e^{itH}$ and the unit modulus of the
    phase give a sample error at most $\rho$. The total replacement error
    is at most $\rho\sum_jw_j\le\rho$. Finally, scalar multiplication does
    not increase operator-Schmidt rank, and rank is subadditive under finite
    sums. These facts yield the simultaneous bound for the same $Q$.
\end{proof}

This theorem concerns operator approximation. Spectral suppression with an
approximate energy estimate and improvement of a viable family are separate
mathematical steps.
