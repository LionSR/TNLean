\section{The actual corrected soft resampling tree}
\label{sec:physical_soft_resampling_tree}

The interval families below are obtained from the original Hamiltonian,
with one choice on every possible long interval before all regional centers
and seed sequences. Only the internal intervals of the actual balanced root
enter the probability estimate. The same regional minima, represented soft
matrices and spectral targets are retained for the energy argument.

The construction uses the corrected soft and cluster estimates in
\cite{gap:alvv16_soft_regional_residuals,gap:alvv16_cluster_approximation},
the corrected complex sphere count in
\cite{gap:alvv16_random_sampling_net_count}, and the terminal full-image
convention in~\cite{gap:alvv16_bootstrap_constants}. It is a corrected
auxiliary construction for~\cite[lines 749--755 and 1777--1817]{Arad2016RigorousRG}.
It is not identified with the printed fourth-order choice or its sharp
dependence on the gap, and no running-time estimate is asserted.

\begin{lemma}[Choosing the interval families while preserving their data]
    \label{lem:canonical_interval_resampling_selection}
    \lean{TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.exists_canonical_interval_resampling_tree_with_certificate}\leanok
    Fix $q\ge1$, a positive leaf width $w$, a common threshold $\theta\ge2$,
    a number of rounds $R$, a family parameter $D$, $0<\xi\le1$ and a
    common count $M_*$ such that
    \[
        q^w\le M_*,\qquad D^2\theta\le M_*.
    \]
    Fix a global target $G$. Suppose that, on every valid long interval
    $J$, there exists a family $F_J$ of $n_J\le D^2$ operators satisfying
    an additional assertion $C_J(n_J,F_J)$. Suppose its actual physical
    dimension is at most $2^R$, and that every fixed $0.04$-viable
    candidate satisfies
    \[
        \mu_J(H_J(W)^c)\le\beta,
    \]
    with the same literal original-target history $H_J(W)$ implying
    $3/200$-viability of the complete parent. Then all families may be
    selected simultaneously, preserving every assertion $C_J$.

    For their same canonical balanced tree, the exact root/list event
    $\mathcal G_{0,L}$ of Definition~\ref{def:canonical_interval_resampling_good}
    is measurable and
    \[
        \mathbb P(\mathcal G_{0,L}^c)\le(L-1)_+\beta.
    \]
    It is the whole seed space if $L\le w$. For every seed and every
    valid retained interval, the space has dimension at most $M_*$
    and at most $M_*$ spanning generators satisfying
    \[
        \xi^2\operatorname{SR}_j(u_i)\le M_*
    \]
    at every internal cut $j$. This bound concerns the recorded generators.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}\leanok
    \uses{lem:actual_interval_original_target_joint_probability,
        lem:canonical_interval_resampling_good_measurable,
        lem:actual_interval_resampling_tree_profile}
    Choose one of the assumed families on each interval, retaining its
    additional data. Apply the existing joint-history estimate and the
    unconditional recorded-rank estimate to those same families.
    A short full root is the full local space and has an empty merge
    list, so its joint event is the whole seed space.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[A physical corrected soft resampling tree]
    \label{thm:physical_soft_resampling_tree}
    \lean{TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.ALVV.exists_physical_soft_resampling_tree,
        TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.ALVV.PhysicalTerminalCertificate}\leanok
    Fix $q\ge2$ and $\gamma>0$, and put $g=\min\{\gamma,1\}$.
    There are natural numbers $u\ge2$, $K,C_{\rm count}\ge1$, a positive
    real $C_\xi$ and a natural $P_\xi$, before every chain length and
    Hamiltonian, with the following properties. Use the numerical
    quantities in (\ref{eq:calibrated_soft_parameters}), evaluated at $g$,
    and denote their approximation precision by $\xi_{\rm MPO}$.
    All residual, positivity, scale and closeness inequalities, and
    the bounds on the contraction in
    Lemma~\ref{lem:uniform_soft_parameters}, are retained.

    Let $H=\sum_i h_i$ on $L$ sites, with nearest-neighbour supports and
    $0\le h_i\le I$. Suppose $\mu\in\operatorname{spec}H$ and every
    spectral value equals $\mu$ or is at least $\mu+\gamma$.
    Put $G=\ker(H-\mu I)$, $r=\dim G$ and $Z=(L+1)(r+1)$. Set
    \begin{align}
        w              & =4\ell,                 & \theta & =K(r+1)(L+2)^2, & R & =qL,\notag \\
        M_{\rm common} & =\max\{q^w,D^2\theta\}.
        \label{eq:physical_soft_tree_schedule}
    \end{align}
    One may choose $0<\xi_{\rm init}\le1$ and one native family $F_J$
    for every valid long interval $J$, before all center estimates,
    seed sequences and final approximation accuracies, such that
    \begin{align}
        \xi_{\rm init}^{-2} & \le C_\xi Z^{P_\xi},  &
        M_{\rm common}      & \le C_{\rm count}Z^2, & n_J & \le D^2.
        \label{eq:physical_soft_tree_size}
    \end{align}

    Each selected family retains the geometry, five attained regional
    minima, common minimizing vector, and represented-target conclusions
    of Lemma~\ref{lem:physical_soft_interval_history}. In particular the
    native matrices $W_{J,i}$ satisfy $F_{J,i}=W_{J,i}$ as operators.
    For every admissible triple of center estimates, its own Hermitian
    represented matrix $\widehat G_J$ is within $\xi_{\rm MPO}$ of the
    same centered five-region soft matrix and has norm at most
    $M+\xi_{\rm MPO}$. Its spectral spaces at $E+g/4$ and $E+3g/4$
    coincide, have dimension $r$, and are mutually $3\tau/200$-close to
    the original $G$. The relation $E+\sum_j e_j=\mu$ is retained.
    For every $0.04$-viable initial candidate, on its same literal
    original-target history the same terminal untrimmed image is $\Delta/(91/100)^2$-viable for every one of
    these targets. Neither a new family nor another target approximant
    is selected for that assertion.

    Form the actual complete-parent balanced tree using these fixed
    families, threshold, radius and finite sequences. Its exact joint
    history event $\mathcal G_{0,L}$ is measurable and
    \begin{align}
        \mathbb P(\mathcal G_{0,L}^c)\le\frac1{(L+1)^2}.
        \label{eq:physical_soft_tree_probability}
    \end{align}
    On this event the actual root and every listed internal interval
    are $3/200$-viable for the original target, and every listed parent
    has its same original-target history. No success at other overlapping
    intervals is asserted. If $L\le w$, this event is the whole seed space.

    For every seed, independently of success, every actual retained
    interval has dimension at most $M_{\rm common}$ and a spanning family
    of at most $M_{\rm common}$ vectors with
    \begin{align}
        \xi_{\rm init}^2\operatorname{SR}_j(u_i)\le M_{\rm common}
        \label{eq:physical_soft_tree_recorded_ranks}
    \end{align}
    at every internal cut. The statement includes empty whole chains
    and flat spectra and assumes neither a reference space nor an
    initial matrix product representation.

    Finally, for every seed in $\mathcal G_{0,L}$ the energy induction of
    Lemma~\ref{thm:successful_tree_energy_induction} holds for these same
    families: one assignment of one-sided estimates of width $49/16$ at the
    root and every listed interval, each listed value three below the least
    eigenvalue of the child-centred soft regional operator compressed to the
    actual terminal image, with middle window
    $[a_J+2\ell,b_J+1)$ of the selected geometry.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}\leanok
    \uses{lem:physical_soft_sampling_data,lem:physical_soft_interval_history,
        lem:canonical_interval_resampling_selection,
        thm:successful_tree_energy_induction}
    Choose the uniform numerical data and the independent original-ground
    reference once. Its common radius supplies every interval's exact
    weighted trimming budget. Apply the physical interval-history
    construction once at each long interval, preserving its complete
    minimum and target data. The original gap implies the same gap with
    $g=\min\{\gamma,1\}$. Every long interval belongs to a nonempty whole
    chain, so the one-interval construction applies without restricting
    the full theorem's chain length.

    Apply the selection lemma to these proven physical assertions.
    The finite interval law has failure coefficient
    \[
        \beta=R\frac{1/q}{(L+2)^4}.
    \]
    The common scalar schedule gives
    $(L-1)_+\beta\le(L+1)^{-2}$. The same selection lemma supplies
    measurability, the short-root identity and the unconditional recorded
    profiles. The reference inverse-square estimate and common-count
    estimate give (\ref{eq:physical_soft_tree_size}). All center-dependent
    target and terminal-image conclusions are those of the same selected
    physical families. For the energy induction, evaluate each family's
    certificate at the exact center triple $e$: its middle minimum and
    target satisfy the hypotheses of
    Lemma~\ref{thm:successful_tree_energy_induction}.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Energy induction on a successful resampling tree]
    \label{thm:successful_tree_energy_induction}
    \lean{TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.ALVV.exists_successful_tree_energy_induction}\leanok
    \uses{lem:root_energy_node_relation_assignment,
        lem:selected_soft_terminal_node_energy,
        def:canonical_interval_resampling_good}
    Retain the interaction, numerical and coordinate assumptions of
    Lemma~\ref{lem:selected_soft_terminal_node_energy}, with $G$ the full
    ground space of $H$ at its least spectral value $\mu$. Fix the families,
    thresholds and finite sequences of an actual balanced resampling tree on
    $[0,L)$. Suppose that every valid long interval $J=[l,r)$ has a middle
    window $[u_J,v_J)$ with $u_J-l,\,r-v_J\le2\ell$ and an attained minimum,
    and a target $T_J$ with $\dim T_J=\dim G$ within directed squared error
    $3\tau/200$ of $G$, such that for every $0.04$-viable initial candidate
    the terminal untrimmed image on its literal original-target history is
    $\Delta/(91/100)^2$-viable for $T_J$.

    Then for every seed sequence in the joint success event
    $\mathcal G_{0,L}$ there is one assignment $(l,r)\mapsto p_{l,r}$ such
    that the root and every listed internal interval carry one-sided
    estimates of width $49/16$ of their original regional minima, exact for
    short children. At every listed interval $J$ with children
    $[l,k)$ and $[k,r)$,
    \begin{align*}
        p_{l,r}=\lambda_J-3,
    \end{align*}
    where $\lambda_J$ is the least eigenvalue of the compression of the
    soft regional operator $R_c=P-M+cI+h_{m,t}(M-cI)$, centred at
    $c=p_{l,k}+p_{k,r}-e_{\mathrm L}-e_{\mathrm R}-2$ with the attained
    collar minima, to the actual terminal image of $J$ for the actual
    merged child candidate and the actual seed. No second family, target
    or image is selected. This is the energy induction in the proof
    of~\cite[Theorem~\texttt{t:alg1}, lines 1779--1818]{Arad2016RigorousRG},
    with the corrected one-sided width of
    \cite{gap:alvv16_soft_regional_energy_centering}.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}\leanok
    \uses{lem:root_energy_node_relation_assignment,
        lem:selected_soft_terminal_node_energy}
    On the success event every listed interval is $3/200$-viable and
    carries its literal history; short children are the full local space.
    The children of a listed interval are short or listed, so their merge
    is $0.04$-viable and the seed lies in that parent's history. Hence the
    terminal image is viable for $T_J$. Lemma~\ref{lem:selected_soft_terminal_node_energy}
    then gives, for all child estimates of width $49/16$, a parent estimate
    $\lambda_J-3$ of the same width. Lemma~\ref{lem:root_energy_node_relation_assignment}
    assigns all estimates simultaneously with these node relations.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[The refined Ritz output of the successful physical root]
    \label{thm:physical_soft_tree_refined_ritz}
    \lean{TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.ALVV.exists_physical_soft_tree_refined_ritz_approximation}\leanok
    \uses{thm:physical_soft_resampling_tree,lem:same_root_polynomial_refinement}
    Retain the primitive gapped chain and the physical soft families of
    Theorem~\ref{thm:physical_soft_resampling_tree}, with
    $\mathrm{count}\le C_{\rm count}((L+1)(r+1))^2$. There are $C>0$ and $k$,
    before every chain, such that for every $\eta>0$ refinement data, chosen
    before every seed, satisfy the following with
    $B_{\rm scale}\le C\bigl((L+1)(r+1)(1+1/\eta)\bigr)^k$. On the joint success
    event, of probability at least $1-(L+1)^{-2}$, the literal filter-and-trim
    refinement of the same actual root has dimension at most $\mathrm{count}$,
    and its Ritz subspace $R$ has dimension $r=\dim G$, is mutually
    $\eta/2$-close to $G$, and consists of matrix product states of bond
    dimension $B_{\rm scale}\,\mathrm{count}^2$. This is the qualitative
    output assertion of~\cite[Theorem~\texttt{t:alg1}, lines
    1777--1818]{Arad2016RigorousRG} for this corrected construction; no
    running time is asserted.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}\leanok
    \uses{thm:physical_soft_resampling_tree,lem:same_root_polynomial_refinement}
    On the success event the root is $3/200$-viable on the whole chain, which
    is $3/200$-closeness of the same space in chain coordinates, and its
    dimension is at most $\mathrm{count}$ for every seed. Apply the same-root
    polynomial refinement to that root.
\end{proof}

\subsection{A unique frustration-free ground state}

A frustration-free chain with a unique ground state $\Gamma$ and spectral
gap $\gamma$ is the case of Assumption~(DG) with ground energy $0$ and
ground-space dimension $1$. The source treats it in
\cite[Theorem~\texttt{t:unique}, lines 1829--1855]{Arad2016RigorousRG},
where the improvement is a running time. The degenerate-gap tree gives its
existence content, with intermediate spaces larger than those of the
source; see~\cite{gap:alvv16_unique_ground_state_tree}.

\begin{lemma}[The ground line of a unique frustration-free ground state]
    \label{lem:unique_ground_state_spectral_span}
    \lean{TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.ALVV.spectralSpanLE_zero_eq_span_singleton,
        TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.ALVV.zero_mem_spectrum_of_mulVec_eq_zero}\leanok
    Let $H$ be a Hermitian matrix and $\Gamma\ne0$ with $H\Gamma=0$, such
    that every vector of the kernel of $H$ is a multiple of $\Gamma$ and every
    spectral value of $H$ is either $0$ or at least some $\gamma>0$. Then $0$
    is a spectral value of $H$, and the span of the eigenvectors with
    eigenvalue at most $0$ is the line $\mathbb C\Gamma$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}\leanok
    All spectral values are nonnegative, so an eigenvector with eigenvalue at
    most $0$ lies in the kernel, and the span lies in $\mathbb C\Gamma$.
    Conversely $\Gamma$ is an eigenvector with eigenvalue $0$, hence lies in
    the span. If $0$ were not a spectral value, every eigenvalue would be at
    least $\gamma$, the span would be zero, and $\Gamma$ would vanish.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[The refined output for a unique frustration-free ground state]
    \label{thm:unique_ground_state_refined_ritz}
    \lean{TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.ALVV.exists_unique_ground_state_refined_ritz_approximation}\leanok
    \uses{thm:physical_soft_tree_refined_ritz,lem:unique_ground_state_spectral_span}
    Let $H=\sum_ih_i$ be a nearest-neighbour chain with $0\le h_i\le\Id$,
    frustration free with a unique ground state $\Gamma$ and spectral gap
    $\gamma>0$. There are constants, chosen before the chain, such that the
    physical soft families on the actual resampling tree have a success event
    of probability at least $1-(L+1)^{-2}$, every node space has dimension at
    most $\mathrm{count}\le C_{\rm count}(2(L+1))^2$, and for every $\eta>0$
    the refinement of the actual root on that event, with its Ritz extraction,
    gives a line mutually $\eta/2$-close to $\mathbb C\Gamma$ consisting of
    matrix product states of bond dimension
    $B_{\rm scale}\,\mathrm{count}^2$, where
    $B_{\rm scale}\le C\bigl(2(L+1)(1+1/\eta)\bigr)^k$. This is the existence
    content of~\cite[Theorem~\texttt{t:unique}, lines
    1829--1855]{Arad2016RigorousRG}, restricted to the degenerate-gap tree:
    the intermediate dimension grows with $L$ instead of being the constant
    $\mathcal D s^2$ of the source, and no running time is asserted.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}\leanok
    \uses{thm:physical_soft_tree_refined_ritz,lem:unique_ground_state_spectral_span}
    By Lemma~\ref{lem:unique_ground_state_spectral_span}, the hypotheses of
    Lemma~\ref{thm:physical_soft_tree_refined_ritz} hold at ground energy
    $0$, and its ground space is $\mathbb C\Gamma$, of dimension $1$.
\end{proof}

The low-density case of~\cite[Theorem~\texttt{t:alg2}, lines
1869--1878]{Arad2016RigorousRG} has no tree counterpart yet. Its area law and
matrix product state approximations, and an orthonormal matrix product state
basis close to the low-energy band, are proved separately; the merge tree run
with low-density improving families remains open.
