\chapter{Fluxes in quantum double models}
\label{ch:peps_anyons_fluxes}

Fluxes sit on the plaquettes of a quantum double model and are created by
inserting group elements on the bonds of a regular $G$-injective
PEPS~\cite[Section~III.B]{Cirac2021Matrix}. This chapter creates fluxes
(Theorem~\ref{thm:peps_regular_cycle_global_flux_creation}), moves a flux
between neighbouring plaquettes by a physical operation
(Theorem~\ref{thm:peps_torus_translated_global_flux_move}), and composes these
moves along strings on the torus; deforming a string across a region of
invariant tensors leaves the physical state unchanged
(Theorem~\ref{thm:peps_dual_flux_deformation}). Charges and their
braiding with fluxes follow in the next chapter.
