\chapter{Fluxes in quantum double models}
\label{ch:peps_anyons_fluxes}

Fluxes sit on the plaquettes of a quantum double model and are created by
inserting group elements on the bonds of a regular $G$-injective
PEPS~\cite[Section~III.B]{Cirac2021Matrix}. This chapter creates fluxes, moves
a flux between neighbouring plaquettes by a physical operation, and composes
these moves along strings on the torus; deforming a string across a region of
invariant tensors leaves the physical state unchanged. Charges and their
braiding with fluxes follow in the next chapter.
