\chapter{The Fundamental Theorem on a chain of fixed length}%
\label{ch:algebraic_ft}

Following Moln\'ar et al.~\cite{Molnar2018NormalPEPS}, this chapter proves the
algebraic Fundamental Theorem for injective and normal matrix product states on
a finite periodic chain of fixed length by working directly in the matrix
algebra $\MN{D}$.
The fixed-length argument rests on two structural lemmas, the virtual bond
gauge (Lemma~\ref{lem:virtual_bond_gauge}) and the proportionality lemma
(Lemma~\ref{lem:tensor_proportional}), which also underpin the injective PEPS
development in Chapter~\ref{ch:peps_ft}.
Together, these two lemmas yield the chain theorem and its corollaries for
translation-invariant chains, site-dependent descriptions of
translation-invariant states, and blocked chains.
For block-diagonal tensors, product-algebra automorphisms decompose into block
permutations and inner conjugations, establishing gauge equivalence for direct
sums and block matching across differing bond dimensions.

Throughout, $D$ denotes the bond dimension and $d$ the physical dimension
with $d \ge D^2$.
