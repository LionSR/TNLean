\chapter{The Algebraic Fundamental Theorem}%
\label{ch:algebraic_ft}

This chapter presents the algebraic Fundamental Theorem for injective and normal
matrix product states on a finite periodic chain of fixed length, following
Moln\'ar et al.~\cite{Molnar2018NormalPEPS}.
The argument works directly in the matrix algebra $\MN{D}$.
The fixed-length development rests on two lemmas: the virtual bond gauge
(Lemma~\ref{lem:virtual_bond_gauge}) and the proportionality lemma
(Lemma~\ref{lem:tensor_proportional}).
These yield the main chain theorem and its corollaries for translation-invariant
chains, site-dependent descriptions of translation-invariant states, and blocked
chains.
Block-diagonal tensors are treated through product-algebra automorphisms and
their block-permutation decompositions, yielding gauge equivalence for direct sums
and block matching in the equal-vector case with differing bond dimensions.
The same foundational lemmas also underpin the injective PEPS development in
Chapter~\ref{ch:peps_ft}.

Throughout, $D$ denotes the bond dimension and $d$ the physical dimension
with $d \ge D^2$.
