\chapter{Approximating thermal states by a cluster expansion}
\label{ch:hastings_cluster_expansion}

The last part of~\cite{Hastings2006Solving} approximates the thermal
operator $e^{-\beta H}$ of a finite-range Hamiltonian by an operator in
matrix product form: a sum of products of single-site operators whose
coefficients have finite range. Trotter products have such a form. Expanding
$e^{-\beta H}$ into words in the terms of $H$ and discarding the words that
contain a large cluster of terms gives a truncated operator of the same form.
At high temperature its error is controlled by a bound on a single cluster
and by a percolation estimate, which rests on counting connected clusters in
a graph of bounded degree. The bound on expectation values then becomes a
bound in trace norm, and products of truncated operators at high temperature
extend the matrix product form and the error estimate to every temperature.
