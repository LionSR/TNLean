\chapter{Symmetries and string order}\label{ch:symmetry}

Through the Fundamental Theorem of MPS
\cite{PerezGarcia2007Matrix,PerezGarcia2008StringOrder}, on-site symmetries of
matrix product states induce projective representations on the virtual bond space. For injective tensors,
invariance under a physical group action determines virtual gauge transformations
and the cohomology classes of their 2-cocycles. The chapter then distinguishes
physical endpoint string order parameters from virtual-boundary nondecay. The
next chapter uses these cohomology classes to classify symmetry-protected
topological phases.
