\chapter{Symmetries, Physical String Order, and Virtual-Boundary Nondecay}\label{ch:symmetry}

On-site symmetries of matrix product states induce projective representations on
the virtual bond space through the Fundamental Theorem of MPS
\cite{PerezGarcia2007Matrix,PerezGarcia2008StringOrder}. For injective tensors,
invariance under a physical group action determines virtual gauge transformations
whose 2-cocycles classify symmetry-protected topological (SPT) phases, while
distinguishing physical endpoint string order parameters from virtual-boundary
nondecay.
