\section{Physical measurement of fluxes}%
\label{sec:peps_flux_measurement}

The holonomy of a regular network around a cycle or a plaquette is a flux.
This section measures its conjugacy class by a projective measurement that
acts on physical sites only.

\begin{lemma}[Conjugacy classes are unchanged by conjugation]%
    \label{thm:peps_conjugacy_class_invariance}
    \lean{ConjClasses.mk_conjugate}
    \leanok
    For every group $G$ and $g,x\in G$, the conjugacy-class quotient satisfies
    $C[xgx^{-1}]=C[g]$. This is the class invariance used in
    \cite[Theorem 6.15 and Section 6.6]{Schuch2010PEPS}.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}\leanok
    The elements $g$ and $xgx^{-1}$ are conjugate, with conjugating element $x$.
    Their images in the conjugacy-class quotient are therefore equal.
\end{proof}
