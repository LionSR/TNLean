\chapter{Source factors and the Gauss law}
\label{ch:mpu_cocycles_gauss_law}

This chapter treats the source factors of a matrix product unitary and the
Gauss operators used to gauge a group represented by matrix product unitaries
\cite{FrancoRubio2025MPUGauging}. For a matrix product unitary in canonical
form~II that is simple together with its adjoint, it shows that the Gram
matrices of the two source factors are scalar and computes their
normalizations. Two source decompositions are related by unitary gauges up to
a positive scalar (Lemma~\ref{thm:mpug_source_decomposition_gauges}), and the
scalar disappears once the factors are isometrically normalized
(Corollary~\ref{cor:mpug_normalized_source_decomposition_gauges}). The chapter
then defines the local Gauss operators, which form a unitary representation
of the group (Lemma~\ref{thm:mpug_gauss_representation}), and their averaging
projectors, and shows that the gauged vector of matter defect states lies in
the gauge-invariant subspace of every unitary completion of the prescribed
defect maps (Lemma~\ref{thm:mpug_gauge_invariant_subspace_finrank}).
