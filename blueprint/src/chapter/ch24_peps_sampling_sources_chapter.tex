\chapter{Expanding and correcting the sources of a distributed circuit}
\label{ch:party_circuit_sampling_sources}

The compression theorem \cite[Theorem~5.2]{OpenAI2026PolynomialPEPS} samples
the sources of the bipartite resources at random and corrects the sampled
terms. This chapter prepares that argument: it expands a distributed circuit
chronologically in its source occurrences, substitutes corrected sources,
places their Schmidt coordinates under one Gaussian law, and estimates the
resulting physical contractions. The sampling itself and the uniform
compression follow in the next chapter.
