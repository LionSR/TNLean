\chapter{Parent Hamiltonians and the intersection property}\label{ch:parent_hamiltonian}

Following~\cite{PerezGarcia2007Matrix,Cirac2021Matrix}, for a
translation-invariant matrix product state with tensor $A$, the \emph{parent
    Hamiltonian} is the translation-invariant sum of local terms, each the
orthogonal projector onto the complement of the length-$L$ local space
$G_L(A)=\operatorname{im}(\Gamma_L)$ with
$\Gamma_L(X)(\sigma)=\tr(A^\sigma X)$. On $N$ sites, the state space is
identified with functions $\{0,\ldots,d{-}1\}^N\to\C$ on the computational
basis.

This chapter defines the local ground spaces and the parent Hamiltonian. It
proves the projector estimate of Fannes, Nachtergaele and
Werner~\cite{Fannes1992Finitely} for the local ground spaces of primitive
tensors, in the geometric form used by Nachtergaele~\cite{Nachtergaele1996Spectral},
and the intersection property
$G_L^{\mathrm{left}}\cap G_L^{\mathrm{right}}=G_{L+1}(A)$ for injective
tensors. The next chapter uses the intersection property to prove uniqueness of the periodic ground state.
