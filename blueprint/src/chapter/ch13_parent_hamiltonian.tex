\chapter{Parent-Hamiltonian Foundations: Injective and Normal Ground Spaces}\label{ch:parent_hamiltonian}

For a translation-invariant matrix product state with tensor $A$, the
\emph{parent Hamiltonian} is the translation-invariant sum of local terms, each
the orthogonal projector onto the complement of the length-$L$ local space
$G_L(A)=\operatorname{im}(\Gamma_L)$, where
$\Gamma_L(X)(\sigma)=\tr(A^\sigma X)$~\cite{PerezGarcia2007Matrix,Cirac2021Matrix}.
We identify the $N$-site space with functions
$\{0,\ldots,d{-}1\}^N\to\C$ on the computational basis.

The local ground spaces satisfy an intersection property that ensures
uniqueness of the periodic ground state $V^{(N)}(A)$ for injective and normal
matrix product states. For injective tensors with positive bond dimension
$D\ge1$, system size $N\ge2$, and interaction range $1<L\le N$, the parent
Hamiltonian is frustration-free with unique ground state $V^{(N)}(A)$.
For normal tensors with positive bond dimension $D \ge 1$ and injectivity length
$L_0$, uniqueness holds whenever $L_0 < L \le N$ with $N \ge L_0 + 1$;
word-span injectivity after blocking determines the boundary-closure identities
that match open-interval restrictions to the periodic chain.
