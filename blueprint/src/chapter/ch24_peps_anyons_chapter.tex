\chapter{Anyons in Quantum Double Models}
\label{ch:peps_anyons}

The excitations of a quantum double model are anyons: fluxes on plaquettes,
charges on vertices, and their composites~\cite{Kitaev2003FaultTolerant}. In a
$G$-injective PEPS they are created by inserting group elements and
representations on the bonds~\cite[Section~III.B]{Cirac2021Matrix}. This
chapter treats the semi-regular bond states and their Fourier coordinates,
creates and moves fluxes along strings, and creates charge pairs and braids a
charge around a flux.
