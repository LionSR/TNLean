\chapter{Charges and charge--flux braiding in quantum double models}
\label{ch:peps_anyons}

Charges sit on the vertices of a quantum double model and are created by
inserting representations on the bonds of a regular $G$-injective
PEPS~\cite[Section~III.B]{Cirac2021Matrix}. This chapter creates charge pairs,
measures their charge labels, and moves a charge along a string by physical
operations. It then moves a charge across the string of a flux from the
previous chapter, the local step of charge--flux braiding, and ends with a
measurement of the charge pair on the original physical spins after its return.
