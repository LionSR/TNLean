\section{The physical rough safe-box bootstrap}
\label{sec:arealaw-physical-box-bootstrap}

The following construction uses the fixed bootstrap scales of the
September 24 manuscript.  Its input is a uniform safe-box estimate at the
current exponent, and its conclusion is an improved exponent in the
original state.  The finite-dimensional replica limit is taken before
changing the physical size.  No sharp marginal-moment estimate is used.

\begin{theorem}[Generic physical charge-energy coefficient]
    \label{thm:arealaw-generic-charge-energy}
    \lean{TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.ScannerExponents.exists_actualCharge_energy_coefficients}
    \leanok
    Fix a local dimension $q\geq1$, a scanner exponent tuple,
    a positive squared-logarithmic radius
    coefficient, a positive charge constant, a nonnegative target-volume
    coefficient, an anchor-multiplicity bound, a polynomial tail exponent,
    and the two transport-energy coefficients.
    There are $C\geq1$, $C_l\geq0$ and $N$ before every finite physical
    system, auxiliary dimension, replica vector, round and interpolation.
    Under the literal rounded scales and actual domain, row, anchor and
    clearance hypotheses, for $W\geq0$ and a nonzero symmetric supplied
    replica vector, the actual energy-error term is bounded by
    \begin{align}
        C W^2 n^\ell D^2(\log n)^{C_l}
        \left((1-p)\left(\frac{Q}{KnD}\right)^{1/8}
        +(1-p)n^{-P}+p\right),
        \qquad 0<p<1.
        \label{eq:arealaw-generic-charge-cap}
    \end{align}
    The bound uses the absolute value of the supplied transport
    coefficient.  The existing all-time and old-prefix history estimates
    supply the physical energy bound at these generic exponents.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
    \leanok
    \uses{lem:al_scanner_scale_facts,
        thm:al_scan_transport_coefficients_log}
    The physical history estimate bounds the energy error by a sum of
    a density term and a tail term. The logarithmic dimension estimate
    bounds both transport coefficients by $C_d(\log n)^{C_l}$.
    The rounded-scale bound $nD^2/(mK)\leq C_s n^\ell D^2$ and
    $a=W/K$ then give (\ref{eq:arealaw-generic-charge-cap}) with
    $C=C_sC_d$. All factors used in these comparisons are nonnegative.
\end{proof}


\begin{theorem}[The same selected actual energy]
    \label{thm:arealaw-generic-selected-energy}
    \lean{TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.CollarScan.actualCharge_defect_le_of_density_energy_bound_at_exponent}
    \leanok
    For positive physical and auxiliary dimensions, $\Delta>0$ and
    total weight $W\geq0$, at any selected actual charge point the transport energy conclusion
    and the preceding coefficient cap imply
    $E_{\rm def}\leq(e+\mathrm{cap}+r_k)/(g/2)$.
    The truncated eigenvalue $e$, physical prevector, selected point and
    actual mean energy are unchanged.  Nonnegativity of the actual
    energy-error integral justifies replacing its signed coefficient by
    its absolute value.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
    \leanok
    The actual energy-error integral is nonnegative, so replacing the
    transport coefficient by its absolute value can only increase the
    numerator. Substitute its supplied coefficient cap and divide by
    the positive normalized gap $g/2$. The point and its prevector
    remain fixed.
\end{proof}


\begin{theorem}[One physical rough comparison source]
    \label{thm:arealaw-source-rough-comparison}
    \lean{TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.CollarScan.exists_sourceTarget_selected_actualCharge_rough_comparison}
    \leanok
    Fix $q\geq1$, range $R$, $J\geq0$, $\Delta>0$, the nonnegative
    target-volume coefficient and positive charge constant with its
    anchor-multiplicity budget, and a real tail exponent.
    The truncation and transport constants are chosen before a scanner
    exponent tuple; the physical energy coefficients and size threshold
    are then chosen before every finite instance and current entropy input.
    Assume a nonempty ambient target and the literal graph, depth,
    rounded scales, rows, anchors and
    weak scanner clearance, and the original-state positive-prefix bound
    $C_e(nL^\eta+n)$.  The numerical radius and separation conditions are
    explicit here.  For total weight four, $K\geq8$, and the actual
    transport-smallness condition, one truncated vector, typical spectral
    set and Schmidt--Bell label sequence yield an actual selected point
    in every fixed interval $0<p_0<p_1<1$ for all large replica counts.
    That same point satisfies the literal endpoint density bound, the
    actual normalized-energy cap, and both rough norm comparisons.
    The upper, lower, endpoint and energy remainders vanish for the fixed
    physical instance.  No improved entropy or low-energy point is supplied.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
    \leanok
    \uses{thm:al_scan_target_typical_window,
        thm:al_global_schmidt_bell_prevector,
        thm:al_positive_prefix_entropy_transfer,
        thm:al_scan_actual_rough_upper,
        thm:arealaw-physical-endpoint-loss,
        thm:arealaw-actual-selected-energy,
        thm:actual_scan_supplied_prevector_rough_lower,
        thm:arealaw-generic-charge-energy,
        thm:arealaw-generic-selected-energy}
    Construct the truncated ground vector and its typical spectral
    window once, then choose one global Schmidt--Bell label sequence.
    Transfer the original positive-prefix entropy bounds to this vector.
    Its rough upper estimate, endpoint estimate and rough lower estimate
    hold with remainders tending to zero. Apply the actual charge
    selection theorem on $[p_0,p_1]$ and use the endpoint estimate to
    bound its density. At this same selected point, combine the generic
    coefficient bound with the transport-energy inequality. Restrict the
    two rough comparisons to this point without choosing a second
    vector, label sequence or interpolation parameter.
\end{proof}


\begin{theorem}[Principal density at the selected point]
    \label{thm:arealaw-rough-selected-density}
    \lean{TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.ScannerExponents.exists_rough_selectedDensity_principal}
    \leanok
    Fix the scanner tuple, $q\geq1$, $C_e,C_t\geq0$, $C_1>0$ and a
    logarithmic exponent.  One $C_\delta>0$ and threshold precede every
    current $e\geq\kappa$ and finite scalar instance.
    The current target, shell, selected-dimension and mass bounds, together
    with the literal rough endpoint numerator and logarithmic transport
    cap, give eventually
    \begin{align}
        \frac{Q}{KnD} & \leq\delta,
                      & \delta      & =C_\delta\epsilon^{-1}
        \left(n^{e-\mu}+a^{1/4}(\log n)^{C_{l,\delta}}\right),
                      & a           & =4/K,                  & \epsilon & =n^{-\nu}.
        \label{eq:arealaw-rough-principal-density}
    \end{align}
    The replica remainder is absorbed only after the instance is fixed.
    Every proposition attached to the original selected point survives.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
    \leanok
    \uses{thm:arealaw-source-rough-budget-power,
        thm:arealaw-selected-density-principal,
        lem:al_scanner_scale_facts}
    The source rough-budget bound and $D\leq2n^e$ bound the selected
    numerator by a constant times $n^{1+e}$ plus its logarithmic term.
    Since $m\geq n^\mu/2$, the non-remainder contribution is at most
    $\delta/2$ after choosing $C_\delta$. At the fixed physical
    instance, the remaining coefficient times the replica error tends
    to zero and is eventually at most $\delta/2$. Keep the supplied
    selected point and all its other conclusions.
\end{proof}


\begin{theorem}[Normalized selected energy at bootstrap scales]
    \label{thm:arealaw-bootstrap-selected-energy}
    \lean{TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.exists_bootstrap_selected_energy_bound}
    \leanok
    Fix $0<e_0<1$, nonnegative cap and density coefficients, real logarithmic
    exponents and a positive physical gap $\gamma$.
    One threshold works for all $e\leq e_0$.
    At the literal bootstrap scales, if $0\leq\rho\leq\delta$,
    $p\in[\epsilon,2\epsilon]$, $p\leq1$, and the truncated eigenvalue
    is at most $n^{-1000}$, then the normalized selected cap is at most
    $(1+\gamma^{-1})n^{-\nu/2}$.
    The physical application uses $\gamma=g/2$ and retains $r_k/\gamma$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
    \leanok
    \uses{thm:area_law_bootstrap_principal_energy}
    From $0\leq\rho\leq\delta$ and $\epsilon\leq p\leq2\epsilon$,
    the expression in parentheses in the cap is at most
    $2(\delta^{1/8}+\epsilon+n^{-100})$. Apply the principal energy
    estimate with coefficient $2C/\gamma$. The eigenvalue term is at
    most $\gamma^{-1}n^{-\nu/2}$ because
    $n^{-1000}\leq n^{-\nu/2}$ for $n\geq2$. Adding the two bounds
    gives the stated coefficient $1+\gamma^{-1}$.
\end{proof}


\begin{theorem}[Restored additive entropy budget]
    \label{thm:arealaw-bootstrap-restored-budget}
    \lean{TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.exists_bootstrap_restored_entropy_budget}
    \leanok
    For $e_*<e_0<1$, $q\geq1$ and $C_e\geq0$, one positive coefficient
    and size threshold work for every $e_*<e\leq e_0$.
    They bound the exact typical-mass, whole-prefix, mismatch,
    binary-entropy and original-state continuity budget by
    $C n^{1+(1-\ell)e}$.
    The hypotheses include $z>0$, $z\geq1-n^{-100}$ and
    $0\leq\tau\leq1$; the displayed budget keeps
    $7(w-\log z)-\log z$ and the additional $n^{-247}$ continuity error.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
    \leanok
    \uses{thm:area_law_bootstrap_comparison_budget}
    For $n\geq2$, the mass estimate gives $z\geq1/2$,
    $z^{-1}\leq2$ and $-\log z\leq1$. The continuity errors are at
    most one. With $C_b=8C_e+12\log q+30$, the complete budget is at
    most $C_b(nL^e+nD+n^{3/5}+h(\tau)/a+1)$.
    The bootstrap comparison-budget estimate bounds this sum by
    $(5+\log2/4)n^{1+(1-\ell)e}$ uniformly over the current exponent.
\end{proof}


\begin{theorem}[Original-state scanner entropy improvement]
    \label{thm:arealaw-source-bootstrap-entropy}
    \lean{TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.CollarScan.exists_sourceBootstrap_entropy_improvement}
    \leanok
    Fix $e_*<e_0<1$, $C_e\geq0$ and the physical and geometric constants
    of the rough source theorem.  There are positive $C_r,C_{\rm out}$
    and $N\geq2$ before every current $e_*<e\leq e_0$ and finite system.
    Use the fixed bootstrap exponents, literal rounded scales and actual
    graph, target, depth, anchors, rows and clearance.
    If the original target entropy is at most $C_e n^{1+e}$ and every
    original positive-depth prefix entropy is at most $C_e(nL^e+n)$,
    then the same original target satisfies
    \begin{align}
        S_\Omega(X) & \leq C_{\rm out}n^{1+(1-\ell(e_0))e}.
        \label{eq:arealaw-source-bootstrap-improvement}
    \end{align}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
    \leanok
    \uses{thm:fixed_weight_four_transport_smallness,
        thm:scanner_rounded_admissibility,
        thm:arealaw-source-rough-comparison,
        thm:arealaw-rough-selected-density,
        thm:arealaw-bootstrap-selected-energy,
        thm:arealaw-bootstrap-restored-budget,
        thm:arealaw-target-entropy-transfer,
        thm:area_law_selected_schmidt_entropy_bounds,
        thm:area_law_rough_typical_entropy_absorption}
    Derive the transport and rounded admissibility conditions and use
    the physical rough source once.  The principal-density and energy
    estimates hold at its unchanged selected points.
    At fixed $n$, put $\tau=n^{-\nu/4}$ and apply the two rough comparisons
    with the coefficient-three lower bound.  The relative loss is
    $6(\tau+E_{\rm def}/\tau)$; the three replica remainders vanish.
    Substitute the typical auxiliary entropy only after this limit,
    then use continuity in the reverse direction to restore $\Omega$.
    The restored additive budget gives the stated improved power.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Weak-rectangle source entropy]
    \label{thm:arealaw-weak-rectangle-source-entropy}
    \lean{TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.exists_weakRectangle_source_entropy_bounds}
    \leanok
    Fix $b>0$ and $C_{\rm box}\geq0$.
    One nonnegative $C_e$ precedes every finite state, weakly safe
    rectangle, row scale $n\geq s$, collar $1\leq L\leq s$, and current
    exponent $e\geq b$.
    If $(D_0+1)L\leq D_0s$ and the same unit vector obeys the pointwise
    safe-box bound at this one exponent, then its literal target and all
    positive-depth prefixes obey the source bounds above.
    One may take $C_e=C_{\rm box}(25+64/(2^b-1))$.
    This uses the actual weak-rectangle dyadic shell cover, including
    thin rectangles; it does not invoke template separation.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
    \leanok
    The safe-box estimate bounds the target by $C_{\rm box}s^{1+e}$.
    The weak-rectangle dyadic shell estimate bounds each positive prefix
    by $C_{\rm box}(24+64/(2^e-1))sL^e$. Since $e\geq b>0$,
    its coefficient is at most $C_{\rm box}(24+64/(2^b-1))$.
    Use $s\leq n$ and enlarge this coefficient by $C_{\rm box}$ to
    obtain the common target and prefix coefficient.
\end{proof}


\begin{theorem}[Physical powers and the box supremum]
    \label{thm:arealaw-box-supremum-powers}
    \lean{TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.regionalEntropy_le_of_boxEntropy_power}
    \leanok
    For $q\geq1$, a positive-scale uniform box bound at exponent
    $1+e>0$ supplies the
    corresponding entropy estimate for every original physical safe
    rectangle, including a possible zero-size case by its cardinal bound.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
    \leanok
    If the rectangle size is positive, its entropy is one member of
    the family defining the box supremum at that size. Apply the
    assumed power bound there. At size zero the rectangle contains
    no sites, so its entropy is zero, as is its positive power.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Bounded rectangle sizes]
    \label{thm:arealaw-box-supremum-small-sizes}
    \lean{TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.boxEntropy_power_le_of_large_rectangles}
    \leanok
    Let $q\geq1$, $C,p\geq0$ and $N\in\mathbb N$.
    If every safe original physical rectangle of size $s\geq N$ has
    entropy at most $Cs^p$, then for every $r\geq1$ the literal box
    supremum is at most $(C+N^2\log q+1)r^p$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
    \leanok
    At sizes at least $N$, apply the assumed estimate and use
    $s^p\leq r^p$ for $s\leq r$. At smaller sizes, the volume bound
    gives entropy at most $N^2\log q$. Since $r^p\geq1$ for
    $r\geq1$ and $p\geq0$, both bounds are at most
    $(C+N^2\log q+1)r^p$. Take the supremum over the same physical
    family.
\end{proof}


\begin{theorem}[Reducing the safety parameter]
    \label{thm:arealaw-box-clearance-comparison}
    \lean{TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.boxEntropy_power_le_of_succ_clearance}
    \leanok
    \uses{def:area_law_box_entropy}
    Fix $q,R\in\mathbb N$ with $q\geq1$ and $J,\Delta\in\mathbb R$.
    Write $F_{\mathrm{box},D}$ for the safe-box entropy at safety
    parameter $D$, with the same fixed physical parameters
    $q,R,J,\Delta$. Let $D\in\mathbb N$ satisfy $D\geq3$, and let
    $C,p\geq0$. Suppose that
    $F_{\mathrm{box},D+1}(r)\leq Cr^p$ for every integer $r\geq1$.
    Then, for every integer $r\geq1$,
    \begin{align}
        F_{\mathrm{box},D}(r)
         & \leq (4C+4\log q+1)r^p.
    \end{align}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}\leanok
    \uses{thm:area_law_box_children,thm:arealaw-box-supremum-small-sizes}
    Fix a physical instance and a $D$-safe rectangle $Q$ of size
    $s\geq2$, and put $r=\lceil s/2\rceil$.
    Theorem~\ref{thm:area_law_box_children}, with $M=2$, partitions $Q$
    into at most four disjoint child rectangles $Q_i$ of size at most
    $r$. Since $D\geq3$ and $s\geq2$, one has $(D+1)r\leq Ds$.
    Each child lies in $Q$, so its points have distance greater than
    $Ds\geq(D+1)\operatorname{size}(Q_i)$ from every cut endpoint.
    Thus each child is $(D+1)$-safe.

    The physical regions $A\cap Q_i$ partition $A\cap Q$.
    For the same normalized ground vector $\Omega$ on the original
    domain, subadditivity and the assumed bound give
    \begin{align}
        S_\Omega(A\cap Q)
         & \leq \sum_i S_\Omega(A\cap Q_i)
        \leq \sum_i Cs^p
        \leq 4Cs^p.
    \end{align}
    Apply Theorem~\ref{thm:arealaw-box-supremum-small-sizes} with
    coefficient $4C$, exponent $p$, and cutoff $N=2$. The volume
    estimate supplies the correction $N^2\log q=4\log q$ for smaller
    sizes. Taking the supremum over the same physical family gives
    the asserted coefficient $4C+4\log q+1$ at every positive integer
    scale.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Uniform physical one-step improvement]
    \label{thm:arealaw-physical-box-step}
    \lean{TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.exists_uniform_boxEntropy_improvement}
    \leanok
    Fix $q\geq1$, $R$, $J\geq0$, $\Delta>0$, $e_*<e_0<1$ and
    $C_{\rm box}\geq0$.  There is $C>0$ before every
    $e_*<e\leq e_0$ and every $D_0\geq1$ such that
    \begin{align}
        F_{\rm box}(r) & \leq C_{\rm box}r^{1+e}\quad(r\geq1)
        \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
        F_{\rm box}(r)\leq Cr^{1+(1-\ell(e_0))e}\quad(r\geq1).
        \label{eq:arealaw-physical-box-step}
    \end{align}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
    \leanok
    \uses{def:bootstrap_scanner_exponents,
        thm:arealaw-source-bootstrap-entropy,
        thm:arealaw-weak-rectangle-source-entropy,
        thm:arealaw-box-supremum-powers,
        thm:arealaw-box-supremum-small-sizes,
        thm:rectangle_bootstrap_scanner_geometry}
    Apply the source improvement to the literal rectangle scanner with
    $n=\lceil24s\rceil=24s$, target-volume coefficient one and the derived
    anchor multiplicity.  Rounded sublinear growth supplies the weak
    clearance $2L+10r_0\leq D_0s$ and the shell safety budget.
    The preceding shell coefficient is fixed using $b=e_*$.
    Since the improved exponent is between zero and two, conversion from
    $n$ to $s$ costs at most $24^2$ uniformly in the current exponent.
    Extend over bounded sizes by the volume entropy estimate and take
    the literal supremum only after obtaining the original-state bound.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Physical safe-box bound at the fixed exponent]
    \label{thm:arealaw-physical-box-fixed-exponent}
    \lean{TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.exists_boxEntropy_le_boxError}
    \leanok
    For $q\geq1$, $R\in\mathbb N$, $J\geq0$ and $\Delta>0$, there is
    $C>0$ before every integer $D_0>2R+10$ and every positive integer $r$ such that
    \begin{align}
        F_{\rm box}(r) & \leq Cr^{1+e_*}, & e_* & =2\cdot10^{-6}.
        \label{eq:arealaw-physical-box-fixed-exponent}
    \end{align}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
    \leanok
    \uses{thm:area_law_initial_box,
        thm:area_law_box_entropy_bounds,
        thm:area_law_finite_exponent_improvement,
        thm:arealaw-physical-box-step}
    Obtain the genuine initial exponent $0<e_0<1$ uniformly in the safety
    parameter.  Apply the established finite exponent iteration to the
    family indexed by $D_0>2R+10$, using the quadratic volume estimate
    and the preceding physical one-step theorem.  The latter is invoked
    only when a current exponent above $e_*$ occurs.  No fixed-exponent
    box estimate is an input of this argument.
\end{proof}

For a real safety parameter $D_0>2R+10$, write
$F_{\mathrm{box},D_0}(r)$ for the supremum in
\cite[Section~3, before Proposition~3.3]{OpenAI2026AreaLaw}, with this
fixed safety parameter. The estimate in
\cite[Proposition~9.5]{OpenAI2026AreaLaw} is
$F_{\mathrm{box},D_0}(r)\leq Cr^{1+e_*}$ for every integer $r\geq1$,
where $e_*=2\cdot10^{-6}$ and $C$ depends only on $q,R,J,\Delta$.
The following gives its pointwise formulation, uniformly over all such
real safety parameters.

\begin{theorem}[Improved entropy bound for safe rectangles]
    \label{thm:arealaw-physical-safe-rectangle-real}
    \lean{TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.exists_regionalEntropy_safe_rect_le_boxError}
    \leanok
    \uses{def:area_law_hamiltonian,def:area_law_ground_entropy,
        def:area_law_safe_rectangle}
    Fix integers $q\geq1$ and $R\geq0$, a term norm bound $J\geq0$,
    and a gap $\Delta>0$. There is $C>0$, depending only on
    $q,R,J,\Delta$, such that the following holds simultaneously for
    every real $D_0>2R+10$.

    Let $\Lambda\subseteq\mathbb Z^2$ be any finite domain, let $H$
    be an interaction Hamiltonian of range $R$ with term norms at most
    $J$, and let $\Omega$ be a normalized eigenvector of energy $E_0$
    satisfying the full-system projector-gap inequality with gap
    $\Delta$. Let $A\subseteq\Lambda$, and let $Z$ be the endpoints of
    the edges of the induced lattice graph crossing $A$.
    If $Q$ is a product of two finite nonempty integer intervals and
    every $t\in Q$ and $z\in Z$ satisfy
    $D_0\operatorname{size}(Q)<\|t-z\|_\infty$, then
    \begin{align}
        S_\Omega(A\cap Q)
         & \leq C\operatorname{size}(Q)^{1+e_*},
         & e_*                                   & =2\cdot10^{-6}.
        \label{eq:arealaw-physical-real-box}
    \end{align}
    Here size counts sites along the longer axis, and $A\cap Q$
    contains only physical sites of $\Lambda$. Empty domains, empty
    cuts, empty physical intersections, disconnected domains, and
    domains with holes are permitted. If $Z$ is empty, the safety
    condition is vacuous, agreeing with distance to the empty set
    being infinite.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}\leanok
    \uses{thm:arealaw-physical-box-fixed-exponent,
        thm:arealaw-box-clearance-comparison,
        thm:arealaw-box-supremum-powers}
    Put $d=2R+10$. The integer safe-box estimate supplies $C_b>0$,
    depending only on $q,R,J,\Delta$, such that
    $F_{\mathrm{box},d+1}(r)\leq C_b r^{1+e_*}$ for every integer
    $r\geq1$. Since $d\geq3$, the safety comparison gives
    \begin{align}
        F_{\mathrm{box},d}(r)
         & \leq C r^{1+e_*},
         & C                 & =4C_b+4\log q+1>0.
        \label{eq:arealaw-physical-real-box-constant}
    \end{align}
    This constant is chosen before $D_0$ and every finite physical
    instance. A rectangle safe at real parameter $D_0>d$ is also
    $d$-safe: for every $t\in Q$ and $z\in Z$,
    $d\operatorname{size}(Q)\leq D_0\operatorname{size}(Q)
        <\|t-z\|_\infty$.
    Set $s=\operatorname{size}(Q)\geq1$. The bound by the safe-box
    supremum for the same $H,\Omega,A,Q$ and
    (\ref{eq:arealaw-physical-real-box-constant}) give
    $S_\Omega(A\cap Q)\leq F_{\mathrm{box},d}(s)
        \leq Cs^{1+e_*}$.
\end{proof}

To recover the supremum formulation, fix a real $D_0>2R+10$ and an
integer $r\geq1$. Every entropy in its defining family is bounded by
$Cs^{1+e_*}\leq Cr^{1+e_*}$, where $s=\operatorname{size}(Q)\leq r$;
here $C>0$ and $1+e_*>0$. Taking the supremum gives
$F_{\mathrm{box},D_0}(r)\leq Cr^{1+e_*}$, with the same constant
uniformly in $D_0$. Conversely, the supremum estimate at
$r=\operatorname{size}(Q)\geq1$ bounds each admissible rectangle
entropy and gives (\ref{eq:arealaw-physical-real-box}). Thus the two
formulations have exactly the same uniformity and hypotheses.
