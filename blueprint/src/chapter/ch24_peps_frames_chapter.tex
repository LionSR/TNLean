\chapter{Handing sites between parties while preserving the ground state}
\label{ch:peps_frames}

The parties of the approximation are indexed by the dyadic squares of
Chapter~\ref{ch:peps_frames_dyadic_geometry}, and sites move between parties
while a reference vector stays equal to the ground state, up to encodings of
small patches \cite[Sections~6 and~7]{OpenAI2026PolynomialPEPS}. This chapter
formalizes encoded frames, splitting a region between two owners, exchanging
regions between two copies of the ground state, and births and deaths of
parties.
