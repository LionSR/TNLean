\chapter{Handing sites between parties while preserving the ground state}
\label{ch:peps_frames}

The parties of the approximation are indexed by dyadic squares, and sites move
between parties while a reference vector stays equal to the ground state, up to
encodings of small patches \cite[Sections~6 and~7]{OpenAI2026PolynomialPEPS}.
This chapter sets up the geometry of the dyadic squares and formalizes encoded
frames, splitting a region between two owners, exchanging regions between two
copies of the ground state, births and deaths of parties, the memory of several
copies, and the information reset that keeps the information bound of
Chapter~\ref{ch:peps_approximation} valid after each change.
