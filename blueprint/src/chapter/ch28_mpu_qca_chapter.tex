\chapter{Quantum Cellular Automata on Spin Chains}
\label{ch:mpu_qca}

Matrix product unitaries are the quantum cellular automata of a spin chain:
automorphisms of the algebra of local observables that move the support of
every observable by a bounded distance~\cite{Cirac2017MPU}. Starting from
the quasi-local algebra of Chapter~\ref{ch:spin_chain_observables}, this
chapter develops supports and commutants of observables, the finite-chain
realization of a periodic matrix product unitary, quantum cellular automata and
their blocking, and the support algebras that decompose an automaton into
two layers of local unitaries.
