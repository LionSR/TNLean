\chapter{Quantum Cellular Automata on Spin Chains}
\label{ch:mpu_qca}

Matrix product unitaries are the quantum cellular automata of a spin chain:
automorphisms of the algebra of local observables that move the support of
every observable by a bounded distance~\cite{Cirac2017MPU}. This chapter
builds the algebras of observables on finite regions and their quasi-local
completion, the support algebras of an observable, translations, and the
finite-chain realization of a periodic matrix product unitary.
