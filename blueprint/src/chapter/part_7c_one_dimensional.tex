\part{Area law and approximation in one dimension}
\label{part:one_dimensional_area_law}

On a chain the two previous results become the area law for a gapped
finite-range Hamiltonian (Theorem~A) and the approximation of its ground state
by an open-boundary matrix product state of bond dimension polynomial in the
length of the chain (Theorem~B) \cite{TNLean2026OneDimensional}. This part
proves both directly on the chain, from a tail bound at a cut and a buffered
entropy discount, and proves Theorem~B a second time by a sequential
preparation that specializes the protocol of
Part~\ref{part:peps_approximation}. Hastings' original proof of the
one-dimensional area law is in Part~\ref{part:gapped_chains}.
