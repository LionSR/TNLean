\section{A constructed basis for full-Hamiltonian filtered terms}

The basis argument of \cite{Hastings2006Solving} can be completed with
corrected constants under a polynomial sphere bound. The geometric
restriction is explicit here; this statement is not the unrestricted
printed equation \texttt{oibu}. See \cite{gap:hastings05_two_dim_ground_basis}
and the companion correction \cite{gap:hastings05_basis_refinement}.

    Restricting $PWP$ to the actual ground eigenspace preserves its norm,
    and also the norm of a commutator of two such compressions.
    Put $S(\beta,k)=k!(2/\beta)^k e^{\beta/2}/(1-e^{-\beta/2})$.
    A sitewise support budget bounds the total weight assigned to any
    one anchor. Degree at most $D$ gives closed balls of cardinality at most
    $(D+1)^r$; meeting-term weights are bounded by the support budget times
    the number of sites in the region. Enlarging or separating supports uses
    the graph triangle inequality. If the spheres have cardinality at most $K_g(r+1)^{k_g}$,
    the weighted exponential sum about any anchor is at most
    $|b_0||K_g|S(\beta,k_g)$ for every $\beta>0$. A list of anchors
    multiplies this bound by its length, including repetitions.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}\leanok
    The ambient compression is the extension by zero of its restriction.
    Group nonnegative weights by their anchors, then group sites by their
    graph distance. Sum the resulting polynomial times exponential series.
    For disconnected components the kernel is zero. Repeated square roots
    turn its rate $\gamma$ into $\gamma/2^n$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Uniform full-Hamiltonian basis bound]
    \label{thm:hastings_full_hamiltonian_uniform_basis}
    \lean{TNLean.Lattice.sum_weight_list_graphDecayKernel_le,
    TNLean.Lattice.exists_groundSpaceBasis_sum_norm_offDiagonalPartFamily_le_uniform}
    \leanok

    Let $H=\sum_i h_i$ be a Hermitian local Hamiltonian on a finite graph,
    with positive on-site dimension and nonempty supports $X_i$, chosen anchors $a_i\in X_i$, support
    diameter at most $R$, support cardinality at most $v_R$, and sitewise
    budget $\sum_{i:x\in X_i}\|h_i\|\le b_0$.
    Assume sphere growth at most $K_g(r+1)^{k_g}$, a nonzero ground
    eigenspace of dimension $n$, and a positive gap $\Delta E$.
    Let $\gamma>0$ and $C$ be the explicit local commutator constants of
    Theorem~\ref{thm:hastings05_blecm_exp}, with the support-separation
    factor $e^{\gamma(2R+1)}$ included in $C$. There is an orthonormal
    ground-space basis in which
    \[
      \sum_i\|\operatorname{offdiag}(P\widetilde h_iP)\|
      \le (n-1)^2\sqrt{n^3C(1+2n^3)^n}\,
         |b_0||K_g|S(\gamma/2^n,k_g).
    \]
    Here $\widetilde h_i$ is the Gaussian filter under the full
    Hamiltonian, with any nonnegative filter parameter. The bound is
    independent of the numbers of terms and sites when $n$ and the
    displayed local, gap and growth parameters are fixed.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}\leanok
    Restrict the actual compressions to the ground eigenspace and use
    their proved pairwise commutator bound. Apply the weighted recursive
    basis theorem. Its list has length at most
    $n-1$. The norm of an off-diagonal part supplies one factor $n-1$;
    summing the list supplies the other. Filtering under the full
    Hamiltonian leaves the ground-space compressions unchanged.
\end{proof}

The source's operators filtered under locally truncated generators require
the additional physical estimate of
Theorem~\ref{thm:hastings_actual_truncated_basis_polynomial_growth}. They are
not identified with the full-Hamiltonian filtered operators in this theorem. The separate mathematical counterexample is given in
\cite{gap:hastings05_tree_basis_counterexample}.
