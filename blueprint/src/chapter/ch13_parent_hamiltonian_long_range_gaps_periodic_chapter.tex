\chapter{Infinite-volume gaps for periodic tensors}
\label{ch:parent_hamiltonian_long_range_gaps_periodic}

This chapter extends the gaps of
Chapter~\ref{ch:parent_hamiltonian_long_range_gaps_primitive_sectors} from
sums of primitive tensors to periodic tensors, by blocking a periodic tensor
into a sum of primitive tensors and controlling the residual boundary terms.
For a normalized periodic tensor it constructs one positive parent interaction
with both a uniform finite-volume gap and an infinite-volume gap in every pure
zero-energy state. The same conclusions hold for finite families of periodic
tensors and for the infinite-chain state of a tensor with a stationary density
matrix, in the existence setting of
\cite[Theorems~1.1--1.2]{Nachtergaele1996Spectral}.
