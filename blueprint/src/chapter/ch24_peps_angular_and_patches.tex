\chapter{Finite-rank constraints on small patches}
\label{ch:peps_angular_patches}

The second analytic input of the approximation
\cite[Section~4]{OpenAI2026PolynomialPEPS}: near a square of side of order
$\log L$, the ground state is projected with high accuracy onto a sum of
orthogonal subspaces, each spanned by polynomially many vectors on a smaller
nested square tensored with all states outside it. This chapter constructs the
projection from minimizers of regularized patch filters and records the
encodings of these subspaces in small registers.
