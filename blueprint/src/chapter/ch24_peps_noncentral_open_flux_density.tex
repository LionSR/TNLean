\begin{lemma}[Native avoiding noncentral flux has the vacuum local density]
    \label{lem:peps_native_avoiding_flux_density}
    \lean{TNLean.PEPS.IsGIsometric.torusAvoidingFlux_normalized_density_eq_vacuum}\leanok
    Let the native torus tensor be regular group-isometric for a finite
    group. Suppose the physical observation graph is connected and its
    actual closed-cell realization is simply connected. A native flux
    path avoiding every plaquette with an observed corner has the vacuum
    normalized observed reduced density, for any insertion element.
    Both actual native cuts are nonzero.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}\leanok
    \uses{lem:peps_flux_untwisted_boundary_component,
        lem:peps_flux_avoiding_incident_identity,
        lem:peps_anchored_twist_density}
    The exterior collar supplies an untwisted component joining all
    complementary boundary endpoints, and avoidance supplies identity
    on the observed incident bonds for the same path. Simple connectedness
    supplies connected complement and nonempty boundary. Apply the
    noncentral contraction theorem and convert its actual ordered-edge
    coefficient cuts to the native torus cuts, including the vacuum cut.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Native noncentral open-pair vacuum density under collar geometry]
    \label{lem:peps_native_noncentral_flux_density}
    \lean{TNLean.PEPS.IsGIsometric.torusFlux_normalized_density_eq_vacuum_of_isSimplyConnected}
    \leanok
    In addition to the preceding physical region conditions, suppose the
    computed forbidden dual plaquettes have simply connected closed-cell
    realization and both native path endpoints lie outside that set.
    Every actual open or closed native flux string then has the vacuum
    normalized observed reduced density, with both cuts nonzero.
    The insertion element may be noncentral and the winding is unrestricted.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}\leanok
    \uses{lem:peps_native_avoiding_flux_density,
        lem:peps_dual_full_support_classification}
    Construct a dual detour avoiding the computed forbidden plaquettes
    with the same lifted displacement as the original path. Full-support
    homotopy completeness and virtual invariance identify their actual
    native coefficient vectors. The preceding result gives the detour's
    normalized density and both nonzero cuts; substitute the actual
    coefficient equality to obtain the conclusion for the original path.
\end{proof}
