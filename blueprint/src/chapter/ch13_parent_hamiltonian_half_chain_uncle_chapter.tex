\chapter{Half-chain spectra and uncle Hamiltonians}
\label{ch:parent_hamiltonian_half_chain_uncle}

This chapter proves two further results on matrix product states and their
parent Hamiltonians. Under the canonical conditions and Condition~C2
of~\cite{PerezGarcia2007Matrix}, the ordered spectrum of the half-chain reduced
density matrix on a ring converges to that of $\Lambda\otimes\Lambda$, where
$\Lambda$ is the dual fixed point~\cite[Theorem~6]{PerezGarcia2007Matrix}. For
two blocks $A$ and $B$ whose combination with off-diagonal walls is injective,
the uncle Hamiltonian, a limit of parent Hamiltonians, has periodic ground space
spanned by $V^{(N)}(A)$ and $V^{(N)}(B)$; for almost every choice of walls it
has positive eigenvalues arbitrarily close to zero on large
rings~\cite{FernandezGonzalez2015Uncle}.
