\part{Gapped chains: Hastings' area law, entropy scaling and algorithms}
\label{part:gapped_chains}

Other approaches to gapped chains. The first two chapters give Hastings'
original proof of the one-dimensional area law: an approximate factorization
of the ground-state projector across a cut, and the area law and the
approximation by matrix product states that follow from it. The next three
chapters treat Hastings' earlier description of gapped Hamiltonians by local
data: filtered local terms that have the ground states as approximate
eigenvectors, a hierarchical projection that finds the ground states of a
chain, and a cluster expansion that approximates thermal states. The next
chapter relates the scaling of R\'enyi entropies to approximability by matrix
product states. Three chapters then build ground-state approximations of a
gapped chain from successive viable sets: the operations on viable sets, their
assembly into a matrix product state, and the polynomial filters of the
renormalization algorithm. The last two chapters derive entropy bounds and
matrix product approximations from the polynomial filters of Arad, Kitaev,
Landau and Vazirani.
