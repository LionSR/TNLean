\section{The actual native compressed regional minimum}

\begin{lemma}[A one-sided estimate from a viable interval space]
    \label{lem:native_regional_compressed_energy_estimate}
    \lean{TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.exists_native_regional_compressed_energy_estimate}
    \leanok
    \uses{lem:regional_ground_energy_bound}
    Let $H=\sum_i h_i$ be a chain of $L$ sites of positive local
    dimension, with $0\le h_i\le I$ supported on sites $i,i+1$.
    Fix an interval $J$ and let $G$ be the full ground space of $H$.
    There is an actual Hermitian matrix $K_J$ on the native Hilbert
    space of $J$ whose extension by the identity equals the sum of
    the bonds internal to $J$. Set $e_J=\lambda_{\min}(K_J)$.
    These data are chosen before every candidate subspace below.

    Identify the full Hilbert space with
    $\mathcal H_J\otimes\mathcal H_{J^c}$ by the standard coordinate
    isometry $T_J$. Let $S\subseteq\mathcal H_J$ be $\delta$-viable
    for $T_JG$, where
    \[
        0\le\delta\le\frac{1}{100(L+1)}.
    \]
    Then $S$ has positive dimension. Its actual orthogonal compression
    $P_SK_J|_S$ has least eigenvalue $\lambda_S$ satisfying
    \[
        e_J\le\lambda_S\le e_J+3.
    \]
    Thus the actual estimate $p_S=\lambda_S-3$ satisfies
    $e_J-3\le p_S\le e_J$. Empty intervals and chain endpoints are
    included; no gap or unique ground vector is assumed.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}\leanok
    Restrict the regional matrix to a fixed complementary configuration
    to obtain $K_J$. Designated support makes its identity extension
    equal to the original regional sum. Restricting positive scalar
    lower bounds to this slice, and lifting the canonical lower bound
    of $K_J$ back to the full space, identifies the two regional minima.

    Pull $S\otimes\mathcal H_{J^c}$ back to the physical space. The
    preceding lemma gives an actual nonzero candidate vector of
    centered regional energy at most three times its squared norm.
    Transport it by $T_J$ and expand along an orthonormal basis of
    the complementary factor. One nonzero slice lies in $S$ and
    has Rayleigh quotient at most $e_J+3$. This bounds the actual
    compressed minimum from above. Positivity of $K_J-e_JI$ gives
    the lower bound. Subtracting three gives the stated estimate.
\end{proof}

The loss bound in this lemma is explicit. A constant-accuracy viable
space does not alone satisfy it: in particular, a recorded loss of
$3/200$ is insufficient. The energy-estimation step of
\cite[Figure~\texttt{fig:algo1}, lines 1631--1632, and lines
1793--1807]{Arad2016RigorousRG} uses a separate untrimmed powered image of the child product as
its energy candidate, and compresses the generated soft regional
Hamiltonian on that space. Its smaller operator norm and the accuracy
of that same construction require a separate argument. The present
lemma proves a conditional estimate for the original regional
Hamiltonian; it does not complete the recursive energy invariant,
the generation procedure, or its running-time estimate.
