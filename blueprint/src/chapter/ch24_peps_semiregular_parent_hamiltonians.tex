\chapter{Parent Hamiltonians of semi-regular projected entangled pair states}%
\label{ch:peps_semiregular_parent_hamiltonians}

When the virtual group action on the bonds is semi-regular with positive multiplicities, physical bond transformations relate the semi-regular regional ground spaces and parent kernels to those of the regular representation via copy and Fourier coordinates \cite[Sections~5 and~7]{Schuch2010PEPS}. On regions cut by four boundary seams, intersection and closure identities classify the native parent ground spaces.
