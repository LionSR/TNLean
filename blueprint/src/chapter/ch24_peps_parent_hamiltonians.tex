\chapter{Parent Hamiltonians of injective and regular $G$-injective PEPS}%
\label{ch:peps_parent_hamiltonians}

For injective PEPS, two-site parent interactions pulled back along left inverses of the site maps isolate the virtual Bell bonds and reconstruct the global ground space when the covering patches intersect \cite[Section~IV.C.1]{Cirac2021Matrix}. For regular $G$-injective PEPS, right-multiplication invariance on shared internal bonds transfers local parent constraints into cycle holonomy and flatness conditions \cite[Section~5]{Schuch2010PEPS}.
