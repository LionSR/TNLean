\chapter{Saturation of the area law and simple tensors}
\label{ch:mpdo_rfp_area_law}

A renormalization fixed point of a mixed state saturates the area law for the
mutual information~\cite{Cirac2016MPDO_arXiv}. This chapter proves the entropy
bounds behind that statement, enlarges the physical space by isometries, and
defines the simple tensors whose local structure is studied in
Chapter~\ref{ch:mpdo_rfp_gibbs}.
