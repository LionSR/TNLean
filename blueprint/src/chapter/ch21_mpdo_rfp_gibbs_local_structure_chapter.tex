\chapter{Local structure and commuting form of simple fixed points}
\label{ch:mpdo_rfp_gibbs_local_structure}

This chapter completes the local structure of simple fixed points. Refinement
and coarse-graining channels in sector and physical coordinates lead to the
local sector structure implied by saturation of the area law and zero
correlation length; the same section treats Examples~4.10--4.12 of
\cite{Cirac2016MPDO_arXiv} and gives a counterexample to the printed
implication (iv)$\Rightarrow$(v) of its Theorem~4.9. The last section brings
the local structure to a commuting form on single bonds and shows that every
injective tensor saturating the area law generates Gibbs states of a
nearest-neighbour commuting Hamiltonian, as defined in
Chapter~\ref{ch:mpdo_rfp_gibbs}.
