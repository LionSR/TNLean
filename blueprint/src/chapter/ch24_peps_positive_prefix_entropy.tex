\section{Entropy transfer on positive-depth prefixes}

The scanner uses one truncated state on an entire family of comparison
prefixes. The following consequence of entropy continuity transfers the
original prefix bound to that same state. The original entropy estimate
and the compact-region volume estimate remain explicit hypotheses.

\begin{theorem}[Uniform entropy transfer on positive-depth prefixes]
    \label{thm:al_positive_prefix_entropy_transfer}
    \lean{TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.eventually_regionalEntropy_positiveDepthPrefix_le}
    \leanok
    \uses{def:al_scan_comparison_prefixes}
    Fix an integer $q\ge1$, a real number $g>0$, and a real constant
    $C_{\mathrm{vol}}$. For all sufficiently large integers $n$, the
    following assertion holds uniformly over the finite domain and all
    the data below.

    Let $\Omega,\Omega_t$ be unit vectors on a finite domain $\Lambda$,
    with local dimension $q$. For a subset $A\subseteq\Lambda$, an
    integer-valued depth function $d$, and a natural number $L$, put
    \begin{align}
        R_L & =\{x\in A:d(x)\le L\},   &
        Q_j & =\{x\in A:0<d(x)\le j\}.
    \end{align}
    Suppose $|R_L|\le C_{\mathrm{vol}}n^2$ and, writing $\Delta$ for
    the trace distance between the two pure states, suppose
    \begin{align}
        \Delta & \le
        \sqrt{\frac{2\min\{n^{-1000},g/4\}}{g}}.
    \end{align}
    Let $C_e,e\in\mathbb R$. If the original vector satisfies
    \begin{align}
        S_\Omega(Q_j) & \le C_e(nL^e+n)
    \end{align}
    for every natural number $j\le L$, then the same prefixes satisfy
    \begin{align}
        S_{\Omega_t}(Q_j) & \le C_e(nL^e+n)+n^{-247}.
    \end{align}
    The threshold depends only on $q,g,C_{\mathrm{vol}}$; in particular,
    it precedes the choices of both vectors, the depth function, and
    $C_e,e$. No sign condition on $C_e,e$ is needed for this implication.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
    \leanok
    \uses{lem:al_continuity_fixed_coefficient,
        thm:area_law_regional_entropy_continuity}
    For $n\ge1$, the assumed trace-distance estimate gives
    $\Delta\le\sqrt{2/g}\,n^{-500}$. Also $Q_j\subseteq R_L$ whenever
    $j\le L$. Entropy continuity
    \cite[Lemma~2.1]{OpenAI2026AreaLaw}, applied in the regional dimension
    $q^{|Q_j|}$, therefore bounds the entropy difference by
    \begin{align}
        |S_{\Omega_t}(Q_j)-S_\Omega(Q_j)|
         & \le4\sqrt{\Delta}\,(1+|Q_j|\log q).
    \end{align}
    Set $C_0=\max\{4,C_{\mathrm{vol}}\}$. The volume bound and
    $\log q\ge0$ bound the right-hand side by
    $C_0\sqrt{\Delta}(1+C_0n^2\log q)$. The fixed-coefficient
    continuity estimate makes this at most $n^{-247}$ for all
    sufficiently large $n$, uniformly over all the given data.
    Adding the original prefix entropy bound proves the assertion.
\end{proof}
