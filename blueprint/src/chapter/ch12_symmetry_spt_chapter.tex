\chapter{Symmetry-protected topological phases}\label{ch:symmetry_spt}

For injective tensors, the 2-cocycles of the virtual gauge transformations of
Chapter~\ref{ch:symmetry} classify symmetry-protected topological (SPT)
phases: within the injective symmetric MPS and parent-Hamiltonian
framework~\cite{Schuch2011Phases}, two such states lie in the same phase
exactly when their virtual projective representations determine the same class
in $H^2(G,\mathrm{U}(1))$. This chapter develops this classification together with
virtual-boundary nondecay for injective symmetric tensors, and then treats
renormalization fixed points of these phases, symmetric gapped paths with bond
regrouping, the degeneracy of the entanglement spectrum, and the obstructions
from time reversal and reflection.
