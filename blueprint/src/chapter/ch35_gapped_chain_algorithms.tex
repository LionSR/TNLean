\chapter{Viable sets and their trimming}
\label{ch:gapped_chain_algorithms}

Landau, Vazirani and Vidick~\cite{Landau2015Published} construct ground-state
approximations by successive viable sets. Their sampling bound depends on
the ground energy, as discussed in~\cite{gap:lvv13_runtime_ground_energy}.
Arad, Landau, Vazirani and Vidick~\cite{Arad2016RigorousRG} give a more
efficient renormalization algorithm. At each cut, a viable set is a small
family of vectors on the sites to its left whose span contains the left
part of a good approximation to the ground state.

This chapter develops the operations on viable sets: approximate
ground-state projectors, gluing at a cut, size and bond trimming, and error
reduction. The next chapter assembles them into successive viable families
and a matrix product state.
