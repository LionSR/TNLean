\chapter{Invariant states, permuted blocks, and domain walls}
\label{ch:mpu_cocycles_permuted_blocks}

This chapter introduces the $L$-symbols of normal matrix product states
permuted by a group of matrix product operators, together with their joint
scalar gauges, and relates them to the anomaly three-cocycle of
Chapter~\ref{ch:mpu_cocycles}. A nontrivial anomaly obstructs invariant normal
states (Theorem~\ref{thm:mpug_anomaly_obstruction}). For blocks permuted by the
group, the $L$-symbols are compatible with the anomaly, and the chapter derives
the fractionalization of the symmetry on the domain walls between blocks, the
exchange relation of two domain-wall strings
(Corollary~\ref{cor:mpug_domain_wall_string_exchange_printed}), and the
detection of the anomaly by the domain walls of an involution
(Lemma~\ref{thm:mpug_domain_wall_involution}) and by truncated symmetries.
