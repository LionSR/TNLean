\chapter{The Fundamental Theorem and index of matrix product unitaries}
\label{ch:mpu_fundamental_theorem}

Building on the simple blocking and canonical representatives of
Chapter~\ref{ch:mpu}, this chapter proves the simple-tensor equivalence
theorem and the Fundamental Theorem of matrix product unitaries
\cite{Cirac2017MPU}: a simple tensor is characterized by unitarity of its two
source operators, and two simple tensors in standard form that generate the
same unitaries have standard forms related by local unitary gauges, with the
converse holding on rings of even length. It then defines the index from the
ranks of the two source cuts and develops the Index Theorem: the index is
invariant under blocking, additive under tensor products and composition, and
constant along continuous paths.
