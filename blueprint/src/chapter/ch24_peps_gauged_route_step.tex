\begin{lemma}[All four elementary physical route operations]
    \label{thm:peps_gauged_route_step}
    \lean{
        TNLean.PEPS.IsGIsometric.exists_unitary_torusRouteSteps,
        TNLean.PEPS.torusRouteStepRegion,
        TNLean.PEPS.torusRouteStepAssignment
    }
    \leanok
    On a finite regular torus with periods $L_x,L_y\ge 4$ and regular $G$-isometric site tensors, there exists a family of six-spin unitaries $\{W_{v,\alpha}\}$ indexed by the starting vertex $v$ and movement axis $\alpha\in\{\mathrm{horiz},\mathrm{vert}\}$ (chosen before all gauge, insertion, exterior, and boundary data) such that for every common vertex gauge, chord insertion $g\in G$, boundary configuration $\theta$, and exterior/crossing assignment $u_{\mathrm{ext}}$, $W_{v,\alpha}$ maps the single-chord gauged insertion $u^{(0)}_g$ to the two-chord insertion $u^{(1)}_g$ and $W_{v,\alpha}^*$ maps $u^{(1)}_g\mapsto u^{(0)}_g$ on both open contractions $\Psi_{R_{v,\alpha}}(\cdot,\theta)$ and closed states $\Psi(\cdot;u_{\mathrm{ext}})$, including across both periodic seams.

    This is a finite-torus common-background consequence of SCP10,
    Theorem~6.16 and the accessible-coordinate construction (local lines
    1765--1920 and 2271--2305). Applying $\{W_{v,\alpha}\}$ along a complete endpoint route
    still requires explicit intermediate assignments; no
    four-endpoint braid or reunion protocol is asserted here
    \cite{gap:rmp_peps_quantum_double_g_isometry}.
    \uses{thm:peps_gauged_horizontal_flux_move,thm:peps_torus_gauged_vertical_flux_move}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}\leanok
    Selecting $W_{v,\mathrm{horiz}}$ from Lemma~\ref{thm:peps_gauged_horizontal_flux_move} and $W_{v,\mathrm{vert}}$ from Lemma~\ref{thm:peps_torus_gauged_vertical_flux_move} at each $v$, and applying $W_{v,\alpha}$ for forward steps (right/up) and $W_{v,\alpha}^*$ for reverse steps (left/down), yields the four directional actions.
\end{proof}
