\chapter{Auxiliary Quantum-Channel Theory}\label{ch:aux_channel_theory}

This chapter collects quantum-channel and matrix-analysis material that is
mathematically independent of the Fundamental Theorem proof chain of
Chapters~\ref{ch:mps}--\ref{ch:ft_proof}. The definitions of positive maps,
quantum channels, irreducibility, and the Kadison--Schwarz inequality that
are needed for the theorem appear in the earlier chapters; the results here
extend that theory but are not required to state or prove the theorem.

The maximally entangled state and the Choi--Jamio\l{}kowski isomorphism
connect completely positive maps to positive semidefinite matrices. The
abstract Schwarz inequality and multiplicative-domain theory express
\cite[Chapter~5]{Wolf2012Quantum} in the operator-system language.  The
exponential-positivity, ergodicity, and spectral-characterization results
complete the Perron--Frobenius programme of
\cite[Chapter~6]{Wolf2012Quantum}.
