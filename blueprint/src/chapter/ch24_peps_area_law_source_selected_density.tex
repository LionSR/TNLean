\section{Source-power density at the same charge point}
\label{sec:arealaw-source-selected-density}

\begin{lemma}[A source-small density and the unchanged sharp comparison]
    \label{thm:arealaw-source-selected-sharp-density}
    \lean{TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.CollarScan.exists_sourceTarget_selected_actualCharge_sharp_density}
    \leanok
    Fix the local dimension, interaction radius, interaction and positive
    spectral-gap bounds, a nonnegative target-volume coefficient, a positive
    scanner coefficient $C_1$, and the anchor multiplicity controlled by
    $C_1/3$.  Fix also the energy-tail exponent $P$.
    There are positive source constants $C_{\rm tr},C_{\rm target},C_B,c_t$,
    real round-energy coefficients and exponents, and cap constants
    $C\geq1$, $C_l\geq0$, chosen before every entropy coefficient $C_e\geq0$.  For each such $C_e$
    there is a size threshold before the finite physical data.

    Let $\Omega$ be the original unit gapped ground state of the given
    finite-domain local Hamiltonian.  The scanner uses the domain
    graph, anchors in their original interaction supports and ambient depth
    from the nonempty target set $T$.  Impose the source quadratic target
    size, whole-row layer counts, anchor multiplicity, collar clearance and
    rounded scales.  Retain the geometric premises
    $2r_0\leq D$ and $4D\leq m$.  Write
    $X=A\cap T$, $L=\lfloor n^{1-\ell}\rfloor$, and let $e_{\mathrm{box}}$
    be the final source box-error exponent.  Assume the original entropy bounds
    \begin{align}
        S(\Omega_X) & \leq C_en^{1+e_{\mathrm{box}}},\nonumber                \\
        S(\Omega_{\{0<\mathrm{depth}\leq j\}\cap A})
                    & \leq C_e(nL^{e_{\mathrm{box}}}+n)\qquad(0\leq j\leq L).
    \end{align}
    Then there is one truncated ground state $\widetilde\Omega$, one
    target spectral window $E$ of mass $z$, and one physical Schmidt--Bell
    label sequence before every weight $1\leq W\leq n^\omega$.
    They retain the source gap, energy, trace, cut-tail and window bounds.

    Set $a=W/K$ and retain the round-step smallness premise
    $a\,\mathrm{transportLogDimBound}(q,r_0)\leq c_t$.
    For each such fixed weight, eventually in replica number $k$ there is
    a charge point $(s,p)$ with
    \begin{align}
        p                    & \in[n^{-\nu},2n^{-\nu}], &
        \frac{Q_k(s,p)}{KnD} & \leq n^{-\nu}.
        \label{eq:arealaw-source-selected-density-power}
    \end{align}
    Here $Q_k$ is the charge entropy defect of the same
    prevector.  At this unchanged point all the previous
    energy and sharp-comparison conclusions hold.  In particular, writing
    $e_0$ for the truncated ground energy and $g/2$ for its physical gap,
    \begin{align}
        0\leq\delta_k(s,p)
         & :=\frac{\mu_k(s,p)-e_0}{g/2}
        \leq\frac{e_0+\mathrm{cap}_k(s,p)+\mathrm{rem}_k}{g/2},\nonumber \\
        \mathrm{cap}_k(s,p)
         & :=CW^2n^\ell D^2(\log n)^{C_l}
        \left((1-p)\left(\frac{Q_k(s,p)}{KnD}\right)^{1/8}
        +(1-p)n^{-P}+p\right).
    \end{align}
    The full sharp inequality keeps the same mapped terminal weights,
    label entropy, physical gap, loss $37K_{\rm mom}a$, mass term and both
    replica remainders.  Its auxiliary parameter remains universally
    quantified over $0<\tau\leq1/2$ at this same point.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
    \leanok
    \uses{thm:arealaw-source-selected-sharp-comparison,
        thm:arealaw-target-entropy-transfer,
        thm:area_law_selected_schmidt_entropy_bounds,
        thm:al_scan_transport_coefficients_log,
        thm:arealaw-selected-density-power}
    The source selected-comparison theorem provides the truncated ground state,
    spectral window, and label sequence.  The retained
    containment $X\subseteq\mathrm{truncationSet}(L)$ and its quadratic
    volume bound allow target entropy transfer at the given trace
    distance.  The original target bound therefore gives
    $S(\widetilde\Omega_X)\leq C_en^{1+e_{\mathrm{box}}}+n^{-247}$.
    The full same-$E$ eigenvalue window gives
    $|\log|E|-S(\widetilde\Omega_X)|\leq n^{3/5}-\log z$.
    The original prefix bound has already given the endpoint rough
    bound for these same labels.

    With the rounded logarithmic radius bounding the fixed round-energy
    coefficient by $C_t(\log n)^{C_l'}$, enlarging the size
    threshold accommodates this cap, the target transfer, and the numerical density
    bound.  Because $n^\nu\geq4$ places the displayed interval of width
    $n^{-\nu}$ strictly inside the interior and $aK=W$, the
    initial selected-density bound furnishes the input to the ordered-limit
    closure, which retains the energy and all sharp
    comparisons as propositions attached to the original point.
\end{proof}

The entropy coefficient and all fixed cap coefficients precede the enlarged
size threshold.  The state, spectral set and label sequence precede the
weight; each replica threshold is chosen only after the physical system
and weight are fixed.  No limit interchange or new point selection is used.
Round-step smallness, the source geometric hypotheses, final energy
absorption, the entropy telescope and the final area law remain separate.
