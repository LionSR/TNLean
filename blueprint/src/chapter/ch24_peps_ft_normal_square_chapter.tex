\chapter{The Fundamental Theorem for normal projected entangled pair states on the square lattice}
\label{ch:peps_ft_normal_square}

A normal tensor becomes injective after blocking a finite region. This chapter
begins the extension of the Fundamental Theorem for injective PEPS of
Chapter~\ref{ch:peps_ft} to normal tensors~\cite{Molnar2018NormalPEPS}.
Injective regions are closed under finite unions when every bond dimension is
positive (Lemma~\ref{lem:peps_injectiveRegion_union_pos}). On the square
lattice, injectivity of small rectangles then gives injective blockings around
every edge and every site. Two normal tensors that generate the same state and
admit such blockings have equal bond dimensions
(Lemma~\ref{thm:peps_bondDim_eq_of_normalBlocking}), and on the square lattice
they are related by horizontal and vertical gauges up to a scalar
(Theorem~\ref{thm:fundamentalTheorem_normalSquarePEPS}). On the open rectangle
this is proved in an interior-window form with one scalar per site
(Corollary~\ref{thm:fundamentalTheorem_normalSquarePEPS_unconditional}). The
torus and general connected graphs follow in the next two chapters.
