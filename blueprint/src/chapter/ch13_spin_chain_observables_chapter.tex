\chapter{Observables and states of an infinite spin chain}
\label{ch:spin_chain_observables}

The long-range spectral gaps of the next chapter and the quantum cellular
automata of Part~\ref{part:operators} are statements about the chain
$\mathbb Z$. This chapter constructs its observables and states: the matrix
algebra of each finite region, the inclusions and translations between these
algebras, their union and its norm completion, the quasi-local algebra, and the
states and pure states of the completion. Grouping consecutive sites into blocks
is a star-algebra equivalence that preserves states and purity. The
construction follows the appendix of~\cite{Cirac2017MPU}.
