\section{Automatic rounded-scale admissibility}
\label{sec:source-rounded-information}

The physical final-scale theorem has a further specialization removing its
three numerical separation hypotheses.  The rounded definitions
imply those inequalities uniformly at large size.  This specialization
retains every source geometry and target/prefix entropy hypothesis.

\begin{theorem}[The final scanner scales are admissible]
    \label{thm:final-scanner-admissibility}
    \lean{TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.exists_finalScanner_admissibility}
    \leanok
    Fix $C_r\geq0$ and the final exponents
    $\mu=1/4$, $\kappa=1/1000000$ and $\ell=1/100000$.
    There is one $N\geq2$ such that every integer $n\geq N$, with
    \[
        r_0=\lceil C_r(\log n)^2\rceil,\quad
        L=\lfloor n^{1-\ell}\rfloor,\quad
        m=\lfloor n^\mu\rfloor,\quad D=\lceil n^\kappa\rceil,\quad
        K=L/(8m),
    \]
    satisfies
    \[
        2r_0\leq D,\qquad 1\leq D,\qquad 4D\leq m,\qquad
        1\leq K,\qquad 8Km\leq L.
    \]
    The division defining $K$ is natural-number division.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
    \leanok
    \uses{thm:al_scan_status_scale_separation,
        thm:al_scan_positive_source_bands}
    The accepted logarithmic-radius estimate gives
    $2r_0\leq n^\kappa$ eventually, so the ceiling bound yields
    $2r_0\leq D$, while the accepted rounded-scale separation and positive
    band-count estimates give the remaining inequalities on a common threshold because
    $0<\kappa<\mu<1-\ell$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Conditional information from rounded scales]
    \label{thm:source-rounded-information}
    \lean{TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.CollarScan.exists_sourceTarget_finalScale_mutualInformation_of_rounded_scales}
    \leanok
    The conditional final-scale physical information theorem holds without
    separately assuming $2r_0\leq D$, $1\leq D$ or $4D\leq m$.
    Retain its original gapped ground state, floor and ceiling
    scales, scanner graph and support anchors, template-volume bound,
    ambient depth and layer bounds, anchor multiplicity, cut clearance,
    and source target/prefix entropy bounds.  Then one constant $C>0$ and
    one uniform threshold give
    \[
        2I_\Omega(X:S_0^c)\leq Cn^{62497/62500},\qquad
        2I_\Omega(X:S_0^c)\leq Cn^{1-1/100000}.
    \]
    The radius coefficient is selected before the entropy coefficient;
    the final constant and size threshold precede every physical instance.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
    \leanok
    \uses{thm:source-final-information,
        thm:final-scanner-admissibility}
    With the radius coefficient and information constant of the
    conditional final-scale theorem and the maximum of its size threshold with
    the preceding numerical threshold, substituting the exact
    rounded-scale equalities into the original theorem for the same
    physical state and scanner yields the bound.  No state, label or selected point is
    changed, and no limit is interchanged.
\end{proof}
