\chapter{Compressing a distributed circuit by random sampling}
\label{ch:party_circuit_sampling}

The compression theorem \cite[Theorem~5.2]{OpenAI2026PolynomialPEPS} samples
the sources of the bipartite resources at random and corrects the sampled
terms. With the corrected sources of
Chapter~\ref{ch:party_circuit_sampling_sources}, this chapter carries out the
sampling, bounds the expected error of the corrected density, and proves the
uniform compression of the distributed density.
