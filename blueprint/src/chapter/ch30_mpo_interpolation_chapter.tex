\chapter{Gapped Interpolations between Symmetric States}
\label{ch:mpo_interpolation}

Two matrix product states with the same matrix product operator symmetry lie in
the same phase when they are joined by a path of parent Hamiltonians with a
uniform gap~\cite{GarreRubio2023MPOSym}. This chapter constructs the mixed
interpolation between two injective endpoint tensors, shows that the symmetry
acts and fuses along it, and proves the uniform gaps on open and closed chains
that the classification uses.
