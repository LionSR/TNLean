\chapter{Asymptotic Structure of Quantum Channels}
\label{ch:channel_asymptotics}

This chapter develops the finite-dimensional mean-ergodic theorem for bounded
linear dynamics and its specialization to positive trace-preserving maps. The
resulting Ces\`aro projection onto the fixed-point space is the starting point
for the broader theory of fixed-point algebras, Choi--Effros products,
peripheral eigenspaces, and cyclic asymptotic structure.
