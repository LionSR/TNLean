\chapter{Matrix Product Operators and Density Operators}\label{ch:mpdo}

Matrix product density operators (MPDOs) and matrix product operators (MPOs)
describe one-dimensional mixed states and quantum operators with bounded virtual
entanglement~\cite{Cirac2016MPDO_arXiv,Cirac2017MPDO_AnnPhys,Cirac2021Matrix}.
For a physical dimension $d$ and a bond dimension $D$, an MPO tensor carries two
physical indices, a ket index and a bra index, together with left and right
virtual indices; Chapters~\ref{ch:mpdo_rfp} and~\ref{ch:mpdo_rfp_algebraic}
subsequently develop the renormalization fixed-point conditions and their
algebraic consequences for these tensors.
