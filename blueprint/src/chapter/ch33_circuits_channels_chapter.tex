\chapter{Circuits of local channels}
\label{ch:circuits_channels}

Circuits of channels on neighbouring pairs, with ancillas attached and
discarded site by site, obey the same light cone as unitary circuits. This
chapter treats such circuits and their realization by unitary circuits with
fresh local ancillas, in the convention of \cite{Piroli2021LOCC} where every
gate between sites acts on physical registers of fixed dimension.
