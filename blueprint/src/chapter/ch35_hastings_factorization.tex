\chapter{Hastings' approximate factorization of the ground-state projector}
\label{ch:hastings_factorization}

Hastings' proof of the one-dimensional area law~\cite{Hastings2007AreaLaw}
rests on one approximation, Lemma~1 of the source. The projector onto the
unique ground state of a gapped chain with finite-range interactions is
close, in operator norm, to a product $O_BO_LO_R$ of three contractions:
$O_L$ acts on the sites to the left of a cut, $O_R$ on the sites to its
right, and $O_B$ on the $2l$ sites around it, and the error decays
exponentially in $l$. The construction splits the Hamiltonian into a left
part, a buffer and a right part, each centred at the ground state. The
cut-offs $O_L$ and $O_R$ are spectral projections of the localized left and
right parts, and $O_B$ is a Gaussian time average, taken in the interaction
picture and localized near the cut. This chapter proves the steps in that
order, replaces $O_B$ by the positive operator $O_B^\dagger O_B$ as in the
note added to the source, fixes the decay length, and assembles the
factorization. The next chapter uses it to prove the area law.
