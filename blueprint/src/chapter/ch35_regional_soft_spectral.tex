\section{Buffered spectra of the constructed regional approximation}
\label{sec:regional_soft_spectral}

The represented Hermitian approximation can be fixed before all spectral
cutoffs and buffers. This gives the following auxiliary for the
approximating Hamiltonian in the efficient-filter construction of
Arad, Landau, Vazirani and Vidick~\cite[lines 1412--1420 and 1446--1457]{Arad2016RigorousRG}.

\begin{lemma}[One represented approximation for every buffered cutoff]
    \label{lem:regional_soft_mpo_buffered_spectra}
    \lean{TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.exists_regional_softTruncations_mpo_buffered_spectral_comparison}
    \leanok
    Assume the local-interaction, regional lower-energy, order, scale and
    accuracy hypotheses of Lemma~\ref{lem:regional_soft_hermitian_mpo},
    and retain its definitions of $G$ and $D$.
    There is one site-dependent operator chain $T$ of virtual dimension
    $D$, with identity closing matrix, such that
    \begin{align}
        G=G^*,\qquad T=T^*,\qquad\|G-T\|\leq\xi.
    \end{align}
    For every $c\in\mathbb R$ and every $w>0$, put
    \begin{align}
        U&=\|G\|+\xi,&a&=\min\{c-1,-U\},\\
        n&=\left\lceil\frac{c-a}{2\xi}\right\rceil,&
        \delta&=\frac{n(2\xi)^2}{(w+\xi)^2}.
    \end{align}
    Then the spectral space $E_T(c+w)$ is $\delta$-close to $E_G(c)$,
    and $E_G(c+w)$ is $\delta$-close to $E_T(c)$.
    The same operator chain serves every cutoff, buffer and vector.
    No gap or restriction on the spectrum between the cutoffs is needed.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}\leanok
    Construct $T$ using Lemma~\ref{lem:regional_soft_hermitian_mpo}.
    Its norm bound gives $\|T\|\leq U$, so both spectra lie above $-U$.
    Apply the two directed buffered comparisons from the companion
    matrix theory to $G$ and $T$. They use the same lower endpoint,
    window count and loss, and place no further condition on $T$.
\end{proof}

This compares the actual regional soft sum with its represented
approximation. It does not establish the comparison with the original
Hamiltonian or the final efficient-filter theorem. The construction
continues to use the corrected interaction-label cluster estimate;
the false printed physical-support estimate and its counterexamples
remain recorded in \cite{gap:alvv16_cluster_approximation}.
