\part{Parent Hamiltonians and correlations}
\label{part:hamiltonians}

Every matrix product state is the ground state of a frustration-free parent
Hamiltonian. This part constructs parent Hamiltonians, characterizes their
ground spaces, builds the observables and states of the infinite chain, bounds
their spectral gaps, and proves the exponential decay of
correlations \cite[Sections~II.C and~IV.C]{Cirac2021Matrix}.
