\chapter{The Fundamental Theorem for Normal PEPS}
\label{ch:peps_ft_normal}

A normal tensor becomes injective after blocking a finite region. This chapter
extends the Fundamental Theorem for injective PEPS of
Chapter~\ref{ch:peps_ft} to normal tensors~\cite{Molnar2018NormalPEPS}: on the
square lattice and on the torus, on connected graphs, and for site-dependent
and vertex-injective tensors, with the balancing of the residual edge
scalars. It ends with the exact contraction of the open square grid.
