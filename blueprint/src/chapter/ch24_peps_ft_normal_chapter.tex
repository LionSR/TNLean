\chapter{The Fundamental Theorem for normal projected entangled pair states}
\label{ch:peps_ft_normal}

This chapter proves the Fundamental Theorem for normal PEPS on a connected
graph~\cite{Molnar2018NormalPEPS}. Two normal tensors that generate the same
state and admit injective blockings around every edge and every site are
related by a local gauge (Theorem~\ref{thm:fundamentalTheorem_normalPEPS} and
Corollary~\ref{thm:fundamentalTheorem_normalPEPS_connected}), unique modulo
balanced edge scalars. This extends the square-lattice and torus cases of
Chapters~\ref{ch:peps_ft_normal_square} and~\ref{ch:peps_ft_normal_torus}. The
theorem specializes to normal matrix product states on closed chains
(Corollary~\ref{cor:fundamentalTheorem_normalMPS}), to site-dependent chains,
and to vertex-injective tensors
(Corollary~\ref{thm:fundamentalTheorem_normalPEPS_of_vertexInjective}). The
chapter ends with the exact contraction of the open square grid.
