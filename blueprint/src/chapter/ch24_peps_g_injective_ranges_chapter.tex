\chapter{Physical ranges and flux measurements of \texorpdfstring{$G$}{G}-injective tensors}%
\label{ch:peps_g_injective_ranges}

A $G$-injective tensor maps its invariant virtual space one-to-one onto its
physical range. This chapter compares $G$-injective tensors through these
ranges, relates them by physical maps, and gives each tensor a normalized
local form. Local operators on the invariant boundary
then give physical measurements of the flux through a cycle or a plaquette.
The last two sections intersect the local boundary spaces along a strip and
describe the ground spaces of open strips.
