\chapter{Fusion isometries of mixed-state renormalization fixed points}
\label{ch:mpdo_rfp_algebraic_fusion}

In \cite[Section~4]{Cirac2016MPDO_arXiv}, the algebraic structure of the
closed-chain operators of Chapter~\ref{ch:mpdo_rfp_algebraic} is governed by
diagonal $\chi$-matrices and by fusion isometries $U_{\alpha,\beta}$. This
chapter treats the fusion isometries. It relates transfer retracts to fusion
coisometries, derives the operator product law and the characterizations of
mixed-state renormalization fixed points through a basis of normal tensors
\cite[Theorem~4.14]{Cirac2016MPDO_arXiv}, decomposes the fixed-point density
operators into commuting factors, and compares the two orders of triple fusion,
proving that the comparison is unitary and that the changes of basis satisfy
the pentagon identity.
