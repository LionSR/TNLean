\section{Physical collar information from the selected source point}
\label{sec:source-actual-information}

The preceding source-selected comparison now bounds physical mutual
information for the original state.  The terminal entropy estimates
eliminate its selected-point-dependent terms before taking the replica
limit.  The source target and positive-prefix entropy hypotheses,
scanner geometry and round-step smallness remain explicit.
The source is \emph{A two-dimensional area law from a global spectral gap},
\texttt{08-scanner.tex}, lines 868--911 and 936--941, at revision
\texttt{adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a}.

\begin{theorem}[The scalar information expression]
    \label{thm:sharp-information-scalar}
    \lean{TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.sharpInformationBound,
        TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.two_information_le_sharpInformationBound}
    \leanok
    For scalar data define
    \[
        F(S,c,B_{\rm pre},\log z,W,\tau,\delta,A,C)
        :=\frac{4S+4c-2\log z}{W}
        +\left(\tau+\frac{\delta}{\tau}\right)
        \bigl(2(S+c)+B_{\rm pre}\bigr)+A+2c+C.
    \]
    Assume $W,\tau>0$, $\delta,S,c,B_{\rm pre}\geq0$, and
    \[
        \log d,S'\leq S+c,\qquad
        G_{\rm pre},g_{\max}\leq B_{\rm pre}.
    \]
    If the sharp comparison is
    \[
        2S'+G_{\rm pre}-G_{\rm post}
        \leq\frac{2(2\log d-\log z)}{W}
        +\tau G_{\rm pre}+2\tau\log d+A
        +\frac{\delta}{\tau}\max(0,2S'+g_{\max})+r
    \]
    and the information bridge gives
    $2I\leq2S'+G_{\rm pre}-G_{\rm post}+2c+C$, then $2I\leq F+r$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
    \leanok
    Monotonicity gives
    $\max(0,2S'+g_{\max})\leq2(S+c)+B_{\rm pre}$.
    Substitute the displayed estimates into the first, smoothing and defect
    terms.  The two smoothing terms combine into
    $\tau\bigl(2(S+c)+B_{\rm pre}\bigr)$.
    Add the unchanged information correction.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The fixed-instance replica limit]
    \label{thm:sharp-information-replica-limit}
    \lean{TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.information_le_of_eventually_sharpInformationBound}
    \leanok
    Keep all the physical and scalar data fixed.  If
    $r_k,u_k,l_k\to0$ and eventually
    \[
        2I\leq F\left(S,c,B_{\rm pre},\log z,W,\tau,
        \delta+\frac{r_k}{g/2},A,C\right)
        +\frac{2u_k}{W}+l_k,
    \]
    then $2I\leq F(S,c,B_{\rm pre},\log z,W,\tau,\delta,A,C)$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
    \leanok
    \uses{thm:sharp-information-scalar}
    The right-hand side converges by continuity of addition, multiplication
    and division by the fixed scalars.  Pass the eventual real inequality to
    the limit.  No convergence of selected stages or parameters is needed,
    since the scalar right-hand side no longer depends on them.  This is only
    the replica limit, with the physical size and weight already fixed.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Original-state information from one source-selected point]
    \label{thm:source-actual-information}
    \lean{TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.CollarScan.exists_sourceTarget_actual_mutualInformation_bound}
    \leanok
    Under the complete hypotheses of the stronger source-selected sharp
    energy theorem, enlarge its size threshold to include the uniform
    positive-prefix continuity threshold.  Retain its same truncated vector
    $\Omega_t$, typical spectral set $E$, mass $z$, selected dimension $d$ and
    physical label sequence.  Put
    \begin{align*}
        S_t         & =S_{\Omega_t}(X),                     & c & =n^{3/5}-\log z, \\
        B_{\rm sh}  & =C_e(nL^{e_{\rm box}}+n)+n^{-247},    &
        B_{\rm pre} & =2S_t+2B_{\rm sh}+6nD\log q,                                 \\
        C_{\rm MI}  & =16\sqrt{d_{\rm tr}(\Omega_t,\Omega)}
        \log(e\dim\mathcal H_X).
    \end{align*}
    For every $1\leq W\leq n^\omega$ satisfying the original round-step
    smallness with $a=W/K$, and every $0<\tau\leq1/2$, define
    \[
        A=(3+1/a)h(\tau)+37(C_0B)a-\frac{\log z}{a}.
    \]
    Then the original-state collar information satisfies
    \[
        2I_\Omega(X:S_0^c)
        \leq F(S_t,c,B_{\rm pre},\log z,W,\tau,n^{-1/2000},A,C_{\rm MI}).
    \]
    The fixed physical and round-step constants precede $C_e$; $C_e$ precedes
    the size threshold and the finite physical system.  The state, spectral
    set and labels precede $W$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
    \leanok
    \uses{thm:arealaw-source-selected-sharp-energy,
        thm:source-actual-terminal-pre-entropy,
        thm:actual-selected-collar-information,
        thm:sharp-information-scalar,
        thm:sharp-information-replica-limit,
        thm:area_law_selected_schmidt_entropy_bounds}
    Invoke the stronger source energy theorem once.  Transfer the original
    positive-prefix entropy input to its same vector.  The scanner geometry
    gives $0\leq G_{\rm pre}\leq B_{\rm pre}$ and
    $g_{\max}\leq B_{\rm pre}$, using $B_{\rm pre}\geq0$.
    The same selected spectrum gives
    $\log d,S'\leq S_t+c$ and $c\geq0$.

    For each sufficiently large replica count, use the very same selected
    stage and parameter that satisfy the sharp and stronger energy bounds.
    The parameter stays in the source consumer's interval
    $[n^{-\nu},2n^{-\nu}]$; it is never selected again.
    Apply the information bridge and the scalar substitution with
    defect bound $n^{-1/2000}+r_k/(g/2)$.  The remaining errors are precisely
    the same upper and lower sharp remainders.  With the physical system,
    $W$ and $\tau$ fixed, send all three remainders to zero.
\end{proof}

The final size-power estimate and its geometric application are separate.
In particular, the source target and positive-prefix inputs are still
hypotheses; no safe-box entropy theorem, Proposition 9.6 or full area law is
claimed by this result.
