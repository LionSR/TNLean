\chapter{Repairing the regions}%
\label{ch:peps_area_law_repair}

Near the marked points of the cells, the initial regions may meet in ways
that no collar separates. They are repaired recursively
\cite[Section~11]{OpenAI2026AreaLaw}: each step removes a small square about
every current mark from all existing regions and adjoins replacement regions
cut from a fan, which may create new marks at a smaller scale. This chapter
defines the repair, proves by a uniform induction that the marks die out after
finitely many steps, separates each new region from the older ones, and gives
explicit thresholds for the constants of the two-family decomposition.
