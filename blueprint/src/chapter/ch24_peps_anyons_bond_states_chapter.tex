\chapter{Semi-regular bond states and Fourier coordinates}
\label{ch:peps_anyons_bond_states}

The excitations of a quantum double model are anyons: fluxes on plaquettes,
charges on vertices, and their composites~\cite{Kitaev2003FaultTolerant}. In a
$G$-injective PEPS they are created by inserting group elements and
representations on the bonds~\cite[Section~III.B]{Cirac2021Matrix}. This
chapter treats the semi-regular bond states and the Fourier coordinates in
which the regular representation splits into irreducible blocks; on the torus
and on finite graphs, the state built from a semi-regular representation in
which each irreducible occurs once is related to the regular state by
isometries on the physical bonds. The next two chapters use these coordinates
to create and move fluxes and charges.
