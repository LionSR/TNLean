\chapter{Ground spaces and reduced densities of PEPS regions}%
\label{ch:peps_regions}

Cutting a PEPS on a finite graph along the boundary of a region $R$ writes the
state as a sum over the bond configurations on the crossing edges of products
of an open contraction on $R$ and one on its complement. The open contractions
on $R$ span the local ground space $\mathcal G_R$, and positive operators whose
kernel is $\mathcal G_R$ assemble into a frustration-free parent Hamiltonian
of the state. The same cut writes the reduced density $\rho_R$ as $M_RM_R^\dagger$
for the coefficient matrix $M_R$ across the cut. For regular $G$-injective
tensors of Chapter~\ref{ch:peps_quantum_double} and a cut whose two sides are
connected, the range of $\rho_R$ is $\mathcal G_R$. A cut with $n+1$ crossing
bonds then has entropy at most $n\log|G|$, and the regular virtual boundary
state has deficient rank, so it is not the exponential of a Hamiltonian on the
full boundary space.
