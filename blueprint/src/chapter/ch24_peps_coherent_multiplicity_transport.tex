\begin{theorem}[Coherent multiplicity restoration]
    \label{thm:peps_coherent_multiplicity_transport}
    \lean{TNLean.PEPS.coherentWeightedBlocks_support_restore,
        TNLean.PEPS.exists_isometric_coherentWeightedBlocks}
    \leanok
    \uses{lem:peps_matching_sector_bond_inclusion,
        thm:peps_full_multiplicity_bond_map,
        thm:peps_full_multiplicity_bond_isometry_extension,
        lem:peps_physical_coherent_transport,lem:peps_bond_coordinate_transport}
    Let $d_i>0$ and let $M(a)=\bigoplus_i\sqrt{d_i}\,D_i(a)$ be a
    family of matrices. Every finite coherent sum of products of these
    matrices belongs to the product of matching-sector bond spaces.
    The multiplicity-restoring map sends it to the same coherent sum
    with bond matrices $\bigoplus_i D_i(a)\otimes I_{d_i}$.
    If an isometric coordinate change identifies these repeated blocks
    with another matrix family, one fixed full-domain product isometry
    gives the corresponding identity for every choice of coefficients
    and bond labels, and preserves all inner products.
    These are auxiliary matrix statements for the construction in
    Section~7 of \cite{Schuch2010PEPS}; an actual contraction formula is still
    required when applying them to a tensor-network state.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}\leanok
    Embed the weighted block entries into matching-sector endpoint pairs.
    The normalized multiplicity map cancels the factor $\sqrt{d_i}$ on
    each bond. Linearity and the product formula prove the coherent identity.
    Extend the supported bond maps to full-domain isometries and then apply
    the isometric coordinate change on every bond.
\end{proof}
