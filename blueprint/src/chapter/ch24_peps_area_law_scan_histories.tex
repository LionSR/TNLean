\section{Finite collar histories and good-history sampling}
\label{sec:al_finite_scan_histories}

Fix a finite domain $\Lambda\subset\mathbb Z^2$, a color $A\subset\Lambda$,
and a nonempty finite target $T\subset\mathbb Z^2$. Write
$d(x)=\min_{t\in T}\|x-t\|_\infty$ and $T_j=T+[-j,j]^2_{\mathbb Z}$.
The graph metric is that of the induced nearest-neighbor graph on $\Lambda$.
The following finite construction follows
\cite[Section~9, moving partitions]{OpenAI2026AreaLaw}.

\begin{definition}[Physical partitions and padded fill slots]
    \label{def:al_scan_physical_partition}
    \lean{TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.PhysicalPartition,
        TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.middle,
        TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.receiving,
        TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.assign,
        TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.initialPartition,
        TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.orientedDepth,
        TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.initialFront,
        TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.fillCount,
        TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.fillSide,
        TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.nominalFront,
        TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.depthRow,
        TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.orientedRow,
        TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.fillSlot,
        TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.fill,
        TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.charge}
    \leanok
    A physical partition assigns each site to the near side $P_0$, the middle
    $Y$, or the far side $V$. For cutoffs $u<v$, initially
    $P_0=\{x\in A:d(x)\le u\}$ and
    $V=(\Lambda\setminus A)\cup\{x\in A:d(x)>v\}$.
    Order each positive-depth row once and pad it to $n$ slots. Sites outside
    $A$ and absent entries are blank. The far side visits rows in decreasing
    depth, using the same fixed order within each row.
    Fills alternate near, far, starting near. After $k$ fills the two consumed
    slot counts are $t_P=\lfloor(k+1)/2\rfloor$ and $t_F=\lfloor k/2\rfloor$;
    the oriented fronts are
    $j_P=u+1+\lfloor t_P/n\rfloor$ and $j_F=-v+\lfloor t_F/n\rfloor$.
    A move of a set $B$ transfers only $B\cap Y$ to its receiving side.
    A charge transfers this set only if $B$ meets both that side and $Y$.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Consumed slots and the actual middle]
    \label{thm:al_scan_consumed_slots}
    \lean{TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.assign_eq_none,
        TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.middle_assign_subset,
        TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.middle_sdiff_middle_assign,

        TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.fill_blank,
        TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.fill_assigned,
        TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.charge_moves_middle_part,
        TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.exists_fillSlot,
        TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.fill_at_side_index,
        TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.side_index_lt,
        TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.initial_middle_depth,
        TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.nominalFront_le_of_unconsumed}
    \leanok
    \uses{def:al_scan_physical_partition}
    A blank or already assigned fill slot makes no physical move but increases
    its receiving side's consumed count by one. Assignments never change sides,
    and the middle only decreases. For a selected set $B$, the removed middle
    is exactly $B\cap Y$. If all rows have at most $n$ sites, a site remaining
    in the middle after $k$ fills has oriented depth at least the corresponding
    nominal front.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}\leanok
    If a site's row is completed, its fixed index is less than $n$, so it occurs
    in a consumed slot. A site initially outside the middle never enters it,
    and consuming its slot removes it from the middle regardless of earlier
    charges. Near slot $t$ is consumed at fill $2t$, far slot $t$ at fill $2t+1$.
\end{proof}

    For each band $0\le g<K$, choose an independent uniform offset
    $r_g\in\{0,\ldots,m-1\}$ and set
    $u_g=8gm+m+r_g$, $v_g=8gm+5m+r_g$.

    uniform slot among $M$ fixed slots. A history after $k$ fill--charge pairs
    consists of these $K$ offsets and $kK$ side--slot choices.

    $(2M)^{-K}$. Evaluate each history recursively: first perform the next
    prescribed fill, then the chosen charge at the resulting front.
    A nonblank labelled slot selects its graph $r_0$-ball; a blank slot makes
    no move.
\end{definition}

    was initially unassigned and avoids every consumed fill slot. Apply
    Theorem~\ref{thm:al_scan_consumed_slots}.
\end{proof}

    shortest graph walk. If a ball meeting $A\cap T_L$ leaves $A$, a path
    of length at most $2r_0$ within the two radii crosses an edge with an
    endpoint of depth at most $L+2r_0$, contradicting the clearance.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual ball sampling on a good history]
    \label{thm:al_scan_good_ball_sampling}
    \lean{TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.CollarScan.IsGoodOldHistory,
        TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.CollarScan.good_split_anchor_bounds,
        TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.CollarScan.existsUnique_selected_of_bounds,
        TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.CollarScan.selected_split_sampling,
        TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.CollarScan.oldChargeState_depth_bounds,
        TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.CollarScan.good_split_sampling_domainGraph}
    \leanok
    \uses{thm:al_scan_history_weights,thm:al_scan_ambient_rows,thm:al_scan_charge_capacity}
    Retain the capacity and clearance assumptions, and suppose $2r_0\le D$.
    An old charge history is good when every assigned site \emph{of $A$}
    has oriented depth at most $j+D/2$ on its receiving side.
    For every labelled graph $r_0$-ball meeting that side and the actual middle,
    the anchor lies in $[j-r_0,j+D]$. Its labelled slot is unique.
    Choosing this side and slot has probability exactly $1/(2M)$, at least
    $c/(nD)$ for $c=1/[2(C_1+1)]>0$, and the extended physical history removes
    exactly the ball's old $Y$-part from $Y$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}\leanok
    An unassigned site has oriented depth at least $j$, so the anchor has depth
    at least $j-r_0$. The ball lies in $A$ by clearance; an assigned site of
    the ball therefore has depth at most $j+D/2$, giving anchor depth at most
    $j+D/2+r_0\le j+D$. The unique-slot and classical marginal formulas give
    the probability. The actual charge transition transfers precisely the
    selected ball intersected with the old middle.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Expected cost of the actual charge moves]
    \label{thm:al_scan_expected_move_cost}
    \lean{TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.CollarScan.splitIncidences,
        TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.CollarScan.mem_splitIncidences,
        TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.CollarScan.chargeMoveCost,
        TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.CollarScan.splitIncidenceCost,
        TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.CollarScan.splitIncidenceCost_band_le_expected_chargeMoveCost,
        TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.CollarScan.splitIncidenceCost_le_expected_chargeMoveCost,
        TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.CollarScan.good_splitIncidenceCost_le_expected_chargeMoveCost_domainGraph}
    \leanok
    \uses{thm:al_scan_good_ball_sampling}
    Under the good-history sampling assumptions, let $F_{g,s}(B)\ge0$ be a
    nonnegative cost of a finite set of physical sites moved toward side $s$
    in band $g$.
    Sum $F_{g,s}(B_i\cap Y_g)$ over every splitting incidence $(g,s,i)$,
    where $s$ is the receiving side and $B_i$ is the labelled charge ball.
    If $\Delta_g(c)$ is the actual set removed from $Y_g$ by the simultaneous
    charge choice $c$ and $s_g(c)$ its selected side, then
    \begin{align}
        \frac{1}{2M}\sum_{(g,s,i)\ \mathrm{split}} F_{g,s}(B_i\cap Y_g)
         & \le \sum_c q_c\sum_g F_{g,s_g(c)}(\Delta_g(c)).
        \label{eq:al_scan_expected_move_cost}
    \end{align}
    For $C_1>0$, the left coefficient may be replaced by
    $1/[2(C_1+1)nD]$. Labels sharing an anchor retain separate summands.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}\leanok
    For each band and realized side--slot pair, at most one labelled incidence
    is selected. Its cost is the cost of the actual removed middle set.
    All other event contributions are zero, and the realized cost is
    nonnegative. Sum this pointwise inequality against the classical choice
    weights, use the $1/(2M)$ marginal for each available labelled slot,
    and interchange the finite sums over choices and bands.
\end{proof}

        j-r_0\le d_a\le j+D+2r_0.

    a selected charge anchor is at most $j+D$, and its ball adds at most $r_0$.

    rows, giving the lower split-anchor bound. An assigned site in the ball gives
    the upper bound. The middle remains in the original compact collar, so cut
    clearance places the entire ball in $A$; ambient depth variation follows
    from induced graph distance. These conclusions hold for all finite history
    lengths, hence throughout the manuscript's finite scan horizon.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Finite uniform-offset counting]
    \label{thm:al_scan_offset_count}
    \lean{TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.sum_historyWeight_offset_mem,
        TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.sum_historyWeight_mul_eq_sum_extendHistory,
        TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.CollarScan.front_injective_offset,
        TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.CollarScan.card_front_interval_le,
        TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.CollarScan.sum_historyWeight_event_le,
        TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.CollarScan.sum_historyWeight_exists_band_side_le,
        TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.CollarScan.historyWeight_mixture_event_le,
        TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.CollarScan.split_interval_band_unique}
    \leanok
    \uses{def:al_scan_actual_histories,thm:al_scan_history_weights}
    At fixed band, side and front time, at most $a+b+1$ offsets place a fixed
    depth in $[j-a,j+b]$. Every history event contained in that interval event
    therefore has probability at most $(a+b+1)/m$, regardless of its dependence
    on later choices. If $a+b\le m$, the centers' spacing $8m$ allows at most
    one band on each side to reach the fixed depth, even across different offsets.
    The union over both sides and all bands has probability at most
    $2(a+b+1)/m$. Common convex mixtures preserve the bound.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}\leanok
    The front is affine with coefficient $1$ or $-1$ in its offset. Inject it
    into the integer interval $[d-b,d+a]$. The exact marginal of an offset set
    is its cardinality divided by $m$. Reindexing a new history into its old
    prefix and last charge gives exactly the leaf weights $w_hq_c$; this
    identity holds for every real-valued function of the new history. For a fixed side the possible band is
    deterministic, because two different band centers cannot both be reached
    after allowing all offsets. Sum the two side bounds. This uses marginal
    domination, not independence of a state-dependent splitting event.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Physical cut boundaries and changed sites]
    \label{thm:al_physical_cut_calculus}
    \lean{TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.mem_edgeBoundary_pair_iff,
        TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.edgeBoundary_compl,
        TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.edgeBoundary_union_subset,
        TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.edgeBoundary_sdiff_subset,
        TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.card_edgeBoundary_union_le,
        TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.card_edgeBoundary_sdiff_le,
        TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.card_edgeBoundary_le_of_endpoint_cover,
        TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.card_edgeBoundary_le_four_mul_card,
        TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.edgeBoundary_subset_union_symmDiff,
        TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.card_edgeBoundary_le_add_four_mul_symmDiff}
    \leanok
    \uses{def:area_law_hamiltonian}
    Let $\Lambda\subset\mathbb Z^2$ be finite and let $B,C\subseteq\Lambda$.
    An unordered pair belongs to $\partial_\Lambda B$ exactly when its
    sites are adjacent in the induced graph and exactly one belongs to $B$.
    Complements are relative to $\Lambda$, and
    \begin{align}
        \partial_\Lambda(B^c)          & = \partial_\Lambda B, \\
        \partial_\Lambda(B\cup C)      & \subseteq
        \partial_\Lambda B\cup\partial_\Lambda C,              \\
        \partial_\Lambda(B\setminus C) & \subseteq
        \partial_\Lambda B\cup\partial_\Lambda C,              \\
        \partial_\Lambda B             & \subseteq
        \partial_\Lambda C\cup\partial_\Lambda(B\mathbin{\triangle} C).
    \end{align}
    Consequently the boundary cardinalities of $B\cup C$ and
    $B\setminus C$ are at most
    $|\partial_\Lambda B|+|\partial_\Lambda C|$.
    If $R\subseteq\Lambda$ contains at least one site of each edge of
    $\partial_\Lambda B$, then $|\partial_\Lambda B|\le4|R|$.
    In particular,
    \begin{align}
        |\partial_\Lambda B| & \le4|B|, \\
        |\partial_\Lambda B| & \le
        |\partial_\Lambda C|+4|B\mathbin{\triangle} C|.
    \end{align}
    The last estimate is the changed-site bound in the proof of
    \cite[Lemma~9.1(2)]{OpenAI2026AreaLaw}.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}\leanok
    \uses{lem:area_law_ambient_boundary_crossing}
    Check membership of the two sites of each physical edge to obtain
    the three inclusions and complement equality. Inclusion of the domain
    into the lattice is injective on unordered pairs. Cover the image of
    the crossing edges by the at most four incident lattice edges at each
    site of $R$, giving $|\partial_\Lambda B|\le4|R|$.
    Taking $R=B$ gives the first cardinality estimate. Apply the same
    estimate to $B\mathbin{\triangle} C$ and count the union in the last
    inclusion to obtain the changed-site bound.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Physical boundaries of depth prefixes]
    \label{thm:al_scan_prefix_boundary}
    \lean{TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.disjoint_ambientDilation_boundaryEndpoints_of_clearance,
        TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.card_edgeBoundary_depthPrefix_le,
        TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.positiveDepthPrefix_eq_sdiff,
        TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.card_edgeBoundary_positiveDepthPrefix_le}
    \leanok
    \uses{thm:al_scan_ambient_rows,def:area_law_cell_counts}
    Let $\Lambda,T\subset\mathbb Z^2$ be finite, let $A\subseteq\Lambda$,
    and let $Z$ consist of both sites of every edge of $\partial_\Lambda A$.
    Fix integers $L\ge1$ and $n,r_0\ge0$, and suppose
    $\|t-z\|_\infty>2L+10r_0$ for every $t\in T$ and $z\in Z$.
    Every ambient dilation $T_j$ with $0\le j\le L$ is disjoint from $Z$.
    Suppose further that $T$ is nonempty and that
    $|T_d\setminus T_{d-1}|\le n$ for each integer $1\le d\le L$.
    Set $\delta(x)=\min_{t\in T}\|x-t\|_\infty$.
    For every integer $j\le L$, define
    $P_j=\{x\in A:\delta(x)\le j\}$ and
    $Q_j=\{x\in A:0<\delta(x)\le j\}$. Then
    \begin{align}
        Q_j                    & = P_j\setminus P_0, \\
        |\partial_\Lambda P_j| & \le4n,              \\
        |\partial_\Lambda Q_j| & \le8n.
    \end{align}
    The set identity holds for every integer-valued function $\delta$.
    These are the full-row edge estimates used in
    \cite[Lemma~9.1(2)]{OpenAI2026AreaLaw}.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}\leanok
    \uses{thm:area_law_physical_ambient_boundary,
        lem:area_law_ambient_boundary_layers,thm:al_physical_cut_calculus}
    A point of $T_j\cap Z$ would have sup-norm distance at most
    $j\le L$ from $T$, contradicting the clearance.
    For $j\ge0$, comparison with the ambient boundary gives
    $|\partial_\Lambda P_j|\le|\partial T_j|$.
    When $1\le j\le L$, each edge leaving $T_j$ has its inside site in
    $T_j\setminus T_{j-1}$; at $j=0$, use its outside site in
    $T_1\setminus T$. In either case the relevant row has at most $n$
    sites, so its incident edges give the bound $4n$.
    For $j<0$, nonnegativity of $\delta$ makes $P_j$ empty.
    Finally $0<\delta(x)$ is equivalent to $\delta(x)\not\le0$, so
    $Q_j=P_j\setminus P_0$. Subadditivity under set difference bounds
    its boundary by $4n+4n=8n$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Entropy of a valid middle-to-side move]
    \label{thm:al_scan_regional_move_entropy}

    let $\theta$ be a pure vector. With regional entropies of its reduced

    \begin{align}
        \eta_P(x;\theta)
         & = I_\theta(x:F\mid P)           \\
         & = S_\theta(x\cup P)-S_\theta(P)
        +S_\theta(Y)-S_\theta(Y\setminus x)\ge0.
    \end{align}

    inequalities require no normalization of $\theta$.

\begin{proof}\leanok

    so regional strong subadditivity gives nonnegativity. Purity identifies
    the entropy of $F\cup P$ with that of $Y$, and the entropy of
    $x\cup F\cup P$ with that of $Y\setminus x$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Entropy and energy transport in an actual scan round]
    \label{thm:al_scan_actual_transport_estimates}
    \lean{TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.CollarScan.actualFill_transport_domainGraph,
        TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.CollarScan.actualCharge_transport_domainGraph}

        def:al_scan_actual_energy_terms,def:al_scan_physical_dimension_cap}

    for an actual charge round. In each case there exist universal real
    constants $c_0,C_{\mathrm{ent}},e_{\mathrm{ent}},C_{\mathrm{en}},e_{\mathrm{en}}$,
    with $c_0>0$, chosen before all the data below.

    Let $\Lambda,T\subset\mathbb Z^2$ be finite with $T\ne\varnothing$,
    let $q,R_{\mathrm{int}}\in\mathbb N$ with $q\ge1$,
    and let $J\in\mathbb R$. Take a local Hamiltonian
    $H=\sum_i h_i$ of range $R_{\mathrm{int}}$ and term norm bound $J$
    on $(\mathbb C^q)^{\otimes\Lambda}$, a vector $\Omega$ with
    $\|\Omega\|=1$, and $\Delta>0$.
    The labels $i$ are the admissible interaction supports, with a fixed
    linear order. Let the scanner have color $A\subseteq\Lambda$,
    parameters $n,m,K,D,r_0\in\mathbb N$ and $C_1\in\mathbb R$,
    the induced nearest-neighbor graph on $\Lambda$, depth
    $d(x)=\min_{t\in T}\|x-t\|_\infty$, and anchors $x_i\in i$.
    Fix a stage $s\in\mathbb N$ and a truncation depth $L\in\mathbb N$
    satisfying
    \begin{align}
        0<n,\qquad s+1\le nm,\qquad r_0\le D,
         & \qquad 1\le D, \\
        4D\le m,\qquad 8Km\le L,
         & \qquad 0<C_1.
        \label{eq:al_scan_transport_scales}
    \end{align}
    Write $T_j=T+[-j,j]^2_{\mathbb Z}$ and let $Z$ consist of both
    endpoints of every edge crossing $A$ in $\Lambda$.
    For every integer $1\le j\le L$ assume
    $|T_j\setminus T_{j-1}|\le n$, and for every $t\in T$ and $z\in Z$
    assume $2L+10r_0<\|t-z\|_\infty$.
    Choose arbitrary independent integers $d_C,d_R\ge1$ for the auxiliary
    factors and put
    \begin{align}
        \mathcal V & = (\mathbb C^q)^{\otimes\Lambda}
        \otimes\mathbb C^{d_C}\otimes\mathbb C^{d_R}, \\
        \ell       & =1+(2r_0+1)^2\log q.
    \end{align}
    Let $a>0$ satisfy $a\ell\le c_0$.

    Use the actual recursive history tree at stage $s$. For the fill
    assertion use its scheduled moves and singleton conditional choices;
    for the charge assertion use its actual pre-charge partitions,
    selected-side moves, and uniform side--slot choice trees.
    In both cases set $t=a/2$ in the band metrics.
    Let $\widetilde h_i$ and $\widetilde X_i$ be exactly the truncated
    terms and designated supports of
    Definition~\ref{def:al_scan_actual_energy_terms} for $H,\Omega,\Delta,L$.
    The energy terms and their replica sum are
    \begin{align}
        E_i          & =\widetilde h_i\otimes I_C\otimes I_R,
                     & D_i                                    & =\widetilde X_i\subset\Lambda, \\
        \widetilde H & =\sum_i\widetilde h_i,
                     & \overline H_r                          & =\frac1r\sum_{b=0}^{r-1}
        (\widetilde H\otimes I_C\otimes I_R)_b.
        \label{eq:al_scan_transport_energy}
    \end{align}
    Here the subscript $b$ means insertion in copy $b$, and
    $\overline H_0=0$.

    There exist sequences $\beta,\varepsilon:\mathbb N\to\mathbb R$,
    depending only on the fixed data above, such that
    $\beta_r=O(\log(r+1))$ and $\varepsilon_r\to0$ as $r\to\infty$,
    with the following properties for every $r\in\mathbb N$ and every
    supplied nonzero symmetric vector
    $\mathrm{pre}\in\operatorname{Sym}^r(\mathcal V)$.
    Write $\mathcal M_r(p)$ for the actual interpolating root and set
    \begin{align}
        N_{r,\mathrm{pre}}(p)^2
         & =\|\mathcal M_r(p)^{-1/4}\mathrm{pre}\|^2, \\
        v_{r,\mathrm{pre}}(p)
         & =\frac{\mathcal M_r(p)^{-1/4}\mathrm{pre}}
        {N_{r,\mathrm{pre}}(p)}.
        \label{eq:al_scan_transport_vector}
    \end{align}
    Here $N_{r,\mathrm{pre}}(p)$ is the nonnegative square root.
    Use the history weights $w_h$, conditional weights $q_{c\mid h}$,
    terminal weights $\pi_j(p)$, transported states $\sigma_{j,u}(p)$,
    coherent measures $\mu_\sigma$, and Fourier weight $m_{1/4}$ of
    \cite[Proposition~7.4]{OpenAI2026AreaLaw} for this actual round and
    the vector in~(\ref{eq:al_scan_transport_vector}).
    Let $C_h=A_h^{-1/2}B_hA_h^{-1/2}$, where $A_h$ is the old metric
    and $B_h$ the conditional choice-tree root. Let $\mathcal J_i$ be
    the terminal leaves splitting $D_i$, and set
    \begin{align}
        \mathcal L_{r,\mathrm{pre}}(p)
               & =\sum_h w_h\int_{\mathbb R}m_{1/4}(u)
        \operatorname{Re}\operatorname{tr}
        (\sigma_{(h,\mathrm{old}),u}(p)\log C_h)\,du,          \\
        \mathcal G_{r,\mathrm{pre}}(p)
               & =\sum_{h,g}w_h\int_{\mathbb R}m_{1/4}(u)
        \int\sum_c q_{c\mid h}\eta_{h,c,g}(\theta)
        \,d\mu_{\sigma_{(h,\mathrm{old}),u}(p)}(\theta)\,du,   \\
        W_i(p) & =\sum_{j\in\mathcal J_i}\pi_j(p),             \\
        \mathcal E_{r,\mathrm{pre}}(p)
               & =\sum_i W_i(p)\sum_{j\in\mathcal J_i}\pi_j(p)
        \int_{\mathbb R}m_{1/4}(u)
        \int\eta_{i,j}(\theta)^{1/8}
        \,d\mu_{\sigma_{j,u}(p)}(\theta)\,du.
    \end{align}
    Here $\eta_{h,c,g}$ is the entropy of the actual move and
    $\eta_{i,j}$ is the entropy of the designated support's unique split.
    Then the following four conclusions hold.
    \begin{enumerate}
        \item For every $0<p<1$, the function
              $-\log N_{r,\mathrm{pre}}(p)^2$ is differentiable at $p$, with
              \begin{align}
                  -\frac{d}{dp}\log N_{r,\mathrm{pre}}(p)^2
                   & =\mathcal L_{r,\mathrm{pre}}(p).
              \end{align}
        \item For every $0<p<1$,
              \begin{align}
                  ra\mathcal G_{r,\mathrm{pre}}(p)
                  -C_{\mathrm{ent}}raK a^{1/4}\ell^{e_{\mathrm{ent}}}
                  -\beta_r
                   & \le\mathcal L_{r,\mathrm{pre}}(p).
              \end{align}
        \item For every $0<p<1$ and $E_0\in\mathbb R$ satisfying
              $\overline H_r\mathrm{pre}=E_0\mathrm{pre}$,
              \begin{align}
                  \operatorname{Re}\langle v_{r,\mathrm{pre}}(p),
                  \overline H_r v_{r,\mathrm{pre}}(p)\rangle
                   & \le 2E_0+C_{\mathrm{en}}a^2\ell^{e_{\mathrm{en}}}
                  \mathcal E_{r,\mathrm{pre}}(p)+\varepsilon_r.
              \end{align}
        \item For every $0\le p_0\le p_1\le1$,
              \begin{align}
                  \int_{p_0}^{p_1}\left(
                  ra\mathcal G_{r,\mathrm{pre}}(p)
                  -C_{\mathrm{ent}}raK a^{1/4}\ell^{e_{\mathrm{ent}}}
                  -\beta_r\right)\,dp
                   & \le\log N_{r,\mathrm{pre}}(p_0)^2
                  -\log N_{r,\mathrm{pre}}(p_1)^2.
              \end{align}
    \end{enumerate}
    The sequences are chosen before $r,\mathrm{pre},p,p_0,p_1,E_0$.
    The eigenvector equation is required only in the third conclusion.
\end{theorem}

        thm:al_scan_augmented_supports,thm:al_scan_nested_band_metrics,
        thm:al_scan_physical_subsystem_dimension,
        thm:al_scan_actual_transport_dimension}
    Take the universal constants from
    \cite[Proposition~7.4]{OpenAI2026AreaLaw} before fixing the physical
    data. By~(\ref{eq:al_scan_transport_scales}), $m\ge4D\ge4$ and
    $M=\lceil C_1nD\rceil>0$; hence the actual fill and charge trees are

    \begin{align}
        1                     & \le\ell,                                   \\
        \log(e\dim x_{h,c,g}) & \le\ell,                                   \\
        \mathcal J_i\ne\varnothing
                              & \ \Longrightarrow\ \log(e\dim D_i)\le\ell.
    \end{align}
    Neither auxiliary factor belongs to a moved subsystem or a designated

    trees and terms, with $a\ell\le c_0$, obtaining $\beta,\varepsilon$
    before the replica count and supplied vector, including its integral
    conclusion on closed subintervals of $[0,1]$.
    Finally, linearity of the copy mean gives
    \begin{align}
        \sum_i\frac1r\sum_{b=0}^{r-1}(E_i)_b
         & =\frac1r\sum_{b=0}^{r-1}
        \left(\left(\sum_i\widetilde h_i\right)
