\chapter{Lieb--Robinson bounds on finite graphs}
\label{ch:circuits_lieb_robinson}

Time evolution under a local Hamiltonian is not a circuit, but Lieb--Robinson
bounds control how fast it spreads. This chapter proves such bounds on finite
graphs, compares an evolution with the evolution under a supported part of the
Hamiltonian, and bounds the change of observables when a physical site is
replaced; the area-law chapters use these estimates.
