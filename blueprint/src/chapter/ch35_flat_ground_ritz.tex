\section{Exact extraction in the flat spectral branch}

\begin{theorem}[Exact Ritz extraction from the same root]
    \label{thm:flat_ground_same_root_ritz}
    \lean{TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.flat_ground_same_root_ritz_exact}
    \leanok
    Let $H$ be a Hermitian matrix on a finite chain of $L$ sites and
    local dimension $q$. Suppose $\mu\in\sigma(H)$, $\gamma>0$ and
    every $z\in\sigma(H)$ satisfies $z=\mu$ or $z\ge\mu+\gamma$.
    Assume there is no $\nu\in\sigma(H)$ with $\mu<\nu$.
    Let $G$ be the full spectral space of energies at most $\mu$,
    and write $r=\dim G$.

    Let $S_0$ be a specified subspace in full-interval coordinates,
    with $\dim S_0\le M$. Suppose its native image is $\delta$-close
    to $G$ for some $\delta<1$. For any real threshold $\xi$, put
    \begin{align}
      K_{\mathrm{raw}}&=I-\|H\|^{-1}H,&
      S&=\operatorname{FilterTrimIterate}(K_{\mathrm{raw}},\xi,S_0,0),\\
      W&=\operatorname{native}(S),&
      R&=\operatorname{Ritz}_r(H|_W).
    \end{align}
    Then $G$ is the whole native Hilbert space, $S_0$ is the whole
    full-interval space, $S=S_0$ and $R=W=G$. In particular
    $\dim R=r\le M$, both projection errors between $R$ and $G$ are
    zero, and every vector of this actual Ritz space has matrix product
    bond dimension at most $r$, hence at most $M$.

    This is the flat branch of a gap-implication-only extension of the
    final extraction in
    \cite[Figure~\texttt{fig:algo1}, lines 1641--1643 and 1814--1817]{Arad2016RigorousRG}.
    No filter is applied in this branch; the raw filter can vanish for
    a scalar Hamiltonian. No independent approximation replaces $S_0$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}\leanok
    \uses{lem:full_interval_viability,thm:chain_cut_rank_of_mps}
    The spectral alternative and absence of an excited value force every
    eigenvalue to equal $\mu$, so $G$ is the whole space. Closeness with
    loss below one gives $\dim G\le\dim\operatorname{native}(S_0)$.
    Equality with the ambient dimension follows, forcing both native
    and interval versions of the given root to be whole spaces.

    The zero-th iterate is exactly $S_0$. The actual Ritz space lies in
    $W$ and has dimension $r=\dim W$, hence equals $W$. Both errors
    therefore vanish. At every internal cut, a vector's cut matrix has
    rank at most the number of rows, which is at most $q^L=r\le M$.
    The cut-rank characterization gives the two bond bounds. If there
    is an internal cut, spectral membership supplies a configuration
    and therefore $q\ge1$; an empty chain has no internal cuts.
\end{proof}
