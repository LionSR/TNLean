\section{Preparation with measurements}\label{sec:ldp_measurements}

Measurements followed by corrections chosen from their outcomes shorten
state preparation \cite{Malz2024Preparation}. The model is that of
\cite{Piroli2021LOCC}: a local circuit, measurements, and local unitaries
depending on the outcomes of all the measurements, which are communicated to
every site at no cost. Only the layers of the circuits are counted. In this
model the states $\ket{\chi_M}=\sum_{i=1}^b\alpha_i\ket{i}^{\otimes M}$ of
Definition~\ref{def:ldp_pair_family_fixed_point} are prepared in a depth
independent of $M$, although their connected correlations do not decay with
distance, while by Lemma~\ref{lem:ldp_light_cone_correlations} those of a
state prepared by a local circuit of depth $T$ vanish beyond distance $2T$.

\begin{definition}[Preparation with measurements]
    \label{def:ldp_measurement_preparation}
    \lean{MPSPreparation.outcomeProj, MPSPreparation.MeasurementProtocol,
        MPSPreparation.MeasurementProtocol.preMeasurement,
        MPSPreparation.MeasurementProtocol.postMeasurement,
        MPSPreparation.MeasurementProtocol.output,
        MPSPreparation.MeasurementProtocol.Prepares,
        MPSPreparation.IsPreparedWithMeasurementsInDepth}
    \leanok
    \uses{def:ldp_local_circuit}
    For a set $S$ of sites of the ring of $N$ sites and a string
    $m\colon S\to\{1,\dots,d\}$, let $P_m$ be the projection onto the span of
    the basis vectors $\ket{x}$ with $x_i=m_i$ for every $i\in S$; these are
    the outcome projections of a measurement of the sites of $S$ in the
    computational basis. A \emph{protocol} consists of a product vector
    $\ket{\pi}$, a local circuit $U$ of depth $T_1$, a set $S$ of measured
    sites, and for every $m$ a local circuit $V_m$ of depth $T_2(m)$. It
    \emph{prepares} a vector $\ket{\psi}$ if $\ket{\pi}\neq0$ and for every
    $m$ with $P_mU\ket{\pi}\neq0$ there is $c\in\mathbb C$ with
    $V_mP_mU\ket{\pi}=c\ket{\psi}$. A vector is \emph{prepared with
        measurements in depth $T$} if a protocol with $T_1+T_2(m)\leq T$ for
    every $m$ prepares it.

    This is the class of preparations of \cite{Piroli2021LOCC}, where it is
    called $\mathrm{QCcc}$, and of the paragraph ``Preparations using
    measurements'' of \cite{Malz2024Preparation}. The correction $V_m$ may
    depend on the whole string $m$: classical communication is global and
    free. Ancillas are sites of the ring, and the local unitaries acting on a
    site and its ancillas, free in the source, are counted here as gates, so
    every preparation in this sense is one in the sense of the source, of no
    larger depth.
\end{definition}

\begin{lemma}[Outcomes of nonzero probability]
    \label{lem:ldp_measurement_outcomes}
    \lean{MPSPreparation.outcomeProj_mulVec_apply,
        MPSPreparation.sum_outcomeProj, MPSPreparation.outcomeProj_empty,
        MPSPreparation.MeasurementProtocol.sum_postMeasurement,
        MPSPreparation.MeasurementProtocol.preMeasurement_ne_zero,
        MPSPreparation.MeasurementProtocol.exists_postMeasurement_ne_zero,
        MPSPreparation.MeasurementProtocol.output_ne_zero,
        MPSPreparation.MeasurementProtocol.Prepares.ne_zero}
    \leanok
    \uses{def:ldp_measurement_preparation}
    The outcome projections satisfy $\sum_mP_m=\mathbb 1$, and $P_m=\mathbb 1$
    when $S$ is empty. Hence, for a protocol with $\ket{\pi}\neq0$, some
    outcome has $P_mU\ket{\pi}\neq0$, then $V_mP_mU\ket{\pi}\neq0$, and every
    vector that the protocol prepares is nonzero.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
    \leanok
    For each configuration $x$ exactly one string $m$, the restriction of $x$
    to $S$, has $P_m\ket{x}\neq0$. So $\sum_mP_mU\ket{\pi}=U\ket{\pi}$, which
    is nonzero because $U$ is unitary; the unitary $V_m$ keeps a nonzero
    vector nonzero, and $V_mP_mU\ket{\pi}=c\ket{\psi}\neq0$ forces
    $\ket{\psi}\neq0$.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Protocols without measurements]
    \label{lem:ldp_measurement_unitary_case}
    \lean{MPSPreparation.isPreparedWithMeasurementsInDepth_of_isPreparedInDepth,
        MPSPreparation.isPreparedInDepth_of_prepares_of_measured_eq_empty,
        MPSPreparation.smul_productVector}
    \leanok
    \uses{def:ldp_measurement_preparation, lem:ldp_measurement_outcomes}
    A nonzero vector prepared in depth $T$ is prepared with measurements in
    depth $T$. Conversely, if a protocol measuring no site, with
    $T_1+T_2(m)\leq T$ for every $m$, prepares $\ket{\psi}$, then
    $\ket{\psi}$ is prepared in depth $T$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
    \leanok
    For the first statement take $S=\emptyset$ and $V_m=\mathbb 1$. For the
    second, $P_m=\mathbb 1$ for the only string $m$, so
    $V_mU\ket{\pi}=c\ket{\psi}$ with $c\neq0$ by
    Lemma~\ref{lem:ldp_measurement_outcomes}. Then
    $\ket{\psi}=V_mU(c^{-1}\ket{\pi})$, where $c^{-1}\ket{\pi}$ is the product
    vector with the vector of one site multiplied by $c^{-1}$, and the circuit
    $V_mU$ of depth at most $T$ is padded with empty layers to depth $T$.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[Layers of permutation gates]
    \label{lem:ldp_permutation_layers}
    \lean{MPSPreparation.IsLocalPerm, MPSPreparation.permMatrix_cfg_mem_unitary,
        MPSPreparation.IsLocalPerm.permMatrix_mem_supportedOperators,
        MPSPreparation.shiftPerm, MPSPreparation.shiftPerm_apply,
        MPSPreparation.isLocalPerm_shiftPerm, MPSPreparation.isLocalPerm_addRight,
        MPSPreparation.permLayer, MPSPreparation.exists_permLayer_op_mulVec,
        MPSPreparation.exists_shiftLayer_op_mulVec}
    \leanok
    \uses{def:ldp_local_circuit}
    Let $\sigma$ be a permutation of the configurations of the ring that
    changes only the sites of a set $X$, the new values at these sites
    depending only on the old values there. Its permutation matrix $P_\sigma$,
    $(P_\sigma v)(x)=v(\sigma x)$, is a unitary acting on $X$. Examples are the
    shift $x\mapsto x+f(x)e_t$ at a site $t\in X$, with values in
    $\mathbb Z_d$ and $f$ reading only the sites of $X\setminus\{t\}$, and the
    translation $x\mapsto x+\delta$ by a configuration $\delta$ vanishing off
    $X$. If $\sigma_k$, $k\in K$, are such permutations for pairwise disjoint
    pairs $\{k,k+1\}$, the layer of the gates $P_{\sigma_k}$ acts as
    $v\mapsto v\circ\rho$, where $\rho(x)$ agrees with $\sigma_k(x)$ on the
    pair $\{k,k+1\}$ and with $x$ off all the pairs. For shifts
    $\sigma_k\colon x\mapsto x+f_k(x)e_{t_k}$ this reads
    $\rho(x)_{t_k}=x_{t_k}+f_k(x)$, and $\rho(x)_i=x_i$ at every site $i$
    that is none of the $t_k$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
    \leanok
    Enumerating $X$ writes $P_\sigma$ as $Y\otimes\mathbb 1$ with
    $Y_{u,w}=1$ if $w=\sigma(x)|_X$ for some $x$ with $x|_X=u$, and $0$
    otherwise; permutation matrices are unitary. For the layer, induct over
    the pairs: $P_{\sigma_a}P_\rho=P_{\rho\sigma_a}$, and $\rho\sigma_a$
    agrees with $\sigma_a$ on the pair $\{a,a+1\}$, which the earlier pairs
    do not meet, and with $\sigma_k$ on an earlier pair $\{k,k+1\}$, because
    $\sigma_a(x)$ agrees with $x$ there.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[GHZ-type states in constant depth with measurements]
    \label{thm:ldp_ghz_measurement}
    \lean{MPSPreparation.site, MPSPreparation.withZeroAncillas,
        MPSPreparation.ghzInitial, MPSPreparation.ghzProtocol,
        MPSPreparation.ghzState_apply, MPSPreparation.ghzProtocol_output,
        MPSPreparation.isPreparedWithMeasurementsInDepth_withZeroAncillas_ghzState}
    \leanok
    \uses{def:ldp_measurement_preparation, lem:ldp_permutation_layers,
        def:ldp_pair_family_fixed_point}
    Let $M\geq1$, $b\geq1$ and $\alpha\in\mathbb C^b$ nonzero. On the ring of
    $2M$ sites of dimension $b$, place the system qudit $n\in\{1,\dots,M\}$ at
    the site $2n-1$ and its ancilla at the site $2n$. The state
    $\ket{\chi_M}=\sum_{i=1}^b\alpha_i\ket{i}^{\otimes M}$ of the system
    qudits, with every ancilla in $\ket{0}$, is prepared with measurements in
    depth $3$. In the protocol below every outcome gives exactly this state.
    This is the construction of \cite[Example~1]{Piroli2021LOCC} for qudits,
    and the preparation of $\ket{\chi_M}$ in constant depth stated in the
    paragraph ``Long-range MPS using measurements'' of
    \cite{Malz2024Preparation}.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
    \leanok
    The amplitude of $\ket{\chi_M}$ at $s$ is $\alpha_{s_1}$ if all $s_n$ are
    equal and $0$ otherwise. Values are taken in $\mathbb Z_b$. Start from the
    product vector with $\sum_i\alpha_i\ket{i}$ on the first system qudit,
    $\sum_i\ket{i}$ on the other system qudits, and $\ket{0}$ on the ancillas.
    A first layer of gates $\ket{k}\ket{a}\mapsto\ket{k}\ket{a+k}$ on the
    system qudit $n$ and its ancilla, and a second layer of gates
    $\ket{a}\ket{k}\mapsto\ket{a-k}\ket{k}$ on the ancilla $n$ and the system
    qudit $n+1$, for $n<M$, give
    \begin{align*}
        \sum_{k}\alpha_{k_1}\bigotimes_{n=1}^M\ket{k_n}
        \bigotimes_{n=1}^{M-1}\ket{k_n-k_{n+1}}\otimes\ket{0},
    \end{align*}
    the last factor being the last ancilla. Measure the ancillas $n<M$. The
    outcome $m$ forces $k_{n+1}=k_n-m_n$, so the system qudits are left in
    $\sum_i\alpha_i\bigotimes_n\ket{i-p_n}$ with the prefix sums
    $p_n=m_1+\cdots+m_{n-1}$. One layer of single-site shifts, $X^{p_n}$ on
    the system qudit $n$ and $X^{-m_n}$ on the ancilla $n$, gives
    $\ket{\chi_M}$ with every ancilla in $\ket{0}$. The three layers are
    layers of permutation gates, and Lemma~\ref{lem:ldp_permutation_layers}
    computes their action configuration by configuration. The product vector
    is nonzero because $\alpha\neq0$.
\end{proof}
