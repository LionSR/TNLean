\section{Upper and lower norm comparisons from an auxiliary pair}
\label{sec:al_comparators_source}

The norm comparisons of \cite[Proposition~8.1]{OpenAI2026AreaLaw} are
stated for a finite product of finite-dimensional local spaces. One common
label sequence serves every finite family of nested status partitions,
every weight and every mean tree, and each remainder is uniform in the
tree, its terminal probabilities and the symmetric vector.

\begin{definition}[Statement of the norm comparisons]
    \label{def:al_comparators_source_statement}
    \lean{TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.Comparators.IsTypicalSchmidtSet,
        TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.Comparators.IsStatusFamily,
        TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.Comparators.IsPrefixData,
        TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.Comparators.IsMarginalMomentData,
        TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.Comparators.prevector,
        TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.Comparators.bandMetric,
        TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.Comparators.meanRoot,
        TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.Comparators.ComparatorsStatement}
    \leanok
    Let $\mathcal H_{\rm phys}$ be the tensor product of finite-dimensional
    local spaces over a finite set of sites. Let $\widetilde H\ge0$ have the
    unit ground vector $\widetilde\Omega$ with ground energy $\widetilde E_0$
    and gap $\widetilde g>0$, that is,
    $\widetilde H\widetilde\Omega=\widetilde E_0\widetilde\Omega$ and
    $\widetilde H-\widetilde E_0\ge\widetilde g(1-|\widetilde\Omega\rangle
        \langle\widetilde\Omega|)$. Fix a set of sites $X$. A typical Schmidt
    set is a nonempty set $E$ of indices of positive eigenvalues $p_i$ of the
    reduced density of $X$ such that, for some $w\ge0$,
    $e^{-S_X-w}\le p_i\le e^{-S_X+w}$ for $i\in E$ and
    $z=\sum_{i\in E}p_i>0$. Put $d_*=|E|$, $p_i'=p_i/z$ and
    $S'=-\sum_{i\in E}p_i'\log p_i'$.

    Append systems $C,R$ of dimension $d_*$, put
    $\mathrm{Bell}_{CR}=d_*^{-1/2}\sum_i|i\rangle_C|i\rangle_R$,
    $\mathcal V=\mathcal H_{\rm phys}\otimes\mathcal H_C\otimes\mathcal H_R$
    and $\mathcal S_k=\operatorname{Sym}^k\mathcal V$. For a label $\lambda$ of the
    symmetric group $S_k$ let $\pi^\lambda_R$ and $\pi^\lambda_C$ be the
    label projections on the $R$ and $C$ copies, and put
    \begin{align}
        \mathrm{pre}_k=\pi_R^{\lambda_k}
        (\widetilde\Omega\otimes\mathrm{Bell}_{CR})^{\otimes k},
        \qquad
        \overline{\widetilde H}=\frac1k\sum_{i=1}^k\widetilde H^{(i)}.\notag
    \end{align}

    A status family is a finite set of statuses $j$, each consisting of $K$
    partitions of the sites into disjoint sets $X,U_{j,g},Y_{j,g},V_{j,g}$,
    $1\le g\le K$, such that $XU_{j,g}Y_{j,g}\subseteq XU_{j',g'}$ for all
    statuses $j,j'$ and all $g<g'$. For a weight $a>0$ put $t=a/2$, $s=1/4$
    and $W=aK$. With the common-dimension Schur metrics $W_Q$ at parameter
    $t$, put
    \begin{align}
        A_{j,g}=(W_{CXU_{j,g}}^{-1}W_{RV_{j,g}}^{-1}W_{Y_{j,g}})^2,
        \qquad A_j=\prod_{g=1}^KA_{j,g},\notag
    \end{align}
    and let $\mathcal M$ be the root, on $\mathcal S_k$, of a finite binary
    mean tree whose leaves carry statuses $j$ with leaf operators $A_j$ and
    terminal probabilities $w_j$. Write
    $\langle f\rangle_{\rm av}=K^{-1}\sum_g\sum_jw_jf_{j,g}$.
    For a vector $\xi\in\mathcal S_k$ put $N=\|\mathcal M^{-s}\xi\|$,
    $v=\mathcal M^{-s}\xi/N$ and
    $E_{\rm def}=(\langle v,\overline{\widetilde H}v\rangle-\widetilde E_0)
        /\widetilde g$. Put
    \begin{align}
        g^{\rm pre}_{j,g}  & =S(XU_{j,g})+S(V_{j,g})-S(Y_{j,g}),\notag \\
        g^{\rm post}_{j,g} & =S(U_{j,g})+S(U_{j,g}Y_{j,g})-S(Y_{j,g}),
        \notag
    \end{align}
    and let $g_{\max}$ be the largest of $0$ and the values
    $g^{\rm pre}_{j,g}$ over the bands and the statuses at the leaves of the
    tree.

    Prefix data consist of a finite nested collection $\mathcal R$ of sets of
    sites disjoint from $X$ and numbers $B_{\rm sh},B_{\rm exc}$ with
    $S(B)\le B_{\rm sh}$ for $B\in\mathcal R$, such that each $Q=U_{j,g}$ and
    $Q=U_{j,g}Y_{j,g}$ has a member $B\in\mathcal R$ containing it or
    contained in it with $1+\log\dim\mathcal H_{B\triangle Q}\le B_{\rm exc}$.
    Moment data with constants $C_0,c_0$ consist of $\mathcal B\ge1$ such that
    every $Q\in\{XU_{j,g},V_{j,g},Y_{j,g},U_{j,g},U_{j,g}Y_{j,g}\}$ obeys
    \begin{align}
        \log\Tr\rho_Q^{1-u}\le uS(Q)+C_0\mathcal Bu^2,
        \qquad |u|\le c_0/\sqrt{\mathcal B},\notag
    \end{align}
    with only positive eigenvalues of the marginal $\rho_Q$ in the trace.

    The statement of the norm comparisons asserts that there are an absolute
    constant $a_0>0$ and a universal constant $C>0$, and for all
    $C_0,c_0>0$ constants $c,C'>0$, with the following property. For every
    such Hamiltonian, set $X$ and typical Schmidt set there is one sequence
    of labels $\lambda_k$ such that, for every $k>0$, $\mathrm{pre}_k$ is
    nonzero, lies in $\mathcal S_k$, has norm at most one, satisfies
    $\overline{\widetilde H}\,\mathrm{pre}_k=\widetilde E_0\,\mathrm{pre}_k$
    and is fixed by $\pi_C^{\lambda_k}$ and $\pi_R^{\lambda_k}$. For every
    partition of the sites into disjoint sets $X,U,Y,V$,
    \begin{align}
        2S_X+g^{\rm pre}-g^{\rm post}=I(X:V)+I(X:YV).
        \label{eq:al_comparators_source_identity}
    \end{align}
    For every status family, $g^{\rm pre}_{j,g}\ge0$, and for every
    $0<a\le a_0$ the following hold, where each $o_k(1)$ is one sequence
    tending to zero that does not depend on the tree, its terminal
    probabilities, the isometric coordinates of $\mathcal S_k$ or the vector
    $\xi$. For prefix data and all sufficiently large $k$, with $\xi=\mathrm{pre}_k$,
    \begin{align}
        -\frac{\log N^2}{k}\le2\log d_*-\log z
        +2sW\Bigl(\frac{2B_{\rm sh}}z+2B_{\rm exc}\Bigr)+o_k(1).
        \label{eq:al_comparators_source_rough_upper}
    \end{align}
    For every $k>0$, every $\xi\in\mathcal S_k$ with $0<\|\xi\|\le1$ fixed by
    $\pi_C^{\lambda_k}$ and $\pi_R^{\lambda_k}$, and every $0<\tau<1/2$,
    \begin{align}
        -\frac{\log N^2}{k}\ge2sW\Bigl[2S'
            -C\Bigl(\tau\log d_*+\frac{h(\tau)}a\Bigr)
            -C\frac{E_{\rm def}}{\tau}S'\Bigr]-o_k(1),
        \label{eq:al_comparators_source_rough_lower}
    \end{align}
    where $h$ is the binary entropy. For moment data with
    $a\le c/\sqrt{\mathcal B}$, for all sufficiently large $k$ and
    $\xi=\mathrm{pre}_k$,
    \begin{align}
        -\frac{\log N^2}{k}\le2\log d_*-\log z
        +2sW\Bigl[\langle g^{\rm post}\rangle_{\rm av}
            +C'\Bigl(a\mathcal B+\frac{|\log z|}a\Bigr)\Bigr]+o_k(1),
        \label{eq:al_comparators_source_sharp_upper}
    \end{align}
    and, for every $k>0$, every such $\xi$ and every $0<\tau<1/2$,
    \begin{align}
        -\frac{\log N^2}{k}\ge2sW\Bigl[2S'+(1-\tau)
            \langle g^{\rm pre}\rangle_{\rm av}
            -C'\Bigl(\tau\log d_*+\frac{h(\tau)}a+a\mathcal B\Bigr)
            -C'\frac{E_{\rm def}}\tau(S'+g_{\max})\Bigr]-o_k(1).
        \label{eq:al_comparators_source_sharp_lower}
    \end{align}
    In each comparison $N>0$.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Upper and lower norm comparisons]
    \label{thm:al_comparators_source}
    \lean{TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.Comparators.comparatorsStatement}
    \leanok
    \uses{def:al_comparators_source_statement,thm:al_global_schmidt_bell_prevector}
    The statement of the norm comparisons holds, with $a_0=1/10$, $C=4$,
    $C'=4+25C_0$ and $c=c_0/10$. This is
    \cite[Proposition~8.1]{OpenAI2026AreaLaw}; the label sequence is chosen
    before the status family and the weight, and the two lower comparisons
    hold for every vector of norm at most one in $\mathcal S_k$ carrying the
    label $\lambda_k$ on both auxiliary systems, in particular for
    $\mathrm{pre}_k$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
    \leanok
    \uses{thm:al_global_schmidt_bell_prevector}
    The normalized typical Schmidt vector chooses the labels, exactly as in
    Theorem~\ref{thm:al_global_schmidt_bell_prevector} for arbitrary local
    dimensions, and its auxiliary marginal has entropy $S'$. The label
    dimensions therefore satisfy $\log\dim\lambda_k=kS'+o(k)$, and the
    label mass is at least inverse polynomial in $k$. Each $A_{j,g}$
    preserves $\mathcal S_k$ and, after compression, the bands commute, so
    $\mathcal M=\prod_g\mathcal M_g$ with $\mathcal M_g$ the root of the
    same tree with leaves $A_{j,g}$.

    With these identifications the four comparisons are the regional
    comparisons of \cite[Section~8]{OpenAI2026AreaLaw} at $t=a/2$ and
    $s=1/4$. The rough upper bound uses the prefix data, and the sharp
    upper bound uses the moment data on $U_{j,g},U_{j,g}Y_{j,g},Y_{j,g}$
    with $6a\le c_0/\sqrt{\mathcal B}$. The two lower bounds use the gap of
    $\widetilde H$ and the label bound $2kS'-\epsilon_k\le2\log\dim\lambda_k$
    with $\epsilon_k=2|\log\dim\lambda_k-kS'|=o(k)$; the sharp lower bound
    also uses the moment data on $XU_{j,g},Y_{j,g},V_{j,g}$ with
    $10a\le c_0/\sqrt{\mathcal B}$. For the sharp lower bound the tree is
    read on the statuses at its leaves, so that $g_{\max}$ runs over them.

    The explicit finite-$k$ bounds have error terms of order
    $\log k+a\epsilon_k$, so after division by $k$ they tend to zero. The
    constants are absorbed as follows, using $a\le1$, $S'\ge0$,
    $g_{\max}\ge0$ and $E_{\rm def}\ge0$:
    $2\tau\log d_*+(3+1/a)h(\tau)\le4(\tau\log d_*+h(\tau)/a)$,
    $25C_0\mathcal Ba\le C'a\mathcal B$,
    $(E_{\rm def}/\tau)(2S'+g_{\max})\le C'(E_{\rm def}/\tau)(S'+g_{\max})$,
    $6KC_0\mathcal Ba^2\le2sWC'a\mathcal B$ and
    $-(K/2)\log z\le2sWC'|\log z|/a$. The identity
    \eqref{eq:al_comparators_source_identity} follows from purity of
    $\widetilde\Omega$, and $g^{\rm pre}_{j,g}=I(XU_{j,g}:V_{j,g})\ge0$ by
    subadditivity.
\end{proof}
