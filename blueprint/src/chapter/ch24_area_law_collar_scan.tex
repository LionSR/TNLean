\chapter{Moving a boundary through a collar: random schedules and norm comparisons}
\label{ch:area_law_collar_scan}

The third stage of the proof of the area law moves a boundary through a
collar, a band of sites around a target region, according to a finite random
schedule, and compares two bounds on the norm of a filtered vector built from
many copies of the ground state \cite[Sections~8 and~9]{OpenAI2026AreaLaw}.
The upper bound projects onto an entangled pair with an auxiliary system; the
lower bound uses the gap, which forces most copies into the ground state at low
energy. This chapter formalizes the schedule and its histories, the probability
of a bad history, the two norm comparisons, and the density of the entropy
defect at the scales of the schedule.
