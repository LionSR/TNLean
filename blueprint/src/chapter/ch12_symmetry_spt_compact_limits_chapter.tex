\chapter{Virtual classes at compact limits}
\label{ch:symmetry_spt_compact_limits}

This chapter proves the converse direction of the classification of
Theorem~\ref{thm:spt_classification} when the minimal bond dimension may change
along the path, for paths whose ground states have a continuous tensor
realization. Uniform rigidity of virtual projective classes, compact limits
of unital tensors, reduction to the stationary support, and propagation bounds
on finite periodic chains combine into one virtual class along a symmetric
gapped interaction path with a continuous exact MPS realization of its unique
finite-chain ground lines (Theorem~\ref{thm:spt_exact_ground_physical_class}).
With the construction of Chapter~\ref{ch:symmetry_spt_virtual_paths}, this
gives the classification for one-site injective endpoints with prescribed
exact virtual covariance: they satisfy the independent exact MPS gapped phase
condition if and only if their factor systems determine the same cohomology
class (Theorem~\ref{thm:spt_exact_phase_injective_classification}). The same
holds when the endpoint symmetries carry scalar characters, since the endpoint
phase gauge removes them
(Corollary~\ref{cor:spt_exact_phase_injective_character_classification}).
