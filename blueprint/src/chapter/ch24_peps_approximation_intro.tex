\chapter{Polynomial PEPS approximation of gapped square-grid ground states}%
\label{ch:peps_approximation}

This chapter follows~\cite{OpenAI2026PolynomialPEPS}, September 24, 2026. The
ground state of a gapped nearest-neighbor Hamiltonian on an $L\times L$ square
grid is approximated by a PEPS whose bond dimension is polynomial in $L$. The
sections below treat the geometry of the dyadic distribution protocol, the
parties and their sources, the information estimates, the compression of the
distributed density, and the routing of the party network.
