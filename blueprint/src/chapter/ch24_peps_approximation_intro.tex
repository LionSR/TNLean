\chapter{Information bounds for separated regions}
\label{ch:peps_approximation}

Chapters~\ref{ch:peps_approximation}--\ref{ch:peps_dyadic_protocol} prove the
polynomial PEPS approximation of~\cite{OpenAI2026PolynomialPEPS}. The physical
sites are handed out step by step to parties indexed by dyadic squares; each
party acts privately on its own sites and takes part in boundedly many
operations with other parties. The distributed state is then compressed and
read off as a PEPS. This chapter proves the first analytic input
\cite[Section~3]{OpenAI2026PolynomialPEPS}: two regions share negligible
information when the band between them narrows linearly toward finitely many
points but stays wider than a large multiple of $\log L$.
