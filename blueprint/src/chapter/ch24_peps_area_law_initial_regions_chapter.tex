\chapter{The initial regions and their local structure}%
\label{ch:peps_area_law_initial_regions}

The primary regions and the runs of fan triangles together form the initial
family of regions \cite[Section~11]{OpenAI2026AreaLaw}. This chapter shows
that their interiors are disjoint, that every point of the integer lattice
lies in exactly one of them, and how their colors meet across their
boundaries. It then describes the family near a marked point, where the eight
sectors of a fan meet.
