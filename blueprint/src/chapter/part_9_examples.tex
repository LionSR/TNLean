\part{Examples}
\label{part:examples}

Prototypical matrix product states: product, GHZ, W, cluster, AKLT and
Majumdar--Ghosh states, product vacua with boundary states, and the Kitaev
chain \cite[Appendix]{Cirac2021Matrix}; then symmetry operators of
spin chains (CZX, Kramers--Wannier, Fibonacci, Ising, clock and anomalous
symmetries and their condensation defects) that illustrate the multi-block
compression theorem.
