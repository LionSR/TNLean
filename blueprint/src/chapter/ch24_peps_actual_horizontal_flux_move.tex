\begin{theorem}[Vacancy in a horizontal two-plaquette block]
    \label{thm:peps_actual_horizontal_vacancy}
    \lean{TNLean.PEPS.regularWalkHolonomy_rightPlaquette_eq_one_iff_treeGauge,
        TNLean.PEPS.regularRegionTreeCycleResidual_middle_eq_one_iff_rightPlaquette,
        TNLean.PEPS.translatedTwoPlaquetteIso_vertex_coordinates}
    \leanok
    \uses{thm:peps_tree_gauge_transport}
    On a finite torus of width at least four and height at least three, consider
    the translated three-by-two block with its derived spanning path.
    As an auxiliary finite-torus criterion for SCP10 (Theorem~6.16,
    lines 2271--2305), where the unrestricted geometry and the complete prescribed
    braid remain separate \cite{gap:rmp_peps_quantum_double_g_isometry},
    the right plaquette is vacant for arbitrary ordered bond operators if and
    only if the middle upward bond has trivial residual in the root-normalized
    tree gauge, with both periodic seams included.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}\leanok
    Because the other three sides of the right plaquette belong to the spanning path,
    applying the three-tree-edge criterion and identifying the fourth edge with
    the derived middle chord reduces the holonomy to the middle residual, while
    directed edge traversal retains the sorting convention at a periodic seam.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[A fixed physical operation on arbitrary patch inputs]
    \label{thm:peps_actual_horizontal_flux_operation}
    \lean{TNLean.PEPS.IsGIsometric.exists_unitary_torusActualHorizontalFluxMove,
        TNLean.PEPS.torusActualHorizontalFluxMove,
        TNLean.PEPS.torusActualHorizontalFluxMove_eq_of_ne,
        TNLean.PEPS.torusActualHorizontalFluxMove_middle_of_vacancy,
        TNLean.PEPS.torusActualHorizontalFluxMove_up_transport_of_vacancy}
    \leanok
    \uses{thm:peps_actual_cycle_physical_permutation,thm:peps_actual_horizontal_vacancy}
    Assume the regular virtual action and original $G$-isometric site tensors
    on the same finite-torus geometry.
    While this auxiliary operation uses derived coordinates and does not assert
    the complete prescribed braid \cite{gap:rmp_peps_quantum_double_g_isometry},
    for each translated horizontal block there is one six-spin unitary,
    chosen before every bond assignment and every outgoing boundary
    configuration, that multiplies the derived middle cycle residual on the left
    by the derived left residual and reconstructs with the original tree gauge.
    It acts on both the open contraction and the closed state,
    retaining every ordered bond other than the middle chord, including all
    crossing and exterior bonds. If the right plaquette is vacant, its new
    middle ordered coefficient is $k_{\mathrm{head}}\omega_{\mathrm{left}}
        k_{\mathrm{tail}}^{-1}$. Equivalently, its directed upward bond element becomes
    $T L B^{-1}$, where $T$, $L$, and $B$ are the original top-left rightward,
    left upward, and bottom-left rightward bond elements, respectively.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}\leanok
    Applying the original-spin cycle permutation to the tree gauge and residuals
    derived from each input recovers the input under reconstruction, and
    controlled multiplication changes only the selected middle residual.
    Since vacancy makes the initial middle residual equal to the identity and the two upward
    chords share the same sorting comparison (including at the vertical seam),
    the two adjacent tree-edge gradients yield the explicit formula $T L B^{-1}$.
\end{proof}
