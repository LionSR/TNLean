\chapter{Amplifying the collar estimate}
\label{ch:peps_area_law_amplification_and_shells}

The fourth stage of the proof of the area law \cite[Section~10]{OpenAI2026AreaLaw}
makes the information bound smaller than any inverse power of the system size
by enlarging the collar by a sublinear radius. Typical spectral subspaces give
an operator that restores a target region moved to an auxiliary reference, and
a product of square-root channels built from the positive constraints
suppresses the excited part of the state. This chapter bounds how far these
channels spread and constructs the restoring operator.
