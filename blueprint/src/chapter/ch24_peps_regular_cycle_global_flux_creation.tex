\begin{theorem}[A global coherent class-creation operation]%
    \label{thm:peps_regular_cycle_global_flux_creation}
    \lean{TNLean.PEPS.exists_unitary_regularCycleGlobalFluxCreation_bondOperators}
    \leanok
    \uses{thm:peps_regular_physical_flux_creation,
        thm:peps_regular_coherent_global_transport,
        lem:peps_regular_bond_extension_transport,
        thm:peps_regular_two_cycle_global_flux_move}
    Let $\Gamma$ be a finite simple graph and $G$ a finite group. Let the original
    site tensors be $G$-isometric for their regular virtual actions. Choose a
    region $R$, a spanning tree of its induced graph, a root, and an internal
    bond $e$ outside the tree. For $g\in G$, put
    \begin{align}
        \gamma_g=\frac{1}{\sqrt{|G|\,|C_G(g)|}}.
    \end{align}
    There is a unitary $W_g$ on the original spins in $R$ whose complementary
    identity extension $\widehat W_g$ is globally unitary and satisfies
    \begin{align}
        \widehat W_g\Psi_1(u)
        =\gamma_g\sum_{z\in G}\Psi_{zgz^{-1}}(u)
    \end{align}
    for every common assignment $u$ of exterior and crossing bond operators.
    Here $\Psi_h(u)$ is the actual global contraction with operator $h$ on
    the chosen ordered bond $e$, identity operators on all other internal
    bonds, and assignment $u$ on all remaining bonds. The unitary depends on
    the requested class but is chosen before $u$.

    This is the global contraction consequence of the coherent local
    operation in \cite[Theorem~6.17]{Schuch2010PEPS} for the stated chosen
    finite cycle block. The concrete flux-pair geometry and unrestricted
    lattice scope remain separate; see
    \cite{gap:rmp_peps_quantum_double_g_isometry}. No energy or
    parent-Hamiltonian membership assertion is made.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}\leanok
    Obtain the original-spin unitary and its normalized action on every
    boundary column from the local creation theorem. An identity vertex
    gauge transfers the exterior crossing operators to one common boundary
    configuration, independently of the internal insertion. The complementary
    site tensors also agree in every summand. The coherent cut-contraction
    identity therefore gives the displayed equality of actual global vectors.
    The physical identity extension preserves unitarity. Summing over all
    conjugating elements retains their centralizer multiplicities, so the
    normalization is exactly $\gamma_g$.
\end{proof}
