\chapter{Length dependence and uniqueness of structure coefficients}
\label{ch:mpdo_rfp_algebraic_coefficients}

The structure coefficients of Chapter~\ref{ch:mpdo_rfp_algebraic} are traces
of powers of positive diagonal matrices, and \cite[Section~4.5]{Cirac2016MPDO_arXiv}
asks whether they can depend on the length of the chain. This chapter
answers the question for chosen presentations. It exhibits a renormalization
fixed point whose structure coefficients depend on the length
(Lemma~\ref{thm:mpdo_length_dependent_rfp_example}), shows that the
$\Z_2$-twisted quantum dimer is a renormalization fixed point
(Lemma~\ref{thm:mpdo_twisted_dimer_is_rfp_via_ts}) whose vertical sectors satisfy
the algebra conditions, and constructs a fixed point whose length dependence
survives every uniform positive rescaling
(Lemma~\ref{thm:mpdo_bnt_label_rescaling_stable_rfp_capstone}). It then
proves that the structure coefficients are unique at every length at which the
closed-chain operators are linearly independent
(Lemma~\ref{thm:mpdo_bnt_label_coeff_unique}), describes how they transform
under a change of vertical representatives, shows that equality of the
periodic operators alone does not determine them
(Lemma~\ref{thm:mpdo_vertical_coefficients_not_presentation_invariant}), and
derives the associativity constraint.
