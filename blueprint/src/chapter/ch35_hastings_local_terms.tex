\chapter{Filtered local terms of a gapped Hamiltonian}
\label{ch:hastings_local_terms}

In an earlier paper, Hastings~\cite{Hastings2006Solving} describes the ground
states of a gapped Hamiltonian with finite-range interactions on a finite
lattice or graph by local data. Each term $H_i$ is replaced by its
Gaussian-filtered version, the Gaussian time average of
$e^{iHt}H_ie^{-iHt}$, and then by an operator $M_i$ supported near the
support of $H_i$. Every ground state is nearly an eigenvector of each $M_i$,
and the sum of the $M_i$ approximates the Hamiltonian on the ground space.
This chapter proves these estimates, with $M_i$ obtained either by a partial
trace or by evolving under the terms near $H_i$ only. When the ground space
is degenerate, the compressions of the $M_i$ to it must nearly commute. The
remaining sections prove this for translation-invariant systems, give the
source's example of terms whose compressions fail to commute without
translation invariance, and prove a variational lower bound on the ground
energy of a translation-invariant Hamiltonian.
