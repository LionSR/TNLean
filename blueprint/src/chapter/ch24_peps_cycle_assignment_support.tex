\begin{theorem}[Unordered endpoints and supported cycle assignments]
    \label{thm:peps_cycle_assignment_support}
    \lean{TNLean.PEPS.Edge.ofAdj_eq_iff_endpoints,
        TNLean.PEPS.Edge.ofAdj_ne_of_endpoint_coordinates,
        TNLean.PEPS.regularTreeCycleAssignment_eq_of_support,
        TNLean.PEPS.regularTreeCycleAssignment_twoSupport}
    \leanok
    Two native edges are equal precisely when their unordered endpoint pairs
    agree. A coordinate constant on the endpoints of one edge separates it
    from any edge with a different endpoint coordinate. Let $R$ have a chosen tree $T$. Any bond assignment supported on
    internal non-tree edges is recovered by restricting it to those edges and
    extending it by the identity. These are auxiliary facts for the accessible
    systems and local movement of \cite[Theorem~6.16]{Schuch2010PEPS}.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}\leanok
    Compare the two sorted endpoint pairs. For the second assertion, an edge
    carrying a nonidentity operator is internal and off the tree by hypothesis;
    all remaining cases carry the identity.
\end{proof}
