\subsection{Sharp lower bounds for the scanner roots}

The sharp lower comparison evaluates the physical spectral gap in the
normalized inverse-root vector of each fill or charge while preserving
the history tree and its conditional choices.
Its terminal partitions consist of the completed histories through a
fixed horizon together with the pre-charge histories before that horizon;
writing $Q_{jg}$ and $Y_{jg}$ for the near and middle regions of terminal
$j$ in band $g$ gives the far region $(Q_{jg}\cup Y_{jg})^c$.

\begin{theorem}[Scanner-root sharp lower estimate]
    \label{thm:actual_scan_sharp_lower_norm}
    \lean{TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.CollarScan.exists_actualScan_sharp_lower_norm}
    \leanok
    \uses{def:al_scan_actual_histories,def:al_scan_actual_energy_terms,
        thm:al_scan_literal_scalars}
    Fix a finite lattice domain $\Lambda$, interaction range $R$, term
    norm bound $J$, positive physical and auxiliary dimensions $q,d$,
    and $a>0$.
    There are $c,C_f\geq0$, chosen before the scanner, finite horizon,
    physical states, moment parameters, copy number, labels and
    interpolation parameter, with the following property.
    Let the scanner be indexed by the ordered admissible interaction
    supports at range $R$, with offset scale $m>0$ and charge capacity $M>0$.
    Let $\widetilde H$ be the sum of the truncated energy terms,
    formed from a fixed reference state, and suppose that a unit vector
    $\widetilde\Omega$ satisfies
    \begin{align}
        \widetilde H-e_0 I
        \succeq \gamma
        \bigl(I-|\widetilde\Omega\rangle
        \langle\widetilde\Omega|\bigr),
        \qquad \gamma>0.
    \end{align}
    For every terminal and band, assume the original marginal moment
    estimate
    \begin{align}
        \log\mathbb E\exp(vZ_U)
        \leq vS(U)+K_{\rm mom}v^2
        \qquad (|v|\leq r_{\rm mom})
    \end{align}
    for $U=Q_{jg},Y_{jg},(Q_{jg}\cup Y_{jg})^c$, where
    $K_{\rm mom}\geq0$, $10a\leq r_{\rm mom}$ and $10a\leq1$.
    Here $Z_U$ is the surprisal under the spectral probability
    distribution, with zero weights contributing zero.
    Entropies and moments refer to the same original state
    $\widetilde\Omega$.
    Fix $k>0$ and a nonzero prevector of norm at most one,
    fixed by simultaneous copy symmetry and both auxiliary label
    projectors $\ell_C,\ell_R$, and assume
    \begin{align}
        2kS'-\varepsilon
        \leq\log\dim\ell_C+\log\dim\ell_R.
    \end{align}
    Define
    \begin{align}
        g_{jg}
                 & =S(Q_{jg})+S((Q_{jg}\cup Y_{jg})^c)-S(Y_{jg}),                          \\
        g_{\max} & =\max\bigl(\{0\}\cup\{g_{jg}\}_{j,g}\bigr),                             \\
        r_k      & =\log(k+1)+c\log(k+2)+a\varepsilon,
                 & b_k                                            & =\tfrac12(k+2)^{-C_f}.
    \end{align}
    At every fill or charge interpolation before the horizon, let
    $N_k$ be its squared filtered norm, $\mu_k$ its
    mean energy, and $w_j$ its terminal weights under the
    inclusion into the common terminal family.
    Its filtered vector has norm one, $N_k>0$, and
    $\delta_k=(\mu_k-e_0)/\gamma\geq0$.
    For every $0<\tau\leq\tfrac12$, put
    \begin{align}
        L_g=2S'+(1-\tau)\sum_jw_jg_{jg}
        -2\tau\log d-(3+a^{-1})h(\tau)-25K_{\rm mom}a.
    \end{align}
    Then
    \begin{align}
        \frac12\sum_g\left[
            ak\left(L_g-\frac{\delta_k}{\tau}
            \max\{0,2S'+g_{\max}\}\right)
            -2|r_k|-|\log b_k|-\log2-\log(3/2)
            \right]
        \leq-\log N_k.
    \end{align}
    The same prevector, labels, gap and moment data serve all these
    interpolations, including a scanner with zero bands.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
    \leanok
    \uses{thm:al_scan_band_nesting,thm:al_augmented_root_compression,
        thm:al_scan_actual_round_global_energy,thm:al_scan_nested_band_metrics,
        thm:al_scan_actual_fill_admission,thm:al_scan_actual_charge_admissible}
    Because the physical terminal partitions have disjoint near and middle
    regions that nest across bands, the regional sharp lower norm theorem in
    original coordinates applies at metric parameter $t=a/2$ and inverse
    exponent $s=1/4$, where the conditions $20t\leq r_{\rm mom},1$ become
    $10a\leq r_{\rm mom},1$.
    Passing an isometric coordinate matrix $Z$ for the simultaneous symmetric
    subspace through the augmented-site coordinate bijection, the root
    compression identity expresses the ordered product $M_k$ of compressed
    band means, its inverse-quarter-power norm and its normalized filtered
    vector as
    \begin{align}
        w_k & =M_k^{-1/4}Z^*\mathrm{pre}_k,
            & N_k                           & =\|w_k\|^2,
            & v_k                           & =Z\frac{w_k}{\|w_k\|}.
    \end{align}
    Consequently, the fill and charge energy identities equate the regional
    defect in $v_k$ to $(\mu_k-e_0)/\gamma$ with
    $\mu_k=\operatorname{Re}\langle v_k,\widetilde H_kv_k\rangle$, while
    embedding the history tree into the common terminal family preserves the
    weighted entropy sum $\sum_jw_jg_{jg}$; substituting $2t=a$ and $2s=1/2$
    then gives the finite-copy lower bound of
    \cite[Section~8, lower-pin and tree-log-lower estimates]{OpenAI2026AreaLaw}.
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}[Lower label defect and replica remainder]
    \label{def:actual_sharp_lower_remainder}
    \lean{TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.sharpLowerLabelDefect,TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.sharpLowerRemainder}
    \leanok
    For a label sequence $\ell_k$ and a real entropy parameter
    $S'$, and for fixed $a>0$ and $c,C_f\in\mathbb R$, define
    $\varepsilon_k$ ($k\in\mathbb N$) and $R_k^-$ ($k>0$) by
    \begin{align}
        \varepsilon_k
              & =2\bigl|\log\dim\ell_k-kS'\bigr|, \\
        R_k^- & =\frac{
            2|\log(k+1)+c\log(k+2)+a\varepsilon_k|
            +|\log(\tfrac12(k+2)^{-C_f})|
            +\log2+\log(3/2)}{ak}.
    \end{align}
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Lower label-defect inequality]
    \label{thm:actual_sharp_lower_label_defect}
    \lean{TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.sharpLowerLabelDefect_label_bound}
    \leanok
    \uses{def:actual_sharp_lower_remainder}
    For every label sequence $\ell_k$ and every $k$,
    $\varepsilon_k\geq0$ and
    \begin{align}
        2kS'-\varepsilon_k\leq2\log\dim\ell_k.
    \end{align}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
    \leanok
    \uses{def:actual_sharp_lower_remainder}
    Apply $kS'-\log\dim\ell_k\leq
        |\log\dim\ell_k-kS'|$ and multiply by two.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Vanishing lower remainder]
    \label{thm:actual_sharp_lower_remainder_limit}
    \lean{TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.tendsto_sharpLowerRemainder_of_label_dimension}
    \leanok
    \uses{def:actual_sharp_lower_remainder}
    Fix $a>0$ and $c,C_f\in\mathbb R$.
    If a label sequence $\ell_k$ satisfies
    $\log\dim\ell_k-kS'=o(k)$, then $R_k^-\to0$ as $k\to\infty$
    through positive integers; in the physical application, this
    asymptotic holds for the Schmidt--Bell label sequence.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
    \leanok
    \uses{def:actual_sharp_lower_remainder}
    Because $\log\dim\ell_k-kS'=o(k)$, we have $\varepsilon_k/k\to0$.
    Since $\log(k+1)/k$ and $\log(k+2)/k$ also tend to zero and
    $\log(\tfrac12(k+2)^{-C_f})=-C_f\log(k+2)-\log2$, dividing the
    numerator of $R_k^-$ by $k$ and by the fixed $a>0$ gives $R_k^-\to0$.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Sharp lower rate for the physical prevector]
    \label{thm:actual_scan_supplied_prevector_sharp_lower}
    \lean{TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.CollarScan.exists_actualScan_suppliedPrevector_sharp_lower}
    \leanok
    \uses{thm:actual_scan_sharp_lower_norm,def:actual_sharp_lower_remainder,
        def:al_scan_status_marginals,def:al_scan_auxiliary_coordinates}
    Under the scanner and gap conditions of
    Theorem~\ref{thm:actual_scan_sharp_lower_norm}, use the horizon $nm$,
    fix $K_{\rm mom}\geq0$, $a>0$, $10a\leq r_{\rm mom}$ and $10a\leq1$,
    and take the augmented replica prevector of a label sequence $\ell_k$
    whose original-coordinate vectors are nonzero and have norm at most
    one for every $k>0$, with $\log\dim\ell_k-kS'=o(k)$ (as holds for the
    physical Schmidt--Bell prevector).
    Assume the marginal moment estimate of that theorem for all five
    regions of Definition~\ref{def:al_scan_status_marginals} at every
    completed status through $nm$ and every pre-charge status before $nm$;
    these five bounds imply all the near, middle and far marginal bounds.
    Then there exist $c,C_f\geq0$, fixed before $k$, scan time and
    interpolation parameter, such that $R_k^-\to0$ and every fill or
    charge obeys
    \begin{align}
        \frac a2\sum_g\left[
            L_g-\frac{\delta_k}{\tau}\max\{0,2S'+g_{\max}\}-R_k^-
            \right]
        \leq\frac{-\log N_k}{k}
        \qquad(0<\tau\leq\tfrac12).
    \end{align}
    Here $N_k>0$, $\delta_k\geq0$, $L_g$, the unit filtered vector and the
    terminal weights are those of the same interpolation in the previous
    theorem.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
    \leanok
    \uses{thm:actual_scan_sharp_lower_norm,thm:actual_sharp_lower_label_defect,
        thm:actual_sharp_lower_remainder_limit,thm:al_augmented_prevector_norm,
        thm:al_augmented_prevector_symmetry,
        def:al_scan_auxiliary_coordinates}
    Because the augmented coordinate bijection preserves nonvanishing and
    the Euclidean norm while the replica prevector is invariant under
    simultaneous copy permutations and carries the label $\ell_k$ on both
    auxiliary factors (giving the two auxiliary singleton projections),
    Theorem~\ref{thm:actual_scan_sharp_lower_norm} applies with
    $\varepsilon_k=2|\log\dim\ell_k-kS'|$.
    Dividing its conclusion by $k>0$ and factoring $ak$ out of the
    per-band error term yields the displayed lower bound with remainder
    $R_k^-$, and Theorem~\ref{thm:actual_sharp_lower_remainder_limit}
    gives $R_k^-\to0$ without requiring $\delta_k$ to be small.
\end{proof}
