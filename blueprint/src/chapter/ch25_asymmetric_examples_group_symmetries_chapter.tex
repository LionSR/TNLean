\chapter{Anomalies and condensation defects of group symmetries}%
\label{ch:asymmetric_examples_group_symmetries}

This chapter treats group symmetries given by matrix product operators,
continuing the examples of the multi-block asymmetric compression theorem
(Theorem~\ref{thm:asym_multiblock}) from
Chapters~\ref{ch:asymmetric_examples}
and~\ref{ch:asymmetric_examples_non_invertible}. The cases treated here, in
chapter order, are the CZX symmetry of Chen, Liu, and
Wen~\cite{ChenLiuWen2011CZX}, whose matrix product unitary is displayed in
Ref.~\cite[Appendix~A, ``The CZX MPU'']{Cirac2021Matrix}, together with its
decorated form~\cite{GarreRubioSchuch2024DomainWall}, whose anomaly class is
shown here to be nontrivial, and the anomalous $\Z_2$ symmetry of the
Levin--Gu edge;
the periodic group matrix product operators built from a normalized
three-cocycle~\cite{GarreRubio2023MPOSym}, evaluated for the group of order
two, for the cyclic groups with the cocycles
of~\cite{GarreRubioSchuch2024DomainWall}, and for $\Z_2\times\Z_2$;
and symmetry tensors for $\Z_2\times\Z_2$ and $\Z_3$ with their condensation
defects, following the anomalous group symmetries of
Refs.~\cite{GarreRubio2023MPOSym,GarreRubioSchuch2024DomainWall,Roumpedakis2023Condensation}
(the anomaly class is nontrivial for $\Z_3$ and for the full dressed
$\Z_2\times\Z_2$ representation, where the invariant detectors match the
type-II class~\cite{gap:glm23_z2z2_anomaly_detector_scope}). As in the
previous chapter, each section builds on the general definitions of
Chapter~\ref{ch:mpo_symmetry_basics}, constructs the explicit local tensors,
evaluates their fusion tables, and computes their periodic compressions and
anomaly invariants.
