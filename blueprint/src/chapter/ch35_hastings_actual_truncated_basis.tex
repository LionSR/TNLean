\section{A ground-space basis for the actual locally truncated filters}

The operators here are the local filters of \cite{Hastings2006Solving},
not their replacements under the full Hamiltonian. The polynomial sphere
bound and the fixed localization parameters remain explicit. The generic
spectral refinement uses the corrected constant of
\cite{gap:hastings05_basis_refinement}; the geometric restriction is
explained in \cite{gap:hastings05_two_dim_ground_basis}.

\begin{lemma}[Compressed commutators of separate local observables]
    \label{lem:hastings_separate_observable_compressed_decay}
    \lean{TNLean.Lattice.norm_groundCompression_observables_commutator_le_exp,
        TNLean.Lattice.norm_groundCompression_observables_commutator_le_exp_edist}
    \leanok
    Let $H=\sum_i h_i$ satisfy the local support, norm-budget, gap and
    polynomial sphere assumptions of
    Lemma~\ref{thm:hastings_full_hamiltonian_uniform_basis}.
    Let $W_j$ be a separate family of observables, supported on sets $K_j$
    containing anchors $c_j$, with diameter at most $R_{\rm obs}$ and
    cardinality at most $v_{\rm obs}$. Put
    $\gamma=\min\{1/4,\Delta E/(8v)\}$, where
    $v=2v_R|b_0|e^{2R}+1$ uses the generator support parameters.
    Put $C_{R,v_{\rm obs}}=2(2+4|b_0|e^{2R})v_{\rm obs}^2|K_g|S(1,k_g)+6$.
    Then
    \[
      \|[PW_jP,PW_iP]\|
      \le C_{R,v_{\rm obs}}e^{\gamma(2R_{\rm obs}+1)}
        \|W_j\|\|W_i\| e^{-\gamma d(c_j,c_i)}.
    \]
    The final factor is zero between disconnected components. The same
    estimate holds without the anchor factor when the two supports are
    separated by the indicated neighborhood radius.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}\leanok
    Filter each observable a second time under the original Hamiltonian.
    Its ground-space compression is unchanged. Apply the existing Gaussian
    leakage and locality estimates with time window $T=l/(4v)$ and filter
    parameter $T\Delta E$. All three errors are bounded by $e^{-\gamma l}$.
    The generator cardinality determines $v$; the observable cardinality
    determines the prefactor. Convert support separation to anchor distance
    by the graph triangle inequality, including disconnected components.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[A uniform basis for locally truncated filters]
    \label{thm:hastings_actual_truncated_basis_polynomial_growth}
    \lean{TNLean.Lattice.exists_groundSpaceBasis_sum_norm_offDiagonalPartFamily_truncated_le}
    \leanok
    Let the positive on-site dimension and the Hamiltonian data satisfy
    Lemma~\ref{thm:hastings_full_hamiltonian_uniform_basis}, with terms
    indexed by sites and $i\in X_i$. Choose retained sets $s_i$ and regions
    $K_i$ such that $X_i\subseteq K_i$ and $X_j\subseteq K_i$ for every
    $j\in s_i$. Assume that the $K_i$ have diameter at most $R_{\rm obs}$
    and cardinality at most $v_{\rm obs}$.
    Define the actual local Hamiltonians and their Gaussian filters by
    \[
      H_i^{\rm loc}=\sum_{j\in s_i}h_j,\qquad
      M_i=\frac{\Delta E}{\sqrt{2\pi r}}
       \int_{\mathbb R} e^{-(t\Delta E)^2/(2r)}
       e^{itH_i^{\rm loc}}h_i e^{-itH_i^{\rm loc}}\,dt
       \quad(r>0).
    \]
    There is an orthonormal basis of the actual $n$-dimensional ground
    eigenspace in which
    \[
      \sum_i\|\operatorname{offdiag}(PM_iP)\|
      \le (n-1)^2\sqrt{n^3 C(1+2n^3)^n}\,
          |b_0||K_g|S(\gamma/2^n,k_g),
      \qquad
      C=C_{R,v_{\rm obs}}e^{\gamma(2R_{\rm obs}+1)}.
    \]
    This constant is independent of the numbers of terms and sites when
    $n$, the generator parameters, the gap, the polynomial-growth constants
    and the localization support bounds are fixed.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}\leanok
    The actual filters are Hermitian, satisfy $\|M_i\|\le\|h_i\|$, and
    remain supported on $K_i$, since each integrand acts there.
    Apply Lemma~\ref{lem:hastings_separate_observable_compressed_decay}
    to their actual ground-space compressions. The constructed weighted
    basis theorem supplies at most $n-1$ original anchors.
    Normalize using the original weights $\|h_i\|$, so their original
    sitewise budget is retained. The off-diagonal norm estimate and the
    anchor-list length give the two factors $n-1$. The weighted shell sum
    completes the bound. No truncated-Hamiltonian gap, supplied basis,
    separated spectrum, conditioning bound or assumed commutator estimate
    is used.
\end{proof}

The uniformity asserted here keeps the localization support bounds fixed.
It does not assert the same constant when those bounds grow with system
size, nor establish the printed sharper quantitative prefactor. The counterexample without the geometric growth restriction is a separate
mathematical construction, described in
\cite{gap:hastings05_tree_basis_counterexample}.
