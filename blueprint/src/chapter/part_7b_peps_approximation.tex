\part{Approximation of gapped ground states by PEPS}
\label{part:peps_approximation}

On the $L\times L$ square grid, every ground state of a gapped
nearest-neighbour Hamiltonian lies within distance $1/L$, up to a phase, of a
projected entangled pair state of bond dimension polynomial in $L$
\cite{OpenAI2026PolynomialPEPS}; the statement is in
Chapter~\ref{ch:two_dimensional_main_results}. The proof bounds the
information shared by separated regions with the collar estimates of
Part~\ref{part:area_laws}, hands the sites out step by step to parties indexed
by dyadic squares, compresses the resulting distributed state, and reads it off
as a PEPS.
