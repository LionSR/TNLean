\section{The scanner estimate with four norm comparisons}
\label{sec:al_scanner_source}

The scanner estimate of \cite[Proposition~9.2]{OpenAI2026AreaLaw} selects
one charge round and one interpolation parameter at which both the density
and energy bounds and all four norm comparisons hold. The coefficients in
the output shell and density bounds may be enlarged after the input entropy
coefficient is fixed, as recorded in~\cite{gap:arealaw2d_scanner_inputs}.

\begin{definition}[Statement of the scanner estimate]
	\label{def:al_scanner_source_statement}
	\lean{TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.CollarScan.ScannerHypotheses,
		TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.CollarScan.typicalWindow,
		TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.CollarScan.scanPrevector,
		TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.CollarScan.ScannerStatement}
	\leanok
	\uses{thm:al_scan_truncation_inputs,thm:arealaw-supplied-target-norm-bounds}
	Fix exponents $0<\ell<1$, $0<\kappa<\mu<1-\ell$, $0<e<1$ with
	$\kappa\le e$, and $\nu>0$; an interaction range $R$, a term bound $J$,
	a gap $\Delta$ and a target-size coefficient $C_T$. Put
	$g=\Delta/\max\{1,J(\|f_{1,\Delta/2}\|_1+1)\}$. At scale $n$, put
	\begin{align}
		L & =\lfloor n^{1-\ell}\rfloor,
		  & m                           & =\lfloor n^\mu\rfloor,
		  & K                           & =\lfloor L/(8m)\rfloor,\notag \\
		D & =\lceil n^\kappa\rceil,
		  & \epsilon                    & =n^{-\nu},
		  & a                           & =W/K.\notag
	\end{align}
	The source hypotheses at scale $n$ specify a local Hamiltonian on $\Lambda$
	with range $R$, term bound $J$, gap $\Delta$, and unit ground vector
	$\Omega$. Using the induced lattice graph, the scan measures depth by the
	ambient sup-norm distance to $T$, and every designated support contains its
	anchor. The collar scan has $n$ slots per depth row, the displayed scales,
	truncation radius $r_0=\lceil C_{\rm tr}(\log n)^2\rceil$, and charge-list
	constant $C_1$. Its finite target $T$ satisfies $|T|\le C_Tn^2$ and
	$|T_j\setminus T_{j-1}|\le n$ for $1\le j\le L$, while every endpoint of a
	boundary edge of $A$ lies at ambient distance greater than $2L+10r_0$ from
	$T$. The target intersection $X=A\cap T$ is nonempty, and for every integer
	$0\le j\le L$ the shells $Q_j=A\cap(T_j\setminus T)$ satisfy
	\begin{align}
		S_\Omega(X)   & \le C_en^{1+e},\notag  \\
		S_\Omega(Q_j) & \le C_e(nL^e+n).\notag
	\end{align}

	The scanner statement asserts that there exists $C_{\rm tr}>0$ such that,
	for every integer $q\ge2$ and every real $C_e$, there exist constants
	$C_1>0$, $c_a>0$, $C>0$, $C_\delta>0$, $C_b,C_{l,b},C_l,C_{l,\delta}$
	and a size threshold with the following property. For every larger $n$ and every
	finite physical system satisfying the source hypotheses, the truncated
	Hamiltonian admits a unit ground vector $\widetilde\Omega$ of energy
	$0\le e_t\le n^{-1000}$ and gap $g/2$ whose trace distance
	$D_{\rm tr}(\rho,\sigma)=\frac12\|\rho-\sigma\|_1$ from $\Omega$ obeys
	\begin{align}
		D_{\rm tr}(\widetilde\Omega\widetilde\Omega^*,\Omega\Omega^*)
		 & \le\sqrt{2\min\{n^{-1000},g/4\}/g}.
		\label{eq:al_scanner_source_trace_distance}
	\end{align}
	For this truncated ground vector $\widetilde\Omega$, the target typical set
	$E$ at width $n^{3/5}$ is nonempty of cardinality $d_*=|E|$, and its mass
	$z$ satisfies $0<1-n^{-100}\le z\le1$. A single label sequence
	$\lambda_k$ defines nonzero replica vectors
	$\mathrm{pre}_k=\pi_R^{\lambda_k}
		(\widetilde\Omega\otimes\mathrm{Bell}_{CR})^{\otimes k}$
	of Euclidean norm at most one for all $k>0$, with both auxiliary dimensions
	equal to $d_*$. All of these choices precede the total weight $W$.

	For every $W\ge1$ with $ar_0^2\le c_a$, comparator parameters
	\begin{align}
		B_{\rm sh}     & \le C_b(nL^e+n),\notag            \\
		B_{\rm exc}    & \le C_bnD,\notag                  \\
		1\le\mathcal B & \le C_bnD(\log n)^{C_{l,b}}\notag
	\end{align}
	bound the labelled cut parameters of every completed and pre-charge status
	cut by $\mathcal B$, while the marginal moment estimate for
	$\widetilde\Omega$ at each physical region uses that region's own labelled
	cut bound. For all sufficiently large replica counts $k$, the final charge
	at interpolation parameter $1$ satisfies
	\begin{align}
		-\frac{\log N_{\rm end,k}^2}{k}
		 & \le2\log d_*-\log z
		+W(B_{\rm sh}/z+B_{\rm exc})+\rho_{\rm end,k},\notag
	\end{align}
	where $\rho_{\rm end,k}\to0$.

	Setting
	\begin{align}
		\delta_n & =C_\delta\epsilon^{-1}
		\left(n^{e-\mu}+a^{1/4}(\log n)^{C_{l,\delta}}\right),\notag
	\end{align}
	there exists $\rho_k\to0$ such that, for every sufficiently large $k$, one
	charge round and one parameter $p_k\in[\epsilon/2,\epsilon]$ satisfy
	\begin{align}
		\frac{\mathcal Q_k(p_k)}{KnD} & \le\delta_n+\rho_k,\notag        \\
		E_{{\rm def},k}               & \le Cn^\ell D^2W^2(\log n)^{C_l}
		\left(\delta_n^{1/8}+\epsilon+n^{-100}\right)
		+Cn^{-1000}+\rho_k,
		\label{eq:al_scanner_source_energy}
	\end{align}
	where $v_k$ is the normalized filtered vector at the selected charge and
	parameter, the filtered norm is positive, and
	$E_{{\rm def},k}=(\langle v_k,\overline{\widetilde H}_k v_k\rangle-e_t)/(g/2)\ge0$.

	With the normalized Schmidt truncation $\psi$, its auxiliary entropy
	$S^\prime$, and the terminal-region functions and charge-tree weights
	of Theorem~\ref{thm:arealaw-supplied-target-norm-bounds} for these same
	physical data, the selected point satisfies the rough upper comparison
	\begin{align}
		-\frac{\log N_k^2}{k} & \le2\log d_*-\log z
		+W(B_{\rm sh}/z+B_{\rm exc})+R_k^{\rm RU},\notag
	\end{align}
	and the rough lower comparison
	(\ref{eq:arealaw-supplied-rough-lower}) for every $0<\tau\le1/2$.
	When $a\sqrt{\mathcal B}\le\min\{c_0,1\}/10$ for $C_0=512\mathrm e(2/g)$ and
	$c_0=[32\sqrt{1+2/g}]^{-1}$, the same state, typical set, labels, charge, and
	interpolation parameter also satisfy both sharp comparisons
	(\ref{eq:arealaw-supplied-sharp-bounds}), with norm remainders that tend to
	zero uniformly in the selected charge and parameter for fixed finite system
	and $W$.

	Throughout these bounds, the input coefficient $C_e$ remains unchanged,
	whereas the output shell coefficient may exceed $\max\{C_e,1\}+1$ and the
	density coefficient $C_\delta$ may be positive even when $C_e=0$;
	see~\cite{gap:arealaw2d_scanner_inputs}.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[The scanner estimate]
	\label{thm:al_scanner_source}
	\lean{TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.CollarScan.scannerStatement}
	\leanok
	\uses{def:al_scanner_source_statement}
	The scanner statement in Definition~\ref{def:al_scanner_source_statement}
	holds for $J\ge0$, $\Delta>0$ and $C_T\ge0$, establishing
	\cite[Proposition~9.2]{OpenAI2026AreaLaw} with all four norm comparisons
	under the coefficient convention in~\cite{gap:arealaw2d_scanner_inputs}.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
	\leanok
	\uses{thm:arealaw-source-selected-energy,thm:arealaw-supplied-target-norm-bounds,
		thm:al_scan_common_status_budget,thm:al_positive_prefix_entropy_transfer,
		thm:al_scan_actual_rough_upper,thm:al_augmented_prevector_norm,thm:al_lattice_counts}
	Choose the truncation coefficient from
	Theorem~\ref{thm:arealaw-source-selected-energy} before fixing $q$.
	Because the anchor of a support of range $R$ has multiplicity at most
	$\mu_*=2^{1+2R(R+1)-1}$, we set $C_1=3\mu_*+1$ and apply that theorem
	with entropy coefficient $\max\{C_e,1\}$. This produces a truncated ground
	vector $\widetilde\Omega$, a typical set $E$, and a label sequence
	$\lambda_k$, together with a selected charge and parameter for each
	admissible $W$ satisfying the density, energy, and rough upper bounds.
	For this same vector and typical set, we retain the trace-distance bound
	(\texttt{08-scanner.tex}, lines~400--403) and the typical-window mass bound
	$0<1-n^{-100}\le z\le1$ (\texttt{08-scanner.tex}, lines~411--414), while the
	augmented-to-global norm identity transfers $\|\mathrm{pre}_k\|\le1$ for
	all $k>0$ (\texttt{07-comparators.tex}, lines~130--140).

	Applying Theorem~\ref{thm:arealaw-supplied-target-norm-bounds} to these same
	data yields a rough lower bound that is uniform over charge rounds and
	parameters. Combining the common cut coefficient $C_{B,\rm cut}$ and norm
	coefficient $C_{B,\rm norm}$, we set
	\begin{align}
		C_b        & =\max\{C_{B,\rm cut},C_{B,\rm norm},
		\max\{C_e,1\}+1,1+3\log q\},\notag                \\
		\mathcal B & =C_bnD(\log n)^{12},\notag
	\end{align}
	since enlarging the marginal parameter preserves both sharp comparisons and
	strengthens their smallness hypothesis.

	Finally, entropy continuity on each whole-row positive-depth prefix yields
	$B_{\rm sh}=\max\{C_e,1\}(nL^e+n)+n^{-247}$, and we set
	$B_{\rm exc}=1+3nD\log q$, both of which obey the stated bounds because
	$n\ge2$ and $D\ge1$. The uniform rough upper estimate at the final charge
	gives the terminal-round bound with its own vanishing remainder, and
	intersecting the eventual selected-point and sharp-comparison bounds at the
	point chosen by the energy theorem completes the proof after summing the
	nonnegative density and energy remainders.
\end{proof}
