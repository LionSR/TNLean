\chapter{Small mutual information across a collar}
\label{ch:area_law_selected_charge}

Chapter~\ref{ch:area_law_collar_scan}, a charge in the terminology
of~\cite[Section~9]{OpenAI2026AreaLaw}. This chapter selects that step, bounds
the energy and the density there, and deduces that a target region shares
little information with the sites beyond its collar.
