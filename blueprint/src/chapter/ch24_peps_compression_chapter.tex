\chapter{Compression of distributed circuits}
\label{ch:party_circuits}

In a distributed circuit the registers belong to parties that act privately on
their own registers and interact only through bipartite vectors and covectors.
\cite[Section~5]{OpenAI2026PolynomialPEPS} replaces these bipartite resources,
whose dimension is unrestricted, by resources of polynomial dimension in the
calculation of the final density. This chapter formalizes distributed circuits,
eliminates the normalized pair effects, and collects the operations of each
party.
