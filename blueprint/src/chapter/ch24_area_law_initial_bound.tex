\chapter{Spectral tails of reduced states and a first entropy bound on boxes}
\label{ch:area_law_initial_bound}

The first stage of the proof of the area law \cite[Section~3]{OpenAI2026AreaLaw}
uses the spectral gap to bound the tails of the negative logarithms of the
eigenvalues of reduced states. Optimizing products of positive operators on
nested rectangles then gives a first bound $S_\Omega(A\cap Q)\le Cr^{1+e_0}$,
with an exponent $e_0<1$, for every box $Q$ of side at most $r$ far enough
from the boundary of the region $A$.
