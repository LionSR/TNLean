\chapter{Geometry of the dyadic squares}
\label{ch:peps_frames_dyadic_geometry}

The parties of the approximation are indexed by dyadic squares, and sites move
between parties while a reference vector stays equal to the ground state, up to
encodings of small patches \cite[Sections~6 and~7]{OpenAI2026PolynomialPEPS}.
This chapter sets up the geometry of the dyadic squares: the bands along the
edges of a repainted square, the angular separation of these bands, the point
treatments, and the counts of the padded dyadic hierarchy. The encoded frames
that carry the ground state through the changes of ownership follow in the next
chapter.
