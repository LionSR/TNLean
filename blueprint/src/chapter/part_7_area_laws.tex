\part{Area laws and efficient approximation}
\label{part:area_laws}

Ground states of gapped local Hamiltonians satisfy area laws and are
approximated efficiently by tensor networks \cite[Section~II.C]{Cirac2021Matrix}.
This part proves the one-dimensional area law and the approximation by matrix
product states, Hastings' area law, the relation between entropy scaling and
approximability, algorithms for the ground state of gapped chains, and in two
dimensions the area law for gapped Hamiltonians and the approximation by
projected entangled pair states of polynomial bond dimension.
