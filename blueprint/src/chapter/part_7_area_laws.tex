\part{The two-dimensional area law}
\label{part:area_laws}

Ground states of gapped local Hamiltonians satisfy area laws and are
approximated efficiently by tensor networks \cite[Section~II.C]{Cirac2021Matrix}.
This part proves that for every ground state of a finite-range Hamiltonian with
a spectral gap on a finite lattice domain, the entropy of a region is bounded by
a constant times the number of edges crossing its boundary
\cite{OpenAI2026AreaLaw}. The proof starts from a first entropy bound on boxes,
improves it by moving a boundary through a collar around a region, makes the
information across a slightly wider collar smaller than any inverse power of
the system size, and divides the region into two families of smaller regions
whose entropies cancel. The first chapter collects the swaps of finite sites,
the information reset, and the fact that a gapped ground state of a sum of two
decoupled Hamiltonians is a product vector; the two following parts use them
too.
