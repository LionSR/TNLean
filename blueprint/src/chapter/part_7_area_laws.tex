\part{Area laws from a global spectral gap}
\label{part:area_laws}

Ground states of gapped local Hamiltonians satisfy area laws and are
approximated efficiently by tensor networks \cite[Section~II.C]{Cirac2021Matrix}.
This part proves the two-dimensional area law for a gapped finite-range
Hamiltonian and the approximation of gapped square-grid ground states by
projected entangled pair states of polynomial bond dimension, and then the
one-dimensional versions of both: the area law on a chain (Theorem~A) and the
approximation by matrix product states (Theorem~B), proved by specializing the
same arguments to a chain.
