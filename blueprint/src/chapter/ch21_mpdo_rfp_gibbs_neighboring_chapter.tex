\chapter{Neighboring operators and trace matrices of simple fixed points}
\label{ch:mpdo_rfp_gibbs_neighboring}

Continuing the analysis of simple fixed points from
Chapter~\ref{ch:mpdo_rfp_gibbs}, following
\cite[Appendix~C.2]{Cirac2016MPDO_arXiv}, this chapter contracts neighboring
sector tensors into the neighboring operators $\eta_{k,h}$ and shows that their
bond products commute. It then closes the sector tensors, rephases them
coherently, prepares normalized states by completed channels, and uses
primitivity to show that the matrix of traces of the neighboring operators has
rank one.
