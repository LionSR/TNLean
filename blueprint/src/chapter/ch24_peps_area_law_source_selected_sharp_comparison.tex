\section{Common truncated ground vector for the selected sharp comparison}
\label{sec:arealaw-source-selected-sharp-comparison}

The source consumer now joins the moment, prevector, endpoint, selector,
energy and comparison constructions without another physical-state or
label choice. It retains an explicit selected-density expression rather
than a final negative power of the system size.

\begin{lemma}[The same truncated ground vector at the selected charge]
    \label{thm:arealaw-source-selected-sharp-comparison}
    \lean{TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.CollarScan.exists_sourceTarget_selected_actualCharge_sharp_comparison,TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.CollarScan.sourceSelectedSharpThreshold,TNLean.PEPS.AreaLaw.Scan.CollarScan.exists_sourceTarget_selected_actualCharge_sharp_comparison_of_threshold_le}
    \leanok
    Fix the local dimension $q\ge1$, range, interaction bound $J\ge0$,
    physical gap $\Delta>0$, target-size coefficient $C_T\ge0$, anchor
    multiplicity $\mathfrak m$, charge coefficient
    $C_1>0$ with $3\mathfrak m\le C_1$, and a real decay parameter $P$.
    There are positive $C_{\rm tr},C_{\rm target},C_B,c_t$, real propagation
    coefficients and exponents, coefficient constants $C\ge1$, $C_\ell\ge0$,
    and a threshold $N\ge2$ preceding
    every finite physical instance, as follows.

    Let $\Omega$ be an original gapped ground vector on the finite domain.
    The scanner uses the induced domain graph, its depth is the ambient
    depth of a nonempty target $T$, and its anchors lie in their original
    supports with multiplicity at most $\mathfrak m$. Assume $n\ge N$,
    $|T|\le C_Tn^2$, and retain the explicit scales
    \begin{align}
        L     & =\lfloor n^{1-1/100000}\rfloor,       &
        m     & =\lfloor n^{1/4}\rfloor,              &
        D     & =\lceil n^{1/1000000}\rceil,\nonumber   \\
        K     & =L\mathbin{\mathrm{div}}(8m),         &
        r_0   & =\lceil C_{\rm tr}\log^2n\rceil,      &
        S.C_1 & =C_1.
        \label{eq:arealaw-source-selected-scales}
    \end{align}
    The target's ambient layers through $L$ have size at most $n$,
    and its clearance exceeds $2L+10r_0$. Assume explicitly that
    $2r_0\le D$, $1\le D$ and $4D\le m$.
    For real $C_e,\eta$, suppose every original-state positive-depth
    prefix through $L$ has entropy at most $C_e(nL^\eta+n)$.

    Put
    \begin{align}
        g           & =\Delta/\operatorname{positiveNormalization}(1,\Delta/2,J), &
        \varepsilon & =\min\{n^{-1000},g/4\},\nonumber                                                                     \\
        B           & =C_BnD(\log n)^{12},                                        &
        C_0         & =512\exp(1)(2/g),\nonumber                                                                           \\
        w           & =n^{3/5},                                                   & B_{\rm sh} & =C_e(nL^\eta+n)+n^{-247}.
        \label{eq:arealaw-source-selected-budgets}
    \end{align}
    One unit truncated ground vector $\Omega_t$, with energy
    $0\le e\le\varepsilon$ and gap $g/2$, has the stated quantitative
    trace distance from $\Omega$. Its target typical set $E$
    has mass $0<1-n^{-100}\le z\le1$ and the complete eigenvalue window
    $[\exp(-S_X-w),\exp(-S_X+w)]$. Its target cut weight is at most
    $C_{\rm target}n(\log n)^{12}$. In particular $d=|E|>0$.

    One label sequence $\lambda_k$ for this same physical vector is
    chosen before $W$. Its initial vectors are nonzero and have norm at
    most one for positive replica counts, and
    $\log\dim\lambda_k-kS'=o(k)$ for the entropy $S'$ of the
    compressed typical auxiliary state. For every
    $0<W\le n^{1/20000}$, set $a=W/K$ and let
    $\ell_{\dim}$ be the physical propagation logarithmic dimension
    bound. Assume explicitly $a\ell_{\dim}\le c_t$.

    Replica-remainder constants and nonnegative sequences can then be
    chosen before every $[p_0,p_1]$ with $0<p_0<p_1<1$ and every selected charge
    point. Eventually in $k$, there are a charge stage $s_k$ and
    $p_k\in[p_0,p_1]$ such that
    \begin{align}
        \frac{\mathcal Q_k}{KnD}
         & \le\frac{2(C_1+1)}{Wnm(p_1-p_0)}
        \left[B_{\rm rough}
            +2nm\,C_{\rm ent}aK a^{1/4}\ell_{\dim}^{e_{\rm ent}}
        +R_{\rm end}(k)\right],\nonumber                                          \\
        B_{\rm rough}
         & =2\log d-\log z+W\left(\frac{B_{\rm sh}}z+1+3nD\log q\right),\nonumber \\
        R_{\rm end}(k)
         & =R_{\rm Bell}(k)+(C_i+2nmC_b)\frac{\log(k+2)}k
        \longrightarrow0.
        \label{eq:arealaw-source-selected-density}
    \end{align}
    At this same point,
    \begin{align}
        0\le\delta_k=\frac{\mu_k-e}{g/2}
         & \le\frac{e+\operatorname{cap}_k+r_k}{g/2}, & r_k & \longrightarrow0,
        \label{eq:arealaw-source-selected-energy}
    \end{align}
    with the density cap from
    (\ref{eq:arealaw-selected-sharp-density-cap}). For every
    $0<\tau\le1/2$, the exact terminal-weight comparison
    (\ref{eq:arealaw-actual-sharp-comparison}) holds with
    $K_{\rm mom}=C_0B$ and $\delta$ replaced by the right side of
    (\ref{eq:arealaw-source-selected-energy}). All replica
    remainder limits fix the physical system and weight first.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
    \leanok
    \uses{thm:arealaw-target-sharp-moments,
        thm:al_global_schmidt_bell_prevector,
        thm:arealaw-actual-selected-energy,
        thm:arealaw-source-endpoint-loss,
        thm:arealaw-rough-selection-numerator,
        thm:arealaw-selected-density-normalization,
        thm:al_scan_actual_charge_energy_coefficients,
        thm:arealaw-actual-sharp-comparison,
        thm:arealaw-selected-sharp-composition}
    Choosing the source and coefficient constants and combining their size
    thresholds with the continuity threshold for volume coefficient
    $C_T+1$ precedes the physical data and $C_e,\eta$.
    Invoking the common target-window moment construction once and then the
    physical Schmidt--Bell selection once gives a single vector that satisfies
    both factor-ten smallness bounds, hence also the factor-six bounds.

    Transferring its exact eigen-equation across the augmented
    copy-mean coordinate identity, applying the selector and
    finite-family propagation theorem to that same initial vector,
    transferring the source prefix entropy to the same truncated vector,
    applying the rough endpoint comparison using its unchanged
    selected-state projection mass, accumulating the $2nm$ logarithmic
    propagation costs, and normalizing the selector's numerator
    gives (\ref{eq:arealaw-source-selected-density}).

    Applying the coefficient theorem and the uniform
    sharp comparison at the unchanged stage and parameter then completes the proof: the
    energy error and $M/\tau$ are nonnegative, so the same cap can be
    substituted, while the lower and upper replica errors retain their
    factors $R_-$ and $2R_+/W$.
\end{proof}

No selected-point, endpoint, coefficient or sharp-comparison conclusion is
assumed by this theorem. It still retains the explicit separation and
propagation smallness premises, the source entropy input, the finite-size
density expression and the pre-gap and maximum. A final prescribed
density power, safe-box entropy bounds and the six-error absorption are
further obligations.
