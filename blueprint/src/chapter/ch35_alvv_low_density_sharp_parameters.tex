\section{Independent scales for sharp low-energy approximation}

The clipping threshold must be chosen independently of the polynomial
window length to retain the three-quarter power of the accuracy logarithm.
The following numerical choice supplies the squared-buffer comparison budget
and the Chebyshev exponent for the same pair of parameters. The physical
approximation and its global matrix product representation are separate.

\begin{lemma}[Sharp scales at a fixed low-energy bandwidth]
    \label{lem:ld_sharp_independent_parameters}
    \lean{Real.exists_sharp_low_density_scalar_parameters}
    \leanok
    For every fixed $\eta>0$ there is $C_\eta\ge21$, chosen before
    $0<g\le1$ and $0<\delta\le1$, with the following property.
    Put $a=16+\log(1/g)+\log(1/\delta)$ and $t=C_\eta a$.
    There is an integer $\ell\ge1$ such that
    \begin{align}
      \ell&\le\frac8g+(7C_\eta)^{1/4}g^{-1/4}a^{3/4},\notag\\
      19+\eta&\le t,\notag\\
      \xi&=288e^{11/8}e^{-(t-\eta)/8}\le1,\notag\\
      2(6+\eta)\xi&\le\frac\delta{100}g^2.
      \label{eq:ld_sharp_scales}
    \end{align}
    For every $0<R\le3t+4\ell$ one also has
    $\bigl(2e^{-2\ell^2\sqrt{g/R}}\bigr)^2\le\delta/8$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}\leanok
    Let $C_0$ be the existing universal independent-threshold constant,
    $B=6+\eta$ and $C_\eta=\eta+C_0(2+\log B)$.
    Apply its residual estimate at buffer $g^2$ and accuracy
    $\tau=\delta/B$. Its logarithmic scale
    $u=1+2\log(1/g)+\log B+\log(1/\delta)$ satisfies
    $u\le(2+\log B)a$, so $t\ge\eta+C_0u$.
    Consequently $\xi\le\tau g^2/10^6$, which gives the residual
    assertions in (\ref{eq:ld_sharp_scales}).
    The fourth-root integer-window choice for $t=C_\eta a$ gives the
    stated bound on $\ell$ and $a\le\ell^2\sqrt{g/R}$.
    Since $a\ge16$ and $\log(1/\delta)\le a$, the squared Chebyshev
    estimate is at most $\delta/8$.
\end{proof}
