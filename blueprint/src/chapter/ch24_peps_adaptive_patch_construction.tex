\section{Adaptive patch constraints}
\label{sec:peps_adaptive_patch_construction}
The logarithmic patch construction combines an entropy bound for the original
ground state with energy estimates for minimizing filtered states. Orthogonalizing
the resulting nested cylinders gives patches whose total inside dimension is
polynomial in the side length.
