\chapter{Spectral Gaps at Long Interaction Ranges}
\label{ch:parent_hamiltonian_long_range_gaps}

Normalizing a normal tensor by its spectral radius and a gauge by the square
root of a positive fixed point extends the martingale bounds of
Chapter~\ref{ch:parent_hamiltonian_martingale} to arbitrary normal tensors.
This chapter proves that every prescribed gap below one is reached by the
periodic parent Hamiltonian at a sufficiently long interaction range, and that
the gap depends continuously on the tensor.
