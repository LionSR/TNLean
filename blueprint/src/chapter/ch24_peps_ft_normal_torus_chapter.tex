\chapter{The Fundamental Theorem for normal projected entangled pair states on the torus}
\label{ch:peps_ft_normal_torus}

A translationally invariant PEPS lives on the torus. This chapter proves the
Fundamental Theorem for translationally invariant normal tensors on the
$n\times m$ torus with $n,m\ge 7$ whose $2\times 3$ and $3\times 2$ regions are
injective~\cite[Theorem~3]{Molnar2018NormalPEPS}: two such tensors that
generate the same state are related by horizontal and vertical gauges and a
scalar $\lambda$ with $\lambda^{nm}=1$
(Corollary~\ref{thm:fundamentalTheorem_normalTorusPEPS}). The proof produces a
translation-covariant gauge generated by one horizontal and one vertical
reference matrix
(Corollary~\ref{thm:fundamentalTheorem_normalTorusPEPS_unconditional}) and
derives the condition on $\lambda$ from proportionalities at every site
(Corollary~\ref{cor:peps_lambda_pow_card_torus_eq_one_of_twoBlockProportional}).
The reference gauges are obtained by comparing overlapping blocking windows on
the torus.
