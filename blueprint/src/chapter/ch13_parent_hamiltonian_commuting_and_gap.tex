\chapter{Commuting Parent Hamiltonians}
\label{ch:parent_hamiltonian_commuting_and_gap}

For normal matrix product states, commutation of neighboring parent-Hamiltonian
terms corresponds to the renormalization fixed-point condition under canonical
and ground-space hypotheses. Commuting support projectors and their idempotent
product then characterize decorrelation across separated regions. For
frustration-free sums of local projections, the anticommutator martingale
criterion and cyclic norm-compression bounds establish uniform lower bounds on
the spectral gap above the ground space.
