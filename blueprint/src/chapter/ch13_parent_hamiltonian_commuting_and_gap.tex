\chapter{Commuting Parent Hamiltonians and Spectral Gaps}
\label{ch:parent_hamiltonian_commuting_and_gap}

This chapter studies commuting parent Hamiltonians and spectral gaps for
matrix product states. For normal tensors, commutation of neighboring parent
terms characterizes renormalization fixed points and zero correlation length,
expressed through commuting support projectors and their idempotent product.
For frustration-free sums of local projections, spectral-gap estimates are
established via the anticommutator martingale criterion and cyclic
norm-compression bounds, yielding uniform lower bounds on the gap above the
ground space.
