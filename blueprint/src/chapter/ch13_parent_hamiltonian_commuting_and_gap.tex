\chapter{Commuting Parent Hamiltonians and Spectral Gaps}
\label{ch:parent_hamiltonian_commuting_and_gap}

For normal matrix product states, the first sections compare commutation of
neighboring parent terms with renormalization fixed points and zero correlation
length under the stated hypotheses. Decorrelation is expressed through
commuting support projectors and their idempotent product.

The remaining sections establish spectral-gap estimates for
frustration-free sums of local projections. The anticommutator form of the
martingale criterion and cyclic norm-compression bounds turn control of
overlapping ground spaces into a uniform lower bound above the ground space.
