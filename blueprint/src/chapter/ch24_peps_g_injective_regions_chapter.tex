\chapter{Regions of regular \texorpdfstring{$G$}{G}-injective tensors on the torus}%
\label{ch:peps_g_injective_regions}

Contracting a finite region of the torus PEPS of
Section~\ref{sec:peps_torus_site_tensor} gives a tensor whose virtual legs are
the bonds leaving the region. This chapter studies these tensors when every
bond carries the regular representation of $G$. It computes their Gram
operators, removes group elements inserted on their bonds by gauge
transformations, and lifts regions of the torus to the plane to control how
their complements wind around it. The main result is the entropy of the
physical cut of a connected region: the reduced state is flat, and its rank is
fixed by the number of bonds that cross the cut \cite{Schuch2010PEPS}.
