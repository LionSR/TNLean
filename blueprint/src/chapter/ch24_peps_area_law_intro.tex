\chapter{Two families of regions and the proof of the area law}
\label{ch:peps_area_law}

Given the two ordered families of Chapter~\ref{ch:peps_area_law_geometry}, two
applications of the chain rule for entropy cancel the entropies of the
individual regions, and the information bounds of the earlier chapters sum to
the area law \cite[Section~11]{OpenAI2026AreaLaw}. This chapter carries out the
cancellation, deduces the area law, and computes the constant produced by the
proof.
