\chapter{The martingale method for spectral gaps}
\label{ch:parent_hamiltonian_martingale}

A parent Hamiltonian is gapped when the smallest nonzero eigenvalue is bounded
away from zero uniformly in the chain length. The martingale method of Fannes,
Nachtergaele and Werner~\cite{Fannes1992Finitely,Nachtergaele1996Spectral}
reduces a uniform gap to an estimate on the overlaps of the ground spaces of
neighbouring local terms. This chapter proves the method and the overlap
estimates it needs.
