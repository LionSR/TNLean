\chapter{Three-Cocycles and the CZX Model}
\label{ch:mpu_cocycles}

The fusion tensors of a group represented by matrix product unitaries are
determined up to scalars, and the scalars define a third-cohomology class. This
chapter studies this gauge freedom and works out the CZX model: its
GHZ--blocked-cluster tensor, the gauge-invariant subspace of its circuit tuple,
and its defects.
