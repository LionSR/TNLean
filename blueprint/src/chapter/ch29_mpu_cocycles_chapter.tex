\chapter{Three-cocycles and the gauge freedom of fusion tensors}
\label{ch:mpu_cocycles}

The fusion tensors of a group represented by matrix product unitaries are
determined up to scalars, and the scalars define a third-cohomology class. This
chapter first develops scalar cochains, their fusion gauges and their
cohomology, and identifies the classes of scalar three-cocycles under fusion
gauges with degree-three group cohomology
(Theorem~\ref{thm:mpug_h3_group_cohomology}). It then constructs the anomaly
three-cocycle from the two fusion trees of three operators
(Theorem~\ref{thm:mpug_anomaly_three_cocycle}), shows that its class does not
depend on the choice of fusion tensors
(Corollary~\ref{cor:mpug_anomaly_class}), and treats daggered fusion tensors
and reductions through action tensors.
