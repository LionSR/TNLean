\chapter{Three-cocycles and the gauge freedom of fusion tensors}
\label{ch:mpu_cocycles}

The fusion tensors of a group represented by matrix product unitaries are
determined up to scalars, and the scalars define a third-cohomology class. This
chapter studies this gauge freedom: it constructs the anomaly three-cocycle,
shows that a nontrivial anomaly obstructs invariant normal states, and treats
blocks permuted by the group, the domain walls between them, and their
$L$-symbols. The next chapter works out the CZX model.
