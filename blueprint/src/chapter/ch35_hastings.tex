\chapter{Hastings' area law for gapped chains}
\label{ch:hastings_window}

Hastings~\cite{Hastings2007AreaLaw} proved that the ground state of a gapped
chain with finite-range interactions obeys an area law: the entropy of every
block of sites is bounded by a constant independent of the length of the
chain. The argument is by contradiction. A cut of large entropy produces a
window of sites on which every cut also has large entropy. On such a window
the approximate factorization of the previous chapter, through the bootstrap
lemma and a lower bound on the mutual information across a cut, shows that
the largest entropy of an interval of $2l$ sites falls short of twice that of
$l$ sites by an amount growing linearly in $l$. Iterating this strengthened
doubling inequality bounds the length of the window, and hence the entropy.
The chapter ends with two further consequences drawn in the source: the
approximation of the ground state by matrix product states, and the decay of
correlations of operators that are diagonal in the Schmidt basis of a cut.
