\chapter{The two-dimensional area law and polynomial PEPS approximation}
\label{ch:two_dimensional_main_results}

This chapter states the main results of~\cite{OpenAI2026AreaLaw}
and~\cite{OpenAI2026PolynomialPEPS}. Theorem~\ref{thm:openai_uniform_area_law}
bounds the entropy of every region of a finite lattice domain by a constant
times the number of edges crossing its boundary, for every unit ground vector
of a finite-range Hamiltonian with a spectral gap; the constant depends only
on the local dimension, the range, the interaction strength and the gap.
Theorem~\ref{thm:openai_square_area_law} is the case of the square grid.
Theorem~\ref{thm:openai_polynomial_peps} approximates, up to a phase and within
distance $1/L$, every gapped ground vector of a nearest-neighbour Hamiltonian
on the $L\times L$ grid by a projected entangled pair state of bond dimension
polynomial in $L$. The chapter also records the constants produced by the
proofs and the consequences for rectangular regions.
Chapters~\ref{ch:area_graph_foundations}--\ref{ch:peps_area_law} prove the
area law, and
Chapters~\ref{ch:peps_approximation}--\ref{ch:peps_dyadic_protocol} prove the
approximation, and
Chapters~\ref{ch:one_dimensional_preliminaries}--\ref{ch:one_dimensional_protocol}
prove the one-dimensional versions of both results.
