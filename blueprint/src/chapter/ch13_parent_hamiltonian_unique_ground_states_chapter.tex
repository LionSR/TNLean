\chapter{Unique ground states of injective and normal matrix product states}
\label{ch:parent_hamiltonian_unique_ground_states}

The local ground spaces of Chapter~\ref{ch:parent_hamiltonian} satisfy an
intersection property that ensures uniqueness of the periodic ground state
$V^{(N)}(A)$ for injective and normal matrix product states. For injective tensors with positive bond dimension
$D\ge1$, system size $N\ge2$, and interaction range $1<L\le N$, the parent
Hamiltonian is frustration-free with unique ground state $V^{(N)}(A)$.
For normal tensors with positive bond dimension $D \ge 1$ and injectivity length
$L_0$, uniqueness holds whenever $L_0 < L \le N$ with $N \ge L_0 + 1$;
word-span injectivity after blocking determines the boundary-closure identities
that match open-interval restrictions to the periodic chain.
The chapter also treats open chains with varying bond dimensions, invertible
on-site maps, and physical blocking.
