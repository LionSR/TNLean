\paragraph{The four boundary contractions.}
Put $A=(0,1)$, $B=(1,1)$, $C=(0,0)$ and $D=(1,0)$.
Write $H_{xy}=((x,y),\mathrm H)$ and $V_{xy}=((x,y),\mathrm V)$.
The four contractions in \cite[Theorem~5.5]{Schuch2010PEPS} are
$\alpha(M)=\mathcal C_{0,0}(M)$, $\beta(N)=\mathcal C_{1,0}(N)$,
$\gamma(P)=\mathcal C_{0,1}(P)$ and $\delta(Q)=\mathcal C_{1,1}(Q)$.
Each central tensor below is one arbitrary joint tensor on eight endpoints;
the four outer wires are the four uncut identity pairings.
A superscript $+$ identifies a head endpoint, and $-$ a tail endpoint.
Thus two wires bearing the same bond label but different superscripts are
independent arguments of the boundary tensor, not an identity pairing.
The uncontracted physical legs are $i_A,i_B,i_C,i_D$.
The drawings move the boundary tensor to the centre without changing any
incidence. Their displayed geometry does not identify parallel bonds.
