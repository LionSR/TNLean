\chapter{Non-normal tensors and preparation with measurements}
\label{ch:log_depth_non_normal}

Tensors that are not normal decompose into a basis of normal tensors, and their
states are prepared block by block. Measurements followed by corrections that
depend on the outcomes shorten the depth further~\cite{Malz2024Preparation}.
This chapter treats both: the preparation of states of non-normal tensors, and
tree circuits with measurements, including registers of several sites, trees
on blocks of any length, and non-normal states in double-logarithmic depth.
