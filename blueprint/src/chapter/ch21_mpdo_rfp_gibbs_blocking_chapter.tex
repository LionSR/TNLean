\chapter{Physical blocking and vertical sectors}
\label{ch:mpdo_rfp_gibbs_blocking}

Renormalization groups adjacent physical sites into
blocks~\cite{Cirac2016MPDO_arXiv}. This chapter shows that blocking a matrix
product operator tensor commutes with the operator product and with closed
chains (Theorem~\ref{thm:mpdo_closed_chain_physical_blocking}), and reads the
blocked tensor in vertical canonical form. On the vertical sectors it
constructs the refinement and coarse-graining maps, proves that they are
completely positive, trace preserving and mutually inverse
(Theorem~\ref{thm:mpdo_transported_vertical_sector_composites_identity}), and
decomposes products of vertical sectors by positive fusion
(Theorem~\ref{thm:mpdo_vertical_product_positive_fusion_decomposition}). The chapters that follow use
these results for the local structure of simple fixed points and for the
algebraic structure of general fixed points.
