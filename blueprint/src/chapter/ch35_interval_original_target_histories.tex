\section{Original-target histories of the actual interval recursion}

Fix the original ground space and all families, thresholds and trimming
parameters before sampling. The following events record the successive
spaces in the repeated-sampling construction of
\cite{Arad2016RigorousRG}, rather than just its final retained space.

\begin{definition}[Original-target history on an interval]
    \label{def:interval_original_target_history}
    \lean{TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.canonicalHalfSampledIntervalHistory,
        TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.canonicalIntervalResamplingHistory}\leanok
    For the interval $J=[p,r)$, identify the full chain with
    $\mathcal H_J\otimes\mathcal H_{J^c}$ and write $G_J$ for the original
    global ground space in these coordinates. Given a candidate $W$, let
    $Q_j(W,u)$ be the actual stopped projection after $j$ of the $R$
    prescribed unitary coordinates. Put
    $V_j(W,u)=I_J\operatorname{ran}Q_j(W,u)$, with the coordinate isometry
    $I_J$ from Definition~\ref{def:complete_interval_resampling_parent}.
    The event $H_J(W)$ consists of the sequences $u$ such that every
    $V_j(W,u)\otimes\mathcal H_{J^c}$, $0\le j\le R$, is $0.04$-close to
    $G_J$, and every active next space
    $V_{j+1}(W,u)\otimes\mathcal H_{J^c}$ is $3/200$-close to $G_J$.
    Here the step $j<R$ is active when
    $\operatorname{rank}Q_j(W,u)\ge\theta_J$.

    For the balanced recursion $S_\omega$, let $W_J(\omega)$ be the actual
    adjacent merge of the two child spaces of $J$. Its original-target
    history event is $\{\omega:\omega_J\in H_J(W_J(\omega))\}$.
\end{definition}

\begin{lemma}[Joint measurability of the original-target history]
    \label{lem:interval_original_target_history_measurable}
    \lean{TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.measurableSet_canonicalHalfSampledIntervalHistory,
        TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.measurableSet_canonicalIntervalResamplingHistory}\leanok
    If $x\mapsto P_{W(x)}$ is measurable, then
    $\{(x,u):u\in H_J(W(x))\}$ is measurable. In particular, the
    original-target history event of each actual long parent is measurable
    in the full collection of interval sequences.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}\leanok
    \uses{def:interval_original_target_history,
        lem:actual_interval_resampling_tree_probability}
    Reindex the candidate projection into the fixed finite coordinates.
    The stopped projections, their ranks and their full-exterior
    closeness tests are jointly measurable. The history event is a finite
    intersection of these tests and of the active-step implications.
    For the actual parent, both child projections and their adjacent
    merge projection are measurable; compose with
    $\omega\mapsto(\omega,\omega_J)$.
\end{proof}

\begin{lemma}[The true section at a parent's own coordinate]
    \label{lem:interval_original_target_history_section}
    \lean{TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.canonicalIntervalResamplingHistory_update_parent}\leanok
    Fix a baseline collection $\omega$. Replacing only $\omega_J$ by $u$
    leaves both actual child spaces unchanged. The resulting global
    history condition at $J$ is therefore exactly
    $u\in H_J(W_J(\omega))$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}\leanok
    \uses{def:interval_original_target_history,
        lem:independent_interval_tree_sections}
    Both children are strictly shorter than $J$. Their projections depend
    only on coordinates of intervals no longer than themselves, so the
    coordinate replacement leaves them unchanged. Substitution into
    Definition~\ref{def:interval_original_target_history} gives the stated
    equivalence.
\end{proof}
