\part{Projected entangled pair states}
\label{part:peps}

Projected entangled pair states extend matrix product states to two
dimensions. This part proves their Fundamental Theorem, treats injective and
$G$-injective tensors and quantum double models, and constructs the ground
spaces of regions and parent Hamiltonians
\cite[Sections~III.B and~IV.B]{Cirac2021Matrix}.
