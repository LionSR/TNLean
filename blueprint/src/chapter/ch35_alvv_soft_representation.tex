\section{Spectral comparison and an MPO for the same soft Hamiltonian}
\label{sec:alvv_soft_representation}

The physical simultaneous soft construction gives both spectral
comparisons and a tensor representation of one and the same matrix.
All ground data precede the comparison widths and the requested
operator approximation accuracy.

\begin{lemma}[The same physical soft Hamiltonian in both constructions]
    \label{lem:alvv_same_soft_representation}
    \lean{TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.ALVV.exists_five_region_soft_representation}
    \leanok
    \uses{lem:alvv_five_soft_residuals,lem:selected_regional_soft_mpo}
    On an $N+1$-site chain, retain the hypotheses and actual choices
    $e_j,E,H,S,p_j,t,m,G,\mu$ of
    Lemma~\ref{lem:alvv_five_soft_residuals}.
    These choices can be made to satisfy the following assertions
    simultaneously, for every selected set and every allowed
    estimate $e_j-10\leq p_j\leq e_j$.

    Write $C=|J|\leq4$, $D=10|S|$. For $\eta\geq0$, put
    $U=2(\eta+D+3C+2)$. Let $h,w>0$, $t,T\geq U$ and
    $T\geq\eta+3C+12$, and define
    \begin{align}
        \rho_H&=|S|\left(8Ce^{-(T-\eta-3C-12)/8}
             +\frac{t}{m}\left(\frac Tt\right)^m\right),\\
        \rho_G&=|S|\left(8Ce^{-(T-U)/8}
             +\frac{t}{m}\left(\frac Tt\right)^m\right),\qquad
        k=\left\lceil\frac{\eta+h}{2h}\right\rceil.
    \end{align}
    Then $0\leq E-\mu\leq\rho_G$. The original space
    $E_H(E+\eta)$ is
    $k(\rho_H+h)^2/(w+h)^2$-close to
    $E_G(\mu+\eta+\rho_G+w)$, while $E_G(\mu+\eta)$ is
    $k(\rho_G+h)^2/(w+h)^2$-close to $E_H(E+\eta+w)$.
    The constructed orthonormal eigenbases are fixed before all
    these comparison parameters. The original two residual bounds
    remain valid for this same $G$.

    For every $\xi>0$ and $t\geq3m$, define
    \begin{align}
        s&=|S|,\qquad \delta=\frac{\xi}{\max\{1,s\}},\qquad
        W=t2^{m+1},\qquad b=2(e^{1/3}-1),\\
        r&=\max\left\{1,
          \left\lceil\frac{\log\max\{1,N^2\}
            -\log\log(1+\delta/W)}{-\log b}\right\rceil\right\},\\
        D_0&=1+Nq^2,\qquad
        \chi=D_0+2s\bigl(D_0+(m+1)r^2q^r\bigr).
    \end{align}
    There is an actual site-dependent MPO $M$ of bond $\chi$, with
    identity closing matrix, such that $M=M^*$ and $\|G-M\|\leq\xi$.
    This tensor family is chosen without using any comparison
    width or spectral cutoff.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}\leanok
    Retain all physical ground data and both residual estimates of
    Lemma~\ref{lem:alvv_five_soft_residuals}.
    The actual normalized soft ground vector belongs to
    $E_G(\mu+\eta)$. Its reverse residual and $H-EI\geq0$ give
    $E-\mu\leq\rho_G$, by the attained-energy estimate in
    \cite[``Attained energies from restricted residuals'']{QICLean2026Blueprint}.
    The order $G\leq H$ and the constructed original ground vector
    already give $\mu\leq E$.

    Apply the finite-window perturbation estimate from
    \cite[``Spectral comparison with a derived ground-energy shift'']{QICLean2026Blueprint}.
    The original and soft lower endpoints are $E-h$ and $\mu-h$,
    so both counts equal $k$. The forward target cutoff exceeds its
    source cutoff by at least $w$ because $E-\mu\leq\rho_G$.
    The reverse target cutoff has the same separation because
    $\mu\leq E$. Neither direction assumes an intervening spectral gap.

    Put $F_j=p_jI+h_{m,t}(A_j-p_jI)$. The original five-region
    decomposition and cancellation of common energy shifts give
    \begin{align}
        G=\sum_i h_i-\Bigl(\sum_j e_j\Bigr)I
          +\sum_{j\in S}(F_j-A_j).
    \end{align}
    The lower bounds $p_j\leq e_j$ and $A_j-e_jI\geq0$ give
    $p_jI\leq A_j$. Positivity and $h_i\leq I$ give Hermiticity and
    $\|h_i\|\leq1$. Apply
    Lemma~\ref{lem:selected_regional_soft_mpo} to exactly the displayed
    matrix, with scalar shift $-\sum_j e_j$.
\end{proof}

These are corrected auxiliary conclusions for
\cite[lines 1373--1392]{Arad2016RigorousRG}.
The represented physical chain is nonempty; the residual theorem also
covers the scalar zero-site case. The explicit cluster estimate,
MPO bond, window count and losses are retained. No efficient GEN,
source-sharp parameter choice or running-time theorem is asserted.
The false printed cluster estimate and its counterexamples remain
recorded in \cite{gap:alvv16_cluster_approximation}.
