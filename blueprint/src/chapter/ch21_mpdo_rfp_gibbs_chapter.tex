\chapter{Gibbs states of commuting Hamiltonians and their local structure}
\label{ch:mpdo_rfp_gibbs}

Gibbs states of nearest-neighbour commuting Hamiltonians are renormalization
fixed points~\cite{Cirac2016MPDO_arXiv}. This chapter defines them as matrix
product density operators, blocks the physical sites, derives the local
structure of simple fixed points from saturation of the area law and zero
correlation length, and brings that structure to a commuting form on single
bonds.
