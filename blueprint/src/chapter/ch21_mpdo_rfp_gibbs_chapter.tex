\chapter{Gibbs states of commuting Hamiltonians and the Markov decomposition}
\label{ch:mpdo_rfp_gibbs}

Gibbs states of nearest-neighbour commuting Hamiltonians are renormalization
fixed points~\cite{Cirac2016MPDO_arXiv}. This chapter defines them as matrix
product density operators and shows that orthogonal sums of commuting sector
products take this form and preserve saturation of the area law. It then
begins the derivation of the local structure of simple fixed points from
saturation of the area law and zero correlation length: it fixes the weights
of the simple canonical form and factors the three-site reduced states through
the Markov decomposition given by equality in strong subadditivity. The next
two chapters complete that derivation and bring the structure to a commuting
form on single bonds.
