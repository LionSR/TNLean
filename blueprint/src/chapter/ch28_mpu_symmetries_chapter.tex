\chapter{Symmetries of matrix product unitaries}
\label{ch:mpu_symmetries}

This chapter classifies matrix product unitaries under conjugation, time
reversal, and transposition \cite{Cirac2017MPU}. Each symmetry is first
characterized by a local relation on the standard form of
Chapter~\ref{ch:mpu_fundamental_theorem}. Equivalence under a symmetry is then
defined. Conjugation-invariant matrix product unitaries are classified up to
strict equivalence when one source rank is odd (Theorem~\ref{conjphs}) and
when both are even (Theorem~\ref{prop:conjsym-strict-equiv}), and the
invariants that equivalent tensors must share are derived from the blocking
parity of the time-reversal sign (Theorem~\ref{prop:sigma1-and-sigma2-equal}).
