\chapter{Symmetries of matrix product unitaries}
\label{ch:mpu_symmetries}

This chapter classifies matrix product unitaries under conjugation, time
reversal, and transposition \cite{Cirac2017MPU}. Each symmetry is first
characterized by a local relation on the standard form of
Chapter~\ref{ch:mpu_fundamental_theorem}. Equivalence under a symmetry is then
defined, and the invariants that equivalent tensors must share, such as the
time-reversal sign, are derived.
