\section{The actual reverse soft residual on one region}
\label{sec:alvv_reverse_soft_residual}

The reverse estimate uses a quantitative high-energy floor for the same
actual soft function. Its positive denominator follows from the actual
soft ground energy and the actual crossing set; neither is an extra
hypothesis. The energy centers in this result are exact regional minima.
Uniformity over the ten-unit interval of estimated centers in the source
remains separate; see \cite{gap:alvv16_soft_regional_residuals}.

\begin{lemma}[Two physical residuals for the same regional soft operator]
    \label{lem:alvv_two_sided_soft_residuals}
    \lean{TNLean.MPS.AreaLaw.ALVV.exists_single_region_soft_truncation_residuals}
    \leanok
    \uses{lem:regional_barrier_hard_residual,lem:alvv_original_soft_residual,
        lem:two_cut_crossing_card,
        lem:three_region_sum_crossings}
    Let $q\geq1$, $0\leq l\leq r\leq L$, and let $0\leq h_i\leq I$
    be supported on the designated neighbouring sites of an $L$-site
    chain. The three actual internal regional sums have attained minimum
    energies $e_j$, with one common unit vector attaining them.
    These data can be chosen, together with an attained original ground
    energy $E\in[0,2]$, before the soft scale and order. Put
    \begin{align}
        H&=\sum_i h_i-\Bigl(\sum_j e_j\Bigr)I,&X&=A_1-e_1I.
    \end{align}
    For every $t>0$ and $m\geq2$, the same actual operator
    \begin{align}
        G=H-X+h_{m,t}(X)
    \end{align}
    is positive semidefinite and lies below $H$. It has an attained
    ground energy $\mu$ satisfying $0\leq\mu\leq E\leq2$.
    For $\eta>0$ and $T\geq\eta+7$,
    \begin{align}
        \|(H-G)|_{E_H(E+\eta)}\|
        \leq32e^{-(T-\eta)/8}+\frac{t}{m}\left(\frac Tt\right)^m.
    \end{align}
    For $\eta\geq0$, put $U=2(\eta+8)$. If $t\geq U$ and $T\geq U$,
    then
    \begin{align}
        \|(H-G)|_{E_G(\mu+\eta)}\|
        \leq16e^{-(T-U)/8}+\frac{t}{m}\left(\frac Tt\right)^m.
    \end{align}
    The operator $G$ and its ground energy are chosen before $\eta,T$.
    There is no spectral-gap or simplicity assumption.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}\leanok
    Retain the actual regional energies and original ground data from
    Lemma~\ref{lem:alvv_original_soft_residual}. Let $Y$ be the sum of
    the actual crossing bonds, and write
    \begin{align}
        B=(A_0-e_0I)+h_{m,t}(X)+(A_2-e_2I),\qquad G=B+Y.
    \end{align}
    All three terms of $B$ and all crossing bonds are positive.
    Hence $G\geq0$, while $h_{m,t}(X)\leq X$ gives $G\leq H$.
    Construct a unit ground vector of $G$. Its positive lower bound
    together with $G\leq H$, tested on the actual original ground
    vector, gives $\mu\leq E\leq2$.

    Disjoint regional supports imply that $B$ commutes with $A_1$.
    For $\varepsilon_{m,t}(R)=(t/m)(R/t)^m$, the actual soft function
    gives the projection order
    \begin{align}
        \bigl(R-\varepsilon_{m,t}(R)\bigr)
          \mathbf1_{[e_1+R,\infty)}(A_1)\leq B.
    \end{align}
    At $R=U$, the scale and order bounds give
    $\varepsilon_{m,t}(U)\leq U/2$. Thus the floor
    $b=U-\varepsilon_{m,t}(U)$ is at least $\eta+8$.
    By Lemma~\ref{lem:two_cut_crossing_card},
    \begin{align}
        b-(\mu+\eta)-2|J|\geq2.
    \end{align}
    Apply the existing weighted restriction argument at projection
    threshold $e_1+U$ and floor $b$. The reparametrization
    $r=e_1+U-b$, $u=b$ separates that threshold from the floor.
    The boundary forcing comes from the original local bonds.
    Factoring the hard excess above $e_1+T$ through this actual
    weighted map bounds its restriction to $E_G(\mu+\eta)$ by
    \begin{align}
        \frac{e^{-(T-U)/8-1}}{1/8}
        \frac{2|J|}{b-(\mu+\eta)-2|J|}
        \leq16e^{-(T-U)/8}.
    \end{align}
    Finally, apply the soft--hard residual comparison to the same
    positive matrix $X$ on this actual soft low-energy space.
    The additional error is $\varepsilon_{m,t}(T)$.
    The original-side assertion is the retained first-direction
    estimate for this same $G$.
\end{proof}

The inequalities are corrected auxiliary bounds for the soft-truncation
argument of Arad, Landau, Vazirani and Vidick~\cite[lines 1195--1255]{Arad2016RigorousRG}.
They do not assert the printed uniform energy estimate or its printed
spectral enlargement. Arbitrary estimated regional centers, several
simultaneous soft replacements, and the complete efficient-filter
parameter bounds remain separate. The false printed cluster estimate
and its counterexamples remain recorded in
\cite{gap:alvv16_cluster_approximation}.
