\chapter{Boundaries and fusion of matrix product operators}
\label{ch:mpo_boundaries}

A matrix product operator symmetry acts on a closed chain, but its fusion rules
and its action on states are read off from open chains with boundary
maps~\cite{Bultinck2017Anyons,GarreRubio2023MPOSym}. This chapter reconstructs
operators from arbitrary boundary maps, compares their decompositions, builds
canonical parents and adjoints, and derives the fusion pentagon for mixed
orientations. It ends with the gauge law of the multiplicity symbols and the
theorem that a state with a single injective block, acted on without
multiplicity, forces trivial $F$-symbols
(Theorem~\ref{thm:glm_single_block_obstruction}).
