\subsection{A uniform gap along the closed mixed interpolation}

Consider the mixed interpolation of \cite[Section~5]{GarreRubio2023MPOSym}.
For each $p\in\{0,1\}$, take any $D_p>0$ and endpoint tensor
$A_p=(A_p^i)_{i=0}^{D_p^2-1}$ whose letters span $\MN{D_p}$.
Thus the hypotheses are one-site injectivity and the stated square
physical alphabets. Write $A(\gamma)$ for their mixed interpolation
on physical dimension $(D_0+D_1)^2$, and write $H'_{\gamma,N}$ and
$H'^{\mathrm{open}}_{\gamma,N}$ for the periodic and nonwrapping sums
of its extended two-site interaction, respectively. All bounds below
concern these given tensors; the constants may depend on them.

\begin{theorem}[Fixed-window continuity of the actual open gap]
    \label{thm:mpo_mixed_open_gap_continuity}
    \lean{MPSTensor.continuous_periodicLocalInteractionES_family,
        MPSTensor.continuous_openInteractionHamiltonianES_family,
        MPSTensor.openInteractionHamiltonianES_parentInteractionES,
        MPSTensor.MPOSymmetry.mixedEndpoint_openInteractionHamiltonianES_eq_of_mem_Ioo,
        MPSTensor.MPOSymmetry.eventually_mixedEndpoint_open_gap,
        MPSTensor.MPOSymmetry.exists_uniform_mixedEndpoint_open_gap_fixed_volume}
    \leanok
    \uses{def:mpo_mixed_extended_support,def:injective}
    At every fixed $N\geq2$, the actual open Hamiltonian and its
    kernel projector depend continuously on $\gamma\in\R$. If it has
    gap $\eta>0$ at $\gamma_0$, then every $\eta'<\eta$ is a lower
    norm bound on the kernel complement of each Hamiltonian in a
    neighborhood of $\gamma_0$. For each fixed volume and each compact
    parameter set, there is a positive gap bound uniform on that set;
    the bound may depend on both. For $0<\gamma<1$, the actual
    open Hamiltonian equals the canonical open parent of $A(\gamma)$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
    \leanok
    \uses{thm:mpo_mixed_support_continuity,
        thm:mpo_mixed_extended_open_kernel,
        thm:kernel_gap_perturbation,thm:compact_kernel_gap}
    Extension by exterior identities and a fixed permutation of the
    physical coordinates preserve operator continuity. Summing the
    finitely many nonwrapping terms gives continuity of the open sum.
    Its exact kernel is the extended boundary range, whose projector
    is continuous. Apply the operator-and-kernel perturbation estimate
    and then compactness. In the interior the extended local projection
    equals the canonical one, so the two open sums coincide.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Actual periodic ground lines and fixed-volume gaps]
    \label{thm:mpo_mixed_periodic_line_continuity}
    \lean{MPSTensor.periodicMpvLineMap_injective_of_mpv_ne_zero,
        MPSTensor.continuous_periodicInteractionHamiltonianES_family,
        MPSTensor.MPOSymmetry.mpv_mixedEndpointInterpolation_zero,
        MPSTensor.MPOSymmetry.mixedEndpointInterpolation_one_swap,
        MPSTensor.MPOSymmetry.mpv_mixedEndpointInterpolation_one,
        MPSTensor.MPOSymmetry.mpv_mixedEndpointInterpolation_ne_zero,
        MPSTensor.MPOSymmetry.mixedEndpoint_periodicInteractionHamiltonianES_eq_of_mem_Ioo,
        MPSTensor.MPOSymmetry.mixedEndpoint_periodic_ker_eq_range_periodicMpvLineMap,
        MPSTensor.MPOSymmetry.mixedEndpoint_periodic_groundSpace_finrank_closedInterval,
        MPSTensor.MPOSymmetry.continuous_mixedEndpoint_periodic_ker_starProjection,
        MPSTensor.MPOSymmetry.exists_uniform_mixedEndpoint_periodic_gap_fixed_volume}
    \leanok
    \uses{def:mpo_mixed_extended_support,def:mpo_mixed_endpoint_swap,
        def:periodic-fixed-interaction,def:injective,def:mpv}
    At each endpoint, the actual weighted periodic vector equals the
    corresponding embedded endpoint vector for every positive length.
    For $N\geq2$ and $\gamma\in[0,1]$, the actual vector
    $\ket{V^{(N)}(A(\gamma))}$ is nonzero and
    \begin{align}
        \ker H'_{\gamma,N}
        =\spn_{\C}\{\ket{V^{(N)}(A(\gamma))}\}.
    \end{align}
    This one-dimensional kernel has a continuous orthogonal projector
    on $[0,1]$.
    For each fixed $N\geq2$, a positive periodic gap works throughout
    the closed parameter interval.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
    \leanok
    \uses{lem:asymex_absorbing_compression,
        thm:mpo_mixed_first_support,thm:mpo_mixed_second_endpoint_comparison,
        thm:mpo_mixed_interior,thm:mpo_mixed_periodic_endpoint_gaps,
        thm:mpv_ne_zero_blk_injective,thm:chain_ground_space_eq_mpv_blk,
        thm:continuous_injective_range_projector,thm:compact_kernel_gap}
    At zero, every weighted letter is right-absorbed by the first
    virtual corner. Compressing a nonempty product into that corner
    preserves its trace and gives the embedded endpoint product.
    Sector exchange gives the identity at one. Endpoint injectivity
    proves nonvanishing there, while interior injectivity proves it
    for $0<\gamma<1$. The endpoint kernel comparisons and the
    canonical interior kernel identify the stated line.
    The map from a scalar to its multiple of the actual periodic
    vector is continuous and injective. Its range projector is
    therefore continuous. Apply the compact finite-dimensional gap
    theorem to the continuous periodic Hamiltonian and this exact
    kernel projector.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Uniform periodic gap on the closed interpolation]
    \label{thm:mpo_mixed_uniform_periodic_path_gap}
    \lean{MPSTensor.MPOSymmetry.exists_uniform_mixedEndpoint_periodic_path_gap}
    \leanok
    \uses{def:mpo_mixed_extended_support,def:periodic-fixed-interaction,def:injective}
    There is $\delta>0$, depending only on the given endpoints and
    independent of $\gamma\in[0,1]$ and $N\geq2$,
    such that every $v\perp\ker H'_{\gamma,N}$ satisfies
    \begin{align}
        \delta\|v\|\leq\|H'_{\gamma,N}v\|.
    \end{align}
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
    \leanok
    \uses{thm:mpo_mixed_open_endpoint_gaps,thm:mpo_mixed_interior,
        thm:injective_strict_knabe_window,thm:open_parent_hamiltonian_kernel_ground_space,
        thm:mpo_mixed_open_gap_continuity,
        thm:mpo_mixed_periodic_endpoint_gaps,
        thm:parent_hamiltonian_gap_from_open_knabe_block,
        lem:compact_uniform_pos_nat_bounds,
        thm:mpo_mixed_periodic_line_continuity}
    At either endpoint, its derived eventual open gap gives a window
    with $m\geq2$ local terms and $m+1$ sites whose gap $\eta$ satisfies
    $1<m\eta$. In the interior, injectivity gives such a strict
    canonical open window, and the actual open operator is the same.
    Choose $1/m<\eta'<\eta$ before applying fixed-window continuity.
    For nearby interior parameters, Knabe's estimate gives the positive
    periodic bound
    \begin{align}
        \delta_K=\frac{m\eta'-1}{m-1}
    \end{align}
    at every $N\geq2m$. At either endpoint use its independently
    derived periodic gap $\delta_E>0$. Thus
    $\min\{\delta_K,\delta_E\}$ is a common bound on a neighborhood
    in the closed interval. A finite subcover gives one positive
    constant and one common lower length threshold. Finally, each of
    the finitely many shorter rings has a positive bound uniform in
    the parameter by the actual periodic kernel-line continuity.
    Taking the minimum of these constants proves the assertion.
\end{proof}

This conclusion concerns the spectral gap of the specified mixed tensor
path in square physical-support coordinates. A reduction from arbitrary
original physical alphabets, covariance under a common MPO symmetry
throughout the path, and the corresponding symmetric-phase classification
are separate assertions.
