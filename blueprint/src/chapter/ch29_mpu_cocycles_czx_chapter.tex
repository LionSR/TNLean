\chapter{The CZX model}
\label{ch:mpu_cocycles_czx}

This chapter works out the CZX model: its GHZ--blocked-cluster tensor, the
gauging of its controlled-$Z$ symmetry, the gauge-invariant subspace of its
circuit tuple, and its defects. Along the way it computes the anomaly and the
$L$-symbols of the CZX matrix product unitary, defined in the previous chapter,
and it ends with the gauged CZX state and its modified Gauss law.
